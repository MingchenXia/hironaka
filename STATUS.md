# Current scope

## Resolution goals

G1 is complete, including its four optional strengthenings. The original
contract combines the intrinsic analytic strong resolution functor and the
characteristic-zero algebraic family. The finite-codimension comparison
constructs literal native towers and transports the recursive invariant,
canonicality and maximum loci on the supported record. See the
[G1 contract](Hironaka/Resolution/Algebraic/HironakaG1OriginalCombinedResolution.lean)
and [finite-codimension comparison](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG1FiniteClosedGraphOriginalComparison.lean).

G8 has a proper embedded resolution in arbitrary positive ambient dimension.
The projection carries a locally finite cover by compatible finite blowup
histories, whose prefixes satisfy the original mark-one test conditions.
Singular phases are canonical for their native Hilbert–Samuel epochs. The
endpoint gives the smooth direct strict transform and its normal crossings
with the full indexed boundary. See the
[ordered boundary endpoint](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8FinalOrderedEndpoint.lean).

Two stronger conditions have formal counterexamples. A mixed smooth and
singular analytic example forces a smooth point into the maximum locus of the
whole original mark-one invariant. Its singular-only canonical towers leave a
singular point unresolved. A certified atlas on countably many complex plane
sheets has a fixed occupied event with arbitrarily many earlier events in its
cofinal charts. Its exact chart order has no natural-number rank. See the
[whole-record obstruction](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8MixedCompactLabelledAlternative.lean)
and [chart-order obstruction](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CountableArrangementNoClock.lean).

For compatible nested charts with finite predecessor sets, a single locally
finite growing sequence satisfies the original mark-one test conditions at
every finite prefix. The counterexample on complex plane sheets also admits an
independent chronology by the surface theorem. An independent chronology in
arbitrary dimension remains open. See the
[finite-ancestry assembly](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8RankedNestedGlobalTest.lean)
and [surface example](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8ArrangementPhysicalChronology.lean).

An all-occupied finite history realizes any permutation that preserves the
order of intersecting footprints, with the same original TEST and an
isomorphic endpoint over the base. A locally finite disjoint family of
admissible singular centres can be blown up in one step. On saturated
isolating opens, that step preserves the entire transformed marked record
under a strict comparison with the selected or empty centre blowup. See the
[finite reordering](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8FiniteSpatialRealization.lean)
and [exact local readback](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8ParallelStepReadback.lean).

On countably many events, well-founded dependency with finite incoming sets
admits distinct natural ranks. Those ranks can order each all-occupied finite
chart when they preserve the order of intersecting footprints. An indexed
swap also retains the exact endpoint comparison on every open missing either
footprint. This comparison composes through a finite swap list on an open
missing one footprint of each pair when it is swapped. A target order that
preserves intersecting pairs and pairs visible on the open supplies such a
list. A finite parallel history with selected or empty traces on a fixed
open constructs a local tape and a strict comparison preserving the whole
marked record. See the
[ranked chart](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8RankSortedSpatialChart.lean),
[indexed comparison](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8IndexedDisjointSwap.lean)
and [local tape](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8SelectedParallelTapeProducer.lean).

The event footprints of nested physical charts restrict exactly and form a
locally finite family. When their spatial dependency relation is well-founded
with finite incoming sets, one rank orders the occupied slots of every raw
singular TEST chart. A reorder of an all-occupied larger tower also preserves
its comparison to a smaller chart when it preserves the order visible there.
See the [spatial event relation](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8SpatialCofinalPrecedence.lean),
[raw chart sorting](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8RankSortedRawChart.lean)
and [nested comparison](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8NestedVisibleReordering.lean).

Charts realizing one spatial rank give the same ordered projected footprint
list on a nested open. Restriction and empty compression also identify exactly
when two specified visible steps become adjacent. Strictly matched disjoint
pairs on nested charts retain their comparison when both are swapped, including
transported suffixes. See the
[ordered traces](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8OrderedFootprintTraces.lean) and
[matched swaps](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8MatchedVisibleSwap.lean).

An actual nested comparison of charts realizing a common sorted event list
preserves the event rank. These comparisons give a single locally finite
growing original TEST. Restriction filters a disjoint exchange word at the
exact visible positions. An actual TEST can be compressed across cuts
around an occupied middle history while preserving the strict middle
comparison. See the
[matched globalization](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8MatchedSortedGlobalTest.lean)
and [cut compression](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8ActualCompressionAtCuts.lean).
For a matched cut in the larger restriction, the actual compressed exchange
preserves the smaller TEST and yields the corresponding whole comparison
with its exact terminal map. See the
[cut exchange theorem](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CompressedSwapComparison.lean).
When both centres meet the smaller open, the comparison applies to the
literal restrictions before and after the larger exchange. See the
[literal nested exchange](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8LiteralCompressedSwapComparison.lean).

The indexed exchange records the actual prefix, reversed pair, strict suffix
transport and terminal map for the same output. Its smaller visible
comparison has the exact filtered trace. A realized occupied indexed word
lifts to the smaller chart given the corresponding geometric step rule. See
the [indexed nested readback](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8NestedMoveReadback.lean)
and the [indexed word lift](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8IndexedSwapTraceLift.lean).

A spatial sorting word now retains all its intermediate towers and exact
geometric maps. Given an original nested comparison, it yields the actual
smaller TEST with the filtered order and terminal comparison. Strict
comparisons on a cover descend with a specified terminal map, and
consecutive nested comparisons give all comparisons in a chain.

One injective natural rank increasing on spatial precedence now suffices
to construct the reordered charts and their mutual literal comparisons.
For a covering original family of singular-centred TEST charts with
smooth direct endpoints, this gives a single locally finite TEST with
proper limit projection. See the
[cofinal spatial-rank endpoint](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CofinalSpatialRankEndpoint.lean).

For represented cofinal events, the common spatial rank exists exactly
when every event admits a compact set meeting every spatial ancestor's
footprint. The compact set may vary with the event. Reverse footprint
inclusion along spatial precedence supplies singleton transversals. See
the [compact transversal criterion](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8SpatialRankByFootprintConfinement.lean).

A covering physical cofinal family of finished original TEST charts with
intrinsically singular centres also yields a single locally finite original
TEST if each event has bounded incoming spatial path length. Equal-rank
events are disjoint and pack into one physical blowup. The limit projection
is proper, is an isomorphism over the original regular locus, and has a
smooth direct strict transform equal to the limit strict transform.
Bounded spatial height for a general native atlas remains open. See the
[bounded-height endpoint](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CofinalWeakRankEndpoint.lean).

An actual compact-cofinal ordered atlas of a complex three-dimensional
analytic arrangement admits no injective natural rank increasing on
spatial precedence. Its charts retain the original TEST and native
Hilbert–Samuel epoch certificates. The obstruction is infinite branching
into a single event. A separate analytic footprint model has unbounded
incoming path lengths even with well-founded precedence and width three.
See the [certified arrangement](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CombCofinalAtlas.lean)
and the [footprint model](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8AnalyticFootprintHeightExample.lean).

On countably many copies of the degree-seven cusp, actual physical
comparisons between finished original TEST charts realize unbounded
spatial path lengths into a common terminal event. The charts have
intrinsically singular centres and smooth terminal strict transforms.
The same analytic set admits a three-step whole original TEST by
placing each local stage at the same position on every sheet. Bounded
spatial height for an arbitrary native Hilbert–Samuel atlas and
independent chronology in the general case remain open. See the
[actual chart example](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CuspSheetsNoWeakRank.lean)
and the [three-stage test](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8SameClockCuspSheets.lean).

When the reordered nested charts have intrinsic singular centres and smooth
terminal strict transforms, the matched globalization preserves the singular
centre condition at every stage. Its limit gives a proper projection with
smooth direct strict transform and is an isomorphism off the original
singular locus. See the
[matched singular endpoint](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8ReorderedSingularEndpoint.lean).

In every positive ambient dimension, an analytic subset that is dense off
the initial SNC boundary and has discrete original singular locus admits a
single finite or locally finite growing original mark-one TEST resolution.
The result gives the actual proper projection and the smooth direct strict
transform, with an isomorphism off the original singular locus. See the
[discrete singular locus theorem](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8DiscreteOriginalSingular.lean).

The same conclusion holds when every connected component of the original
singular locus is compact. The components can have positive dimension. Their
ambient local finiteness follows by grouping the locally finite irreducible
components. See the [compact connected components theorem](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CompactConnectedSingular.lean).

Compact isolation of an analytic singular support is equivalent to compactness
of its connected components. A finite TEST on a base with compact singular
locus also pulls back along a holomorphic submersion as a finite TEST with a
proper smooth endpoint. The cylinder over two intersecting complex lines is
one such example with connected noncompact original singular locus. See the
[isolation criterion](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CompactIsolationCharacterization.lean) and
[submersion theorem](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CompactBaseSubmersionTest.lean).

When a base singular locus has compact connected components, its entire
finite or growing original TEST resolution pulls back along any holomorphic
submersion. The source singular components can be noncompact. In the growing
case every finite stage is an actual Cartesian pullback and each projected
centre is the exact inverse image of its base footprint. See the
[growing submersion endpoint](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8SubmersionCompactConnectedTest.lean).

When finished finite original TESTs cover the singular locus and
distinct open overlaps avoid that locus, one locally finite original TEST
exists even with unbounded local tower lengths. Its limit projection is
proper, surjective, and holomorphic, and its direct strict transform is
smooth. See the [singular-cover theorem](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8SingularCoverTestLimit.lean).
The [native symmetry theorem](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8ScheduledCuspNativeObstruction.lean)
excludes the staggered cusp schedule from the boundary-free native class.

If all local finite TEST lengths share a bound, the literal global stage
at that bound is a complete finite TEST. The regular-overlap theorem also
accepts locally finite growing TESTs on the singular cover. Stable finite
cuts preserve the restricted marked ideal, singular centres, and endpoint.
See the [bounded endpoint](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8BoundedOpenTestEndpoint.lean)
and [growing singular-cover endpoint](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8GrowingSingularCoverTestLimit.lean).

The complete native history preserves each occupied footprint under input
symmetries. A common compact set of singular-orbit representatives gives
finite serial rank on the cofinal charts. Transitivity within each occupied
footprint suffices for injective natural rank on spatial ancestry.
For charts transitive on the whole singular locus, there is a proper
intrinsic global original TEST endpoint.
See [compact orbit rank](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CompactOrbitReference.lean),
[slotwise spatial rank](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8NativeSlotwiseSpatialRank.lean),
and the [homogeneous endpoint](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8NativeHomogeneousEndpoint.lean).

Physical comparisons of local windows transport the count of predecessors
visible through a point when they carry the chosen occupied event.
A uniformly bounded weak spatial rank gives a finite global TEST, including
when infinitely many original events share the available ranks.
A growing TEST supported in a closed ambient set contained in its window

See [local point counts](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8CofinalLocalPointRank.lean),

## De Jong alterations over arbitrary fields

The alteration theorem is part of the Hironaka library under
`Hironaka.Algebra` and `Hironaka.Geometry`. The source inventory records the
revision used to import the proof and the hashes of the normalized modules.
The original contributor and license headers are preserved.
`Hironaka.deJongAlterationTheorem` has only `(k : Type u) [Field k]` as inputs;
`Hironaka.exists_alterationWithBoundary` specializes it to an actual variety and
prescribed proper closed subset. Both are available from `import Hironaka.DeJong`
and the full `import Hironaka`.

The output retains the original variety, genuine alteration, regular projective
compactification and the whole strict SNC boundary, with its exact prescribed
support. Geometric integrality and perfectness are not assumed. The separate
perfect-field generically-étale refinement and other independent paper results
are outside this integration. See [the API and source provenance](docs/DEJONG.md).
The standalone source check verifies all 1,806 integrated modules and their import closure.

## Singular strata

A finite or locally finite growing TEST for a subset of the original
singular locus can be used on the whole original carrier along the same
physical towers. A smooth strict transform of the subset meeting the
accumulated boundary normally can be the next centre.
See [the stratum lift](Hironaka/Resolution/HironakaG8SingularStratumTestLift.lean).
