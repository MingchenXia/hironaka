\section{Canonical centre compatibility from any genuine monic family}

Let $J\subseteq S=\mathbb C[[x_1,\ldots,x_n]]$, fix a monomial order,
and let $V$ be its actual initial vertices. Write $R_J$ for the unique
normal representative modulo the entire ideal $J$, supported outside its
initial diagram. For a coordinate centre $A$, write $C_A$ for its formal
ideal and $w_A$ for the corresponding coordinate weight.

\begin{lemma}[The canonical normal representative preserves the whole centre filtration]
\label{hir:actual-canonical-remainder-centre-power-from-family}
\uses{hir:actual-monic-family-canonical-remainder,hir:actual-monic-family-centre-power-division}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCanonicalCentreCompatibility.actualFormalRemainder_mem_coordinate_power_of_family}
\leanok
Suppose a genuine monic vertex family $(B_\alpha)$ satisfies
$B_\alpha\in C_A^{w_A(\alpha)}$ for every actual vertex. Then for every
$q\geq0$ and every $F\in C_A^q$, one has $R_J(F)\in C_A^q$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The same-family weighted recursion preserves the weight lower bound $q$
of $F$: every coefficient of its actual remainder at weight less than $q$
vanishes. Indeed, the coefficient formulation of each hypothesis
$B_\alpha\in C_A^{w_A(\alpha)}$ is exactly the lower-weight vanishing
condition required by that recursion. Likewise the whole power membership
of $F$ gives the required numerator coefficient vanishings. The coefficient
criterion for the entire coordinate power therefore puts this actual family
remainder in $C_A^q$. Independence of the normal representative from the
chosen monic family identifies this same remainder with $R_J(F)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Centre compatibility of the canonical reduced members is equivalent to that of some monic family]
\label{hir:actual-canonical-centre-compatibility-iff-monic-family}
\uses{hir:actual-canonical-remainder-centre-power-from-family,hir:actual-reduced-basis-entire-filtered-division}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCanonicalCentreCompatibility.actualReducedVertexSeries_mem_coordinate_power_of_family,Hironaka.HironakaActualCanonicalCentreCompatibility.actualReducedVertexCentreCompatibility_iff_exists_family}
\leanok
Every centre-compatible monic vertex family forces the actual canonical
reduced members $F_\alpha$ to lie in $C_A^{w_A(\alpha)}$.
Consequently the existence of such a monic family is equivalent to this
same literal compatibility for all canonical reduced members.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine monomial $x^\alpha$ belongs to $C_A^{w_A(\alpha)}$ by the
whole coordinate-power coefficient criterion. The preceding lemma puts
$R_J(x^\alpha)$ in the same power. The actual canonical reduced vertex
member is $F_\alpha=x^\alpha-R_J(x^\alpha)$; subtraction in that entire
ideal proves its required membership. This proves one implication of the
equivalence. Conversely the existing canonical reduced family is itself
a genuine monic vertex family, with actual ideal membership, coefficient
$1$ and all preceding coefficients zero. Literal compatibility of its
members thus supplies the required family. No assertion of convergence
of these formal canonical members is used in either direction.
\end{proof}
