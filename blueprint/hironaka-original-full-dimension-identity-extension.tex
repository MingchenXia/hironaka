\section{The actual full-dimensional identity branch}

The full-dimensional branch is geometric: its original vanishing ideal
vanishes on an open component, so no nonzero-along invariant-domain
hypothesis is imposed. Initial SNC boundaries are retained as whole
ordered lists. No compactness of the ambient manifold or smooth source
is needed.

\begin{lemma}[An open carrier is transverse to the entire SNC boundary]
\label{hir:original-open-carrier-snc-transverse}
\uses{hir:snc}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFullDimensionIdentity.isTransverse_of_isOpen}
\leanok
Let $W$ be any open subset of a complex manifold and let $E$ be an
original ordered SNC boundary. Then $W$ is transverse to $E$ and hence
has normal crossings with it.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At any point, use the actual simultaneous SNC coordinates for the
entire boundary and take the empty set of equations for the ambient
manifold. The existing boundary coordinate singletons are disjoint
from this empty set, so these same coordinates prove transversality
of the whole ambient manifold. At any point of $W$, openness makes
$W$ and the ambient manifold agree on a neighbourhood. Locality of
adapted coordinate families therefore proves transversality of $W$
with the same entire ordered boundary. Transversality implies normal
crossings.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The full-dimensional original elementary map and its actual smooth square]
\label{hir:original-full-dimension-identity-smooth-square}
\uses{hir:original-open-carrier-snc-transverse,hir:submersion-nc-entire-snc-preimage,hir:submersion-cartesian-tower-whole-universal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFullDimensionIdentity.full_dimension_identity_smooth_square}
\leanok
Let $V\subseteq X^n$ be an original closed irreducible analytic
subvariety of dimension $n$, and let $E$ be any original ordered SNC
boundary. The actual identity tower $T$ resolves this full-dimensional
branch: its conventional strict carrier is exactly $V$, a closed
connected submanifold of dimension $n$, transverse to $E$; the actual
exceptional locus and intrinsic singular locus of $V$ are empty. Its
map is holomorphic, proper, surjective and an isomorphism everywhere,
and it is an admissible empty blowup sequence with the unchanged
entire ordered boundary $E$.

For every actual holomorphic submersion $b:Y^m\to X^n$, the identity
tower $Q$ on $Y$, with upper arrow exactly $b$, is the genuine actual
Cartesian pullback of this same $T$. Its conventional strict carrier
is exactly $b^{-1}V$, a closed submanifold regular of dimension $m$
at all its points and transverse to the entire literal boundary
$E'=E.map(b^{-1})$. Its map has the same empty exceptional, admissible,
proper, surjective and everywhere-isomorphic dictionary. The whole
strict-transform square commutes and the actual holomorphic universal
property holds. No source connectedness, source compactness or global
inequality $n\le m$ is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine full-dimensional irreducible-subvariety theorem makes
$V$ a closed connected submanifold of dimension $n$. Every one of its
points is therefore regular of ambient dimension, so its carrier is
open as well as closed and its intrinsic singular locus is empty.
Apply the preceding lemma to the actual initial boundary. The identity
map has empty exceptional locus, and the conventional strict transform
is the closure of the original carrier; closedness identifies it
with that carrier. The actual zero-step sequence is admissible with
unchanged boundary; holomorphicity, properness, surjectivity and its
everywhere inverse are those of the identity.

The continuous map $b$ pulls the same open and closed carrier back to
an open and closed source carrier. At a source point it agrees locally
with the whole source manifold, which is regular of dimension $m$;
locality of actual regular coordinates proves the claimed regularity.
The genuine smooth SNC pullback theorem supplies the entire ordered
boundary $E'$. Apply the preceding lemma again. The actual zero-step
pullback constructor gives $Q,T$ and upper arrow exactly $b$, whose
actual universal property gives the Cartesian square. Both literal
strict-transform formulas now give the whole strict-set equality,
including the empty-source case. All source identity-map properties
follow as in the target. No vanishing-ideal invariant domain is used.
\end{proof}
