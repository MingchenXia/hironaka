\section{Intrinsic Bennett nonincrease for an actual constant-Hilbert--Samuel centre}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-hs-constant-centre-bennett}

All Hilbert--Samuel functions below are those of the entire original
reduced vanishing ideals. The strict transforms and blowups are the
actual geometric ones. A fixed-flattening intermediate records the
exact use of canonical reduced normal powers; the geometric endpoints
produce that property from the original hypotheses.

\begin{theorem}[Reduced normal powers in the supplied chart give actual geometric nonincrease]
\label{hir:actual-fixed-chart-reduced-centre-geometric-bennett}
\uses{hir:actual-fixed-flattening-original-blowup-hs-comparisons,hir:actual-reduced-centre-geometric-bennett}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSConstantCentreBennett.hilbertSamuelAt_actual_fixed_flattening_blowup_le}
\leanok
Let $\pi:Y\to X$ be the genuine blowup along $C$ in dimension
$d+t$, and fix a genuine centred flattening $\Phi$ of the same
centre at $\pi(z)$ to $\mathbb C^d\times0$. For any $W\subset X$,
let $I_0$ be its whole original vanishing origin ideal in this
same flattening, including the actual zero translation.
Suppose each canonical reduced vertex series of the actual
whole Taylor ideal of $I_0$ belongs to the normal-coordinate
ideal to the normal weight of its vertex. Then for every
order $\ell$,
\[
 H_{\operatorname{strict}_\pi W,z}(\ell)
 \le H_{W,\pi(z)}(\ell).
\]
This intermediate hypothesis is precisely the genuine canonical
reduced normal-power property for the supplied original ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the fixed-flattening blowup comparison theorem to the
supplied $\Phi$. It internally constructs true source coordinates,
an actual normal pivot $J\ge d$ and parameters, retaining the same
target map and the literal model subset $A_\Phi(W)$. Its whole
origin-ideal equality identifies $I_0$ with the model vanishing
origin ideal, so the given canonical reduced normal powers
apply to that identical ideal.

Since $J<d+t$ and $J\ge d$, the integer $J-d$ is a genuine
normal index $j<t$, with $J=d+j$. Thus the actual whole polynomial
square is exactly the normal-indexed model blowup used by the
proved reduced-centre geometric Bennett theorem. That model
theorem gives its Hilbert--Samuel inequality at zero. The two
proved exact intrinsic comparisons identify the left side with
the actual strict-transform value at $z$ and the right side
with the original value at $\pi(z)$. Substituting them proves
the assertion, including exceptional points and every order.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original constant-Hilbert--Samuel regular-centre geometry gives actual nonincrease]
\label{hir:actual-original-hs-constant-centre-geometric-bennett}
\uses{hir:actual-original-centre-reduced-normal-powers,hir:actual-fixed-chart-reduced-centre-geometric-bennett}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSConstantCentreBennett.hilbertSamuelAt_actual_hs_constant_centre_blowup_le}
\leanok
Let $\pi$ be a genuine blowup along $C$ in dimension $d+t$,
and let $z\in Y$. Suppose $C$ is regular of dimension $d$ at
$\pi(z)$ and $W$ is an original complex analytic subset.
Suppose its entire intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel function is
constant along $C$, with value that at $\pi(z)$.
Then the preceding actual strict-transform inequality holds
at $z$ in every order. No chart, free-module, monic-family,
canonical-series or compatibility certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The whole original vanishing ideal of $W$ is coherent by the
proved analytic coherence theorem. Apply the original-only
canonical reduced-normal-power producer to this same ideal,
the given regular centre and its all-order intrinsic constancy.
It chooses one actual flattening $\Phi$, its true domain,
zero-centering and whole centre-membership equivalence, and
proves canonical reduced normal powers for its same origin
ideal $I_0$.

Retain that $\Phi$ before constructing any blowup source
coordinates. Apply the preceding fixed-chart theorem directly
to this supplied flattening and the internally proved normal
powers. It constructs the actual source square for that very
$\Phi$ and gives the asserted intrinsic inequality. There is
no identification of independently chosen coordinate maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every point of the genuine blowup satisfies Bennett nonincrease]
\label{hir:actual-all-point-hs-constant-smooth-centre-bennett}
\uses{hir:actual-original-hs-constant-centre-geometric-bennett,hir:actual-blowup-off-centre-intrinsic-reduced-hs}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSConstantCentreBennett.hilbertSamuelAt_actual_hs_constant_smooth_centre_blowup_le}
\leanok
Let $\pi:Y\to X$ be the genuine blowup along a closed complex
submanifold $C$ of an ambient complex manifold of dimension $n$.
Let $W\subset X$ be complex analytic, and suppose every two
points of $C$ have the same entire intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel
function for $W$. Then for every $z\in Y$ and every order
$\ell$ the actual strict-transform value at $z$ is at most
the original value at $\pi(z)$.
The centre may have different dimensions on different components.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $\pi(z)\notin C$, the genuine complement biholomorphism
identifies the whole actual strict-transform stalk with the
original stalk, so their intrinsic values are equal.
If $\pi(z)\in C$, closed-submanifold regularity supplies its
actual local centre dimension $d\le n$. Write $n=d+t$ using
the genuine nonnegative difference. Pairwise constancy on $C$
gives the entire original function at every centre point equal
to that at $\pi(z)$. Apply the preceding original-only theorem
in this actual dimension decomposition. It proves the desired
inequality for that point and every order. These two cases
cover all points of the actual blowup.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original singular geometry gives a proper singular-centre blowup with all-point nonincrease]
\label{hir:actual-original-singular-centre-proper-bennett-blowup}
\uses{hir:actual-original-hs-constant-singular-centre-proper-blowup,hir:actual-all-point-hs-constant-smooth-centre-bennett}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSConstantCentreBennett.exists_actual_original_singular_centre_proper_blowup_bennett}
\leanok
Let $W$ be a compact complex analytic subset, pure of dimension
$d<n$, which is not a closed complex submanifold. Let $E$ be
the entire original ordered simple-normal-crossings boundary.
Then the original geometry supplies a nonempty centre
$C\subset W\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_d(W)$ admissible for
the original marked vanishing ideal with this same boundary,
and a genuine proper blowup $\pi:Y\to X$ along $C$, such that
the entire original intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel function is
constant along $C$ and
\[
 H_{\operatorname{strict}_\pi W,z}(\ell)
 \le H_{W,\pi(z)}(\ell)
 \quad\text{for all }z\in Y\text{ and }\ell\ge0.
\]
The blowup is an isomorphism over the complement of the current
singular locus and retains the full genuine transformed SNC
boundary. The centre is the actual canonical maximum for the
same internally formed auxiliary Hilbert--Samuel reference ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the original compact pure nonsmooth singular-centre
producer with the entire given boundary. It chooses a genuine
singular reference point, a nonempty admissible centre $C$
inside that same current singular locus, and the proper
blowup, regular-open inverse and transformed SNC boundary.
It proves constancy of every original intrinsic HS order
on this same centre, relative to the chosen reference point,
and identifies the centre with the actual auxiliary canonical
maximum.

Admissibility supplies genuine closed-submanifold regularity
of $C$. If $x,y\in C$, their HS values both equal the chosen
reference value in every order, hence are equal to one another.
The preceding all-point original smooth-centre Bennett theorem
therefore applies to the very same $C$ and actual blowup.
It gives the displayed inequality at every point, including
over $C$, while all previously constructed geometric and
boundary witnesses are retained unchanged.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The assertions concern actual one-step geometric nonincrease.
They do not identify the auxiliary centre invariant with the
original driver invariant, prove strict decrease, or assert a
finite terminating resolution sequence whose centres lie in
each current strict-transform singular locus.
\end{remark}
