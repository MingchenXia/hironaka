\begin{lemma}[The stopped local maps agree at every actual quotient representative]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-local-quotient-independence}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-atlas-transition-square,hir:sharp-actual-endpoint-saturated-local-range,hir:sharp-actual-endpoint-local-range-biholomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stopped_local_inclusion_eq}
\leanok
For the SAME retained actual atlas, fix a natural stage $k$. Whenever
two actual chart histories have their literal full lengths at most $k$,
points representing the SAME actual stage point have stopped terminal
points representing the SAME original terminal point. No representation
independence or quotient map is supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual original projections agree for the two representations. The
second representation therefore puts the first point in the ENTIRE real
stage overlap. The genuine quotient transition relation sends this point
to a point in the second chart with exactly the same inclusion. Actual
open-embedding injectivity identifies it with the given second point.
Apply the already proved square for the SAME chosen stage and terminal
transitions. The true terminal quotient transition relation identifies
its terminal inclusions. This proves the required equality without any
injectivity of the original blowdown projection.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual stopped maps glue to a genuine biholomorphism on their entire stopped-open union]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-open-global-biholomorphism}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-local-quotient-independence,hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-stage-terminal-range}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stoppedBaseOpen,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.mem_stoppedBaseOpen,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stoppedStageOpen,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stoppedTerminalOpen,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stoppedOpenMap,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stoppedOpenMap_bijective,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stoppedOpenMap_contMDiff,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stoppedOpenEquiv_symm_contMDiff,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stoppedOpenBiholomorphism,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stoppedOpenBiholomorphism_projection,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedOpenGluing.stoppedOpenBiholomorphism_local}
\leanok
Let $U_k$ be the union of the original opens whose actual full histories
have length at most $k$. On the ENTIRE inverse image of $U_k$ in the
actual global stage $Y_k$, the stopped local identifications glue to a
genuine biholomorphism onto the ENTIRE inverse image of $U_k$ in the
original terminal endpoint. It has exactly the previously constructed
local stopped maps, and its whole original projection square commutes.
No global map, inverse, overlap certificate or stopping bound is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A point of the first inverse image has an actual stopped chart over its
original projection. The ENTIRE saturated range dictionary supplies an
actual chart representative. Send it by its genuine stopped terminal
biholomorphism and then by the original terminal inclusion. The first
lemma proves independence of every representative and hence gives the
exact local formula on each stopped range. The stopped projection square
puts the image in the second entire inverse image and proves the whole
projection identity.

To prove injectivity, choose a stopped chart above the projection of one
source point. Equality of terminal images and the projection square put
both source points in this SAME saturated stage range. Their local
formulas, the genuine terminal inclusion embedding and stopped local
biholomorphism give equality of the source representatives. For
surjectivity, choose a stopped chart over any target projection. Its
whole saturated terminal range gives a representative; the actual inverse
of its stopped biholomorphism gives a source representative. Both true
inverse laws and the local formula give the prescribed target.

The resulting actual bijection is holomorphic on each stopped range by
its genuine local formula. These ranges are open and cover its entire
source open, so it is holomorphic everywhere. Its actual inverse has,
on each stopped terminal range, precisely the inverse genuine local
biholomorphism, by the proved bijection and the exact forward local
formula. These actual target ranges are also open and cover its entire
target open. Thus its inverse is holomorphic as well. All open-subtype
maps are genuine restrictions and inclusions. These two actual maps and
inverse laws give the asserted biholomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original curve data give increasing stopped opens covering every original point]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-stopped-open-cover}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-open-global-biholomorphism,hir:sharp-actual-original-retained-local-recipe-family}

\leanok
Positive ambient dimension and ONLY the original smooth pure curve,
ordered SNC and density data internally give the preceding stopped-open
biholomorphisms. The opens $U_k$ increase with $k$ and cover the ENTIRE
original ambient space. Each original point belongs to $U_L$, where $L$
is its own actual retained history length. No uniform bound or caller
atlas is supplied. Compact uniform finite stopping remains separate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the SAME original retained atlas and its genuine pointwise
open cover from the original geometric data. All hypotheses of the
preceding construction are thereby internally supplied. The defining
union uses the literal full length of each retained history, so any
history counted at $k$ is also counted at a later stage. This proves
monotonicity directly. For an arbitrary original point choose its own
actual atlas chart, which contains that point by the original producer.
At its actual finite full length that chart occurs in the defining
union. Thus every point belongs to one of these increasing opens. No
maximum over the infinite point-indexed family is used. Together with
the preceding genuine maps this identifies the actual history with the
terminal endpoint locally over every original point; finite uniform
stopping under compactness is not asserted by this covering statement.
\end{proof}
