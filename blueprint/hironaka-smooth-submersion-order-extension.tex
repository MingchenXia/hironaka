\section{Actual order under holomorphic submersions}

This chapter produces the first order layer of smooth functoriality from an
actual holomorphic submersion between manifolds of possibly different
dimensions. It does not assume a section or an equality of invariants.
Compatibility of the full higher invariant and of its selected blowup
sequence remains a separate obligation.

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:submersion-local-section}
Let $f:Y\to X$ be a holomorphic submersion at $y$. There is a map
$s:X\to Y$ that is holomorphic on a neighbourhood of $f(y)$, with
$s(f(y))=y$ and $f\circ s=\operatorname{id}$ on a neighbourhood of $f(y)$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionOrder.exists_holomorphic_localSection}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the genuine submersion charts $\phi$ at $y$ and $\psi$ at $f(y)$,
and the complex linear equivalence $L$ for which
$\psi\circ f\circ\phi^{-1}=\operatorname{pr}_1\circ L$.
Put $v=\operatorname{pr}_2 L(\phi(y))$ and define
$s(z)=\phi^{-1}(L^{-1}(\psi(z),v))$.
The original identity at $y$ gives
$L(\phi(y))=(\psi(f(y)),v)$, so $s(f(y))=y$.
The open chart targets and continuity give a neighbourhood of $f(y)$
on which every expression stays in its actual chart domain. Chart
holomorphicity and the affine complex linear slice prove that $s$ is
holomorphic there. On that neighbourhood the submersion chart equation
gives $\psi(f(s(z)))=\psi(z)$; injectivity on the actual chart source
then gives $f(s(z))=z$. This produces all section properties internally.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:submersion-order-ge}
For an actual ideal of holomorphic germs $I$ on $X$, at a point where $f$
is a holomorphic submersion, and every natural $q$,
$I_{f(y)}\subseteq\mathfrak m_{f(y)}^q$ if and only if
$(f^*I)_y\subseteq\mathfrak m_y^q$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionOrder.orderGE_pullback_iff}
\leanok
\uses{hir:submersion-local-section}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For the forward direction, each actual pullback generator is $a\circ f$
with $a\in I_{f(y)}$. The existing genuine pullback theorem for powers
of point ideals places it in $\mathfrak m_y^q$. Closure under finite
holomorphic combinations gives the entire pullback ideal inclusion.
Conversely, for each original $a\in I_{f(y)}$, its actual pullback
$a\circ f$ belongs to $(f^*I)_y$, hence to $\mathfrak m_y^q$.
Compose with the internally produced holomorphic section through $y$.
Pullback of point-ideal powers gives $(a\circ f)\circ s$ in
$\mathfrak m_{f(y)}^q$; the genuine local identity $f\circ s=\operatorname{id}$
and locality of germ membership identify this germ with $a$.
Both directions apply to the original full ideal, not just one generator.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:submersion-order}
For the same original data,
$\operatorname{ord}_y(f^*I)=\operatorname{ord}_{f(y)} I$ in
$\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\}$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionOrder.order_pullback_eq}
\leanok
\uses{hir:submersion-order-ge}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual order is the supremum of the natural $q$ for which the full
stalk lies in the $q$-th point-ideal power. The preceding equivalence
identifies these indexing predicates exactly. Their suprema are equal,
including infinite order and the zero ideal.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:submersion-cosupport}
For a holomorphic submersion $f$ and a coherent original ideal $I$, every
marked cosupport satisfies
$\operatorname{cosupp}(f^*I,q)=f^{-1}\operatorname{cosupp}(I,q)$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionOrder.cosupport_pullback_eq}
\leanok
\uses{hir:submersion-order-ge}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Coherence supplies holomorphicity of every original ideal germ.
At each actual source point, apply the preceding pointwise order
equivalence to the genuine submersion at that point. Unfolding the
actual marked cosupport gives equality of the two sets by extensionality.
No compactness, equal-dimensional inverse, or section certificate is an input.
\end{proof}
