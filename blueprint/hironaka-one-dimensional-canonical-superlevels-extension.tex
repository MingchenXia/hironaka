\begin{lemma}[Actual zero-dimensional analytic geometry]
\label{hir:analytic-dimzero-all-points-regular}

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOneDimensionalCanonicalSuperlevels.regularAtDim_zero_of_analytic_dimLE_zero,Hironaka.HironakaOneDimensionalCanonicalSuperlevels.isDiscrete_of_analytic_dimLE_zero}
\leanok
An actual analytic set of geometric dimension at most zero consists
entirely of genuine regular zero-dimensional points and is discrete.
The statement includes ambient dimension zero and the empty set.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Express the actual analytic set as the union of its actual global
regular-locus components. The dimension bound forces the generating
regular point of each component to have dimension zero, and every
point in that regular-locus component has the same genuine dimension.
Its preconnectedness and the actual zero-dimensional regular patch
force it to lie in the singleton of its generating point. The closed
singleton also contains its closure. Thus any point of the analytic
set is that actual generating regular point. Its genuine flattening
gives an open neighbourhood meeting the whole set precisely in that
singleton, which proves discreteness.
\end{proof}
