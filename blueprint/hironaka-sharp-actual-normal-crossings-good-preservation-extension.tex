\section{Dynamic higher-dimensional prefixes preserve the entire original NC-good locus}

Let $W$ be a closed smooth carrier in a complex manifold of positive ambient
dimension, of fixed positive dimension $d+1$, and let $E$ be the entire ordered
original SNC divisor. Assume $W\subset\overline{W\setminus\bigcup E}$.
For an actual finite tower $T$, write $J_{i,T}$ for the independently defined
chronological member ideals, $P_T$ for their actual carrier-relative product,
and $Z_T$ for the FIRST defect: the product order exceeds the number of
distinct proper whole germ ideals. Empty positions and boundary slots are
retained. The original good open is the complement of the literal original
sharp bad locus; it includes every point outside $W$ and every point where
$W$ has normal crossings with the whole original $E$.

\begin{theorem}[The entire FIRST defect through a genuine empty history]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-empty-history-first-defect}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-original-composite-total-all-exceptional-residual,hir:original-all-empty-whole-boundary,hir:original-all-empty-tower-projection,hir:sharp-actual-original-controlled-history-current-first-order,bcalc:nonmonomial-order-local,bcalc:restrict-empty-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualNormalCrossingsGoodPreservation.chronologicalDistinctDefect_of_isEmptyTower}
\leanok
For every actual finite tower $T$ all of whose centres are empty,
\[
 Z_T=T^{-1}(Z_{\rm original}),
\]
where $Z_{\rm original}$ is independently defined using the original product
and the quotient of the proper original trace germ ideals. This is a whole-set
equality, including points outside the carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An actual blowup of the empty centre is a biholomorphism, so induction makes
the literal composite projection a biholomorphism. Every closed original set
has its actual inverse image as strict transform. The entire born divisor is
the ordered list of $\operatorname{length}(T)$ empty members. The whole
composite-total residual theorem identifies each $J_{i,T}$ with the
nonmonomial part of the literal original member pullback. Appending an empty
member does not change that part. Removing each empty entry therefore gives
the actual carrier restriction of this raw pullback.

For every holomorphic germ on the current ambient manifold, composition with
the actual inverse projection identifies membership in this entire restricted
ideal with membership in the original trace ideal. Conversely, composition
with the projection and its two inverse identities recover every original
germ. Thus both directions of equality of whole member germs are preserved,
as is properness, tested by the unit germ. The induced equivalence of the
actual quotient sets takes the image of precisely the proper positions onto
the corresponding original image. Their finite cardinalities are equal;
no indexed incidence count is substituted for the quotient count.

The actual controlled/chronological product theorem identifies the order of
$P_T$ with the nonmonomial order of the unchanged original positive marked
product along $T$. Empty-prefix order transport identifies this with the
original ordinary product order. The actual carrier is the inverse image of
$W$. These carrier, count and order identities give both directions of the
displayed whole defect equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual dynamic prefix avoids the original good open]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-dynamic-prefix-original-good-avoidance}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-empty-history-first-defect,hir:sharp-original-chronological-distinct-defect-open,hir:sharp-actual-dynamic-full-maximum-history,hir:sharp-original-distinct-defect-disjoint-entire-good-locus,bcalc:restrict-empty-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualNormalCrossingsGoodPreservation.dynamic_history_centresAvoid_originalGood,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualNormalCrossingsGoodPreservation.dynamic_history_centresOver_originalSharpBad}
\leanok
Let $T$ be an actual dynamic full-maximum history and an actual test history
for the unchanged original positive marked product. Every centre of $T$
avoids the inverse image of the entire original good open. Equivalently,
every centre lies over the literal original sharp bad locus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual finite tower. The assertion is vacuous for its empty
prefix. Suppose it holds for a prefix $P$. Restrict $P$ to the entire
original good open $U$. Its centres are genuinely empty by the restriction
calculus. The whole chronological FIRST restriction identity, followed by
the preceding empty-history identity, identifies the restriction of $Z_P$
with the inverse image of the original FIRST defect for the original data
restricted to $U$.

At every point of $U$ inside its carrier, the original NC coordinates and
the original density give genuine transversality. The actual original
product-order/distinct-trace theorem therefore says that original FIRST
defect is empty there; outside the carrier it is empty by definition.
Equivalently one may use the whole original FIRST restriction identity and
its already proved disjointness from the original good locus. Hence $Z_P$
has no point above $U$. A nonempty dynamic next centre is the whole full
maximum on $Z_P$, so is a subset of $Z_P$ and also misses $U$. If the defect
has cleared, the recorded next centre is actually empty and misses $U$ as
well. This proves the induction without assuming avoidance. Taking the
complement of $U$ gives the stated whole centre support.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original finite dynamic prefixes preserve all original NC-good points]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-original-dynamic-good-preserving-prefix}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-dynamic-prefix-original-good-avoidance,hir:sharp-actual-original-dynamic-full-boundary-prefix,hir:admissible-sequence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualNormalCrossingsGoodPreservation.exists_original_dynamic_good_preserving_prefix}
\leanok
If $W$ is compact, then for each prescribed finite length $k$ there is one
actual dynamic tower of that length which retains the original positive
test history, the full ambient original-plus-born boundary test history,
and the full ordered SNC divisor. Its centres lie over the original sharp
bad locus, and its same composite projection is proper and is an isomorphism
over every original smooth NC-good point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine original-input dynamic-prefix construction chooses one actual
tower $T$ before any added conclusion. Apply the preceding theorem to that
same tower and its actual original test history. Its second actual test
history is for the ambient unit, zero-mark template with initial boundary
the entire original $E$. Together with the now proved centre support, this
gives a genuine admissible blowup sequence over the literal original bad
locus, with the same composite projection and the same whole ordered final
boundary. The actual admissible-sequence theorem makes this projection an
isomorphism over the complement of that locus, which is exactly the whole
original good open. Properness is the actual finite-blowup properness
theorem. The retained SNC conclusion is the whole original-strict-plus-born
list already supplied by the same original prefix construction.
\end{proof}

This gives preservation for each genuine finite dynamic prefix in every
fixed positive carrier dimension. It neither constructs a uniform clearing
time nor identifies a restarted full invariant with its chronological lower
history. Strict modified-invariant decrease, higher-dimensional sharp
termination and the full G4 final endpoint remain separate obligations.
