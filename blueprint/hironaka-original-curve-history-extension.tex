\begin{lemma}[Actual whole monomial transport on every original suffix]
\label{hir:original-interior-all-tail-whole-monomial-transport}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-interior-empty-geometric-dictionary,bcalc:monomial-invariant-empty-member}

\[
 \gamma(M,H\epsilon S,y)=\operatorname{shiftVal}(|M.E|+|H|)

\]
No ambient marked-record equality or nonempty-suffix premise is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}

carrier and mark. Actual closedness of $K$ and both actual coherent ideals

along EVERY suffix. This theorem includes the empty suffix, where the ideals
need agree only along their literal carrier; after a genuine blowup their
whole records agree. The actual empty transform is the empty-step base with
an empty boundary inserted at $|K.E|=|M.E|+|H|$. The complete actual indexed
monomial insertion identity supplies the displayed shift, and restricted
equivalence supplies the whole old gamma value on the SAME $S$. Thus no false
ambient equality at the immediate empty step is needed.
\end{proof}
