\section{Original whole ideals internally produce convergent canonical reduced vertices}

\begin{theorem}[Actual original ideal elements lift every finite jet of their whole formal extension]
\label{hir:actual-original-ideal-finite-jet-lift}
\uses{hir:actual-original-formal-whole-jet-map-bijective,hir:actual-formal-variable-power-coefficient-kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualOriginalVertexConvergence.exists_original_ideal_finite_jet_lift}
\leanok
For every actual ideal $J$ of original holomorphic germs, every $F$ in
its whole original Taylor extension, and every natural $q$, there is an
actual $g\in J$ whose original Taylor coefficients agree with those of
$F$ at every exponent of degree less than $q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose the original Taylor map with the quotient by the genuine
formal variable ideal's $q$th power. This actual map is surjective:
the original polynomial evaluation of the degree-$q$ truncation of
any formal series has exactly its quotient class. The class of $F$
lies in the image of $J$ under this same map, by composition of ideal
images. Surjectivity identifies membership in that image with the
existence of an actual preimage $g\in J$. Equality of the quotient
classes says that the Taylor difference belongs to the genuine
variable power. Its proved coefficient kernel gives every asserted
coefficient equality, including the vacuous case $q=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual formal vertices have internally produced original monic germ witnesses]
\label{hir:actual-original-monic-vertex-family}
\uses{hir:actual-original-ideal-finite-jet-lift,hir:actual-formal-variable-power-coefficient-kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualOriginalVertexConvergence.exists_original_monic_vertex_family}
\leanok
For every actual original germ ideal $J$, its genuine whole formal
Taylor extension has a monic degree-lexicographic vertex family all
of whose actual series are original Taylor images of internally
chosen actual members of $J$. No family or analytic witness is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each genuine vertex $a$ already has a monic member of the whole formal
ideal. Apply the preceding theorem at $q=|a|+1$. Its original witness
has coefficient one at $a$ and retains every lower coefficient:
every degree-lexicographically smaller exponent has degree at most
$|a|$. These lower coefficients are zero for the actual formal vertex
member. The witness's Taylor image lies in the same whole ideal by
the original ideal image map. Choose these genuine witnesses at the
internally finite vertex set and use their unchanged Taylor images
as the family, with the just-proved monicity and lower vanishing.
This construction also applies when the vertex set is empty.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An arbitrary finite original germ family has an internally common positive coefficient radius]
\label{hir:actual-original-finite-family-coefficient-radius}
\uses{hir:actual-original-taylor-positive-coefficient-mass,hir:actual-anisotropic-coefficient-mass}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualOriginalVertexConvergence.exists_finite_originalTaylor_common_mass}
\leanok
For every finite family of original holomorphic germs, there is one
positive uniform radius at which every unchanged original Taylor
series has finite absolute coefficient mass. No individual or common
radius is an input; the assertion includes an empty family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the finite index set. For the empty set choose radius one.
For an inserted germ, original holomorphy produces a positive radius
for its actual Taylor series. Take the minimum of that radius and
the positive radius supplied for the preceding family. Positivity
is retained and coefficient mass is monotone under decreasing each
positive coordinate radius, so all original series converge at this
same smaller radius. Apply the result to the actual finite universe.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every whole original ideal has genuinely convergent canonical reduced formal vertices]
\label{hir:actual-original-ideal-reduced-vertex-convergence}
\uses{hir:actual-original-monic-vertex-family,hir:actual-original-finite-family-coefficient-radius,hir:actual-deg-lex-vertex-small-radius,hir:actual-coefficient-polydisc-holomorphy}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualOriginalVertexConvergence.exists_original_reduced_vertices_finite_mass}
\leanok
For every original holomorphic germ ideal $J$, there is an internally
produced positive polyradius at which all canonical reduced vertex
series of its unchanged whole formal Taylor extension have finite
absolute coefficient mass. Their actual evaluations are holomorphic
on the genuine open polydisc. No chosen family, convergence certificate,
radius, weight, inverse or reduced-basis certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the genuine original monic vertex family and its actual
original germ witnesses by the second theorem. The third theorem
supplies a single positive radius for all their unchanged Taylor
series. Each true leading monomial has finite coefficient support,
so subtracting it retains finite absolute coefficient mass at this
same radius. The genuine small-radius vertex theorem now internally
constructs positive separating weights and a smaller polyradius with
actual perturbation norm less than one. Its proved Neumann division
therefore gives finite coefficient mass for every canonical reduced
vertex of the same whole ideal. Actual convergent evaluation is
holomorphic on the open polydisc by the proved several-variable
holomorphic evaluation theorem. No witness or radius is imported
from the desired conclusion.
\end{proof}

Realization of these convergent formal series as original Taylor
images, uniform current-history Hilbert--Samuel tracking and global
resolution remain separate results.
