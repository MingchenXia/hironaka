\section{The genuine original associated ring sheaf}

The actual ring presheaf $G$ retains its independently constructed section
products. Its sheafification is taken in commutative rings on the original
manifold, so the sheaf has genuine ring operations before any whole stalk
comparison.

\begin{definition}[The associated ring sheaf and original ambient morphism]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-ring-sheaf}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-section-ring-presheaf,hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-section-ambient-ring-map}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingSheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSheaf,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingSheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSheafUnit,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingSheaf.actualNormalAssociatedAmbientRingSheafMap,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingSheaf.actualNormalAssociatedAmbientRingSheafMap_app}
\leanok
There is a genuine associated ring sheaf $G^{\#}$, with its natural ring
presheaf unit $G\to G^{\#}$ and natural structure-ring sheaf morphism
$\mathcal O_M\to G^{\#}$. The latter sends every original holomorphic
section to the sheafification of its original degree-zero class.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The standard ring-valued sheafification functor on the open-set site
constructs $G^{\#}$ and its natural unit. Compose the already constructed
actual degree-zero structure-ring presheaf morphism with this unit.
Both its source and target are ring sheaves, so this composite is a
ring sheaf morphism. The raw degree-zero section formula and the
definition of the composite give the asserted formula on every open.
The ring operations are those of ring-valued sheafification; none are
transported through a whole stalk equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual ring sheaf stalk retains the whole original ring]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-ring-sheaf-whole-stalk-equivalence}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-ring-sheaf,hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-ring-whole-stalk-equivalence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingSheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingUnitStalkIso,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingSheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSheafStalkWholeEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingSheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSheafStalkWholeEquiv_unit}
\leanok
Original coherence of $A$ gives a ring isomorphism
$(G^{\#})_y\simeq\bigoplus_q L_q(R_y,I_y,J_y)$ for every original point
$y$. On the image of an arbitrary raw ring stalk element under the
sheafification unit it equals the original whole ring-stalk map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The ring sheafification unit induces an isomorphism on every stalk.
Invert this genuine ring stalk isomorphism, then compose with the
previously proved whole original ring-stalk isomorphism. This gives
the asserted isomorphism for the independently constructed sheaf
ring operations. Evaluating on the image of a raw stalk element
cancels the inverse unit isomorphism against the unit isomorphism,
leaving exactly the original whole ring-stalk map.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Every original whole section germ is retained by the ring sheaf]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-ring-sheaf-original-germs}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-ring-sheaf-whole-stalk-equivalence,hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-ring-stalk-whole-map}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingSheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSheafStalkWholeEquiv_germ,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingSheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSheafStalkWholeEquiv_germ_of}
\leanok
The germ in $G^{\#}$ of the unit image of any actual original section
maps to its original whole graded-ring germ class. In particular this
holds in every original homogeneous degree and for every actual power
representative, with the same representative and degree.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Naturality of the induced stalk morphism says that the stalk unit sends
a raw section germ to the sheaf germ of that section's unit image.
Apply the preceding cancellation identity. The existing raw ring-stalk
section formula identifies the result with the original whole ring
section germ. Its homogeneous inclusion formula then gives the same
whole layer class in the same degree. Substituting a genuine power
representative in that formula gives the stated original power-class
formula.
\end{proof}
