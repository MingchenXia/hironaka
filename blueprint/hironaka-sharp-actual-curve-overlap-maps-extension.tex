\section{Actual overlap maps for retained local sharp curve recipes}

Let $W\subset X$ be a closed smooth pure curve in positive ambient dimension,
let $E$ be its original ordered SNC family and assume density of the complement
of all original members in $W$. For actual opens $U,V\subset X$, let $O\subset U\cap V$ be ANY genuine common open.
View $O$ as the true nested open $A_U\subset U$ and $A_V\subset V$.
Their canonical maps to $O$ give a genuine biholomorphism
$\Phi_{UV}:A_U\longrightarrow A_V$, equal to the identity on underlying points.

\begin{theorem}[Genuine intersection base maps retain the original product and ordered family]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-overlap-original-data}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-original-curve-biholomorphic-base-inputs,hir:sharp-original-member-product-actual-open-pullback}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOverlapMaps.commonOpenBase,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOverlapMaps.common_open_original_product_dictionary}
\leanok
Construct $\Phi_{UV}$ from the two actual nested-open biholomorphisms.
The twice-restricted original carrier and ordered family on $A_U$ are exactly
the inverse images of those on $A_V$. For every mark, the genuine pullback of
the whole original product record on $A_V$ is the actual original product
record on $A_U$, including all four fields. Original empty or repeated member
positions are retained, and the initial marked exceptional list is empty.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each nested-open map sends a point to the same underlying point of $X$ and
has its actual nested inclusion as holomorphic inverse. Their composite is
therefore a true biholomorphism. Membership in the two twice-restricted
carriers is membership of that same underlying point in $W$, proving the
carrier identity in both directions. For each original position, membership
in the two restricted members is membership of that same point in that
original member. Applying the two literal ordered-list maps proves the whole
family identity, with no reindexing or deletion. Restriction of the original
SNC family to $V$ and then to $A_V$ supplies its genuine SNC geometry. The
whole original product pullback theorem for this actual biholomorphism and
that genuine family then gives all four fields of the original marked record.
Substitute the proved carrier and list identities. No ideal identity is
inferred solely from supports.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual local FIRST blowups have genuine intersection comparisons]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-overlap-first-blowups}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-overlap-original-data,hir:sharp-original-curve-first-defect-open-restriction,hir:sharp-original-curve-biholomorphic-base-stage-centres,hir:sharp-actual-stage-matched-blowup-unique-lift}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOverlapMaps.exists_actual_common_open_first_comparison}
\leanok
Take any actual blowup of the computed FIRST centre for the original data on
$U$, and any such actual blowup on $V$. Restrict these actual first histories
to $A_U$ and $A_V$. Construct a genuine stagewise comparison over $\Phi_{UV}$,
with its actual next biholomorphism. No matching-centre, original TEST or
geometric certificate on the intersection is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Open restriction of the original smooth carrier, SNC coordinates and regular
curve dimension gives the true data on $U,V$ and on the nested opens.
The open inclusion is an open continuous map, so the preimage of the original
closure is the closure of the preimage. Together with the literal ordered
union dictionary this constructs the whole-family density on each open.
FIRST-centre open naturality applied to each reflexive history identifies its
computed nested FIRST centre with the trace of its actual local FIRST centre.
The preceding carrier and ordered-family identities and the genuine base
biholomorphism give exact FIRST-centre naturality between the nested opens.
Thus the actual trace centres are genuine inverse images under $\Phi_{UV}$.
The restrictions of both given arrows are actual blowups of those trace
centres. Their actual same-centre universal property constructs both inverse
holomorphic maps and the real commuting square. The reflexive base comparison
extends by this actual step to the required restricted first-history
isomorphism, retaining even an empty trace centre as a real successor.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Terminated retained local recipes have actual intersection maps]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-overlap-terminal-recipes}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-overlap-first-blowups,hir:sharp-original-curve-terminal-difference-recipe-open-restriction,hir:sharp-actual-curve-biholo-terminal-padded-recipes}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOverlapMaps.exists_unique_actual_common_open_recipe_comparison}
\leanok
On each of the two actual local FIRST blowups, take a literal difference
recipe whose independently computed terminal whole difference is empty.
Restrict its tail to the actual inverse image of $A_U$ or $A_V$ under that
first blowup. At every common length at least the two original tail lengths,
append the genuine identity empty-centre blowups. Construct a unique actual
full stagewise comparison of the restricted padded histories over
$\Phi_{UV}$. The unpadded prefixes and tails are the actual open restrictions of the
retained recipes; the added padding consists of actual identity blowups of
the empty centre, with every original position and empty trace step present.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All original open data needed for the two local curve products are constructed
by the preceding argument. Actual original FIRST admissibility gives an
original local TEST on each given first blowup. The genuine terminating
recipe restriction theorem then constructs each actual restricted literal
tail, exact retained length, full original restricted TEST and actual terminal
difference emptiness. Construct the actual first-prefix comparison by the
preceding theorem. The target first-prefix TEST is supplied independently by
true original TEST restriction and the whole initial product pullback identity.
The proved carrier and ordered-family identities identify the actual source
restricted recipe with the source input of the genuine-base terminal recipe
comparison theorem. Apply that theorem to these exact histories, lengths and
computed empty differences. It constructs the actual empty padding and unique
full stagewise biholomorphism. No compactness of $O$, prescribed endpoint map,
comparison centre or source-history certificate is used. This is an actual
pairwise overlap map; compatibility on triple overlaps and geometric descent
remain further assertions.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Actual pairwise intersections supply the common open internally]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-literal-intersection-terminal-recipes}

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOverlapMaps.exists_unique_actual_overlap_recipe_comparison}
\leanok
For two retained actual local recipes on $U,V$ as above, construct the unique
actual padded full comparison on their literal intersection $U\cap V$,
without any supplied common open or inclusion witness. Every common cut of
the actual retained tail lengths is allowed.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Take the actual open $O=U\cap V$. Its two inclusions into $U$ and $V$ are the
true defining intersection inclusions. Apply the preceding common-open
theorem to these exact opens and the given actual first blowups and literal
terminating tails. All source geometry, restricted original TESTs, computed
centres, actual retained lengths and genuine padding are constructed by that
theorem; no new mathematical premise is introduced.
\end{proof}
