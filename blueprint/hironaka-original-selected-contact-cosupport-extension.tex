\section{The actual selected zero branches and the complete contact cosupport}

The nonzero lower contact model must not silently discard actual components
on which the coefficient ideal is identically zero. Retain their genuine
strict transforms and reconstruct the WHOLE cosupport from those zero
branches and the true nonzero model. This is the geometric zero/nonzero
component split in \cite[Step I, Case A]{bierstone-milman08-functoriality},
for actual ORIGINAL histories; no full higher-invariant comparison is inferred.

\begin{theorem}[True strict transforms of an actual initial cosupport subset stay in the actual cosupport]
\label{hir:actual-initial-cosupport-subset-strict-persistence}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0016}
\uses{hir:wellformed-tower,bcalc:cosupport-analytic,bcalc:transform-off-centre,bcalc:strict-submanifold}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalSelectedContactCosupport.strictAlong_subset_cosupp_of_subset}
\leanok
For ANY genuinely closed smooth marked carrier, coherent ideal and actual
initial subset $S$ of its cosupport, EVERY genuine test tower sends the
TRUE geometric strict transform of $S$ into the actual transformed cosupport.
No nonzero, principal, invariant-domain, selected-component or persistence
certificate is a premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct over the actual maps. Initially the assertion is the given subset
inclusion. At a genuine next blowup the actual previous and next carriers
are closed smooth and their true ideals coherent. The actual next
cosupport is therefore closed. Off the actual centre, the proved BOTH
cosupport membership dictionary carries every point lying over the previous
strict transform of $S$ into the new cosupport. The real new geometric
strict transform is the closure of precisely that off-centre preimage.
Closedness therefore contains its closure. This proves the induction step
for the actual maps and every position, including empty centres.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The WHOLE actual contact cosupport is its selected strict branch union the true nonzero model image]
\label{hir:actual-selected-nonzero-contact-whole-cosupport-decomposition}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0016}
\uses{hir:actual-initial-cosupport-subset-strict-persistence,hir:zero-contact-remainder-restricted-test-tower,hir:zero-contact-selected-zero-germ,hir:member-tail-actual-clopen-strict}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalSelectedContactCosupport.whole_cosupp_selected_remainder_decomposition}
\leanok
Let $L$ have a genuine closed smooth carrier and coherent ideal, and let
$S$ be its ACTUAL selected component union. Let $G=S^c$, and let $A$ be
the TRUE restriction to $G$ of its remaining contact record, with any
permitted number of empty historical boundary entries. For EVERY genuine
test tower $P$ of $L$, the WHOLE actual transformed cosupport equals
\[
 \operatorname{strict}_P(S)\ \cup\
 \iota\bigl(\operatorname{cosupp}(A_{P|_G})\bigr),
\]
where $\iota:P^{-1}G\hookrightarrow Y$ is the actual inclusion. Both
sets on the right are independently defined actual objects. No desired
cosupport decomposition, lower test or transformation identity is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each actual selected component lies in the original cosupport by its
literal definition, so the first theorem puts its WHOLE strict transform
in the new cosupport. Independently, the genuine selected-complement
restricted-test theorem internally constructs $P|_G$ as a test of $A$;
its carrier, ideal and mark are the true restrictions of the full
transformed $L$. Thus BOTH cosupport directions give exactly the trace
of the latter cosupport under $\iota$.

The selected and remaining unions are disjoint closed smooth sets and
partition $L.N$. True strict-transform geometry of a closed partition says
that the selected strict transform is precisely the strict transform of
$L.N$ intersected with the actual inverse image of $S$. A point of the
full cosupport lies in that strict carrier. If its actual projection lies
in $S$, it is therefore in the selected strict branch; otherwise it is a
real point of $P^{-1}G$, and the independently proved trace dictionary
places it in the image of the lower cosupport. Conversely selected strict
points belong by the first theorem and lower-image points by the true
trace dictionary. This proves equality of the entire sets, not merely
pointwise equality near an originally chosen point.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ONLY original noncanonical inputs construct a fixed complete future contact cosupport presentation]
\label{hir:original-noncanonical-complete-contact-cosupport-presentation}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0016}
\uses{hir:original-noncanonical-uniform-nonzero-contact-presentation,hir:actual-selected-nonzero-contact-whole-cosupport-decomposition,hir:original-noncanonical-uniform-contact-test-presentation}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalSelectedContactCosupport.exists_original_test_complete_contact_cosupport_presentation}
\leanok
ONLY the original maximal-order domain of dimension bound $k+2$, ANY
genuine ORIGINAL test prefix and actual current cosupport point internally
choose ONE actual $U,w,L,G,A$ BEFORE all genuine ORIGINAL future
extensions. The true nonzero model $A$ has lower domain $k+1$ and its
SAME actual nested restricted future is a genuine test with true future
domain. On the entire inverse image of $U$, the SAME ORIGINAL transformed
cosupport is EXACTLY the union of the actual selected strict branch of
$L$ and the inclusion image of this actual nonzero lower cosupport.
Canonicality and desired lower-domain/test/whole-cosupport certificates
are absent.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual original-input nonzero presentation producer, keeping all
choices BEFORE the future tower. For each genuine ORIGINAL extension,
actual coefficient equivalence and the contact-tail theorem internally
supply the full $L$ test and BOTH ORIGINAL/contact cosupport directions.
Genuine boundary recursion gives the old-prefix length inequality.
Apply the WHOLE decomposition theorem to these independently constructed
objects and substitute the already proved ORIGINAL/contact cosupport
identity. The original-input producer independently supplies the actual
lower test and its future domain. Thus both actual zero and nonzero
branches are covered without pretending the chosen point lies in the
nonzero open, and all original positions and actual maps are retained.

This is a full geometric coefficient-cosupport presentation over genuinely
extended ORIGINAL tests. It does not identify earlier frozen full lower
histories, infer original lower canonicality, cover unrestricted local
tests which fail to extend, or close exceptional/infinitesimal semicoherence,
higher invariant decrease or general termination.
\end{proof}
