\section{Actual locally ringed models of embedded analytic subspaces}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-analytic-subspace}

Let $M$ be a complex manifold, $C\subset M$, and $i:C\hookrightarrow M$
its actual subspace inclusion. Write $\mathcal O_M$ for the genuine
holomorphic structure sheaf and $\mathcal I_C$ for the whole vanishing
ideal sheaf. The construction below applies to every subset; for a closed
analytic $C$ it gives the locally ringed model needed for an analytic-space
atlas. It does not assume smoothness of $C$, resolution data, or compatibility
of local resolutions.

\begin{definition}[The actual subspace structure sheaf]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-structure-sheaf}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-whole-sheaf-stalk}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspace.actualSubspaceSheaf}
\leanok
Form the genuine ambient quotient ring sheaf
$\mathcal Q_C=(U\mapsto\mathcal O_M(U)/\mathcal I_C(U))^\#$.
Define $\mathcal O_C=i^{-1}\mathcal Q_C$, namely the sheafification of
the actual inverse-image presheaf on the subspace $C$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Whole original quotient stalks on the actual subspace]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-whole-stalk}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-structure-sheaf,hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-whole-sheaf-stalk}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspace.actualSubspaceStalkWholeEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspace.actualSubspaceStalkChartEquiv}
\leanok
For every $x\in C$, the actual stalk of $\mathcal O_C$ is naturally isomorphic
to $\mathcal O_{M,x}/\mathcal I_{C,x}$, and thence to the whole original
preferred-chart holomorphic germ quotient. The quotient numerator of every
original ambient germ is retained by these identifications.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The sheafification unit induces a stalk isomorphism. The inverse-image
presheaf stalk at $x$ is canonically isomorphic to the original ambient
quotient-sheaf stalk at $i(x)$, by the neighbourhood-colimit inverse-image
stalk isomorphism. The already constructed whole quotient-sheaf stalk
dictionary identifies this last ring with
$\mathcal O_{M,i(x)}/\mathcal I_{C,i(x)}$.
Compose these three actual ring isomorphisms in that order. The original
whole holomorphic stalk isomorphism maps the entire vanishing ideal onto
the entire preferred-chart vanishing ideal; it therefore induces the
claimed quotient ring isomorphism. On each original quotient numerator,
the old quotient-stalk formula, the inverse-image unit and the
sheafification unit give exactly its original ambient germ class.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine locally ringed subspace]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-locally-ringed}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-whole-stalk}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspace.actualSubspaceLocallyRingedSpace}
\leanok
The actual topological subspace $C$ with $\mathcal O_C$ is a locally ringed
space, without any smoothness assumption on $C$. Its quotient stalks are
nonzero at every actual point of $C$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original holomorphic stalk is a local ring. At an actual point $x\in C$,
every germ in the whole vanishing ideal evaluates to zero at $x$; hence the
ideal lies in the maximal ideal and is proper. Its quotient is consequently
nonzero and is a local ring, since the quotient homomorphism is surjective.
The preceding actual stalk isomorphism transports that local ring structure
to the literal subspace sheaf stalk. Use the actual subspace topology and
constructed sheaf, with these internally proved local-ring structures, to
form the locally ringed space.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine inclusion has the whole quotient stalk map]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-inclusion-stalk}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-locally-ringed,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-whole-stalk}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspace.actualSubspaceInclusion,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspace.actualSubspaceInclusion_stalk}
\leanok
The actual inclusion $i:C\hookrightarrow M$ underlies a morphism of locally
ringed spaces. Under the whole stalk dictionary its stalk map is exactly
$\mathcal O_{M,x}\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal O_{M,x}/\mathcal I_{C,x}$.
In particular every stalk map is surjective and its kernel is the whole
original vanishing ideal. If $C$ is closed, its underlying continuous map
is a closed topological embedding.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose the actual ambient structure-sheaf quotient morphism with the
unit of the inverse-image/direct-image adjunction and then with the
direct image of the actual sheafification unit. This gives the genuine
ringed-space morphism on the actual inclusion.
Represent any ambient stalk element by an original holomorphic section.
The stalk-map germ formula, the inverse-image unit formula and the
sheafification stalk formula identify its image with the old ambient
quotient germ. The preceding dictionary sends this to precisely the
quotient class of the same original germ. Since every ambient germ has
such a section representative, this proves the whole stalk formula.
The quotient map is surjective, so the actual stalk map is surjective.
A surjective homomorphism from a local ring onto a nonzero ring is local;
therefore the ringed-space morphism is a locally ringed-space morphism.
The displayed formula and injectivity of the dictionary show that its
kernel is exactly the whole original vanishing ideal. The underlying map
is the actual subspace inclusion, whose image is $C$; when $C$ is closed
it is a closed embedding by the subspace topology.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual subspace has reduced local rings]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-reduced-stalks}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-whole-stalk,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-locally-ringed}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspace.actualSectionIdeal_vanishing_iff,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspace.actualWholeVanishingStalkIdeal_isRadical,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspace.actualSubspaceStalk_isReduced}
\leanok
The actual section ideal on $U$ consists exactly of the sections vanishing on $C\cap U$. Every whole vanishing stalk ideal is radical. Consequently every actual
subspace stalk is a reduced local ring. Neither smoothness, coherence nor
a reducedness certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First, the actual section ideal on an original open $U$ consists precisely
of the holomorphic sections whose values vanish on $C\cap U$.
Indeed, membership in the whole stalk ideal at an actual point of $C$
implies zero value, because that ideal lies in the genuine maximal ideal.
Conversely, a section vanishing on $C\cap U$ represents at every point of
$U$ a genuine function germ vanishing on $C$ nearby; this germ is one of
the generators of the actual whole vanishing stalk ideal. This proves
both inclusions of the asserted section description. If a positive power
of a section belongs to this section ideal, its values on $C\cap U$ have
zero corresponding powers in $\mathbb C$, so they are zero. The section
ideal is therefore radical.

Now let an ambient germ $r$ have a power in the whole vanishing stalk
ideal at $x$. Represent $r$ by a true section $s$ on an original open
neighbourhood $U$. The proved whole ideal-section germ-image dictionary
represents the germ of the power $s^m$ by an actual vanishing-ideal section
on another neighbourhood. Equality of the two genuine stalk germs holds
as section equality after restriction to a common smaller open $V$
containing $x$. Hence $(s|_V)^m$ belongs to the actual section ideal on
$V$. Its radicality gives $s|_V$ in that same ideal. Taking its germ and
using restriction compatibility proves $r$ belongs to the whole original
vanishing stalk ideal. Thus the whole ideal is radical.

A nilpotent element of its quotient has an original ambient lift whose
power belongs to the ideal; radicality puts that lift in the ideal, so
the quotient element is zero. The whole actual subspace stalk dictionary
transfers this reducedness to the literal subspace sheaf stalk. Its
locality was proved above, giving the asserted reduced local rings.
\end{proof}

This constructs actual singular locally ringed models and their full
ambient quotient maps. Presentation-independent comparisons on overlaps,
effective analytic-space atlas gluing, and resolution descent remain
separate obligations.
