\begin{theorem}[Synchronized terminal native phases]
\label{hir:g8-native-simultaneous-terminal-comparison}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaG8NativeFilteredSimultaneousTerminal.native_simultaneous_terminal_emptyAware_of_filtered_boundary}
\uses{hir:g8-simultaneous-native-centre-comparison,hir:g8-native-common-history-clock}
Two complete native phases with the same Hilbert--Samuel label and
carrier compare on their common actual base. The ordered boundary
lists may differ by globally empty slots. The isomorphism matches the
actual stages up to empty steps. The native windows and their common
positive marks may be chosen independently.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Filter each ordered boundary by the mask of ambient empty slots. The
same mask on every native window retains canonicality, active cosupport
and simultaneous maxima. To erase one globally empty blowup, normalize
its map to an identity step. Restriction retains the identity step and
literal suffix on each window, so every active rank changes by the
same strictly monotone recursive clock shift. The surviving actual
centres therefore retain their simultaneous maximum certificates.
Repeat until every centre is nonempty. At each pair of matched
prefixes the simultaneous-centre comparison identifies the next centre,
and uniqueness of blowups gives the physical comparison on that step.
The shorter history cannot finish earlier than the longer one because
both window systems detect the same remaining active points.
\end{proof}
