\section{The same canonical coefficient functions have genuine finite support domains}

\begin{theorem}[Finite whole-diagram coefficient identities hold on one true common open]
\label{hir:actual-canonical-finite-diagram-support-domain}
\uses{hir:actual-canonical-analytic-normal-diagram-support,hir:actual-local-principal-coefficient-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCanonicalCoefficientDomains.exists_finite_diagram_support_shrink}
\leanok
Suppose an actual canonical coefficient datum $P$ has the proved original
coefficient-germ constants on the whole pure normal diagram. For every finite
normal-degree cutoff $q$, there is an actual datum $Q$ obtained by shrinking
only its genuine domain. It retains the literal generator functions and all
literal coefficient functions. On this ONE actual open neighbourhood of zero,
every coefficient of every unchanged generator at a pure diagram label of
degree less than $q$ is actually the prescribed constant zero or one at EVERY
basepoint. The common neighbourhood is selected before all these finite labels.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Index the required functions by the product of the actual finite vertex set
and the genuine finite set of normal exponents of degree less than $q$.
For a label in the whole pure normal diagram, the proved original germ
identity is precisely eventual equality of the actual coefficient function
with its prescribed constant near zero. For a label outside the diagram,
the desired implication is vacuous. Finiteness intersects these genuine
neighbourhood conditions into one neighbourhood of zero. Select a true open
neighbourhood contained in it and intersect it with the actual original
datum domain. Restrict the datum's regularity and coherence to this true
open while retaining EVERY original generator and coefficient function.
The actual common-neighbourhood condition gives every asserted equality at
every point of this same domain. The empty index set and $q=0$ are included.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original centre geometry fixes the same exact family before every finite support domain]
\label{hir:actual-original-hs-centre-finite-canonical-support-domains}
\uses{hir:actual-original-hs-centre-canonical-coefficient-data,hir:actual-canonical-finite-diagram-support-domain}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCanonicalCoefficientDomains.exists_original_hs_constant_centre_finite_support_domains}
\leanok
Original regular-centre geometry, coherence and all-order Hilbert--Samuel
constancy internally produce ONE genuine flattening and ONE actual exact
canonical coefficient datum $P$, retaining its whole generator normal powers.
For EVERY finite cutoff $q$, there is a true common open shrink as in the
preceding theorem, with the SAME literal generators and coefficient functions
of that one fixed $P$. There is no independently selected chart or family
when the cutoff changes.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Call the genuine original exact canonical coefficient producer once. It fixes
the actual chart and original ideal, and supplies one datum with exact whole
canonical Taylor images, its entire original diagram coefficient-germ support,
and actual whole generator normal powers. Retain that chart and datum before
quantifying over cutoffs. For an arbitrary $q$, apply the preceding theorem
to this SAME datum and its already produced coefficient-germ identities.
This gives the actual common open shrink without altering any original
function, and retains the original generator normal powers and exact Taylor
identities from the one original choice.
\end{proof}

The domain may depend on the finite cutoff. A single domain for all infinitely
many cutoffs, whole transformed Hilbert--Samuel persistence equivalence and
current-history termination are not asserted.
