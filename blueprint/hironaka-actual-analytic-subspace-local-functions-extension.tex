\section{Local ambient representatives and intrinsic morphism faithfulness}

Let $C\subset M$ be an actual subset of a complex manifold, with the genuine
reduced quotient sheaf $\mathcal O_C$ constructed from the entire original
vanishing ideal. No smoothness or coherence of $C$ is required below.

\begin{theorem}[Whole local ambient representatives of actual quotient sections]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-local-ambient-section}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-inclusion-stalk}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceLocalFunctions.actualSubspaceSection_local_ambient}
\leanok
For an actual open $W\subset C$, a section $s\in\mathcal O_C(W)$ and $x\in W$,
there is an actual ambient open neighbourhood $O\subset M$ of $x$ and an
original holomorphic section $t$ on $O$, such that $C\cap O\subset W$ and
at every $y\in C\cap O$ the entire germ $s_y$ equals the image of the
original germ $t_y$ under the genuine quotient inclusion stalk map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Surjectivity of the genuine inclusion stalk map lifts $s_x$ to an original
ambient stalk element. Represent that element by an original holomorphic
section $t_0$ on an ambient neighbourhood $O_0$. The actual stalk-map germ
formula says that the pullback section of $t_0$ and $s$ have equal germs at
$x$. The genuine colimit equality criterion therefore gives an actual open
neighbourhood $Q\subset C$ on which their restrictions agree. By the
subspace topology write $Q=C\cap T$ for an ambient open $T$. Take
$O=T\cap O_0$ and restrict $t_0$ to $O$. For each $y\in C\cap O$, equality
of these actual restricted sections, the actual restriction-germ formulas
and the inclusion stalk-map germ formula give the asserted equality of
entire germs. The same restriction inclusion gives $y\in W$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Point values determine entire actual quotient sections]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-section-value-ext}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-local-ambient-section,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-stalk-residue-evaluation,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-reduced-stalks}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceLocalFunctions.actualSubspaceSection_eq_zero_of_eval,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceLocalFunctions.actualSubspaceSection_ext_eval}
\leanok
An actual section whose genuine residue evaluation is zero at every point
of its domain is zero. Consequently two actual sections with identical
actual point values on their whole domain are equal as sections.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a point and take the whole local ambient representative just constructed.
At every point of the actual intersection with $C$, its original value
is the residue evaluation of the given section, by the whole original
inclusion numerator formula and original chart-germ evaluation. Thus the
ambient section vanishes pointwise on that whole intersection. The proved
actual section-ideal criterion puts it in the entire original vanishing
ideal on that ambient open. Its germ at the chosen point therefore lies
in the entire kernel of the actual inclusion stalk map, so the original
section germ is zero. Actual sheaf stalk extensionality now proves the
whole section zero. Apply this result to the difference of two sections
to obtain equality from equal point values. In particular this argument
uses the genuine local vanishing ideal, rather than an inference from
reducedness of an individual local ring.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual intrinsic complex morphisms are determined by their base map]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-intrinsic-map-faithful}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-section-value-ext,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-scalar-map-point-values}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceLocalFunctions.actualSubspaceMap_eq_of_base_eq}
\leanok
Two genuine locally ringed morphisms between actual quotient-subspace
models, each commuting with the original structural map to the complex
point, are equal if their continuous base maps are equal. No ambient
extension or supplied equality of sheaf maps is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a target open and an actual section. Equality of base maps identifies
the two actual source preimage opens. At every point of that whole open,
the stalk-map germ formula identifies the germ of each pullback section
with the pullback of the original target germ. Compatibility with the
actual complex point gives residue-evaluation naturality for each genuine
morphism. Hence these two pullback sections have identical point values
at every point, because the target point and target germ are the same.
The preceding whole-section extensionality proves that the two pullback
sections are equal. Extensionality of ring homomorphisms and natural
transformations proves equality of the entire sheaf maps; together with
the given base equality this proves equality of the actual locally ringed
morphisms. The argument compares values throughout the genuine open,
not just the residue value of one stalk element.
\end{proof}

These local-function facts provide genuine input for lifting an arbitrary
intrinsic complex morphism to an ambient holomorphic map by finitely many
coordinate sections. They do not assert analytic atlas resolution descent
or a terminal resolution driver.
