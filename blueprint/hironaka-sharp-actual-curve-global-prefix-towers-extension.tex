\section{Genuine global finite blowup prefixes over the original space}

\begin{theorem}[Actual global successor blowups construct every literal finite prefix]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-genuine-global-prefix-tower}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-global-successor-blowup,hir:sharp-actual-curve-global-successor-quotient-map}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.uniformGlobalTower,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.uniformGlobalTower_length,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.uniformGlobalTower_projection}
\leanok
For actual retained histories covering the original complex manifold,
every natural $k$ determines a genuine global blowup tower from the actual
zero stage to the actual $k$th stage, with literal length $k$. Its steps
are precisely the already constructed actual global centres and successors,
including every genuine empty step. Composing its projection with the
actual zero-stage projection is exactly the original $k$th projection.
No finite-prefix tower or blowup certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At zero take the actual reflexive history of the actual zero-stage
manifold. Once the first $k$ actual steps have been constructed, append
the actual global centre and actual successor at $k$. Its complete original
blowup predicate has already been proved, so this is a genuine additional
constructor of the original blowup-tower type. Its endpoint is precisely
the actual stage $k+1$, and its length is the previous length plus one.
Induction therefore gives literal length $k$, without deleting any step.
For the projection identity, the zero case is the identity. At a
successor the tower projection is the previous projection composed with
the SAME actual successor. The inductive identity followed by the exact
global successor projection square gives the original stage-$k+1$
projection at every point. Thus the equality holds on the entire source.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual prefixes are genuine blowup histories over the original base]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-global-prefix-tower}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-genuine-global-prefix-tower,hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-initial-stage,hir:tower-transport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.uniformOriginalTransport,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.uniformOriginalTransport_zero,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.uniformOriginalPositiveTower,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.uniformOriginalPositiveTower_length,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.uniformOriginalPositiveTower_projection,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.uniformOriginalPositiveTower_successor,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.originalUniformPositiveTower,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.originalUniformPositiveTower_length,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalPrefixTowers.originalUniformPositiveTower_projection}
\leanok
For the same actual histories and cover, transport the genuine prefixes
along the internally proved inverse of the actual zero-stage projection.
The zero history is reflexive over the original space. For every positive
length $k+1$ the endpoint is precisely the actual stage $k+1$, its entire
projection is precisely the original stage projection, and its literal
length is $k+1$. The positive prefixes extend each other by the SAME
actual successor maps and centres at all subsequent positive stages.
Only original positive-dimensional smooth pure curve, ordered SNC and
density data internally supply these genuine original-base prefixes.
Neither an initial isomorphism nor a desired finite history is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the original tower-transport construction to the actual finite
prefix and the inverse of the actual initial projection biholomorphism.
At the reflexive history this produces the reflexive history on the
original base. At a genuine first blowup, the original target transport
moves its centre by the actual inverse initial biholomorphism and composes
its projection with the actual initial projection. The genuine blowup
universal property is preserved by this actual biholomorphic base change.
Its endpoint biholomorphism is the identity of the actual first stage.
Every further step therefore has exactly its original endpoint, centre
and successor map. The construction does not change any literal length.
This also proves the exact positive-prefix extension identity.

The original transport projection square states that the inverse initial
biholomorphism composed with the transported projection equals the actual
zero-base tower projection composed with its endpoint identification.
At positive length that endpoint identification is the identity. Apply
the actual initial biholomorphism and use both inverse laws. The preceding
whole projection identity then identifies the resulting map with the
original actual stage projection. Finally instantiate the internally
constructed original histories and cover. All histories, centres and
maps are genuine original objects. No global finite upper bound on the
local stopping lengths, canonical scheduler, or terminal identification
is inferred from these finite-prefix statements.
\end{proof}
