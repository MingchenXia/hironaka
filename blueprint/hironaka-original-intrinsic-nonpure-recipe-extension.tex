\section{Intrinsic canonical elementary arrows for compact nonpure input}

\begin{definition}[Intrinsic ordered elementary recipe]
\label{hir:original-intrinsic-ordered-recipe}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-ordered-filtered-choice}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalIntrinsicNonpureRecipe.IntrinsicOrderedRecipe}
\leanok
For original $W\subset X^n$ and ordered boundary $E$, an intrinsic
ordered recipe is a genuine actual separator/head/tail tree on some
finite raw labelled list whose nonempty filter is exactly the
increasing intrinsic dimension-part list of $W$. Its bad-set index is
the intrinsic singular set together with $W$ on the original boundary.
Empty raw padding is allowed. This condition contains no desired
geometry, target map, smooth square or comparison certificate.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Whole elementary geometric output]
\label{hir:original-intrinsic-elementary-geometry-output}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalIntrinsicNonpureRecipe.HasElementaryGeometry}
\leanok
The elementary output comprises actual admissibility over the original
intrinsic bad set, the whole final SNC boundary, actual exceptional and
new-boundary inclusions, the singular inverse-image inclusion and
isomorphism off the bad set, the whole closed regular strict carrier,
its intrinsic dimension-part union and regularity, pairwise disjoint
strict dimension parts, normal crossings, holomorphy, properness and
surjectivity. This transparent predicate is used as a conclusion of the
original-only existence theorem.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Every actual ordered tree retains its own whole SNC boundary]
\label{hir:original-ordered-tree-own-snc}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-ordered-filtered-choice}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalIntrinsicNonpureRecipe.ordered_tree_final_snc}
\leanok
A genuine ordered tree starting at an actual SNC boundary ends with
its own entire ordered SNC boundary, retaining empty positions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on its actual provenance. The nil tree retains the initial
boundary. For a cons tree the actual pure-head stage has a genuine
invariant domain and actual canonicality. Canonicality gives genuine
geometric testness; the invariant-domain transform theorem therefore
supplies the entire actual head-output SNC boundary. Apply induction
to the real tail starting at precisely this boundary.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Any two independent intrinsic recipes define one elementary arrow]
\label{hir:original-intrinsic-ordered-recipe-choice}
\uses{hir:original-intrinsic-ordered-recipe,hir:original-canonical-ordered-filtered-choice}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalIntrinsicNonpureRecipe.intrinsic_recipe_choice}
\leanok
Any two actual intrinsic ordered recipes for the same original $W,E$
have a unique actual final biholomorphism over the original base.
It carries their whole ordered filtered final boundaries and gives the
whole strict-transform square for every original subset, including
nonclosed subsets. No common history, index or empty-padding pattern
is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Unpack only the genuine raw-tree provenance and the intrinsic
nonempty-list equality from the two recipe records. Their input
filters are therefore equal. Apply the proved entire ordered-tree
choice theorem over the actual identity base. Its genuine constructed
map and filtered whole-boundary identity give the assertions, and
its every-subset strict square reduces to the stated identity square.
Its actual projection uniqueness gives uniqueness with these properties.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original compact nonpure data give an intrinsic canonical elementary arrow with independent smooth sources]
\label{hir:original-intrinsic-nonpure-elementary-independent-smooth}
\uses{hir:original-intrinsic-ordered-recipe,hir:original-intrinsic-elementary-geometry-output,hir:original-intrinsic-ordered-recipe-choice,hir:original-ordered-tree-own-snc,hir:original-nonpure-canonical-elementary-smooth-projection}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalIntrinsicNonpureRecipe.exists_original_intrinsic_nonpure_resolution}
\leanok
Let $W$ be an original compact analytic subset of empty interior and
let $E$ be an original ordered SNC boundary with $W$ dense off it.
These original data internally construct an intrinsic ordered recipe
$T$ with the whole elementary geometry. Fix this SAME target before
any holomorphic submersion $b:Y^m\to X^n$. Internally construct an
intrinsic source recipe $Q$ with its own whole elementary geometry,
actual Cartesian blowup square, full strict-carrier and shifted
intrinsic-dimension-part squares and actual upper submersion.

Every independently chosen intrinsic source recipe $S$ has its own
whole SNC boundary and is uniquely isomorphic to this SAME $Q$ over
$Y$, with exact filtered whole-boundary and every-subset strict
squares. The induced upper map to $T$ is holomorphic submersive and
has the true entire holomorphic Cartesian universal property. Its
own strict carrier is a closed submanifold with normal crossings
against its own entire final boundary. Thus the elementary arrow is
independent of raw empty padding and all auxiliary canonical choices.
No source compactness, source history or comparison certificate is
an original input. No particular normalized finite history is claimed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the genuine original compact nonpure producer to the original
analytic carrier and boundary. Its actual raw tree uses the whole
intrinsic dimension list, so it is an intrinsic recipe. All its whole
elementary geometry is exactly the geometric output recorded above.
For any given smooth $b$, take the actual source supplied by that
same producer. Its actual raw shifted dimension list has exactly
the intrinsic source nonempty filter, by the proved whole intrinsic
dimension-list pullback theorem. Thus it is a genuine intrinsic
source recipe even when $Y$ is noncompact or empty. Its own whole
elementary geometry, actual upper submersion and Cartesian strict
squares are those of the same actual source.

For any independent source recipe $S$, apply the preceding choice
theorem to it and $Q$. The resulting actual biholomorphism transports
the whole strict carrier, hence its closed-submanifold property.
The preceding SNC lemma gives its own whole final SNC list.
Filter only empty entries in the actual boundary identity; normal
crossings ignores precisely those entries. Genuine smooth inverse-image
normal crossings therefore gives normal crossings with $S$'s OWN
whole final boundary. Compose the actual upper map of $Q$ with this
actual biholomorphism to obtain a genuine upper submersion for $S$.
For every holomorphic cone, the genuine universal lift to $Q$ followed
by the inverse biholomorphism is the unique lift to $S$. This proves
the entire Cartesian universal property, not merely a commuting
point square. Projection uniqueness gives the unique comparison.
This is final-arrow canonicity for the actual intrinsic algorithm;
it uses no fictitious source compactness or assumed filtered history.
\end{proof}
