\section{Actual analytic tangent coefficients under the normal-unit coordinates}

\begin{theorem}[Every original tangent germ is fixed by the actual normal-unit pullback]
\label{hir:actual-normal-unit-entire-tangent-coefficients}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-unit-local-biholomorphism,hir:actual-normal-unit-coordinate-germ,hir:relative-normal-entire-germ-retraction}
Use the original splitting into $d$ tangent and $t$ normal coordinates,
the actual normal block $A$, an actual $j\in\{0,\ldots,t-1\}$ and
$e=d+j$. Let $F$ and $E$ be the proved actual normal-unit coordinate
map and its original whole germ pullback. Then the genuine tangent
projection satisfies $\mathrm{pr}_{\mathrm{tan}}\circ F=
\mathrm{pr}_{\mathrm{tan}}$. For every entire original tangent germ
$a\in\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$,
$E(\mathrm{tangentPullback}(a))=\mathrm{tangentPullback}(a)$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalUnitAnalyticCoefficients.tangentProjection_actualNormalUnitForward,Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalUnitAnalyticCoefficients.actualNormalUnitGermEquiv_tangentPullback}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every tangent index is outside the actual last normal block and differs
from $e$. The literal formula for $F$ therefore fixes it at every
actual point. Equality of every projected coordinate proves the actual
projection identity. Represent an arbitrary original tangent germ by
its genuine holomorphic representative $f$. The original
$\mathrm{tangentPullback}$ is composition by that genuine projection;
the original $E$ is composition by the proved actual $F$. Their
representatives are $f\circ\mathrm{pr}_{\mathrm{tan}}\circ F$
and $f\circ\mathrm{pr}_{\mathrm{tan}}$, respectively. The actual
projection identity makes them equal, hence equal as whole original
germs. No polynomial approximation of $f$ or formal evaluation at a
nonzero point is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual normal-unit pullback commutes with every finite tangent-coefficient evaluation]
\label{hir:actual-normal-unit-tangent-coefficient-evaluation}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-unit-entire-tangent-coefficients}
Let $P$ be any actual finite normal polynomial with coefficients in the
entire original tangent holomorphic germ ring. For every assignment
$q_i$ of its variables to original ambient germs,
\[
 E\bigl(\operatorname{eval}_2(\mathrm{tangentPullback},q)(P)\bigr)
 =\operatorname{eval}_2(\mathrm{tangentPullback},E(q))(P).
\]
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalUnitAnalyticCoefficients.actualNormalUnitGermEquiv_eval₂}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An actual ring homomorphism composed with finite polynomial evaluation
is evaluation with both its coefficient map and variable assignment
composed with that homomorphism. Apply this original polynomial
universal-property identity to $E$. The preceding entire tangent-germ
identity identifies the composite coefficient map with the original
$\mathrm{tangentPullback}$. All coefficients are genuine tangent
holomorphic germs, so the equality concerns the entire original
ambient germ ring.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact normal-unit dehomogenization with arbitrary actual tangent germ coefficients]
\label{hir:actual-normal-unit-analytic-coefficient-dictionary}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-unit-tangent-coefficient-evaluation,hir:actual-normal-unit-coordinate-germ,hir:relative-normal-basis-actual-derivation-evaluation}
Let $P$ be an actual finite homogeneous normal polynomial of degree
$q$ with coefficients in $\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$. Its coefficients
are evaluated by the original $\mathrm{tangentPullback}$. Let $x_i$
denote the original normal coordinate germs, $p_j=1$ and $p_i=b_i$
for $i\ne j$, and let $v$ be the genuine reciprocal germ of
$1+x_j$. With the actual dehomogenizing normal entries $r_j=1$,
$r_i=b_i+x_i$ for $i\ne j$, the exact whole germ equality is
\[
 E\bigl(\operatorname{eval}_2(\mathrm{tangentPullback},r)(P)\bigr)
 =v^q\operatorname{eval}_2(\mathrm{tangentPullback},x+p)(P).
\]
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalUnitAnalyticCoefficients.actualNormalUnitGermEquiv_analytic_dehomogenization}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding finite-evaluation identity moves the actual pullback
through evaluation while preserving every whole tangent coefficient.
In coordinate $j$, the evaluated left entry is $1=v(1+x_j)$ by
the genuine reciprocal identity. In every other normal coordinate,
the literal actual coordinate germ formula gives
$b_i+E(x_i)=v(b_i+x_i)$. Thus all normal polynomial variables are
scaled by the same actual unit $v$. Expand $P$ in its finite supported
coefficient monomials. Every such exponent has total normal degree
$q$, so its evaluated product acquires precisely the factor $v^q$.
Factor that common power from the finite sum. This proves the exact
equality inside the original entire germ ring for arbitrary original
tangent coefficients, including the zero polynomial and degree zero.
\end{proof}

These theorems transport actual analytic tangent coefficients already
present as whole original germs. They do not assert that an arbitrary
formal coefficient series has an analytic representative, or supply a
coefficient-cosupport, normal-flatness or Bennett certificate. General
analytic coefficient realization and positive-dimensional permissible
centre Bennett remain open.
