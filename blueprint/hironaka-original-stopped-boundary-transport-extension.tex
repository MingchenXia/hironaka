\section{The actual boundary of an independently stopped smooth source}

For an ordered boundary $E$, write $E^{\ne\varnothing}$ for the list obtained
by deleting its empty members, without changing the order of any surviving
member.  Empty steps and different stopping indices are permitted throughout.
The original source marked record remains literally $b^*M$.

\begin{lemma}[Empty boundary members do not affect normal crossings]
\label{hir:normal-crossings-delete-empty-boundary}
\uses{hir:snc}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStoppedBoundaryTransport.hasNormalCrossings_filter_nonempty_iff}
\leanok
For every carrier $C$ and finite ordered boundary $E$, $C$ has normal
crossings with $E$ if and only if it has normal crossings with
$E^{\ne\varnothing}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At a point of $C$, adapted coordinates are required only for boundary
members containing that point.  Restrict a system for $E$ to the
surviving indices to obtain a system for the filtered list.  Conversely,
for each member of $E$ containing the point, the member is nonempty and
has an index in the filtered list.  Assign to it the coordinate set at
such an index.  Assign an arbitrary coordinate set to every member not
containing the point.  The same coordinate chart therefore describes
$C$ and every required boundary member; the coordinate sets of the
members through the point still have cardinality one.  This proves both
directions, including the empty filtered list.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual empty compression retains every nonempty boundary member]
\label{hir:original-empty-compression-filtered-boundary}
\uses{hir:original-empty-compression-preserves-first-open,hir:original-actual-empty-family-canonical-deletion,hir:original-interior-empty-geometric-dictionary,hir:tower-isomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStoppedBoundaryTransport.empty_free_compression_preserves_boundary}
\leanok
Let $n>0$, let $M$ belong to the original invariant domain of bound $k$,
and let $T$ be an actual canonical history whose centres avoid an
original open $O$.  One can construct a canonical history $Q$ with
nonempty actual centres and a biholomorphism $\chi$ from its endpoint to
that of $T$ over the original base.  They retain the whole cosupport and
invariant-value dictionary, avoidance and the next-maximum encounter,
and also satisfy the ordered equality
\[
 ((\chi^{-1}E_T))^{\ne\varnothing}=E_Q^{\ne\varnothing}.
\]
Here $\chi^{-1}E_T$ means taking inverse images member by member.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual finite length.  If every centre is nonempty, use
the same history and the identity map.  Otherwise split off an actual
empty step with its whole suffix.  The genuine empty blowup is
biholomorphic to its base, and transporting the suffix identifies the
whole original history with a history containing an explicit empty
identity step.  The tower isomorphism transports the entire ordered
boundary by inverse image.  The explicit identity step inserts exactly
one empty entry in the ordered boundary of the deleted history, at the
actual chronological position.  Filtering deletes this entry and no
other entry.  Apply induction to the strictly shorter deleted history.
Inverse image under a bijection preserves and reflects nonemptiness,
so filtering commutes with this inverse image.  Composition gives the
asserted ordered equality for the same endpoint map as the cosupport,
value and avoidance dictionaries.  Those dictionaries transport the
whole maximal locus by an order embedding and the projection identity,
and hence retain its encounter with $O$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The filtered boundary is intrinsic to the first-meeting map]
\label{hir:original-first-meeting-filtered-boundary}
\uses{hir:original-empty-compression-filtered-boundary,hir:original-first-meeting-nonempty-length,hir:original-actual-whole-projection-biholomorphic-map-uniqueness,hir:tower-isomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStoppedBoundaryTransport.first_meeting_boundary_transport}
\leanok
Let $T_1,T_2$ be actual original canonical prefixes of the same marked
record in its ambient invariant domain.  Suppose both avoid the same
original open, and their next whole maximum loci meet its inverse
images.  For every biholomorphism $\eta$ between their endpoints over the
original base,
\[
 E_{T_1}^{\ne\varnothing}
   =((\eta^{-1}E_{T_2}))^{\ne\varnothing}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compress both histories with the preceding theorem.  Their nonempty
lengths agree by avoidance and the next-maximum encounter.  Their actual
equal-length canonical tower isomorphism transports the entire ordered
compressed boundaries.  Combining this equality with the two filtered
compression equalities gives the assertion for the composite endpoint
biholomorphism.  Actual finite-tower projection uniqueness identifies
that composite with the supplied over-base $\eta$.  In dimension zero
the original domain forces the transformed cosupport to be empty, so a
next maximal point cannot exist.  
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The independent Cartesian source has its own transported boundary]
\label{hir:original-independent-stopped-own-boundary}
\uses{hir:original-first-meeting-filtered-boundary,hir:original-pulled-stopped-source-next-encounter,hir:original-independent-source-stopped-cartesian-comparison,hir:submersion-relative-control-joint-tower,hir:normal-crossings-delete-empty-boundary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStoppedBoundaryTransport.independent_stopped_square_boundary}
\leanok
Let $V$ be a closed irreducible analytic subvariety with $\dim V<n$
and coherent vanishing ideal, let $M=(X,X,\varnothing,I_V,1)$, and let
$N=b^*M$ for a holomorphic submersion $b$.  Let $T$ be an actual
canonical target history avoiding the target good open and whose next
whole maximum encounters that open.  Let $S$ be
any independent canonical source history avoiding its inverse image;
when $b^{-1}V$ is nonempty, require its next whole maximum encounter.
For any actual holomorphic Cartesian square with upper submersion $G$
and vertical maps $p_S,p_T$, the actual boundaries satisfy
\[
 E_S^{\ne\varnothing}
  =((G^{-1}E_T))^{\ne\varnothing}.
\]
Thus, whenever the conventional source strict carrier is the inverse
image of the target strict carrier, target normal crossings implies
normal crossings of the source strict carrier with its own $E_S$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct an actual smooth pullback tower $Q$ of this same $T$.  Its
whole transformed record gives $E_Q=g^{-1}E_T$.  The two genuine
Cartesian universal properties construct inverse holomorphic maps
between the endpoints of $S$ and $Q$, hence a biholomorphism $\eta$
over the source base with $g\eta=G$.  If $b^{-1}V$ is nonempty, both
$S$ and $Q$ have the required whole maximal encounter; apply the
preceding theorem.  If it is empty, both histories have only empty
actual centres.  Their initial boundary is empty, and induction on the
actual steps shows that their transformed boundary lists have no
nonempty members.  The equality for $Q$ and $g\eta=G$ then give the
same conclusion.  Pull back the target normal-crossings coordinates
under the submersion $G$, use the literal strict-carrier equality and
replace its pulled boundary by $E_S$ using the filtered equality and
the empty-member lemma.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original embedded smooth comparison with the actual source boundary]
\label{hir:original-independent-embedded-smooth-own-boundary}
\uses{hir:original-independent-stopped-smooth-source-producer,hir:original-independent-stopped-own-boundary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStoppedBoundaryTransport.exists_original_independently_stopped_smooth_source_own_boundary}
\leanok
Under the original compact-target, coherent vanishing-ideal and
positive-codimension hypotheses, every holomorphic submersion constructs
the same full elementary stopped-source comparison as the independently
stopped source theorem, with the additional conclusion that its
conventional strict carrier has normal crossings with its own actual
transformed boundary $E_S$.  This retains the internally selected
target prefix, independent source prefix, both tails, actual canonical
marked histories, Cartesian upper submersion, strict carriers, regular
dimensions, admissibility, SNC boundaries, proper surjective maps and
good-open isomorphisms.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Invoke the original independently stopped source theorem once.  Retain
all its witnesses and conclusions.  Its target and source canonical
prefixes have exactly the avoidance and encounter properties required
above, including the conditional encounter and genuine empty branch.
Its upper map is a true Cartesian holomorphic submersion and its whole
conventional strict carriers satisfy the required literal equality.
The preceding theorem therefore supplies normal crossings with this
same source's actual boundary.  Adjoin that conclusion to the unchanged
elementary geometric dictionary.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Only empty boundary entries are deleted in the comparison.  No equality
of the unfiltered ordered lists or actual stopping indices is asserted.
The canonical source record is $b^*M$; identifying its whole ideal with
the reduced vanishing ideal of $b^{-1}V$ requires the separate singular
vanishing-ideal smooth-pullback theorem.
\end{remark}
