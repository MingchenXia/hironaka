\section{Actual coherent weighted coefficient marked ideals on their real domain}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-coherent-weighted-coefficient-marked}

\begin{theorem}[Literal finite holomorphic functions give a genuine coherent germ ideal]
\label{hir:actual-finite-function-coherent-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoherentWeightedCoefficientMarked.finiteFunctionIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoherentWeightedCoefficientMarked.isCoherent_finiteFunctionIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoherentWeightedCoefficientMarked.mem_cosupport_finiteFunctionIdeal_iff}
\leanok
Let $c_i$ be finitely many genuine holomorphic functions on a complex manifold.
Define the actual germ ideal by all local holomorphic combinations of those
literal functions. It is coherent. At every point $x$, its cosupport at mark
$q$ consists exactly of the points where every original $c_i$ has order at
least $q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Enumerate the finite index set by a finite ordinal. On the whole manifold
these very functions are finite holomorphic generators, proving coherence
without extending any function. Each original generator belongs to the
ideal at every point. Thus the cosupport condition implies the indicated
order for each generator. Conversely all germs in the ideal are genuine
finite holomorphic combinations of the generators. Functions of order at
least $q$ are closed under such combinations, proving the reverse implication.
The same proof includes an empty generating family.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole common-mark cosupport is exactly the original weighted cosupport]
\label{hir:actual-commonmark-function-cosupport}
\uses{hir:actual-finite-function-coherent-ideal,hir:actual-weighted-coefficient-common-mark,hir:order-power}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoherentWeightedCoefficientMarked.weightedFunctionIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoherentWeightedCoefficientMarked.cosupport_weightedFunctionIdeal}
\leanok
Let $c_i$ be finitely many genuine holomorphic functions with positive weights
$w_i$ and $D=\prod_iw_i$. Form the actual germ ideal generated by the literal
functions $c_i^{D/w_i}$. Its entire cosupport at mark $D$ is
\[
 \bigcap_i\operatorname{cosupp}((c_i),w_i),
\]
where each principal ideal is formed from the same original function.
This is an equality at every point of the actual manifold, not merely at a
selected germ or a finite jet.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At any point, the preceding result characterizes the compressed cosupport by
the individual orders of $c_i^{D/w_i}$. Each quotient $r_i=D/w_i$ is positive,
with $w_ir_i=D$. Positive-power order reflection therefore says that this
condition is exactly that $c_i$ has order at least $w_i$. For completeness,
one implication follows by identifying vanishing order with membership in
the actual maximal-ideal power and applying the proved positive-power
reflection. The other follows by multiplying genuine order conditions
$r_i$ times; at exponent zero the unit has order zero. Applying the preceding
principal-generator characterization gives the displayed whole intersection.
For an empty family both sides are the whole manifold.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original analytic ideal internally supplies a positive well-formed coefficient marked ideal]
\label{hir:actual-original-coherent-weighted-coefficient-marked}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-vertex-coefficient-data,hir:actual-original-weighted-coefficient-compression,hir:actual-commonmark-function-cosupport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoherentWeightedCoefficientMarked.coefficientDomain,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoherentWeightedCoefficientMarked.coefficientMarked,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoherentWeightedCoefficientMarked.exists_original_coherent_weighted_coefficient_marked}
\leanok
Any original analytic germ ideal in coordinates of dimensions $k,s,t$
internally supplies its genuine analytic vertex coefficient data on one
actual open neighbourhood $U$ of the origin. On the genuine complex
manifold $U$, the literal restricted low coefficients with their unchanged
positive difference weights define a well-formed marked ideal with carrier
$U$, empty ordered boundary and positive common mark $D$. Its whole
cosupport is exactly the intersection of the principal coefficient
cosupports with their individual weights, all on that same actual $U$.
No coefficient family, globally extended coefficient function, coherence,
marked ideal or desired cosupport identity is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Select actual analytic vertex coefficient data from the original ideal.
Its bounded normal exponents give the actual finite low-coefficient index
set, and the strict degree bounds give positive difference weights. The
actual common open domain is a neighbourhood of the origin. Every selected
coefficient function is holomorphic there. Compose it only with the genuine
subtype inclusion $U\hookrightarrow\mathbb C^{k+s}$; the local holomorphic
composition theorem proves holomorphicity on the whole manifold $U$.

Form the literal powered finite-function ideal on that subtype and use the
positive common product as mark. The first theorem gives global coherence
on $U$, and the second gives the entire asserted cosupport equality.
The carrier is the whole complex manifold $U$, hence a closed submanifold
of itself; the empty boundary is SNC and transverse to it. These facts
prove well-formedness of the actual marked record. Product positivity
proves positivity of its mark. Every object uses the same selected original
coefficient data and the same real domain.
\end{proof}

The coefficient ideal may be zero, including for an empty family. No
nonzero-along-domain condition or invariant-domain membership is inferred.
A canonical terminating driver needs that additional genuine geometric
condition, as well as the still separate persistent equal-HS presentation
and its transformation compatibility. Neither HS-locus identification nor
strict decrease or general G8 closure is asserted here.
