\section{Actual tangent cuts of the joint normal layers}

Let $S=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^{d+n},0}$ over the actual tangent germ
ring $B=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$, let $N$ be its whole displacement
ideal, and let $\sigma:S\to\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^n,0}$ be the proved
zero-base trace. Write $Q_q(K)=S/(K+N^q)$ and
$G_q(K)=\operatorname{im}(N^q\to Q_{q+1}(K))$.

\begin{theorem}[Actual complex tensor symbol injectivity]
\label{hir:actual-joint-cut-complex-symbol}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualJointTangentCuts.complexTensorSymbol_injective_of_ambient_regular}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-joint-base-fibre-layer-symbols,hir:actual-principal-parts-residue-layer,hir:actual-principal-parts-residue-symbol-square}
Suppose the actual lower whole quotient $Q_q(K)$ is flat over $B$.
If multiplication by $\sigma v$ is a regular degree-$q$ symbol on the
actual ambient maximal-ideal layer of $\sigma_*K$, for $v\in N$, then
the actual symbol $G_q(K)\to G_{q+1}(K)$ remains injective after
complex origin-residue tensoring.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual lower-flatness hypothesis makes the named residue-layer map
injective. The whole zero-base layer isomorphism transports multiplication
by $v$ on the actual fibre to multiplication by $\sigma v$ on the
actual ambient layer. Regularity makes this ambient symbol injective:
if two representatives $g,h$ have equal products modulo the next whole
denominator, then $\sigma v(g-h)$ lies in that denominator, so
regularity puts $g-h$ in the current denominator. Thus the actual fibre
symbol is injective. The literal tensor/residue symbol square then
shows that equal tensor-symbol values have equal residue-layer images;
injectivity of the actual residue-layer map proves equality of the
original tensors.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual short exact filtration retains all free truncations]
\label{hir:actual-joint-cut-layer-truncation-free}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualJointTangentCuts.normalTruncations_free_of_layers,Hironaka.HironakaActualJointTangentCuts.normalLayers_finite_free_of_truncations}
\leanok
\uses{hir:relative-normal-uniformtrunc-original-short-exact}
If every whole $G_q(K)$ is free over $B$, then every whole $Q_q(K)$
is free over $B$. Conversely, finite free truncations give finite free
whole layers. Zero-order quotients and layers retain their literal meanings.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual sequence $0\to G_q(K)\to Q_{q+1}(K)\to Q_q(K)\to0$
is exact. The zero quotient $Q_0(K)$ is zero and hence free. Inductively,
projectivity of the free last term supplies a section, giving
$Q_{q+1}(K)\simeq G_q(K)\oplus Q_q(K)$ and proving freeness.
For the converse, projectivity of $Q_q(K)$ splits the same sequence,
so $G_q(K)$ is a direct summand of the finite free $Q_{q+1}(K)$.
Its projection is surjective, proving finite generation; its inclusion
has that projection as retraction, proving projectivity. A finite
projective module over the local ring $B$ is free.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One actual tangent cut preserves every free layer]
\label{hir:actual-joint-cut-layer-free}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualJointTangentCuts.normalLayers_after_parameter_finite_free}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-joint-cut-complex-symbol,hir:actual-joint-cut-layer-truncation-free,hir:actual-base-complex-symbol-criterion}
Suppose all actual whole $G_q(K)$ are finite free over $B$, and the
actual ambient maximal-ideal symbol of $\sigma v$ is regular in every
degree for $\sigma_*K$. Then every whole layer of
$K+(v)$ with respect to the unchanged actual displacement ideal $N$
is finite free over $B$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding filtration theorem gives freeness, hence flatness, of
every actual lower $Q_q(K)$. The preceding complex-symbol theorem
therefore proves injectivity of every actual complex-residue tensor
symbol. The canonical base-residue-field dictionary identifies this
with injectivity after tensoring by the genuine local residue field of
$B$. The local splitting criterion applies to the finite source layer
and finite free target layer: the symbol inclusion splits over $B$.
For positive degrees, the actual kernel theorem identifies the quotient
layer for $K+(v)$ with the cokernel of that split symbol, hence a finite
projective and therefore free module. In degree zero the actual layer
quotient map is an isomorphism, so the same conclusion holds directly.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every distinct actual coordinate cut retains the whole free filtration]
\label{hir:actual-joint-all-coordinate-cuts-free}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualJointTangentCuts.jointCoordinateCuts,Hironaka.HironakaActualJointTangentCuts.map_jointCoordinateCuts,Hironaka.HironakaActualJointTangentCuts.normalLayers_after_coordinate_cuts_finite_free}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-joint-cut-layer-free,hir:actual-initial-coordinate-quotient-ordered-cuts}
Let $L$ be a list of distinct original ambient coordinate indices and
let $K_L$ be obtained from the unchanged joint ideal $K$ by successively
adding the corresponding actual normal coordinate germs. Suppose all
whole $G_q(K)$ are finite free over $B$, and the genuine homogeneous
initial ideal of the Taylor extension of $\sigma_*K$ is stable under
partial differentiation in every coordinate of $L$. Then every whole
$G_q(K_L)$ is finite free over $B$. Moreover $\sigma_*K_L$ is literally
obtained from $\sigma_*K$ by the same ordered ambient coordinate cuts.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the ordered list. The empty list changes neither ideal nor
filtration. Suppose the next index is $i$ and the tail is $L'$. The
proved actual initial-symbol theorem makes coordinate $i$ a regular
symbol on every actual ambient maximal-ideal layer. The preceding
one-cut theorem therefore gives finite freeness of every whole joint
layer after adding its actual displacement coordinate. Ideal extension
preserves sums and generated principal ideals, and the true zero-base
trace sends this displacement coordinate to the same ambient coordinate;
thus the new fibre ideal is exactly the ambient coordinate quotient.
The proved initial-coordinate-quotient theorem identifies its genuine
initial ideal with the old initial ideal plus that formal coordinate.
Because no remaining index equals $i$, differentiation in each remaining
coordinate preserves both the old initial ideal and the added coordinate
ideal, by the proved remaining-derivative stability theorem. These are
exactly the hypotheses for the tail. Induction proves the assertion
for the entire ordered list in every degree, including degree zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original constant-Hilbert--Samuel geometry supplies every tangent cut]
\label{hir:actual-original-joint-all-tangent-cuts-free}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualJointTangentCuts.exists_original_hs_constant_centre_all_tangent_cut_layers_free}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-joint-all-coordinate-cuts-free,hir:actual-joint-cut-layer-truncation-free,hir:actual-coupled-centre-free-jets-initial-symbols}
Let $A$ be a coherent germ ideal on a complex manifold, and let $x$ lie
on a dimension-$d$ regular centre along which the entire intrinsic
Hilbert--Samuel function is constant. Then one actual flattening at
$x$ supplies its original origin ideal $I_0$ and its true joint
extension $J=L^*I_0$ such that every whole displacement layer remains
finite free over the original tangent germ ring after successively
killing all $d$ actual tangent-displacement coordinates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the coupled original geometric producer once, retaining its one
flattening and its actual original ideal $I_0$. It simultaneously gives
all positive joint truncations finite free and the actual initial
partial-derivative stability along every tangent coordinate. Its literal
origin-generator equality identifies the same joint ideal with $L^*I_0$.
The zero truncation is the actual zero module, so every joint truncation
is finite free. The proved short exact filtration theorem therefore
gives all initial whole joint layers finite free. The entire zero-base
retraction sends the same $J$ to $I_0$. Apply the preceding ordered-cut
theorem to the list of all tangent coordinate indices; these indices
are distinct because the actual coordinate inclusion is injective.
All hypotheses are thus produced from the original geometric data, and
the result holds in every degree for one unchanged actual family.
\end{proof}

The final theorem supplies the entire tangent-cut transport from one
common original coherent constant-Hilbert--Samuel geometric family.
The subsequent actual zero-displacement quotient must still identify
these layers with the original centre-normal filtration. The complete
higher Hilbert--Samuel transformation theorem remains separate.
