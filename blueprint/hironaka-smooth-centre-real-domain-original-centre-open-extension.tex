\section{An actual relatively open original centre locus}

All points and opens live in the original manifold and centre. A coordinate
nonvanishing open need not contain zero; its actual original centre image is
relatively open and accumulates at the original point. One fixed genuine
flattening is retained. Density on unrelated components and origin flatness
are not asserted.

\begin{lemma}[The same genuine flattening gives an original relatively open centre image]
\label{hir:real-domain-fixed-flattening-original-relative-centre-open}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-centre-domain-actual-flattened-coherent-family}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainOriginalCentreOpen.originalCentreBaseOpen,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainOriginalCentreOpen.isOpen_originalCentreBaseOpen,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainOriginalCentreOpen.originalCentreBaseOpen_centre_eq_image,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainOriginalCentreOpen.originalCentreBaseOpen_mem_closure}
\leanok
Fix a genuine original centre flattening $\Phi$ centred at $x$, with its
source in the true chart target and $\Phi(e(x))=0$. Let $U$ be a real base
open meeting every neighbourhood of zero whose tangent inclusion lies in
$\Phi$'s actual target. Write $F(y)=\Phi(e(y))$, $\theta$ for tangent inclusion
and $p$ for tangent projection. The ORIGINAL open
\[
 O=\operatorname{flatDomain}(\Phi)\cap (p\circ F)^{-1}(U)
\]
has its entire centre intersection equal to
\[
 C\cap O=\{\operatorname{flatInverse}(\theta(a)):a\in U\}.
\]
Every point of $O$ lies in the actual original chart and flattening domain,
and $x\in\overline{C\cap O}$. Thus the displayed original centre locus is
relatively open and meets every original neighbourhood of $x$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original chart is continuous at each point of its open source, and the
true flattening is holomorphic, hence continuous, at each point of its open
source. Their composite $F$ and the linear tangent projection are therefore
continuous at every point of the open flattening domain. At a point of $O$,
intersect that domain neighbourhood with the inverse image of the base open
under this locally continuous map. This is a neighbourhood contained in $O$,
so $O$ is open and remains in the stated real domain.
For an actual $a\in U$, the true inverse target/chart identities put its
inverse point inside the flattening domain and give its flattened value
$\theta(a)$. This value lies in the coordinate centre plane; the original
centre flattening relation puts the inverse point in $C$. Tangent projection
of $\theta(a)$ is $a$, so it also lies in $O$. Conversely, for $y\in C\cap O$
the true centre relation puts $F(y)$ in the coordinate plane. On that plane
its normal coordinates are zero, and its tangent coordinates are precisely
$p(F(y))$, so $\theta(p(F(y)))=F(y)$. The actual left inverse chart/flattening
identity now gives $y=\operatorname{flatInverse}(\theta(p(F(y))))$; the base
point lies in $U$ by the definition of $O$. These prove both complete image
inclusions. At zero the genuine inverse is $x$. It is continuous near zero
on its true target, and tangent inclusion is continuous. Given any original
neighbourhood of $x$, their actual composite preimage is a neighbourhood of
zero. The assumed base-open accumulation supplies a point of $U$ in that
preimage, whose genuine inverse belongs to $C\cap O$. Thus every original
neighbourhood meets the displayed locus and $x$ belongs to its closure.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original inputs supply all-degree stalk freeness on a true relatively open centre locus]
\label{hir:real-domain-original-relative-centre-open-all-stalk-degrees-free}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-fixed-flattening-original-relative-centre-open,hir:real-domain-original-all-nearby-relative-stalks-free-open}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainOriginalCentreOpen.allRelativeIdealDegreesFree,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainOriginalCentreOpen.exists_original_regular_relative_centre_open_all_stalk_degrees_free}
\leanok
ONLY original regular-centre geometry, original ideal coherence and original
ideal containment supply one genuine original flattening and one ORIGINAL
open $O$ in its actual domain, with $x\in\overline{C\cap O}$. At EVERY original
point $y\in C\cap O$, ALL genuine original relative degree layers are finite
free and flat over their independently descended original fixed-chart centre
stalk quotient, for ALL natural degrees simultaneously. The ENTIRE original
associated direct sum is free and flat for that same centre action. No
coordinate-to-original locus, rank, flatness, chart bridge or free-open
certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original true stalk-freeness producer internally supplies the SAME genuine
flattening and actual base open $U$, with its whole original nonlinear stalk
dictionaries and all-degree canonical centre-module freeness/flatness.
Apply the preceding actual topological producer to obtain $O$ and its ENTIRE
centre intersection/image equality, retaining that same flattening. For an
arbitrary original $y\in C\cap O$, the equality supplies an actual $a\in U$
whose true inverse point is exactly $y$. The internally proved original
stalk statement at that point gives the claimed whole and ALL-degree
properties. Substitute this actual point equality. Its original chart-source
membership proofs are propositions, so the original whole ideals, power
layers and their independently descended centre actions are the same
actual objects. Thus the original stalk conclusion holds at EVERY original
point of the relatively open centre locus, with no degree-dependent
shrinking. The first theorem gives closure at $x$ and the actual original
geometric domain. This is still a genuine fixed-chart stalk statement;
intrinsic chart independence and a literal sheaf normal-cone identification
remain subsequent obligations.
\end{proof}
