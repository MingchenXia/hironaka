\section{Whole actual pullback saturation computes the geometric strict ideal}

\begin{theorem}[Coherent off-divisor membership forces a whole exceptional power]
\label{hir:actual-coherent-off-divisor-membership-power}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-colon-dictionary,lag:prime-nullstellensatz}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictPullbackSaturation.exists_coordinate_power_mul_mem_of_off_divisor_membership}
\leanok
Let $\mathcal J$ be any actual coherent ideal sheaf near the origin of
$\mathbf C^n$, let $G$ be an actual holomorphic function near zero, and
let $e$ be a coordinate. If $G_y\in\mathcal J_y$ at every sufficiently
nearby actual point with $y_e\ne0$, then for some $N\ge0$ the whole
original germ $z_e^N[G]$ belongs to $\mathcal J_0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Genuine Oka coherence gives a finite actual holomorphic presentation of
the colon sheaf $\mathcal J:G$ on one neighbourhood of zero. At each
nearby point off $z_e=0$, the hypothesis says the constant function one
belongs to this colon, so its finite generators cannot all vanish at
that point: evaluating a genuine local expression for one would give
$1=0$. Thus $z_e$ vanishes on the common zero locus of those actual colon
generators near zero. Every original generator germ belongs to the whole
colon $\mathcal J_0:[G]$, by its actual colon membership and the original
whole-germ dictionary. The analytic Nullstellensatz applied to these
actual generators puts $z_e$ in the radical of this whole colon. Some
power $z_e^N$ therefore belongs to $\mathcal J_0:[G]$, which says exactly
$z_e^N[G]\in\mathcal J_0$. The finite colon presentation and the power
are constructed internally.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual chart inverse identifies whole pullback ideals off the divisor]
\label{hir:actual-normal-blowup-off-divisor-whole-pullback}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-blowup-exceptional-locus-exact,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictPullbackSaturation.actualBlowupInverse,Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictPullbackSaturation.pullback_vanishingIdeal_mem_iff_off_coordinate}
\leanok
For an actual normal-coordinate blowup chart $\pi$, any original subset
$W$, and any actual point $y$ with $y_e\ne0$, the independently defined
whole functional ideals satisfy
\[
 (\pi^*\mathcal I_W)_y=\mathcal I_{\pi^{-1}W,y}.
\]
The inverse chart is constructed from the literal functions
$x_e$, $x_i/x_e-b_i$ on selected nonpivot coordinates, and $x_i$ on
unselected coordinates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On the actual open set $x_e\ne0$, the displayed functions are holomorphic.
Substituting them in the actual polynomial chart and conversely gives
the identity in every coordinate. For a function vanishing on the actual
inverse image of $W$ near $y$, compose it with this true inverse. It is
holomorphic near $\pi(y)$ and vanishes on $W$ there: the actual inverse
maps a neighbourhood into the original vanishing neighbourhood, and
$\pi\circ\pi^{-1}$ is the identity there. This source vanishing function
pulls back to the original target function on a neighbourhood of $y$,
by the other inverse identity. A single genuine pulled-back generator
with coefficient one proves its whole pullback-ideal membership. The
other direction follows by substituting actual pulled-back vanishing
functions and their finite holomorphic coefficients. No regularity or
coherence of $W$ is needed for this off-divisor equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole actual sheaf pullback origin ideal is the literal ring pullback]
\label{hir:actual-holomorphic-whole-origin-pullback}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality,hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-colon-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictPullbackSaturation.originalOriginIdeal_holomorphic_pullback_eq}
\leanok
For an actual holomorphic map fixing zero and an original coherent ideal
sheaf, the whole original origin ideal of its independently defined sheaf
pullback equals the ring-theoretic image ideal under the actual germ
composition map.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every actual original pulled-back member, its defining finite local
functional span expresses it as a finite holomorphic combination of
actual compositions of source members. The original germ of each such
composition is literally the germ homomorphism image of its original
source germ; the genuine finite functional identity therefore places
the whole target germ in the ring image ideal. Conversely every source
generator germ has an actual representative belonging to the original
source sheaf. Its actual composition is a defining generator of the
independently constructed pullback sheaf, so its whole germ belongs to
that pullback's origin ideal. Actual coherence of the holomorphic
pullback gives the required whole original membership dictionary. Closure
under finite sums and arbitrary multipliers proves both entire ideal
inclusions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original analytic strict ideals are the whole exceptional saturation]
\label{hir:actual-original-analytic-geometric-strict-whole-saturation}
\uses{hir:actual-coherent-off-divisor-membership-power,hir:actual-normal-blowup-off-divisor-whole-pullback,hir:actual-holomorphic-whole-origin-pullback,hir:actual-geometric-strict-exceptional-parameter-regular}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictPullbackSaturation.mem_original_analytic_strict_ideal_iff_exists_coordinate_power,Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictPullbackSaturation.exists_original_analytic_strict_fixed_power_colon}
\leanok
For any original analytic subset $W$, any actual normal-coordinate blowup
chart with normal dimension at least two, and any whole original target
germ $g$,
\[
 g\in I(W')\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists N\ge0:\ z_e^Ng\in\pi^*I(W).
\]
One internally chosen finite exponent $N_0$, independent of every target germ, also gives the whole ideal equality $I(W')=(\pi^*I(W)):(z_e^{N_0})$.
Consequently the whole actual exceptional saturation is radical, and the
radical in the preceding radical-colon formula need not be supplied as
an independent ideal operation to compute the strict ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original analyticity gives genuine coherence of $\mathcal I_W$ and its
actual holomorphic pullback $\mathcal J$. Suppose $g$ belongs to the
whole geometric strict ideal and choose an actual holomorphic
representative $G$. The exact exceptional hyperplane dictionary writes
the strict set as the closure of $\pi^{-1}(W)\setminus\{z_e=0\}$.
Thus $G$ vanishes on that defining set in a genuine neighbourhood of
zero. At any nearby point off the divisor, shrink within that
neighbourhood and off the divisor; $G$ then belongs to the actual whole
vanishing ideal of $\pi^{-1}W$. The off-divisor theorem identifies this
with actual whole pullback membership. The coherent off-divisor criterion
now gives $z_e^N[G]\in\mathcal J_0$ for some internally produced $N$.
The whole origin pullback dictionary identifies this with the displayed
ring ideal. Conversely a whole member of that pullback ideal vanishes
on $\pi^{-1}W$ near zero, hence on its off-divisor defining subset.
Continuity extends the vanishing to its closure, so $z_e^Ng\in I(W')$.
The already proved whole exceptional-power saturation of the actual
geometric strict ideal cancels that power and yields $g\in I(W')$.
Both implications concern the entire original ideals and actual functions,
not only point values or finite jets. The actual holomorphic germ ring is Noetherian. Its ascending sequence of whole colons by successive powers of $z_e$ therefore stabilizes at one internally chosen index $N_0$. Each power-membership condition is the corresponding whole colon membership, so any member of their union belongs to the $N_0$th colon: pass to the larger of its index and $N_0$ and use stabilization. Conversely every member of that colon already belongs to the union. The whole ideal equality follows from the displayed equivalence. Since a whole vanishing ideal is radical, the computed saturation is radical as a consequence.
\end{proof}

This does not yet identify the ideal generated by normalized canonical
members with this saturation, prove transformed standard-family relations,
or establish a reverse current Hilbert--Samuel persistence criterion.
