\section{Exact empty-boundary and compact-ambient original pure canonical forms}

\begin{theorem}[Original compact pure input with literal empty boundary]
\label{hir:original-pure-compact-empty-canonical-specialization}
\uses{hir:original-compact-pure-fixed-target-independent-family}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureCanonicalSpecializations.exists_original_compact_pure_empty_boundary_family}
\leanok
For an original compact analytic carrier $W$ of pure dimension $d<n$,
the actual canonical pure target precedes every arbitrary smooth
source, with the entire independent-source output. The SAME target
and each internally stopped source have literal exceptional locus
equal to the union of the whole final boundary and to the inverse
image of their own singular set. No boundary-density premise is needed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set the original boundary equal to the empty list in the genuine
fixed-target compact pure producer. Its sole boundary-density premise
then reduces to the actual inclusion $W\subseteq\overline W$.
Keep its selected full terminal tower, actual stopping prefix, tail,
and entire later independently stopped intrinsic-source family.
The output already proves, for these SAME witnesses, the two exact
support equalities when the initial boundary is empty. Apply its
empty-boundary implication to reflexivity on the target and on each
source. No old existential witness is selected and no source
compactness is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original pure canonical families on compact ambient manifolds]
\label{hir:original-pure-compact-ambient-canonical-specialization}
\uses{hir:original-compact-pure-fixed-target-independent-family,hir:original-pure-compact-empty-canonical-specialization}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureCanonicalSpecializations.exists_original_pure_boundary_family_of_compactSpace,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureCanonicalSpecializations.exists_original_pure_empty_boundary_family_of_compactSpace}
\leanok
On a compact ambient complex manifold, every original analytic pure
$W$ of dimension $d<n$ has the entire actual fixed-target canonical
pure family relative to an original SNC boundary with the original
explicit density condition. With empty boundary this density premise
is unnecessary and the SAME target and each actual intrinsic source
retain their exact whole exceptional support equalities. The sources
may be empty, disconnected or noncompact.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original analyticity makes $W$ closed; compactness of the ambient
manifold therefore gives actual compactness of $W$ internally.
Apply the genuine compact pure canonical producer to the unchanged
original carrier, dimension, regular density, SNC boundary and
boundary density. Its entire target and source result is retained.
For empty boundary apply the preceding exact compact-carrier
specialization with this same internally derived compactness.
All conclusions concern the original pure datum and its own singular
bad set, rather than a larger mixed-carrier bad set.
\end{proof}
