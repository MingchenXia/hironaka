\section{Biholomorphic naturality of the actual parallel histories}
\label{sec:hironaka-componentwise-history-naturality}

All histories in this section retain their absolute stage positions,
including the actual empty blowups after local termination. A tower
isomorphism includes the stage maps, the projection squares and the
whole centres at every preceding stage.

\begin{theorem}[Whole parallel centres transport under actual tower isomorphisms]
\label{hir:componentwise-parallel-centre-biholomorphic-transport}
\uses{hir:componentwise-canonical-centre-exact-trace,mc:tower-isomorphism-basic,mc:tower-isomorphism-transform,mc:tower-iso-opens,mc:contact-marked-canonical,hir:centre-function-seq,hir:open-restriction-marked}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseHistoryNaturality.pullbackOpen,Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseHistoryNaturality.localMaximum_transport,Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseHistoryNaturality.parallelCentre_transport}
\leanok
Let $\Phi:X'\simeq X$ be an actual biholomorphism, $M$ an original
marked ideal in the domain of a proved invariant $R$, and
$X=\coprod_iU_i$ a disjoint open partition. Let $U'_i$ be matching
opens, so $U'_i=\Phi^{-1}(U_i)$. Suppose actual towers $T'$ and $T$
are isomorphic over $\Phi$, with last map $\chi$, and that each
actual restriction $T|_{U_i}$ is canonical for $M|_{U_i}$.
Then the entire parallel centres satisfy
\[
 \chi^{-1}C_R(M,T,(U_i))
   =C_R(\Phi^*M,T',(U'_i)).
\]
In particular the equality concerns every whole centre, including
empty centres, with no centre correspondence assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The base and last-stage biholomorphisms restrict to the matching
true opens. Restricting the actual tower isomorphism gives a
tower isomorphism between $T'|_{U'_i}$ and $T|_{U_i}$ over the
restricted base biholomorphism. Pullback along the literal
commuting open-inclusion square identifies the original marked
record on $U'_i$ with the pullback of the original one on $U_i$,
including its ordered boundary.

The genuine transformed cosupports are inverse images under
the last restricted biholomorphism. Property (6) of $R$ equates
their invariant values at corresponding cosupport points,
since the target restricted prefix is canonical. Surjectivity
therefore equates their entire maximum loci, even when empty.
For a point on the source stage choose the unique partition
member containing its target original image. The exact whole
trace formula for each parallel union reduces membership in
both sides to this same corresponding local maximum. This
proves the claimed entire centre equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual parallel sequences are isomorphic independently of blowup choices]
\label{hir:componentwise-parallel-history-choice-independence}
\uses{hir:componentwise-parallel-centre-biholomorphic-transport,hir:componentwise-canonical-growing-history,mc:tower-isomorphism-basic,ana:blowups}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseHistoryNaturality.parallel_sequences_isTowerIso}
\leanok
For matching disjoint original open partitions under $\Phi$, let
$s$ and $s'$ be any actual growing parallel whole-maximum histories
of $M$ and $\Phi^*M$, with the restrictions of $s$ canonical on
each member of its partition. Then for every absolute index $j$
their actual recorded prefixes are isomorphic over $\Phi$.
No isomorphism between stages is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At index zero both actual prefixes are reflexive, so $\Phi$
itself gives their tower isomorphism. Suppose the preceding
prefix isomorphism has last map $\chi$. The preceding theorem
internally proves that the two actual next whole parallel
centres correspond under $\chi$.

Compose the target next blowup with $\chi^{-1}$. It is an
actual blowup along the source next centre. The two genuine
blowups along that same centre are biholomorphic over the
source preceding stage by their universal properties: each
factors uniquely through the other, and uniqueness makes
the composites identities. The resulting next-stage
biholomorphism commutes with the actual blowup maps. Together
with the internally proved centre correspondence this extends
the preceding tower isomorphism. Induction gives every prefix
isomorphism, without deleting empty positions or identifying
independently chosen blowups by definitional equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original input internally gives biholomorphically corresponding locally terminal histories]
\label{hir:original-componentwise-histories-biholomorphic}
\uses{hir:componentwise-parallel-history-choice-independence,hir:original-componentwise-compact-locally-terminal-history,hir:componentwise-history-locally-finite-empty-tails}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseHistoryNaturality.exists_original_componentwise_histories_biholomorphic}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an original marked ideal of the canonical invariant's
domain, with compact original cosupport on every connected
ambient component, and let $\Phi:X'\simeq X$ be an actual
biholomorphism. The original input internally constructs two
actual growing global test histories on $M$ and $\Phi^*M$,
using the literal original components $U_i$ and their actual
preimages $\Phi^{-1}(U_i)$. All their corresponding restricted
prefixes are canonical. Their actual parallel steps retain
every empty position, and at every absolute index the whole
recorded global prefixes are isomorphic over $\Phi$.
On each corresponding pair of component opens, both actual
restrictions are resolutions after one common local terminal
time. Both sequences have locally finite actual centre images.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the first actual history from the original componentwise
compact input. Pull back the literal disjoint component cover
under $\Phi$; it is a disjoint open cover of $X'$. The original
domain condition transports under $\Phi$, so the parallel
construction internally supplies a second actual test history
on that pulled-back original record and cover. Its blowup
choices may be independent of those of the first history.
Apply the preceding choice-independence theorem to these
two actual constructed sequences. This internally gives all
whole-prefix tower isomorphisms over $\Phi$.

The first history resolves on an original component after
its internally obtained terminal time. Restrict the actual
prefix isomorphism at every later index to the corresponding
component opens. The literal original pullback square and
the transformed-cosupport dictionary transport the genuine
test and empty-cosupport conditions, so the second restricted
history resolves at those same indices. Finally apply the
proved local-terminal-history criterion to the second
sequence to obtain local finiteness of its actual centre
images; the first sequence already has that property.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This is naturality under ambient biholomorphisms for the constructed
componentwise histories. It does not assert naturality under an
arbitrary smooth submersion, a common finite global terminal time,
or an unrestricted connected noncompact elementary scheduler.
\end{remark}
