\section{Entire-current-cosupport controlled split for prescribed actual blowups}

\begin{theorem}[One actual comparison and whole controlled split at every current point]
\label{hir:disjoint-actual-global-controlled-split}
\uses{hir:disjoint-actual-controlled-transport,hir:disjoint-actual-controlled-strict-contact,hir:disjoint-matched-whole-coherent-trace,hir:disjoint-same-flattening-normal-kernel,hir:actual-closed-contact-full-restricted-blowup}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaDisjointActualGlobalControlledSplit.exists_actual_global_controlled_split}
\leanok
Let $N$ be a closed regular $d$-dimensional submanifold of a complex manifold
$A$ of dimension $d+t$, equipped with its existing native intrinsic atlas.
Let $D\subset N$, let $\pi:Y\to A$ be its genuine ambient blowup along the
entire image of $D$, and let $\rho:Z\to N$ be a prescribed genuine original
intrinsic blowup of $D$. Suppose the original ambient coherent ideal $J$
satisfies $\mathcal I_N\subset J\subset\mathcal I_{\operatorname{val}(D)}$.
Set $I=\operatorname{val}_N^*J$, retaining its entire possibly nonradical
structure. All controlled transforms have original mark one.

Internally construct ONE genuine biholomorphism $E$ from this prescribed $Z$
to the entire geometric strict transform of $N$, over $\rho$. For EVERY
point $y$ in the actual ambient controlled cosupport, including points away
from the source centre, internally construct a centered actual ambient
flattening $\Theta$ and matching old native coordinates $\Psi$ at
$z=E^{-1}(y)$, with the entire actual strict-contact plane equation, and prove
\[
 \operatorname{map}(\operatorname{planeTrace})(J'_\Theta)=I'_\Psi,
 \qquad
 J'_\Theta=\operatorname{comap}(\operatorname{planeTrace})(I'_\Psi).
\]
No source joint flattening, coordinate-centre presentation, chart, comparison,
trace or split certificate is an input. The same actual $E$ works before
quantifying over the entire current cosupport.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual closed-contact blowup universal properties produce the whole
strict-contact native manifold and the biholomorphism $E$ over the prescribed
$\rho$, independently of any current point. Its full strict contact is regular
of constant dimension $d$. Whole actual functional controlled restriction
identifies the ambient controlled ideal restricted to this native contact
with the controlled transform of its literal original restriction $I$.
Actual total-transform and exceptional-factor transport through $E$ therefore
identify its entire pullback with the controlled ideal of the prescribed
$\rho$. This keeps the original mark and every nonradical ideal member.

Fix any actual current-cosupport point $y$. Monotonicity from
$\mathcal I_N\subset J$, and the genuine mark-one controlled-transform
formula for the REGULAR full normal ideal, put $y$ on the geometric strict
contact. Actual regularity at this point itself supplies a genuine centered
ambient flattening $\Theta$, its true source inclusion and the ENTIRE local
strict-contact plane equation. This step applies both over and away from the
source centre; no source-centre chart or prior matching condition is required.
Apply the genuine matched-coordinate theorem to THIS chosen $\Theta$ and the
already constructed actual $E$. It constructs $\Psi$ in the existing old
native atlas and gives whole coherent-ideal trace for their actual pullback.
Substituting the preceding global functional controlled equality gives the
first asserted ideal identity.

The ambient controlled ideal contains the entire vanishing ideal of the
strict full contact, by the same source normal-ideal monotonicity. In THIS
same $\Theta$, actual normal-coordinate vanishing germs and relative Hadamard
division prove that its entire plane-trace kernel lies in the adapted
controlled ideal. Since actual plane trace is surjective, its map followed by
comap adds only that kernel, which is already contained. Substitution of the
proved whole old-controlled trace gives the entire second equality. Only the
regular full normal contact is replaced by its vanishing ideal; no radical
strict-support replacement of $I$ or $I'$ is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The result is a whole actual one-step transformation law on the entire
current cosupport, on arbitrary source ambient manifolds or genuine open
sets. It does not assert that source opens are products. Constructing a
specific original ambient extension $J=I+(t)$ and coupling all successive
marked histories and recursive canonical values remain separate tasks;
no full ambient-independent resolution theorem is concluded here.
\end{remark}
