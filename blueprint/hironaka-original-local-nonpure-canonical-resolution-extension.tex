\section{Original mixed analytic data for the actual local canonical algorithm}

All carriers below are original analytic subsets; no compactness of the
carrier is imposed. The increasing list ranges over $0\le k<n$.
Its completeness as a decomposition of $W$ uses empty interior; the
locally good conditions on these individual lower-dimensional parts
do not need that additional hypothesis.

\begin{lemma}[Original intrinsic parts supply every local algorithm condition]
\label{hir:original-local-nonpure-intrinsic-input}
\uses{hir:embedded-resolution-dimension-parts,hir:embedded-resolution-analytic-local}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalNonpureCanonicalResolution.intrinsic_local_input}
\leanok
Let $W\subseteq X^n$ be original analytic, let $E$ be an original
ordered SNC boundary, and suppose $W\subseteq\overline{W\setminus|E|}$.
Put $F=W_{\rm sing}\cup(W\cap|E|)$, using the entire intrinsic
all-dimensional singular set, and
$L=[(W^{(0)},0),\ldots,(W^{(n-1)},n-1)]$.
Then $F$ is closed, every entry of this actual whole ordered list is
locally good relative to $(E,F)$, and every pair of distinct list
positions has carrier intersection contained in $F$. No locally good
part, bad-set or intersection certificate is an input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The intrinsic singular set is closed for a closed analytic carrier.
The finite union of the actual closed boundary members is closed,
so its intersection with $W$ and then the displayed union are closed.
Every intrinsic dimension part is analytic, and its label belongs
to the actual range $0,\ldots,n-1$. Its nonregular locus is contained
in the whole original singular set. Its intersection with the boundary
is contained in $W\cap|E|$. The genuine dimension-part density theorem,
applied to the original density off the boundary, places the whole
part in the closure of its complement of $F$. These are exactly all
locally good fields. Finally distinct intrinsic dimension parts
intersect only in the original whole singular set. The actual range
has no repeated labels, so this gives the ordered pairwise statement
for the entire literal list, including empty parts.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Whole strict geometry assembles from the genuine resolved dimension parts]
\label{hir:original-local-nonpure-whole-geometry}
\uses{hir:embedded-resolution-dimension-parts,hir:original-nonpure-canonical-elementary-smooth-projection,ana:strict-transform-composition}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalNonpureCanonicalResolution.whole_geometry_of_dimension_parts}
\leanok
Let $W\subseteq X^n$ be original analytic with empty interior, let
$T$ be an actual blowup tower, and let $E_f$ be an actual ordered
boundary on its endpoint. Suppose the actual conventional strict
transforms of the intrinsic dimension parts are pairwise disjoint,
each lower-dimensional part is everywhere regular with its actual
label, and each has normal crossings with $E_f$. Then the whole
strict transform of $W$ is a closed submanifold, is the literal finite
union of those strict parts, is regular of dimension $k$ along every
actual dimension-$k$ strict part, and has normal crossings with the
same whole boundary. These hypotheses concern the part geometry;
whole strict geometry is derived, rather than assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Empty interior makes every part of dimension at least $n$ empty and
expresses $W$ as the finite union of its $n$ lower-dimensional intrinsic
parts. Conventional strict transform commutes with this finite union:
expand its defining closure, distribute inverse image and removal of
the exceptional locus over the union, then use closure of a finite
union. Every strict part is closed by its actual definition.

At a point of one nonempty strict part, remove the finite union of
all other closed strict parts. Pairwise disjointness makes the
complement an open neighbourhood of that point. On this neighbourhood
the entire strict carrier equals exactly the chosen strict part.
Locality of genuine regular coordinates transfers its actual dimension
to the whole carrier. The finite union formula supplies such a part
at every point, so the whole carrier is a closed submanifold.
The same local equality and locality of adapted normal-crossings
coordinate families transfer normal crossings from the chosen part
to the whole carrier, with the same entire ordered boundary. Empty
parts, empty ambient spaces and dimension zero are covered by the
same finite-union argument; no uniform nonemptiness is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original mixed analytic data produce a genuine canonical local target]
\label{hir:original-local-nonpure-canonical-target}
\uses{hir:original-local-nonpure-intrinsic-input,hir:original-local-nonpure-whole-geometry,hir:original-local-canonical-ordered-resolution-producer,hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-regular-locus,hir:original-dimension-part-smooth-shift}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalNonpureCanonicalResolution.exists_original_local_nonpure_target}
\leanok
Let $W\subseteq X$ be original analytic with empty interior, let $E$
be original SNC, and suppose $W\subseteq\overline{W\setminus|E|}$.

these original data internally supply an actual finite ordered

$F_U=(W_U)_{\rm sing}\cup(W_U\cap|E_U|)$.

SNC, exceptional and newly born boundary support over $F_U$, whole
singular preimage in the exceptional locus, isomorphism off $F_U$,
closed smooth conventional strict carrier, exact intrinsic finite
part union, its actual dimensions along each part, pairwise disjoint
strict parts, whole normal crossings, holomorphicity, properness
and surjectivity. No incoming history, local part, comparison or
compactness of $W$ is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first lemma internally supplies the actual original bad set and
all natural ordered locally good part hypotheses. Apply the genuine
local ordered-tree producer to the original inclusion of the same
prescribed $U$. Its compact-enclosed range is supplied by the given
compact set. This constructs the actual tree, complete initial and
final boundaries, real centre support, admissibility, and regularity,
normal crossings and pairwise disjointness of all labelled strict parts.

The actual open inclusion is a holomorphic submersion of relative
dimension zero. The whole intrinsic singular-set and dimension-part
pullback equalities therefore identify its bad set and full labelled
input list with the intrinsic ones on $U$. Openness preserves empty
interior and analytic inverse image preserves analyticity. Parts with
label at least $n$ are genuinely empty. Consequently the preceding
whole-geometry lemma applies to these actual resolved intrinsic parts
and proves the entire strict carrier, its finite union, dimensions and
normal crossings. Actual admissibility supplies the whole exceptional
and new-boundary formulas and the isomorphism off the same bad set.
The genuine singular-preimage theorem applied to the whole actual
smooth strict carrier gives the singular exceptional assertion.
The actual finite blowup projection is holomorphic, proper and
surjective. All conclusions concern the same internally constructed
$T$ and its whole boundary.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A genuine original local mixed resolution around every compact set]

\begin{proof}

\end{proof}
