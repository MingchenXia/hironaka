\section{The original global terminal projection principalizes the true ideal}

The local principalization argument of \cite{wlodarczyk05-hironaka},
Section 2.3, applies to every finite terminal chart.  The gluing route of
\cite{bierstone-milman08-functoriality} then yields an invertible ideal on
the already constructed global terminal manifold.  This argument establishes
principalization by the genuine proper holomorphic projection; it does not
construct a globally scheduled sequence of blowups.

\begin{lemma}[Invertibility descends from an open covering]
\label{hir:invertibility-descends-actual-open-cover}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalGlobalPrincipalization.isInvertible_of_open_cover}
\leanok
\uses{hir:open-restriction-ideal}
Let $\mathfrak a$ be a coherent ideal on a complex manifold $Y$ and
$(V_i)$ an open covering.  If the actual restricted ideal
$\mathfrak a\mathcal O_{V_i}$ is invertible on every $V_i$, then
$\mathfrak a$ is invertible on $Y$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix $y\in Y$ and choose $i$ with $y\in V_i$.  Invertibility on $V_i$
gives a neighborhood $W$ of $y$ in $V_i$ and a holomorphic generator $h$
which vanishes identically on no nonempty open subset of $W$.  Extend $h$
by zero outside $V_i$.  Its restriction to $W$, viewed as an open subset
of $Y$, is holomorphic.  An open nonempty subset of this neighborhood
pulls back to an open nonempty subset of $W$, so the same generator is
nonzero somewhere on it.  At every point of $W$, restriction and extension
give inverse operations on holomorphic germs.  Membership in the coherent
ideal depends only on the germ; finite holomorphic linear combinations
also restrict and extend.  Consequently the actual ambient ideal is
generated by the extended function on $W$.  These neighborhoods for all
$y$ prove invertibility.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original terminal range carries the true finite principalization ideal]
\label{hir:original-terminal-range-true-principalized-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalGlobalPrincipalization.originalTerminalRange_pullback_ideal,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalGlobalPrincipalization.originalTerminalRange_pullback_isInvertible}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-terminal-local-actual-saturated-range-biholomorphism,hir:original-terminal-local-actual-range-whole-projection-dictionary,hir:geo-transform-ambient}
Let $M$ be an original marked ideal in the invariant domain with whole
ambient submanifold and mark one.  Write $P:Y\to X$ for its constructed
global terminal projection and $e_i:Y_i\simeq V_i$ for the actual local
terminal biholomorphism onto its saturated range.  The restriction of
the true ideal $P^*M.\mathfrak a$ to $V_i$ is exactly
$(e_i^{-1})^*\mathcal O(-E_{T_i})$, where $T_i$ is the internally
constructed finite terminal tower.  In particular this restriction is
invertible; no local principalization certificate is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original terminal tower resolves the restricted marked ideal, whose
ambient submanifold is the whole original open and whose mark is one.
The mark-one principalization identity gives
$T_i^*(M.\mathfrak a|_{U_i})=\mathcal O(-E_{T_i})$.  The genuine
projection dictionary gives the equality of entire maps
$P|_{V_i}=\iota_{U_i}\circ T_i\circ e_i^{-1}$.
Functoriality of coherent-ideal pullback therefore identifies the true
restricted global ideal with $(e_i^{-1})^*\mathcal O(-E_{T_i})$.
The genuine inverse biholomorphism has no exceptional points, so pullback
preserves invertibility of this effective-divisor ideal.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same original global projection principalizes the true ideal]
\label{hir:original-global-terminal-true-ideal-invertible}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalGlobalPrincipalization.originalTerminalGluedProjection_pullback_isInvertible,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalGlobalPrincipalization.originalTerminalPrincipalizationDivisor}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:invertibility-descends-actual-open-cover,hir:original-terminal-range-true-principalized-ideal,hir:original-input-global-terminal-genuine-complex-manifold,hir:original-input-global-terminal-whole-projection-holomorphic}
For an original marked ideal $M$ in the invariant domain, with whole
ambient submanifold and mark one, the actual ideal $P^*M.\mathfrak a$
is invertible on the already constructed original global terminal
manifold.  Thus it is the ideal of a genuine effective divisor $D$ on
that same manifold, with $P^*M.\mathfrak a=\mathcal O(-D)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true holomorphic map $P$ pulls the original coherent ideal back to a
coherent ideal.  Actual joint surjectivity of the original local
inclusions says that their true open ranges cover every point of the
global terminal manifold.  The preceding theorem proves invertibility
of the actual restricted ideal on every one of those ranges.  Open-cover
descent proves the invertibility of $P^*M.\mathfrak a$ itself.  Take this
actual ideal and this proved invertibility as the effective divisor $D$;
its ideal equality is literal.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual global principalization divisor has the true inverse-image support]
\label{hir:original-global-principalization-true-support}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalGlobalPrincipalization.originalTerminalPrincipalizationDivisor_ideal,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalGlobalPrincipalization.originalTerminalPrincipalizationDivisor_support}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-global-terminal-true-ideal-invertible}
The genuine effective divisor just constructed satisfies
$\operatorname{supp}D=P^{-1}Z(M.\mathfrak a)$ on the same original
global terminal manifold.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By definition the support is the zero locus of the actual divisor ideal,
which is the true pullback $P^*M.\mathfrak a$.  For a coherent ideal and
a holomorphic map, a point belongs to the zero locus of its pullback
exactly when every original germ vanishes at its image: one direction
evaluates each pulled-back germ, and the other evaluates each finite
holomorphic combination of such germs.  Apply this equality to the
proved holomorphic original projection $P$.
\end{proof}
