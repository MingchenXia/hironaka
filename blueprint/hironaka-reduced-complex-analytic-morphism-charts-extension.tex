\section{Actual local holomorphic coordinates for abstract analytic morphisms}

Objects are the genuine Hausdorff second-countable locally ringed spaces
over the original complex point which admit actual analytic-subspace charts.
Their morphisms are the original morphisms in that slice, without supplied
ambient holomorphic extensions.

\begin{theorem}[A genuine chart receives an intrinsic model map after actual ambient restriction]
\label{hir:reduced-analytic-morphism-local-chart-factor}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-open-restriction-iso,hir:reduced-analytic-complex-point-models}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticMorphismCharts.exists_actual_model_chart_factor}
\leanok
Let $Z$ be an actual locally ringed space over the complex point and
$F:C\to Z$ a genuine complex-point-compatible morphism from an actual
subspace model $C\subset M$. Let $j:D\to Z$ be a genuine open-immersion
chart over the complex point, with $D\subset V\subset\mathbf C^m$.
If $F(x)$ belongs to the actual image of $j$, there are an actual ambient
open $T\subset M$ containing $x$ and an internally constructed genuine
complex-point-compatible model morphism $H:(C\cap T)\to D$ whose whole
composite with $j$ equals the actual restriction of $F$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual image of the open immersion $j$ is open. Its inverse image
under the continuous base of $F$ is therefore a genuine open of $C$
containing $x$. The induced subspace topology writes that open as $C\cap T$
for an actual ambient open $T$. The composite of the genuine actual
open-subspace map with $F$ consequently has its entire topological image
in the image of $j$. The native universal property of an actual locally
ringed open immersion constructs the lift $H$ and proves the whole
factorization, including structure sheaves. Compose that equality with
the structural map of $Z$ to the complex point. The genuine structural
triangles of $j$, $F$ and the actual open-subspace inclusion prove the
required complex-point triangle for $H$. Neither $H$ nor a sheaf
factorization is supplied as input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every abstract intrinsic analytic morphism has genuine local holomorphic chart form]
\label{hir:reduced-analytic-morphism-actual-holomorphic-charts}
\uses{hir:reduced-analytic-morphism-local-chart-factor,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-intrinsic-local-holomorphic-lift,hir:reduced-analytic-complex-point-models}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticMorphismCharts.exists_actual_holomorphic_morphism_charts}
\leanok
For any morphism $F:A\to B$ in the constructed category of reduced complex
analytic spaces and any $x\in A$, internally choose actual analytic
models $C\subset U\subset\mathbf C^n$ and $D\subset V\subset\mathbf C^m$
with genuine complex-point-compatible open charts $i:C\to A$ and
$j:D\to B$, and a point $c\in C$ with $i(c)=x$. There are actual source
opens $T\subset U$ containing $c$ and $W\subset T$ containing the same
point, and a genuine holomorphic $f:W\to V$ carrying the corresponding
actual restricted source subset into $D$, such that
\[
  j\circ f_* = F\circ i\circ r_T\circ r_W
\]
as whole locally ringed morphisms. Here $r_T,r_W$ are the independently
constructed actual quotient-subspace open inclusions and $f_*$ is the
actual morphism induced by the original holomorphic map. The source
composite $i\circ r_T\circ r_W$ is a genuine open immersion whose image
contains $x$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual local-model property of $A$ chooses $i$ and $c$ over $x$;
that of $B$ chooses $j$ over $F(x)$. The original slice-morphism equation
shows that the composite $F\circ i$ is complex-point compatible. The
preceding theorem constructs the actual ambient open $T$ and the whole
intrinsic factor $H$ into $D$. Apply genuine intrinsic local holomorphic
lifting to $H$ at the actual point of $C\cap T$. It constructs an actual
open $W\subset T$, an original holomorphic $f:W\to V$, the actual subset
mapping property, and the entire intrinsic square
$f_*=H\circ r_W$. Compose this square with $j$ and use the proved whole
factorization $jH=F i r_T$. Associativity gives exactly the asserted
whole locally ringed square. Both actual restriction maps and $i$ are
open immersions, so their composite is one. The literal nested-subtype
point formed from $c\in T$ and $c\in W$ maps to $x$, proving actual
coverage. All charts, restricted opens and holomorphic representatives
are constructed from the original object/morphism inputs; the two opens
are honest successive open restrictions of the original coordinate model.
\end{proof}

This gives intrinsic morphisms of the actual abstract analytic category
their genuine local holomorphic realization. It does not assert that
resolution towers or their coherent ideals descend across arbitrary
analytic charts, nor that such a tower terminates.
