\section{Original local pure boundary resolution with one target chosen before every smooth source}
\label{sec:hironaka-original-local-pure-boundary-canonical-prefix}

The original carrier is globally analytic and pure, but need not be
compact. An open set $U\subset X$ is prescribed together with a compact
set containing $U$. All local marked data use the whole intrinsic
vanishing ideal of $W\cap U$, not a supplied ideal-identification or
local-resolution certificate. The original boundary is ordered SNC
and $W$ is dense away from it.

\begin{theorem}[Whole original pure boundary data on the prescribed open]
\label{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-whole-open-record}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalPureBoundaryCanonicalPrefix.original_open_record,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalPureBoundaryCanonicalPrefix.original_open_data}
The actual pullback to $U$ of the original marked datum
$(X,E_0,\mathcal I_W,1)$ is the intrinsic datum
$(U,E_0|_U,\mathcal I_{W\cap U},1)$. Its carrier is analytic,
its dimension-$d$ regular locus is dense, its literal restricted
boundary is SNC, and it is dense away from that same boundary.
\uses{hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-regular-locus,hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-pure-boundary-density,hir:actual-singular-vanishing-ideal-pullback-submersion}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual open inclusion is a holomorphic submersion, including for
an empty open set. The whole vanishing-ideal submersion pullback
identity identifies the ideal field exactly, while inverse images
identify the whole carrier, boundary list and mark fields.
Analyticity pulls back under this actual holomorphic inclusion.
The exact regular-locus submersion formula has zero relative dimension,
so its regular dimension is the unchanged $d$. Openness of the
inclusion commutes with closure and therefore pulls the original
pure regular density to the whole local carrier. Actual SNC
submersion pullback gives the literal restricted SNC list, and the
same openness and closure formula pulls back the original density
off the whole boundary. No compactness of the local carrier is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original local input internally produces its full canonical terminal history and literal embedded prefix]
\label{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-canonical-prefix-producer}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalPureBoundaryCanonicalPrefix.exists_local_original_pure_boundary_canonical_prefix}
Suppose $W$ is an original analytic carrier of pure dimension $d<n$,
$E_0$ is original SNC, and $W$ is dense away from $E_0$. For the
prescribed open $U$ contained in the given compact set, these data
internally produce an actual terminal canonical history $P$ of the
intrinsic local original datum, and a literal factorization
$P=T\mathbin{+}R$ before its first whole maximum meeting its intrinsic
boundary-free regular open. The same $T$ is canonical and admissible
over its actual original local singular-plus-boundary bad set.
Its whole strict carrier is closed smooth of dimension $d$, with
normal crossings with its literal accumulated SNC boundary. The
actual exceptional locus lies in the whole new boundary, this
boundary lies over the bad set, and the inverse image of the original
local singular set is exceptional. Its actual projection is
holomorphic, proper, surjective and an isomorphism off the bad set.
Empty local carriers use the actual identity prefix with the same
whole terminal history as tail.
\uses{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-whole-open-record,hir:resolution-relatively-compact,hir:original-pure-boundary-given-terminal-first-prefix}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The globally analytic pure carrier and original SNC list give the
actual original invariant-domain record. Apply the genuine local
canonical resolution driver with the prescribed open and its compact
container. It internally chooses a finite actual canonical terminal
history on this open; no local history is an input. The preceding
whole-record identity converts its actual pulled marked datum into
the independently defined intrinsic local original datum.

The preceding open-data theorem supplies every actual pure and
boundary-density hypothesis on this same open. Apply the genuine
terminal-prefix theorem to this very internally chosen terminal
history. It cuts a literal first-meeting prefix and its actual tail,
retaining canonicality, avoidance of the actual good open and its
next attained whole maximum when the local carrier is nonempty.
All the listed whole-carrier, exact boundary, permitted-centre,
exceptional, singular-preimage and off-bad-set geometry then concerns
this same prefix and projection. The empty case is the true identity
prefix with the entire same terminal history as tail, so no history
position or initial boundary member is silently removed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every smooth source compares independently stopped intrinsic local histories with that same target]
\label{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-same-target-independent-source}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalPureBoundaryCanonicalPrefix.exists_local_original_pure_boundary_stopped_smooth_source}
The target terminal history and embedded prefix above can be chosen
before every smooth source. For every genuine holomorphic submersion
$b:Y^m\to U$, internally construct an actual smooth pullback $Q$ of
that same target prefix, with its actual upper submersion and
holomorphic Cartesian projection square. Independently stopped
canonical histories of the intrinsic source carrier $b^{-1}(W\cap U)$,
its own pulled initial boundary and shifted dimension, avoiding their
own boundary-free regular open and stopping at its next whole
maximum meeting that open, compare uniquely with $Q$ over $Y$.
The comparison preserves the whole conventional strict carrier and
the entire ordered boundary after deleting exactly empty members;
it gives the source's own testness, SNC boundary, regularity,
shifted dimension and normal crossings. No source compactness,
incoming compatible source history or ideal/comparison certificate
is assumed.
\uses{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-canonical-prefix-producer,hir:original-pure-boundary-independent-intrinsic-comparison}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the actual target prefix once by the preceding original-only
producer, before quantifying over sources. For a given $b$, actual
smooth blowup pullback constructs a whole actual tower $Q$ and its
upper submersion. The whole original source ideal is the actual
pullback ideal by the genuine whole vanishing-ideal theorem; actual
regular and boundary-good loci are their exact submersion inverse
images.

Apply the proved independent intrinsic first-meeting comparison to
this actual pullback and the chosen target's genuine canonicality,
good-open avoidance and first attained maximum. For every source
history stopped by its own intrinsic first-meeting rule it gives a
unique biholomorphism over the original source base, the same
Cartesian upper square, and the whole strict-carrier identity.
Its exact boundary formula removes only genuinely empty members,
retaining all initial members and every actual historical position
before this explicit filtering. Actual target regularity and normal
crossings supply the source's own geometric assertions through this
comparison. Empty sources are included by the comparison's genuine
empty-history argument without imposing a false global dimension
bound. Every source is compared with the same target chosen before
it; this asserts neither a finite global noncompact resolution nor
a mixed-dimensional part recursion.
\end{proof}
