\section{Actual noncompact histories from compact connected components of the original support}

All components in this section are the actual connected components of the
original analytic support, including components containing intersecting
irreducible branches. The ambient manifold may be connected and noncompact.
There is no assumption of a bound on the invariants or terminal lengths
across components. Compactness of each support component is an explicit
geometric restriction; arbitrary connected noncompact support remains outside
this theorem.

\begin{lemma}[Connected components of a genuine analytic support are locally finite]
\label{hir:original-analytic-support-connected-components-locally-finite}
\uses{rem:global-components}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.supportComponents,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.component_isClosed,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.component_pairwise_disjoint,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.sUnion_supportComponents,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.locallyFinite_supportComponents}
\leanok
Let $S$ be a genuine analytic subset of a complex manifold. Its actual
nonempty connected components form a locally finite family of closed,
pairwise disjoint subsets whose union is $S$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each connected component is preconnected, contained in $S$, and contains
its defining point. Because $S$ is closed, the closure of such a component
is still a preconnected subset of $S$ through that point. Maximality of
the connected component places the closure in the component, proving
closedness. Two components meeting at a point are the component through
that same point and hence coincide. Their union is $S$ by the defining
point of every component.

Use the genuine locally finite global irreducible components of $S$.
Each is the closure of a connected component of the full regular locus,
so is preconnected and lies in one actual connected component of $S$.
Near any ambient point, choose an open set meeting only finitely many
global irreducible components. Every connected component meeting this
open has a point in it; the actual irreducible-component union supplies
an irreducible component through that point. This irreducible component
meets the same open and lies wholly in that connected component. Thus
the connected components meeting the open are contained in the image
of a finite set of irreducible components. They form a finite set,
which proves local finiteness without assuming local connectedness as
an extra input.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original components internally supply exact compact support windows]
\label{hir:original-compact-support-component-windows}
\uses{hir:original-analytic-support-connected-components-locally-finite}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.componentWindow,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.componentWindow_inter_support,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.componentWindow_cover,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.componentWindow_support_disjoint,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.isCompact_restricted_component_cosupp}
\leanok
For an original marked ideal with genuine analytic cosupport $S$, let $C$
be an actual connected component of $S$. The complement of the union of
all other whole connected components is an actual open $U_C$, and
$U_C\cap S=C$. These internally defined opens cover $S$ and are pairwise
disjoint on $S$. If $C$ is compact, the entire original restricted
cosupport on $U_C$ is compact.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A subfamily of a locally finite closed family has closed union. Its
complement therefore gives the stated actual open. Pairwise disjointness
puts every point of $C$ in that complement; conversely a point of $S$
is in some actual component and can belong to this complement only if
that component is $C$. This proves the whole trace equation, and hence
the cover and support-disjointness assertions. The exact coherent
cosupport restriction law identifies the restricted cosupport with the
inverse image of $C$. The actual subtype inclusion is an inducing map
and $C$ lies entirely in its range. Compactness therefore lifts to its
inverse image. No centre is truncated to make its support compact.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original component compactness internally constructs a real locally finite history]
\label{hir:original-compact-support-components-actual-history}
\uses{hir:original-compact-support-component-windows,hir:support-disjoint-canonical-global-refinement,hir:support-disjoint-canonical-growing-history,hir:compact-cosupport-maximum-sequence-terminates}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.LocallyTerminalComponentHistory,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.exists_original_component_compact_locally_terminal_history}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an original marked ideal in the actual canonical invariant
domain. Suppose every actual connected component of its original
cosupport is compact. These original data internally produce one
actual growing sequence of global elementary blowups. Every prefix is
an original test tower and its restriction to each $U_C$ is canonical
for the same original restricted marked datum; every restricted actual
next centre is the whole actual maximum locus, with empty positions
retained. On each $U_C$ this same constructed history is eventually a
resolution. The actual centre images on the original base are closed
and locally finite. Around every ambient point all sufficiently late
actual blowup maps are isomorphisms, and every finite restricted tail
is a genuine all-empty tower. No sequence, compatible atlas, schedule,
terminal time or global maximum is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Coherence and the original invariant domain make the whole original
cosupport analytic. Apply the preceding lemmas to its actual connected
components, thereby constructing the whole support-disjoint open cover
and the compact original restricted cosupports internally.

Iterate the genuine support-disjoint canonical refinement theorem on
the state consisting of the actual original global prefix, its test
property and all its actual restricted canonical prefixes. At every
step that theorem constructs a globally closed smooth admissible whole
union of local maximum centres and an actual proper blowup. The current
cosupport maps into the original one, so its whole centre trace on each
window is exactly that window's maximum; overlaps outside the support
contribute empty centres. This constructs the actual growing sequence
before any terminal time is known.

Fix one original component window. Its original restricted cosupport
is compact. The actual per-sequence compact centre-count bound applies
to the same restricted canonical history. If its cosupport never became
empty, compactness and upper semicontinuity would give an attained
nonempty whole maximum at every actual step, increasing that count at
every step and contradicting the bound. Once empty, the true test
transforms remain empty, so this same history is eventually a resolution.

The support-disjoint centre trace now proves eventual avoidance of each
window. Outside the original closed cosupport, every actual centre
already misses the original open complement. Thus every ambient point
has an open neighbourhood meeting only finitely many actual centre
images. Their images are closed under actual proper prefix maps.
The actual chosen successor maps are isomorphisms over the avoided
neighbourhood, and literal restriction of each actual finite tail has
only empty centres. The argument covers empty support and uses no
uniform bound across components.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Original coherent ideals with component-compact zero locus]
\label{hir:original-coherent-ideal-component-compact-scheduler}
\uses{hir:original-compact-support-components-actual-history}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCompactSupportComponentHistory.exists_original_ideal_component_compact_history}
\leanok
An original coherent holomorphic ideal, nonzero along the original
ambient manifold, together with an original ordered SNC boundary,
has the preceding actual global locally finite history whenever every
actual connected component of its zero locus is compact. The literal
original marked datum is $(X,X,E_0,I,1)$. No invariant-domain or history
certificate is an extra input.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The original coherence, nonzero condition and SNC boundary internally
give the genuine canonical invariant domain for this literal marked
datum. Its whole cosupport equals the original zero locus by the
coherent marking-one formula. Rewrite the actual connected-component
compactness assumption through that whole equality and apply the
preceding original-only history producer. All intermediate global
blowups and all local terminal/empty-tail conclusions belong to that
same internally constructed history.
\end{proof}
