\section{Whole maxima on every homogeneous coordinate-model canonical prefix}
\label{sec:hironaka-homogeneous-canonical-tower-maxima}

Fix an actual coordinate union $H_S$ in the lifted complex coordinate
model and its unchanged original datum $M_S=(X,X,\varnothing,I_{H_S},1)$.
Let $R$ be a proved resolution invariant whose dimension bound is at
least the ambient dimension. Every canonical prefix below is an actual
finite tower. Empty historical positions are retained; no terminal or
no-empty-prefix uniqueness theorem is used.

\begin{theorem}[Actual automorphism lifts along an exact canonical prefix]
\label{hir:homogeneous-exact-canonical-prefix-automorphism-lift}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalTowerMaxima.exists_canonical_tower_automorphism}
For any original invariant-domain datum and any actual base
biholomorphism preserving its whole marked record, every exact finite
canonical prefix internally supplies a genuine tower automorphism over
that base map. Its literal empty positions and full-maximum positions
are retained.
\leanok
\uses{hir:general-nc-one-step-history-universal-comparison}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual given prefix. At the reflexive prefix the supplied
base biholomorphism is the required lift. Suppose an actual prefix lift
has been constructed. The whole marked-data preservation and whole
history-transform law identify the current cosupport with its actual
preimage under the lifted biholomorphism. The invariant's genuine
tower-isomorphism law identifies all whole values on that cosupport.
Consequently the entire full maximum pulls back to itself, using actual
surjectivity of the lift. The next actual centre is either the literal
empty set, which also pulls back to itself, or that entire maximum.
Thus the exact next centre is preserved in both cases.

The actual one-step universal comparison constructs a biholomorphism
between the two genuine blowups of this preserved centre. Its exact
projection equation extends the previously constructed history
isomorphism by the actual step constructor. This yields the desired
whole-prefix automorphism with the same actual empty positions. No
comparison with a terminal prefix or another independently chosen
sequence is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual homogeneous original records and their whole prefix values]
\label{hir:homogeneous-model-entire-record-and-prefix-value}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalTowerMaxima.homothety_pullback_original,Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalTowerMaxima.canonical_automorphism_value}
Every nonzero scalar homothety preserves the whole original coordinate
record. Its actual lift along an exact canonical prefix preserves the
literal transformed cosupport and every entry of the whole prefix value.
\leanok
\uses{hir:homogeneous-exact-canonical-prefix-automorphism-lift,hir:general-nc-diagonal-monomial-chart-equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Nonzero scalar multiplication preserves each selected coordinate zero
predicate, hence the whole original carrier. The genuine homothety and
its reciprocal inverse transport the entire holomorphic vanishing-ideal
predicate; the full ambient carrier, empty boundary and marking one
agree literally. Thus its pulled-back original marked record is exactly
the original one. Apply the preceding actual prefix-lift producer.
Whole history-transform naturality then preserves the current literal
cosupport. The proved invariant tower-isomorphism law, on this actual
cosupport, gives equality of the entire prefix values. No equality is
inferred merely from current coordinates or boundary incidences.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An actual compact representative set for every whole prefix value]
\label{hir:homogeneous-model-proper-compact-value-representatives}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalTowerMaxima.exists_compact_value_representatives}
For every actual finite canonical prefix of $M_S$, there is an actual
compact subset of its current cosupport on which every whole current
value is represented.
\leanok
\uses{hir:homogeneous-model-entire-record-and-prefix-value,bcalc:tower-preimage-compact}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the lifted closed unit ball in the original coordinate base and
the intersection of its full tower preimage with the actual current
cosupport. The base ball is compact. Actual finite blowup towers have
compact preimages of compact sets, and actual test-transform cosupports
are closed. Thus this precise intersection is compact and is contained
in the literal current cosupport.

Given a current cosupport point, let $v$ be its actual original coordinate
image. Put $t=1/(\lVert v\rVert+1)>0$ and regard $t$ as a nonzero complex
scalar. Its genuine prefix lift takes the point to another actual
cosupport point with exactly the same whole value. Exact commutation of
the history projections identifies the new base coordinate with $tv$.
Its norm is $\lVert v\rVert/(\lVert v\rVert+1)\leq1$, so this actual
image point belongs to the same fixed compact intersection. The compact
set is constructed from the prefix, not supplied as a certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine whole maximum at every nonempty homogeneous canonical prefix]
\label{hir:homogeneous-model-every-prefix-whole-maximum}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalTowerMaxima.homogeneous_maxLocus_nonempty}
Every actual finite canonical prefix of $M_S$ with nonempty transformed
cosupport attains its entire whole invariant maximum on that cosupport.
\leanok
\uses{hir:homogeneous-model-proper-compact-value-representatives,hir:invariant-order}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The compact representative set is nonempty because each point of the
nonempty current cosupport has an actual representative there. Restrict
the invariant's proved upper semicontinuity to that compact subset.
Its genuine compact maximum theorem supplies an actual representative
point dominating every value on the subset. Every whole value on the
entire current cosupport equals one of those representative values.
Hence this same actual point dominates the entire current cosupport
and belongs to its actual whole maximum locus. No compactness of the
ambient coordinate manifold is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual whole-maximum successor at every nonterminal homogeneous prefix]
\label{hir:homogeneous-model-every-prefix-actual-canonical-successor}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalTowerMaxima.exists_homogeneous_canonical_successor}
At every such nonterminal prefix, the internally nonempty entire whole
maximum supplies a genuine smooth-centre blowup extending the literal
prefix as an actual canonical test tower.
\leanok
\uses{hir:homogeneous-model-every-prefix-whole-maximum}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding theorem gives nonemptiness of the literal whole maximum.
The proved invariant maximum-admissibility law makes it a closed smooth
admissible centre for the same current marked datum. The actual domain
and test-tower cosupport theorem gives empty interior for this maximum.
Actual smooth-centre blowup existence constructs a genuine blowup of
that very entire centre. Appending it to the literal prefix gives a
test step by admissibility and a canonical step by its exact full-maximum
identity. This is an every-prefix successor producer; an actual strict
progress and finite termination driver is still required before a
terminal model resolution can be asserted.
\end{proof}
