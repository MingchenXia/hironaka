\section{Curve FULL comparison on genuinely local reference domains}

Throughout, the ambient dimension is positive. The original raw reference
need not be in DOMAIN$(1)$ on its whole ambient space.

\begin{theorem}[Actual nested restriction transports local curve geometry and every FULL value]
\label{hir:original-curve-local-domain-nested-full-comparison}
\uses{hir:original-curve-any-test-full-tower-isomorphism,hir:original-curve-any-test-full-open-restriction,bcalc:inv-domain-stable,bcalc:restrict-open}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCurveLocalReferenceComparison.local_curve_full_nested_open}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an original marked ideal with coherent ideal, let $V\subseteq W$
be actual original opens, and let $T$ be an actual ambient blowup history.
Suppose ONLY that the true restriction $M|_W$ lies in DOMAIN$(1)$ and the
actual restricted history $T|_W$ is its TEST. Then $M|_V$ lies in
DOMAIN$(1)$, $T|_V$ is its genuine TEST, and its ENTIRE current cosupport
is precisely the preimage of the preceding current cosupport under the
actual same-point inclusion. At every point in that cosupport and EVERY
positive depth, the two unchanged original FULL values are equal.
No global DOMAIN or TEST of the raw reference $M$ is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Inside the genuine open manifold $W$, take the actual nested open
corresponding to $V$. Ordinary open DOMAIN preservation and actual TEST
restriction give the true double-restricted reference and history there.
The genuine nested-open biholomorphism and stagewise double-restriction
isomorphism identify this history with $T|_V$. Coherence of the ORIGINAL
ideal gives the entire initial marked-record pullback identity under this
actual biholomorphism, not merely an equality of carrier sets. Genuine
biholomorphic DOMAIN and TEST transport therefore prove the asserted
DOMAIN and TEST for $M|_V$.

The actual stagewise isomorphism transports the entire transformed
cosupport. The true open-restriction cosupport theorem then identifies
the double-restricted cosupport with the original $W$ current cosupport.
Both actual endpoint maps retain exactly the same underlying original
point. Composing these TWO whole identities proves BOTH cosupport
directions in the statement.

Apply the proved noncanonical curve FULL tower-isomorphism identity to
the genuine double-restricted DOMAIN$(1)$ reference and its actual TEST.
The real whole initial-record equality identifies its source value with
that of $M|_V$ on $T|_V$. Apply the genuine curve FULL open-restriction
identity inside $W$ to compare the double-restricted value with that of
$M|_W$ on $T|_W$. Their actual endpoint inclusion is precisely the same
underlying original point. Composing these true numerical identities
proves every positive-depth FULL equality. No canonicality or comparison
certificate is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Two independently local curve references compare without a global curve hypothesis]
\label{hir:original-curve-two-local-domains-full-comparison}
\uses{hir:original-curve-local-domain-nested-full-comparison}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCurveLocalReferenceComparison.local_curve_full_two_opens}
\leanok
Let $M$ have coherent original ideal and let $U,V$ be ANY two actual
original opens. Assume the ordinary DOMAIN$(1)$ and genuine TEST
conditions separately for $M|_U$ on $T|_U$ and $M|_V$ on $T|_V$.
At every original endpoint projecting into $U\cap V$, the two ENTIRE
current cosupport conditions are equivalent. Whenever they hold, the
unchanged original FULL values on those two genuine histories agree at
EVERY positive depth. The raw reference need not be globally nonzero
along its carrier, and neither canonicality nor inclusion between the two
opens is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual intersection open $U\cap V$, with its TWO genuine
inclusions. The preceding theorem applies first to the ordinary DOMAIN
and TEST on $U$, and then independently to those on $V$. Both conclusions
refer to the SAME whole original restricted record $M|_{U\cap V}$ and
SAME actual history $T|_{U\cap V}$. At the actual subtype point with the
same underlying original endpoint, their entire cosupport equivalences
therefore identify the two original local cosupport conditions in both
directions. If these hold, the two actual numerical identities compare
each local value to that SAME intersection value at EVERY positive depth.
Symmetry of the first equality followed by the second gives the claimed
comparison. No global original DOMAIN, local-data certificate, guessed
common history or target numerical equality is inserted.
\end{proof}

These actual local-domain tools apply to kept-contact and member references
whose nonzero-along-carrier condition is proved only on their genuine
masks. They do not assert that such a raw reference is globally in the
invariant domain or that a higher-dimensional driver has terminated.
