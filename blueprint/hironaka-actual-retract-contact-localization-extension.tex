\begin{theorem}[A split holomorphic section retains the entire original centre ideal]
\label{hir:actual-retract-contact-centre-whole-ideal}
\uses{hir:actual-graph-section-whole-ideal-pullback}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualRetractContactLocalization.section_centre_pullback,Hironaka.HironakaActualRetractContactLocalization.range_section_closed,Hironaka.HironakaActualRetractContactLocalization.image_centre_eq}
\leanok
Let $X$ and $A$ be complex manifolds of dimensions $d$ and $d+1$,
and let $s:X\to A$ and $p:A\to X$ be actual holomorphic maps with
$ps=1_X$. For every original subset $D\subset X$,
\[
 s^*\mathcal I_{s(D)}=\mathcal I_D
\]
as entire independently defined original functional ideals. The actual
range of $s$ is closed, and $s(D)=\operatorname{range}(s)\cap p^{-1}D$.
No coherence, holomorphic extension or ideal-equality certificate is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A genuine ambient vanishing germ at $s(x)$ composed with $s$ is holomorphic
and vanishes on $D$ near $x$, by actual continuity. The true finite-span
relation preserves this entire vanishing condition. Conversely any original
$a\in\mathcal I_{D,x}$ gives the literal ambient function $a\circ p$,
holomorphic near $s(x)$ and vanishing on $s(D)$ there because $ps=1$.
Its exact pullback is $a$, so the one-term true span proves the reverse
whole membership. The actual range is precisely the fixed locus of the
continuous map $sp$, hence is closed in Hausdorff $A$. For a range point
$s(x)$, membership of its projection in $D$ is exactly $x\in D$; this
proves the stated whole subset identity and in particular preserves
closedness of original closed centres inside the actual manifolds.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual split contact geometry produces the whole intrinsic restricted blowup]
\label{hir:actual-retract-contact-restricted-blowup}

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualRetractContactLocalization.restrictedProjection_isBlowupAlong}
\leanok

$N=\operatorname{range}s$ is originally regular of dimension $d$, and
$D$ is an original smooth closed nowhere dense centre of $X$. For any

complex manifold structure and the literal map $p\pi|_S:S\to X$ is an
actual blowup along $D$. The ordinary range regularity is geometric input;
no restricted blowup or comparison map is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Whole centre pullback from the first theorem and true cross-dimensional
composition make the composed map from an internally chosen lower blowup
$\rho:Z\to X$ have invertible entire ambient centre ideal. Actual ambient
universality constructs its unique holomorphic lift $\kappa$.
The identity $p\pi\kappa=\rho$ and genuine properness of the lower blowup
make $\kappa$ proper. Actual exceptional-complement density and ambient

$S$: continuity gives containment in the strict closure, while lower
surjectivity and ambient complement injectivity give the opposite dense
subset containment in its closed image. Actual range regularity and the
strict-submanifold theorem construct the genuine intrinsic flattening

Since $\pi|_S=sp\pi|_S$, the first theorem and whole composition identify
this ideal with $(p\pi|_S)^*\mathcal I_D$. Lower universality constructs
a holomorphic reverse comparison. Lower universal uniqueness proves one
inverse identity; ambient universal uniqueness for the entire invertible

biholomorphisms. Composing every lower universal lift with these actual

stratum is replaced by an incorrect full preimage.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original scalar graphs on true coordinate opens supply the actual contact geometry]
\label{hir:actual-open-graph-contact-restricted-blowup}

\leanok

scalar graph function holomorphic on this $U$, and an original smooth

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

identities and actual blowup comparisons are thereby internally supplied.
The true domain $U$ is retained throughout; no globally closed centre or
holomorphy outside $U$ is invented.
\end{proof}
