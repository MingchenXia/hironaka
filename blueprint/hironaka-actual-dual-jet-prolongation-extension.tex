\section{Genuine differential prolongation of original ideal annihilators}

Let $G_i$ be a finite family of actual holomorphic functions on a real
open $U\subset\mathbb C^{d+t}$, let $T$ be the actual linear tangent
inclusion, and let $V\subset\mathbb C^d$ be real open with
$T(V)\subset U$. Write $J_q$ for the unchanged original finite jet
map, whose derivative orders are strictly less than $q$. A row index
$r$ is an actual coordinate word of length $|r|<q$, with its original
coordinate-direction tuple $v_r$. Suppose the actual scalar functions
$\eta_r(c)$ are holomorphic on $V$ and annihilate every column of the
unchanged original $M_q(Tc)$ there.

\begin{definition}[The actual differential prolongation functional]
\label{hir:actual-dual-jet-differential-prolongation}
\uses{hir:actual-moving-macaulay-annihilator-successor-connection}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualDualJetProlongation.actualDualJetProlongation}
\leanok
For $c\in V$ and an actual direction $a\in\mathbb C^d$, define a
complex linear functional on the original order-$(q+1)$ jet space by
\[
 (\nabla_a\eta)_c(H)=\sum_{|r|<q}
 \bigl(D\eta_r(c)(a)H_{|r|}(v_r)+
       \eta_r(c)H_{|r|+1}(Ta,v_r)\bigr).
\]
Both orders in each summand are genuine components of the unchanged
multilinear-map product that is the codomain of the original jet map.
Restriction to its actual finite-jet image is used for ideal
annihilation. No surjectivity onto this product, independence of word
evaluations or higher-order annihilator is assumed.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The inequalities $|r|<q<q+1$ and $|r|+1<q+1$ place both components
in the stated original product of multilinear-map spaces. Evaluation
of either fixed component on its fixed tuple is complex linear in
$H$. Multiplying these evaluations by the actual scalar coefficients
$D\eta_r(c)(a)$ and $\eta_r(c)$ and summing over the finite original
row set gives a complex linear functional. In particular, when
$q=0$ the sum is empty and the functional is zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The constructed prolongation kills every actual next-order Macaulay column]
\label{hir:actual-dual-jet-prolongation-all-next-columns-zero}
\uses{hir:actual-dual-jet-differential-prolongation,hir:actual-moving-macaulay-annihilator-successor-connection,hir:actual-new-maximal-word-generator-next-jet-zero}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualDualJetProlongation.actualDualJetProlongation_macaulay_column_zero}
\leanok
Fix $c\in V$ and suppose every actual $G_i(Tc)$ is zero. Then for
every actual word $w$ of length less than $q+1$ and every $i$,
\[
 (\nabla_a\eta)_c
 \bigl(J_{q+1}(w\,G_i(\,\cdot+Tc))\bigr)=0.
\]
The word germ and translated generator are the unchanged original
ones. All old and new maximal-length columns are included.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $|w|<q$, regard the same word as an original column of $M_q(Tc)$.
Applying the defining functional to the true finite jet of its actual
word-times-generator germ gives exactly the derivative-row term plus
the successor-jet term of the genuine moving-annihilator identity.
That identity proves the sum zero. Reindexing the same word changes
only the proof of its degree bound, not its actual coordinate product
or translated germ.

Otherwise $|w|<q+1$ implies $|w|=q$. The actual word germ belongs to
the original maximal-ideal power $\mathfrak m^q$, and the genuine
translated generator belongs to $\mathfrak m$ because $G_i(Tc)=0$.
Their product belongs to $\mathfrak m^{q+1}$ and therefore has zero
original order-$(q+1)$ jet by the exact jet-kernel dictionary. The
constructed complex linear functional sends this zero jet to zero.
This argument includes the empty maximal word when $q=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The internally constructed higher functional annihilates the entire original ideal]
\label{hir:actual-dual-jet-prolongation-whole-original-ideal-zero}
\uses{hir:actual-dual-jet-prolongation-all-next-columns-zero,hir:actual-every-original-ideal-finite-jet-columns}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualDualJetProlongation.actualDualJetProlongation_whole_ideal_zero}
\leanok
Under the same hypotheses, the actual constructed functional
$(\nabla_a\eta)_c$ annihilates the order-$(q+1)$ jet of every germ
in the entire original ideal generated by the same translated
$G_i(\,\cdot+Tc)$. No ideal-image, spanning or higher-annihilator
certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original whole-ideal Macaulay-range theorem at order $q+1$
expresses the finite jet of every such germ as an actual finite scalar
linear combination of the original word-times-generator columns at
that order. Every one of those columns is killed by the preceding
theorem, including the new maximal-length columns. Complex linearity
of the constructed functional carries the finite linear combination
to the same combination of zeros, hence to zero. This is a genuine
prolongation identity for the original point-jet ideal annihilators;
identifying its filtration with the normal-adic graded object is a
separate assertion.
\end{proof}
