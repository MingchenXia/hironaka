\section{Actual weighted generator transforms and every exceptional jet}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-weighted-generator-transform-jets}
Let $B=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$, $R=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^{d+t},0}$ and $N=(z_1,\ldots,z_t)$.
Fix the genuine $j$-th normal blowup chart, with exceptional coordinate $e=z_j$ and its actual translated parameters $b$.
Write $P_k(g)$ for the genuine finite normal homogeneous polynomial of degree $k$ of $g$, with its entire original $B$-germ coefficients, and $D_{j,b}$ for its literal dehomogenized evaluation ($z_j=1$, $z_i=b_i+z_i$ for $i\ne j$).

\begin{theorem}[The whole finite normal band is the actual Taylor truncation]
\label{hir:actual-weighted-transform-normal-band}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-homogeneous-polynomial,hir:actual-normal-power-canonical-homogeneous-polynomial}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedGeneratorTransformJets.normalBandPolynomial,Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedGeneratorTransformJets.actualNormalTaylorPolynomial_succ,Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedGeneratorTransformJets.normalBandPolynomial_eq_Taylor,Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedGeneratorTransformJets.normalBand_remainder}
\leanok
For $g\in N^m$ and every $q\ge0$,
$\sum_{r<q}P_{m+r}(g)$ is exactly the genuine normal Taylor polynomial of $g$ below degree $m+q$.
Consequently $g-\sum_{r<q}P_{m+r}(g)(z)\in N^{m+q}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The coefficient at exponent $\gamma$ of the degree-$k$ polynomial is the stable actual analytic normal coefficient of $g$ when $|\gamma|=k$, and zero otherwise.
Thus the $(k+1)$-truncation is the $k$-truncation plus $P_k(g)$, coefficient by coefficient: degrees below $k$ agree by actual coefficient stability, degree $k$ is supplied precisely by $P_k$, and higher degrees vanish on both sides.
The $m$-truncation is zero because $g\in N^m$ forces all its entire low normal coefficient germs to vanish; higher coefficients of the truncation already vanish.
Induction on $q$, using the preceding successor equality, proves the displayed band identity.
Its evaluation remainder is the proved actual normal Taylor remainder in the whole ideal $N^{m+q}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual blowup keeps every weighted normal-band term]
\label{hir:actual-weighted-transform-band-factorization}
\uses{hir:actual-weighted-transform-normal-band,hir:actual-normal-polynomial-blowup-factorization,hir:actual-normal-dehomogenized-polynomial-family-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedGeneratorTransformJets.weightedTransformJet,Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedGeneratorTransformJets.blowup_normalBand_factorization}
\leanok
Define $J_{m,q}(g)=\sum_{r<q}e^rD_{j,b}(P_{m+r}(g))$ using the actual entire analytic germs.
The actual blowup pullback of the evaluated band is exactly $e^mJ_{m,q}(g)$.
These are the literal dehomogenized coefficient germs over the entire original tangent ring.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each $P_{m+r}(g)$ is genuinely homogeneous of degree $m+r$ over $B$.
The actual analytic blowup fixes every tangent coefficient and takes every normal variable to $e$ times its displayed dehomogenized coordinate.
The proved whole homogeneous factorization is therefore $e^{m+r}D_{j,b}(P_{m+r}(g))$ for that term.
Add the finitely many identities and factor $e^m$ using $e^{m+r}=e^me^r$.
Dehomogenized evaluation contains no exceptional coordinate: the pivot has been replaced by $1$, every other normal coordinate is fixed by its contraction, and all tangent germs are fixed.
The genuine contraction theorem hence fixes each entire coefficient germ, not only its finite jets.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual weighted quotient has all its literal exceptional jets]
\label{hir:actual-weighted-transform-all-exceptional-jets}
\uses{hir:actual-weighted-transform-band-factorization}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedGeneratorTransformJets.actual_weighted_quotient_jet_remainder,Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedGeneratorTransformJets.actual_weighted_quotient_jet_class}
\leanok
If $g\in N^m$ and $e^mH=\operatorname{blowup}^*g$ is the genuine analytic quotient equation, then for every $q$,
\[
 H-J_{m,q}(g)\in(e^q),\qquad [H]_{(e^q)}=[J_{m,q}(g)]_{(e^q)}.
\]
Thus every actual exceptional jet is expressed by the literal normal homogeneous coefficients of the same original generator.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The band remainder is in $N^{m+q}$.
The actual blowup maps this whole ideal to $(e^{m+q})$, so its pullback is $Ke^{m+q}$ for an actual analytic germ $K$.
The quotient equation and band factorization identify this pullback with $e^m(H-J_{m,q}(g))$.
Since the genuine coordinate germ $e$ is nonzero in the analytic germ domain, cancel $e^m$ to obtain $H-J_{m,q}(g)=e^qK$.
This proves whole principal-ideal membership and hence equality in the true quotient ring.
The same argument includes $m=0$ and $q=0$.
\end{proof}
