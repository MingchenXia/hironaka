\section{Geometric strict transforms in genuine open coordinate squares}

\begin{lemma}[Actual open coordinate embeddings have bijective differentials]
\label{hir:actual-open-coordinate-embedding-differential}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualOpenCoordinateStrictSquare.mfderiv_actual_diffeomorph_bijective,Hironaka.HironakaActualOpenCoordinateStrictSquare.mfderiv_actual_open_coordinate_bijective}
\leanok
A genuine biholomorphism has a bijective original complex manifold
differential at every point. Composing such a biholomorphism onto a true
open subset with the original open inclusion again has bijective
differential. The source manifold and the target manifold may live in
different universes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Differentiate the true inverse identity. The composition of the inverse
differential and the forward differential is the identity of the same
finite-dimensional complex model. Bijectivity of a composition of
endomorphisms of this model implies bijectivity of each factor. The
original open inclusion has bijective differential in its actual
preferred charts. The actual chain rule therefore proves bijectivity
also after composing with this inclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original geometric strict transform commutes with a genuine open coordinate square]
\label{hir:actual-open-coordinate-geometric-strict-square}
\uses{hir:actual-open-coordinate-embedding-differential}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualOpenCoordinateStrictSquare.strictTransform_actual_open_coordinate_square}
\leanok
Let $f:Y\to X$ and $g:Y'\to X'$ be actual holomorphic maps between
equidimensional original complex manifolds. Let $a:X'\simeq V\subseteq X$
and $b:Y'\simeq U\subseteq Y$ be actual biholomorphisms onto true open
subsets, and suppose the actual maps satisfy $f\circ b=a\circ g$.
For every subset $A\subseteq X$, the original geometric strict transform
of $a^{-1}A$ by $g$ is exactly the inverse image under $b$ of the original
geometric strict transform of $A$ by $f$. No analyticity or closedness
hypothesis is needed, and the coordinate manifolds may have a different
universe from the original manifolds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding lemma makes the two genuine open coordinate differentials
bijective. Differentiate the actual commutative square. The two chain
rule compositions are equal. Since all four tangent models are the same
finite-dimensional complex vector space, bijectivity of either composed
differential is equivalent to bijectivity of the differential of $f$,
respectively of $g$. Thus the actual exceptional locus of $g$ is exactly
the inverse image of the actual exceptional locus of $f$.
The map $b$ is continuous and open, being a true biholomorphism followed
by a true open inclusion. Its inverse image therefore commutes with
closure. The actual commutative square identifies the inverse images of
$A$, and inverse images commute with set difference. Apply these three
identities to the unchanged definition of geometric strict transform
as closure of the inverse image outside the actual differential
exceptional locus. This proves equality of the entire actual sets.
\end{proof}
