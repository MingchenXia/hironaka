\section{Independent finite ordered trees determine the same elementary map}

The auxiliary trees retain every original empty position and every whole
chronological boundary. The set $G$ passed to a tail merely restricts its
allowed support in the original existence proof; it is not an input to
any auxiliary canonical choice. Consequently the comparison below allows
arbitrary, unrelated $G,G'$. All base maps are actual biholomorphisms.
Write $cL$ for the labelled list of closures of carriers, $L^+$ for
deletion of precisely empty carriers, and $\Phi^*L$ for labelled inverse
image. No incoming comparison of stages is assumed.

\begin{theorem}[Independent actual trees compare from closure-normalized ordered input]
\label{hir:original-canonical-ordered-closure-choice}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-separation-chain-filtered-choice,hir:original-pure-head-unique-empty-base-comparison,hir:original-empty-filtered-parts-tree-projection-neutrality,hir:original-ordered-recursion-literal-boundary,hir:original-separation-chain-whole-strict-normal-form}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalOrderedChoice.ordered_choice_over_base_closedParts}
\leanok
Let $\Phi:X'\simeq X$ be an actual original biholomorphism. Let $T,S$
be any actual finite ordered canonical resolution trees on original
input $(E,G,L)$ and $(E',G',L')$, respectively. Suppose
\[
 (E')^+=(\Phi^*E)^+,
 \qquad (cL')^+=\Phi^*((cL)^+).
\]
There is a unique actual final biholomorphism $\eta$ satisfying
$T\eta=\Phi S$. This same map carries the entire ordered final boundary
after deletion of precisely empty members. Moreover for every original
subset $P\subseteq X$, the entire conventional strict transform of
$\Phi^{-1}P$ under $S$ equals $\eta^{-1}$ of its strict transform under
$T$. No closedness of deeper raw carriers is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use strong induction on the sum of the two raw list lengths. The
literal closure-normalized lists distinguish only genuinely empty
carriers: closure is empty exactly when the original carrier is empty,
and an actual biholomorphism preserves emptiness. If the normalized
list is empty, both actual trees contain only empty centres. Their
genuine projection biholomorphisms identify their final spaces over
$\Phi$. The exact whole chronological boundary formula consists of
the pulled initial boundary followed by the original number of empty
members. Filtering this literal formula and the initial boundary
identity gives the result for the same composed map. A zero-length
tree is handled directly, so no closed-boundary assumption is invented
for an empty original list.

Otherwise expose the two actual head/tail constructors. If an exposed
head is empty, its entire actual separator chain has empty centres.
The actual pure head also has empty original cosupport and hence all
centres empty. Their actual prefix projection is a biholomorphism;
its entire boundary is the original pulled boundary followed by empty
positions. For any deeper carrier, the conventional strict transform
through this prefix is the inverse image of its actual closure.
Thus the remaining actual tail and the unchanged opposite tree satisfy
the normalized input and filtered boundary identities over the composed
base biholomorphism. Apply the induction hypothesis, whose raw length
sum has decreased, then compose the actual projection equations. This
retains all original stages and merely compares their maps; none is
silently deleted.

When both exposed heads are nonempty, equality of the ordered
closure-normalized lists gives equal labels and corresponding head
closures. Replace the head of each actual separator chain by its own
closure: a nil chain remains nil, while a nonnil chain already proves
that its actual original head is analytic and closed, so its whole
record remains unchanged. Their remaining closure-normalized tails
also correspond. The genuine separator-chain comparison therefore
constructs the actual upper biholomorphism for these same two actual
separator towers, with entire filtered boundary and whole strict-set
transport. The strict transform depends only on the original closure,
so these identities identify the same actual pure-head inputs. Genuine pure-head choice comparison then constructs its
actual upper biholomorphism and filtered boundary identity. Apply whole
strict-set transport twice to the remaining labelled carriers. The
strict transform depends only on their actual closures, is closed,
and takes an originally empty carrier to empty. Consequently the
remaining actual tails again have matching ordered normalized input
and filtered initial boundaries. Their raw length sum is smaller.
The induction hypothesis gives the final actual biholomorphism;
composition of the two prefix squares yields $T\eta=\Phi S$ and
retains the same final boundary identity.

In every case actual holomorphic projection uniqueness gives uniqueness
of this final map. Finally the genuine projection square, its actual
biholomorphic upper and lower maps, and closure-normal-form strict
transform comparison give the whole strict-set identity for every
original subset $P$. No equality of raw lengths, boundary positions,
whole mixed-ideal canonical history or stopping indices is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Literal filtered input already determines the elementary map]
\label{hir:original-canonical-ordered-filtered-choice}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-ordered-closure-choice}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalOrderedChoice.ordered_choice_over_base}
\leanok
The preceding conclusion holds when its labelled input premise is
replaced by the literal identity $(L')^+=\Phi^*(L^+)$. Thus independently
chosen finite algorithms with different empty labelled slots and empty
chronological boundary positions have the same actual elementary map
up to their unique over-base biholomorphism.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply labelled closure to the literal filtered-list identity. Closure
commutes with inverse image under the actual base homeomorphism, and
taking closure preserves and reflects carrier nonemptiness. Therefore
closure commutes with deletion of precisely empty carriers, giving
exactly the normalized input identity of the preceding theorem.
Apply that theorem to the two original actual trees. Its unique map,
whole ordered filtered boundary and whole strict-set identities are
unchanged.
\end{proof}
