\begin{lemma}[Actual order-one contact gives an AMBIENT unit cofactor]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-order-one-curve-member-ambient-unit-contact}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-order-one-member-true-contact-unit-dictionary,hir:sharp-actual-individual-restricted-jet-tangent-derivative-drop}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualOrderOneCurveTransverseCoordinates.exists_actual_order_one_member_ambient_unit_contact}
\leanok
ONLY an actual closed smooth carrier $N$, true singleton ambient SNC
member $H$, actual density $N\setminus H$, an actual carrier point on
$H$ and EXISTING ordinary relative order one internally give a genuine
tangent contact $u$ and actual member coordinate $f$, with
$u=c f$ as AMBIENT germs and actual $c(x)\ne0$. The equality is not
restricted to the carrier and no common-coordinate or unit certificate
is supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Actual order one and genuine original contact construction supply $u$,
its true tangent field with nonzero derivative, and actual membership
of $u$ in the original ambient vanishing ideal of $H$. Choose the true
singleton SNC coordinate $f$ for $H$. Its independently proved BOTH
ambient principal germ directions express $u=c f$ as an actual ambient
germ with holomorphic $c$. If $c(x)=0$, true point-power multiplication
would force restricted order at least two for $u$, contradicting the
nonzero genuine tangent derivative. Thus $c$ is a genuine ambient unit
near $x$. On a real open neighbourhood inside the actual SNC chart,
$H$ is therefore EXACTLY $u=0$ in the whole ambient space.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[True order-one contact constructs an actual common curve/member coordinate family]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-order-one-curve-member-simultaneous-coordinates}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-order-one-curve-member-ambient-unit-contact}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualOrderOneCurveTransverseCoordinates.actual_order_one_curve_member_simultaneous_coordinates}
\leanok

dimension one at the point. Internally construct a genuine centered
holomorphic coordinate system in which $N$ is the first coordinate
line and $H$ is the hyperplane of its first coordinate. In particular
the actual member coordinate is disjoint from the actual normal

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

nonzero tangent derivative of $u$ forces its actual first flat partial
to be nonzero; there is only one tangent coordinate. Replace this first
flat coordinate by $u$, retaining EVERY normal coordinate. The proved
actual contact-coordinate derivative calculation gives an injective
complex differential. Holomorphicity on the actual common open and
the real inverse theorem construct genuine centered coordinates
$\Theta$, with their actual image and inverse laws.

At the base point the entire new coordinate vector is zero: the old
flattening is centered and $u(x)=0$. On the actual inverse-theorem
neighbourhood the normal coordinates remain EXACTLY the original
normal coordinates, so membership in $N$ is equivalent to all new
coordinates except the first vanishing. The first new coordinate is
EXACTLY $u$. The first lemma's actual ambient open equality identifies
$H$ with that coordinate's zero hyperplane. These are BOTH directions
of actual ambient memberships, not merely a reduced intersection or
dimension assertion. The first-coordinate singleton has cardinality
one and is genuinely disjoint from the entire normal-coordinate set.
This constructs the actual coordinate-family witness internally.
\end{proof}
