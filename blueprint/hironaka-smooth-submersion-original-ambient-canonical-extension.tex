\[
 A_{n,m}:\mathcal V_n\longrightarrow\mathcal V_m,
 \qquad A_{n,m}(v)=D_{m-n,n}(v),

equality. For $n>0$, the unchanged ambient invariant is literally the
original depth-$n$ family.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine original domain pullback producer gives well-formedness,
positive marking, nonzero germs along the source marked submanifold, and

linear orders reflect all three comparisons. If $n$ is positive, writing

\end{proof}
