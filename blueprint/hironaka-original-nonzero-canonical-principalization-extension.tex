\begin{theorem}[Literal noncontainment gives the same full canonical principalization family]
\label{hir:original-literal-nonzero-canonical-principalization}

with the literal original nonzero condition
$\neg(I\le I_X)$, where $I_X$ is the vanishing ideal of the whole
ambient manifold. For compact original zero locus and every original
ordered SNC boundary, the full same-target canonical boundary
principalization result holds, including every finite and glued smooth
source of that unchanged chosen canonical target. On a compact ambient
manifold no separate compactness premise for the zero locus is needed.
The literal empty-boundary specialization retains the lower original
connected principalization hypothesis, without replacing it by an
unproved equivalent condition.
\uses{mc:factorization-domain,hir:original-principalization-public-canonical-producer,hir:original-principalization-public-canonical-result}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The connected whole ambient manifold is genuinely a closed connected
submanifold of itself. Apply the proved coherent identity-principle
noncontainment theorem directly to this actual submanifold and the
literal original $\neg(I\le I_X)$ hypothesis. It gives nonzero-along
at every original ambient point, without choosing a nonzero germ or
assuming an everywhere-nonzero certificate. Feed this derived fact,
the unchanged original ideal and the entire original SNC boundary to
the genuine canonical result producer. Its explicit result preserves
the literal chosen compact-cosupport canonical tower, all its original
principalization geometry and every full finite and glued smooth
source. On a compact ambient manifold, original coherence makes the
zero locus analytic and closed, hence compact, supplying that input
internally. No old existential principalization witness is selected,
and no accepted canonical producer or existing natural hypothesis is
changed.
\end{proof}
