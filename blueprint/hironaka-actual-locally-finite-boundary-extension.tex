\section{The entire chronological boundary on an actual locally finite limit}
\label{sec:actual-locally-finite-boundary}
This is the full boundary companion to the locally finite composite in
\cite{bierstone-milman97-invent-math}, Theorem~13.4 and Lemma~13.7
(printed pages 298--301), with the finite ordered boundary convention of
\cite{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}, Definitions~2.1--2.2.
The history in this chapter is a genuine growing sequence of blowups of a complex
manifold $X$, with locally finite images of its actual centres.  It is not an
assumption that an arbitrary original problem admits such a history.  All strict
transforms here are centre-relative closures.  Let $M$ be an original well-formed
marked ideal and suppose that every recorded prefix is an actual test tower for
$M$.  Write $E_j$ for its entire ordered transformed boundary at stage $j$ and
write $E_j[k]=\varnothing$ beyond the length of that list.  Empty births are
retained.  Let $p_j:X_\infty\to X_j$ be the already constructed actual factors.

\begin{lemma}[Finite chronological positions and genuine empty tails]
\label{hir:actual-lf-boundary-finite-calculus}
\uses{hir:geo-transform-basic,bcalc:tower-restrict,hir:actual-locally-finite-history-finite-stage-factor-compatibility}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.isClosed_boundaryReadout,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.boundaryReadout_old_slot,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.boundaryReadout_empty_tower,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.mem_boundary_stage_of_empty_tail}
\leanok
Every prefix boundary member is closed.  Each original position of the boundary
of any marked ideal becomes exactly its centre-relative strict transform along
any finite tower.  Along a tower with only empty centres, every natural-number
readout is the inverse image of the original readout.  Consequently, on an open
set on which the actual tail from $N$ to $N+r$ is empty, membership in every slot
of $E_{N+r}$ is equivalent to membership in the same slot of $E_N$ after the
actual tail projection.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A transformed old member is a closure, hence is closed, and a new member is the
inverse image of the closed actual centre.  This proves closedness by induction.
For a position in the original list, the map-and-append definition of the full
boundary selects the mapped old position at each step.  Induction therefore
identifies its successive transforms with the strict transform along the tower.
If the centre is empty, the strict transform of a closed set $H$ is exactly
$\pi^{-1}H$, since that inverse image is closed.  The newly appended member is
empty.  Induction shows that the entire transformed list is its original list
pulled back by the actual projection, followed by one empty position for every
step.  Padding a list by empty positions does not change any readout, including
out-of-range positions.  Finally restrict the actual tail over the given open.
The genuine marked restriction theorem identifies both full finite boundary
lists with their ambient traces, and the preceding empty-tail identity supplies
the asserted equivalence on their actual common endpoint.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The independently defined entire limit boundary]
\label{hir:actual-lf-boundary-definition}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.limitBoundaryMember}
\leanok
For every natural-number position $k$, define an actual subset of the actual limit by
\[
 E_\infty[k]=\bigcap_{r\geq0}p_{k+1+r}^{-1}\bigl(E_{k+1+r}[k]\bigr).
\]
There is one index for each original position followed by every actual exceptional
birth; a position is never removed when its member becomes empty.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exact whole-prefix boundary in each stable window]
\label{hir:actual-lf-boundary-stable-window}
\uses{hir:actual-lf-boundary-finite-calculus,hir:actual-lf-boundary-definition,hir:actual-lf-limit-stable-window-biholomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.stage_length,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.localInclusion_preimage_limitBoundaryMember}
\leanok
Let $U$ be the actual stable window chosen from the local finiteness of this same
history, let $N$ be its actual stable index, and identify its endpoint with the
limit over $U$ by the constructed biholomorphism.  Simultaneously for every $k$,
the pullback of $E_\infty[k]$ is exactly the trace of $E_N[k]$.  In particular the
whole window misses every position $k\geq\#E_0+N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The prefix at stage $j$ has length $j$, by induction from the actual empty stage
and the actual one-step equalities.  For the forward implication in the displayed
intersection take $r=N$, so that its selected stage is $k+1+N$.  The tail from
$N$ to that stage is genuinely empty over $U$.  The finite lemma identifies its
readout with $E_N[k]$, and compatibility of the actual factor with that tail,
together with the exact stable-index factor identity, gives the assertion.
For the reverse implication fix an arbitrary selected stage $J=k+1+r$.  Advance
the given stable endpoint to stage $N+J$.  Its tail from $N$ is empty over $U$,
so the finite lemma gives membership in the $k$th boundary there.  Now regard
that stage as $J+N$.  Since $J\geq k+1$, position $k$ is already present in the
full stage-$J$ boundary, regardless of the initial boundary length.  Its boundary
at stage $J+N$ is its centre-relative strict transform along that actual tail.
This strict transform lies in the inverse image of the closed original member.
Projecting along the actual tail, and using compatibility of the actual factors,
gives membership in $E_J[k]$.  This holds for every $r$, proving the entire
intersection equality.  The final assertion is the literal list-length formula
$\#E_N=\#E_0+N$, with empty positions still counted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Closed analytic members and a genuinely locally finite family]
\label{hir:actual-lf-boundary-analytic-locally-finite}
\uses{hir:actual-lf-boundary-stable-window,hir:snc,ana:analytic-preimage,hir:curve-predicates-locally-finite-union}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.isClosed_limitBoundaryMember,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.locallyFinite_limitBoundaryMember,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.isComplexAnalyticSubset_limitBoundaryMember,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.isComplexAnalyticSubset_limitBoundarySupport}
\leanok
Every $E_\infty[k]$ is closed and complex analytic, and the complete indexed family
$(E_\infty[k])_{k\in\mathbb N}$ is locally finite.  Its entire union is a closed complex analytic subset.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each defining intersection is an intersection of inverse images of closed actual
finite boundary members by continuous actual stage factors, so it is closed.
For a point of the limit choose one of its actual stable endpoint representatives.
The image of that entire endpoint is an open neighbourhood.  By the previous
theorem only positions less than $\#E_0+N$ can meet this neighbourhood; the finite
set of these natural numbers proves local finiteness in the usual topological
sense.  Well-formedness and the genuine test property imply that every finite
prefix boundary has simple normal crossings.  Each of its members is therefore
locally a coordinate hyperplane, hence analytic; an absent position is empty and
analytic.  The exact whole-window equality identifies the trace of each limit
member with that finite analytic member under the actual window biholomorphism.
Analyticity pulls back by that holomorphic inverse and is local on an open cover,
which proves analyticity of the entire independently defined limit member.
Local finiteness gives a neighbourhood on which only finitely many members can
meet; the union there is the corresponding finite union of analytic sets.
Hence the entire union is analytic, and therefore closed.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Locally finite indexed simple normal crossings boundary]
\label{hir:actual-lf-boundary-indexed-snc}
\uses{hir:snc}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.IsLocallyFiniteSNCDivisor}
\leanok
A family $(D_i)_{i\in I}$ of subsets of a complex $n$-manifold is a locally finite
simple normal crossings boundary if it is locally finite, each member is closed,
and at every point one centred holomorphic coordinate system identifies all
members through that point with distinct coordinate hyperplanes.  Empty members
are allowed and retain their indices.  This is the indexed locally finite
boundary convention used when a finite boundary is continued along a locally
finite history.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Exact finite-list and indexed-family interface]
\label{hir:actual-lf-boundary-list-indexed-interface}
\uses{hir:actual-lf-boundary-indexed-snc,hir:snc}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.isLocallyFiniteSNCDivisor_boundaryReadout_iff}
\leanok
For every finite ordered list $E$, the family $k\mapsto E[k]$, padded by empty
sets, is a locally finite indexed simple normal crossings boundary if and only
if $E$ is a simple normal crossings divisor in the original finite-list sense.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Only positions below the length of $E$ can meet any neighbourhood, so the padded
family is locally finite.  From the finite-list coordinate witness at a point,
assign its specified singleton coordinate set to each in-range position and
assign the empty coordinate set to every later position.  A later member is
empty, so imposes no coordinate condition.  The coordinate equations and
singleton cardinalities for every member through the point are therefore
exactly those of the original list.  The original injectivity of its coordinate
sets gives injectivity for the passing natural-number positions, since both are
in range.  Closedness is unchanged, with empty sets outside the list.  Conversely
restrict the indexed coordinate witness along the literal injection of the
finite index set into the natural numbers.  The same equations, cardinalities
and injectivity prove the original finite-list condition; closedness is read at
the same actual positions.  No nonempty position or historical index is merged.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The constructed entire limit boundary has simple normal crossings]
\label{hir:actual-lf-boundary-whole-indexed-snc}
\uses{hir:actual-lf-boundary-list-indexed-interface,hir:actual-lf-boundary-stable-window,hir:actual-lf-boundary-analytic-locally-finite,hir:actual-lf-limit-stable-window-biholomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteBoundary.isLocallyFiniteSNCDivisor_limitBoundaryMember}
\leanok
The complete constructed family $(E_\infty[k])_{k\in\mathbb N}$ is a locally
finite indexed simple normal crossings boundary on the genuine actual limit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Closedness and local finiteness have already been proved for the independently
defined complete family.  At a given limit point take an actual stable endpoint
representative and its actual whole window.  The original well-formed marked
ideal and testness of its same finite prefix give its genuine finite simple
normal crossings boundary.  Restrict that entire list to the prefix endpoint
open; simple normal crossings is preserved by this actual inclusion.  The
finite-list interface supplies one simultaneous coordinate witness for the
entire padded endpoint family, with singleton and injectivity conditions at
precisely the passing indices.  The whole-prefix equality identifies that
family with the pullback of the constructed limit family.  The inverse of the
actual window biholomorphism pulls its adapted coordinates to the limit window,
with the same coordinate index sets.  Adapted coordinates
on an open subset are adapted ambient coordinates at the same point.  Thus the
same witness proves the required ambient indexed-family condition.  This uses
the actual constructed maps and full boundary lists; no boundary comparison,
normal-crossings certificate or scheduler is assumed.
\end{proof}
