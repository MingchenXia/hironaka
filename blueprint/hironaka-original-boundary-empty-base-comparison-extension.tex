\section{Actual canonical stage comparisons over a genuine base biholomorphism}

Let $\Phi:X'\simeq X$ be an actual biholomorphism of original complex
manifolds of the same dimension. Source initial records below have the
same whole submanifold, ideal and marking as the actual $\Phi^*M$;
their ordered initial boundaries agree after deleting only empty members.
No transported tower, comparison map or insertion history is input.

\begin{lemma}[The genuine transported tower supplies the entire stage dictionary]
\label{hir:original-empty-base-actual-transport-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyBaseComparison.canonical_transport_dictionary}
\uses{hir:tower-isomorphism,hir:canonical-centres}
\leanok
For an original invariant-domain datum $M$ and an actual canonical tower
$T$, the actual tower transport $Q=\Phi^*T$, with its internally
constructed final biholomorphism $\beta$, is canonical for the actual
ambient canonical invariant on $\Phi^*M$. Its domain is genuine, the
whole projections commute, and its entire transformed marked record is
exactly $\beta^*(M_T)$. Its whole actual maximum locus is the inverse
image under $\beta$ of the target whole maximum locus. Target terminality
implies source terminality, and every original open avoided by all target
centres has its actual $\Phi$-preimage avoided by all transported centres.
These statements include ambient dimension zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Genuine tower transport constructs each actual source centre and blowup,
and its actual tower isomorphism gives the commuting projections and
the final biholomorphism. The original domain pulls back under the real
base biholomorphism. Actual canonical-invariant isomorphism invariance
therefore gives canonicality of this same transported tower in every
ambient dimension. Whole marked-transform comparison gives the entire
record equality and its whole cosupport pullback. The true invariant
isomorphism formula at all actual target cosupport points, together with
surjectivity of $\beta$, gives equality of the entire maximum loci.
Actual resolution invariance under this genuine tower isomorphism gives
terminality whenever the target is terminal. Finally induct through this
actual isomorphism. At each stage the source centre is exactly the inverse
image of the target centre, and the projection square identifies the
source preimage of $\Phi^{-1}O$ with the inverse image of the target
preimage of $O$. Target disjointness therefore gives source disjointness
at every actual centre. No zero-dimensional exceptional case is replaced
by a positive-dimensional invariant.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Terminal stages compare over the prescribed base biholomorphism]
\label{hir:original-empty-boundary-terminal-base-comparison}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyBaseComparison.terminal_choice_over_base_across_empty_boundaries}
\uses{hir:original-empty-base-actual-transport-dictionary,hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-terminal-choice,hir:original-actual-whole-projection-biholomorphic-map-uniqueness}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an original invariant-domain datum on $X$, and let the original
source record $M'$ on $X'$ have the same whole submanifold, ideal and
marking as $\Phi^*M$, with the same literal ordered nonempty initial
boundary. Any actual canonical terminal histories $S$ for $M'$ and $T$
for $M$ internally have a unique actual final biholomorphism $\eta$ with
\[
 T\eta=\Phi S.
\]
The same map transports their entire ordered final boundaries after
deleting precisely empty members. A source domain or final comparison
certificate is not added as a premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Internally construct the actual transport $Q,\beta$ of the same chosen
$T$ by the preceding lemma. It is canonical and terminal for the literal
whole record $\Phi^*M$ and has its actual pulled domain. The source
record $M'$ differs from that record only in empty original boundary
positions. The proved same-base empty-filter comparison on $X'$ therefore
constructs its unique actual over-base map $\kappa:S_{\rm final}\simeq
Q_{\rm final}$, carrying the entire ordered filtered final boundary.
Set $\eta=\beta\kappa$. The actual transport projection equation and
the proved equation for $\kappa$ give $T\eta=\Phi S$. Whole transport
of the marked record gives the exact entire boundary pullback under
$\beta$. Since this map is bijective, filtering nonempty members commutes
with its inverse-image list map. Compose that identity with the actual
filtered-boundary identity for $\kappa$; literal list-map composition
gives the claimed equality for the same $\eta$. Any other actual final
biholomorphism satisfying the displayed projection equation has the same
projection through the actual tower $T$, so intrinsic actual projection
uniqueness identifies it with $\eta$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[First-meeting stages compare over the prescribed base biholomorphism]
\label{hir:original-empty-boundary-first-meeting-base-comparison}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyBaseComparison.first_meeting_choice_over_base_across_empty_boundaries}
\uses{hir:original-empty-base-actual-transport-dictionary,hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-first-meeting-choice,hir:original-actual-whole-projection-biholomorphic-map-uniqueness}
\leanok
Under the same original-record conditions, let actual canonical prefixes
$S,T$ avoid respectively $\Phi^{-1}O,O$, and let their next actual whole
maximum loci meet these corresponding original opens. Then their final
manifolds have a unique actual biholomorphism $\eta$ satisfying
$T\eta=\Phi S$, carrying the entire ordered final boundaries after
deletion of precisely empty members. Terminality, a common stopping
index and a stage comparison are not assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first lemma internally transports $T$ to $Q,\beta$ on $X'$ and gives
actual canonicality and avoidance of $\Phi^{-1}O$. Choose the inverse
image under the actual bijection $\beta$ of the original next-maximum
point for $T$. The proved whole maximum-locus equality places it in the
actual whole maximum locus of $Q$, and the true projection square places
its projection in $\Phi^{-1}O$. Thus $S$ and this same $Q$ satisfy the
genuine same-base empty-filter first-meeting comparison. It produces
$\kappa$ and the full ordered filtered-boundary identity without
terminality or a common stopping index. Compose with $\beta$ exactly as
in the terminal case. Whole transport of the marked record and literal
inverse-image list composition prove the asserted boundary equality.
The actual projection uniqueness theorem gives uniqueness of the same
composed $\eta$ over the prescribed $\Phi$.
\end{proof}
