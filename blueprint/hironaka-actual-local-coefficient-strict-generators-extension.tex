\section{Genuine local coefficient cosupport and whole reduced strict generation}

The coefficient ideals are required coherent only on an actual open
neighbourhood $U$ of the origin. Cosupport containment is required only on
$U\cap\{y=0\}$. No global extension of local holomorphic functions or coherent
ideals is assumed. The real flattening pullback already produces exactly
this local coherence notion. This is the local coefficient-centre component
of \cite[Theorem 9.4, pp.~277--278]{bierstone-milman97-invent-math}; full
presentation construction, transformed derivative/Jacobian properties and
Bennett remain separate goals.

\begin{lemma}[Local coherent germs propagate on their real domain]
\label{hir:actual-local-coefficient-germ-propagation}
\uses{hir:real-centre-domain-actual-flattened-coherent-family}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocalCoefficientStrictGenerators.eventually_mem_of_coherentOn}
\leanok
Let $\mathfrak a$ be an original ideal coherent on an actual open domain
$U$, and let $x\in U$. Every original membership $f\in\mathfrak a_x$
remains a membership at all sufficiently nearby points.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take the actual finite original generating family and its membership
characterization near $x$, supplied by local coherence. Express $f$ as a
finite holomorphic linear combination of these actual generators. Shrink
to a common open where the coefficients, $f$ and this literal equality
remain holomorphic and valid. At each point of that open the same finite
relation, read in the original membership characterization, proves the
claimed membership. No extension outside the real domain is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Local plane cosupport produces actual whole germ powers]
\label{hir:actual-local-coefficient-cosupport-normal-power}
\uses{hir:actual-local-coefficient-germ-propagation,hir:relative-normal-actual-recursive-power-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocalCoefficientStrictGenerators.mk_mem_normalIdeal_pow_of_local_cosupport,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocalCoefficientStrictGenerators.originalTaylor_mem_coordinate_power_of_local_cosupport}
\leanok
Let $U\subset\mathbb C^{k+s}$ be open with $0\in U$, and let
$\mathfrak a$ be coherent only on $U$. Suppose
$U\cap\{y=0\}\subset\operatorname{cosupp}(\mathfrak a,q)$.
For every actual original member $f\in\mathfrak a_0$ holomorphic at zero,
its entire germ lies in the actual normal-coordinate ideal power
$(y_1,\ldots,y_s)^q$. Its genuine Taylor series consequently lies in the
same whole formal coordinate ideal power.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Propagate the original membership on a neighbourhood contained in $U$.
At every nearby point of the coordinate plane the genuine cosupport
condition puts $f$ in the actual maximal-ideal power of degree $q$.
The original chart-derivative dictionary therefore makes all iterated
ordinary derivatives of length less than $q$ vanish along that plane near
zero. The proved multivariable Hadamard--Taylor decomposition gives the
actual recursive normal-power expression of degree $q$. Its genuine
whole-germ normal-power dictionary yields membership in $(y)^q$.
Apply the actual Taylor ring homomorphism and its whole coordinate-ideal
identity to obtain the formal power. The argument includes $q=0$ and an
empty normal block, and uses neither coherence away from $U$ nor a globally
smooth coordinate-centre certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[True local coefficient data produce the whole formal centre power]
\label{hir:actual-local-coefficient-presentation-centre-power}
\uses{hir:actual-local-coefficient-cosupport-normal-power,hir:actual-formal-centre-power-relative-coefficients}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocalCoefficientStrictGenerators.mem_actualPresentationCentre_power_of_local_coefficient_cosupport}
\leanok
Let $F(w,z)$ be an actual formal series. On a genuine open neighbourhood
$U\ni0$ in $w=(u,y)$ suppose its low literal normal coefficients are
Taylor expansions of original holomorphic coefficient functions
$c_\gamma\in\mathfrak a_{\gamma,0}$, where $\mathfrak a_\gamma$ is coherent
only on $U$ and $U\cap\{y=0\}$ lies in its genuine cosupport of weight
$q-|\gamma|$. Then $F\in(y,z)^q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For every low normal exponent, the preceding theorem gives its whole
formal tangential-centre power of degree $q-|\gamma|$. The literal
coefficient identity and the proved entire formal coefficient-power
criterion imply $F\in(y,z)^q$. That criterion treats every original
coefficient, including the high normal exponents whose weight alone
already exceeds the required bound. No factorial convention or unproved
identification with raw derivatives is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Local original coefficients internally produce entire reduced strict generators]
\label{hir:actual-local-coefficient-reduced-strict-generators}
\uses{hir:actual-local-coefficient-presentation-centre-power,hir:actual-reduced-basis-entire-formal-strict-generation}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocalCoefficientStrictGenerators.ActualLocalReducedCoefficientPresentation,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocalCoefficientStrictGenerators.actualLocalReducedCoefficientPresentation_centre_powers,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocalCoefficientStrictGenerators.exists_actual_reduced_strict_generators_of_local_coefficients}
\leanok
Suppose the actual canonical reduced members $G_a$ have the preceding
literal local coefficient data, with weight equal to their actual selected
vertex weight. In every genuine blowup chart of $(y,z)$ there is an
internally constructed finite family generating the ENTIRE existing formal
strict transform and satisfying the literal exceptional-product identities
for these same reduced members. Centre-power membership and strict
generation are not inputs to the coefficient data.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding producer supplies every reduced member's required actual
centre power from the original local coherence, local cosupport and
literal coefficient identities. Genuine reduced filtered division now
expands every original ideal member with the actual quotient powers.
Normalize the same reduced members and those actual quotients in the true
chart; formal-domain cancellation proves the whole strict-ideal equality
and the actual exceptional-product identities. Thus the previously proved
whole reduced strict-generation theorem applies without any global
extension premise. Existence of all source coefficient presentations and
the later analytic/formal and Hilbert--Samuel transformation arguments
are not asserted.
\end{proof}
