\section{One genuine analytic open for all powers of an actual coherent saturation}
Let $X=\mathbb C^n$, let $\mathfrak a$ be an ORIGINAL coherent ideal of
holomorphic germs on $X$, and let $h$ be an actual function holomorphic
near the origin. Membership is recorded by entire functions and their
WHOLE germs; no map from all origin germs to arbitrary nearby stalks is
postulated. Put $C_0=\mathfrak a$ and $C_{q+1}=(C_q:h)$, with
$(C_q:h)_y=\{g\in\mathcal O_{X,y}:gh\in(C_q)_y\}$.
Thus $C_q$ is the literal colon by $h^q$. The same argument applies to
an ideal locally generated by an actual finite family of holomorphic
functions. The stable exponent and neighborhood below are produced
internally from these original data.

\begin{lemma}[Entire finite analytic spans and genuine origin ideals]
\label{hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-finite-span}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCoherentSaturation.inSpanAt_eventually,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCoherentSaturation.inSpanAt_substitute,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCoherentSaturation.inSpanAt_mul_actual,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCoherentSaturation.inSpanAt_zero_iff_mem_origin_span}
\leanok
For a finite actual holomorphic family $s_i$, membership of an entire
function $g$ in its analytic span at the origin is equivalent to
$[g]_0\in( [s_i]_0)_i$ in the TRUE ring of holomorphic germs.
If $g$ has this membership at the origin, it has membership in the
same actual analytic span at every point of one neighborhood of the
origin. More generally, if each member of a finite family $t_j$ is in
the $s_i$ span at the origin, then their entire analytic span is
contained in the $s_i$ span at every point of one actual neighborhood.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An analytic span witness consists of finitely many whole holomorphic
coefficient functions $a_i$ and the eventual identity
$g=\sum_i a_i s_i$. Taking WHOLE germs gives the literal ring ideal
membership. Conversely, finite ideal membership gives an actual
coefficient tuple of whole germs. Choose genuine holomorphic function
representatives for those finitely many coefficients; the ring
identity becomes an eventual identity of the actual entire functions.
For nearby membership, shrink to an open neighborhood where all
coefficient functions and all $s_i$ are holomorphic and the identity
holds. At every point of this SAME open the identity holds as an
entire germ, hence gives the required analytic span witness. There
are only finitely many $t_j$, so intersect their actual neighborhoods.
Substitution of any actual analytic combination of the $t_j$ into
these finite representations gives an actual analytic combination
of the $s_i$. The coefficient product and finite sum are genuinely
holomorphic at that nearby point. Empty families use the literal
zero sum and the same eventual-identity argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The literal original colon sheaves are coherent and retain their entire origin dictionaries]
\label{hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-colon-dictionary}

\leanok
\uses{hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-finite-span,oka:relation-ideals,oka:coherent-colon}
Every $C_q$ is coherent near the origin. Internally select a finite
actual family generating $\mathfrak a$ on a neighborhood and let
$A_0$ be its TRUE origin-germ ideal. Define
$A_{q+1}=(A_q:[h]_0)$ in $\mathcal O_{X,0}$. For every $q$ and every
actual holomorphic function $g$, original membership
$g\in(C_q)_0$ is equivalent to $[g]_0\in A_q$.
At every point where $h$ is holomorphic and the original ideal is
coherent, $g\in(C_q)_y$ is equivalent to
$g\in\mathcal O_{X,y}$ and $g h^q\in\mathfrak a_y$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original coherence hypothesis supplies actual finite holomorphic
generators on a genuine open containing zero; these are selected
internally. The preceding entire-span dictionary identifies their
literal origin ideal with original membership. Oka's theorem applied
to the row $(h,s_1,\ldots,s_r)$ constructs actual finitely many
holomorphic relations on an open neighborhood. Their first
components generate the literal colon at EVERY point of that open.
Iterating this genuine colon-coherence construction proves coherence
of every $C_q$; no next-colon presentation is supplied by a caller.
Taking WHOLE germs of the actual product $gh$ gives
$[gh]_0=[g]_0[h]_0$, so induction gives the exact origin dictionary
for every $A_q$. Finally repeated colon membership is exactly
membership of $g h^q$ in the original ideal: at each induction step
the extra holomorphicity condition on $gh$ follows from that of
$g$ and the actual $h$, and the identity
$(gh)h^q=g h^{q+1}$ holds for the entire functions. The zero power
uses original coherent membership, which already implies
holomorphicity.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The stable exponent is internally selected from the actual origin Noetherian chain]
\label{hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-origin-stability}

\leanok
\uses{hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-colon-dictionary,lag:germ-noetherian}
There exists an internally produced integer $N$ such that
$A_N=A_{N+1}$, and hence $A_{N+q}=A_N$ for every $q\geq0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every ideal is contained in its colon by a single element: if
$a\in A_q$, then $a[h]_0\in A_q$. Consequently the actual $A_q$
form an increasing chain of TRUE ideals. The holomorphic germ ring
$\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^n,0}$ is Noetherian, so its genuine ascending
chain condition produces an integer $N$ after which this very chain
is constant. In particular $A_N=A_{N+1}$. Applying the same colon
operation to both sides and using induction proves all later
equalities. The integer is selected from the original chain, rather
than assumed as a stabilization or saturation certificate. The
zero-dimensional case uses the same proved Noetherian ring.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One actual analytic open has exact equality of the two actual coherent colon sheaves]

$(C_N)_y=(C_{N+1})_y$ for EVERY $y\in U$ and every WHOLE
holomorphic germ at $y$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the genuine coherent-colon constructions to the actual
internally selected $N$ and $N+1$. They supply finite actual
holomorphic families $s_i$ and $t_j$ generating $C_N$ and
$C_{N+1}$ at EVERY point of respective neighborhoods of zero.

Thus each ACTUAL $t_j$ is an actual analytic combination of the

with both original coherent presentation opens and an actual
holomorphic open for $h$. At every point of the resulting SAME

of the $t_j$, hence one of the $s_i$, and belongs to $C_N$.
Conversely $C_N\subset C_{N+1}$ at every such point: in the literal
ideal $C_N$, multiplication by the actual holomorphic $h$ preserves
membership. This ideal closure follows directly from the actual
finite analytic span presentation there. The two inclusions prove
WHOLE stalk equality for EVERY point of this same $U$. An equality
only at the origin would not suffice; the finite functional
identities and the genuine coherent presentation at ALL points
are essential.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Only original coherence and an actual holomorphic function produce uniform all-power saturation]

$y\in U$, every $q\geq N$, and every actual holomorphic $g$,
\[
 g h^q\in\mathfrak a_y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad

\begin{proof}

no new shrinking as $r$ varies. Write $q=N+r$ and use the literal
power-colon dictionary to obtain the displayed equivalence.
All earlier $C_q$ are contained in $C_N$ by actual ideal closure,

Original finite generators and the finite generators of both

\end{proof}
