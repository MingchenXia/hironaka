\begin{lemma}[Every actual target point has the whole original fixed-flattening stalk dictionary]
\label{hir:real-domain-fixed-flattening-all-original-whole-stalks}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-centre-domain-original-pullback-coherence,hir:real-centre-domain-whole-nearby-translated-ideals,hir:nonlinear-nearby-actual-generator-transport,hir:nonlinear-nearby-original-whole-stalk-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainOriginalStalkFreeness.whole_original_stalk_dictionary_at_actual_target}
\leanok
Let the original ideal be coherent and fix ANY genuine flattening whose
source lies in the real original chart target. At EVERY actual point of
this fixed flattening target, the entire actual translated flattened ideal
maps under the genuine recentered nonlinear germ equivalence to the WHOLE
original fixed-chart stalk ideal at the actual inverse image point. Its
whole inverse ideal image is the corresponding entire comap. No different
flattening, nearby ideal-map witness or global model coherence is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At the actual inverse image point choose the original coherent ideal's true
finite holomorphic generators and complete local membership span. The real
inverse chart is holomorphic at every point of the actual flattening target.
Its actual pullback generators therefore present the existing flattened
ideal on a genuine smaller open containing the stated target point and
mapping into the original generator domain. Translate these ACTUAL functions
at that point to zero. The real finite-generator translation dictionary
identifies their entire ideal span with the WHOLE translated flattened
origin ideal. The true original finite-generator dictionary in the fixed
chart identifies its WHOLE original stalk with the entire chart-germ span
of the same original functions. The actual recentered nonlinear germ
equivalence maps each translated composite function germ to its true
original chart-function germ. Mapping every finite ideal sum in both
directions therefore gives the whole ideal image equality. The nonlinear
germ map is a genuine ring equivalence, so applying its comap gives the
entire inverse ideal equality. This argument is local at each actual point
of one fixed target and asserts nothing outside that real domain.
\end{proof}
