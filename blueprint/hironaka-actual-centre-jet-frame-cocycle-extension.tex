\section{A genuine orderwise inverse system of original centre jet frames}

\begin{theorem}[Identity of the actual original frame transition]
\label{hir:actual-centre-jet-frame-truncation-identity}
\uses{hir:actual-fixed-entire-scalar-jet-representatives,hir:actual-whole-quotient-frame-truncation}
For one genuine original family and one actual nonzero pivot, the
literal whole-germ transition from its fixed quotient model to itself
is the identity. The original quotient factor at equal orders is the
identity algebra map.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentreJetFrameCocycle.actualJetFrameTruncation_refl}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every vector of the actual fixed model is represented by its once-fixed
entire original germ. Its true whole quotient coordinates are exactly
that same vector, by the genuine right-inverse and original pivot
projection identities. This is precisely the defining transition at
equal orders, hence equality of the entire linear maps. The original
quotient identity is the usual quotient-factor identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole original three-order cocycle]
\label{hir:actual-centre-jet-frame-truncation-cocycle}
\uses{hir:actual-whole-quotient-frame-truncation,hir:actual-centre-jet-frame-truncation-identity}
For actual orders $q\le r\le s$, a SAME original family at a SAME point,
and genuine pivots whose sizes equal the actual ranks at those three
orders, the literal transitions satisfy
$\tau_{q,r}\circ\tau_{r,s}=\tau_{q,s}$ on the ENTIRE upper fixed model.
The ordinary factors of the SAME original quotients compose to the
direct factor.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentreJetFrameCocycle.actualJetFrameTruncation_comp}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The entire original upper whole-coordinate map is surjective. Thus
any upper model vector is the upper coordinates of one original germ
$g$. The proved every-numerator identity first carries it to the middle
coordinates of this SAME $g$ and then to its lower coordinates. The
direct transition also gives the lower coordinates of $g$. Surjectivity
therefore proves equality on every upper vector and hence equality of
the entire linear maps. The original quotient inclusions are the true
power inclusions $I+\mathfrak m^s\subseteq I+\mathfrak m^r\subseteq
I+\mathfrak m^q$, so the ordinary quotient-factor composition theorem
identifies their composition with the direct original factor. No
selected-column or finite-jet representative assertion replaces this
whole quotient statement.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original HS-constant centres give one orderwise compatible system through zero]
\label{hir:actual-hs-constant-centre-orderwise-jet-system}
\uses{hir:actual-hs-constant-centre-origin-pivot-whole-frames,hir:actual-centre-jet-frame-truncation-identity,hir:actual-centre-jet-frame-truncation-cocycle,hir:actual-jet-frame-truncation-holomorphic-entries}
From ONLY the original coherent ideal, actual regular centre at the
original point and constancy of ALL original intrinsic HS values on
the centre, internally choose the SAME original finite generators,
flattening and ambient $U\ni0$ BEFORE all orders. Choose one actual
maximal pivot and one real pivot domain $D_\ell\ni0$ at EVERY order
$\ell+1$, once as functions of $\ell$ and before every pair or triple.
For every ordered pair, on its true finite intersection the actual
transition has holomorphic entries; on the actual centre it is the
surjective original whole quotient factor and preserves every original
numerator. For every ordered triple, its real intersection and true
tangent preimage are open and contain zero, and the SAME orderwise
chosen frame transitions satisfy the entire cocycle there on the
actual centre. Equal-order transitions are identities.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentreJetFrameCocycle.exists_original_hs_constant_centre_orderwise_jet_system}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the original HS-constant origin-pivot producer once. Preserve
its actual common generators, flattening, ambient domain, entire
translated-ideal dictionary and all-order intrinsic HS/rank dictionary.
Choose its proved existential pivot and domain data simultaneously as
functions of the order, without changing those common objects. Every
chosen domain is open, contained in $U$ and contains zero. Pair and
triple intersections are therefore genuine real opens through zero;
continuous actual tangent inclusion gives the same property of their
tangent preimages. Original centre rank constancy identifies each
chosen pivot size with its actual rank at every centre point in the
corresponding domain. Apply the genuine whole truncation theorem to
every pair, its fixed-array projection formula for holomorphic entries,
and the preceding identity and three-order theorem to these SAME
chosen pivots. The existing whole frames intertwine the literal
original quotient factors on every quotient class. All choices precede
the pair/triple quantifiers, so there is one actual orderwise system.
\end{proof}

Only finite intersections of order-dependent domains are asserted.
There is no common all-order open, no tangent differential compatibility,
no normal-adic reconstruction or normal-flatness conclusion. This
actual maximal-ideal jet inverse system is not a substitute definition
for the original normal filtration or for compatible analytic vertices.
