\section{One original flattening couples normal freeness and the initial cylinder}

\begin{theorem}[Actual finite free normal layers and the same initial cylinder]
\label{hir:actual-original-coupled-normal-free-initial-cylinder}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoupledNormalFreeness.exists_original_hs_constant_centre_coupled_normal_data}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-coupled-centre-free-jets-initial-symbols,hir:actual-joint-all-coordinate-cuts-free,hir:actual-tangent-cut-whole-layer-dictionary,hir:actual-whole-initial-normal-extension-of-polynomial-tangent-stability}
Let $C$ be regular of dimension $d$ at $x$ in a complex manifold of
 dimension $d+t$, and let $\mathcal A$ be a coherent ideal whose entire
 intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel function is constant along $C$.
There is a single genuine local flattening $\Phi$ of $C$ with
$\Phi(x)=0$, retaining the actual chart-source and coordinate-plane
membership dictionaries, such that the following hold for the unchanged
origin ideal $I_0$ of $\mathcal A$ in that flattening. Every whole
normal layer $G_q(I_0)$ is finite free over
$B=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$. The actual homogeneous initial ideal
$J=\operatorname{in}(\widehat I_0)$ is stable under every tangential
formal derivative, including the literal polynomial derivatives, and
\[
 J=\rho(\rho^{-1}(J))\,\mathbb C[[u,x]],
 \qquad \rho:\mathbb C[[x]]\longrightarrow\mathbb C[[u,x]]
\]
is the actual normal-variable renaming map. No free-layer,
initial-stability or cylinder certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the original coupled-symbol producer once. Keep its single
flattening, coherent generators, exact origin-ideal equality, finite
free joint normal truncations, and both initial-derivative stability
statements for that same origin ideal. Identify the whole joint ideal
with the image of $I_0$ under the actual centre joint pullback using the
retained generator equality. The order-zero joint quotient is zero;
every positive joint normal truncation is finite free by the coupled
producer. The actual split normal-filtration sequence therefore makes
all joint normal layers finite free.

Cut each distinct tangential displacement coordinate in order. At each
cut the current initial-derivative stability and genuine symbol square
give injectivity on the current residue layer; the established cut
recursion supplies finite freeness of every resulting layer. The whole
image of the final cut ideal under the actual displacement-zero trace
is exactly $I_0$, and that trace kills the whole displacement ideal.
Its actual $B$-linear layer isomorphisms transfer finite freeness to
every whole original normal layer of $I_0$.

No flattening or ideal was reselected during this construction, so the
two original initial-derivative stability conclusions still apply to
this very $I_0$. Apply the proved polynomial differential-stability
contraction--extension theorem to obtain the displayed equality of
whole formal ideals under the literal normal-variable renaming map.
The construction also covers order zero and empty tangent or normal
coordinate blocks. It proves these simultaneous conclusions only;
canonical reduced-vertex normal powers and blowup decrease require
further arguments.
\end{proof}
