\section{Extending prescribed finite histories over the entire cosupport}
\label{sec:open-cosupport-history-extension}
This is a geometric continuation bridge for a prescribed actual finite history,
not an existence assumption for local resolving histories or an unrestricted
locally finite scheduling rule.  We retain the same original marked ideal,
its complete ordered boundary and every actual admissibility condition.  The
open set contains the entire cosupport.  Compactness is not required.

\begin{lemma}[Closed entire cosupport makes the actual centre globally closed]
\label{hir:open-cosupport-global-centre}
\uses{hir:geo-transform-wellformed,hir:base-change-open}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOpenCosupportHistoryExtension.isClosed_image_val_of_closed_carrier,Hironaka.HironakaOpenCosupportHistoryExtension.isAdmissibleCentre_image_val}
\leanok
Let $M$ be a well-formed marked ideal on a complex manifold $X$ and let the open
$U$ contain its entire cosupport.  Every centre admissible for the actual marked
restriction to $U$ has globally closed image in $X$, and that entire image is an
admissible centre for the same original $M$ and its complete ordered boundary.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
We first give the topological argument.  Let $F\subset U\subset X$ with $F$
closed in $X$, and let $C$ be closed in $U$ with its image contained in $F$.
Write $C=U\cap B$ for some closed $B\subset X$, using the induced topology.
The image of $C$ is exactly $F\cap B$: one inclusion follows from its containment
in $F$, and for the other $F\subset U$.  Hence that image is closed in $X$.
Apply this to the actual original cosupport $F$, whose closedness follows from
coherence and the closed smooth original carrier.  The exact cosupport formula
for open marked restriction puts the centre image inside $F$.  Its local
regularity is transported by the genuine open inclusion, so its closed image is
a closed submanifold.  The same cosupport formula proves admissibility along the
whole image; the actual simultaneous normal-crossing coordinates for the local
centre and all original ordered boundary members descend along the open
inclusion.  These are precisely all original admissibility conditions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Extend a prescribed actual step through its universal comparison]
\label{hir:open-cosupport-actual-step-extension}
\uses{hir:open-cosupport-global-centre,ana:blowups,bcalc:restrict-open,hir:biholomorphism-blowup}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOpenCosupportHistoryExtension.exists_step_extension}
\leanok
With the preceding original inputs, let $\pi:Y\to U$ be a prescribed actual
blowup along such an admissible centre $C$.  There is an actual blowup
$q:Z\to X$ along the entire image of $C$, with that image admissible for the
original $M$, and a genuine biholomorphism
$Y\simeq q^{-1}U$ over the original inclusion $U\to X$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The image centre is a genuine globally closed submanifold by the lemma.  Empty
interior passes from $C$ to its open image, so actual blowup existence applies
to this centre in $X$.  Restrict that resulting blowup over $U$.  Its centre is
literally $C$, since the inclusion is injective.  The genuine universal
uniqueness theorem for two blowups along the same entire ideal produces the
biholomorphism from the prescribed $Y$, its genuine holomorphic inverse and the
exact projection square.  No map, global closedness proof, inverse or comparison
square is an incoming assumption.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Extend the same entire finite history and transformed marked record]
\label{hir:open-cosupport-actual-finite-history-extension}
\uses{hir:open-cosupport-actual-step-extension,hir:geo-transform-basic,bcalc:tower-restrict,hir:tower-isomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOpenCosupportHistoryExtension.exists_extension}
\leanok
Every prescribed actual finite test tower $T$ of the marked restriction of the
same original well-formed $M$ to $U$ is the open restriction of an actual ambient
test tower $Q$ of $M$, through a constructed isomorphism of the entire towers.
The whole transformed marked record of $T$ is exactly the pullback of the
original transformed marked record of $Q$ by its actual endpoint map over $U$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the prescribed tower.  For the empty history use the actual identity.
Suppose the whole shorter history has been extended to $Q$, with its actual
endpoint biholomorphism over $U$ and whole marked restriction identity.  Move
the next actual centre and blowup through that biholomorphism.  The whole record
identity transports the given actual local admissibility to the open preimage
of $U$ in the endpoint of $Q$.  Every point of the current ambient cosupport
projects into the original cosupport, by the actual test projection theorem.
Thus its entire current cosupport lies in that open preimage.  The current
ambient marked ideal is well-formed by genuine admissible transformation.
The preceding step theorem therefore constructs its actual ambient next
blowup and universal endpoint comparison without any compactness hypothesis.
Append that step to $Q$.  The constructed projection square and the literal
identity of its centre traces satisfy the step constructor for an actual
isomorphism of the two entire towers.  The original marked-ideal open
restriction theorem and whole-record transport along this actual tower
isomorphism prove the final equality of all transformed fields, including the
entire ideal and ordered boundary.  Hence the ambient tower is an actual test
tower, and neither its testness nor its output comparison was supplied.
\end{proof}
