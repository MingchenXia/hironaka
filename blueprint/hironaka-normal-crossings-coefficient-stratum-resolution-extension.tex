\section{The actual selected-zero append resolves the whole coefficient stratum}
\label{sec:nc-coefficient-stratum-resolution}

Selected and remaining components refer to the actual smooth connected
components of the original coefficient carrier. The selected components are
kept even when the coefficient ideal vanishes identically along them. The
finite construction below uses a real blowup of their whole strict union;
it does not assume an invariant-domain nonvanishing condition on that union.

\begin{theorem}[The entire selected strict union remains a genuine permissible centre]
\label{hir:nc-coefficient-selected-strict-geometry}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCoefficientStratumResolution.selected_strict_geometry}
\leanok
Let $L$ be well formed, $F$ its actual selected component union and $R$ its
remaining component union. Along ANY genuine $L$-test tower whose actual
centres lie in the strict transforms of $R$, the strict union $F_T$ is closed
smooth, transverse to the WHOLE current boundary, and contained in the SAME
current $L_T.\mathrm{cosupp}$. The entire current carrier is the disjoint
union $R_T\cup F_T$.
\uses{hir:smooth-selected-components,hir:dimtwo-unselected-smooth-components,hir:original-all-level-contact-remainder-nonzero-geometry,hir:wellformed-tower,bcalc:transform-off-centre}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Initially $F$ and $R$ are disjoint closed smooth component unions with union
$L.N$, and the defining selection gives $F\subseteq L.\mathrm{cosupp}$.
Strict transforms preserve their finite union and their disjointness. Near
any point of $F_T$, the complement of the closed $R_T$ identifies the entire
$F_T$ germ with the actual smooth current carrier germ. This proves smoothness
and inherits transversality to the whole current boundary from the genuine
well-formed transform of $L$.

Prove the cosupport inclusion by induction on the actual tower. At a next
centre, its containment in $R_T$ and disjointness from $F_T$ put every point
above $F_T$ off that centre. The genuine strict projection inclusion sends
it into $F_T$, where the induction hypothesis supplies actual current
cosupport membership. The true off-centre controlled-transform law then
supplies the next cosupport membership. This uses the SAME ideal and mark at
every stage, including all exceptional points of earlier blowups.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A positive-codimension smooth carrier has an internally constructed whole resolution]
\label{hir:nc-coefficient-zero-selected-append-resolution}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCoefficientStratumResolution.exists_resolution_of_positive_codimension_carrier}
\leanok
For a closed smooth marked carrier with coherent ideal, positive marking,
empty original boundary, compact original cosupport and a regular dimension
bound $k<n$, there is an actual finite resolution of the WHOLE marked datum.
Every actual centre lies in its literal strict carrier. No nonvanishing,
phase, maximum, or test-history certificate is an input.
\uses{hir:nc-coefficient-selected-strict-geometry,hir:nc-coefficient-remainder-finite-parent-phase,ana:blowups,bcalc:transform-off-centre}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true nonzero-remainder phase internally chooses a finite canonical terminal
remainder tower $P$, and derives genuine parent testness on the SAME tower.
Its actual final parent cosupport misses $R_P$. By the preceding theorem,
$C=F_P$ is a closed smooth centre in the SAME parent cosupport and transverse
to its whole accumulated boundary.

The genuine dimension bound for test-tower strict carriers is still $k<n$.
If $C$ had an interior point, it would be regular there of ambient dimension
$n$; inclusion into the current carrier would force $n\le k$, a contradiction.
Thus $C$ has empty interior, and the actual smooth-centre blowup exists, also
when $C$ is empty. Append it to $P$. All actual parent centre fields are now
proved, so this is a genuine parent test tower.

The current parent carrier is $R_P\cup C$. Blowing up the whole component
$C$ erases its strict transform. The new carrier is consequently the strict
transform of $R_P$. Any point of its new cosupport projects into $R_P$ and
lies off $C$, by the genuine strict projection inclusion and disjointness.
The true off-centre reverse cosupport law would put its image in the preceding
parent cosupport, contradicting its already proved avoidance of $R_P$.
The new whole cosupport is empty. The true parent test history itself supplies
all centre-in-carrier containments, so the asserted finite whole resolution
has been constructed from original data alone.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original marked NC data internally resolve the whole highest coefficient stratum]
\label{hir:nc-original-whole-coefficient-stratum-resolution}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCoefficientStratumResolution.exists_original_nc_coefficient_stratum_resolution}
\leanok
Let an original coherent ideal have positive marking and compact original
cosupport, and let an actual NC carrier have branch multiplicity everywhere
at most $r>0$. Set $Z=\{\operatorname{mult}_H\ge r\}$ and
$L=\mathcal C(M)|_Z$, where $M$ is the SAME whole-ambient empty-boundary original
marked datum. There is an internally constructed finite ACTUAL $L$-resolution
which is a genuine ORIGINAL $M$-test tower with every centre in the literal
strict transforms of $Z$, and
\[
 M_T.\mathrm{cosupp}\cap Z_T=\varnothing.
\]
\uses{hir:nc-coefficient-zero-selected-append-resolution,hir:general-nc-highest-multiplicity-smooth-centre,mc:coefficient-restriction-full,mc:coefficient-restriction-support}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true highest stratum is closed smooth of regular dimension $n-r$.
The actual coefficient ideal is coherent; its marking is the product of
the positive derivative markings, and its boundary is empty. Its entire
original cosupport is exactly the original marked cosupport intersected with
$Z$, hence is compact. If $n>0$, positivity of $r$ gives $n-r<n$, so the
preceding producer constructs the actual whole $L$-resolution. In dimension
zero the positive highest stratum is empty, since actual branch multiplicity
is at most zero; the identity tower is already such a resolution.

The full coefficient-restriction tower equivalence supplies true original
$M$-testness and all centres in $Z_T$ on the SAME tower. Its exact entire
cosupport dictionary identifies the empty terminal $L$-cosupport with
$M_T.\mathrm{cosupp}\cap Z_T$, proving the assertion. This clears the actual
marked cosupport from the strict highest stratum. It does not assert a smaller
GLOBAL old branch count away from the permitted cosupport, nor a subsequent
open-history extension or complete G9 iteration.
\end{proof}
