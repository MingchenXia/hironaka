\section{Actual canonical stages across empty original boundary positions}

For an original marked datum $M$, write $M^{\mathrm{ne}}$ for the same
entire record with its original boundary replaced by the literal ordered
list $M.E.\mathrm{filter}(\mathrm{Nonempty})$. Only empty positions are
deleted. No deletion history, index correspondence or final comparison
is supplied as input.

\begin{lemma}[Literal filtering preserves actual domains and whole test histories]
\label{hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-geometry}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.nonemptyBoundary,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.isInvDomain_nonemptyBoundary,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.isGeoTestTower_nonemptyBoundary_iff,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.cosupp_nonemptyBoundary_along,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.isGeoResolution_nonemptyBoundary_iff,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.filtered_boundary_nonemptyBoundary_along}
\uses{hir:original-invariant-empty-actual,hir:original-empty-boundary-test-history}
\leanok
Every original invariant domain of $M$ is an actual domain of
$M^{\mathrm{ne}}$. For every actual tower $T$, testness and terminality
for the two original records are equivalent, their whole transformed
cosupports agree, and their whole transformed boundary lists agree after
deleting precisely the empty members.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The filtered list is an actual sublist of the original ordered list.
The actual SNC charts and transverse charts restrict to those original
positions, while the closed submanifold, whole ideal, marking and
nonzero-along conditions are unchanged. Thus the actual domain is
preserved. To prove the history assertions without a supplied deletion
history, induct over the original finite list with an arbitrary retained
prefix. A nonempty head is retained and incorporated into that prefix.
An empty head is exactly insertion of one empty member at the length of
the prefix. The proved actual insertion test theorem, whole marked
transform theorem and cosupport theorem therefore remove that head.
This induction gives whole testness equivalence and whole cosupport
equality for the same actual $T$. Terminality is exactly their conjunction
with empty final cosupport. For the boundary assertion, actual insertion
commutes with the whole chronological marked transform, and filtering
the resulting empty member removes exactly that position. The same
finite-list induction proves equality of the final filtered lists,
retaining all nonempty positions in their original order.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual canonical centres and histories ignore only empty original positions]
\label{hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-canonical}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.isCanonical_nonemptyBoundary_iff,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.maxLocus_nonemptyBoundary}
\uses{hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-geometry,hir:original-invariant-empty-canonical-histories}
\leanok
In every ambient dimension, for every actual tower $T$ and subset $S$
of its final manifold, the whole maximum locus of the actual ambient
canonical invariant on $S$ is the same for $M^{\mathrm{ne}}$ and $M$.
Actual canonicality of $T$ for these two records is equivalent. The
whole invariant values themselves are not asserted equal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In positive ambient dimension the actual recursively constructed value
after one empty insertion is the proved strictly order preserving
embedding of the old value. Its whole maximum loci therefore agree,
and the proved actual canonical insertion theorem gives canonicality
equivalence for the same $T$. Apply the preceding genuine finite-list
deletion induction to these two assertions. In ambient dimension zero,
the actual canonical invariant is the constant base-case function.
Its maximum locus on every $S$ is independent of the boundary. Induction
through the actual tower, using the already proved whole cosupport
insertion equality at every prefix, gives canonicality equivalence.
The same finite-list deletion induction completes this dimension as
well. Thus no value-equality substitution or dimension-positivity guard
is hidden in the result.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Entire filtered boundaries of actual canonical terminal choices]
\label{hir:original-terminal-filtered-boundary-transport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.terminal_filtered_boundary_transport}
\uses{hir:original-empty-compression-filtered-boundary,hir:original-ambient-terminal-choice-intrinsic-unique-comparison,hir:original-empty-histories-filtered-boundary-comparison}
\leanok
For the same original invariant-domain record $M$, any two actual
canonical terminal towers and any actual over-base biholomorphism of
their final manifolds have equal whole ordered final boundaries after
pullback and deletion of exactly the empty members.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In positive dimension use genuine actual empty compression on each
history, retaining the whole filtered-boundary identities and final
cosupport identities. The compressed histories have only nonempty
centres, are canonical and remain terminal. Actual canonical terminal
tower uniqueness gives a genuine whole tower isomorphism between these
compressed histories, so whole marked-transform comparison gives their
entire boundary pullback equality. Compose this isomorphism with the
two actual compression biholomorphisms. The resulting map is over the
original base; intrinsic uniqueness of actual over-base biholomorphisms
identifies it with the supplied map. Pull back the two filtered-boundary
identities through these genuine bijections. Inverse image under a
surjection preserves nonemptiness, so filtering commutes with these
list maps. Cancellation of the bijective pullback proves the stated
entire ordered equality. In ambient dimension zero actual canonicality
and the original bound-zero domain force every actual centre to be
empty: the current cosupport is empty, and each centre is empty or that
whole maximum locus. The actual all-empty-tower boundary formula then
gives the same filtered-boundary identity directly from the projection
equation. No nonempty-stage certificate is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual terminal stages compare across literal empty-position changes]
\label{hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-terminal-choice}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.terminal_choice_across_empty_boundaries}
\uses{hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-geometry,hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-canonical,hir:original-terminal-filtered-boundary-transport,hir:original-ambient-terminal-choice-intrinsic-unique-comparison}
\leanok
Let two original marked records have the same closed-submanifold field,
whole ideal and marking, and equal literal ordered lists after filtering
only empty boundary members. Suppose the first is an actual invariant
domain. Any actual canonical terminal tower for each record internally
has a unique final over-base biholomorphism. That same map transports
their whole final ordered boundaries after deleting precisely empty
members. No stage comparison or insertion history is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two actual filtered records are literally equal in every field.
The first lemma gives their actual common domain and transfers whole
testness and terminality; the second transfers actual canonicality.
Apply actual terminal projection uniqueness to these same two towers
on the common filtered record, obtaining its unique actual over-base
biholomorphism. The preceding terminal-boundary lemma transports the
whole filtered transformed boundaries for that record. The first lemma's
same-history filtered-boundary identities replace both sides by the
corresponding whole boundaries for the original two records. Thus the
same genuine map has every asserted property.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual first-meeting stages compare across literal empty-position changes]
\label{hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-first-meeting-choice}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryEmptyFilterComparison.first_meeting_choice_across_empty_boundaries}
\uses{hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-geometry,hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-canonical,hir:original-first-meeting-prefix-map-unique,hir:original-first-meeting-filtered-boundary}
\leanok
Under the same natural original-record conditions, let two actual
canonical prefixes both avoid the same original open and let their next
whole actual maximum loci meet that open. Then their final projections
have a unique over-base biholomorphism, which transports the entire
ordered final boundaries after deleting exactly empty members.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Replace each initial record by its literal filtered record. These
records coincide in every field and have the actual common domain.
Canonicality transfers by the second theorem, while the same actual
towers and centres still avoid the same original open. The exact whole
maximum-locus and cosupport equalities transfer both actual next-maximum
encounters. Genuine original first-meeting projection uniqueness gives
the unique over-base biholomorphism of these same prefixes. Its actual
first-meeting boundary transport gives the ordered filtered-boundary
equality. Finally use the first lemma's whole same-history filtered
boundary identities to recover the boundary lists of the two original
records. No common stopping index or terminality assumption is added.
\end{proof}
