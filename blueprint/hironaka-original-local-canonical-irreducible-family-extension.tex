\section{Original local irreducible canonical families in every dimension}

The original irreducible analytic carrier need not be compact. Local restrictions
and smooth inverse images may be empty or disconnected. No connectedness of
these restrictions is asserted.

\begin{theorem}[The same local pure family has exact empty-boundary exceptional fields]
\label{hir:original-local-pure-same-family-exact-exceptional}
\uses{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-internal-source-producer}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.HasExactIndependentLocalPureBoundaryResolution,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.exists_local_original_pure_exact_family,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.exists_local_pure_empty_exact_family,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.exists_pure_empty_exact_family_near_compact}
\leanok
Let $W\subseteq X^n$ be original analytic, $d<n$, and
$W\subseteq\overline{\operatorname{Reg}_d W}$. Let $E_0$ be an original
ordered SNC boundary with $W\subseteq\overline{W\setminus|E_0|}$.
For every prescribed open $U$ contained in a compact subset of $X$, the
entire actual local pure canonical family can be chosen before all genuine
smooth sources, with every previously proved target, terminal-tail,
first-meeting, intrinsic independent-source, whole-boundary, strict-transform,
admissibility, SNC and Cartesian field retained. In addition, if $E_0=[]$,
the same chosen stopped target $T$ and every internally chosen stopped source
$S$ satisfy
\[
 \operatorname{Exc}(T)=|E_T|=T^{-1}(W_U\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_dW_U),\qquad
 \operatorname{Exc}(S)=|E_S|=S^{-1}(W'\setminus
   \operatorname{Reg}_{d+m-n}W').
\]
Here $W'=b^{-1}W_U$ and the dimension shift is interpreted at actual source
points, so empty sources require no global ambient-dimension inequality.
The result records actual canonical stages and adds no incoming history or
comparison certificate. The empty-boundary prescribed-open specialization
and the form with an internally chosen relatively compact neighbourhood of
any original compact set retain this entire same exact family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the original-only local pure producer to choose its actual full terminal
history, stopped target and tail before every source. Retain these same
witnesses and every one of their actual fields. When the original boundary is
empty, its restriction and every literal smooth inverse-image boundary are
empty, so dropping their lengths drops zero entries. The actual weak fields
therefore form the genuine cycle
$\operatorname{Exc}(T)\subseteq|E_T|\subseteq T^{-1}(F)
\subseteq\operatorname{Exc}(T)$, with
$F=W_U\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_dW_U$. Antisymmetry gives both exact
equalities. For each actual smooth source, keep the very same terminal
pullback, literal pulled prefix, internally independently stopped prefix and
unique comparison. Its actual weak source fields give the identical cycle
with its own shifted singular set. Apply antisymmetry there as well. No
witness is reselected and no incoming exceptional equality is used.
For the empty-boundary specialization, SNC and density off the boundary
are literal. For a compact-set input, local compactness supplies one
actual open with compact closure, then the prescribed-open construction
applies to that same open before every source.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original irreducible singular locus restricts literally]
\label{hir:original-local-irreducible-own-singular-locus}
\uses{hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-regular-locus,hir:original-local-pure-same-family-exact-exceptional}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.singular_locus_restrict,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.local_irreducible_proper_family}
\leanok
Let $V\subseteq X^n$ be an original closed irreducible analytic subvariety.
On every actual open $U$, putting $W_U=V\cap U$ gives the whole equality
\[
 W_U\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_{\dim V}W_U
   =\operatorname{val}^{-1}(V_{\rm sing}).
\]
If $\dim V<n$ and $U$ is contained in a compact subset, the entire exact
local pure family just proved is available for the same $W_U$ with empty
initial boundary. Thus its exact target and source exceptional fields can
also be read over the literal inverse image of the original $V_{\rm sing}$.
No irreducibility or connectedness of $W_U$ is required.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual open inclusion is a holomorphic submersion of relative dimension
zero. The genuine whole regular-locus pullback equality identifies its
entire $\dim V$ regular locus. Distribute inverse image over difference and
use the original definition of $V_{\rm sing}$ to obtain the displayed
literal equality. Original irreducibility supplies density of the original
regular locus in $V$. The empty boundary is SNC and its complement has the
original carrier as its closure. These original facts are all hypotheses of
the preceding local pure producer. Apply it to the same prescribed $U$.
The retained source singular-set pullback equality identifies every own
shifted singular set with the inverse image of this same restricted
original singular locus.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The full-dimensional local branch is the actual identity]
\label{hir:original-local-full-dimension-identity-family}
\uses{hir:original-full-dimension-identity-smooth-square}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.FullDimensionLocalCanonicalFamily,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.full_dimension_local_canonical_family}
\leanok
Let $\dim V=n$ and let $U\subseteq X$ be any actual open. The local
carrier $W_U$ has a literal zero-length identity blowup tower, empty final
boundary and exact exceptional identities over the restricted original
singular locus. Its whole conventional strict carrier is $W_U$, a closed
submanifold regular of dimension $n$, transverse to the empty boundary.
It retains admissibility, holomorphicity, properness, surjectivity and the
isomorphism over the whole base. For every actual holomorphic submersion
$b:Y^m\to U$, the literal identity source tower with upper arrow $b$ is the
actual Cartesian pullback. Its strict carrier is $b^{-1}W_U$, with the
whole smooth, empty-boundary, exceptional and map fields; it is regular
of dimension $m$ at all its points. No connectedness of $U$ or its carriers
is assumed, and no vanishing-ideal invariant domain is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original full-dimensional irreducible carrier is open and closed and
every one of its points is regular of dimension $n$; its original singular
locus is empty. Restriction to the actual open $U$ preserves both openness
and closedness. For the literal identity tower, conventional strict
transform is the closure of the carrier, hence the carrier itself.
Its open carrier is regular of the full ambient dimension. The empty
boundary gives SNC and transversality; the exceptional locus and the
original singular inverse image are empty. Identity admissibility,
properness, holomorphicity, surjectivity and the whole-base isomorphism
are literal actual map facts.

For every smooth source, continuity preserves closedness and openness,
and the literal identity source tower has exactly the same elementary
properties in dimension $m$. Its actual pullback is the reflexive
constructor of the genuine Cartesian tower relation, so the whole
projection square and its holomorphic universal property hold. Every
strict-set equality is the literal identity calculation. This proof
uses openness and closedness, never connectedness of a restriction or
a dimension inequality on an empty source.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[One original neighbourhood and actual family in every irreducible dimension]
\label{hir:original-local-all-dimension-irreducible-family}
\uses{hir:original-local-irreducible-own-singular-locus,hir:original-local-full-dimension-identity-family}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.exists_original_local_irreducible_canonical_family}
\leanok
For every original closed irreducible analytic subvariety $V\subseteq X^n$
and every compact $K\subseteq X$, there is one actual open neighbourhood
$U\supseteq K$ with compact closure and the corresponding full actual
local family: the same exact canonical pure target and all its intrinsic
independently stopped smooth sources when $\dim V<n$, or the literal
identity target and all actual smooth identity sources when $\dim V=n$.
The neighbourhood is fixed before the target and every source. No
compactness of $V$ or functorial choice of neighbourhood is asserted.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Local compactness supplies an actual open neighbourhood of the original
compact set with compact closure. The original dimension bound gives
$\dim V<n$ or $\dim V=n$. In the first case apply the exact local pure
family to the same chosen neighbourhood, using its compact closure as
the enclosure, together with the literal singular-locus restriction
proved above. In the second case use the actual local identity family
on that same neighbourhood. Both constructions depend only on the
original data and produce their targets before all source maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Own original dimension parts have the complete exact local family]
\label{hir:original-local-own-dimension-part-empty-exact-family}
\uses{hir:original-local-pure-same-family-exact-exceptional,hir:embedded-resolution-dimension-parts}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.exists_local_dimension_part_empty_exact_family,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.exists_dimension_part_empty_exact_family_near_compact}
\leanok
Let $W\subseteq X^n$ be any original analytic subset and $k<n$.
Its own intrinsic dimension part $W^{(k)}$, with its own reduced ideal
and pure-dimensional singular set, has the entire exact local pure
family with empty initial boundary. Both the prescribed compact-enclosed
open form and the form choosing one relatively compact neighbourhood of
any compact set are available. The same actual target and every
independently stopped smooth source retain the exact exceptional,
whole-boundary and own-singular inverse-image equalities. Neither
compactness nor empty interior of the whole original $W$ is assumed.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The actual intrinsic dimension part is analytic and is contained in the
closure of its own dimension-$k$ regular locus. These are genuine
original dimension-part facts, independent of other components or their
bad sets. Since $k<n$, apply the empty-boundary local pure producer to
this literal carrier and its own dimension label. For a prescribed open,
use its actual compact enclosure. For a compact-set input, use the
proved neighbourhood form of that same producer. All target and source
witnesses and every exact field belong to this own pure input, so no bad
set of the original whole mixed carrier is substituted.
\end{proof}
