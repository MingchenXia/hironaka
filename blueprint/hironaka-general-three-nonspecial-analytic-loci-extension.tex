\begin{lemma}[A genuine ambient open neighbourhood from two nested opens]
\label{hir:general-three-nested-open-analytic-transfer}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaGeneralThreeNonspecialAnalyticLoci.exists_ambient_open_analytic_transfer}
\leanok
For an actual open $U$ in a complex manifold and actual open $V$ in
$U$, its actual image under inclusion is an open $W$ of the original
manifold. Every analytic preimage of an original set on $V$ gives
the corresponding analytic preimage on this actual $W$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The real inclusion of open $U$ is an open map, so its actual image of
$V$ is open. Every point of this image has a unique lift to $U$ and
then to $V$, obtained from its actual image membership. Both lifts
are holomorphic: the open-subtype lifting theorem applies twice to
the actual inclusion, which is holomorphic. Their underlying original
point is unchanged. Holomorphic preimage of the real analytic set on
$V$ is therefore analytic on $W$; the exact unchanged-point identity
makes that preimage exactly the original set's true preimage on $W$.
No analyticity of an arbitrary image, closed-phase argument or descent
certificate is assumed.
\end{proof}
