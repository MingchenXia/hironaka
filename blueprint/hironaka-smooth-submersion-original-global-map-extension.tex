the preceding exact local formula.

biholomorphism to obtain its actual representative in the first local
final manifold. Its source leg equals the first lift's source leg by
the whole local range projection dictionary. Its target leg equals the

joint injectivity of the first genuine entire source-chart square identifies

source values. Thus all internally constructed local lifts agree on the

open. Its source leg puts its actual value in the same true saturated
source range. The genuine inverse gives a local representative with the

injectivity identifies the representatives and then the global source

global universal property is a caller input.
\end{proof}
