\begin{theorem}[Actual zero-level finite cuts internally supply the fixed lower formula]
\label{hir:original-finite-cut-zero-fixed-lower-value}
\uses{hir:original-finite-cut-specified-level-lower-data,hir:original-zero-curve-lower-value-rebase,hir:original-finite-stepone-cut-actual-middle-history,hir:step-one-compare}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFiniteCutFixedLowerValues.finite_cut_zero_stepone_eq_fixed_lower}
\leanok
Let the ORIGINAL $J$ lie in DOMAIN$(2)$ and have maximal order. Fix
$d\ge0$ and an actual finite original Step One cut history for the lower
invariant of depth $d+1$. At an actual nonspecial current-cosupport point
$z$ of level zero, form its ORIGINAL computed run, head $H$, tail $S$,
mask $W$ and lower reference $K$. At EVERY actual current-cosupport point
$p$ of $K$ along the actual restricted tail $S|W$, the ORIGINAL Step One
value is $(0,\operatorname{FULL}_{d+1}(K,S|W,p))$. No lower DOMAIN, TEST,
mask membership, tangent-direction comparison, all-centre avoidance,
CAN or numerical identity is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine finite cut gives an actual upper TEST and a level-cut history.
The specified-level lower-data producer supplies the real lower DOMAIN$(1)$,
TEST, current point and WHOLE cosupport dictionary at $z$, BEFORE any
numerical comparison. Thus each actual lower-cosupport point $p$ is in the
original upper cosupport and has level zero. The real mask/regime law makes
$p$ nonspecial with run at least the length of the tail $S$; the actual
run-length bound identifies this tail length with the run at $z$.
Apply the same lower-data producer independently at $p$. It internally
gives its own earlier actual lower DOMAIN, TEST, mask point and current
cosupport. The true head-regime law supplies the actual zero head level.

Enlarge only the original dimension witness from $2$ to $d+2$, keeping the
same well-formed record, positive mark and nonzero-along-carrier proof.
The genuine finite middle-history producer now constructs the real tower
between these two computed heads, BOTH exact concatenation identities,
and EVERY-centre avoidance of the earlier mask. The same upper TEST gives
the actual later head cosupport and final upper cosupport. Apply the actual
zero-level curve lower comparison to this constructed middle history.
All ordinary lower hypotheses are the independently constructed geometric
facts above. In particular actual tangent directions and their whole kept
contact records are produced by that comparison, rather than identified
by a certificate. Transport its ENTIRE quantified lower-value equality
along the two exact tower concatenation identities, keeping the actual
subtype endpoint and its proofs. Finally unfold the unchanged nonspecial
original Step One branch at $p$ and use its true zero level. This gives
precisely the asserted finite original value, without CAN.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual finite-cut computed lower reference gives the unchanged Step One value]
\label{hir:original-finite-cut-all-level-fixed-lower-value}
\uses{hir:original-finite-cut-zero-fixed-lower-value,hir:original-finite-cut-positive-fixed-lower-value}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFiniteCutFixedLowerValues.finite_cut_stepone_eq_fixed_lower}
\leanok
Under ONLY the same original DOMAIN$(2)$, maximal order, genuine finite
Step One cuts and actual nonspecial current-cosupport reference $z$, let
$K$ and its actual restricted tail be computed by the ORIGINAL current
level and regime run at $z$. At EVERY actual current-cosupport point $p$
of this $K$, the ORIGINAL Step One value equals its level at $z$ paired
with the ORIGINAL FULL value of this SAME fixed computed $K$ and tail at
$p$. This holds at ALL current levels, including zero. No original or
lower canonicality, lower TEST, point or numerical certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Regard the ENTIRE fixed-reference formula, including its genuinely computed
run, mask, lower record, restricted history and EVERY actual endpoint, as
one proposition indexed by a natural level. If the real level at $z$ is
zero, the first theorem proves that entire proposition at zero. Transport
it back along the actual level equality. Otherwise the actual natural
level has the form $s+1$ and the previously proved original positive finite
cut theorem supplies the entire proposition at that level. Transport the
WHOLE proposition back along this actual equality. These two exhaustive
cases prove the ORIGINAL unchanged function formula at all levels while
retaining the SAME real reference, history and endpoint. This closes the
fixed-lower numerical identity in carrier bound two; it does not prove
arbitrary-history canonicity, higher carrier bounds, maximal-locus analytic
semicoherence or a terminating global driver.
\end{proof}
