\section{Intrinsic complex morphisms have genuine local holomorphic lifts}

The input throughout is a genuine locally ringed morphism between the
constructed quotient-subspace models which commutes with the original
complex-point structural maps. No ambient extension is supplied.

\begin{theorem}[Local original representatives of intrinsic scalar pullbacks]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-intrinsic-scalar-lift}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-local-ambient-section,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-scalar-map-point-values}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceIntrinsicLifting.actualSubspaceMap_local_scalar_lift}
\leanok
Let $F:(C,\mathcal O_C)\to(D,\mathcal O_D)$ be such a morphism, where
$C\subset M$ and $D\subset N$ are actual subsets of complex manifolds.
For every original global holomorphic section $a$ of the ambient target
and every $x\in C$, there are an actual ambient neighbourhood $O$ of $x$
and an original holomorphic section $t$ on $O$ such that
$t(y)=a(F(y))$ for every $y\in C\cap O$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Pull back $a$ by the composite of $F$ with the genuine target inclusion.
This is a section of the genuine source quotient sheaf on the actual
preimage of the whole target, which contains $x$. The whole local ambient
representative theorem constructs $O$ and $t$, retaining equality of the
entire germs at every point of $C\cap O$. Apply genuine residue evaluation
to that equality. Actual stalk-map composition and the actual germ formula
identify its right side with the pullback under $F$ of the target inclusion
germ of $a$. Complex-point compatibility gives intrinsic evaluation
naturality. The original target inclusion numerator formula identifies the
remaining evaluation with $a(F(y))$. The original source numerator formula
identifies the other side with $t(y)$, proving the whole-neighbourhood
pointwise identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual local ambient holomorphic lift and whole intrinsic morphism square]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-intrinsic-local-holomorphic-lift}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-intrinsic-scalar-lift,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-intrinsic-map-faithful,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-open-restriction-iso,hir:reduced-analytic-complex-point-models}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceIntrinsicLifting.exists_actualSubspaceMap_local_holomorphic_lift}
\leanok
Let $C\subset M$, let $V\subset\mathbf C^m$ be an actual open and
$D\subset V$. Let $F$ be any genuine complex-point-compatible locally
ringed morphism from the actual model of $C$ to the actual model of $D$.
For every $x\in C$ there exist an actual ambient open neighbourhood
$O\subset M$ of $x$ and a genuine holomorphic map $f:O\to V$, carrying
$C\cap O$ into $D$, such that the entire induced quotient-sheaf morphism
is exactly the restriction of $F$ by the actual open-subspace inclusion.
In particular the equality holds for the whole structure-sheaf maps,
not just their continuous base maps or residue values.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each of the finitely many standard coordinate functions on $V$ is an
original global holomorphic section. Apply the preceding theorem to these
sections and intersect their actual ambient neighbourhoods. Their original
holomorphic representatives assemble, through the genuine finite-dimensional
linear identification of coordinate tuples with $\mathbf C^m$, into a map
$g$ holomorphic at every point of that intersection. At every point of its
intersection with $C$, coordinate equality proves $g(y)=F(y)$ in the ambient
$\mathbf C^m$. In particular $g(x)\in V$. Continuity at $x$ and openness
of $V$ give a genuine smaller ambient open neighbourhood on which $g$ takes
values in $V$. Restrict $g$ there to obtain $f:O\to V$. Its holomorphy
follows from that of $g$ and the actual open-subtype smoothness criterion.
For $y\in C\cap O$, the proved equality $f(y)=F(y)$ puts $f(y)$ in $D$.

The quotient-sheaf morphism induced by $f$ is compatible with the actual
complex point. So is the composite of the actual open-subspace inclusion
with $F$, by the same genuine structural triangles and functoriality.
Their continuous base maps agree at every source point by the proved
coordinate identities. Intrinsic model-map faithfulness therefore equates
the entire locally ringed morphisms, yielding the asserted whole square.
An empty coordinate family ($m=0$) gives the whole first intersection and
the unique map to $\mathbf C^0$; the same continuity and faithfulness
arguments apply without a nonzero-dimension assumption.
\end{proof}

This proves local ambient lifting of arbitrary intrinsic complex morphisms
between the actual reduced embedded models. Effective resolution descent
on arbitrary abstract analytic spaces and its terminal resolution driver
remain separate obligations.
