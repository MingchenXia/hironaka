\section{Actual canonical stages of the ordered part recursion}

The objects below retain actual finite blowup towers and their literal
ordered boundary lists. A canonical sum stage means a terminal canonical
history of the actual mark-one sum of the two original vanishing ideals,
with its genuine invariant-domain proof. It does not mean a canonical
history of the union of the two parts.

\begin{definition}[A finite ordered canonical separator chain]
\label{hir:original-canonical-ordered-separator-chain}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-sum-intrinsic-source-record}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalPartListResolution.CanonicalSeparationChain}
\leanok
For an original ordered boundary $E$, head carrier $A$ and labelled
list $L$, an actual ordered canonical separator chain is defined
recursively. For $L=[]$ it is the identity tower with boundary $E$.
For $L=(B,e)::L_0$, first take an actual canonical terminal sum stage
for $(I_A+I_B,E,1)$. Continue with the strict transform of $A$, the
ordered strict transforms of $L_0$, and the entire actual accumulated
boundary of this sum stage. The chain is the literal append of the
first stage and this recursively constructed tail. The dimension
labels are retained at every stage, including empty-carrier labels.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Original good parts internally construct the same canonical ordered separator and smooth source]
\label{hir:original-canonical-ordered-separator-producer}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-ordered-separator-chain,hir:original-canonical-sum-selected-terminal-separator,hir:original-canonical-sum-same-separator-smooth-square,hir:original-stopped-strict-transform-smooth-square,hir:actual-smooth-cartesian-tower-append}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalPartListResolution.exists_canonical_ordered_separator}
\leanok
Let $E$ be an original SNC list, $G$ closed, $(A,d)$ an original
compact good part relative to $(E,G)$, and $L$ an original finite
ordered list of such good parts. Suppose $A\cap B\subseteq G$ for
every carrier $B$ in $L$. These original data internally construct an
actual finite ordered canonical separator chain $T$ and its whole
boundary $E_T$. It is admissible over $G$, has SNC boundary, and its
head strict transform is disjoint from every strict tail part.

After fixing this same $T$, every genuine holomorphic submersion
$b:Y^m\to X^n$ internally supplies an actual source tower $Q$ and
upper submersion $g$ with holomorphic Cartesian projection square.
The source is an ordered canonical separator chain for the intrinsic
pulled carriers and dimension labels shifted by $m-n$, its entire
boundary is literally $g^{-1}E_T$, and it is admissible over $b^{-1}G$.
The whole strict transform of the head and of each original list
part is the $g$-inverse image of its target strict transform. In
particular their actual source strict transforms are disjoint.
No source compactness or incoming finite history is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the ordered list length. The empty list uses the actual
identity tower; its smooth source is the actual identity square.
For $(B,e)::L_0$, original good-part hypotheses give genuine analytic
carriers, empty interior of $A$, compact $A\cap B$ and density of
each carrier away from the other. Apply the canonical sum separator
once, fixing its actual target tower before any source is chosen.
Its actual centres lie over $A\cap B\subseteq G$. The admissible
strict-transform theorem carries every remaining original good part
to a compact good part relative to the same whole accumulated SNC
boundary and the inverse image of $G$. Apply the induction hypothesis
to this actual transformed ordered list and head. Append the actual
towers. Admissibility composes. The first separated pair stays
disjoint under all later strict transforms; the induction hypothesis
gives disjointness from the remaining parts.

For a given $b$, construct the actual source of the first sum stage
using its proved intrinsic sum-record dictionary and actual universal
blowup square. Apply the induction hypothesis to its actual upper
submersion. Append these actual source towers; genuine actual-pullback
append gives a Cartesian square for the same full target append.
Every original part is dense away from $G$, and the actual target
centres lie over $G$. Thus the genuine preserved-open strict-square
theorem identifies all whole source strict transforms, including
exceptional points. It also identifies the source labels used by
the next separator stage with the intrinsic pulls of those target
labels. Literal ordered boundary pullback identifies the entire
intermediate boundaries. These actual equalities let the source
canonical sum stages and recursive chain be appended with their true
intrinsic inputs. The projection identity identifies the recursive
source bad sets, so the source admissible sequences compose over
$b^{-1}G$. Final whole boundary equality follows from the last
recursive stage, and source disjointness follows by inverse image
of the proved target disjointness. All choices were internal and
all original stage positions are retained.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This is the genuine ordered finite separation subroutine. The next
mixed-dimensional resolution step must resolve the separated head,
cut the later bad set down to the remaining parts, and iterate while
retaining this same composite map. No assertion that such a composite
is one canonical history of the whole mixed-dimensional ideal is
made here.
\end{remark}
