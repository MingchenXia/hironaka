\section{The actual convergent Taylor coefficients reconstruct the original function}

\begin{theorem}[Actual formal polynomial evaluation is the original polynomial evaluation]
\label{hir:actual-taylor-polynomial-evaluation}
\uses{hir:actual-polydisc-evaluation-additive-algebra}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualTaylorReconstruction.evaluation_polynomial}
\leanok
For every genuine finite polynomial $P$ and every original coordinate
tuple $z$, actual series evaluation of its true formal coercion is
exactly the original polynomial evaluation $P(z)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true formal coefficients equal the original polynomial coefficients
and vanish outside its genuine finite support. Thus the actual infinite
sum reduces to precisely that finite support sum. Its monomial products
over all coordinates equal the products over each actual exponent's
support, since omitted exponents are zero. This is the original
polynomial evaluation formula, without any radius or convergence premise.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual absolute evaluation regroups into genuine homogeneous polynomial bands]
\label{hir:actual-taylor-homogeneous-evaluation-regrouping}
\uses{hir:actual-taylor-polynomial-evaluation,hir:actual-monomial-owner-polydisc-convergence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualTaylorReconstruction.evaluation_eq_sum_homogeneous_bands}
\leanok
Suppose $F_\alpha=(P_{|\alpha|})_\alpha$ and every genuine polynomial
$P_k$ is homogeneous of degree $k$. If $F$ has finite actual coefficient
mass at $\rho$, then at every point of the closed polydisc its actual
evaluation is $\sum_k P_k(z)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual absolute summability permits regrouping the unchanged monomial
sum by its genuine total-degree fibres. On the degree-$k$ fibre the
literal coefficient dictionary identifies each summand with that of
$P_k$. Homogeneity kills every coefficient of $P_k$ outside this
fibre, so its restricted sum equals its entire formal evaluation.
The preceding theorem identifies this with the original finite
polynomial evaluation. The genuine fibrewise sum theorem proves
the entire asserted equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original holomorphy produces a convergent Taylor sum equal to the same function]
\label{hir:actual-original-taylor-function-reconstruction}
\uses{hir:actual-taylor-homogeneous-evaluation-regrouping,hir:actual-multilinear-coordinate-polynomial,hir:actual-original-taylor-multilinear-coefficient,hir:actual-original-taylor-positive-coefficient-mass}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualTaylorReconstruction.originalTaylor_evaluation_eventually}
\leanok
For every original function $f$ holomorphic near zero, its already
constructed original Taylor algebra map has internally produced
positive uniform coefficient radius, and its literal convergent
evaluation equals that same original function throughout some
genuine neighbourhood of zero. No analytic expansion, coefficient
dictionary, radius, summability or reconstruction equation is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original holomorphy gives an actual analytic multilinear expansion
$p$ at zero. The proved unconditional original-germ mass theorem
produces a positive uniform coefficient radius for this same
original Taylor map. The original Taylor coefficient dictionary
identifies its degree bands with the genuine homogeneous coordinate
polynomials of $p$. On a neighbourhood of zero all original coordinate
norms lie within the produced radius, by continuity of the finitely
many coordinate projections and positivity. The preceding regrouping
theorem therefore writes the actual Taylor evaluation as the sum
of these same coordinate polynomials. Each original polynomial
evaluates to the genuine multilinear diagonal $p_k(z,\ldots,z)$.
On a possibly smaller neighbourhood the original analytic expansion
has sum exactly $f(z)$. Intersecting these two genuine neighbourhood
conditions proves the asserted equality for the unchanged original
function and original Taylor map.
\end{proof}

This proves faithful evaluation reconstruction for original Taylor
images. Realizing every arbitrary convergent formal series as a germ
with that exact original Taylor image, analytic reduced-basis transport,
uniform current-history HS tracking and termination remain further steps.
