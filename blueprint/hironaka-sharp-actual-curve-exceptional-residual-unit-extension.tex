\section{Actual chronological curve residuals are units at EVERY accumulated exceptional point}

Remove the FIRST-good hypothesis from the exceptional-point assertion.
On a real smooth curve transverse to the actual accumulated SNC boundary,
each incident member cuts out an isolated point. A genuine no-factor
witness therefore cannot vanish at that point. Apply this to the
independently constructed chronological ideals; no order-one, reduced
trace, previously-good point or unit certificate is assumed.

\begin{lemma}[A true transverse member cuts an actual pure curve in an isolated point]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-transverse-curve-member-isolating-open}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0088}
\uses{bcalc:member-intersection-along,hir:sharp-original-member-product-actual-normal-form}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveExceptionalResidualUnit.exists_actual_curve_member_isolating_open}
\leanok
For actual regular dimension one of $N$ at $x$, an actual SNC boundary
transverse to $N$, and a true incident boundary member $H$, internally
construct a genuine ambient open neighborhood $U$ with
$(N\cap H)\cap U\subset\{x\}$. No intersection-dimension or isolated
trace certificate is supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The real simultaneous carrier/SNC normal form supplies the ACTUAL carrier
plane with cutoff $k$ and a free incident coordinate of index less than
$k$. Its actual regular dimension is $\min(k,n)$, by real flattening.
Uniqueness of actual regular dimension compares it to the given original
curve dimension, forcing $\min(k,n)=1$. The incident free index is then
zero. At any true chart point of $N\cap H$, coordinate zero vanishes by
membership in $H$, while every other actual coordinate vanishes by the
carrier-plane equation. Its actual flattened point is therefore zero,
as is the original point's. The genuine flattening and original-chart
inverse laws on their TRUE domains identify it with $x$. Their real
common flat-domain open is the required isolating neighborhood.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A genuine no-factor witness on the curve forces a real unit at an incident transverse member]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-no-factor-member-unit}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0088}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-transverse-curve-member-isolating-open,bcalc:member-trace-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveExceptionalResidualUnit.actual_curve_no_factor_member_unit}
\leanok
Let $I$ be any actual coherent ideal on a genuine smooth carrier $N$
regular of dimension one at an incident boundary point. An actual germ
of $I$ outside the TRUE relative member-ideal span internally gives
$1\in I_x$. No nonzero value, order bound, reduced support, FIRST-good
hypothesis or unit certificate is input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose the genuine no-factor witness $a\in I_x$. If $a(x)=0$, the real
isolating neighborhood of the preceding lemma makes $a$ vanish on the
literal trace $N\cap H$ near $x$. Actual holomorphicity of that SAME
member and the independently proved BOTH-direction transverse member
trace-ideal dictionary then place $a$ in the actual relative span of the
boundary ideal, contrary to its genuine witness property. Hence $a(x)$
is nonzero. Its true holomorphic inverse and real coherent ideal-span
closure give $1\in I_x$. No relative principality or assumed residual
unit is used in this argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ONLY original curve inputs make every chronological member a unit over the WHOLE accumulated exceptional family]
\label{hir:sharp-original-curve-exceptional-all-residual-units-first-defect-disjoint}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0088}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-no-factor-member-unit,hir:sharp-actual-template-history-pure-regular-dimension,hir:sharp-original-all-member-finite-chronological-no-exceptional-factor,hir:sharp-original-chronological-full-product-geometry-order-sum}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveExceptionalResidualUnit.original_curve_exceptional_residual_units_and_defect_disjoint}
\leanok
ONLY original smooth pure curve, original ambient SNC boundary,
whole-original-boundary density and ANY actual finite template history
internally give this at EVERY actual current carrier point of the ENTIRE
literal accumulated exceptional family: EVERY original chronological
member contains the genuine unit germ, the actual full chronological
product has ordinary trace order zero, and the point lies outside literal
current FIRST Z. No previously-FIRST-good, member-unit, factor, current
curve or selected history certificate is supplied. Empty original and
exceptional lists and empty steps are retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual finite template wellformedness gives the SAME real upper carrier,
its actual transverse accumulated SNC list, and its closedness. True
original curve purity transports through the SAME actual history. For
each original position, independent chronological geometry supplies its
actual coherence, and true chronological continuation gives a genuine
no-common-factor witness for EVERY incident actual accumulated position.
The preceding producer therefore constructs its actual unit at every
such point, including points not assumed FIRST-good.

Induct over the actual full chronological member list: units in both
actual factors give the true unit in their ideal product, and actual
carrier normalization preserves it. The empty product is the original
unit ideal. The real full-product ordinary-order dictionary now excludes
order at least one by testing its actual unit germ, so its ordinary order
is zero. Since the true distinct proper-germ count is nonnegative, it
cannot be strictly smaller than this zero order; the literal FIRST-defect
inequality therefore fails. FIRST Z is consequently disjoint from the
ENTIRE actual accumulated exceptional family in this true curve scope.
This does not erase Z elsewhere, identify it with Sigma, prove off-centre
FIRST-defect transport, a full sharp driver or higher-dimensional
termination.
\end{proof}
