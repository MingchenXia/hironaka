\section{Actual analytic centre powers and exceptional quotients from local coefficient cosupport}

The coefficient functions are the actual relative coefficients of genuine analytic
germs. We use their literal coefficients, not an unproved identification with
unnormalized derivatives. The construction concerns the coefficient-centre step
of \cite[Theorem 9.4]{bierstone-milman97-invent-math}; arbitrary prescribed formal
reduced vertices and the full transformed presentation remain separate.

\begin{lemma}[Whole analytic and formal tail-coordinate powers have the same original members]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-formal-tail-coordinate-power-contraction}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-normal-power-contracts-exact-original-analytic-power,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-exceptional-quotient-formal-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientAnalyticBlowup.actualTangentCentreVariables_eq_tail,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientAnalyticBlowup.mem_analytic_tail_pow_iff_actualTaylor_mem,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientAnalyticBlowup.actualPresentationCentreVariables_eq_tail}
\leanok
For every $k\le n$, let $A=\{i\in\{0,\ldots,n-1\}:k\le i\}$. The inverse
image under the genuine whole Taylor map of the formal coordinate ideal
power $(x_i:i\in A)^q$ is exactly the $q$-th power of the actual analytic
coordinate-germ ideal with these same generators. The three-block
presentation centre $(y,z)$, in coordinates $(u,y,z)$, is literally this
tail-coordinate centre with cutoff $\dim u$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $n=k+t$. A finite index belongs to the range of the original inclusion
$i\mapsto k+i$ precisely when its value is at least $k$: the reverse witness
is its value minus $k$, which lies below $t$. Consequently the selected
coordinate set is exactly the original standard normal-coordinate set.
The analytic ideal is the span of those same original coordinate germs.
The previously proved entire normal-power contraction therefore proves the
claimed equivalence for every germ and every $q$. For the three-block centre,
split an index into the $(u,y)$ and $z$ blocks. In the first block membership
means its value is at least $\dim u$, by the same range calculation; every
index in the $z$ block satisfies this bound and is a centre variable.
This proves equality of the actual coordinate sets, including empty blocks.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[True local coefficient cosupport supplies the whole actual analytic centre power]
\label{hir:actual-local-coefficient-cosupport-analytic-centre-power}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-formal-tail-coordinate-power-contraction,hir:actual-local-coefficient-presentation-centre-power,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-literal-formal-taylor-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientAnalyticBlowup.mem_analyticPresentationCentre_pow_of_local_coefficient_cosupport}
\leanok
Let $g(u,y,z)$ be a genuine analytic germ. On a real open neighbourhood
$U$ of the origin choose actual representatives $c_\gamma(u,y)$ of its
original relative coefficient germs. For every $|\gamma|<q$ suppose
$c_\gamma$ belongs at zero to an original ideal $\mathfrak a_\gamma$
coherent on $U$, and
\[
 U\cap\{y=0\}\subset\operatorname{cosupp}
  (\mathfrak a_\gamma,q-|\gamma|).
\]
Then $g$ belongs to the entire actual analytic ideal $(y,z)^q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The literal whole Taylor coefficient dictionary identifies the normal
coefficient of the Taylor series of $g$ with the Taylor series of the
chosen original coefficient germ. The already proved real-domain local
coefficient-cosupport theorem therefore puts the entire Taylor series of
$g$ in the actual formal presentation-centre power $(y,z)^q$. The preceding
entire inverse-image identity, and the literal equality of the presentation
centre with the tail-coordinate centre, now place the original analytic
germ itself in the whole analytic power. This uses neither a supplied
analytic power nor convergence of an arbitrary formal member.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite analytic families internally produce coefficient data and genuine exceptional quotients]
\label{hir:actual-finite-coefficient-centre-analytic-exceptional-quotients}
\uses{hir:actual-finite-local-coherent-coefficient-ideals,hir:actual-local-coefficient-cosupport-analytic-centre-power,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-exceptional-quotient-formal-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientAnalyticBlowup.exists_actual_finite_coefficient_analytic_blowup}
\leanok
For every finite actual analytic family $g_i(u,y,z)$ and bound $q$, one
can choose a real common low-coefficient holomorphic neighbourhood, actual
relative coefficient representatives and their genuine coherent principal
germ ideals internally. If the actual centre in that neighbourhood lies
in each of the low coefficient cosupports of weight $q-|\gamma|$, then
in every genuine coordinate chart with exceptional index belonging to
the centre, the actual analytic pullback of $g_i$ has an actual analytic
quotient by the exceptional coordinate to power $q$. Its whole Taylor
series satisfies the exact normalized formal exceptional-product identity.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the proved finite actual coefficient construction. It gives genuine
representatives and their real common domain, original principal-ideal
coherence and original membership without assuming them. Its stable finite
Taylor polynomial coefficients identify those same functions with every
original relative coefficient germ. Under the stated ordinary cosupport
containments, the previous theorem puts $g_i$ in the whole actual analytic
centre power. Genuine analytic exceptional division then constructs its
holomorphic quotient with the actual product identity. Applying the true
Taylor homomorphism, its literal coordinate dictionary and its whole
blowup naturality gives the displayed formal exceptional-product identity.
The exceptional index is required to belong to the centre; no exceptional
chart is fabricated for an empty centre. The formal exceptional variable
is nonzero, so this product identity also uniquely determines the existing
normalized formal member.
\end{proof}
