\section{Independent actual separation chains with empty labelled parts}

Every chain below is the literal original chronological chain of canonical
terminal stages for the successive original sum vanishing ideals. Its
whole accumulated boundary is retained, including empty positions. The
head is fixed, while labelled tail parts may contain empty sets in arbitrary
positions. The input lists are compared only after deleting these empty
carriers, with all surviving labels and their order retained.

\begin{lemma}[Whole strict carriers and genuine closed-carrier list normal forms]
\label{hir:original-separation-chain-whole-strict-normal-form}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSeparationChainChoice.strictTransform_closure_eq,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSeparationChainChoice.strictTransform_preimage_of_projection_square,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSeparationChainChoice.closedParts}
\uses{ana:strict-transform-composition}
\leanok
The ordinary strict transform under any actual holomorphic map depends
only on the closure of its original carrier. Consequently any genuine
biholomorphic square of actual projections transports the whole ordinary
strict set of every carrier. Replacing each labelled carrier by its true
closure preserves its label and nonemptiness. These closed-carrier lists
commute with original biholomorphic inverse images and actual successive
strict transforms after the latter are closed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Compose the actual holomorphic map with the identity on its base. The
proved strict-transform composition formula says its strict transform
equals the strict transform of the identity's strict set. The identity's
differential exceptional locus is empty, so that strict set is exactly
the original closure. This proves the first assertion. For a genuine
biholomorphic projection square apply the known whole strict-square
formula to that original closure. A homeomorphism commutes with closure
and its inverse image; applying the first assertion on both source and
target removes those closures and gives the assertion for every carrier.
Closure does not change nonemptiness, and a strict transform is already
closed by its original definition. Apply these whole set equalities at
every literal labelled list position. Labels and order do not change.
This normal form is derived from the actual sets; it is not an input
tracking or deletion certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[An empty labelled sum stage has its actual unique neutral projection]
\label{hir:original-separation-chain-empty-sum-domain-projection}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSeparationChainChoice.empty_sum_stage_projection}
\uses{hir:original-empty-sum-stage-unique-projection-neutrality,hir:original-ambient-terminal-choice-intrinsic-unique-comparison}
\leanok
Let an actual chain stage have original analytic head $A$, an empty
tail carrier $B$, its original sum marked datum $M$, its genuine domain,
and an actual canonical stage tower $T$. Its projection is an actual
biholomorphism $\beta$, and every original closed strict set is its
literal inverse image. The entire output boundary is
$M.E.\mathrm{map}(\beta^{-1})$ followed by exactly $T.length$ empty
born positions. Its filtered boundary is precisely the filtered actual
pullback of the original boundary. The genuine domain already supplied
by the chain is used; empty interior of $A$ is not an extra premise.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Coherence and marking one identify the actual cosupport with the whole
zero locus of the original sum ideal, which is $A\cap B=\varnothing$.
The original domain and actual canonicality therefore force every actual
centre empty and make the same stage terminal. Compare this actual
terminal tower with the actual identity tower for the same sum datum.
Genuine terminal projection uniqueness gives its unique actual
biholomorphism $\beta$, equal to the actual projection at every point.
Its differential exceptional locus is empty, so the original strict
transform of a closed set is its actual inverse image. The whole
all-empty-tower boundary formula retains every original member and
appends one empty member per actual chronological step. Filtering those
empty born members gives the last assertion. No different stage or
projection has been substituted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Independent original separation chains have one whole comparison over the prescribed base]
\label{hir:original-canonical-separation-chain-filtered-choice}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSeparationChainChoice.separation_chain_choice_over_base_closedParts,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSeparationChainChoice.separation_chain_choice_over_base}
\uses{hir:original-separation-chain-whole-strict-normal-form,hir:original-separation-chain-empty-sum-domain-projection,hir:original-empty-boundary-terminal-base-comparison,hir:original-actual-whole-projection-biholomorphic-map-uniqueness}
\leanok
Let $\Phi:X'\simeq X$ be an actual original biholomorphism. Suppose
two actual canonical separation chains have fixed heads $\Phi^{-1}A,A$,
original boundaries agreeing after actual $\Phi$-pullback and deletion
of empty members, and labelled tail lists whose ordered nonempty
carriers and labels agree by the same inverse image. Their actual final
manifolds internally have a unique biholomorphism $\eta$ over $\Phi$.
That same map transports the entire ordered final boundary after deleting
precisely empty members, and transports every whole original closed
strict set. No chain comparison, insertion history, common stage count
or nonempty-stage list is input.
More generally, the same conclusion holds when the ordered nonempty
labelled lists agree after replacing every carrier by its actual closure.
No closedness premise on the deeper raw entries is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First prove the stronger induction statement comparing the genuine
closed-carrier normal forms of the labelled tails. Induct strongly on
the sum of the two original raw tail lengths. If both lists are empty,
their actual chains are identity towers, and the required map is
exactly $\Phi$; the stated original filtered-boundary identity gives
the boundary assertion.

If the source raw head is empty, its actual first sum stage has the
genuine neutral projection $\beta$ of the preceding lemma. Its actual
remaining chain has the strictly smaller raw tail. Regard its base
biholomorphism as $\Phi\beta$. The actual closed source head pulls back
exactly, the whole boundary differs by only the proved empty born
positions, and the first lemma transports the genuine closed-carrier
tail normal form. Apply the induction hypothesis to this same remaining
source chain and the unchanged target chain. The actual projection
equation for $\beta$ turns its final square into the required square for
the original full source chain. If instead the target raw head is empty,
perform the symmetric construction with the actual target neutral
projection and base map $\beta^{-1}\Phi$. Again one raw length decreases,
and the actual projection equation recovers the original full square.

Otherwise both raw heads are nonempty. The equality of ordered filtered
closed-carrier lists identifies their labels and their actual current
analytic carriers by $\Phi$; these current carriers are closed by their
actual chain constructors. The original whole sum-pullback dictionary
identifies the submanifold, whole ideal and marking of the two first
stages, and their initial boundaries have the stated filtered equality.
The genuine base-biholomorphism terminal-stage comparison therefore
constructs an actual first-stage map $\chi$ with its entire ordered
filtered-boundary identity. The first lemma transports the actual strict
head and the whole labelled closed-carrier tails through $\chi$. Apply
the induction hypothesis to the two actual remaining chains over
$\chi$; both raw lengths decrease. Composing the two actual projection
equations proves the required square of the original entire appended
chains, and the actual final filtered-boundary assertion is exactly
that of the induction hypothesis.

Finally the public literal ordered nonempty-tail hypothesis implies the
closed-carrier normal-form hypothesis by the first lemma, so no added
closedness assumption on deeper raw entries is needed. Any two actual
final maps with the proved original projection square are equal by
actual tower projection uniqueness. Applying the whole strict-square
formula to this same map gives the asserted whole closed strict sets.
Thus the map, boundary and strict-set assertions all concern the same
original chain projections.
\end{proof}
