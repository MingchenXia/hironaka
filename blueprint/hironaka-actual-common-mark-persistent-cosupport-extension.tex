\begin{theorem}[The same weighted family gives exact common-mark control]
\label{hir:actual-common-mark-weighted-family-control}
\uses{hir:actual-weighted-coefficient-compression-transform}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCommonMarkPersistentCosupport.map_span_common_mark_factor,Hironaka.HironakaActualCommonMarkPersistentCosupport.colon_span_common_mark_eq,Hironaka.HironakaActualCommonMarkPersistentCosupport.colon_span_common_mark_le_normalized_span}
\leanok
Let $f:R\to S$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings, let $S$ be a domain, and let $e\ne0$. Suppose the same families $G_a,H_a$ satisfy $e^{w_a}H_a=f(G_a)$, and $m\le w_a$ for every $a$. Then
\[
 f((G_a)_a)=(e^m)(e^{w_a-m}H_a)_a,
 \qquad (f((G_a)_a):e^m)=(e^{w_a-m}H_a)_a\subseteq(H_a)_a.
\]
The family may be empty, and $m$ may be zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each unchanged index $a$, the equality $m+(w_a-m)=w_a$ gives $f(G_a)=e^m(e^{w_a-m}H_a)$. Mapping an entire generated ideal gives the ideal generated by the images, and multiplication by a principal ideal gives the ideal generated by its products with those same generators. This proves the first equality in both directions. A ring element $y$ is in the displayed colon precisely when $e^my=e^mz$ for some $z$ in the residual generated ideal. Since $e^m$ is nonzero in the domain, cancellation gives $y=z$. Conversely each such $z$ has its required product in the original mapped ideal. Every residual generator is a multiple of the corresponding unchanged $H_a$, proving the inclusion. These arguments also apply to empty spans and to $e^0=1$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine Hilbert--Samuel profile detects every whole point-power inclusion]
\label{hir:actual-hs-profile-point-power-detection}
\uses{hir:actual-hilbert-samuel-point-value,hir:actual-whole-point-quotient-jet-rank,hir:actual-finite-linear-jet-kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCommonMarkPersistentCosupport.le_maximal_pow_iff_hilbertSamuelValue_eq_bot,Hironaka.HironakaActualCommonMarkPersistentCosupport.le_maximal_pow_iff_of_entire_hs_eq}
\leanok
Let $I,J$ be original analytic germ ideals at the same model origin, with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$. For every $q\ge0$, equality of their entire genuine Hilbert--Samuel profiles implies
\[
 I\subseteq\mathfrak m^q\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad J\subseteq\mathfrak m^q.
\]
For $q=\ell+1$, either condition is detected by the single actual equality $H_I(\ell)=H_0(\ell)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The $q=0$ inclusion is automatic. For $q=\ell+1$, the kernel of the genuine finite derivative-jet map is exactly $\mathfrak m^q$. Thus $I\subseteq\mathfrak m^q$ is equivalent to the entire jet image of $I$ being zero. The actual quotient-rank formula gives $H_I(\ell)=\dim V-\dim W_I$, where $V$ is the range of that finite jet map and $W_I$ is the entire ideal image inside $V$. The actual jet target is finite dimensional, so $\dim W_I\le\dim V$. The zero ideal has zero image and $H_0(\ell)=\dim V$. Equality with that value therefore forces $\dim W_I=0$, and finite dimensionality forces $W_I=0$. This proves the one-order characterization in both directions. Equal profiles transfer that equality between $I$ and $J$ and hence transfer every whole point-power inclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual centre admissibility internally supplies the entire common-mark ideal]
\label{hir:actual-common-mark-analytic-centre-quotients}
\uses{hir:actual-common-mark-weighted-family-control,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-exceptional-quotient-formal-dictionary,hir:actual-analytic-normalized-member-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCommonMarkPersistentCosupport.exists_actual_common_mark_quotients,Hironaka.HironakaActualCommonMarkPersistentCosupport.actual_common_mark_control_le_normalized}
\leanok
Fix an actual coordinate centre $C_A$, an actual pivot $e\in A$, any actual chart directions, and an original ideal $I$ with genuine whole centre-power membership $I\subseteq I_{C_A}^m$. For any prescribed original family $G_a$ generating that same $I$, actual analytic exceptional division internally constructs one family $K_a$ such that
\[
 e^mK_a=B^*G_a,\qquad
 B^*I=(e^m)(K_a)_a,\qquad
 (B^*I:e^m)=(K_a)_a\subseteq N_B(I),
\]
where $N_B(I)$ is the entire actual normalized-member ideal. No assertion about individual vertex weights is needed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each prescribed generator belongs to the whole original ideal and hence to its assumed actual centre power. Apply actual analytic exceptional division at the common mark $m$ to those same numerators, choosing the resulting actual quotients once. Mapping the prescribed whole generating equality and factoring their actual product equations proves the first ideal equality exactly as in the first theorem, now with every weight equal to $m$. Cancellation of the nonzero actual exceptional coordinate power proves the colon equality. Each $K_a$ itself is an actual normalized original member, witnessed by mark $m$, original numerator $G_a$, its original membership, its derived centre-power membership and its actual quotient equation. Its entire generated ideal is therefore contained in the actual normalized-member ideal. One may in particular use the entire original ideal as the prescribed generating family, so the final inclusion does not require any incoming choice of generators.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual persistent Hilbert--Samuel points retain common-mark cosupport]
\label{hir:actual-persistent-common-mark-cosupport}
\uses{hir:actual-common-mark-analytic-centre-quotients,hir:actual-hs-profile-point-power-detection,hir:actual-persistent-normalized-geometric-ideal,hir:whole-point-power-ring-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCommonMarkPersistentCosupport.actual_common_mark_control_le_maximal_pow_of_persistent_hs,Hironaka.HironakaActualCommonMarkPersistentCosupport.actual_common_mark_quotient_vanishes_of_persistent_hs}
\leanok
Work in the genuine normal-centre model $(u,z)\in\mathbf C^d\times\mathbf C^t$, with $t\ge2$, original subset $W$, and its entire actual origin vanishing ideal $I$. Retain a prescribed actual analytic vertex family whose original members satisfy their genuine normal vertex powers. Suppose $I\subseteq(z)^m$, and suppose the actual reduced geometric strict transform at a chosen actual normal-pivot chart origin has the same entire genuine Hilbert--Samuel profile as $I$. Then
\[
 (B^*I:e^m)\subseteq\mathfrak m^m.
\]
In particular every actual common-mark quotient of every original member belongs to the entire point power; each holomorphic representative vanishes to order at least $m$ at that actual chart origin.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original centre admissibility gives the whole common-mark controlled ideal inclusion into the actual normalized-member ideal. At this actual persistent point, the proved normalized-versus-geometric rigidity theorem identifies that entire normalized ideal with the entire actual reduced strict vanishing ideal $J$. Every normal coordinate vanishes at the origin, so $(z)^m\subseteq\mathfrak m^m$ and the original ideal $I$ belongs to $\mathfrak m^m$. Actual persistence identifies the entire genuine profiles of $I$ and $J$, including the actual model-origin Hilbert--Samuel dictionary. The preceding whole-power detection therefore gives $J\subseteq\mathfrak m^m$. Combining these true whole ideal inclusions proves the claim. For an actual product equation $e^mH=B^*g$ with $g\in I$, the product lies in $B^*I$, so $H$ belongs to the actual colon and hence to $\mathfrak m^m$. The genuine original point-power dictionary identifies membership of any holomorphic representative with its actual order-$m$ point vanishing.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}

It neither replaces common-mark control by the false controlled transform of a reduced old Hilbert--Samuel maximum locus, nor asserts individual transformed vertex orders equal to their original weights. A joint centre--maximal-contact coordinate producer, recursive coefficient-presentation tracking, strict progress and a finite persistent phase remain separate obligations.
\end{remark}
