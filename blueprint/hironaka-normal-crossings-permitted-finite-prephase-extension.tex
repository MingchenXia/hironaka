\section{Finite permitted old-branch simplification near the whole compact cosupport}
\label{sec:nc-permitted-finite-prephase}

Here $M=(X,X,E,I,d)$ has coherent ideal, positive mark and compact
cosupport. The original NC carrier $H$ is adapted to the entire retained

\begin{lemma}[Actual test histories retain compact whole cosupport]
\label{hir:nc-compact-current-cosupport-without-domain}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsPermittedFinitePrephase.current_cosupport_compact_of_wellFormed,Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsPermittedFinitePrephase.restricted_cosupport_compact}
\leanok
Let $M$ be well formed and have compact cosupport. For every actual finite
test tower $T$, the whole current cosupport is compact and lies in the
proper projection preimage of the original cosupport. No nonzero or
invariant-domain hypothesis is required for this assertion.
For any actual open containing the whole cosupport, its literal restricted
marked record also has compact whole cosupport, with no compact-intersection
hypothesis on that open.
\uses{hir:wellformed-tower,bcalc:cosupport-analytic,bcalc:transform-off-centre-tower}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
True well-formedness preservation gives a closed smooth current carrier
and a coherent current ideal. Its whole current cosupport is closed by
the actual order-locus theorem. At every point of that cosupport, the
actual test-history projection law puts its image in the original
cosupport. The finite blowup projection is proper, so the preimage of
the original compact cosupport is compact. The current cosupport is a
closed subset of this genuine compact preimage. These arguments use
neither the nonzero condition nor a canonical or terminal history.
For the open restriction, the exact cosupport dictionary identifies its
cosupport with the inverse image of the ambient cosupport. Its image
under the subtype inclusion is exactly that entire compact set, since
the open contains it. The subtype embedding therefore proves compactness.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual open image has a genuine restricted biholomorphism]
\label{hir:nc-open-image-actual-equivalence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsPermittedFinitePrephase.exists_open_image_equiv}
\leanok
For a genuine open holomorphic embedding $g:Z\to Y$ and a genuine open
$V\subset Z$, there is an actual open $W=g(V)\subset Y$ and a
biholomorphism $V\simeq W$ whose map to $Y$ is literally $g$ on $V$.
\uses{hir:base-change-open}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Unpack the actual open embedding as a biholomorphism from $Z$ onto its
open image $O$ in $Y$. The image of $V$ in $O$ is open, and the inclusion
of $O$ into $Y$ is open. Their composite therefore has the actual open
image $W=g(V)$. Restrict the genuine biholomorphism and its genuine
inverse to $V$ and $W$; the inverse identities follow from the original
inverse identities and injectivity. Holomorphicity of both restrictions
follows from the open-subtype holomorphicity criterion. Hence this is a
real biholomorphism, with the stated literal numerator map.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A finite actual permitted prephase simplifies the old carrier near every current permitted point]
\label{hir:nc-original-finite-permitted-count-prephase}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsPermittedFinitePrephase.exists_original_finite_permitted_prephase}
\leanok
Only the original coherent ideal $I$, positive $d$, compact cosupport and
actual adapted pair $(H,E)$ are inputs. They supply an actual finite
original test tower $T:X_T\to X$ and a literal open $V\subset X_T$
containing its \emph{entire} current original controlled cosupport. The
trace on $V$ of $\operatorname{strict}_T H$ is adapted to every member
of the entire current boundary trace and has old branch count at most
one. Thus the old trace is smooth and transverse to that boundary, and
adjoining it on this same $V$ gives an actual SNC boundary. No repair is
asserted outside the current permitted neighbourhood.
\uses{hir:nc-original-phase-whole-cosupport-count-drop,hir:nc-compact-current-cosupport-without-domain,hir:nc-open-image-actual-equivalence,hir:compact-cosupport-open-tower-extension,hir:general-nc-whole-biholomorphic-carrier-pair,hir:general-nc-biholomorphic-intrinsic-multiplicity,hir:general-nc-count-one-smooth-terminal-pair}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Prove the assertion simultaneously for every ambient manifold and every
adapted pair with a global old-count bound $r$, by strong induction on
$r$. If $r\le1$, use the identity tower and the whole ambient open.
Exact intrinsic open transport gives the same count, and the existing
count-one coordinate theorem gives smoothness, transversality and SNC.

If $r>1$, the actual original coefficient-stratum phase supplies a genuine
finite original test tower $P$, a global current adapted pair, and an
actual open $U$ containing \emph{all} of the current original cosupport
on which old count is at most $r-1$. Current cosupport is compact by the
first lemma. Its literal restriction to $U$ is compact: its image under
the open inclusion is exactly that entire compact cosupport. The current
ideal is coherent, the mark is unchanged, and the entire current boundary
is used in the restricted marked record. Apply the induction hypothesis
to these actual restricted data on $U$, obtaining a genuine local suffix
$S$ and its final whole-cosupport neighbourhood.

The actual reverse open-history theorem extends this \emph{entire}
finite local suffix to a genuine global suffix $Q$ for the current record
after $P$. It internally constructs a whole tower isomorphism between
$S$ and the restriction of $Q$ over $U$, its actual upper open embedding,
and the literal current-record and strict-carrier inverse-image
identities. Every current permitted point of $Q$ projects to the old
current cosupport, hence to $U$; the exact record identity puts its unique
local representative in the local current cosupport, hence in the open
supplied by induction. Take the actual open image of that open under the
upper embedding. It therefore contains the \emph{whole} global current
cosupport. Write $\chi$ for the genuine restricted biholomorphism to this
image. Its literal inverse-point identity $g(\chi^{-1}w)=w$ identifies the
inverse image of the local strict carrier with the trace of the ambient
strict carrier. The same identity identifies every member of the entire
finite boundary list, with all its positions. Pull back the actual local
adapted coordinates through this genuine inverse. The intrinsic
biholomorphic branch-count law then identifies the count at $w$ with
the count at $\chi^{-1}w$, so the latter's bound by one holds on this
same ambient open. The literal append $P Q$ is an actual
original test tower, and actual append dictionaries identify its whole
current marked record and strict original carrier with the ones just
constructed. Since $r-1<r$, this is a finite induction on actual original
data, with no supplied phase, suffix, maximum, terminal-history or
extension certificate. Finally every actual NC carrier has old count
bounded by the ambient dimension, which supplies the initial $r$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[An original general NC carrier needs no pre-existing simple boundary certificate]
\label{hir:nc-original-empty-retained-finite-permitted-prephase}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsPermittedFinitePrephase.exists_original_nc_finite_permitted_prephase}
\leanok
For an original general NC carrier $H$, coherent positive-mark ideal and
compact cosupport, the preceding genuine finite prephase exists with
initial retained boundary empty. Every final born boundary slot is
retained. The final old-plus-born SNC assertion is only on the actual
open containing the whole current controlled cosupport.
\uses{hir:nc-original-finite-permitted-count-prephase,hir:general-nc-boundary-empty-producer}
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The original NC coordinates themselves give the genuine adapted pair
$(H,[])$, with no additional certificate. Apply the theorem to this same
ideal and mark. Its actual tower transforms the empty retained boundary
into the literal accumulated born list, including all empty slots. Its
whole current old trace and this very list satisfy the stated conclusion
on the actual produced neighbourhood. This does not make the entire old
carrier globally simple, does not resolve an unrelated identically zero
ideal, and does not supply the still separate general-NC canonical
history/naturality endpoint.
\end{proof}
