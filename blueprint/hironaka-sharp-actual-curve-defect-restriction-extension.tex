\begin{theorem}[The actual FIRST defect is the chronological product's second-order cosupport]
\label{hir:sharp-original-curve-first-defect-actual-product-second-cut}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-product-order-le-one-true-count-dictionary,hir:sharp-original-curve-first-good-product-order-le-one,hir:sharp-original-all-member-actual-finite-chronological-history}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveDefectRestriction.original_curve_first_defect_eq_product_cosupport_two}
\leanok
For EVERY genuine original TEST prefix $T$,
\[
 Z_T=\operatorname{cosupp}_{A_T.N}(J_T,2).
\]
This is equality of entire ambient subsets, including points outside the
actual carrier, and retains every original member position.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual original domain and TEST internally supply the true geometric
template history. Each chronological member is coherent, nonzero along
the actual carrier and locally principal there; the actual finite product
has the same properties. In particular its actual relative order is
finite at every carrier point, and its order-two cosupport is precisely
where that order is at least two.

At a carrier point outside $Z_T$, true separation of the chronological
traces and their actual proper positions bounds the product order by one.
Conversely, product order at most one makes each proper factor have order
one and gives at most one proper position. The quotient map from these
positions to the genuine full ideal-germ classes is then injective; its
actual image cardinal equals the product order. The defining strict
inequality for $Z_T$ cannot hold. Thus outside $Z_T$ is equivalent to
product order at most one, or equivalently membership in $Z_T$ is product
order at least two. Both $Z_T$ and the actual relative cosupport are empty
outside the actual carrier, proving the entire equality. This argument
constructs the required distinct-class count; it does not substitute an
indexed incidence count at bad points.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual FIRST centre commutes with every genuine open restriction]
\label{hir:sharp-original-curve-first-defect-open-restriction}
