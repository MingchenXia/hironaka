\section{Reconstructing actual stages across empty boundary positions}

\begin{theorem}[Whole actual stage dictionary for equal filtered initial boundaries]
\label{hir:original-same-filtered-boundary-stage-dictionary}
\uses{hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-geometry,hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-canonical}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStageReconstruction.same_filtered_boundary_stage_dictionary}
\leanok
Let $M$ be actual marked data with whole ambient carrier and a genuine
invariant domain, and let $E'$ be an actual SNC list with the same
ordered nonempty filter as $M.E$. For every actual tower $T$, replacing
only the initial boundary by $E'$ gives a genuine invariant domain,
the same canonical-history, test-history and terminal-history
conditions, exactly the same whole final cosupport and every whole
maximum locus on every subset, and the same filtered whole final
boundary. No transformed-boundary or invariant-value equality is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Keep the original closed carrier, coherent ideal, dimension bound,
mark and nonzero-along data. The new SNC hypothesis and the actual
transversality of the whole ambient supply the new well-formed
boundary fields, hence the real invariant domain. After filtering
empty initial members, both marked records are literally equal:
their carrier, whole ideal and mark never changed. Apply the genuine
all-dimension empty-boundary equivalences to each original record
and this same tower. Equality of the filtered records then identifies
canonicality, geometric testness and terminality. The genuine
cosupport, arbitrary-subset maximum and whole filtered-boundary
identities through empty filtering identify the remaining outputs.
The real ambient-dimension-zero branch is included in these proved
empty-boundary theorems.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A real stage reconstructed over a prescribed biholomorphic base]
\label{hir:original-canonical-stage-reconstruction-base}
\uses{hir:original-same-filtered-boundary-stage-dictionary,hir:original-empty-base-actual-transport-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStageReconstruction.canonical_stage_reconstruction_over_base}
\leanok
Let $M$ have whole ambient carrier and a genuine invariant domain,
let $T$ be an actual canonical history, and let $\Phi:X'\simeq X$
be a prescribed genuine biholomorphism. Let $E'$ be an actual SNC
boundary whose ordered nonempty filter is that of the literal
$\Phi$ pullback of $M.E$. Internally transport the actual $T$ over
$\Phi$ to the actual tower $S$ with actual endpoint map $\beta$.
For the literal pulled whole carrier, ideal and mark with boundary
$E'$, this SAME $S$ is in the genuine invariant domain and is
canonical. Its whole cosupport and every whole maximum locus on any subset of
the target cosupport are the $\beta$ inverse images of the target ones. Its entire ordered
filtered final boundary is the corresponding inverse-image list.
Target terminality implies source terminality, and avoidance of any
actual target open implies avoidance of its actual base inverse
image. The actual tower isomorphism and whole projection square are
constructed, and every original closed subset satisfies the whole
strict-transform square. No source history, desired comparison,
chosen common index or compactness is supplied as an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the genuine recursive transport of the actual blowup tower along
$\Phi$. The proved canonical transport dictionary internally gives
its actual tower isomorphism, endpoint biholomorphism, projection
square, pulled invariant domain and canonicality. It also gives the
whole pulled marked record, every actual whole maximum locus,
terminality transport and preserved opens. The pulled carrier is
the whole source ambient.

The preceding theorem applies to that actual pulled record and
$E'$. It reconstructs the genuine domain and canonicality for the
literal desired boundary on this SAME transported tower. Its
cosupport and arbitrary-subset maximum equalities retain the actual
transported whole loci on every subset of the true cosupport. Its filtered final-boundary equality, combined
with the genuine full boundary pullback formula, gives the claimed
whole ordered filtered list: a bijective inverse image commutes with
filtering nonempty members. Terminality transfers by the same
record dictionary, and actual preserved opens depend only on the
same underlying tower. Finally the actual biholomorphic projection
square proves the whole strict-transform identity for every closed
original subset. All choices in the conclusion are actual recursive
transport constructions from the original stage.
\end{proof}
