\section{Actual point-chart lifts of canonical initial relations}

\begin{lemma}[Analytic normalization preserves a whole weighted syzygy]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-normalized-weighted-syzygy}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-blowup-exceptional-quotient-formal-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalizedInitialRelations.exists_actual_normalized_syzygy}
\leanok
Let $G_a,C_a$ be a finite family of actual holomorphic origin germs,
let $d_a\le D$, and suppose $C_a\in\mathfrak m^{D-d_a}$ and $\sum_a G_aC_a=0$.
In an actual point blowup chart write $e$ for its exceptional coordinate
and $\pi^*$ for the genuine analytic pullback. For any actual quotients
$e^{d_a}H_a=\pi^*G_a$, there are actual holomorphic germs $L_a$
with $e^{D-d_a}L_a=\pi^*C_a$ and the whole relation
$\sum_a H_aL_a=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The whole point maximal ideal maps to the principal ideal $(e)$ under
the actual chart. Consequently the given whole powers supply analytic
quotients $L_a$ of $\pi^*C_a$. For every index, the equality
$d_a+(D-d_a)=D$ gives
$e^D H_aL_a=(e^{d_a}H_a)(e^{D-d_a}L_a)=\pi^*(G_aC_a)$.
Sum these actual identities. The source whole relation makes their sum
zero. The genuine coordinate germ $e$ is nonzero in the actual analytic
germ domain, so cancellation of $e^D$ proves the claimed whole relation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every canonical homogeneous initial relation lifts in the same actual normalized family]
\label{hir:actual-original-point-normalized-initial-relation-lifts}
\uses{hir:actual-original-homogeneous-initial-analytic-syzygy,hir:actual-analytic-normalized-weighted-syzygy,hir:actual-normal-quotient-canonical-exceptional-restriction,hir:actual-original-formal-taylor-maximal-power-kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalizedInitialRelations.exists_original_point_normalized_initial_relation_lifts}
\leanok
For an arbitrary original analytic origin ideal $J$, fix its actual
canonical reduced analytic family $G_a$ and vertex degrees $d_a$.
Every actual point chart internally supplies one family $H_a$ with
$e^{d_a}H_a=\pi^*G_a$, before all following choices of relations.
Let $D\ge d_a$ for every index, and let $P_a$ be actual analytic germs
whose whole Taylor series are homogeneous of degree $D-d_a$.
Assume the literal homogeneous initial relation
\[
 \sum_a T(P_a)\operatorname{hom}_{d_a}T(G_a)=0.
\]
Then this same actual normalized family admits analytic germs $L_a,V_a,R_a$
with
\[
 \sum_a H_aL_a=0,\qquad
 e^{D-d_a}V_a=\pi^*P_a,\qquad L_a=V_a+eR_a.
\]
In particular the exceptional restriction of $L_a$ is the literal
finite dehomogenization of the actual degree $D-d_a$ normal polynomial
of $P_a$:
\[
 L_a-\operatorname{dehom}_{j,b}
    \operatorname{hom}^{\rm normal}_{D-d_a}(P_a)\in(e).
\]
All sums and equalities concern whole actual analytic germs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The degree-first lower support of the actual canonical reduced Taylor
series gives $G_a\in\mathfrak m^{d_a}$ by the whole original Taylor
maximal-power dictionary. Thus the genuine chart internally supplies
one analytic family $H_a$ with the displayed exceptional identities;
fix it now.

For the given literal homogeneous relation, actual analytic initial
syzygy lifting supplies germs $C_a$ with $\sum_aG_aC_a=0$ and
$P_a-C_a\in\mathfrak m^{D+1-d_a}$. Homogeneity of the entire Taylor
series of $P_a$ gives $P_a\in\mathfrak m^{D-d_a}$. Since
$D+1-d_a=(D-d_a)+1$, the correction belongs to this same lower power,
so $C_a$ does too. Apply the preceding normalization lemma to the
fixed $H_a$ and these actual $C_a$, obtaining analytic $L_a$ and the
whole target relation.

Actual exceptional division also gives analytic $V_a,R_a$ satisfying
$e^{D-d_a}V_a=\pi^*P_a$ and
$e^{D+1-d_a}R_a=\pi^*(C_a-P_a)$. Subtract the first equation from
the corresponding equation for $L_a$. Since the two exponents differ
by one, cancellation of the nonzero $e^{D-d_a}$ gives exactly
$L_a-V_a=eR_a$. Finally the true normal homogeneous polynomial of
$P_a$ has remainder in $\mathfrak m^{D+1-d_a}$, so its actual
normalized quotient has the established exceptional restriction.
That restriction for $V_a$, followed by the literal $eR_a$ correction,
is the stated restriction for $L_a$.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[Literal polynomial initial relations have exact analytic normalized lifts]
\label{hir:actual-original-point-polynomial-initial-relation-lifts}
\uses{hir:actual-original-point-normalized-initial-relation-lifts,hir:actual-normal-polynomial-blowup-factorization,hir:actual-original-polynomial-low-coefficient-separation,hir:actual-original-formal-taylor-algebra-embedding}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalizedInitialRelations.exists_original_point_polynomial_relation_lifts}
\leanok
The same original ideal and actual point chart internally fix one actual
normalized canonical family $H_a$ before all following relations. If finite
complex polynomials $p_a$ are homogeneous of degree $D-d_a$, where
$D\ge d_a$, and satisfy the literal whole formal relation
$\sum_a p_a\operatorname{hom}_{d_a}T(G_a)=0$, there are actual analytic
$L_a,R_a$ with the whole target relation $\sum_aH_aL_a=0$ and the exact
whole-germ formula
\[
 L_a=\operatorname{dehom}_{j,b}(p_a)+eR_a.
\]
Here polynomial dehomogenization uses the actual chart coordinates,
retains every original complex coefficient as its constant germ,
and sets the pivot homogeneous coordinate to one.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Evaluate each $p_a$ at the actual original coordinate germs to obtain
$P_a$. The genuine Taylor algebra homomorphism sends this evaluation
to the original polynomial $p_a$ included in formal series: this follows
on constants and variables from its genuine coordinate formula, and
then on the whole polynomial by its additive and multiplicative laws.
Thus the preceding theorem applies and supplies $L_a,V_a,R_a$ for the
same already fixed $H_a$.

Map $p_a$ to the normal polynomial ring over the zero-dimensional
actual base germ ring by its literal constant germs. Coefficientwise
this preserves its stated degree homogeneity. Actual polynomial
blowup factorization therefore gives
$\pi^*P_a=e^{D-d_a}\operatorname{dehom}_{j,b}(p_a)$.
The independently supplied normalization gives
$\pi^*P_a=e^{D-d_a}V_a$. Cancel the genuine nonzero analytic germ
$e^{D-d_a}$ to identify the whole $V_a$ with this literal polynomial
dehomogenization. Substitute into the already proved whole analytic
relation and exact $eR_a$ correction.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This is a genuine lift of polynomial initial relations to whole
analytic relations in the same actual point-normalized family. The
explicit condition $D\ge d_a$ is retained. Generation of all completed
exceptional-fibre relations, exceptional regularity of the normalized
ideal, equality with the total-pullback saturation, reverse current
Hilbert--Samuel persistence and uniform history remain separate.
\end{remark}
