\section{Actual coefficient summability from genuine homogeneous analytic bounds}

\begin{theorem}[Degree-band coefficient majorants imply actual whole-series summability]
\label{hir:actual-degree-band-coefficient-summability}
\uses{hir:actual-monomial-owner-absolute-bounds}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHomogeneousCoefficientSummability.finite_mass_of_degree_band_majorants}
Let $F$ and $P_k$ be actual formal series with
$F_\alpha=(P_{|\alpha|})_\alpha$. Suppose every $P_k$ has finite
actual coefficient mass at a fixed positive polyradius $\rho$,
$|P_k|_\rho\leq b_k$, and $\sum_k b_k$ converges. Then the entire
actual series $F$ has finite coefficient mass at $\rho$.
No homogeneity of unused coefficients of $P_k$ is required.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Partition all actual exponent vectors by their genuine total degree.
On the degree-$k$ part the exact coefficient dictionary identifies
the actual summand with that of $P_k$. Restricting a genuinely
summable nonnegative family preserves summability, and its sum is
at most the sum over the entire original exponent type. Thus every
degree part is summable and its total is bounded by $|P_k|_\rho$,
hence by $b_k$. The nonnegative comparison test makes the sequence
of these actual degree-part sums summable. The genuine countable
partition summability theorem then proves summability of the entire
original coefficient family, which is exactly the asserted mass.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A positive multilinear radius and actual polynomial band bounds produce a genuine coefficient radius]
\label{hir:actual-multilinear-bands-positive-coefficient-radius}
\uses{hir:actual-degree-band-coefficient-summability}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHomogeneousCoefficientSummability.exists_uniform_radius_of_multilinear_bands}
Let $p$ be an actual formal multilinear series of positive convergence
radius, and let $P_k$ be genuine finite coordinate polynomials.
Suppose their actual uniform-radius coefficient masses satisfy
$|P_k|_r\leq n^k\|p_k\|r^k$ for every $r>0$.
There is a single $r>0$, chosen before $F$, such that every actual
series with $F_\alpha=(P_{|\alpha|})_\alpha$ has finite absolute
coefficient mass at the uniform polyradius $r$.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose a positive finite $s$ strictly inside the actual convergence
radius of $p$, and put $r=s/(n+1)$. Then $r>0$ and $nr\leq s$.
The genuine multilinear-radius theorem gives summability of
$\|p_k\|(nr)^k=n^k\|p_k\|r^k$. Every original $P_k$ has finite
coefficient support and therefore finite mass without an analytic
certificate. The displayed actual mass bound and exact degree-band
coefficient dictionary now satisfy the previous theorem. This
produces one genuine positive coefficient radius, including $n=0$,
without assuming the desired whole-series coefficient summability.
\end{proof}

The polynomial/analytic Taylor coefficient dictionary and finite
polynomial mass estimate are explicit inputs here; a separate actual
Taylor producer must derive them for original holomorphic germs.
The genuine multilinear convergence radius alone is not substituted
for the whole coefficient-mass conclusion.
