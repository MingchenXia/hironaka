\section{A genuine finite global history for compact original ambient spaces}

\begin{lemma}[A proved whole stopped open gives a genuine entire terminal biholomorphism]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-whole-stopped-prefix-terminal-biholomorphism}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-compact-finite-prefix-terminal-identification}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactGlobalFiniteEndpoint.wholePositivePrefixTerminalBiholomorphism,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactGlobalFiniteEndpoint.wholePositivePrefixTerminalBiholomorphism_projection}
\leanok
For the SAME actual atlas and positive stage $N+1$, if its actual stopped
open contains every original point, the SAME genuine original-base global
prefix endpoint is genuinely biholomorphic to the ENTIRE SAME terminal
endpoint, over the original space. The actual finite prefix keeps every
one of its $N+1$ original global blowup constructors, including empty steps.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every point of the actual prefix endpoint projects into the whole stopped
open, and so does every point of the retained terminal endpoint. The two
true open-subset inclusions therefore have genuine inverses given by
forming the actual subtype point. These inverse laws are literal, and
both directions are holomorphic by genuine open inclusion and holomorphic
subtype formation. Compose the first of these genuine whole-open
biholomorphisms, the preceding actual positive-prefix terminal open
biholomorphism, and the inverse of the second whole-open biholomorphism.
Their point maps and the already proved whole original projection identity
give the entire original projection square. Nothing replaces or compresses
the actual global finite prefix.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Only original sharp-curve data and ordinary ambient compactness give a genuine finite global history coupled to the full sharp endpoint]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-compact-global-finite-sharp-endpoint}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-whole-stopped-prefix-terminal-biholomorphism,hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-compact-subset-finite-stopping,hir:sharp-actual-original-global-sharp-endpoint-existence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactGlobalFiniteEndpoint.exists_original_compact_finite_sharp_endpoint}
\leanok
Assume ONLY the original positive-dimensional smooth pure curve, ordered
SNC and density data, and ordinary compactness of the original ambient
space. There exist a natural $N$, an actual endpoint manifold, a genuine
original-base blowup tower of length $N+1$, the SAME full original global
sharp endpoint, and a genuine biholomorphism from the actual tower endpoint
to that sharp endpoint whose ENTIRE projection agrees with the tower
projection. Thus the original boundary, conventional strict curve, reduced
born Cartier divisor and actual local original presentations remain those
of the SAME full endpoint, coupled to a genuine finite blowup history.
Every actual global centre at stages at least $N+1$ is empty. No global
stopping bound, final history, endpoint isomorphism or geometry certificate
is supplied. This is the compact ambient corollary; a noncompact global
finite maximum is not claimed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the proved compact-subset stopping construction to the ordinary
compact entire original ambient space. Its internally constructed natural
bound gives a stopped open containing every original point. The preceding
lemma produces the genuine entire terminal biholomorphism for the SAME
actual original-base positive prefix at this bound.

Take the full sharp endpoint already constructed internally from the SAME
original curve and ordered boundary data. Its ambient and projection are
exactly the retained terminal ambient and projection used by the preceding
maps. Its complete coupled output retains the original conventional strict
curve, locally finite indexed original/birth boundary, true SNC and
transverse coordinate readouts, reduced coherent born Cartier ideal,
properness, surjectivity, good-locus isomorphism and actual finite original
presentations. None is supplied as an assumption. Use this actual endpoint,
the SAME genuine positive prefix, its proved literal length and the actual
entire terminal biholomorphism. The projection identity supplies precisely
the required coupling, rather than an unrelated existential endpoint.

The compact stopping theorem also makes every later actual centre disjoint
from the entire projection preimage of the whole original ambient space.
That preimage is the entire stage, hence every such actual centre is empty.
All already existing empty positions in the finite tower remain genuine
constructors. No infinite family is compressed or assigned a fictitious
finite maximum, and no canonicity or higher-dimensional scheduling theorem
is inferred.
\end{proof}
