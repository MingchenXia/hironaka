\section{Actual blowup coordinates for a fixed centre flattening}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-fixed-flattening-blowup-coordinates}

The centre coordinates used to study an original ideal must also be
the coordinates used to express its actual blowup. The following
construction starts with a supplied genuine flattening and retains
that same map throughout.

\begin{theorem}[The whole blowup square retains a supplied centre flattening]
\label{hir:actual-fixed-flattening-blowup-coordinate-square}
\uses{bcalc:monomial-lift,hir:actual-monomial-local-inverse-complex-injective,hir:actual-smooth-centre-monomial-recentring,ana:local-holomorphic-inverse}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFixedFlatteningBlowupCoordinates.exists_actual_fixed_flattening_blowup_coordinates}
\leanok
Let $\pi:Y\to X$ be a genuine blowup along $C$ in complex dimension
$n$, and let $z\in Y$. Fix a genuine local holomorphic coordinate
equivalence $\Phi$ at $\pi(z)$, defined inside its original chart,
with $\Phi(\pi(z))=0$, such that throughout its domain
\[
 u\in C\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \Phi(u)\in\mathbb C^k\times0.
\]
There are an actual normal pivot $j\ge k$, parameters $b$ with
$b_j=0$ and $b_i=0$ for $i<k$, and genuine local coordinates
$\Theta$ centred at $z$, with $\Theta(z)=0$, such that the entire
source coordinate neighborhood maps into the domain of this same
fixed $\Phi$ and
\[
 \Phi(\pi(u))=
 \pi^{\mathrm{normal}}_{k,j,b}(\Theta(u)).
\]
Here the displayed coordinate expressions include the original
ambient charts. The target flattening is exactly the supplied
$\Phi$; no further centre flattening is chosen.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The point $\pi(z)$ lies in the true domain of $\Phi$. Apply the
actual blowup monomial local-inverse theorem using the supplied
source inclusion and centre-membership equivalence. It produces a
normal pivot $j\ge k$, a genuine open neighborhood $O$ of $z$,
and a holomorphic map $W$ on $O$ with
\[
 m_{k,j}(W(u))=\Phi(\pi(u)).
\]
All images $\pi(u)$ lie in the domain of the supplied $\Phi$.
The actual monomial lift for this same $\Phi$ is a local left
inverse of $W$. Therefore the complex differential of $W$ at $z$
is injective. The holomorphic inverse function theorem gives true
centred local coordinates $\Theta$ whose expression on a smaller
open neighborhood is $W(u)-W(z)$.

At $z$ the monomial square equals zero. Its tangent rows are
$W(z)_i$ for $i<k$, and its pivot row is $W(z)_j$, so all these
coordinates vanish. Put $b=W(z)$. The exact monomial recentering
identity gives
\[
 m_{k,j}(W(u))=
 \pi^{\mathrm{normal}}_{k,j,b}(W(u)-W(z))
\]
at every point of the smaller neighborhood, including points on
the exceptional divisor. Combining these two literal identities
proves the whole square. Every invocation used the original fixed
$\Phi$, and the source inclusion is inherited from the same
local-inverse construction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original constant-Hilbert--Samuel centre geometry supplies the same free-layer and blowup coordinates]
\label{hir:actual-original-free-normal-layers-fixed-blowup-coordinates}
\uses{hir:actual-original-centre-normal-layers-finite-free,hir:actual-fixed-flattening-blowup-coordinate-square}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFixedFlatteningBlowupCoordinates.exists_original_hs_constant_centre_free_normal_blowup_coordinates}
\leanok
Let $\pi:Y\to X$ be a genuine blowup along $C$ in dimension
$d+t$, and let $z\in Y$. Suppose $C$ is regular of dimension $d$
at $\pi(z)$, and let $\mathcal A$ be a coherent ideal whose entire
intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel function is constant along $C$.
There is one genuine centred flattening $\Phi$ of $C$ such that
every whole original normal layer of the actual unchanged origin
ideal $I_0$ of $\mathcal A$ in these coordinates is finite free
over $\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$, and this same $\Phi$ occurs in
the whole actual blowup square of the preceding theorem at $z$.
The source coordinates, actual normal pivot and parameters are
produced together with these same witnesses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the original-only normal-layer producer at $\pi(z)$ to
the given centre regularity, original coherence and all-order
intrinsic constancy. It gives one actual centred flattening
$\Phi$, its whole original centre-membership equivalence and,
for the same actual origin ideal $I_0$, finite freeness of every
normal layer. Retain those witnesses. Apply the preceding theorem
to this very $\Phi$ and its genuine source and membership data.
The resulting source coordinates, pivot and parameters express
the original blowup in this retained target flattening. Since
the target map has not changed, the definition of $I_0$ and every
previously obtained normal-layer freeness statement remain the
same. This proves the simultaneous conclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The first theorem needs only the supplied actual flattening data
and the full blowup predicate. The second theorem obtains the
normal-layer and target-chart witnesses from original geometry,
without a freeness or compatibility certificate as input.
Neither assertion proves reduced-vertex normal-power compatibility,
general Bennett nonincrease or termination of a singular-centre
resolution sequence.
\end{remark}
