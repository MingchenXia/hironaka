\section{Independence of actual ambient extensions on reduced subspace models}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-analytic-subspace-presentation-independence}

Let $C\subset M$ and $D\subset N$ be subsets of complex manifolds. The
locally ringed models remain the literal reduced vanishing-quotient sheaves
on the actual topological subtypes. The two ambient dimensions may differ.
All morphisms below are the previously constructed genuine quotient-sheaf
maps, rather than supplied structure-sheaf maps.

\begin{theorem}[Ambient extensions agreeing on the actual subset have equal quotient pullbacks]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-ambient-eqOn}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-holomorphic-map,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-reduced-stalks}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspacePresentationIndependence.actualAmbientRestriction_eqOn}
\leanok
Suppose actual holomorphic maps $f,g:M\to N$ agree on $C$. Then the two
actual locally ringed morphisms from the model of $C$ into the actual
ambient manifold $N$, obtained by the source inclusion followed by $f$
and $g$, are equal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Their actual underlying continuous maps are equal by the given pointwise
agreement on $C$. Fix $x\in C$ and an actual target holomorphic section
$s$ over an open $U$ containing their common image. Both pullbacks are
defined on the original opens $f^{-1}U$ and $g^{-1}U$. Restrict them to
the genuine common open $W=f^{-1}U\cap g^{-1}U$, which contains $x$.
For every $z\in C\cap W$ their difference has literal value
$s(f(z))-s(g(z))=0$. The actual section vanishing criterion places the
entire difference in the original whole vanishing section ideal. Its
germ at $x$ therefore belongs to the original whole vanishing stalk
ideal, so the two actual quotient stalk classes coincide. Restriction
of sections leaves their germs unchanged. The actual stalk pullback
formula identifies these classes with the two composite stalk maps on
the germ of $s$. Every target stalk element is such a genuine section
germ; the canonical stalk transport between the equal image points
retains that same numerator. Hence the two composite stalk maps agree.
The genuine sheaf stalk extensionality theorem, together with equality
of the underlying maps, gives the asserted locally ringed equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual subspace morphisms depend only on their values on the source subset]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-map-eqOn}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-ambient-eqOn,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-inclusion-mono,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-holomorphic-map}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspacePresentationIndependence.actualSubspaceMap_eqOn}
\leanok
If $f,g:M\to N$ are actual holomorphic maps carrying $C$ into $D$ and
agreeing on $C$, their internally constructed morphisms between the
actual locally ringed subspace models are equal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose both proposed subspace morphisms with the actual target
inclusion. Their proved inclusion squares identify the composites with
the source inclusion followed by the two actual ambient holomorphic
maps. The preceding theorem makes these composites equal. Cancel the
genuine monomorphism given by the actual target inclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Mutually inverse ambient maps only on the subsets give actual presentation isomorphisms]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-mutual-restriction-iso}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-map-eqOn,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-map-functor-laws}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspacePresentationIndependence.actualSubspaceMutualRestrictionIso}
\leanok
Let $f:M\to N$ and $g:N\to M$ be actual holomorphic maps carrying $C$
into $D$ and $D$ into $C$. Suppose $g(f(x))=x$ for every $x\in C$ and
$f(g(y))=y$ for every $y\in D$. They internally construct an actual
isomorphism of the two reduced locally ringed subspace models, with
literal underlying restrictions of $f$ and $g$ and their genuine
ambient inclusion squares. No ambient inverse identity outside the
specified subsets or ambient equality of dimensions is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the two actual holomorphic maps and the two subset-containment
hypotheses to construct the previously proved genuine locally ringed
subspace morphisms. Their composites are the actual restrictions of
$gf$ and $fg$ by the proved composition law. Each composite ambient
map agrees with the relevant identity on the actual source subset.
The preceding independence theorem equates its constructed subspace
map with the constructed identity restriction. The actual identity
law makes these maps the two identity morphisms. Thus the two actual
constructed morphisms are mutually inverse and form the claimed
locally ringed isomorphism. Their inclusion squares are already proved
for the same actual holomorphic maps.
\end{proof}

This is a genuine comparison when actual ambient holomorphic extensions
of the inverse subset maps are given. Holomorphic extension of arbitrary
intrinsic overlap maps, effective atlas gluing, and descent of a resolution
to arbitrary abstract analytic spaces remain separate obligations.
