\section{Whole vanishing ideals and strict-transform Hilbert--Samuel values in fixed blowup coordinates}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-fixed-flattening-hs-comparison}

Fix a genuine centred target flattening $\Phi$ at $x\in X$ and
write $V=\Phi.\mathrm{target}$. For any original subset $W\subset X$,
its literal model set is
\[
 A_\Phi(W)=\{a\in V:e_x^{-1}(\Phi^{-1}(a))\in W\}\subset\mathbb C^n.
\]
It is empty outside the true target. No global extension of the
coordinate inverse is asserted holomorphic.

\begin{theorem}[The same flattened original vanishing ideal is the whole model vanishing ideal]
\label{hir:actual-fixed-flattening-whole-model-vanishing-ideal}
\uses{hir:real-centre-domain-actual-flattened-coherent-family,hir:actual-flat-coordinate-open-biholomorphism,hir:actual-any-subset-open-whole-vanishing-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFixedFlatteningHSComparison.actualFixedFlatteningSet,Hironaka.HironakaActualFixedFlatteningHSComparison.flattened_vanishing_mem_iff,Hironaka.HironakaActualFixedFlatteningHSComparison.originalFlattenedVanishingOriginIdeal_eq_model}
\leanok
Let $\Phi$ have its genuine source inside the original chart and
$\Phi(x)=0$. For every $a\in V$, pulling back the original whole
vanishing ideal of $W$ by the actual inverse flattening gives,
at $a$, exactly the vanishing germs of $A_\Phi(W)$. Consequently
the actual original origin ideal obtained by flattening that
vanishing sheaf and translating by zero is literally
$\mathcal I_{A_\Phi(W),0}$ inside the unchanged origin germ ring.
This holds for any subset $W$; no analyticity or coherence of
$W$ is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The inverse flattening is holomorphic near each point of $V$.
Every pulled original vanishing function vanishes on the literal
model set. Finite holomorphic linear combinations do too, proving
one membership implication.

Conversely, suppose a holomorphic germ $f$ at $a$ vanishes on the
model set. On the original flat domain define its inverse-coordinate
expression $g(u)=f(\Phi(e_x(u)))$. Both factors are holomorphic
there, so $g$ is holomorphic near the inverse image of $a$.
This inverse expression vanishes on $W$ near that point: the
forward coordinate lies in $V$, and the actual inverse identity
places it in $A_\Phi(W)$. Its pullback is exactly $f$ near $a$
by the true right inverse identity. Thus a single genuine pulled
vanishing germ generates $f$.

Zero lies in $V$ because $\Phi(x)=0$. Translation by zero is
the actual identity map. Applying the membership equivalence
to its finite generating expressions identifies the resulting
origin generators with the genuine model vanishing germs;
the vanishing ideal is already closed under finite holomorphic
combinations. Taking the whole ideal spans proves the literal
origin-ideal equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original Hilbert--Samuel value is computed in the same model set]
\label{hir:actual-fixed-flattening-original-intrinsic-hs-equality}
\uses{hir:actual-flat-coordinate-inverse-any-subset-intrinsic-hs,hir:actual-any-subset-open-intrinsic-hs}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFixedFlatteningHSComparison.hilbertSamuelAt_eq_fixedFlattening_model}
\leanok
For the same actual flattening and any subset $W\subset X$,
the intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel function of its whole vanishing
ideal at $x$ equals the intrinsic function of the whole model
vanishing ideal of $A_\Phi(W)$ at zero, at every order.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual inverse flattening is a biholomorphism from the true
target open $V$ to the original flat domain. Its inverse sends
zero to $x$. Invariance under that genuine biholomorphism and
the original open inclusion identifies the intrinsic function
at $x$ with that of the inverse-image subset on $V$. The
inverse-image subset equals $V\cap A_\Phi(W)$ by the literal
definition. The genuine open-restriction dictionary identifies
its whole stalk with the ambient model whole stalk at zero.
Their actual ambient maximal-power quotients therefore have
the same dimensions at every order.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A fixed actual polynomial blowup square retains the whole strict set and its intrinsic function]
\label{hir:actual-fixed-flattening-strict-set-hs-equality}
\uses{hir:actual-open-coordinate-geometric-strict-square,hir:actual-flat-coordinate-inverse-any-subset-intrinsic-hs,hir:actual-any-subset-open-intrinsic-hs}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFixedFlatteningHSComparison.strictTransform_eq_fixedFlattening_preimage,Hironaka.HironakaActualFixedFlatteningHSComparison.hilbertSamuelAt_strict_eq_fixedFlattening_model}
\leanok
Let $\pi:Y\to X$ be holomorphic, $z\in Y$, and let the genuine
centred local coordinates $\Phi$ at $\pi(z)$ and $\Theta$ at
$z$ give the whole actual square
\[
 \Phi(e_{\pi(z)}(\pi(u)))=
 P(\Theta(e_z(u))),\qquad
 P=\pi^{\mathrm{normal}}_{k,j,b},
\]
with all source images in the true flat domain of $\Phi$.
Put $U=\Theta.\mathrm{target}$ and let $c:U\to Y$ be its
genuine inverse coordinates. Then for any subset $W\subset X$
the whole strict sets satisfy
\[
 c^{-1}(\operatorname{strict}_\pi W)
 =U\cap\operatorname{strict}_P A_\Phi(W).
\]
Consequently the intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel function of the
actual strict-transform vanishing ideal at $z$ equals that
of the model strict-transform vanishing ideal at zero, at
every order. This requires no monic family, normal-power
property or Hilbert--Samuel inequality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual inverse coordinate biholomorphisms
$a:V\to X$ and $c:U\to Y$. The given whole square makes
$P$ map $U$ into $V$, so it restricts to a genuine
holomorphic map $Q:U\to V$ with $\pi c=aQ$.
The open-coordinate strict-transform theorem applied first
to $(\pi,Q,a,c)$ gives
\[
 \operatorname{strict}_Q(a^{-1}W)
 =c^{-1}(\operatorname{strict}_\pi W).
\]
Applied to the actual open inclusions for $(P,Q)$, it gives
\[
 \operatorname{strict}_Q(V\cap A_\Phi(W))
 =U\cap\operatorname{strict}_P A_\Phi(W).
\]
The two input subsets on $V$ are equal by definition, proving
the whole strict-set identity. These identities include
closures and the genuine exceptional loci; they do not
discard points on the exceptional divisor.

Zero lies in both genuine coordinate targets, and $c(0)=z$.
The inverse-coordinate whole-stalk dictionary transports
the original strict-transform intrinsic function to its
preimage under $c$. Replace that whole subset by the
proved strict-set identity, and then use the actual open
inclusion dictionary on $U$. This gives the asserted
equality at each order.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original blowup internally supplies the same fixed-chart Hilbert--Samuel comparisons]
\label{hir:actual-fixed-flattening-original-blowup-hs-comparisons}
\uses{hir:actual-fixed-flattening-blowup-coordinate-square,hir:actual-fixed-flattening-original-intrinsic-hs-equality,hir:actual-fixed-flattening-strict-set-hs-equality,hir:actual-fixed-flattening-whole-model-vanishing-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFixedFlatteningHSComparison.exists_actual_fixed_flattening_blowup_hs_comparisons}
\leanok
Let $\pi$ be the genuine blowup along $C$, and fix an actual
centred flattening $\Phi$ of that same centre at $\pi(z)$.
For every original subset $W$, the blowup internally supplies
a normal pivot, parameters and genuine source coordinates
such that both preceding intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel equalities
hold at every order, using this unchanged supplied $\Phi$
and its same whole model vanishing origin ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the actual blowup square for the supplied flattening
by the fixed-flattening coordinate theorem. Apply the whole
model vanishing-ideal equality and original intrinsic equality
to that same $\Phi$. Apply the whole strict-set and intrinsic
equality theorem to the genuine source coordinates and literal
square just constructed. Retain all source domains, the
original target flattening and the same model set. Every
order uses these unchanged witnesses, proving the conclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
These are exact coordinate and whole-ideal dictionaries.
They supply the geometric transport required to apply a
model Bennett inequality once the actual canonical reduced
vertex normal-power condition is proved for this same
original ideal. That condition and a terminating driver
with centres in the current singular locus remain separate.
\end{remark}
