\section{Actual higher-dimensional dynamic prefixes with the full ambient boundary}

Fix a positive ambient dimension, a compact smooth carrier $W$ of pure
positive dimension $d+1$, an ordered SNC divisor $E$, and
$W\subset\overline{W\setminus\bigcup E}$.  The original positive marked
input is the product of the original member ideals on $W$, with mark one
and empty auxiliary boundary.  At every actual prefix $T$, let $N_T$ and
$B_T$ be its actual strict carrier and entire born boundary, let $J_{i,T}$
be the independently saturated chronological member ideals, and let
$Z_T$ be the literal defect where their product order exceeds the number
of distinct proper ideal germs.  The value used below is the full
canonical invariant of their actual current product record.

\begin{definition}[Actual full-maximum choice history]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-dynamic-full-maximum-history}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-actual-dynamic-distinct-max-centre}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualDynamicIncidentHistory.DynamicMaximumHistory}
\leanok
A finite actual blowup history is a dynamic full-maximum history if each
of its prefixes has the same property and its last centre is the whole
full-invariant maximum on $Z_T$ when that locus is nonempty, whereas it
is empty when $Z_T$ is empty.  This definition records actual centre
choices; it imposes no factor identity, ambient boundary compatibility,
decrease, or termination requirement.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Original geometry internally produces every finite compatible dynamic prefix]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-original-dynamic-full-boundary-prefix}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-dynamic-full-maximum-history,hir:sharp-actual-full-maximum-original-member-orders,hir:sharp-actual-incident-centre-all-exceptional-residual,hir:sharp-actual-factor-forces-trace-incidence,hir:sharp-local-incident-containment-actual-centre-normal-crossings,hir:sharp-actual-dynamic-first-defect-original-admissible-centre,hir:nonmonomial-transform}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualDynamicIncidentHistory.exists_original_dynamic_incident_prefix}
\leanok
For every natural number $k$, the stated original data internally produce
an actual $k$-step dynamic full-maximum history $T$.  It is a genuine test
history of the same original marked product and also of the whole ambient
record with carrier $X$ and original boundary $E$.  Consequently its full
ordered original-strict-plus-born divisor is SNC.  For every original
position $i$, put $H_{i,T}=\operatorname{Strict}_T(E_i)$ and
$I_{i,T}=\operatorname{Res}_{N_T}\mathcal I_{H_{i,T}}$.
Then $H_{i,T}$ is smooth, $I_{i,T}$ is coherent and nonzero along $N_T$,
and $N_T\subset\overline{N_T\setminus H_{i,T}}$.  As whole germ ideals,
\[
 \mathcal N_{N_T}(I_{i,T},B_T)=J_{i,T},\qquad
 I_{i,T}=\operatorname{Res}_{N_T}\bigl(D_{i,T}J_{i,T}\bigr),\quad
 D_{i,T}=\mathcal M_{N_T}(I_{i,T},B_T).
\]
No compatible history, centre-incidence profile, or desired ideal equality
is a hypothesis.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At the identity prefix, the original singleton SNC members give all trace
geometry and their residuals are exactly the original relative ideals.
Suppose an actual prefix has been constructed.  If $Z_T$ is empty, append
an actual blowup along the empty centre.  Its centre conditions
are vacuous, and all the continuation arguments below apply unchanged.
Otherwise compactness of the true current carrier gives an attained
maximum of the full invariant on the analytic $Z_T$.  The actual maximum
centre theorem supplies a smooth admissible centre, constancy of every
chronological member order and proper-position set, and constancy of
every born-boundary incidence there.  The original controlled-product
comparison makes this same centre admissible for the unchanged original
positive marked input.

Fix a centre point.  The induction hypothesis and the genuine
monomial/nonmonomial factor theorem identify each whole ambient trace
with its greatest born-monomial factor times its chronological member.
Born incidence constancy and local constancy of the actual divisor
exponents fix whether that monomial factor is a unit.  Constancy of the
proper-position set fixes whether its chronological member is a unit.
The zero locus of a coherent product is the union of its zero loci, so
both facts together fix incidence with every original strict member.
Thus, locally, the centre is contained in each member of the entire
original-strict-plus-born divisor which it meets.  The genuine common
coordinate insertion theorem makes it a normal-crossings centre for
that entire SNC divisor.  Its actual blowup therefore appends a test
step for the full ambient boundary record.

For each original position, the higher-dimensional incident-centre
continuation theorem compares the next true ambient trace and next raw
pullback after removal of the entire old-plus-new born divisor.  Genuine
total-transform transport of old monomial factors and the induction
hypothesis replace the old residual by the same chronological member.
The independent all-power strict recursion identifies its new residual
with the next chronological member.  Hence the whole trace dictionary
holds at the next prefix.  The incident-centre theorem independently
supplies its smoothness, coherence, nonzero witnesses and density.
Induction constructs the required actual history at every finite length.
The final greatest-factor theorem supplies the stated whole factorization.
This construction proves continuation and full ambient SNC preservation;
it does not assert that the dynamic defect becomes empty, that the
original good locus has already been preserved, or that a sharp invariant
strictly decreases.
\end{proof}
