\section{Marking-one controlled compatibility of the actual double-locus prephase}
\label{sec:hironaka-normal-crossings-double-locus-marked-compatibility}

Let $H$ be a genuine NC carrier, and let $E$ be an actual finite SNC
boundary simultaneously adapted to $H$. Let $I$ be any coherent ambient
ideal, with marking one. The geometric input in this section is only
$D_2(H)\subseteq V(I)$: the ideal vanishes on the original multiple-branch
locus. Neither the entire carrier $H\subseteq V(I)$ nor any transformed
cosupport, centre-admissibility or canonical-history certificate is assumed.

\begin{lemma}[Axes force a zero constant and linear jet]
\label{hir:general-nc-double-axes-zero-first-jet}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsDoubleLocusMarkedCompatibility.inNormalPow_two_of_axis_vanishing}
\leanok
A holomorphic germ $F$ at zero in positive-dimensional complex coordinate
space, vanishing near zero on every coordinate axis, belongs to the square
of its maximal ideal.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The zero vector lies on any coordinate axis, so $F(0)=0$. Along the $i$th
axis the actual one-variable germ $t\mapsto F(te_i)$ is zero. Its derivative
at zero is therefore zero. The actual chain rule identifies that derivative
with $dF_0(e_i)$. The coordinate vectors form a basis, so $dF_0=0$. Every
iterated derivative of order less than two is either the value or a first
directional derivative and consequently vanishes at zero. The proved
holomorphic Taylor ideal-power criterion gives membership in the square
of the maximal ideal, with the same original germ.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual NC double locus has no linear equations at a triple point]
\label{hir:general-nc-double-locus-square-point-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsDoubleLocusMarkedCompatibility.mem_pointIdeal_sq_of_vanishes_doubleLocus}
\leanok
If $x$ has at least three actual local branches of $H$, every holomorphic
germ at $x$ vanishing on $D_2(H)$ near $x$ belongs to $\mathfrak m_x^2$.
\uses{hir:general-nc-double-axes-zero-first-jet,hir:general-nc-intrinsic-branch-multiplicity}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take an actual centred NC chart with selected original branch set $S$.
The intrinsic branch dictionary gives $|S|\geq3$. On each coordinate
axis, every selected coordinate except possibly its own index vanishes.
Thus at least two selected branch coordinates vanish there. The actual
nearby branch-count dictionary puts the inverse image of that axis in
$D_2(H)$. Pull the given holomorphic germ through the genuine chart
inverse. The resulting holomorphic germ at zero vanishes on every
coordinate axis, so the previous lemma gives its zero value and zero
linear jet and membership in the square of the coordinate maximal ideal.
The actual flattening coordinates all vanish at $x$; the proved ideal-power
pullback criterion therefore puts the unchanged original germ in
$\mathfrak m_x^2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual square containment along a highest triple-or-higher stratum]
\label{hir:general-nc-double-locus-ideal-square-highest-centre}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsDoubleLocusMarkedCompatibility.ideal_le_highestCentre_square_of_doubleLocus_subset_zeroLocus}
\leanok
Suppose $r\geq3$ bounds every branch count of $H$, let $C$ be the actual
whole $r$-branch stratum, and suppose $D_2(H)\subseteq V(I)$ for a coherent
ideal $I$. Then $I\subseteq I_C^2$.
\uses{hir:general-nc-double-locus-square-point-ideal,hir:general-nc-highest-multiplicity-smooth-centre}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A germ belonging to the coherent ideal at a point belongs to that same
ideal throughout some actual neighbourhood. Every such germ therefore
vanishes on $D_2(H)$ in that neighbourhood by the given zero-locus
containment. At each point of $C$ there are at least three branches, so
the previous lemma proves order at least two for every germ of $I$ there.
Thus $C\subseteq\operatorname{cosupp}(I,2)$. The actual highest-stratum
theorem proves that $C$ is a closed smooth centre. The established
coherent Hadamard theorem applied to this actual centre and the derived
order-two cosupport yields $I\subseteq I_C^2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[An extra exceptional factor remains after the actual marking-one division]
\label{hir:general-nc-square-centre-exceptional-controlled-cosupport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsDoubleLocusMarkedCompatibility.exceptional_subset_controlled_cosupport_of_square}
\leanok
For an actual blowup of $C$, if $I\subseteq I_C^2$,
the entire exceptional divisor belongs to the cosupport of the actual
marking-one controlled transform of $I$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Actual ideal pullback takes $I_C^2$ to $I_D^2$, where $D$ is the exceptional
divisor, so $\pi^*I\subseteq I_D^2$. The marking-one controlled transform
is the actual colon $(\pi^*I:I_D)$. Monotonicity of the numerator gives
$(\pi^*I:I_D)\subseteq(I_D^2:I_D)=I_D$, where the last equality is the
proved cancellation theorem for the invertible exceptional ideal. Every
germ of the controlled transform therefore vanishes on the entire
exceptional divisor. For marking one and full ambient carrier, the actual
cosupport-one dictionary is exactly that zero locus, proving the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual transformed double locus remains in controlled cosupport]
\label{hir:general-nc-double-locus-controlled-one-step}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsDoubleLocusMarkedCompatibility.doubleLocus_strict_subset_controlled_cosupport}
\leanok
Under $D_2(H)\subseteq V(I)$, every actual higher highest-stratum blowup
is admissible for $(I,1)$ relative to the same old boundary, and the double
locus of the actual strict original is contained in its actual controlled
cosupport.
\uses{hir:general-nc-double-locus-ideal-square-highest-centre,hir:general-nc-square-centre-exceptional-controlled-cosupport,hir:general-nc-higher-count-over-image}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual centre has branch count $r\geq2$, so it lies in $D_2(H)$ and
therefore in the marking-one cosupport. The simultaneous pair coordinates
give its genuine smoothness and normal crossings with the current old
boundary, proving actual admissibility. Away from the centre the genuine
blowup chart identifies the strict original branch count with its original
count at the image. A point of the new double locus there therefore maps
into the original double locus, and the proved off-centre controlled-transform
cosupport dictionary puts it in the new cosupport. Over the centre, if
$r=2$ the actual global strict count is at most one, so there is no new
double-locus point. If $r\geq3$, the preceding square-containment and
exceptional-factor lemmas put every exceptional point in the new controlled
cosupport. These cases cover the whole actual transformed double locus.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same actual NC boundary prephase is an unrelated marking-one test tower]
\label{hir:general-nc-double-locus-original-ideal-test-prephase}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsDoubleLocusMarkedCompatibility.higherSeparationCentres_isGeoTestTower_and_double_subset_cosupp,Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsDoubleLocusMarkedCompatibility.naturalSeparation_isGeoTestTower_of_doubleLocus_subset_zeroLocus}
\leanok
Under the original geometric input $D_2(H)\subseteq V(I)$, the internally
generated natural NC separation is a genuine test tower for the unchanged
marked datum $(X,X,E,I,1)$. Every actual intermediate double locus lies
in the same current controlled cosupport. Its terminal boundary is the
literal strict-old and exceptional list, and its actual projection is an
isomorphism outside the original double locus. In particular every centre
lies over the original $V(I)$ and the actual projection is an isomorphism
on the entire original complement $V(I)^c$. The same test-tower and current
double-locus containment assertions hold for any actual finite tower of
genuine higher-separation centres whose current strict original is NC.
\uses{hir:general-nc-double-locus-controlled-one-step,hir:general-nc-permitted-double-locus-prephase}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on an actual finite higher-stratum tower. Initially
the carrier is the whole ambient manifold, the ideal is the given original
$I$, and the double-locus cosupport assertion is exactly the given geometric
zero-locus containment and the marking-one dictionary. The one-step theorem
derives admissibility of the actual next internally chosen centre and
preserves the double-locus containment for the actual controlled transform.
The actual transformed full ambient carrier is again the full ambient
manifold, the marking remains one, and the literal boundary is precisely
the strict old list followed by the actual exceptional preimage. The real
test-tower step constructor then records the whole unchanged-ideal history.
The actual differential strict-transform composition dictionary identifies
the terminal carrier of this same composite. Apply this proved induction
to the internally generated natural higher-centre separation. The previous
geometric separation theorem gives its length bound, terminal SNC geometry
and original-double-locus complement isomorphism. The original zero locus
is closed by coherence and contains the double locus by hypothesis. Its
actual permitted-set separation consumer puts every centre over $V(I)$
and proves the isomorphism on $V(I)^c$. No canonical lower-history identity
or marking greater than one is asserted.
\end{proof}
