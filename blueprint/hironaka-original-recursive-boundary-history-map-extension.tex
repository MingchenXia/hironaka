\section{Actual canonical initial-boundary compatibility at every recursive depth}

Keep every original indexed member and entire original canonical history.
The recursive chronological history maps differ from the uniform metadata
insertion maps on arbitrary symbols. Their equality on ACTUAL canonical
values is proved from the true lower canonical data and original empty
prefixes. This is the ordered-boundary recursive route of
\cite{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}, adapted by
constructing each lower input internally from the original history.

\begin{lemma}[Recursive chronological maps on unchanged whole value types]
\label{hir:original-recursive-chronological-history-value-maps}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-invariant-empty-map,hir:original-invariant-empty-map-strict,hir:original-short-history-step-map}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.recursiveHistoryShift,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.recursiveStepShift,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.recursiveHistoryShift_succ,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.strictMono_recursiveHistoryShift,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.strictMono_recursiveStepShift,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.recursiveHistoryShiftEmbedding,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.recursiveStepShiftEmbedding,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.recursiveHistoryShift_zero,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.recursiveHistoryShift_one}
\leanok
On the unchanged original types define
\[
 H_{0,j}=\mathrm{id},\qquad
 S_{k,j}(\ell,v)=(\ell,\operatorname{map}(H_{k,j-\ell})(v)),\qquad
 H_{k+1,j}=\operatorname{generalShift}(j,S_{k,j}),
\]
where subtraction is clamped at zero and the masked map fixes infinity.
Every $H_{k,j}$ and $S_{k,j}$ is strict and therefore an order embedding.
For every depth, $H_{k,0}=\operatorname{shiftFamily}(k,0)$; also
$H_{1,j}=\operatorname{shiftFamily}(1,j)$.
No assertion on actual history values follows from these algebraic facts alone.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on $k$, simultaneously for all natural indices. At zero the family
map is the identity. For the Step One map, strict first-coordinate
inequalities are retained. If both first coordinates equal $\ell$, the
same index $j-\ell$ occurs on both lower coordinates and strictness of
$H_{k,j-\ell}$, extended by infinity, reflects the lower inequality.
The established strict general map therefore makes $H_{k+1,j}$ strict.
At index zero, $0-\ell=0$ for every level, so $S_{k,0}$ is the pair map
of $H_{k,0}$. The induction identity at zero gives precisely the defining
recursion of the original uniform zero-index family shift. At depth one,
$S_{0,j}$ is the identity pair map for every level; the defining original
first-depth shift has that same pair map. These are identities on the
unchanged recursive types; the zero-index monomial map is not replaced
by an identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual original canonical Step One values have initial-boundary compatibility]
\label{hir:original-recursive-canonical-step-initial-boundary-compatibility}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-recursive-chronological-history-value-maps,hir:original-initial-boundary-actual-lower-history-prefix,hir:step-one-canonical-tail,hir:original-crossing-run-base-regime,hir:original-invariant-empty-actual}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.original_canonical_step_initial_boundary_compatibility}
\leanok
For EVERY family depth $k$, original maximal-order record $M$ in the actual
bound-$(k+1)$ domain, its OLD entire Step One $R_k$-canonical history $T$,
actual transformed cosupport point $y$, and true ORIGINAL index
$j\leq |M.E|$, internally prove
\[
 S_{k,j}(\operatorname{StepOne}(R_k;M,T,y))
 =\operatorname{pairShift}(\operatorname{shiftFamily}(k,0))
    (\operatorname{StepOne}(R_k;M,T,y)).
\]
No lower-domain, lower-canonicality, empty-prefix, branch or value certificate
is a caller premise. The family depth induction is discharged internally.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The proof is simultaneous with the following entire-family theorem by
natural-number depth induction. At lower depth zero the map is the identity.
Assume the actual entire-family initial-boundary comparison at depth $k$.
Original Step One canonicality internally gives barred testness, actual
barred cosupport and genuine canonical lower data at every nonspecial
cosupport point. A special point has infinite lower value, fixed by both
maps. At a nonspecial point put $\ell=\operatorname{level}(M,T,y)$ and
split at its actual own-level run, obtaining the true head $K$, suffix $Q$
and actual point $p=Qy$. Current regime membership and the true suffix
length imply that the head point has this SAME level $\ell$; whole barred
testness supplies actual head testness. Original canonical-tail data give
the actual lower marked record $L$, its bound-$k$ domain, OLD entire
restricted $R_k$-canonical history, mask membership and genuine cosupport.
The proved original initial-boundary prefix theorem internally supplies
$d=j-\ell\leq |L.E|$ and the actual first $d$ entries empty, including the
true chosen-contact branch at zero level. Apply the depth induction hypothesis
to THIS actual lower record and history at index $d$. It identifies
$H_{k,d}$ on its whole original value with the original uniform shift at
$d$. The actual specific-family empty-prefix theorem makes that uniform
shift equal the zero-index shift on THIS value. Expanding the true masked
value and retaining first coordinate $\ell$ gives the displayed whole
comparison. Every lower property used here is constructed from original
canonical input, rather than supplied as a desired conclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual original canonical family has recursive initial-boundary compatibility]
\label{hir:original-recursive-canonical-family-initial-boundary-compatibility}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-recursive-canonical-step-initial-boundary-compatibility,hir:original-recursive-chronological-history-value-maps,hir:general-canonical-tail,hir:original-canonical-general-crossing-companion-data,hir:original-invariant-empty-actual}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.original_canonical_family_initial_boundary_compatibility,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalRecursiveBoundaryHistoryMap.original_canonical_family_empty_initial_boundary_compatibility}
\leanok
For EVERY depth $k$, ONLY original bound-$k$ record $M$, its OLD whole
$R_k$-canonical history $T$, actual transformed cosupport point $y$ and
true ORIGINAL index $j\leq |M.E|$, internally prove
\[
 H_{k,j}(R_k(M,T,y))=\operatorname{shiftFamily}(k,j)(R_k(M,T,y)).
\]
Consequently a genuine first-$j$ empty original boundary prefix gives
$H_{k,j}(R_k(M,T,y))=H_{k,0}(R_k(M,T,y))$.
No caller comparison, lower canonicality, branch or arbitrary-invariant
certificate is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At depth zero both maps are the identity. Given the entire depth-$k$ result,
the preceding argument proves Step One initial-boundary compatibility for
its actual lower family. Now consider original depth $k+1$ and inspect its
actual outer order. At order zero, both maps apply exactly the same
monomial chronological insertion at index $j$, so they agree on the ENTIRE
actual family leaf without a lower premise. At positive order original
full canonical-tail data internally supply the actual assignment head,
order mask, true companion $J$, bound-$(k+1)$ domain, maximal order, OLD
whole Step One $R_k$-canonical restricted tail, mask membership and cosupport.
The entire companion boundary retains $|M.E|+|K|$ entries, so the ORIGINAL
bound $j\leq |M.E|$ gives $j\leq |J.E|$. Apply the Step One result just
proved to THIS actual companion. Thus its true recursive inner map agrees
with the original uniform zero-index pair map. The outer general map
expansion is exactly the original uniform depth-$(k+1)$ shift at index $j$.
This completes the genuine specific-family depth induction, uniformly over
all original records, real restricted carriers and whole histories. Finally,
if the first $j$ original members are empty, the established actual
specific-family empty-prefix theorem equates the uniform shifts at $j$ and
zero on the actual original value; the first lemma identifies that zero
shift with $H_{k,0}$. The comparison is at a valid INITIAL boundary index;
it supplies the actual compatibility needed for future lower records, whose
initial lists include earlier chronological exceptional members. Arbitrary
higher interior history transport and noncompact scheduling remain further
obligations.
\end{proof}
