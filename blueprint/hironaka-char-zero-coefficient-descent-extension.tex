\section{Actual finite coefficient descent in characteristic zero}

Let $K$ be an arbitrary characteristic-zero field. The constructions in
this chapter require no embedding of $K$ in the complex numbers. They
produce actual finitely generated fields of definition and exact scalar
extension isomorphisms. They do not construct resolution centres,
algebraize analytic centres, or assert resolution of schemes or abstract
analytic spaces.

\begin{theorem}[A finite system has an actual finite coefficient field]
\label{hir:char-zero-finite-relations-coefficient-descent}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCharZeroCoefficientDescent.finite_relations_descend}
\leanok
\uses{hir:relative-normal-coefficient-polynomial-quotient-base-change}
For every finite family $P_1,\ldots,P_r\in K[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$
there are an actual intermediate field $\mathbb Q\subset L\subset K$,
finitely generated as a field over $\mathbb Q$, and polynomials
$Q_i\in L[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ whose coefficient extensions are exactly
$P_i$. The algebra $A_0=L[X]/(Q_1,\ldots,Q_r)$ is finitely presented
over $L$, and there is an actual $K$-algebra isomorphism
\[
 K\otimes_L A_0\simeq K[X]/(P_1,\ldots,P_r).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $S$ be the union of the finite coefficient sets of the finitely
many $P_i$, and take the actual subfield $L=\mathbb Q(S)\subset K$.
The set $S$ is finite, so $L$ is finitely generated as a field over
$\mathbb Q$. Every coefficient of each $P_i$ belongs to $L$.
Replacing each coefficient by the same element regarded in $L$
therefore gives $Q_i$ with coefficient extension exactly $P_i$.

The ideal generated by the finite family $Q_i$ is finitely generated,
so its polynomial quotient $A_0$ is finitely presented. Extension of
ideals commutes with generating spans: the extension of $(Q_i)_i$
is the ideal generated by the extended $Q_i$, and hence is precisely
$(P_i)_i$. The actual polynomial-quotient tensor base-change
isomorphism, applied to the whole ideal $(Q_i)_i$, now gives the
displayed isomorphism. No relation, coefficient, or ideal is replaced
by an ideal with only the same zero locus.
\end{proof}



\begin{theorem}[Every actual finite-type algebra has a finite coefficient model]
\label{hir:char-zero-finite-type-algebra-coefficient-model}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCharZeroCoefficientDescent.finite_type_algebra_descends}
\leanok
\uses{hir:char-zero-finite-relations-coefficient-descent}
Let $A$ be a finite-type commutative $K$-algebra. There are an actual
finitely generated intermediate field $\mathbb Q\subset L\subset K$
and a finite polynomial presentation $A_0=L[X_1,\ldots,X_n]/(Q_1,
\ldots,Q_r)$ such that $A_0$ is finitely presented over $L$ and
$K\otimes_L A_0$ is isomorphic to the original $A$ as a $K$-algebra.
The presentation and its relations are constructed internally from
$A$; no model or scalar-extension isomorphism is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A field is Noetherian. Hence the given finite-type algebra is finitely
presented: choose a surjective map from a polynomial ring in finitely
many variables to $A$. The polynomial ring is Noetherian, so the
kernel of this actual map is generated by a finite family $P_i$.
The quotient by $(P_i)_i$ is therefore isomorphic to the original
$A$ by the quotient-kernel isomorphism theorem.

Apply the preceding finite coefficient construction to these actual
relations. It supplies $L$, the lifted relations $Q_i$, finite
presentation of $A_0$, and a scalar-extension isomorphism from
$K\otimes_L A_0$ to $K[X]/(P_i)_i$. Compose that isomorphism with
the actual quotient-kernel isomorphism to $A$. This constructs all
the asserted data and retains the original algebra $A$ exactly.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An actual integral algebra has an integral coefficient model]
\label{hir:char-zero-finite-type-domain-coefficient-model}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCharZeroCoefficientDescent.finite_type_domain_descends}
\leanok
\uses{hir:char-zero-finite-type-algebra-coefficient-model}
If the original finite-type $K$-algebra $A$ is a domain, the same
construction supplies a finitely presented domain $A_0$ over an
actual finitely generated rational subfield $L\subset K$, with
$K\otimes_L A_0\simeq A$ as $K$-algebras.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the coefficient model and actual scalar-extension isomorphism
from the preceding theorem. Since $L$ is a field, $A_0$ is flat as
an $L$-module. The inclusion $L\hookrightarrow K$ is injective;
tensoring it with this flat module, and identifying
$L\otimes_L A_0$ with $A_0$, shows that
$A_0\to K\otimes_L A_0$, $a\mapsto1\otimes a$, is injective.
Compose with the actual isomorphism to $A$. This embeds $A_0$ in
the original domain $A$, so $A_0$ is a domain. The field, finite
presentation and scalar-extension data are unchanged.
\end{proof}
