\section{Actual locally terminal histories from componentwise compact cosupport}
\label{sec:hironaka-componentwise-canonical-history}

The fixed partition throughout this section is a disjoint open cover of
the original ambient manifold. Restriction retains every actual stage,
including empty steps on components which have already terminated.

\begin{theorem}[Parallel whole maxima construct a growing actual history]
\label{hir:componentwise-canonical-growing-history}
\uses{hir:componentwise-canonical-global-refinement,hir:centre-function-seq,hir:open-restriction-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseCanonicalHistory.ParallelSteps,Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseCanonicalHistory.exists_parallel_canonical_sequence,Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseCanonicalHistory.restricted_steps_are_maxima}
\leanok
Let $R$ be a proved resolution invariant, $M$ an original marked ideal
of its domain, and $X=\coprod_aU_a$ a disjoint open cover. Then the
original input internally constructs an actual growing sequence
$T_j:X_j\to X$, starting with the reflexive original tower. Every
$T_j$ is a genuine test prefix. Every actual restriction $T_j|_{U_a}$
is canonical for $M|_{U_a}$, and the next actual blowup has centre
\[
 C_j=\bigcup_a\iota_{a,j}
 \operatorname{maxLocus}(\operatorname{inv}(M|_{U_a},T_j|_{U_a}),
                 \operatorname{cosupp}(M|_{U_a})_{T_j|_{U_a}}).
\]
In particular, each true restricted sequence is a growing sequence
whose next centre is its whole canonical maximum.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take as states the actual towers over $X$ equipped with the facts
that they are test prefixes and that all their restrictions to
the fixed opens $U_a$ are canonical. The reflexive tower is a state.
The global refinement theorem constructs a next state by blowing
up the displayed whole union; its global admissibility supplies
the test property and its exact traces supply the canonical
properties of every restricted next tower. Choose such a next
state for each state and iterate, beginning with the reflexive
one. The recorded chosen blowups give the growing sequence
equalities, without removing any stage.

Restrict each recorded next blowup to $U_a$. The actual restricted
blowup centre is the inverse image of $C_j$ in $T_j^{-1}(U_a)$.
The exact trace theorem identifies this whole inverse image with
the displayed local maximum. Thus the restricted growing sequence
has exactly the required maximum steps.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A maximum sequence with compact original cosupport terminates]
\label{hir:compact-cosupport-maximum-sequence-terminates}
\uses{hir:centre-function-seq,hir:componentwise-canonical-growing-history,hir:resolution-driver}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseCanonicalHistory.eventually_resolves_maximum_sequence}
\leanok
Let $M$ be in the domain of $R$ with compact original cosupport.
Every actual growing sequence, all of whose prefixes are canonical
and all of whose next centres are the whole current maximum,
is a resolution at every sufficiently late stage.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The current cosupport is closed and lies inside the actual
proper preimage of the original compact cosupport, and hence
is compact at every stage. If it were nonempty at every stage,
upper semicontinuity would give a nonempty whole maximum centre
at every stage. Every such centre meets that same proper
preimage of the original cosupport. Therefore the count of
centres meeting the original cosupport at stage $j$ is exactly
$j$, by induction on the recorded actual next-step equality.
This contradicts per-sequence local finiteness of $R$ on that
original compact set.

Consequently some stage has empty cosupport. For every following
admissible step, the image of the next cosupport is contained
in the preceding cosupport. Induction therefore makes every
later cosupport empty as well. Those later prefixes are still
test prefixes because they are canonical, so they are resolutions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original componentwise compact input gives locally terminal actual histories]
\label{hir:original-componentwise-compact-locally-terminal-history}
\uses{hir:componentwise-canonical-growing-history,hir:compact-cosupport-maximum-sequence-terminates,hir:original-componentwise-compact-canonical-first-step,hir:componentwise-history-locally-finite-empty-tails}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseCanonicalHistory.exists_original_componentwise_locally_terminal_history}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an original marked ideal in the domain of the actual
canonical invariant. Suppose the original cosupport on every
connected ambient component is compact. Then the original input
internally constructs a growing actual global test history,
with the whole parallel component centres above, such that on
each original component all sufficiently late actual restrictions
are canonical resolutions. The required terminal stage may
depend on the component; no common finite stage or global
maximum invariant is assumed. Its actual centre images are closed
and locally finite, and its actual later tails have the properties
of the following general theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The literal connected components of a complex manifold are
open and form a disjoint cover. Use these fixed original opens
in the constructed parallel sequence. Restricting that actual
sequence to a component retains all of its recorded actual
stages and gives whole maximum steps. The domain condition
and the genuine restricted-cosupport dictionary identify
its original cosupport with the compact set in the hypothesis.
Apply the preceding lemma separately to every component.
The resulting component terminal times refer to this same
constructed global history, rather than to independently
chosen local histories. Apply the following general local-tail
theorem to this sequence and these internally obtained terminal
times to obtain the closed, locally finite centre images and
the actual empty and isomorphic tails.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The constructed centres are locally finite and all late local tails are empty]
\label{hir:componentwise-history-locally-finite-empty-tails}
\uses{hir:componentwise-canonical-growing-history,bcalc:growing-seq-tail,hir:open-restriction-tower,hir:growing-canonical-sequence-closed-images,hir:growing-canonical-sequence-isomorphism-tail,hir:growing-sequence-empty-restricted-tails}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseCanonicalHistory.centreImage_parallel_sequence,Hironaka.HironakaComponentwiseCanonicalHistory.locally_finite_empty_tails_of_component_terminal}
\leanok
Let an actual parallel whole-maximum history be eventually a
resolution on each member of its fixed original disjoint open
partition. Then the images in $X$ of its actual next centres
are closed and locally finite. Every member of the partition
is a genuine open neighbourhood on which every sufficiently
late actual step is an isomorphism and every entire finite
later tail restricts to an empty-centre tower.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a late stage on one original component the restricted
cosupport is empty. Its whole maximum centre is therefore
empty. Exact whole traces imply that the actual global
next centre misses the full preimage of that component.
The actual centre image consequently misses this component
at every late index. Only the finitely many earlier indices
can meet that component, which proves local finiteness.
Each actual centre is closed and its actual prefix projection
is proper, so its entire image is closed.

An actual blowup is an isomorphism off its actual centre,
hence is an isomorphism over the preimage of this same open
component at every late stage. The actual finite tail is
formed by appending precisely these later recorded blowups.
The prefix--tail projection identity identifies each tail
centre's preimage of the component with its global one.
Induction gives that every centre of the finite restricted
tail is empty. No empty-step position is erased.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The global histories here need not be globally canonical:
different components can have different maxima, with no
largest value among them. Their actual restrictions to the
fixed original components are canonical. Componentwise
compact original cosupport is an explicit extra geometric
hypothesis. This does not construct the unrestricted
connected noncompact resolution scheduler.
\end{remark}
