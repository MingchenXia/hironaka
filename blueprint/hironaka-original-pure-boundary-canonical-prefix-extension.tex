\section{The actual canonical embedded prefix for a pure analytic carrier}

Let $W$ be an original compact analytic subset of a complex manifold $X$,
pure of dimension $d<n$, and let $E_0$ be an ordered SNC divisor such that
$W\subseteq\overline{W\setminus|E_0|}$.  Write
\[
 M=(X,X,E_0,I_W,1),\qquad
 O=X\setminus((W\setminus W_{\mathrm{reg},d})\cup|E_0|),\qquad
 F=(W\setminus W_{\mathrm{reg},d})\cup(W\cap|E_0|).
\]
The ambient manifold need not be compact, and $W$ may be empty or reducible.

\begin{theorem}[Original pure-carrier data retain the actual first-meeting canonical prefix]
\label{hir:original-pure-boundary-canonical-first-prefix}
\uses{hir:embedded-resolution-boundary,hir:canonical-resolution-compact-cosupport,hir:original-cosupport-persistence-avoided-open,hir:original-canonical-boundary-principalization-compact-cosupport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureBoundaryCanonicalPrefix.boundaryGoodOpen,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureBoundaryCanonicalPrefix.exists_original_pure_boundary_canonical_prefix}
\leanok
From only these original data, internally choose the actual finite
canonical terminal tower $P$ of $M$.  There are an actual canonical
prefix $T:D\to X$ and its actual tail $R$ with $T\mathbin{+\!+}R=P$,
whose centres avoid $O$.  If $W$ is nonempty, the next whole maximum
locus of this same prefix meets its inverse image of $O$.  If $W$ is
empty, $T$ is the identity and every centre of $P$ is empty.

The same $T$ is an admissible sequence relative to $E_0$, all its
centres lie over $F$, and its actual accumulated divisor $E_T$ is SNC.
Its conventional strict carrier $W_T$ is a closed submanifold, regular
of dimension $d$ everywhere, with normal crossings with its own $E_T$.
Moreover,
\[
 \operatorname{Exc}p_T\subseteq\bigcup(E_T\text{ after }|E_0|)
       \subseteq p_T^{-1}F,\qquad
 p_T^{-1}(W\setminus W_{\mathrm{reg},d})\subseteq\operatorname{Exc}p_T.
\]
The same projection is holomorphic, proper, surjective, and an
isomorphism over $X\setminus F$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original analytic, pure positive-codimension carrier and original
SNC divisor put $M$ in the invariant domain; its cosupport is exactly
$W$.  Compactness of $W$ therefore supplies an actual finite canonical
terminal tower $P$.  The original density hypotheses give
$W\subseteq\overline{W\cap O}$, and each original boundary member is
disjoint from $O$.

If $W$ is nonempty, $W\cap O$ is nonempty.  Were every centre of $P$
to avoid $O$, lift such an original point through the surjective whole
projection.  Cosupport persistence over the avoided open would put its
lift in the final cosupport, contradicting terminality.  Thus the
actual first-centre splitting selects a prefix $T$, actual tail $R$,
canonicality, avoidance and a next whole maximum point over $O$.
Homogeneity of the original pure-dimensional carrier over the
boundary-free $O$ identifies this whole maximum there with precisely
$p_T^{-1}W$.  Its actual maximal locus is a smooth admissible centre.
The analytic strict-transform continuation theorem then identifies
$W_T$ locally at each of its points with that smooth centre, giving
dimension $d$ and its normal crossings with $E_T$.

If $W$ is empty, choose the identity prefix and the whole $P$ as tail.
Empty original cosupport forces every actual canonical centre of $P$
to be empty.  Its strict carrier is empty, so all the asserted regular
and normal-crossings conditions hold directly.

In both cases canonicality gives actual testness and the SNC boundary.
All actual centres lie over $W$ and, by avoidance, over $X\setminus O$;
their intersection is contained in $F$.  This gives the admissible
sequence and exceptional-divisor support over $F$.  Its support is
exactly the union of the new boundary members, and contains the entire
exceptional locus, yielding the two displayed containments on the
left.  Smoothness and dimension $d$ of the strict carrier give the
displayed singular-preimage containment.  The finite blowup projection
is holomorphic, proper and surjective; its exceptional image lies in
$F$, so it is an isomorphism over the complement.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same pure-carrier canonical prefix has a genuine smooth source]
\label{hir:original-pure-boundary-prefix-smooth-comparison}
\uses{hir:original-pure-boundary-canonical-first-prefix,hir:original-stopped-strict-transform-smooth-square,hir:smooth-original-ambient-canonical-history-values,hir:submersion-relative-control-joint-tower,hir:submersion-relative-regular-geometry,hir:submersion-relative-nc-transversality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureBoundaryCanonicalPrefix.exists_original_pure_boundary_stopped_smooth_source}
\leanok
For any holomorphic submersion $b:Y\to X$, the same original data
internally construct the preceding actual target prefix $T$, its tail,
and an actual smooth pullback prefix $Q:C\to Y$ for the literal marked
record $N=b^*M$.  Its upper map $g:C\to D$ is a holomorphic submersion
in a genuine holomorphic Cartesian square, and $Q$ has the same actual
length as $T$.  Its whole transformed record is $g^*M_T$, hence
$E_Q=g^{-1}E_T$ as entire ordered lists.  The source is canonical for
this literal $N$, with its own SNC divisor and an admissible sequence
relative to $b^{-1}E_0$, all centres lying over $b^{-1}F$.

The conventional strict carriers satisfy the whole equality
\[
 \operatorname{Strict}_{Q}(b^{-1}W)=g^{-1}\operatorname{Strict}_{T}(W).
\]
The source strict carrier is a closed submanifold, regular of dimension
$d+(\dim Y-\dim X)$, with normal crossings with its own actual $E_Q$.
Its projection is holomorphic, proper and surjective; its exceptional
locus lies in the union of its new boundary members, this union lies
over $b^{-1}F$, and it is an isomorphism over $Y\setminus b^{-1}F$.
The complete target dictionary of the preceding theorem is retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Invoke the preceding construction once and retain its same target
prefix and tail.  Pull that actual prefix back one genuine blowup at
a time under $b$.  The actual blowup universal properties supply the
upper submersion and Cartesian square.  Original ambient invariant
transport gives canonicality of $Q$ for $b^*M$, and the whole controlled
record dictionary gives its testness and the literal ordered boundary
equality.  The exact projection equations at each actual stage pull
the whole target centre containment back to $b^{-1}F$.  Consequently
the source sequence is admissible relative to its actual original
boundary, and actual well-formedness gives its SNC divisor.

The target centres avoid $O$, and $W\cap O$ is dense in $W$.
Openness of both the original submersion and the actual upper
submersion commutes inverse image with the relevant closures.  The
genuine Cartesian projection equation and the strict-transform
avoided-open dictionary therefore give the displayed whole strict
carrier equality, including exceptional points.  Pulling the actual
target regular coordinates back under $g$ gives the source dimension
and closed smooth carrier.  Pulling its normal-crossings coordinates
back gives normal crossings with the literal $g^{-1}E_T=E_Q$.
The source new-boundary support formula and its actual centres over
$b^{-1}F$ give its exceptional containments.  The actual finite source
projection is holomorphic, proper and surjective, and is an isomorphism
off this bad-set preimage.  No compactness of $Y$ is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The source canonical datum is literally $b^*M$.  No equality with a
separately reduced vanishing ideal of $b^{-1}W$ is assumed or inferred.
This source is the actual pullback of the selected target prefix;
comparison with a separately stopped source and arbitrary empty-step
counts is a further construction.
\end{remark}
