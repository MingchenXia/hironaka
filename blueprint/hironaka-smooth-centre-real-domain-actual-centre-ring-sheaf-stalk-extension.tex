\section{The whole original centre ring-sheaf stalk}

Keep the already constructed original centre quotient ring presheaf and
its genuine ring sheaf. Let $R_y$ be the actual original structure-ring
stalk and $J_y$ the whole original vanishing ideal of $C$ in that stalk.

\begin{definition}[Original centre quotient germs]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-original-quotient-germs}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-quotient-ring-sheaf,hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ideal-section-whole-stalk-equivalence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingSectionGermHom,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingSectionGermHom_mk,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingGermCocone,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingStalkToWholeHom,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingStalkToWholeHom_germ}
\leanok
Every actual quotient section on an original neighbourhood maps to
$R_y/J_y$ by taking the same original numerator germ. These genuine ring
maps form the neighbourhood cocone, hence give a ring map from the
actual centre quotient presheaf stalk to the whole original quotient.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Every centre section germ belongs to $J_y$ by its pointwise definition,
so the quotient-ring universal property constructs the quotient germ
map. Actual germ restriction keeps every numerator, proving the
neighbourhood cocone naturality. The genuine ring colimit yields the
whole stalk map and its formula on every original quotient section germ.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual centre quotient presheaf stalk is the whole original quotient]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-whole-presheaf-stalk}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-original-quotient-germs,hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ideal-section-whole-stalk-equivalence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingStalkToWholeHom_surjective,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingStalkToWholeHom_injective,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingStalkWholeEquiv}
\leanok
The actual quotient germ ring map is bijective and hence a ring
isomorphism onto the whole original $R_y/J_y$. Every original quotient
numerator retains exactly its original germ class.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Surjectivity follows by choosing an ambient representative of any
quotient class, then an actual holomorphic section germ representing it.
For injectivity represent two actual stalk elements by actual quotient
sections and restrict their numerators to one common original open.
Equality of their whole quotient germs says their difference germ lies
in $J_y$. The independently proved whole centre-section germ image
identity provides a true original centre section representing that
difference germ, without any coherence assumption. Actual germ equality
then gives a smaller original open where the restricted numerator
difference equals that centre section. It belongs to the actual centre
section ideal there, so the two quotient sections agree there. Actual
stalk germ extensionality proves equality of the original stalk elements.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine centre ring sheaf has the whole original quotient stalk]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-whole-sheaf-stalk}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-whole-presheaf-stalk,hir:real-domain-actual-centre-quotient-ring-sheaf}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingUnitStalkIso,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingSheafStalkWholeEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingSheafStalkWholeEquiv_unit,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingSheafStalkWholeEquiv_germ,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingSheafStalkWholeEquiv_ambientHom}
\leanok
The genuine original centre ring sheaf stalk is ring-isomorphic to
$R_y/J_y$. Every unit image of an original quotient section germ keeps
the same whole original quotient numerator, and the actual ambient
stalk morphism becomes the original quotient map $R_y\to R_y/J_y$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine ring sheafification unit is internally an isomorphism on
actual ring stalks. Compose its inverse with the preceding whole
presheaf-stalk isomorphism. Its unit and germ identities retain every
original quotient section germ. Represent any ambient scalar by a true
original section; stalk functoriality for the actual quotient sheaf
morphism and the quotient numerator identity give exactly its original
whole quotient class.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The global centre ring-sheaf action agrees with the original whole quotient action]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-sheaf-whole-action}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-whole-sheaf-stalk,hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-sheaf-stalk-factorization,hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-ring-sheaf-centre-ring-map}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheafStalk.actualCentreRingAssociatedSheafStalkHom_whole}
\leanok
For coherent original $A$, identifying the genuine centre ring-sheaf
stalk with the whole original $R_y/J_y$ makes its actual ring homomorphism
to the associated ring-sheaf stalk exactly the previously independently
constructed quotient ring homomorphism. Every whole quotient scalar
therefore keeps the same original ambient product.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose an ambient numerator $r$ for an arbitrary whole quotient scalar.
The actual ambient centre stalk image is sent by the preceding genuine
ring isomorphism to $[r]$, so it is the inverse image of this scalar.
The global sheaf factorization sends it to the same original ambient
associated-ring stalk image of $r$. The earlier genuine quotient ring
homomorphism has exactly this numerator formula. Quotient numerator
surjectivity therefore proves equality for every scalar.
\end{proof}
