\begin{proof}

$(\beta_e+1)(G_a)_{\beta+\mathbf1_e}$ in the indicated homogeneous

entire derivative is normal outside that diagram. The actual source
initial diagram equals the original diagram, and a member of the
whole initial ideal with all its diagram coefficients zero is zero.
Thus the derivative vanishes. In characteristic zero, at every positive
pivot exponent the derivative coefficient is a nonzero natural scalar
times the original coefficient. Its vanishing kills every such
coefficient, proving actual pivot freeness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual homogeneous current members normal on the source diagram vanish]
\label{hir:actual-current-initial-source-normal-independence}

again source normal. The induction hypothesis gives $G=0$, and
therefore $F=0$. This uses no old/current diagram inclusion.
