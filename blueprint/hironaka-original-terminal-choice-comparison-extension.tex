\section{Actual terminal choice comparison after internally omitting empty steps}

Keep the ORIGINAL marked record and every original indexed boundary member.
Two actual finite canonical histories may use different genuine blowup
choices and different numbers of actual empty steps. The route of
\cite{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality} is adapted by
internally compressing their actual empty steps BEFORE the genuine
canonical terminal-history comparison. The original histories need not
have equal recorded length and are not asserted to be isomorphic as
uncompressed recorded towers.

\begin{lemma}[Actual nonempty canonical histories satisfy the true canonical segment condition]
\label{hir:original-actual-nonempty-canonical-segment}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:canonical-segments,hir:blowup-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalChoiceComparison.isCanonicalSegment_of_actual_nonempty_centres}
\leanok
A whole canonical history whose EVERY actual recorded centre is nonempty
is a genuine canonical segment with no empty steps. This supplies the
entire segment condition from true whole canonicality and the existing
every-centre predicate, rather than a chosen list of active centres.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual tower. The empty tower satisfies both whole conditions.
At a next genuine blowup, whole canonicality supplies canonicality of its
actual prefix and the alternative that its actual centre is empty or the
entire original maximum locus. The actual every-centre condition supplies
prefix nonemptiness and actual centre nonemptiness. The empty alternative
is therefore impossible. The induction gives the prefix canonical segment,
and this genuine nonempty maximum centre gives the entire next canonical
segment. No existence of a maximum is inferred beyond the given true
canonical centre.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original actual terminal histories compare after internal empty compression]
\label{hir:original-terminal-histories-actual-choice-comparison}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-terminal-histories-actual-biholomorphic-base-comparison}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalChoiceComparison.original_terminal_histories_actual_choice_comparison}
\leanok
At EVERY actual specific family depth, ONLY original bound-$k$ marked
input and TWO GIVEN entire actual canonical terminal histories internally
supply an actual biholomorphism between their ORIGINAL final manifolds
commuting with their WHOLE original projections to the SAME original base.
Their recorded lengths and actual empty-step choices may differ. No
nonempty-centre, compressed history, canonical segment or final comparison
certificate is supplied by a caller, and no compactness is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the following genuine original base-biholomorphic comparison with the
identity of the SAME original base. Original domain coherence makes its
actual identity pullback literally the original marked record. The TWO
GIVEN original canonical terminal histories therefore supply its actual
original and pulled-back inputs. That producer internally constructs both
nonempty canonical terminal histories before whole canonical terminal
comparison, and the composed true final biholomorphism retains the original
whole projection formula. Since the base map is identity, its exact formula
is the asserted equality of original projections. No compressed condition
or comparison witness is supplied by the caller. The original histories
may have different empty-step counts; only their actual final spaces are
compared, without an uncompressed tower or arbitrary-map uniqueness claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual terminal choices compare over a genuine original base biholomorphism]
\label{hir:original-terminal-histories-actual-biholomorphic-base-comparison}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-actual-nonempty-canonical-segment,hir:original-finite-family-empty-free-canonical-compression,hir:canonical-resolution}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalChoiceComparison.original_terminal_histories_biholomorphic_base_comparison}
\leanok
Let the original base map be a genuine biholomorphism $\Phi:X'\to X$.
At EVERY actual family depth, original bound-$k$ $M$ over $X$ and TWO
GIVEN actual canonical terminal histories of $M$ and its ACTUAL $\Phi$
pullback internally supply a genuine original-final-space biholomorphism
$\eta:Y'\to Y$ satisfying $p_T\eta=\Phi p_{T'}$. No pulled-back domain,
nonempty/compressed history, equal length or terminal comparison certificate
is a caller input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original domain is stable under the genuine base biholomorphism;
its actual holomorphic pullback internally supplies the correct original
pulled-back marked record and domain. Apply finite actual empty compression
to both GIVEN original whole canonical histories. Each result retains its
original record, internally constructs all actual centres nonempty and
whole canonicality, and gives the true final biholomorphism, whole
projection identity and terminal iff. Given terminality then internally
gives both compressed terminal histories. The first lemma gives the
true canonical segments. The established per-sequence canonical terminal
comparison over $\Phi$ aligns these ACTUAL original and pulled-back
records and gives their whole tower isomorphism, internally with equal
compressed length. Compose its final map with the inverse source
compression final map and target compression final map. Their true whole
projection identities give precisely $p_T\eta=\Phi p_{T'}$ at EVERY
original source point. Empty histories/manifolds and differing original
empty-step counts are included. Noncompact global scheduling, intrinsic
uniqueness/cocycles and global resolution gluing remain distinct obligations.
\end{proof}
