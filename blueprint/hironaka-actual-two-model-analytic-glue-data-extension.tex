\section{Native two-model gluing data from actual holomorphic overlap maps}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-two-model-analytic-glue-data}

For $i\in\{0,1\}$, take actual coordinate opens $U_i\subset\mathbf C^{n_i}$
and original closed analytic subsets $C_i\subset U_i$, with their
independently constructed actual reduced quotient-subspace models. Let $O_{ij}\subset U_i$ be actual overlap
opens. Suppose ordinary original holomorphic maps
$f_{ij}:O_{ij}\to O_{ji}$ carry $C_i\cap O_{ij}$ into $C_j\cap O_{ji}$,
and $f_{ji}f_{ij}(x)=x$ for $x\in C_i\cap O_{ij}$ when $i\ne j$.
No whole ambient inverse identity or locally ringed transition map is input.

\begin{theorem}[The original overlap maps construct genuine model isomorphisms]
\label{hir:actual-two-model-overlap-isomorphisms}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-mutual-restriction-iso,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-open-restriction-iso}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualTwoModelAnalyticGlueData.actualOverlapIso}
\leanok
For distinct $i,j$, the original overlap maps internally construct an actual
locally ringed isomorphism between the actual models of $C_i\cap O_{ij}$
and $C_j\cap O_{ji}$. Their maps into their own original chart models are
the genuine actual open immersions constructed from the original open
inclusions. The two actual transition maps are inverse on the entire
structure sheaves.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the previously proved actual restriction-isomorphism construction
to $f_{ij},f_{ji}$ on the two actual overlap subsets. Its ordinary inputs
are precisely holomorphicity, the two subset containments and the two
inverse identities only on those subsets. It gives actual mutually
inverse morphisms of the whole reduced quotient sheaves. Independently,
each overlap-to-chart map is the previously constructed actual
open-subspace map and hence a genuine open immersion. No locally ringed
isomorphism or open-immersion certificate was supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual holomorphic two-model input constructs native gluing data]
\label{hir:actual-two-model-native-glue-data}
\uses{hir:actual-two-model-overlap-isomorphisms}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualTwoModelAnalyticGlueData.actualTwoModelGlueData}
\leanok
The two actual original chart models, their actual open overlap models
and the constructed transition morphisms internally form genuine native
locally ringed space gluing data. No native gluing datum or structure-sheaf
cocycle is part of the input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the native gluing-data variant whose off-diagonal overlaps are indexed
only by distinct indices. Put the actual two chart models in its chart
positions, the actual overlap models in its off-diagonal positions, and
the actual open-subspace inclusions in its inclusion positions. Their
proved open-immersion property supplies both monomorphisms and all required
pullbacks. Use the actual morphism of the first theorem in each transition
position. The actual inverse identities of those constructed isomorphisms
prove the required two-transition identity. There are no three pairwise
distinct indices in the two-element indexing type, so every off-diagonal
triple-overlap obligation is eliminated by that proved indexing fact.
The native conversion fills the diagonal positions with the actual charts
and identities and derives its full repeated-index cocycle laws. The
resulting whole gluing datum has actual open inclusion morphisms, since
the new diagonal identifications are actual isomorphisms and the original
off-diagonal inclusions are the proved actual open immersions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual overlap retains the original complex structural morphism]
\label{hir:actual-two-model-complex-structural-compatibility}
\uses{hir:actual-two-model-native-glue-data,hir:reduced-analytic-model-holomorphic-morphisms}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualTwoModelAnalyticGlueData.actualTwoModelGlueData_point_compatible}
\leanok
For the native gluing datum $D$, let $p_i:D.U_i\to\mathrm{point}_{\mathbf C}$
be the actual chart model's original complex structural morphism. Then
for every pair $i,j$ the genuine original compatibility is
\[
 D.f_{ij}\,p_i=D.t_{ij}\,D.f_{ji}\,p_j.
\]
Thus these same actual gluing data carry compatible actual complex
structural maps; no scalar-compatibility triangle is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each off-diagonal open inclusion is an actual holomorphic subspace map,
so its proved structural-map compatibility identifies its composite with
$p_i$ with the original overlap model's complex structural map. Each
actual transition is the actual holomorphic subspace map of the unchanged
original $f_{ij}$, so its structural-map compatibility identifies its
composite with the reverse overlap model's structural map with that same
source overlap structural map. Combining the two actual identities proves
the displayed equation. The native diagonal positions and transitions
are actual identities, so their equations follow from the identity laws.
This retains the original complex structure on the entire overlap maps.
\end{proof}

These are genuine two-chart native gluing data from ordinary original
holomorphic maps, including maps between different ambient dimensions.
Gluing two Hausdorff charts can produce a non-Hausdorff space, so the
Hausdorff condition for an analytic-space result is a separate natural
topological hypothesis. An arbitrary-index atlas requires genuine
triple-overlap comparison and cocycle producers beyond this two-model
construction. No resolution or resolution-descent conclusion is asserted.
