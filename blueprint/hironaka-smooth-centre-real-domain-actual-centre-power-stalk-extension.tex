two actual power sections to their true common open and add. For arbitrary
ring multiplication choose a true structure-sheaf section representing
the scalar, restrict it and the power section to a common actual open,
and multiply. Pointwise ideal-power membership is preserved. The preceding
power-germ containment proves one ENTIRE inclusion.

For the reverse inclusion induct on $q$. At $q=0$ any actual ring stalk
element has a true section representative, and the zeroth section ideal
power is the entire section ring. For the successor use
$J_y^{q+1}=J_y^qJ_y$. The induction hypothesis gives a true $q$th power
section representative for every element of $J_y^q$, and the entire actual
centre-ideal stalk dictionary gives a true centre section representative
for every element of $J_y$. Restrict both to their SAME actual common
open. Their literal product belongs to the $(q+1)$st section ideal power
and its actual ring germ is exactly the product of the original germs.
Thus the entire ideal product lies in the genuine power-section germ
image, because that image is a real ideal and contains every such product.
This gives EVERY element of the ENTIRE successor power a true section
representative, completing the induction without selecting finite origin
generators or evaluating an arbitrary origin germ at a nearby point.
\end{proof}
