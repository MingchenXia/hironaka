\section{The complete original General value at a special companion}

The original positive General branch evaluates the actual Step One companion
formed at its own order-regime head. An interior identity empty step preserves
that head in the reaching branch. Its whole restricted tail must still be
compared without assuming that the newly inserted history is canonical.
The actual special companion branch permits this comparison directly, including
its level-zero case. This is original indexed history bookkeeping for
W\l odarczyk's General step and Bierstone--Milman's trivial-blowup convention
\cite{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}.

\begin{theorem}[Actual whole restricted special-companion dictionary]
\label{hir:original-whole-restricted-empty-special-dictionary}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalGeneralSpecialCompanion.whole_restricted_empty_special_dictionary}
\leanok
\uses{hir:original-interior-empty-history,hir:original-interior-empty-test-history,hir:original-crossing-restricted-empty-history,hir:step-one-tower-iso}
Let $U$ be an actual original open in a stage $D$, let $J$ be originally
well formed on $U$ with mark at least one, and let $B,S$ be actual original
histories after $D$. Suppose the WHOLE old restricted $BS$ is an actual
test history of $\overline J$. For every actual final point $y$ whose
projection lies in $U$, the whole restricted $B\epsilon S$ also contains
$y$. The actual level and special predicate at that point agree for the
two WHOLE restricted histories, including empty $S$ and level zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original empty identity step leaves the composite projection unchanged,
so old open membership gives new membership. Normalize the whole old
restriction into $(B|_U)(S|_{B^{-1}U})$ by the actual restriction-append
tower isomorphism. Its base map is the identity of the original $U$, whose
pullback of the coherent $J$ is literally $J$. The genuine geometric tower
isomorphism dictionary transfers bar-testness and identifies actual levels
and special predicates at the point with the same underlying $y$.
Its bar-test prefix supplies real geometry for the interior identity empty
step. The actual original interior-empty dictionaries preserve level and
specialness, with no contact-choice or canonical-history input, and the true
interior-empty test equivalence gives bar-testness of the normalized inserted
history. The whole restricted-empty-history isomorphism identifies that
inserted normalization with the entire new restriction, again over the
identity on the SAME original $U$ and with the same underlying point.
Its geometric level/special dictionary completes the two comparisons.
All three point identifications follow from the literal underlying point;
no newly inserted canonical segment or desired predicate equality is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Entire original General value preserved at its special companion]
\label{hir:original-canonical-general-special-companion-value}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalGeneralSpecialCompanion.original_canonical_general_special_companion_value}
\leanok
\uses{hir:original-canonical-general-crossing-companion-data,hir:original-interior-general-crossing-splits,hir:original-whole-restricted-empty-special-dictionary,hir:step-one-bridge}
Let $n>0$ and let $M$ belong to the original dimension-$(k+1)$ invariant
domain. Let $HS$ be canonical for its actual full $R_{k+1}$. Let $y$ lie
in its actual transformed cosupport, have actual positive order $o$, and
have original order-regime run $r$ reaching $S$. Put
$K=\operatorname{head}(H,r-|S|)$, $B=\operatorname{tail}(H,r-|S|)$,
$U=\operatorname{ordMask}(M_K,o)$ and $J$ its actual whole companion.
Original data internally give actual old mask membership, companion domain,
maximal order, whole Step One canonical old tail and real cosupport.
If that internally supplied companion point is special on the OLD whole
restricted $BS$, then the ENTIRE original $R_{k+1}$ value of $M$ at $y$
on $H\epsilon S$ equals its entire value on $HS$. No companion-domain,
new canonicity, contact-choice or value comparison certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the original full canonical-tail producer to construct the stated
companion data. Its actual Step One bridge supplies bar-testness of the
whole old companion restriction. The preceding dictionary therefore supplies
new mask membership, equal actual companion levels and specialness at the
new whole restricted point. The actual Step One definition at a special
point is the entire pair $(\ell,\top)$, so both complete companion values
agree, including when $\ell=0$. Original full canonical testness supplies
the actual head geometry. The true original all-tail order dictionary and
reaching-run split computation give the same positive outer order and
both WHOLE selected General records $(K,BS)$ and $(K,B\epsilon S)$.
Their companion construction uses literally the same original $M,o,K$.
Both mask memberships make the actual General lower value the finite
inclusion of its whole companion Step One value. Expanding the positive
branch of the entire original successor family and substituting the two
whole splits consequently gives equal outer order and equal finite right
branch. This is equality of the complete actual original $R_{k+1}$ values.
No canonicity of the newly inserted companion or outer history is used.
\end{proof}
