\section{Actual fixed-order whole-quotient frames on an internally produced pivot patch}
\label{sec:hironaka-holomorphic-jet-quotient-frames}

These are genuine frames for the entire point quotients at one fixed truncation
order, constructed from original holomorphic functions. The patch is internally
chosen where their actual finite Macaulay rank attains its maximum on the given
nonempty original open domain. A single nonzero minor at an arbitrary point does
not imply constant rank on a neighbourhood. No density, maximal-Hilbert--Samuel
centre or normal-flatness for all relative powers is asserted here.

\begin{definition}[The fixed actual whole scalar-jet space]
\label{hir:actual-fixed-whole-scalar-jet-range}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHolomorphicJetQuotientFrames.scalarJet,Hironaka.HironakaHolomorphicJetQuotientFrames.scalarJetRange,Hironaka.HironakaHolomorphicJetQuotientFrames.ker_scalarJet}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-finite-jet-coordinate-evaluation,hir:whole-point-power-ring-dictionary}
For fixed $q$, let $J_q$ be the genuine entire first-$q$ scalar-coordinate jet
map and $S_q=\operatorname{range}J_q$. This is a fixed finite-dimensional
complex subspace of the actual coordinate-word array space, independent of
any point or ideal. Its actual kernel is the entire maximal-ideal power
$\mathfrak m^q$.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The real scalar-coordinate evaluation is injective on the entire original
multilinear jet target. Hence its composite with the actual whole-germ jet has
exactly that jet's kernel. The previous actual point-power/kernel theorem
identifies this kernel with the complete maximal-ideal power. The actual range
is a subspace of a finite coordinate array, so is finite-dimensional. No
independence of repeated word coordinates or artificial full-array surjectivity
is asserted for $q>2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual selected columns and the complete ideal image]
\label{hir:actual-selected-columns-entire-ideal-image}

For original holomorphic family $G$ at $p$, let $C(p)$ be its true word-times-
generator Macaulay matrix. Given actual row and column selections of size $k$,
let $F$ select those rows, $B(p)$ combine those selected actual columns, and
$K(p)=FB(p)$. Every selected and unselected actual column belongs to $S_q$;
the complete column image equals the whole original translated ideal jet image.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual matrix entry dictionary is precisely $J_q(z^\alpha G_i(p+z))$.
Thus every genuine column is in the fixed range $S_q$. Finite linear combination
of the selected columns gives $B(p)$ and direct finite-sum evaluation gives
$FB(p)=K(p)$. The genuine whole-ideal jet-image/Macaulay formula supplies ALL
original ideal membership germs, not only a selected family of derivatives.
Consequently the full matrix image is exactly that entire ideal image; selected
columns span a subspace of it. This applies equally to empty selections and
empty families.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Internally attained maximal rank and a real nonempty open pivot patch]

is holomorphic, the real Macaulay rank attains an actual maximum $k$. Internally
chosen row and column selections give a nonzero actual $k$-minor at a maximizing
point. On an internally constructed nonempty open pivot patch, this minor stays
nonzero and the ENTIRE actual matrix rank equals $k$ at every point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All genuine matrix ranks are natural numbers bounded by the fixed finite array
dimension, so their image on the nonempty original domain is a nonempty finite
set. Choose its actual maximum and a genuine point realizing it. The true
rank/minor dictionary produces an actual $k$-square minor with nonzero determinant
at that point (for $k=0$ its true empty determinant is one). Original holomorphic
matrix entries give holomorphic finite minors; continuity on the original open
domain makes its nonzero locus open. Intersect with that domain to obtain the
real nonempty patch. At every point of it, the actual nonzero minor forces rank
at least $k$, and the internally chosen GLOBAL maximum on that domain forces
rank at most $k$. Thus rank is exactly $k$. The upper bound is indispensable;
the proof does not infer it from a single arbitrary nonzero minor. No compactness
or caller rank/attainment/patch certificate is assumed.
\end{proof}

$P(p)=1-B(p)K(p)^{-1}F$ restricts to a surjection from $S_q$ to the fixed space
$L=S_q\cap\ker F$. It is identity on $L$, and its kernel inside $S_q$ is precisely
the ENTIRE actual ideal jet image. Its unmodified original finite array entries
are holomorphic on the real pivot patch.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual determinant is nonzero, so $K(p)$ has its genuine inverse. Original
matrix entries are holomorphic and the adjugate/determinant formula expresses
every inverse entry as a genuine finite polynomial divided by the same nonzero
original determinant, hence holomorphic. Finite products/sums give the actual

Conversely $Pv=0$ expresses $v=B K^{-1}Fv$, so its kernel is exactly the selected

They lie in the actual complete image, and on the real patch the actual complete
image has the same dimension $k$. The finite-dimensional inclusion and equal
actual dimensions identify selected image with ENTIRE ideal image. This proves
the claimed full kernel, with no selected-image equality premise.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine entire point quotients have fixed local frame coordinates]
\label{hir:actual-entire-point-quotient-pivot-frame}

\leanok

For EVERY point of the internally produced pivot patch, $P(p)J_q$ gives genuine
coordinates identifying the literal ENTIRE point quotient
$R/(I_p+\mathfrak m^q)$ with the SAME fixed complex space $L$. Its actual dimension
is $\dim S_q-k$. The original finite projection entries vary holomorphically on
the patch.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The entire scalar jet surjects onto its actual range $S_q$ by definition; $P$
surjects from that range onto $L$. Their composite therefore surjects onto $L$.
If $P J_q g=0$, the complete kernel theorem supplies an original entire ideal
member $a$ with $J_qg=J_qa$. Thus $g-a$ belongs to the genuine kernel
$\mathfrak m^q$, proving $g\in I_p+\mathfrak m^q$. Conversely an actual ideal
member maps into the complete ideal image and is killed by $P$, while every
actual maximal-ideal-power member is killed by $J_q$. This proves the exact
ENTIRE kernel. The genuine first-isomorphism theorem produces a linear
equivalence from the literal ring-ideal quotient to $L$. Because $F$ restricts
to a surjection on $S_q$ (using the actual invertible pivot columns), true
rank-nullity gives $\dim L=\dim S_q-k$. At $q=0$, $S_q=0$ and the actual quotient
by $\mathfrak m^0=R$ is zero. Empty original families have $k=0$ and give the
actual entire truncated ambient quotient; unit ideals give a zero quotient.
No full-array dimension is substituted for the actual possibly redundant
whole-jet range.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Coherence alone internally produces whole intrinsic quotient frames]

At every original base point of a coherent ideal on a genuine complex manifold,
there is an original open neighbourhood and an internally chosen nonempty open
subpatch on which its ENTIRE intrinsic fixed-$q$ point quotients have the same
actual finite complex frame model. The actual preferred chart point quotient
is transported by genuine nonlinear original chart algebra equivalences.
The subpatch need not contain the initial base point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original coherence internally produces one genuine Cartan family, its original
holomorphic neighbourhood and the ALL-membership whole-stalk ideal dictionary
in the initial original chart. Move that neighbourhood into the actual chart
coordinates: the real target/preimage intersection is open and nonempty at the

open patch back through the original chart; it stays inside the Cartan
neighbourhood and is nonempty because every chosen coordinate point belongs
to the true target. At every point of this subpatch the ALL-membership dictionary
identifies the fixed-chart reduced whole ideal with the same actual translated
family ideal, so the preceding entire quotient frame applies. The actual
translated nonlinear overlap between the preferred point chart and this fixed
original chart internally induces a full germ algebra equivalence; it maps
ALL original membership germs and preserves every genuine maximal-ideal power.
Its actual quotient algebra equivalence transports the preferred ENTIRE point
quotient into those same frame coordinates. No caller ideal-map equality,
quotient/frame isomorphism, matrix-rank constant or analytic chart certificate
is supplied. The actual fixed-$q$ Hilbert--Samuel value is constant on this
chosen genuine open patch by the real quotient dimension.
\end{proof}
