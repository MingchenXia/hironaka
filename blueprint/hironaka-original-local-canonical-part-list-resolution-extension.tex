\section{The actual ordered individual-sum separator on a relatively compact open base}
\label{sec:hironaka-original-local-canonical-part-list-resolution}

\begin{theorem}[The same ordered canonical separator retains an actual relatively compact host]
\label{hir:original-local-canonical-ordered-separator-host}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalPartListResolution.exists_local_canonical_ordered_separator}
Let $E$ be the actual ordered SNC boundary on $X^n$, $G$ a closed bad
set, and $(A,d)$ and the finite ordered list $L$ original locally good
parts relative to $(E,G)$. Thus the parts are analytic, of the stated
proper pure dimensions, regular and boundary-free off $G$, and dense
there; compactness of parts is not assumed. Suppose $A\cap B\subset G$
for every $(B,e)\in L$. Let $\iota:B_0\to X$ be a genuine open
holomorphic embedding whose whole range lies in a compact set.

These original data internally produce a finite actual tower $T$ over
$B_0$ with the genuine ordered canonical separation-chain provenance
for precisely the individual sums in $L$, the entire accumulated
boundary, and centres over $\iota^{-1}G$. All the corresponding whole
strict parts are separated from the head.

In addition they produce an actual final host $H$, its ordered SNC
boundary $E_H$ and closed bad set $G_H$, a transformation $P$ of
original sets, and an actual open holomorphic embedding $j$ of the
source endpoint into $H$ whose whole range lies in a compact set.
The entire source boundary is $j^{-1}E_H$, and
$j^{-1}G_H=T^{-1}(\iota^{-1}G)$. The operation $P$ carries every
original locally good part to a locally good part with the same
label, preserves disjointness of whole closed sets, and transports every
closed-pair intersection containment $S\cap R\subset G$ to
$P(S)\cap P(R)\subset G_H$. For every original
closed set $S$ dense off $G$ gives the whole equality
\[
 S_T(\iota^{-1}S)=j^{-1}P(S).
\]
The host head $P(A)$ is disjoint from every host tail part $P(B)$.
No canonical history, host, compact intersection or comparison
certificate is an input.
\uses{hir:original-local-canonical-sum-prescribed-open-producer,hir:actual-open-tower-upper-embedding,hir:actual-open-tower-compact-host,hir:original-canonical-ordered-separator-chain}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual length of $L$. For the empty list use the actual
identity history, the original host $X$, $P=\mathrm{id}$ and the given
$\iota$. The whole boundary and bad-set identities are literal, the
whole identity strict transform of any closed set is itself, and all
remaining assertions follow from the original data.

For $(B,e)::L'$, original local goodness and the actual intersection
condition give both mutual-density inclusions. Choose a relatively
compact open $U$ containing the compact closure of the entire original
range of $\iota$. Apply the proved original-only local individual-sum
producer on $U$ to $I_A+I_B$. It constructs an actual canonical terminal
target stage $T_0$ and, along the actual corestriction of $\iota$, an
actual canonical terminal source stage $Q_0$ with its whole intrinsic
sum input. Its actual upper map $g_0$ is an open holomorphic embedding
and has a compact enclosure by the genuine universal-square and proper
host theorems.

On the actual endpoint host of $T_0$ let $P_0(S)$ be the conventional
strict transform of $S\cap U$; let $G_0=T_0^{-1}(G\cap U)$ and let
$E_1$ be the entire actual boundary. Admissibility over $G\cap U$
transports every original locally good part to a locally good part
$P_0(S)$ with its same label. Whole strict transforms lie in whole
preimages, so $P_0$ preserves disjointness of closed sets and transports every closed-pair
intersection containment into the whole inverse-image bad set; in
particular the intersections of
$P_0(A)$ with the remaining parts still lie in $G_0$.

Apply induction to these genuine host data and to the actual compact-
enclosed open embedding $g_0$. This gives an actual source suffix
$Q_1$ and a genuine final host with transformation $P_1$. Append the
actual towers $Q_0,Q_1$, and set $P=P_1\circ P_0$. The exact whole
boundary identity of the first actual Cartesian stage and its whole
strict squares identify the independently intrinsic input of the suffix.
Consequently the genuine separation-chain constructor records exactly
these same canonical terminal individual-sum stages, in the same order.
Admissibility and centres-over compose on these actual towers; the
actual projection square identifies the whole final bad-set inverse
image. The final host and compact enclosure are the actual ones supplied
by induction, not new assumed witnesses.

For the whole strict dictionary take an original closed $S$ dense off
$G$. Open inverse images preserve this genuine density, and its strict
transform $P_0(S)$ is closed and dense off $G_0$ under the actual first
admissible stage. The preserved-open actual Cartesian strict-square
theorem identifies the whole $Q_0$ strict transform with
$g_0^{-1}P_0(S)$. Apply the inductive whole dictionary to $P_0(S)$, then
the genuine strict-transform composition theorem for the two actual
source stages. This proves the asserted entire equality, including all
exceptional points. Inductive local-goodness and disjointness transport
prove the corresponding whole properties of $P$. For the additional
closed-pair intersection dictionary, whole strict-transform containment
in the original closed preimages sends $S\cap R\subset G$ to
$P_0(S)\cap P_0(R)\subset G_0$; apply the same inductive dictionary
to these two genuinely closed strict transforms. The host separation
of the first pair is retained because $P_1$ preserves disjointness;
all remaining pairs are separated by induction. Pulling these whole
host separations back through $j$ proves source separation. This is the
same ordered individual-sum algorithm, without any compactness premise
on the original parts or their intersections.
\end{proof}
