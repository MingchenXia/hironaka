\section{Arbitrary actual convergent series realize their original Taylor coefficients}

\begin{theorem}[Literal finite degree bands and genuine scalar-line expansions]
\label{hir:actual-convergent-scalar-line-expansion}
\uses{hir:actual-taylor-polynomial-evaluation,hir:actual-monomial-owner-polydisc-convergence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualConvergentTaylorRealization.degreePolynomial_coeff,Hironaka.HironakaActualConvergentTaylorRealization.scalarLine_hasFPowerSeriesOnBall,Hironaka.HironakaActualConvergentTaylorRealization.scalarLine_hasFPowerSeriesAt}
\leanok
Let $F$ have finite actual absolute coefficient mass at a positive polyradius
$\rho$. Define the genuine finite polynomial $P_k$ to be the homogeneous
part of degree $k$ of the true truncation of $F$ below degree $k+1$.
Then $(P_k)_\alpha=F_\alpha$ when $|\alpha|=k$ and is zero otherwise.
For each $z$ in the closed $\rho$-polydisc the actual function
$t\mapsto\operatorname{eval}(F,tz)$ has the genuine scalar multilinear
expansion $q_k(t_1,\ldots,t_k)=P_k(z)\prod_jt_j$ at zero, with radius at
least one. No expansion,
coefficient or output convergence certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true homogeneous projection and truncation coefficient formulas give
the asserted literal coefficients. At $z$, absolute summability of the
unchanged monomials allows grouping by total degree. Each degree fibre is
exactly $P_k(z)$, since its coefficients agree on the fibre and vanish
off it. Thus the $P_k(z)$ form a summable complex series; finite-dimensional
absolute summability gives $\sum_k|P_k(z)|<\infty$. The norm of the scalar
multilinear map $q_k$ is exactly $|P_k(z)|$, so the genuine convergence
radius is at least one. For $|t|<1$, the point $tz$ remains in the original
closed polydisc. The same absolute degree regrouping gives a sum of
$P_k(tz)$; the finite homogeneous polynomial identity gives
$P_k(tz)=t^kP_k(z)=q_k(t,\ldots,t)$. These facts construct the actual
power-series-on-a-ball expansion and hence the asserted expansion at zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Near-zero actual evaluation is injective on convergent formal series]
\label{hir:actual-convergent-evaluation-injective}
\uses{hir:actual-convergent-scalar-line-expansion,hir:actual-polydisc-evaluation-additive-algebra}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualConvergentTaylorRealization.eq_zero_of_evaluation_eventually_zero,Hironaka.HironakaActualConvergentTaylorRealization.eq_of_evaluation_eventually_eq}
\leanok
If $F$ has finite actual coefficient mass at some positive polyradius and
its literal evaluation is zero near zero, then $F=0$. Consequently two
series with actual positive-radius finite mass and identical evaluations
near zero are equal, even when their input radii differ.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix any $z$ in the closed input polydisc. Continuity of $t\mapsto tz$
pulls the zero neighbourhood back to a zero neighbourhood on the scalar
line. Genuine one-variable analytic uniqueness forces every multilinear
coefficient of the preceding expansion to vanish. Evaluating at the tuple
of ones gives $P_k(z)=0$ for every $k$. Each coordinate's open positive
radius disk is infinite, because it is a neighbourhood in the nondiscrete
complex field. Polynomial function extensionality on their product therefore
forces every $P_k$ to be the zero polynomial. The literal degree-coefficient
formula then forces every coefficient of $F$ to vanish. For two input
series, shrink to the coordinatewise minimum radius, which retains positive
radii and both genuine masses by termwise comparison. Their difference
also has finite mass, and actual convergent addition and negation identify
its local evaluation with zero. Apply the proved zero statement.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual absolutely convergent series has its exact original Taylor germ]
\label{hir:actual-convergent-original-taylor-realization}
\uses{hir:actual-convergent-evaluation-injective,hir:actual-original-taylor-function-reconstruction,hir:actual-original-taylor-positive-coefficient-mass,hir:actual-coefficient-polydisc-holomorphy}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualConvergentTaylorRealization.evaluationGerm,Hironaka.HironakaActualConvergentTaylorRealization.originalTaylor_evaluationGerm}
\leanok
For every formal $F$ with finite actual coefficient mass at a positive
polyradius, its actual evaluated holomorphic function, in the unchanged
original Euclidean coordinates, defines a genuine original holomorphic
germ whose already constructed original Taylor algebra image is exactly
$F$. Neither this coefficient equality nor an expansion is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual mass gives holomorphy on the positive open polydisc. Compose with
the genuine original coordinate projections; their continuity and the
positivity of the radii give a neighbourhood of zero on which the composed
function is holomorphic. Thus it defines the asserted actual original
germ. The unconditional original Taylor mass theorem supplies a positive
radius for the Taylor image of this same germ. Original Taylor function
reconstruction says that its literal Taylor evaluation agrees near zero
with the original evaluation function of $F$. The true continuous
coordinate inverse carries this equality to the original coordinate tuple
neighbourhood. Apply the preceding actual evaluation injectivity theorem
to conclude that the two whole formal series agree.
\end{proof}

This realizes arbitrary convergent series as actual original germs. It does
not yet identify convergent whole-ideal division with a presentation-invariant
current-history Hilbert--Samuel locus or establish a terminating resolution
driver.
