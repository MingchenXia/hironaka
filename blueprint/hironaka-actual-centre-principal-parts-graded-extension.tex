\begin{theorem}[The entire truncation kernel is the EXISTING literal original graded piece]
\label{hir:actual-centre-principal-parts-canonical-graded-kernel}
\uses{hir:actual-centre-principal-parts-whole-order-system,hir:relative-normal-graded-literal-layers}
For the SAME original joint ideal $J$ and every natural cutoff $q$,
the ENTIRE kernel of the original quotient map
$R/(J+N_z^{q+1})\to R/(J+N_z^q)$ is precisely the EXISTING literal
\texttt{originalNormalGradedPiece} for these SAME joint objects.
The induced genuine $B$-linear equivalence preserves every original
numerator class and the unchanged original scalar action.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalPartsGraded.ker_actualPrincipalPartsTruncation_eq_originalGradedPiece,Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalPartsGraded.actualPrincipalPartsKernelGradedEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalPartsGraded.actualPrincipalPartsKernelGradedEquiv_apply,Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalPartsGraded.actualPrincipalPartsZero_subsingleton}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take ANY whole original upper quotient class and choose its original
numerator $g$. The lower class vanishes exactly when
$g\in J+N_z^q$. Split that membership into $g=j+h$ with $j\in J$ and
$h\in N_z^q$. Since $j$ vanishes already in the upper original quotient,
its class is the class of the actual normal-power member $h$, hence
lies in the existing original power-image submodule. Conversely any
class represented by $h\in N_z^q$ maps to zero in the lower quotient.
Equality of upper classes preserves this conclusion because
$J+N_z^{q+1}\subseteq J+N_z^q$. These are BOTH inclusions of ENTIRE
original submodules, not equality of dimensions. Their equality gives
the genuine identity-on-underlying-classes $B$-linear equivalence,
so every original power numerator and the original scalar action are
unchanged. The argument also covers $q=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original HS-constant geometry supplies ALL literal displacement graded layers finite free]
\label{hir:actual-hs-constant-centre-displacement-graded-free}

over the ORIGINAL tangent germ ring at EVERY degree.

\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

retaining SAME original flattening, finite generators, real domain,
whole ideal and HS dictionaries and fixed origin holomorphic germ family
before all degrees. For a positive degree $q=a+1$, the pair of orders

and free. Transfer all three properties along the preceding canonical
whole submodule equivalence to the existing literal graded piece.
At degree zero, $N_z^0$ is the entire original ring, so its image in
$R/(J+N_z)$ is the entire quotient. The original order-zero principal
parts are already internally finite free; the actual top-submodule
identity transfers those same properties to the literal degree-zero
piece. No normal-graded piece of the original unshifted ideal is
substituted: these are the genuine displacement pieces of SAME $J$
in the joint ring. Parameter-zero base change, strict elimination of
tangent displacements, original normalflatness and general G8 remain
separate obligations.
\end{proof}
