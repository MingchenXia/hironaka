\section{Finite weighted analytic coefficient compression and its actual controlled transform}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-weighted-coefficient-compression}

All coefficient ideals in this section are ideals of entire original holomorphic germs.
The finite coefficients come from an internally constructed analytic vertex family of the
original ideal. Their positive individual weights are retained. The resulting common-mark
ideal is a bounded algebraic prerequisite for a persistent Hilbert--Samuel presentation;
no identification with a current Hilbert--Samuel locus is asserted.

\begin{lemma}[A common positive mark retains every individual coefficient weight]
\label{hir:actual-weighted-coefficient-common-mark}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedCoefficientCompression.weightedCommonMark_pos,Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedCoefficientCompression.weight_mul_compressionExponent,Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedCoefficientCompression.compressionExponent_pos}
\leanok
Let $L$ be finite and let $w_i>0$ for $i\in L$. Put
$D=\prod_{i\in L}w_i$ and $r_i=D/w_i$. Then $D>0$ and
$w_ir_i=D$ for every $i$. In particular $r_i>0$.
For a family of actual analytic germs $c_i$, its compressed weighted ideal is
$J(c,w)=(c_i^{r_i}:i\in L)$ with the single positive mark $D$.
The empty family has $D=1$ and $J=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A finite product of positive natural numbers is positive, including the empty
product. Each factor $w_i$ divides this product. Exact division therefore gives
$w_i(D/w_i)=D$. If $D/w_i$ were zero, this equality would contradict $D>0$.
The statements about the empty family follow from the empty product and empty span.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole compressed ideal has the correct actual one-blowup transform]
\label{hir:actual-weighted-coefficient-compression-transform}
\uses{hir:actual-weighted-coefficient-common-mark,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-exceptional-quotient-formal-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedCoefficientCompression.exists_actual_weighted_compression_blowup}
\leanok
Let $c_i$ be a finite family of original analytic germs, with positive weights $w_i$.
Let $A$ be a coordinate centre, $e\in A$ a genuine pivot and $\pi$ its actual local
analytic blowup map, with arbitrary direction parameters. Suppose
$c_i\in I_A^{w_i}$ for every $i$. Then genuine analytic germs $c_i'$ are internally
constructed such that
\[
 x_e^{w_i}c_i'=\pi^*c_i,\qquad
 \pi^*J(c,w)=(x_e^D)J(c',w),\qquad
 (\pi^*J(c,w):x_e^D)=J(c',w).
\]
Each Taylor series of $c_i'$ is the actual formal exceptional quotient of the
Taylor series of that same $c_i$ at weight $w_i$.
Both displayed ideal equalities concern the entire ideals, not their finite jets.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual coordinate-centre powers pull back to the entire principal exceptional
powers. Thus each given original member admits a genuine analytic quotient
$c_i'$ with the first equality. Applying the actual Taylor homomorphism and
using its exact blowup dictionary identifies this quotient with the actual
formal exceptional quotient; uniqueness follows from cancellation of the
nonzero formal exceptional variable.

For $r_i=D/w_i$, the retained quotient equation gives
$\pi^*(c_i^{r_i})=(x_e^{w_i}c_i')^{r_i}=x_e^D(c_i')^{r_i}$.
The image of an ideal span is the span of its actual generator images, and the
product with a principal ideal is the span of these literal products. These
facts give both inclusions of the whole second equality.

For the last equality, membership of $h$ in the colon means
$h x_e^D=x_e^D k$ for some $k\in J(c',w)$, by the preceding whole ideal equality.
The original analytic germ ring is a domain and the coordinate germ $x_e$ is
nonzero, so cancellation gives $h=k$. Conversely, any such $h$ satisfies this
membership by multiplication. This proves the whole colon equality, including
an empty coefficient family.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An original analytic ideal internally supplies the weighted coefficient compression]
\label{hir:actual-original-weighted-coefficient-compression}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-vertex-coefficient-data,hir:actual-local-coefficient-cosupport-normal-power,hir:actual-weighted-coefficient-compression-transform}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedCoefficientCompression.exists_original_weighted_coefficient_compression}
\leanok
Let $I$ be any original analytic germ ideal in coordinates $(u,y,z)$ of dimensions
$k,s,t$. Internally choose its genuine analytic monic vertex generators and
actual coefficient functions $c_{a,\gamma}(u,y)$ on their common real open
neighbourhood $U$. Retain only the finite pairs with
$|\gamma|<m_a$, where $m_a$ is the actual selected-centre vertex weight, and mark
$c_{a,\gamma}$ by $m_a-|\gamma|>0$. These original coefficients and positive
marks determine the common-mark compressed ideal above.

For any genuine $y$-coordinate blowup pivot, suppose the actual local plane
$U\cap\{y=0\}$ lies in the cosupport of each actual principal coefficient ideal
at its own mark. Then the entire compressed ideal has both exact blowup and
colon identities of the preceding theorem, using the actual analytic divided
coefficient germs and the same original weights. No coefficient family,
compressed ideal, exceptional quotient or transformed-ideal equality is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original analytic vertex-coefficient producer supplies all actual generators,
all literal Taylor coefficient identities, the real common open neighbourhood
and coherence of every low principal coefficient ideal on that neighbourhood.
There are finitely many vertices. For each vertex, the normal exponents of
degree below its finite weight form a finite set, so the displayed pairs form a
finite dependent union. The strict degree inequality gives positivity of every
retained difference mark, and the common product gives a positive mark even if
this union is empty.

For each retained pair, its actual coefficient function belongs to its true
principal ideal at the origin. Coherence on $U$ and the given local-plane
cosupport imply that its entire original germ belongs to the $y$-coordinate
ideal to its retained difference mark. This uses the actual local normal-power
criterion, with no global extension of the coefficient function. The analytic
normal coordinate ideal is exactly the coordinate-centre ideal. Apply the
preceding theorem simultaneously to this finite family. Its internally
constructed quotients yield the stated entire image and colon identities, and
its Taylor dictionary retains each original coefficient numerator.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This construction retains a genuine finite weighted analytic coefficient family
and proves its whole controlled-transform algebra. A separate transformation
theorem must still identify this presentation with the equal-Hilbert--Samuel
locus of each literal current strict transform, with actual admissibility and
singularity of the resulting centres. Strict phase drop and termination are not
consequences of the identities proved here.
\end{remark}
