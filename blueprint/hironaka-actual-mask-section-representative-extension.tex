\section{The actual contact mask and its section comparison}
\begin{definition}[The true section mask overlap]
\label{hir:actual-section-mask-overlap}
\uses{hir:actual-open-zero-section-models}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualMaskSectionRepresentative.baseMask,Hironaka.HironakaActualMaskSectionRepresentative.overlap,Hironaka.HironakaActualMaskSectionRepresentative.toMask,Hironaka.HironakaActualMaskSectionRepresentative.sectionToMask,Hironaka.HironakaActualMaskSectionRepresentative.overlapProjection,Hironaka.HironakaActualMaskSectionRepresentative.overlapRetraction}
\leanok
For an actual open mask $O\subseteq p^{-1}U$, put $W=s^{-1}O$ and
$V=O\cap p^{-1}W$. The genuine maps are $q:V\to O$, $s_O:W\to O$,
$p_V:V\to W$ and $r_V:V\to V$, with $r_V(z)=s(p(z))$.
\end{definition}
\begin{lemma}[Whole section representatives on the genuine mask overlap]
\label{hir:actual-mask-section-whole-representative}
\uses{hir:actual-section-mask-overlap,hir:actual-retraction-whole-restricted-representative,hir:submersion-choice-image-trace-whole-pullback}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualMaskSectionRepresentative.restrictEquiv_actual_mask_section}
\leanok
For $A\subseteq U$ and an ideal $J$ on the original mask $O$ with holomorphic
members, the records on $V$ with the same carrier $s(A)\cap V$, same list $E$
and mark $d$, and whole ideals $q^*J$ and $p_V^*(s_O^*J)$ are restricted equivalent.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Continuity of the genuine section and projection makes $W,V$ open.
If $z\in V$, then $p(z)\in W$, so $s(p(z))\in O$; the actual identity
$p(s(x))=x$ also gives $p(s(p(z)))\in W$. Thus $r_V$ is a genuine typed map,
not an assumed endomap of $O$. All four maps are holomorphic by their actual
subtype inclusions and the original section and projection.
For $z$ in the same carrier, its normal component vanishes, so the true linear
splitting identity gives $r_V(z)=z$. The preceding retraction theorem gives
restricted equivalence between $q^*J$ and $r_V^*(q^*J)$.
The literal equality $q\circ r_V=s_O\circ p_V$ and whole holomorphic germ-ideal
pullback composition identify this latter ideal with $p_V^*(s_O^*J)$.
This proves both whole restricted containments on the original lifted carrier.
No equality of $O$ with a cylinder, or global map $O\to O$ induced by $s\circ p$,
is assumed.
\end{proof}
