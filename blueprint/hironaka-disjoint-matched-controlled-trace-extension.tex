\section{Matched native coordinates and whole coherent normal trace}

\begin{lemma}[The given flattening induces an actual native intrinsic chart]
\label{hir:disjoint-matched-intrinsic-chart}
\uses{ana:submanifold-complex-structure}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaDisjointMatchedControlledTrace.exists_matched_coordinates}
\leanok
Let $S$ be a regular $d$-dimensional submanifold of a complex manifold
of dimension $d+t$, with its existing native flattening atlas. Let $y\in S$,
and let $\Theta$ be a genuine ambient flattening at $y$, centered at zero,
which identifies the entire local $S$ with the standard $d$-plane. Given any
genuine biholomorphism $E:Z\to S$, with $z=E^{-1}(y)$, construct actual local
coordinates $\Psi$ at $z$ in the existing native atlas, centered at zero, such
that the inverse coordinate maps satisfy near zero
\[
 \operatorname{val}_S E(\Psi^{-1}(u))
   =\Theta^{-1}(u,0).
\]
No matching chart or germ comparison is an input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Package the given ambient chart and the given $\Theta$ as a flattening datum
for $S$, retaining its literal plane equation. The intrinsic chart supplied
by this datum belongs to the existing native atlas, whose atlas contains
every such genuine flattening datum. Its inverse is the original ambient
inverse coordinate map evaluated at the standard plane inclusion, as follows
from the explicit inverse-chart construction. Pull this intrinsic chart back
through the genuine $E$. Compose its coordinate map with the inverse preferred
chart at $z$. Smoothness of both $E$ and its inverse, and holomorphic native
chart transitions, make this an actual local biholomorphism between original
coordinate opens. Its value at the preferred coordinate of $z$ is zero.
The true inverse-chart identities cancel the preferred chart and $E$, giving
the displayed entire inverse-coordinate equation on a genuine neighbourhood
of zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole coherent ideal trace in the matched coordinates]
\label{hir:disjoint-matched-whole-coherent-trace}
\uses{hir:disjoint-matched-intrinsic-chart,hir:relative-normal-whole-hadamard}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaDisjointMatchedControlledTrace.exists_matched_coherent_trace}
\leanok
The same constructed $\Psi$ works for EVERY coherent ambient ideal $A$:
\[
 \operatorname{map}(\operatorname{planeTrace})
       (\operatorname{adaptedIdeal}_{y,\Theta}(A))
 =\operatorname{adaptedIdeal}_{z,\Psi}
       ((\operatorname{val}_S\circ E)^*A).
\]
This is an equality of entire germ ideals, including all nonradical structure.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The inverse-coordinate equation identifies the trace of the adapted germ of
every original ambient holomorphic member with the adapted germ of its actual
composition by $\operatorname{val}_S\circ E$. This composition is a genuine
member of the whole pullback ideal, so mapping the entire ambient adapted
span gives one inclusion. Conversely take any generator of the adapted whole
pullback ideal. Its defining original pullback membership gives a finite sum
of composed original ambient members with holomorphic intrinsic coefficients
on an actual neighbourhood. Move this same finite span through the actual
intrinsic inverse coordinates. Every resulting generator is the trace of the
corresponding original adapted ambient member by the preceding equation.
The finite span therefore belongs to the mapped ambient ideal. Taking all
adapted generators gives the other inclusion. Coherence supplies holomorphicity
of original members; no flatness of a closed inclusion, radical vanishing
replacement, target trace certificate or finite target presentation is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This dictionary applies on arbitrary genuine ambient open manifolds; the
flattening only asserts a local plane equation and does not assert that the
original source open is a product. To obtain the full controlled split for
prescribed ambient and base blowups, one must additionally construct their
canonical strict-contact biholomorphism and apply actual whole controlled
restriction with the original mark one. Until that consumer is supplied,
this theorem alone does not assert that a particular base controlled ideal
is the resulting trace, nor does it prove ambient-independent histories.
\end{remark}
