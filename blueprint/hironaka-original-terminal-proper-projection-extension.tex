\section{Properness of the original terminal glued projection}

The local-to-global resolution route of \cite{wlodarczyk05-hironaka}
uses proper local terminal projections. We adapt this step directly to the
actual point-indexed terminal atlas: its genuine saturated inclusion ranges
identify the whole fibres and spread closed-image complements. This does not
require a compact original manifold or an assumed proper global projection.
The space here retains its actual glued topology; its complex structure is
constructed independently.

\begin{lemma}[Whole original glued fibres are compact local-fibre images]
\label{hir:original-terminal-glued-whole-compact-fibres}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProperProjection.originalTerminalGluedProjection_fibre,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProperProjection.originalTerminalGluedProjection_fibre_isCompact}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-input-terminal-glued-local-range-whole-open-preimage,hir:original-input-covering-local-canonical-terminal-atlas}
Let $M$ be an original marked ideal in the domain of the actual canonical
invariant, let $A$ be its internally constructed local terminal atlas, and
let $P:G\to X$ be its actual glued projection. For every $x\in X$, put
$u_x=(x\in U_x)$. Then the ENTIRE fibre satisfies
\[
 P^{-1}\{x\}=\Gamma_x\bigl(\pi_x^{-1}\{u_x\}\bigr),
\]
and is compact. No local-history, covering, fibre or properness certificate
is a caller premise.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual original singleton local producer supplies $x\in U_x$ and the
finite terminal tower $\pi_x:F_x\to U_x$. Each genuine finite blowup tower
projection is proper, so its inverse image of the compact singleton
$\{u_x\}$ is compact. For $z$ in the whole left-hand fibre, its projection
belongs to $U_x$. The actual saturated range equality therefore writes
$z=\Gamma_x(y)$. The entire local projection equation gives
$\pi_x(y)$ with underlying original point $x$; injectivity of the open
inclusion identifies it with $u_x$. Conversely a point in that local fibre
maps under $\Gamma_x$ to projection $x$ by the same entire local equation.
Thus both inclusions give the stated equality. The actual open embedding
$\Gamma_x$ is continuous, so its image of the compact local fibre is compact.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original entire glued projection is closed]
\label{hir:original-terminal-glued-whole-closed-projection}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProperProjection.originalTerminalGluedProjection_isClosedMap}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-input-terminal-glued-local-range-whole-open-preimage,hir:original-input-local-terminal-glued-whole-continuous-projection,hir:original-input-covering-local-canonical-terminal-atlas}
For the same original input, $P$ maps EVERY closed subset of the actual
whole glued space to a closed subset of the original manifold.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix a closed subset $C\subseteq G$ and $x\notin P(C)$. The true local
embedding is continuous, so $C_x=\Gamma_x^{-1}(C)$ is closed in $F_x$.
Properness of the actual local tower makes $\pi_x(C_x)$ closed in $U_x$.
Its open complement $V_x$ contains $u_x$: otherwise a local representative
in $C_x$ above $u_x$ would, by the entire local projection equation, give
$x\in P(C)$. Since $U_x$ is an original open subset, its inclusion maps
$V_x$ to an open original neighborhood of $x$.
This ENTIRE neighborhood avoids $P(C)$. Indeed, if an original point
$z$ in it were $P(w)$ with $w\in C$, then $P(w)\in U_x$ and the true
saturated range equality would give $w=\Gamma_x(y)$. The whole local
projection equation and injectivity of the open inclusion identify
$\pi_x(y)$ with the original representative of $z$ in $V_x$. But $y\in C_x$,
so this representative belongs to $\pi_x(C_x)$, contradicting the definition
of $V_x$. Every point outside $P(C)$ therefore has an original open
neighborhood in its complement. That complement is open, and $P(C)$ is closed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original-input proper terminal glued projection]
\label{hir:original-terminal-glued-projection-proper}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProperProjection.originalTerminalGluedProjection_isProperMap}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-terminal-glued-whole-compact-fibres,hir:original-terminal-glued-whole-closed-projection,hir:original-input-local-terminal-glued-whole-continuous-projection}
For ONLY an original marked ideal in the actual canonical invariant domain,
the internally constructed whole glued projection $P:G\to X$ is proper.
The original manifold need not be compact, and neither global separation
nor a supplied global resolution or scheduled history is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual glued projection is continuous by its genuine topological
coequalizer construction. The preceding lemma proves it is closed on the
whole space, and the first lemma supplies compactness of every entire
fibre. The topological characterization of proper maps by continuity,
closedness and compact fibres now proves properness of this actual $P$.
This constructs properness of the glued map; identification of its
geometric resolution data and construction of a noncompact scheduled
blowup history remain separate source obligations.
\end{proof}
