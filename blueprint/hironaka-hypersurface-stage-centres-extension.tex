\section{Fresh maximal multiplicity centres}

\begin{lemma}[The reduced ideal has order one at a regular point]
\label{hir:reduced-ideal-order-one-regular}
\uses{hir:order-taylor,hir:monomial-invariant-geometry}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHypersurfaceStageCenters.order_vanishingIdeal_eq_one_of_regularAtDim}
\leanok
If $W$ is regular at $x$ of dimension $d<n$, then its actual vanishing
ideal has order one at $x$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every member of its vanishing ideal vanishes at $x$, so the order is at
least one. Choose a local holomorphic flattening of $W$ to
$\mathbb C^d\times\{0\}$. Its $d$th coordinate is an actual holomorphic
germ in $\mathcal I_{W,x}$. A coordinate of a local biholomorphism has
a nonzero first directional derivative: composing the derivatives of
the chart and its inverse gives the identity. It consequently does not
belong to the square of the point ideal, by the forward Taylor-order
theorem. The vanishing ideal therefore does not have order at least two.
Every integer in its defining order supremum is at most one, proving
the asserted equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[High multiplicity lies in the current singular locus]
\label{hir:high-multiplicity-in-singular-locus}
\uses{hir:reduced-ideal-order-one-regular}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHypersurfaceStageCenters.cosupport_high_mark_subset_diff_regularLocus}
\leanok
Let $W$ be closed, $d<n$, and $\mu\geq2$. The actual cosupport of
$(\mathcal I_W,\mu)$ is contained in $W\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_d W$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The cosupport for mark $\mu$ is contained in the cosupport for mark one,
which equals $W$. If a point there were regular of dimension $d$, the
preceding lemma would give order one, whereas cosupport membership gives
order at least $\mu\geq2$. This is impossible.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual fresh maximal-multiplicity centre]
\label{hir:actual-fresh-maximal-multiplicity-centre}
\uses{hir:high-multiplicity-in-singular-locus,hir:fresh-order-bounded-current-residual-order,hir:centre-function}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHypersurfaceStageCenters.exists_fresh_maximal_order_centre}
\leanok
Let $W$ be compact analytic, with regular points of dimension $d<n$ dense
in $W$, and let $E$ be SNC. Suppose some point of $W$ has reduced ideal
order at least two. Then the actual maximum multiplicity $\mu\geq2$
and a nonempty smooth centre $C$ are produced internally, with
\[
 C\subset W\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_d W,
 \qquad\operatorname{ord}_c\mathcal I_W=\mu\quad(c\in C).
\]
This $C$ is admissible for the actual marked ideal
$(X,X,E,\mathcal I_W,\mu)$; in particular it has normal crossings with
the given boundary. The reduced order is at most $\mu$ on all of $W$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Cartan coherence and empty interior put $(X,X,E,\mathcal I_W,1)$ in the
actual domain. The reduced order is upper semicontinuous by its closed
derivative-ideal superlevel sets, so it attains a maximum on compact $W$.
At the point attaining this maximum, initialize instead with empty
boundary and mark one. The previous current-reduced-order comparison for
the empty tower bounds its order by the finite nonmonomial order of this
actual fresh initialization. The maximum is therefore a finite integer
$\mu$. The given high-order point forces $\mu\geq2$.

Changing the mark to $\mu$ preserves well-formedness, dimension and
nonzero germs. Its cosupport is a nonempty closed subset of compact $W$:
it contains the point of maximum reduced order. The actual canonical
invariant attains a maximum on this compact cosupport. Its own
admissibility theorem produces the nonempty smooth maximum centre $C$
with normal crossings with $E$. The preceding high-multiplicity lemma
puts $C$ inside $W\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_d W$. Its points have order
at least $\mu$ by admissibility and at most $\mu$ by maximality, hence
exactly $\mu$. No centre-position or constant-multiplicity data is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual blowup with exact exceptional multiplicity]
\label{hir:actual-high-multiplicity-blowup}
\uses{hir:actual-fresh-maximal-multiplicity-centre,hir:hadamard-bridge,bcalc:controlled-not-exceptional,bcalc:controlled-transform}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHypersurfaceStageCenters.exists_fresh_maximal_order_blowup}
\leanok
Under the preceding hypotheses there are an actual produced centre $C$,
its blowup $\pi$, and the actual maximum multiplicity $\mu\geq2$, such that
$C$ is nonempty, admissible, and contained in the present singular locus.
The actual controlled ideal $\mathfrak a'$ is coherent and satisfies
\[
 \pi^*\mathcal I_W=\mathcal I_D^\mu\mathfrak a',
 \qquad D=\pi^{-1}C,
\]
where $\mathcal I_D$ denotes the actual exceptional invertible ideal.
At every point of $D$, some germ of $\mathfrak a'$ does not belong to
the vanishing ideal of $D$. The next strict transform is actually analytic
and compact, and the actual accumulated boundary is SNC.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Produce $\mu$ and $C$ by the preceding theorem. Since $C\subset W$ and
$W$ has empty interior, the actual blowup-existence theorem applies.
Hadamard divisibility produces $\mathcal I_W\subset\mathcal I_C^\mu$;
pulling back and taking the colon by the invertible exceptional power
gives the stated actual factorization and coherence.

At a point $z$ of the exceptional divisor the order at $\pi z$ is exactly
$\mu$. The exceptional-ideal pushforward theorem says that if every
controlled germ at $z$ belonged to the exceptional ideal, the original
order at $\pi z$ would be at least $\mu+1$. This contradicts the proved
constant multiplicity. Its contrapositive produces the required germ.
Every germ vanishing on $D$ belongs to the exceptional ideal, by the
actual SNC blowup coordinate theorem, so the produced germ also fails
membership in the vanishing ideal of $D$.

The actual test blowup preserves well-formedness, proving that the
accumulated boundary is SNC. The actual analytic strict-transform theorem
and properness of the blowup give analyticity and compactness of the next
strict transform.
\end{proof}

The missing hypersurface step is the actual identity between the reduced
strict-transform ideal and the $\mu$-controlled ideal after blowing up this
produced centre. Exact exceptional multiplicity is supplied by the actual
nondivisibility producer above; reducedness of the whole residual ideal
remains to be proved. The maximal-multiplicity centre and blowup are genuine producers,
but no finite restarted tower or general G8 completion is asserted here.
