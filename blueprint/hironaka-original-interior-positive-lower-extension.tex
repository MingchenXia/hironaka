\section{The actual positive lower record after an interior empty step}

The initial marked ideal, original members and complete original head remain
fixed.  This chapter computes the actual lower marked ideal at a positive
Step One level after insertion of an interior identity empty blowup.  It uses
actual full records, not an assumed equality of lower values.

\begin{lemma}[Future empty insertion in a member restriction]
\label{hir:interior-future-empty-member-restriction}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorPositiveLower.future_memberRestrict_insertEmpty}
\leanok
\uses{hir:positive-member-empty-filter,hir:original-interior-empty-geometric-dictionary}
Let $J$ be any marked ideal, $E_0$ its specified original-member list,
$p$ a point, and $j\leq |J.E|$.  Write
\[
 c=\bigl|(J.E.\operatorname{take}(j)).\operatorname{filter}
       (W\notin E_0\text{ or }p\notin W)\bigr|.
\]
Inserting an empty future member at $j$ in $J$, while leaving $E_0$ unchanged,
commutes with the actual member restriction through $p$, with insertion
index $c$ in the restricted boundary list.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The empty member never passes through $p$.  It is therefore never among the
selected members, even if $E_0$ already contains an empty member.  Insertion
changes neither the carrier intersection nor the coefficient ideal nor the
mark of the member restriction.  Its actual boundary is the original whole
boundary filtered by failure to be an original member through $p$.
The inserted empty member is retained by this filter.  Induction on the
first $j$ boundary elements shows that filtering an insertion of a retained
element inserts it after exactly the retained elements of that prefix.
This is precisely $c$, and proves equality of all literal marked fields.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The actual lower index of the interior future member]
\label{hir:original-interior-actual-positive-lower-index}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorPositiveLower.interiorPositiveLowerIndex,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorPositiveLower.interiorPositiveLowerIndex_le_boundary}
\leanok
\uses{hir:interior-future-empty-member-restriction}
For original histories $H:X\leftarrow A$ and $S:A\leftarrow Y$, set
$J=\overline M_{HS}$, $E_0=\operatorname{origMembers}(M,HS)$,
$j=|M.E|+|H|$ and let $c$ be the preceding filtered-prefix cardinal.
Then $c$ is at most the length of the actual boundary of the original
positive lower marked ideal, at every positive Step One level.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The actual positive lower marked ideal is the pullback of the member
restriction to the actual member mask.  Its boundary is the filtered whole
boundary $J.E$, then mapped by the mask inclusion preimage.  Mapping retains
its length.  The filtered first $j$ elements form a sublist of this whole
filtered boundary, so its length $c$ is bounded by the actual lower boundary
length.  This argument does not require zero lower nonmonomial order or a
bound inside an empty original prefix.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine original positive lower record]
\label{hir:original-interior-actual-positive-lower-record}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorPositiveLower.original_positive_lower_record}
\leanok
\uses{hir:original-interior-empty-masks,hir:original-interior-empty-step-one-dictionary,hir:original-interior-actual-positive-lower-index}
Let $M$ be originally well formed and $H$ an actual test tower of
$\overline M$.  For every nonempty actual tail $S$, every final point $p$,
and every positive Step One level $s+1$, the actual masked lower marked
ideal for $M$ at $H\epsilon S,p$ equals the actual lower marked ideal for
$M$ at $HS,p$ with an empty boundary member inserted at the computed
index $c$.  Equality is heterogeneous only because the equal mask opens
are kept as their respective literal carrier types.  All fields agree
under the equality of those actual masks.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The nonempty-tail whole-record producer gives
$\overline M_{H\epsilon S}=\operatorname{insertEmpty}_j(\overline M_{HS})$.
The original-member producer supplies the unchanged entire $E_0$ and
$j\leq|J.E|$ follows from the actual transformed boundary length.
Apply the future member-restriction lemma to these actual records.  The
member mask producer gives equality of the two literal open masks.
Pullback of an empty insertion maps its empty member to the empty set and
commutes with insertion at the same computed index.  Substituting the mask
equality therefore proves the stated actual masked lower-record equality.
The masked positive lower definition is exactly this member-mask pullback.
\end{proof}

The boundary index is computed from the actual retained prefix; it may extend
past the empty original prefix.  No assertion of a common recursive value
shift, equality of lower values, or global order/scheduling follows here.
The zero-contact lower carrier still needs its own local closedness comparison
on the chosen contact open.  Actual restricted lower tail histories and full
higher recursive invariant comparisons remain open.
