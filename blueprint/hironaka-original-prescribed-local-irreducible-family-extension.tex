\section{All original irreducible dimensions on the same prescribed local domain}

\begin{theorem}[The prescribed-open irreducible canonical family in every dimension]
\label{hir:original-prescribed-local-all-dimension-irreducible-family}
\uses{hir:original-local-irreducible-own-singular-locus,hir:original-local-full-dimension-identity-family}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPrescribedLocalIrreducibleFamily.exists_original_irreducible_canonical_family_of_subset_isCompact}
\leanok
Let $V$ be an original closed irreducible analytic subvariety of $X^n$,
and let $U\subseteq X$ be the prescribed actual open contained in an
original compact set. The SAME $U$ has the full original canonical
local family in every possible dimension of $V$: the exact actual
pure canonical target and all its independently stopped smooth sources
if $\dim V<n$, or the literal identity target and all its actual
smooth identity sources if $\dim V=n$. The proper branch retains the
whole equality of the restricted own singular set with the inverse
image of the original $V_{\rm sing}$. No compactness of $V$, or
connectedness of a local restriction or smooth source, is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the intrinsic original bound $\dim V\le n$ and split into strict
inequality or equality. In the strict case the actual prescribed-open
proper irreducible producer applies to precisely this $U$ and its
original compact enclosure. It supplies the entire exact pure family
and literal singular-set restriction, with target before every source.
In the equality case the actual full-dimensional local identity
producer applies to this same $U$ directly. Retain the corresponding
whole branch. No new neighbourhood is selected and no field or natural
hypothesis is discarded.
\end{proof}
