\section{Original HS-constant centres give genuine jet frames at the original point}

\begin{theorem}[The true original jet-image bound makes HS subtraction faithful]
\label{hir:actual-centre-jet-rank-bound-and-hs-inversion}
\uses{hir:actual-translated-point-quotient-macaulay-rank,hir:actual-entire-point-quotient-pivot-frame}
For actual finite original holomorphic functions $H_i$ near any actual
point $p$ and every finite truncation $q$, the rank of their original
Macaulay matrix is bounded by the dimension of the actual original
whole jet image. Consequently equality of the actual translated-ideal
HS values at two genuine points, at order $\ell$, implies equality
of their original Macaulay ranks at truncation $\ell+1$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSConstantCentreJetFrames.finrank_originalMacaulay_le_actualJet,Hironaka.HironakaActualHSConstantCentreJetFrames.originalMacaulay_rank_eq_of_translated_hs_eq}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every original column is the actual scalar-coordinate jet of an
original monomial times one of the actual translated generator germs.
Thus the entire original column image lies in the scalar jet range.
The original coordinate-array map is injective on the genuine whole
jet image, so these two actual jet-image dimensions agree. Monotonicity
of dimension gives the asserted rank bound. The proved original HS
rank dictionary at the two points reads $D-r_p=D-r_{p'}$, with this
same genuine jet dimension $D$. Both ranks are at most $D$ by the
proved bound, so ordinary natural-number subtraction cancels faithfully
and yields $r_p=r_{p'}$. No rank bound is assumed as a certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One real common original generator family gives every intrinsic HS and centre rank dictionary]
\label{hir:actual-hs-constant-centre-common-real-jet-ranks}
\uses{hir:actual-centre-jet-rank-bound-and-hs-inversion,hir:real-centre-domain-whole-nearby-translated-ideals,hir:smooth-centre-fixed-real-original-ambient-hs-dictionary,hir:actual-intrinsic-whole-stalk-hilbert-samuel-value}
Let $A$ be an original coherent germ ideal on the original manifold
of dimension $d+t$. Let $C$ be actually regular of dimension $d$ at
$x$, and suppose its original intrinsic HS function at every point
of $C$ equals that at $x$ at every order. Then original geometry and
coherence internally give one true centred flattening $\Phi$, one
finite family of original functions $G_i$ generating $A$ on one real
original open $O\ni x$, and one real ambient open $U\ni0$ inside
$\Phi.\mathrm{target}$. The functions
$H_i=G_i\circ\mathrm{actualFlatteningInverseMap}(x,\Phi)$ are
holomorphic on that SAME $U$ and generate the unchanged actual
pullback sheaf there, with the entire original translated-ideal
identity at every actual point of $U$.
For every $p\in U$ and every $\ell$, the original intrinsic HS at
the actual inverse-coordinate point is the genuine whole jet dimension
minus the rank of the original Macaulay matrix of these SAME $H_i$
at $p$. On $U\cap(\mathbb C^d\times0)$ this actual matrix rank
equals its rank at $0$ for every order. The common generators and
ambient $U$ are chosen once, before any order.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSConstantCentreJetFrames.exists_original_hs_constant_centre_common_jet_ranks}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original regularity supplies a genuine centred flattening, and original
coherence and the actual inverse-coordinate holomorphicity supply
actual original generators $G_i$, their original open $O$, and one
smaller real open $U\ni0$ on which their literal composites $H_i$
generate the actual pulled-back sheaf. The existing entire translated
membership dictionary identifies its whole origin ideal with the
span of these very translated germs. Actual affine translations in
that dictionary and in the original Macaulay map agree by commutativity
of addition, so no ideal is replaced.
At every actual $p\in U$, the proved true nearby germ algebra
equivalence identifies this entire ideal with the original whole chart
stalk. Original chart invariance therefore identifies its HS with the
unchanged intrinsic HS. Apply the genuine matrix-rank dictionary to
obtain every asserted finite-order equality on this one $U$.
If $p$ is in the actual plane, the true flattening inverse identities
put its original inverse-coordinate point in $C$. Its entire intrinsic
HS equals that at $x$ by the original source hypothesis, and inverse
coordinates carry $0$ to $x$. Thus the two translated-ideal HS values
are equal at every order. The preceding proved rank bound and faithful
subtraction yield equality of the actual original ranks at $p$ and
$0$, without any caller-supplied rank or quotient-frame data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every order has a genuine maximal pivot at zero and whole centre quotient frames on a real neighbourhood]
\label{hir:actual-hs-constant-centre-origin-pivot-whole-frames}
\uses{hir:actual-hs-constant-centre-common-real-jet-ranks,hir:actual-entire-point-quotient-pivot-frame,hir:real-domain-fixed-flattening-all-original-whole-stalks}
The SAME original flattening, finite generators and common real
ambient $U$ can be chosen so that for EVERY $\ell$, there exist an
actual pivot size $k$, actual original word rows and columns, and a
real ambient open $D_\ell\subseteq U$ containing $0$ such that the
original pivot determinant is nonzero throughout $D_\ell$.
Here $k$ is the rank of the actual original Macaulay matrix at $0$;
its entire scalar projection has holomorphic entries on $D_\ell$.
The actual tangent open $V_\ell=\mathrm{tangentInclusion}^{-1}(D_\ell)$
is open and contains $0$. At every actual plane point of $D_\ell$,
the pivot-projection kernel is the ENTIRE original Macaulay image,
the whole germ-coordinate kernel is the actual translated ideal plus
the genuine maximal-ideal power, and the ENTIRE original nearby
chart-stalk quotient is linearly equivalent over $\mathbb C$ to the
same fixed original quotient model. These assertions therefore hold
at every tangent parameter in $V_\ell$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSConstantCentreJetFrames.exists_original_hs_constant_centre_origin_pivot_frames}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the preceding original common data once. Fix $\ell$ and let
$k$ be the actual matrix rank at $0$. The genuine finite-column
minor criterion says that rank less than $k$ is equivalent to the
vanishing of all actual $k$ by $k$ minors. The rank at zero equals
$k$, so one such original minor is nonzero at zero. This also covers
$k=0$, whose empty determinant is one. Intersect $U$ with the actual
nonzero locus of this genuine holomorphic minor. This is a real
ambient open $D_\ell$ containing zero, on which that very pivot is
invertible. Its original inverse-pivot projection entries are
holomorphic by the actual adjugate/determinant proof.
At an actual plane point of $D_\ell$, the preceding source-derived
rank constancy gives the actual rank $k$, while the selected original
pivot is nonzero there. The proved original pivot kernel and whole
germ-coordinate kernel theorems therefore apply to the entire
original ideal, not only a selected subspace. They construct its
whole translated-ideal quotient frame. The true original nearby germ
algebra equivalence carries this entire ideal and every genuine
maximal-ideal power to the original whole chart stalk; composing the
inverse of its quotient equivalence with that actual frame gives the
stated original quotient frame. Finally the actual continuous linear
tangent inclusion has open preimage $V_\ell$ and carries zero to
zero, so this tangent neighbourhood contains zero and every one of
its points maps to the actual plane. No generic free locus missing
the original point is used.
\end{proof}

These are source-derived finite-order quotient frames at the original
point, for one genuine common original generator family. The pivot
neighbourhood may depend on the order; no common neighbourhood for
all orders, derivative/filtration compatibility between orders,
normal-flatness, canonical reduced centre-power property, compatible
vertex-family selection or general positive-dimensional Bennett is
asserted. Those further obligations remain separate. An analytic
subset $W$ supplies the original coherent ideal $A=I_W$ by Cartan
coherence; no global analytic extension of the flattened local data
is required.
