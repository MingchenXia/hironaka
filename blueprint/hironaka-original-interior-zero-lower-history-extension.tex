\section{The original zero-contact lower record across an interior empty step}

The initial marked ideal and its actual chosen tangent direction are retained.
The argument is an explicit adaptation of the Step One contact construction
and empty-history functoriality: the contact zero set is closed only on the
chosen tangent open, so it is first replaced there by a genuinely closed
representative.  No contact, lower-record, tail or value comparison is an
input.  An absent tangent choice is handled by its actual empty open.

\begin{lemma}[Closed carriers across an interior identity empty blowup]
\label{hir:original-interior-empty-closed-strict-carrier}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorZeroLowerHistory.strictAlong_closed_interior_empty}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-interior-empty-history,bcalc:strict-submanifold}
For every closed subset $B\subseteq X$ and actual histories $H,S$,
the entire strict transforms of $B$ along $H\epsilon S$ and $HS$ are equal,
where $\epsilon$ is the genuine identity empty blowup.  No testness of either
history is required.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The strict transform $B_H$ is closed: at the empty history this is the
hypothesis, and at every last blowup step the strict transform is a closure.
The identity empty blowup takes $B_H$ to the closure of $B_H$, hence to $B_H$
itself.  Strict transformation along a concatenation is transformation first
along its head and then along its tail, by induction on the actual last steps
of that tail.  Applying the same tail $S$ to these equal carriers proves the
claim, including an empty $H$ or $S$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A closed representative of the actual chosen contact carrier]
\label{hir:original-chosen-contact-closed-representative}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorZeroLowerHistory.chosen_contact_closed_representative}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:step-one-lower-domain,mc:tangent-direction-along,mc:contact-empty-prefix}
Let $M$ be well formed and $x\in X$.  Put $(U,w)=\operatorname{tangentChoice}(M,x)$
and $A=M.N\cap V(w)$.  The actual set $U$ is open, and there exists a closed
$B\subseteq X$ such that $A\cap U=B\cap U$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If the original tangent-direction existence condition fails, the actual
choice is $(\varnothing,0)$; take $B=\varnothing$.  The open is empty and
both intersections are empty.  Otherwise the choice specification gives
a tangent direction on its actual open $U$.  Its derivative-ideal membership
and coherence of the original ideal imply holomorphicity of $w$ at every
point of $U$, hence continuity there.  Set
$B=M.N\cap\overline{V(w)\cap U}$, which is closed because $M.N$ is closed.
If $z\in A\cap U$, then $z\in B\cap U$ by inclusion into the closure.
Conversely, if $z\in B\cap U$ and $w(z)\ne0$, continuity at $z$ supplies
a neighbourhood on which $w$ is nonzero.  Since
$z\in\overline{V(w)\cap U}$, that neighbourhood meets $V(w)\cap U$, a
contradiction.  Thus $w(z)=0$ and $z\in A\cap U$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole actual masked contact carrier]
\label{hir:original-interior-zero-contact-carrier}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorZeroLowerHistory.masked_contact_carrier_dictionary}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-chosen-contact-closed-representative,hir:original-interior-empty-closed-strict-carrier,hir:original-interior-empty-masks,mc:strict-along-local}
Let $M$ be originally well formed, $H,S$ actual histories and $p$ a final
point.  The strict transforms of the actual original chosen contact carrier
in the two histories $HS$ and $H\epsilon S$ have equal traces on the actual
zero-contact mask for $HS,p$.  The tangent direction in the second history
is the same literal chosen direction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The composite projections of the two histories are equal, so their
projections of $p$ and hence their original tangent-choice input agree.
Choose the closed representative $B$ supplied by the preceding lemma for
this actual input.  Strict transforms are local over an open subset:
sets agreeing on $U$ have transforms agreeing over the projection preimage
of $U$.  To see this along a tower, induct on its actual last steps.  At
one step, intersecting a closure with the open projection preimage equals
the closure relative to that open; the agreed preimages outside the same
centre therefore give agreed strict transforms there.  The induction gives
the whole-tower statement.  Both contact transforms thus agree, over this
same projection preimage, with their respective transforms of $B$.
Those entire transforms of $B$ are equal by the closed-carrier lemma.
Every point of the actual mask belongs to its defining set, since the mask
is its interior; its projection therefore lies in $U$.  Restricting the
preceding equality to the entire actual mask proves equality of its contact
carrier traces.  This also covers the absent-choice branch, whose actual
mask has no points.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The entire original zero-contact lower marked record]
\label{hir:original-interior-actual-zero-lower-record}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorZeroLowerHistory.zeroLowerIndex_le_boundary,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorZeroLowerHistory.original_zero_lower_record}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-interior-zero-contact-carrier,hir:original-interior-empty-geometric-dictionary,hir:original-interior-empty-masks,hir:step-one-value-masked}
Let $M$ be originally well formed, $H$ an actual test history of $\overline M$,
$S$ a nonempty actual tail, and $p$ any final point.  Put
$j=|M.E|+|H|$.  The actual original masked lower marked ideal at level zero
for $H\epsilon S,p$ equals that for $HS,p$ with one empty boundary entry
inserted at $j$.  The index $j$ is bounded by the actual whole lower boundary.
Equality is heterogeneous only because the two equal actual masks remain
their respective literal carrier types.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The nonempty-tail producer supplies the entire actual record
\[
 \overline M_{H\epsilon S}=\operatorname{insertEmpty}_j(\overline M_{HS}).
\]
The coefficient construction along the carrier depends on its carrier,
ideal and mark and retains the entire boundary, so it commutes with this
future empty insertion.  The zero lower record changes its carrier to the
strict transform of the chosen original contact carrier.  The preceding
theorem identifies the full trace of that carrier on the actual old mask.
The original mask dictionary identifies the two whole actual mask opens.
Pullback along their inclusions therefore gives equal carrier, coefficient
ideal and mark.  Its boundary is the full transformed boundary mapped by
preimage; mapping commutes with list insertion and takes an empty set to
an empty set.  This proves equality of all four marked fields with the
stated insertion.  The old lower boundary has length
$|M.E|+|H|+|S|$, because coefficient formation retains the full boundary
and mapping by inclusion preserves length.  It therefore bounds $j$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual current-level short splits at every level]
\label{hir:original-interior-selected-all-level-short-splits}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorZeroLowerHistory.actual_short_all_level_splits}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-interior-empty-regime-run,hir:original-interior-empty-step-one-dictionary,hir:tower-split}
Let $M,H,S,y$ be as above, with $S$ any tail.  Set
$\ell=\operatorname{level}(M,HS,y)$ and
$r=\operatorname{run}_{M,\ell}(HS,y)$, and suppose $r<|S|$.
The actual current-level run in $H\epsilon S$ is precisely $r$.
Writing $D=\operatorname{stage}(S,r)$, $P=\operatorname{head}(S,r)$ and
$Q=\operatorname{tail}(S,r)$, the two entire actual split records are
$(D,HP,Q)$ and $(D,H\epsilon P,Q)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual level dictionary identifies the new current level with $\ell$.
The original run formula uses its first branch because $r<|S|$, and gives
the same $r$.  Since $r\leq|S|$, the actual concatenation split is obtained
by removing precisely the last $r$ steps from $S$, leaving head $HP$ and
tail $Q$.  Induction on those last steps supplies the full split record,
not merely the lengths.  Applying the same computation to the actual
prefix $H\epsilon$ gives head $H\epsilon P$, with exactly the same stage
$D$ and tail $Q$.  No positivity of $\ell$ is required.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine selected short zero-contact lower diagram]
\label{hir:original-interior-selected-zero-short-lower-diagram}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalInteriorZeroLowerHistory.actual_short_zero_lower_diagram}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-interior-selected-all-level-short-splits,hir:original-interior-actual-zero-lower-record,hir:original-interior-empty-masks,bcalc:tower-restrict}
Let $M$ be originally well formed, $H$ an actual test history of $\overline M$,
$S$ an actual tail and $y$ an actual final point with
$\operatorname{level}(M,HS,y)=0$.  Assume that its actual zero-level run
$r$ satisfies $r<|S|$.  Put $P=\operatorname{head}(S,r)$,
$Q=\operatorname{tail}(S,r)$, $p=Q.\operatorname{proj}(y)$ and
$j=|M.E|+|H|$.  The new actual current-level run is $r$, its selected
zero lower record is the old actual selected zero lower record with an
empty boundary entry inserted at $j$, and $j$ is a valid actual lower
boundary index.  The two whole actual restricted tails of the common
literal $Q$ are equal under equality of the respective actual masks.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding all-level short split theorem applies at the actual current
level zero.  It supplies both whole splits and the actual shared stage and
tail $Q$.  The selected prefix $P$ has length $|S|-r>0$, derived directly
from the given strict inequality.  Thus the original zero lower record
theorem applies to $H,P,p$ and supplies its full record comparison and the
valid whole-boundary index.  The original all-level mask dictionary at
level zero identifies the two actual masks; replacing one mask by that
proved equal open makes the entire restricted $Q$ records reflexively
equal, including all their centres, blowup data and projections.
No contact, selected-prefix nonemptiness, lower-index, lower-record or
restricted-tail comparison is supplied by the caller.
\end{proof}

These statements compare the actual zero-contact carrier, whole lower
records and whole restricted tails in the short-run branch.  They do not
assert transport of complete higher recursive invariant values, a global
maximum, noncompact scheduling or completion of the G3 parent.
