\section{The original mixed-dimensional elementary projection and its actual smooth source}

\begin{theorem}[Original analytic data construct the same canonical part-resolution projection]
\label{hir:original-nonpure-canonical-elementary-smooth-projection}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-ordered-resolution-producer,hir:original-increasing-dimension-list-smooth-pullback,hir:original-dimension-part-smooth-shift,hir:original-dimension-part-below-smooth-fibre-empty,hir:original-nonpure-singular-set-smooth-pullback,hir:original-stopped-strict-transform-smooth-square,hir:embedded-resolution-analytic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalNonpureCanonicalResolution.exists_original_nonpure_canonical_resolution}
\leanok
Let $W\subseteq X^n$ be an original compact analytic set with empty
interior, let $E_0$ be an original ordered SNC boundary, and suppose
$W\subseteq\overline{W\setminus|E_0|}$. Put
$G=\operatorname{Sing}W\cup(W\cap|E_0|)$ and list its actual
dimension parts in increasing dimension, with their natural labels.
These original inputs internally construct an actual finite ordered
canonical resolution tree $T$, its same elementary projection $p_T$,
and its whole ordered boundary $E_T$.

The projection is holomorphic, proper and surjective, admissible over
$G$, and an isomorphism off $G$. Its new-boundary union contains its
exceptional locus and lies over $G$; the inverse image of the whole
intrinsic singular set lies in the exceptional locus. The whole
conventional strict carrier is a closed submanifold and is the union
of the pairwise disjoint strict dimension parts. Along the strict
part of dimension $k$ it is intrinsically regular of dimension $k$,
and it has normal crossings with this same SNC boundary.

After fixing this same actual $T$, any original holomorphic
submersion $b:Y^m\to X^n$ internally constructs a literal actual
smooth source tree $Q$ and upper submersion $g$ with genuine
holomorphic Cartesian projection square and whole boundary
$E_Q=g^{-1}E_T$. The same assertions hold for the whole intrinsic
source carrier $b^{-1}W$, its own singular set and bad set
$\operatorname{Sing}(b^{-1}W)\cup(b^{-1}W\cap|b^{-1}E_0|)$, and
its own dimension parts. Whole source strict carriers and shifted
dimension parts are actual inverse images of their target strict
carriers. The source tree retains the raw pulled labelled list;
its nonempty-label filter is literally the increasing intrinsic
nonempty source dimension list. No source compactness, equality of
ideals, or incoming source construction is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the original ordered list $(W^{(k)},k)$ for $0\le k<n$.
Original analytic dimension decomposition makes each part analytic
and pure of its labelled dimension. Closedness inside the compact
$W$ makes every part compact. Its singular points and intersections
with distinct dimension parts lie in the original whole singular
set. Original boundary density restricts to every dimension part.
These are precisely the original good-part and pairwise-intersection
hypotheses of the actual ordered resolution producer. Apply that
producer once and retain its same actual canonical-stage tree,
projection and entire boundary.

Conventional strict transform commutes with this finite union.
The resolved strict parts are pairwise disjoint and closed. Near any
one of their points, the complement of the finite union of the other
strict parts is a genuine neighbourhood on which the whole strict
carrier equals that one part. Actual local equality therefore
transfers its intrinsic labelled regularity and normal-crossings
coordinates to the whole strict carrier. This proves the target
submanifold and whole normal-crossings statements. Parts of dimension
at least $n$ are genuinely empty since the original interior is
empty. The actual admissible sequence supplies the new-boundary and
exceptional containments and the isomorphism off $G$; whole strict
smoothness gives the singular-preimage exceptional containment.

For a given $b$, use the actual same-tree source already internally
constructed by the producer. Entire intrinsic singular-set equality
and literal ordered boundary inverse image identify the pulled $G$
with the source's own bad set. Openness of $b$ preserves empty
interior. Every original dimension part is dense outside $G$, so
the actual preserved-open strict-square theorem gives whole strict
inverse-image equalities, including exceptional points. The same
argument for $W$, whose finite dimension parts are each dense away
from $G$, gives the whole source strict-carrier square.

True intrinsic dimension-part pullback identifies the source part
of dimension $k+(m-n)$ with $b^{-1}W^{(k)}$. Parts below the fibre
dimension are actually empty by regularity reflection. Thus every
nonempty intrinsic source strict part is the inverse image of the
corresponding target strict part; smooth regular coordinates give
its intrinsic dimension, and inverse image preserves the proved
disjointness. The analytic source decomposition gives the whole
source strict carrier as their finite union. The same actual upper
submersion pulls back target whole submanifold and normal-crossings
coordinates with the entire source boundary. The actual source
admissible sequence supplies its own exceptional/new-boundary and
off-bad isomorphism dictionary, and source whole smoothness gives
its intrinsic singular-preimage containment. Finally the proved
ordered dimension-list identity gives precisely the stated
nonempty-input-list equality, including an empty source without a
false global ambient-dimension inequality.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This theorem is the original-input compact mixed-dimensional
elementary projection with a genuine compatible same-tree smooth
source. It retains all raw empty label and boundary positions.
Independent choices after filtering those positions still require
the separate recursive uniqueness and empty-stage-neutrality
theorems; no such assertion is inferred from list equality alone.
Relatively compact local mixed-dimensional assembly is also separate.
\end{remark}
