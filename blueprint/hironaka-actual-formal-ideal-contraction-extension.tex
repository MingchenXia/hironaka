\section{Actual formal extension contracts to the same entire original ideal}

Let $R=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^n,0}$, $S=\mathbb C[[X_1,\ldots,X_n]]$
and let $\mathcal T:R\hookrightarrow S$ be the independently constructed
GENUINE original complex Taylor algebra homomorphism. Original
coefficients, both actual ring products and original maximal powers are
unchanged. This is a convergent-to-formal ideal bridge for the
standard-basis route in \cite[\S7]{bierstone-milman97-invent-math}, not a
formal division or general strict-transform theorem.

\begin{lemma}[Every formal coefficient has an actual original finite polynomial approximation]
\label{hir:actual-formal-coefficients-original-polynomial-approximation}
\uses{hir:actual-original-formal-taylor-algebra-embedding,hir:actual-original-formal-taylor-constants-coordinates}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.originalTaylorAlgHom_polynomialEval,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.actualFormalApproximation,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.actualFormalApproximation_coefficient,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.actualFormalApproximation_mul_coefficient}
\leanok
For EVERY genuine formal series $A\in S$ and $k\ge0$, evaluate its
actual finite total-degree truncation below $k+1$ at the ORIGINAL
coordinate germs, obtaining $a_k\in R$. Then
$[X^\alpha]\mathcal T(a_k)=[X^\alpha]A$ whenever $|\alpha|<k+1$.
Moreover for ANY $B\in S$ these same genuine coefficients give
$[X^\alpha](\mathcal T(a_k)B)=[X^\alpha](AB)$ in this range.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine original polynomial evaluation followed by $\mathcal T$
is the usual actual polynomial-to-formal-series map: prove this on
constants and original variables, then under polynomial addition and
multiplication. The actual formal total-degree truncation retains
exactly the displayed original coefficients, giving the first equality.
In each actual product antidiagonal $\beta+\gamma=\alpha$ the true
nonnegative total degree of $\beta$ is at most $|\alpha|$; the genuine
first equality therefore replaces every left coefficient in the actual
convolution formula. This proves the product equality without any
convergence assumption on the arbitrary original formal input $A$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every finite actual formal ideal expression has true original jet representatives]
\label{hir:actual-formal-ideal-combination-original-jet-representatives}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-coefficients-original-polynomial-approximation,hir:actual-original-formal-taylor-maximal-power-kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.formal_combination_actual_jet_approximation}
\leanok
Let $g,f_1,\ldots,f_r\in R$ and $A_1,\ldots,A_r\in S$ be actual
original numerators and actual formal coefficients. If their literal
formal identity is $\mathcal T(g)=\sum_i A_i\mathcal T(f_i)$, then for
EVERY $k\ge0$ the preceding internally constructed original
approximants satisfy
\[
 g-\sum_i a_{i,k}f_i\in\mathfrak m^{k+1}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the genuine algebra map to the stated original whole remainder.
Its $\alpha$ coefficient, for $|\alpha|<k+1$, is the difference between
the coefficient of the given literal formal identity and the sum of the
coefficients of $\mathcal T(a_{i,k})\mathcal T(f_i)$. The first lemma
identifies EACH of these actual product coefficients with
$A_i\mathcal T(f_i)$, so the difference is zero. The independently
proved actual ORIGINAL maximal-power/low-formal-coefficient dictionary
then gives the displayed ENTIRE original power membership. Empty finite
generator families are included.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original whole ideal is recovered from its actual formal extension]
\label{hir:actual-formal-extension-whole-original-ideal-contraction}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-ideal-combination-original-jet-representatives,hir:actual-whole-ideal-maximal-filtration-separated,hir:actual-every-original-ideal-finite-jet-columns}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.mem_original_ideal_iff_formal_extension,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.comap_map_originalTaylorAlgHom,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.original_formal_ideal_extension_injective}
\leanok
For EVERY original ideal $I\subset R$ and EVERY original $g\in R$,
\[
 g\in I\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\mathcal T(g)\in S\mathcal T(I).
\]
Thus $\mathcal T^{-1}(S\mathcal T(I))=I$ as ENTIRE original ideals,
and actual formal extension is injective on the set of all original
ideals.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The forward implication is actual original image-ideal membership.
For the reverse, genuine original Noetherianity internally chooses finite
original generators $f_i$ of $I$. Actual image-of-span equality
identifies the ENTIRE formal extension with the ideal generated by
$\mathcal T(f_i)$. The assumed actual formal membership therefore
provides a finite literal formal identity of precisely the type in the
preceding theorem. At every $k$ its genuine original polynomial
combination lies in $I$ and its genuine original remainder lies in
$\mathfrak m^{k+1}$. Hence $g\in I+\mathfrak m^{k+1}$ at EVERY
order. Actual whole original Krull separation forces $g\in I$.
Both ideal directions give the contraction equality. Contract two equal
actual formal extensions to recover the two original ideals, proving
injectivity. There is no supplied ideal equality, faithful-flatness or
formal-completion certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original maximal thickenings extend to the same genuine variable-power thickenings]
\label{hir:actual-original-formal-variable-power-extension}
\uses{hir:actual-original-formal-taylor-constants-coordinates,hir:relative-normal-actual-base-jet-complex-monomial-dictionary,hir:actual-formal-extension-whole-original-ideal-contraction}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.map_original_maximalIdeal_eq_formal_variables,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.map_original_maximal_pow_eq_formal_variables_pow,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalIdealContraction.map_original_thickening_eq_formal_thickening}
\leanok
Write $\mathfrak n=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)\subset S$. Actual formal extension
sends the original $\mathfrak m$ to $\mathfrak n$, sends EVERY
$\mathfrak m^q$ to $\mathfrak n^q$, and sends EVERY original
$I+\mathfrak m^q$ to $S\mathcal T(I)+\mathfrak n^q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine original zero-tangent Hadamard dictionary identifies the
ENTIRE original $\mathfrak m$ with the ideal spanned by the SAME original
coordinate germs. Actual image-of-span and the independently proved
coordinate formula $\mathcal T(x_i)=X_i$ give precisely
$S\mathcal T(\mathfrak m)=\mathfrak n$. Actual algebra image preserves
ideal products and powers, and preserves ideal sums; these give the other
two equalities. All are equalities of the genuine ENTIRE ideals, including
$q=0$ and $n=0$. No completed quotient dimension, flatness or division is
inferred in this step.
\end{proof}
