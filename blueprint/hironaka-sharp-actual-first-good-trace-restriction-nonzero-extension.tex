\section{Genuine remaining-member nonvanishing on the actual reduced traces}

The recursive intersection step of the sharp argument needs actual
noncontainment in the OTHER original members. Separate reduced smooth
traces do not supply this by themselves. Here true order-one unit
cofactors convert containment into equality of full ideal germs, which
contradicts the internally constructed proper-germ quotient injection.
The conclusion concerns the actual chronological members; it does not
identify them with intersections with arbitrary old ambient strict
boundaries. FIRST Z remains distinct from the full sharp defect Sigma.

\begin{lemma}[Order-one containment of genuine relative principal germs forces equality]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-order-one-trace-containment-forces-germ-equality}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0088}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-relative-principal-order-one-reduced-trace}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstGoodTraceRestrictionNonzero.actual_orderOne_distinct_trace_nonzero}
\leanok
Let the actual closed smooth carrier be $N$, and let $I,J$ be coherent,
nonzero along $N$, and genuinely relatively principal at a point $x$ of
$N$. Suppose that both true trace orders are one and their WHOLE ideal
germs at $x$ are distinct. Then $J$ is nonzero along the literal trace
$S=N\cap V(I)$ at $x$. No reduced trace, selected contact, nonzero
restriction, or cofactor unit is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose actual relative generators $f$ and $g$. The genuine order-one
contact theorem constructs BOTH full germ directions between $I$ and
the vanishing ideal of the literal trace $S$. If $g$ vanished on $S$
near $x$, then $g$ would belong to $I$, so $g=cf$ on $N$ near $x$ for a
holomorphic cofactor $c$. Actual order one makes $f(x)=0$ and excludes
order at least two for $g$. If $c(x)=0$, then the product of the two true
order-one vanishing germs would make $g$ vanish to order at least two
on $N$, a contradiction. Thus $c$ is a genuine unit near $x$; its actual
holomorphic inverse expresses $f$ as a multiple of $g$ on $N$. Substituting
both actual relative generators gives BOTH full ideal germ inclusions,
contradicting their distinctness. Hence $g$ does not vanish identically
on $S$ near $x$. Negating the neighborhood vanishing condition gives
actual points of $S$ with $g\ne0$ in every neighborhood of $x$, and $g$
is an actual member of $J$. This is precisely the true nonzero restriction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original inputs internally produce genuine other-member noncontainment on every FIRST-good trace]
\label{hir:sharp-original-first-good-other-member-trace-nonzero}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0088}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-order-one-trace-containment-forces-germ-equality,hir:sharp-actual-first-good-chronological-factor-rigidity}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstGoodTraceRestrictionNonzero.original_first_good_other_member_nonzero,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstGoodTraceRestrictionNonzero.original_first_good_trace_not_contained_in_other_zero}
\leanok
ONLY original smooth carrier, ambient SNC boundary, whole-boundary density
and a genuine finite template history internally supply the following.
At EACH point of a chosen actual chronological trace outside literal
current FIRST Z, EACH OTHER original member ideal is nonzero on that
literal trace. Moreover, every actual ambient neighborhood of that point
contains a point of the SAME trace outside the OTHER member's true zero
locus. Neither a nonzero restriction nor a germ-distinctness certificate
is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original finite-history geometry independently gives actual coherence,
nonzero and relative generators of every member on the SAME current
carrier. The genuine FIRST-good factor rigidity supplies order one for
every actual proper member and injectivity of the true full ideal-germ
quotient map. The chosen trace point makes its member proper. If the
other member is proper, the two different original positions have
DIFFERENT full ideal germs by that quotient injection. Apply the preceding
unit-cofactor producer. If the other member is a unit, choose its actual
unit germ; it is nonzero at the chosen trace point and cannot vanish on
that trace in a neighborhood. Both branches therefore produce a genuine
member nonzero on the actual trace arbitrarily near the point.

For actual geometric noncontainment, coherence continues this genuine
member to every point of a sufficiently small ambient neighborhood.
Intersect that neighborhood with its actual frequent nonzero trace
points. A point where this continued member is nonzero cannot lie in the
OTHER ideal's literal zero locus. Such points therefore occur in EVERY
ambient neighborhood. This is the needed source-backed noncontainment
of the true lower trace; simultaneous trace/exceptional SNC, recursive
intersection resolution, a FIRST-Z-erasing driver, higher original
invariant decrease, exceptional tauD and termination remain separate.
\end{proof}
