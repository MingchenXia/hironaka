\section{Canonical elementary principalization with an original boundary}

The initial ordered SNC boundary may be nonempty.  Compactness is required
only of the original ideal's zero locus; the ambient manifold and a smooth
source may be noncompact.  All total transforms below are whole germ ideals.

\begin{lemma}[The entire ordered boundary of an actual empty history]
\label{hir:original-all-empty-whole-boundary}
\uses{hir:geo-transform,hir:blowup-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryCanonicalPrincipalization.boundary_of_all_empty_tower}
\leanok
Let $M$ be any original marked record whose initial boundary members are
closed, and let $T$ be any actual finite blowup tower with every actual
centre empty.  For its whole projection $p$,
\[
 E_T=(p^{-1}E_0)\mathbin{+\!+}
        [\varnothing,\ldots,\varnothing]_{\operatorname{length}T}.
\]
The first list consists of the inverse images of every original member,
in its original order.  No coherence, canonicality, terminality or
incoming boundary comparison is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual tower.  The identity tower has its original ordered
boundary and length zero.  At an actual empty-centre blowup $\pi$, the
strict transform of a closed old member $H$ is
$\overline{\pi^{-1}(H\setminus\varnothing)}=\pi^{-1}H$, since $\pi$ is
continuous.  Each inverse image of an original closed member remains
closed.  The strict transform of an empty member is empty, and the newly
appended member is exactly $\pi^{-1}\varnothing=\varnothing$.
Distribute this operation over the induction formula.  The original
inverse images compose to those under the new whole projection, and
the empty suffix gains exactly one member.  This proves the literal
ordered equality, including zero-length and empty ambient cases.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Canonical boundary principalization with compact zero locus]
\label{hir:original-canonical-boundary-principalization-compact-cosupport}
\uses{hir:canonical-resolution-compact-cosupport,hir:principalization-boundary,hir:admissible-sequence,hir:original-global-terminal-finite-whole-biholomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryCanonicalPrincipalization.compactCosupportCanonicalTower,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryCanonicalPrincipalization.canonical_boundary_principalization}
\leanok
Let $I$ be a coherent ideal on a complex manifold $X$, nonzero as a germ
at every point, with compact zero locus.  Let $E_0$ be an original ordered
SNC divisor and $M=(X,X,E_0,I,1)$.  The actual compact-cosupport canonical
driver internally supplies a finite tower $T:D\to X$.  Retain this same
$T$, its projection $p$, its effective exceptional divisor $D_T$, and its
actual accumulated boundary $E_T$.  Then $T$ is canonical and terminal,
$p$ is holomorphic, proper and surjective, all its centres lie over
$Z(I)$, and it is an admissible sequence relative to $E_0$.  Moreover,
\[
 p^*I=I_{D_T},\qquad E_T\text{ is SNC},\qquad
 \operatorname{supp}D_T
   =\bigcup_{H\in E_T\text{ after its first }|E_0|\text{ members}} H
   =p^{-1}Z(I).
\]
The exceptional locus lies in $\operatorname{supp}D_T$, and $p$ is an
isomorphism over $X\setminus Z(I)$.  There is a genuine biholomorphism
$\eta_X$ from the original glued terminal manifold of $M$ to $D$ with
$p\eta_X=P_M$, its original glued projection.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original SNC boundary is transverse to the whole ambient manifold.
Together with coherence and germwise nonvanishing, this puts the literal
$M$ in the ambient invariant domain.  Its original cosupport is exactly
$Z(I)$, hence is compact.  Apply the actual compact-cosupport driver to
this record and choose one of its genuine finite canonical terminal
towers.  Every actual centre lies over the original cosupport, and the
test-tower dictionary gives admissibility relative to the original
ordered boundary.  A finite actual blowup projection is holomorphic,
proper and surjective.

At mark one and whole ambient carrier, the actual terminal dictionary
gives the asserted equality of whole total-transform ideals.  Actual
well-formedness gives SNC for the same $E_T$.  The tower exceptional
support formula removes exactly the original $|E_0|$ members, giving the
first support equality.  The zero-locus pullback formula and the whole
ideal equality give the second.  Exceptional-locus containment therefore
puts its image over $Z(I)$, so the actual blowup sequence is an
isomorphism over the complement.  Apply the original finite-terminal
identification to this same canonical terminal tower to obtain
$\eta_X$ and its literal projection identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same boundary principalization under an arbitrary smooth base map]
\label{hir:original-canonical-boundary-principalization-smooth}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-boundary-principalization-compact-cosupport,hir:original-global-finite-smooth-source-history,hir:smooth-original-finite-terminal-intrinsic-smooth-map,hir:original-global-finite-terminal-smooth-map-cartesian,hir:submersion-relative-control-joint-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryCanonicalPrincipalization.canonical_boundary_principalization_smooth_base_change}
\leanok
Retain the preceding original data and its same internally chosen
canonical tower $T$.  Every holomorphic submersion $b:Y\to X$ internally
constructs an actual finite canonical terminal tower $S:C\to Y$ for
$N=b^*M$, with initial boundary $b^{-1}E_0$.  No compactness of $Y$ or
of the pulled zero locus is required.  Put $q=S.\mathrm{proj}$.
Its whole ideal is principalized by its own exceptional divisor $D_S$,
and its own accumulated boundary $E_S$ is SNC.  There are holomorphic
submersions $g:C\to D$ and
$G:\widetilde Y_N\to\widetilde X_M$ such that
\[
 pg=bq,\qquad P_MG=bP_N,\qquad
 \eta_XG=g\eta_Y,\qquad E_S=g^{-1}E_T.
\]
Both projection squares are holomorphic Cartesian squares.  Here
$\eta_X,\eta_Y$ are the genuine finite-to-glued terminal
biholomorphisms for these same histories.  The same target whole-ideal
principalization and SNC boundary are retained in the conclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Retain the target selected by the preceding theorem.  Construct its
actual smooth pullback one blowup at a time.  Its source centres are the
literal inverse images of the target centres, and each upper map is a
holomorphic submersion with the genuine Cartesian universal property.
The whole marked-record dictionary and original ambient invariant
transport give canonicality and terminality for the literal $N$ on this
finite source history.  Its domain supplies coherence and SNC; applying
the mark-one terminal identity to its whole ambient ideal gives
$q^*(b^*I)=I_{D_S}$.

The source whole-record dictionary gives the literal indexed equality
$E_S=g^{-1}E_T$ for the actual upper map.  The intrinsic finite terminal
map equals this actual map by its genuine terminal Cartesian uniqueness,
so the equality holds for the same $g$ used in the projection comparison.
The finite/global comparison conjugates this $g$ by $\eta_X,\eta_Y$ to
the stated $G$.  Biholomorphic conjugation preserves submersion and the
Cartesian universal property.  Its definition gives
$\eta_XG=g\eta_Y$, and the finite identification identities give both
glued projection equations.  Thus no independent source compactness or
source-history certificate has entered the construction.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
These are full terminal ideal principalizations, with an arbitrary
original SNC boundary.  They are distinct from the stopped prefix used
for embedded resolution.  The source ideal is the literal $b^*I$;
no singular reduced vanishing ideal is relabeled as this pullback.
\end{remark}
