\section{A genuine modified trace-order centre preserves the original smooth normal-crossings region}

The original embedded marked ideal uses the ambient carrier and can blow up
already resolved boundary intersections. Bierstone--Milman 1997,
Section~12, pp.~292--294, instead uses the ideals of the original boundary
restricted to the smooth current variety. For one original smooth boundary
member $H$, its first actual defect is order at least two of
$\mathcal I_H\mathcal O_W$. We implement this genuine first centre-selection
phase. The whole multi-member product/distinct-branch defect, recursive
intersection phases, exceptional monomial cleanup and termination remain
separate mathematical producers.

\begin{lemma}[A true transverse boundary coordinate has restricted order one]
\label{hir:sharp-single-boundary-transverse-trace-order-one}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.originalBoundaryTraceMarked_cosupp_eq_empty_of_normalCrossings}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0087}
\uses{hir:snc,hir:monomial-case,hir:monomial-part-along,hir:nc-density-transverse}
\leanok
Let $W$ be a closed smooth submanifold of a positive-dimensional complex
manifold, let $[H]$ be SNC, and suppose $W$ has normal crossings with $[H]$
and is dense off $H$. Then the actual restricted marked ideal
$(N=W,E=[],\mathcal I_H,2)$ has empty cosupport.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Density excludes a boundary coordinate containing $W$, so the actual
normal-crossings family is transverse to $W$. At a point of $W\cap H$, use
the genuine simultaneous adapted coordinates: $W$ is a coordinate plane,
and $H$ is one of its free coordinate hyperplanes. The original ambient
coordinate function belongs to the actual original vanishing ideal of
$H$. Its restriction to $W$ has order one: the true local retraction onto
the coordinate plane preserves this free coordinate, while the original
ambient flat-coordinate order theorem says it is not in the square of
the actual point ideal. Thus it cannot have restricted order two, which
contradicts cosupport membership. At a point of $W\setminus H$, mark-one
cosupport is the actual zero locus and is already empty. Mark-two
cosupport is contained in mark-one cosupport. This proves emptiness at
every point without assuming a desired order or cosupport certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual original trace marked ideal lies in the original driver domain]
\label{hir:sharp-single-boundary-trace-original-domain}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.originalBoundaryTraceMarked,Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.originalBoundaryIdeal_isNonzeroAlongAt_of_dense,Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.originalBoundary_isClosedSubmanifold,Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.originalBoundaryTraceMarked_isInvDomain,Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.originalBoundaryTraceMarked_cosupp_subset_inter}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0087}
\uses{hir:snc,hir:centre-function}
\leanok
Let $W$ be closed and smooth of fixed dimension $d\le n$, and let $H$ be
a smooth original boundary with $[H]$ SNC. Suppose
$W\subset\overline{W\setminus H}$. The actual marked ideal
$(N=W,E=[],\mathcal I_H,2)$ is in the original canonical invariant domain.
Its actual cosupport is closed and contained in $W\cap H$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original boundary vanishing ideal is coherent because $H$ is a
closed smooth submanifold. Its actual restriction to $W$ is nonzero at
every point. Outside $H$ use the original unit germ. At a point of $H$,
the genuine SNC chart gives a local boundary coordinate generating a
germ of $\mathcal I_H$. Density of $W\setminus H$ gives arbitrarily nearby
points of $W$ where this original coordinate is nonzero. Hence it does
not vanish identically on $W$ near the point. The true smooth carrier,
empty divisor, positive mark two and fixed dimension give every original
domain field. Actual coherent restricted cosupport is closed. Its
containment in $W\cap H$ follows from monotonicity in the mark and the
original mark-one zero-locus identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual trace defect avoids every genuine original good normal-crossings neighborhood]
\label{hir:sharp-single-boundary-trace-avoids-good-nc-region}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.originalSmoothNCRegion,Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.originalSmoothNCRegion_isOpen,Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.originalBoundaryTraceMarked_cosupp_disjoint_goodRegion}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0087}
\uses{hir:sharp-single-boundary-transverse-trace-order-one,hir:sharp-single-boundary-trace-original-domain}
\leanok
Define the original good region as the union of the genuine original open
neighborhoods on which the actual restriction of $W$ has normal crossings
with the actual restriction of $[H]$. The original trace marked ideal's
mark-two cosupport is disjoint from this whole good region.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix a witnessing actual original open. Actual open restriction preserves
the smooth carrier, SNC divisor and the density away from $H$, by the
open-map closure identity. The first lemma gives empty cosupport of the
literal restricted marked ideal. The genuine marked open-restriction
cosupport dictionary is the inverse image of the original cosupport.
Thus no point of that open is in the original cosupport. This holds for
every genuine witnessing open, giving disjointness from their whole union.
The good region is open by its actual open-cover definition; no desired
preservation or comparison certificate is part of this definition.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Only original data produce a true modified smooth centre]
\label{hir:sharp-single-boundary-actual-modified-trace-centre}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.exists_originalBoundaryTraceCentre}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0087}
\uses{hir:sharp-single-boundary-trace-original-domain,hir:sharp-single-boundary-trace-avoids-good-nc-region,hir:centre-function,hir:monomial-case,hir:drop-containing-members}
\leanok
Under the preceding original hypotheses, suppose $W$ is compact and the
actual mark-two trace cosupport is nonempty. The genuine original
canonical invariant of the actual trace marked ideal produces a nonempty
closed smooth centre $C$ in that actual cosupport. In particular
$C\subset W\cap H$, $C$ avoids the entire original good normal-crossings
region, and $C$ has normal crossings with the true original boundary
$[H]$. It is genuinely admissible for both the actual trace marked ideal
and the original embedded marked ideal $(X,X,[H],\mathcal I_W,1)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true trace cosupport is closed inside compact $W$. The original
canonical invariant is upper semicontinuous there and attains a maximum.
Its actual maximum locus is nonempty and is a closed smooth admissible
centre by the original centre-production theorem, applied to the true
trace domain and literal zero-length tower. This is an internally
produced centre, not a supplied maximum or desired smooth-centre field.
Its actual cosupport containment supplies $C\subset W\cap H$ and
disjointness from the original good region. Normal crossings with the
empty trace divisor follow from admissibility. Since the true original
member $H$ contains the whole centre, the proved containing-member
normal-crossings theorem adds that member and proves normal crossings
with $[H]$. Mark-one cosupport of the original embedded vanishing ideal
is exactly $W$. Consequently the same genuine smooth centre is also
admissible for the original embedded marked ideal, with its actual
original boundary.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The produced actual blowup preserves every original good normal-crossings point]
\label{hir:sharp-single-boundary-actual-modified-blowup}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpSingleBoundaryTraceCentre.exists_originalBoundaryTraceBlowup}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0087}
\uses{hir:sharp-single-boundary-actual-modified-trace-centre,hir:smooth-current-ideal-along-tower,bex:blowup-exists,hir:admissible-sequence}
\leanok
The preceding original data produce an actual blowup along the internally
selected centre, with proper holomorphic original projection and genuine
isomorphism over the entire original good normal-crossings region. Its
actual strict transform of $W$ is smooth, and the actual transformed
original boundary consists of the genuine geometric strict transform
$\overline{\pi^{-1}(H\setminus C)}$ of $H$ followed
by the true exceptional divisor and is SNC.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The centre is contained in the true restricted cosupport, which has
empty interior by original domain membership. The existing genuine
blowup construction therefore constructs the actual map and manifold.
The projection is proper and holomorphic. Centre disjointness from the
original good region and the true off-centre blowup isomorphism prove
the asserted preservation over that whole region. Since $C\subset H$
and $W$ is dense away from $H$, it is dense away from $C$. The genuine
coordinate blowup theorem for a smooth submanifold containing a smooth
centre makes its geometric strict transform smooth, and the actual
density dictionary identifies that transform with the true strict
transform. Original embedded admissibility and genuine transformed
well-formedness give SNC of the literal original transformed boundary,
retaining its original member and the actual exceptional member.
No termination of a freshly initialized or whole modified driver is
claimed.
\end{proof}
