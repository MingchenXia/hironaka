\section{Actual native manifold charts at original regular model points}

All quotient models are the genuine reduced locally ringed subspaces
constructed from the entire original vanishing sheaf. Regularity is the

\begin{theorem}[The actual model of the whole manifold is the native manifold]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-univ-native-manifold}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-inclusion-stalk,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-reduced-stalks,hir:reduced-analytic-complex-point-models}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceManifoldCharts.actualSubspaceUnivIso,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceManifoldCharts.actualSubspaceUnivIso_hom,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceManifoldCharts.actualSubspaceUnivIso_inv_point}
\leanok
The actual quotient-subspace model of the whole complex manifold is
internally isomorphic to its native locally ringed space. The forward
map is the original actual subspace inclusion, and the inverse commutes
with the original complex-point structural maps.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every actual original section in the whole-manifold vanishing ideal
is zero at each point of its domain, hence is the zero function.
The genuine entire ideal-section germ-image equality therefore proves
that the whole actual vanishing stalk ideal is zero. The original
inclusion stalk map has that entire kernel and is surjective, so it is
a genuine ring isomorphism at each point. The literal whole-subtype
inclusion is a topological open embedding. The native stalk criterion
therefore makes the same actual locally ringed inclusion an open
immersion. Its image is the whole manifold, so the native universal
property lifts the identity map through it. This constructs its actual
inverse; open-immersion monicity gives both inverse identities. The
original inclusion structural triangle, composed with this genuine
inverse identity, proves the complex-point triangle for the inverse.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Different-dimensional plane projection gives an actual native manifold model]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-coordinate-plane-native-manifold}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-univ-native-manifold,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-mutual-restriction-iso}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceManifoldCharts.actualCoordinatePlaneManifoldIso,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceManifoldCharts.actualCoordinatePlaneManifoldIso_inv_point}
\leanok
Let $\Omega\subset\mathbf C^{d+t}$ be an actual open. Write $\pi$ for the
first-coordinate projection and $\iota$ for the zero-normal insertion.
Put $V=\iota^{-1}\Omega$ and $R=\Omega\cap\pi^{-1}V$. The actual model
of $R\cap(\mathbf C^d\times0)$ is internally isomorphic over the complex
point to the native manifold $V\subset\mathbf C^d$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The domains $V$ and $R$ are actual opens because $\pi$ and $\iota$ are
continuous linear maps. Projection is a genuine holomorphic map
$R\to V$, and insertion is a genuine holomorphic map $V\to R$.
Their composition on $V$ is the identity. On the actual coordinate-plane
subset of $R$, insertion after projection is also the identity. They
carry that whole subset and the whole $V$ into each other. The proved
ambient-extension-independent quotient-subspace construction therefore
produces an actual different-dimensional model isomorphism, using
inverse identities only on those subsets. Compose it with the preceding
native identification of the whole $V$ model. The actual holomorphic
map structural triangles and the native-model triangle prove compatibility
with the complex point. In particular projection of arbitrary points of
$\Omega$ is not presumed to stay in $\Omega$; the actual shrinking $R$
is part of the construction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original regularity supplies a genuine native manifold open chart]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-regular-native-manifold-chart}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-coordinate-plane-native-manifold,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-open-restriction-iso,hir:actual-flat-coordinate-open-biholomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceManifoldCharts.exists_actual_regular_subspace_manifold_chart}
\leanok
If $C\subset M^{d+t}$ and $\operatorname{RegularAtDim}(C,x,d)$ in the
original geometric sense, internally construct an actual open
$V\subset\mathbf C^d$ and a genuine locally ringed morphism from the
native manifold $V$ to the actual model of $C$. It is an actual open
immersion over the complex point and its genuine image contains $x$.
No manifold structure, isomorphism of sheaves, chart or stalk-equivalence
certificate on $C$ is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the same actual centred flattening $\Phi$ from original regularity.
Its true flat domain is genuinely biholomorphic to the actual open
$\Omega=\Phi.\operatorname{target}$. Use $R$ and $V$ from the preceding
theorem. Pull $R$ back through that actual flat-coordinate biholomorphism
to a genuine open of the flat domain. Restricting the actual coordinate
functions and their actual inverses gives a biholomorphism from this
open to $R$. The original whole-set regularity equivalence identifies
its restricted $C$ with the whole coordinate-plane subset of $R$.
The actual quotient-subspace mutual restriction theorem therefore gives
an internally constructed whole model isomorphism. Compose it with
the preceding native plane-model isomorphism. Invert this isomorphism
and follow it by both genuine actual open-subspace inclusions into $C$.
The result is a genuine open immersion, and each actual structural
triangle gives its complex-point compatibility. The original point
maps to zero under the centred flattening; zero belongs to $R$ and its
inverse image is the original point. The actual inverse identities
therefore give a point of $V$ mapping to $x$, proving image coverage.
The construction also applies when $d=0$ or $t=0$.
\end{proof}

These are genuine smooth local charts for the actual reduced embedded
models. Abstract resolution descent and termination remain distinct G1
obligations.
