\section{Literal centre positions for initially smooth sets}

\begin{lemma}[The current ideal for an initially smooth set]
\label{hir:smooth-current-ideal-along-tower}
\uses{hir:geo-transform,hir:stage-cosupport-off-boundary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothStageCenters.smooth_strictTransform_and_ideal_geoTransformAlong_of_centresAvoid}
\leanok
Let $W$ be a closed smooth submanifold, and let $O\subset X$ be open with
$W\subset\overline{W\cap O}$. Every actual multiple test tower of
$(X,X,E_0,\mathcal I_W,1)$ whose centres avoid the preimages of $O$ has
smooth current strict transform at every prefix. Moreover its current
controlled ideal is exactly $\mathcal I_{W_j}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the recorded tower. The initial equality and smoothness are
immediate. At a step after prefix $T$, induction gives a smooth current
$S=W_T$ and ideal $\mathcal I_S$. The ambient carrier stays the whole
manifold and the mark stays one, so the current cosupport is exactly $S$.
Thus admissibility puts the actual centre $C$ inside $S$.

Density transports to the strict transform:
$S\subset\overline{S\cap T^{-1}O}$. The actual centre avoids $T^{-1}O$,
so $S\subset\overline{S\setminus C}$. The geometric strict transform
$\overline{\pi^{-1}(S\setminus C)}$ therefore equals the library strict
transform, including possible codimension-one components of the centre.
The coordinate blowup theorem for a smooth submanifold containing the
centre makes that transform smooth and proves the actual ideal identity
\[
 \pi^*\mathcal I_S=\mathcal O(-\pi^{-1}C)\mathcal I_{S'}.
\]
Its controlled transform with mark one is exactly $\mathcal I_{S'}$,
by cancellation of the invertible exceptional factor. Strict-transform
composition identifies $S'$ with the strict transform for the extended
prefix. This proves both induction claims without assuming the current
centre containment or current ideal equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Literal centre position for an initially smooth set]
\label{hir:smooth-current-centres-in-boundary}
\uses{hir:smooth-current-ideal-along-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothStageCenters.stageCentresSmoothInBoundary_of_isGeoTestTower}
\leanok
Under the preceding assumptions, if all centres lie over $W\cap|E_0|$,
then every current strict transform $W_j$ is smooth and every recorded
centre satisfies $C_j\subset W_j\cap|E_j|$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply Lemma~\ref{hir:smooth-current-ideal-along-tower} at each prefix to
obtain smoothness and equality of the actual current cosupport with $W_j$.
Admissibility puts $C_j$ in $W_j$. Its image belongs to $|E_0|$ by the
assumed original bad locus. A point outside the current boundary has image
outside the initial boundary, by the actual boundary-transform induction.
Consequently every point of $C_j$ belongs to $|E_j|$. These are exactly the
smooth-current alternative in the literal classical centre condition.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual boundary resolution with literal smooth-current centres]
\label{hir:smooth-boundary-resolution-stage-centres}
\uses{hir:smooth-current-centres-in-boundary,hir:embedded-resolution-boundary,hir:strict-transform-density-transverse}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0009}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothStageCenters.exists_tower_embeddedResolution_smooth_boundary_stage_centres}
\leanok
Let $W$ be a compact smooth analytic set, regular of fixed dimension $d<n$
at every point. Let $E_0$ be SNC and assume $W\subset\overline{W\setminus|E_0|}$.
There is an actual finite admissible resolution tower over $W\cap|E_0|$
whose final strict transform is smooth of dimension $d$, has normal crossings
with its SNC accumulated divisor, and is transverse to that divisor.
At every recorded step the current strict transform is smooth and the
actual centre is contained in its intersection with the actual current
divisor. Thus this existence theorem satisfies the literal smooth-current
alternative at every step, with no centre-containment assumption.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The dense pure-dimensional regular locus and $d<n$ give empty interior.
Cartan coherence, nonzero germs, and the SNC initial boundary construct
the domain of the actual canonical invariant for
$(X,X,E_0,\mathcal I_W,1)$. Its cosupport is the given compact $W$.
The actual compact-cosupport driver supplies a finite canonical resolution.
Take $O=X\setminus|E_0|$. It misses all initial boundary members, the set
$W$ is regular of dimension $d$ there, and $W\cap O$ is dense in $W$.
The first-centre construction for the original boundary existence theorem
therefore produces an actual prefix tower whose centres avoid the preimages
of $O$ and whose final strict transform has normal crossings with the
accumulated divisor.

Every centre lies over $W$, by the actual cosupport-transport producer,
and over $|E_0|$, because it avoids $O$. Intersect these two proved containments
to obtain admissibility over $W\cap|E_0|$. Well-formedness of the actual
marked transform gives the SNC output boundary. The earlier density
transversality theorem applies, since adjoining $W\cap|E_0|$ to the bad
set $|E_0|$ changes nothing. Finally apply
Theorem~\ref{hir:smooth-current-centres-in-boundary} to this produced tower:
it proves the exact current-stage centre condition for every recorded step.
The existence consumer carries actual geometric data, rather than accepting
a caller-supplied centre-position certificate.
\end{proof}

The initially singular case of the literal centre-position obligation remains
open. Its controlled ideal can retain exceptional branches away from the
strict transform, so the preceding smooth-input ideal identity does not
extend to that case by dropping smoothness.
