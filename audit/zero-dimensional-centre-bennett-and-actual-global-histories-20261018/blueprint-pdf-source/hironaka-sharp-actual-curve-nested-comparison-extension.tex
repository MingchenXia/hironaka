\section{Actual nested restrictions of chosen comparison maps}

A direct comparison on a smaller common open must be compared with the
actual restriction of the previously chosen map, rather than replaced by
an unrelated existential witness. All constructions below retain every
empty trace centre and every real padding position.

\begin{lemma}[Actual matching smaller windows give a genuine smaller base map]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-nested-comparison-base}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-comparison-open-biholomorphism,bcalc:restrict-nested}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveNestedComparison.nested_window_preimage,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveNestedComparison.nestedBaseComparison,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveNestedComparison.nestedBaseComparison_val}
\leanok
Let $W'\subset X'$ and $W\subset X$ be actual opens and let
$\Phi:W'\to W$ be a genuine biholomorphism. Let $V'\subset W'$ and
$V\subset W$ be genuine smaller ambient opens, whose memberships correspond
under the actual underlying map $\Phi$. Construct a genuine biholomorphism
$V'\to V$. Its underlying point map is exactly the old $\Phi$ applied to
the actual inclusion of the smaller source point into $W'$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual window membership equivalence identifies the nested source open
inside $W'$ with the inverse image of the nested target open inside $W$.
Restrict the genuine $\Phi$ to those exact opens, with its actual inverse.
Precompose with the inverse of the true source nested-open flattening and
postcompose with the true target nested-open flattening. Both flattenings
are identity on the original ambient points. Their real inverse equations
therefore prove the stated exact underlying point map. This is a genuine
map of actual opens, with no assumed smaller-window comparison.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual comparisons of restricted towers restrict and flatten again]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-nested-stagewise-comparison}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-nested-comparison-base,hir:sharp-actual-stagewise-comparison-open-restriction,bcalc:restrict-nested}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveNestedComparison.exists_nested_tower_comparison}
\leanok
For two actual original towers and an actual stagewise comparison of their
restrictions to $W'$ and $W$, construct a genuine stagewise comparison of
their literal restrictions to $V'$ and $V$ over the smaller base map just
constructed. The terminal point map is the original comparison applied to
the actual inclusion of the smaller source endpoint into the old restricted
endpoint. No history position is removed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First restrict the genuine stagewise comparison to the nested target open
presenting $V$ inside $W$. The preceding actual window identity identifies
its genuine inverse-image source open with the nested open presenting $V'$
inside $W'$. The stagewise restriction theorem supplies all true trace
centres and blowup squares before flattening. The existing genuine
nested-restriction tower isomorphisms identify the two double restrictions
with the literal restrictions of the original towers to $V'$ and $V$.
Invert the source identification, compose the actual restricted comparison,
then compose the target identification. Its base map is exactly the
constructed smaller map. The two real flattenings and their inverses are
identity on ambient endpoint points. The resulting underlying point map
is therefore exactly the claimed old terminal map on the actual included
point. This does not infer stagewise isomorphism from a commuting composite
projection square.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Chosen padded comparisons have genuine smaller-window compatibility]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-nested-padded-comparison}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-nested-stagewise-comparison,hir:sharp-actual-padded-history-open-flattening,hir:sharp-actual-terminal-recipe-padding-independence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveNestedComparison.exists_nested_padded_comparison,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveNestedComparison.nested_padded_comparison_direct}
\leanok
For two actual original prefix-and-tail histories, suppose a genuine
stagewise comparison has been chosen between their padded restrictions to
$W'$ and $W$. On the matching actual smaller windows, construct a stagewise
comparison between the literal padded smaller restrictions with the exact
old terminal point map. Every direct comparison of those smaller histories
has that same map, including any different genuine padding cut which,
together with the old cut, bounds both actual original tail lengths.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the actual padded-history flattenings on both large windows.
Invert their direction as needed to conjugate the chosen comparison into
a genuine comparison of the literal restrictions of the two full original
padded histories. Each conjugating map is identity on ambient endpoint
points, so the raw terminal point map is still the chosen one.
Apply the preceding nested stagewise theorem to those exact original full
padded histories. Independently construct the genuine padded-history
flattenings on the two smaller windows, and conjugate the real smaller
comparison into the literal restricted prefix-and-tail histories. All
flattenings and inverse flattenings preserve the actual ambient point, so
the constructed terminal map has the old underlying map on the actual
included source point.

Restriction preserves both actual tail lengths. Thus any other stated cut
still bounds the same smaller tails. Extend the two genuine padded
comparisons to the common sum of the cuts by actual empty-centre identity
blowups, retaining all positions. The real fixed-history blowup uniqueness
theorem identifies their terminal maps. This proves compatibility for
chosen comparisons and their actual smaller-window restrictions; it does
not assert that an arbitrary endpoint witness carries a retained recipe,
or that geometric manifold descent or global scheduling is complete.
\end{proof}
