\section{Genuine whole lower invariant cuts inherited from original Step One cuts}

A lower TEST alone does not give the full lower invariant cut needed for
recursive preparation. This chapter derives that cut from the unchanged
original Step One values and the genuine canonical lower comparison. The
first numerical cut lemma permits any original reference level. Only the
positive-level consumer constructs actual appended lower TEST geometry;
no zero-contact admissibility or next canonicality is inferred.

\begin{theorem}[The actual original cut forces lower cosupport and the ENTIRE lower value cut]
\label{hir:original-stepone-whole-cut-inherited-lower-value}
\uses{hir:original-frozen-recursive-lower-domain-whole-cosupport,hir:original-frozen-recursive-lower-whole-threshold-dictionary,hir:step-one-canonical-tail,hir:step-one-mask-tower,hir:step-one-value-masked-basic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStepOneInheritedLowerCut.original_stepone_cut_inherited_lower_values}
\leanok
Let $n>0$, $M\in\mathrm{DOMAIN}(k+1)$ have maximal order, and let $T$ be
CAN for its unchanged original Step One invariant built from depth $k$.
Fix an actual nonspecial upper current cosupport reference $y$. Form the
ACTUAL original regime head $H$, tail $S$, mask $W$ and selected lower
record $J$ at $y$, using its actual level $\ell$. Take a genuine ORIGINAL-
admissible centre $C$ such that at EVERY old upper cosupport reference $x$
its original Step One value is at most the value at EVERY $c\in C$.
Then at EVERY actual trace point $c\in S^{-1}W$ over $C$, internally prove
\[
 c\in\operatorname{cosupp}J_{S|_W},\qquad
 \forall p\in\operatorname{cosupp}J_{S|_W},\quad
 R(J,S|_W,p)\le R(J,S|_W,c),
\]
where $R$ is the ORIGINAL depth-$k$ invariant. No lower cut or lower-centre
cosupport certificate is input, and this statement constructs no next TEST.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Old original canonicality supplies the true old lower DOMAIN/CAN/TEST and
its exact whole current-cosupport dictionary. It independently supplies the
true regime head predicate. Take any actual centre trace point. The original
cut at the actual old reference $y$, through the literal lexicographic first
coordinate, gives level at least $\ell$. Actual membership in the old mask
and the genuine closed original members give level at most $\ell$.
Original admissibility puts the centre point in the original upper cosupport.
The proved exact lower cosupport dictionary therefore gives its actual
lower membership. This is established before evaluating its lower invariant.

Take any actual old lower cosupport point $p$. The same dictionary puts it
in upper cosupport. Genuine canonical lower comparison expresses the
unchanged original Step One values of BOTH $p$ and the actual centre point
as $(\ell,\operatorname{coe}R(J,S|_W,-))$ for the SAME $J,S,W$, not each
point's independently re-chosen lower record. Apply the original whole cut
at $p$, substitute these TWO proved value formulas, and cancel the equal
lexicographic first coordinate. The genuine finite-to-top order embedding
then reflects the resulting inequality to the ORIGINAL lower values.
This proves the whole lower cut at every actual trace point and every actual
old lower cosupport point, without a numerical comparison certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A real positive preparation has an actual lower TEST and trace inside the whole lower maximum]
\label{hir:original-positive-stepone-inherited-lower-max-cut}
\uses{hir:original-stepone-whole-cut-inherited-lower-value,hir:original-positive-stepone-cut-actual-lower-test,hir:original-frozen-recursive-lower-domain-whole-cosupport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStepOneInheritedLowerCut.original_positive_stepone_cut_lower_maximum}
\leanok
Under those SAME original hypotheses, suppose the reference's ACTUAL level
is positive $s+1$, and take an actual blowup $\pi$ along the SAME $C$.
For the SAME old positive selected record, internally construct its original
DOMAIN$(k)$, genuine old lower CAN prefix, actual appended restricted lower
TEST, and containment of the ENTIRE actual centre trace in the ORIGINAL
whole lower maximum locus on its actual old cosupport. Empty trace centres
remain genuine steps. This does not identify the trace with the ENTIRE
maximum locus and does not assert new lower CAN.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual original cut at $y$ satisfies precisely the proved positive-cut
lower TEST theorem; it constructs this SAME old selected record's initial
domain and actual appended lower TEST. Old canonical lower data independently
supplies the genuine old lower CAN prefix. Transport its actual level
equation through the literal original regime head, tail, mask and record,
without choosing another reference. The first theorem applies to each point
of the actual centre trace: its output is true lower cosupport membership
and the inequality from EVERY old lower cosupport point to that point.
These TWO outputs are exactly the definition of the genuine whole maximum
locus for the ORIGINAL lower invariant. Thus the whole actual trace lies in
that maximum while the real appended lower TEST has already been constructed.
A proper subset of a maximum is not silently called the canonical maximum;
zero-contact admissibility, higher recursive prepared value comparison and
general global preparation termination remain independent producers.
\end{proof}
