\section{Canonical centre-ring freeness on one original analytic open}

Put $B=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$, $R=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^{d+t},0}$ and
$Q=R/J$.  The action of $Q$ on the original layers and their whole direct
sum is the actual action already descended from ambient multiplication and
proved $J$-annihilation.  It is not defined by a chosen tangent action.
The actual plane quotient isomorphism $e:Q\simeq B$ is used below only for
comparison.  This supplies an intrinsic local-algebra ingredient for the
Bierstone--Milman Hilbert--Samuel equisingularity route; a coherent sheaf
identification, original centre containment, nonlinear stalk/chart transport
and full ambient Hilbert--Samuel descent remain subsequent producers.
The higher full truncations remain modules over the split tangent ring $B$;
no $Q$-action on those truncations is asserted.

\begin{lemma}[Actual identity semilinear dictionaries for centre scalars]
\label{hir:relative-normal-canonical-centre-trace-semilinear}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-actual-centre-trace-scalar-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCanonicalCentreFreeness.originalNormalGradedPieceCentreTraceEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCanonicalCentreFreeness.originalNormalGradedPieceCentreTraceEquiv_apply,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCanonicalCentreFreeness.originalNormalGradedPieceCentreTraceEquiv_symm_apply,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCanonicalCentreFreeness.originalNormalAssociatedGradedCentreTraceEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCanonicalCentreFreeness.originalNormalAssociatedGradedCentreTraceEquiv_apply,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCanonicalCentreFreeness.originalNormalAssociatedGradedCentreTraceEquiv_symm_apply}
\leanok
For every actual original whole germ ideal $I$ and every $q$, the identity
on the existing layer $L_q$ is a genuine $e$-semilinear equivalence from
its canonical $Q$-module to its old tangent $B$-module.  The same is true
on the entire existing direct sum $G$.  Both equivalences and their
inverses fix every actual element, in particular every original power
class and every original degree inclusion.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the identity bijection on the unchanged original additive groups.
It preserves addition.  The proved actual scalar dictionary is precisely
$c x=e(c)x$ for EVERY $c\in Q$ and actual element $x$, on a layer and on
the whole sum.  This is the needed semilinear identity.  The inverse uses
the actual inverse ring isomorphism: writing $b=e(c)$ in this identity
gives $b x=e^{-1}(b)x$.  The two functions compose to the identity and
fix actual classes and inclusions because both functions are literally
the identity on those carriers.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Freeness and finite generation under the actual centre dictionary]
\label{hir:relative-normal-canonical-centre-free-flat-dictionary}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-canonical-centre-trace-semilinear}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCanonicalCentreFreeness.originalNormalGradedPieceCentre_free_flat_of_tangent_free,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCanonicalCentreFreeness.originalNormalGradedPieceCentre_finite_of_tangent_finite,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCanonicalCentreFreeness.originalNormalAssociatedGradedCentre_free_flat_of_tangent_free}
\leanok
If an actual layer or whole direct sum is free for its old tangent
$B$-action, it is free and flat for its independently constructed
canonical $Q$-action.  If an actual layer is finitely generated over $B$,
it is finitely generated over $Q$.  These are conditional algebraic
helpers; no desired canonical-action or flatness premise is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take an actual $B$-basis.  Retain the same actual vectors as a $Q$-family.
For any element, its unique finite $B$-linear expansion becomes a finite
$Q$-linear expansion by applying the true inverse isomorphism $e^{-1}$
to every coefficient and using the semilinear dictionary.  A vanishing
$Q$-linear relation maps under $e$ to a vanishing $B$-relation, so every
coefficient is zero.  Thus this is a true $Q$-basis, for the canonical
action already defined.  The same coefficient conversion changes any
finite $B$-generating family into a finite $Q$-generating family.  A free
module is flat: tensoring its direct-sum basis representation is a direct
sum of copies of the given module map, so it preserves injectivity.
No new scalar action is used in any of these arguments.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original coherent input supplies one canonical centre free open]
\label{hir:relative-normal-original-nearby-canonical-centre-free-flat-open}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-canonical-centre-free-flat-dictionary,hir:relative-normal-coefficient-algebra-nearby-associated-free-open,hir:relative-normal-coefficient-algebra-all-degree-free-open}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalCanonicalCentreFreeness.exists_original_nearby_canonical_centre_free_flat_open}
\leanok
Let $\mathcal I$ be the actual original coherent ideal.  There exists one
open $U\subset\mathbb C^d$, meeting EVERY neighbourhood of the origin,
such that for every $a\in U$ the entire original normal associated sum
of the WHOLE translated stalk ideal $I_a$ is free and flat over its actual
centre quotient ring $Q$.  EVERY actual original degree layer of this
same $I_a$ is finite, free and flat over its canonical $Q$-action, on this
SAME $U$ and simultaneously for all degrees.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original coherent-input whole associated-module producer internally
constructs the common true coefficient domain, complete relation/kernel
spreading, actual nearby homomorphisms and single denominator nonvanishing
open $U$.  It gives whole tangent $B$-freeness at every $a\in U$, with
$U$ open and meeting every origin neighbourhood.  Keep exactly this $U$.
Apply the preceding actual semilinear coefficient-conversion helper at
the WHOLE translated ideal $I_a$ to obtain canonical $Q$-free and flat
whole sum.  The proved genuine homogeneous layer summand and finite
homogeneous symbol surjection give finite free $B$-layers internally from
that same whole free sum.  Apply the same actual layer dictionary to each
$q$ to obtain finite free and flat canonical $Q$-layers.  The choices
of open and denominator preceded $q$, so one and the same $U$ works for
ALL actual original degrees.  No basis, coefficient presentation, whole
kernel, free/flat statement or centre-action equality is supplied by the
original coherent input caller.
\end{proof}

The open need not contain the origin and is not asserted dense on every
arbitrary component.  The theorem concerns the actual original graded
modules and their canonical local centre quotient action.  It supplies
neither a bundled sheaf result nor an assertion that a centre is contained
in the original zero space, and makes no ambient mixed-degree Hilbert--Samuel
or blowing-up descent conclusion.
