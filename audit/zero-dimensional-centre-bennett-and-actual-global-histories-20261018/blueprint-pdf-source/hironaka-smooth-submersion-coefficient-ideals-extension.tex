\section{Entire coefficient ideals under actual smooth pullback}

The map, original coherent ideal, carrier and boundary are the actual inputs.
All products, powers and finite weighted sums below are the original entire
germ ideals. No pullback algebra or coefficient comparison certificate is an
input. The coefficient equality does not assert blowup base change or complete
canonical smooth functoriality.

\begin{lemma}\label{hir:crossdim-entire-product-pullback}
For an actual holomorphic map $f:Y^m\to X^n$ and original coherent ideals
$I,J$, the entire ideals satisfy $f^*(IJ)=(f^*I)(f^*J)$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionCoefficientIdeals.pullback_mul}
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A generator of the product is a product of original ideal members. Pull a
finite holomorphic expression for a product-ideal member back along the actual
map: its coefficients remain holomorphic, and each original product pulls
back to the product of its two actual pulled members. The resulting entire
finite span gives the first inclusion. For the reverse inclusion, write each
actual pulled member as its genuine finite holomorphic combination of original
pulled ideal members. Multiply both sums and distribute. Each term is a
holomorphic coefficient times the pullback of a product of actual members of
$I$ and $J$. Coherence makes those original members holomorphic and their
product a genuine product-ideal member. Transitivity of the original entire
finite spans gives the reverse inclusion. Apply membership extensionality.
The construction uses neither dimension equality nor a local inverse.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{hir:crossdim-entire-unit-power-pullback}
The actual unit ideal pulls back to the actual unit ideal. For every original
coherent ideal $I$ and every $q\ge0$, $f^*(I^q)=(f^*I)^q$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionCoefficientIdeals.pullback_unitIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionCoefficientIdeals.pullback_idealPow}
\leanok
\uses{hir:crossdim-entire-product-pullback}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every pulled unit-ideal member is holomorphic, so lies in the genuine target
unit ideal. Conversely every target holomorphic germ is itself its coefficient
times the pullback of the original constant one; hence it belongs to the
entire pulled unit ideal. For powers induct on $q$. The genuine zero power
is precisely this unit ideal. The genuine successor power is the product of
the previous power with the original ideal, and both are coherent. Apply the
actual product equality and replace the previous power using induction.
This covers the actual zero and unit ideals and literal zeroth powers.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{hir:crossdim-entire-finite-sum-pullback}
For any actual finite family $I_i$ of coherent original ideals,
$f^*(\sum_{i<r}I_i)=\sum_{i<r}f^*I_i$, as entire germ ideals.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionCoefficientIdeals.pullback_finSumIdeal}
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A member of the original finite sum is a genuine finite holomorphic
combination of members of the actual summands. Pull this whole expression
back; every pulled summand member is an original generator of its entire
pulled summand and hence of the target finite sum. Span transitivity gives
the first inclusion. Conversely a member of a pulled summand is a genuine
finite holomorphic combination of pullbacks of its original members. Each
original member is a member of the original full finite sum by its defining
span and holomorphicity from coherence. Thus this whole pulled summand is
contained in the pullback of that finite sum. Span transitivity gives the
opposite inclusion. With no summands the same empty-span argument applies.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}\label{hir:crossdim-literal-marked-pullback}
The actual marked pullback of $(N,E,I,d)$ along $f:Y^m\to X^n$ is the
literal marked ideal $(f^{-1}N,(f^{-1}E_i)_i,f^*I,d)$ on $Y$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionCoefficientIdeals.markedPullback}
\leanok
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Use the original inverse image of the carrier, the original ordered boundary
list mapped by inverse image, the entire original germ-ideal pullback and the
same original mark. These are actual data of the target marked-ideal type,
and their four projections are exactly the displayed data. This definition
does not supply or presume centre admissibility or canonical tower equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:submersion-entire-coefficient-ideal-equality}
For an original marked ideal with coherent $I$ and a genuine holomorphic
submersion $f:Y^m\to X^n$, the actual coefficient marked ideal commutes with
this literal marked pullback, including all original carriers, ordered
boundary data, marks and entire ideal members.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionCoefficientIdeals.coeffMarked_pullback_eq}
\leanok
\uses{hir:crossdim-literal-marked-pullback,hir:crossdim-entire-unit-power-pullback,hir:crossdim-entire-finite-sum-pullback,hir:submersion-entire-iterated-derivative-equality,mc:coefficient-ideal}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original coefficient ideal is the finite marked sum of actual iterated
derivative ideals $\mathcal D^i I$, with original marks $d-i$ and the original
weighted exponents obtained by multiplying the other marks. The genuine
submersion already gives equality of ENTIRE pulled iterated derivative ideals.
Each original iterated derivative ideal is coherent. Apply the proved entire
power equality to its actual weighted power and the entire finite-sum equality
to the original finite weighted family. Replace each actual iterated pullback
using the derivative equality. The result is precisely the original coefficient
ideal of the literal target marked data. Both actual constructions retain the
inverse-image carrier and ordered boundary and have the same numerical mark,
because the original source marks are unchanged. Extensionality of the actual
marked records gives the stated entire marked-ideal equality. No threshold,
coefficient-space or selected generator equality is used as a hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:submersion-actual-coefficient-cosupport-preimage}
For an original ambient marked ideal $N=X$ with coherent $I$, the cosupport
of its actual pulled coefficient marked ideal is exactly the inverse image of
its original coefficient cosupport under the genuine holomorphic submersion.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionCoefficientIdeals.coefficient_cosupport_pullback_eq}
\leanok
\uses{hir:submersion-entire-coefficient-ideal-equality,hir:submersion-cosupport,mc:coefficient-ideal}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original finite weighted sum of derivative powers is coherent, by the
actual coherent finite-sum and power constructors. The previous result identifies
the entire target coefficient ideal with the genuine pullback of this same
original coherent ideal and identifies its mark and full ambient carrier.
The actual original-point section of the genuine submersion gives equality of
all entire germ order thresholds for this original coefficient ideal, so its
marked cosupport is exactly the real inverse image. Substitute the true
coefficient-ideal equality. The empty weighted family and literal zero mark
retain the original definitions throughout; no positivity assumption is added.
\end{proof}
