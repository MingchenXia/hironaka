\begin{lemma}[Strict transforms of actual closed component partitions]
\label{hir:member-tail-actual-clopen-strict}
\uses{hir:member-lower-extension-actual-remaining-germ,hir:dimtwo-unselected-smooth-components,bcalc:member-intersection-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaStepOneMemberTailExtension.strictAlong_eq_inter_preimage_of_closed_partition,Hironaka.HironakaStepOneMemberTailExtension.eventually_mem_strictAlong_iff_of_closed_partition}
\leanok
Let a closed set H be the disjoint union of closed A and B. Along any genuine
blowup tower T, their actual strict transforms satisfy
\[
 A_T=H_T\cap T^{-1}A,
\]
and at every point of $A_T$ its actual local germ equals that of $H_T$. In
particular this holds for the genuine remaining and selected smooth components
of the original member intersection.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For one blowup, preimages commute with unions and removing the centre does too;
closure of a finite union is the union of the closures. Induction proves
$H_T=A_T\cup B_T$. Each strict transform of an initially closed set is closed
and lies over that set, by continuity of the actual projection and the closure
in the strict-transform definition. Since A and B are disjoint, these two
preimage containments make $A_T$ and $B_T$ disjoint. The first inclusion of the
displayed identity follows from monotonicity and the preimage containment.
Conversely, a point of $H_T$ over A belongs to $A_T$ or $B_T$; the latter case would
put its image in the disjoint set B. The complement of the closed $B_T$ is a
neighbourhood of every $A_T$ point, and on it $H_T$ and $A_T$ are equal. This is the
asserted genuine local-germ equality, without a chosen component chart.
\end{proof}
