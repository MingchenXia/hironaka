\begin{lemma}[Actual stopped terminal identifications restrict to their true matching opens]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-terminal-open-biholomorphism}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-prefix-terminal-biholomorphism,hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-prefix-open-terminal-square}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedAtlasTransition.stoppedTerminalOpenBiholomorphism,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedAtlasTransition.stoppedTerminalOpenBiholomorphism_val,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedAtlasTransition.stoppedTerminalOpenBiholomorphism_flattening}
\leanok
For a genuine finite history, an original base open and a stage at least
the literal history length, its actual stopped terminal biholomorphism
restricts to a genuine biholomorphism between the ENTIRE stage preimage
of that base open and its ENTIRE original terminal preimage. Its point
map is exactly the already constructed stopped terminal identification.
For any genuine open-prefix flattening, composing with this restricted
map is exactly the stopped restricted-history identification.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine stopped projection square identifies the stage projection
with the terminal projection composed with the actual terminal
biholomorphism. Taking inverse images of the original open gives the
ENTIRE matching-open equality. Restrict the actual biholomorphism to
those two opens; its genuine inverse laws and both holomorphic maps
restrict as well. Its underlying terminal point formula is unchanged.
The preceding open-prefix terminal-square theorem gives equality of
the underlying terminal points after any genuine flattening. Both
values lie in that SAME original terminal open, so subtype equality
proves the asserted entire restricted-map identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same actual padded terminal comparison gives the chosen restricted-stage square]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-restricted-comparison-square}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-terminal-open-biholomorphism,hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-comparison-terminal-square,hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-prefix-open-terminal-natural}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedAtlasTransition.stopped_restricted_comparison_square}
\leanok
Consider genuine finite histories and genuine base opens on both sides.
Let their actual whole restricted histories, with actual identity
padding, have a genuine comparison with terminal map $e$. At a stage
at least BOTH literal original lengths, suppose an actual full
comparison of their restricted uniform prefixes has already been
chosen. The two genuine matching-open stopped terminal maps then
intertwine THIS chosen stage map with THIS same terminal map $e$
on EVERY actual point. No desired square or arbitrary projection-map
uniqueness is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Internally construct the genuine uniform-prefix open flattening on
each side. Conjugate the given restricted-stage map by the first
flattening and the inverse of the second. Composing their genuine
whole-history isomorphisms gives an actual comparison of the two
uniform prefixes of the whole restricted histories, over the SAME
original base map. Open restriction preserves both literal lengths,
so both prefixes are stopped. Apply the actual chosen stopped-prefix
terminal-square theorem to this very comparison and the given genuine
padded terminal comparison.

The first lemma identifies each stopped restricted-prefix map with
the corresponding matching-open terminal map composed with its
actual flattening. Evaluate the obtained square at the inverse of
the source flattening applied to an arbitrary original restricted-stage
point. The source inverse law makes its source point the given point;
the target inverse law makes the target flattening of its conjugated
image precisely the chosen restricted-stage image. Substitution gives
exactly the asserted ENTIRE square, for the already chosen maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual uniform-stage transitions preserve the same retained terminal atlas]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-atlas-transition-square}

\leanok

at least BOTH full local-history lengths. The actual uniform-stage
transition sends stopped terminal points by exactly the SAME original
terminal transition, throughout their ENTIRE genuine overlap. The
stopped source point lies in the true original terminal overlap by its
actual projection, not by an assumed domain equality. Only original
positive-dimensional smooth pure curve, ordered SNC and density data
internally give this square at every stage equal to the sum of the two
actual local lengths plus any natural number. No atlas, history,
terminal-square or compatibility certificate is supplied to that
original-only consumer.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write each actual full local history as its retained prefix followed
by its retained tail. On the actual intersection base open, genuine
concatenation restriction gives a full isomorphism from the restriction
of that WHOLE local history to the concatenation of its two actual
restrictions. Its terminal map is the genuine equal-open map, retaining
all underlying terminal points. Append on each side precisely the
identity padding already present in the actual terminal atlas
comparison. Conjugating that SAME comparison by these two genuine
append-restriction maps supplies a true padded comparison between the
two canonical whole restricted histories. Its terminal map is precisely
the SAME terminal atlas map conjugated by identity-on-points equal-open
maps; no new comparison is chosen.

The actual uniform-stage atlas already supplies a full comparison on
the two restricted uniform prefixes: its reflexive tail and zero cut
remove none of their actual steps. Apply the preceding theorem to this
SAME chosen comparison and the just constructed genuine padded comparison.
For an actual stage overlap point, use its actual equal-open map into
the common-base endpoint open. The stopped projection square puts its
stopped terminal point in the original terminal overlap. All genuine
append and overlap equal-open maps retain the same underlying terminal
point. Taking that point in the preceding ENTIRE square therefore gives
exactly the stated original terminal transition applied to the stopped
point. No original projection injectivity or exceptional-point removal
is used.

Finally instantiate the internally constructed original retained atlas
and the stage given by the sum of its two actual finite local lengths
plus the additional natural number. Both required bounds hold for these
actual lengths. All original opens, histories, comparisons and domain
memberships have thus been internally supplied. Gluing these compatible
maps over the union of stopped opens, and any compact uniform finite
stopping theorem, remain subsequent conclusions.
\end{proof}
