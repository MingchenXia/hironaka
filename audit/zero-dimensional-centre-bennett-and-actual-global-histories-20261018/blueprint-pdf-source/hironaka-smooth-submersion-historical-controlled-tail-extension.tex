\section{Actual open restrictions and the earlier formed companion's controlled tail}
The primitive inputs are genuine smooth submersions, actual original blowup towers, an original marked datum in its invariant domain, and the original target canonical segment. Target canonicality is a property of the original target history; source canonicality is not assumed. Every retained stage, including an empty centre, is preserved. The construction transports the literal earlier formed companion, not a fresh companion at the endpoint. This is the smooth pullback of the multiple test blow-ups of \cite{wlodarczyk05-hironaka}, Section 2.4, applied after the Step 2a companion formation; the statements retain all indexed boundary fields and do not infer higher invariant functoriality.

\begin{lemma}[A genuine projection square identifies the actual chosen lift]
\label{hir:historical-control-lift}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionHistoricalControlledTail.IsActualTowerPullback.step_of_actual_square}
\uses{hir:submersion-blowup-lift-actual-lift,hir:submersion-cartesian-tower-actual-history}
\leanok
Let $a:Y\to X$ be a genuine holomorphic submersion and let $\rho:Y'\to Y$ and $\pi:X'\to X$ be actual blowups along $a^{-1}C$ and $C$. Any actual holomorphic map $b:Y'\to X'$ satisfying $\pi b=a\rho$ equals the internally produced geometric lift. Consequently a genuine simultaneous history followed by these two actual blowups is again the same simultaneous history with endpoint $b$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The entire pullback of the true ideal of $C$ under $a\rho$ is the actual source exceptional ideal, hence invertible. The genuine universal property of the actual target blowup therefore supplies a unique holomorphic map over $a\rho$. Both the internally constructed lift and $b$ satisfy that universal property, so they agree as actual maps. Substitute this equality in the simultaneous history's one-step constructor. Equality of a source centre with its literal inverse image suffices to make this substitution; no source-lift identity or desired history property is input.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

By the preceding lift-uniqueness lemma this actual restricted map is the genuinely produced one-step lift, so the simultaneous-history constructor applies. This proves the claim by induction. The existing genuine simultaneous-history length and whole holomorphic universal-property producers now apply to precisely these literal restricted towers. \end{proof}
