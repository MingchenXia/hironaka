\section{Whole original relative geometry under genuine smooth submersions}

\begin{theorem}[Every original simultaneous coordinate family]
\label{hir:submersion-relative-coordinate-families}
\uses{hir:submersion-nc-full-lifted-coordinates}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRelativeGeometry.isCoordinateFamilyAt_preimage_submersion}
For a genuine holomorphic submersion $f:Y^m\to X^n$ and any original
coordinate family at $f(y)$, there is an internally produced coordinate
embedding $\rho:\{1,\ldots,n\}\hookrightarrow\{1,\ldots,m\}$ such that
every entire preimage member is the coordinate subspace cut out by the
image under $\rho$ of precisely its original cutting-coordinate set.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the genuine lifted product-coordinate construction to the actual
centered original chart. It supplies a centered chart at $y$, defined on
a genuine ambient open neighborhood, whose coordinates indexed by
$\rho$ are exactly all original coordinates composed with $f$.
Every point of its domain maps into the original chart domain. Substitute
this complete dictionary into each original member equation. A vector
vanishes on the mapped finite index set exactly when its original
coordinates vanish on the original index set, since $\rho$ is injective.
Thus the same internally constructed chart simultaneously gives all
original preimage members; no preimage-chart certificate is an input.
\end{proof}
\leanok

\begin{theorem}[Original regular points and closed submanifolds]
\label{hir:submersion-relative-regular-geometry}
\uses{hir:submersion-relative-coordinate-families}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRelativeGeometry.regularAtDim_preimage_submersion,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRelativeGeometry.isClosedSubmanifold_preimage_submersion}
If the original set $N$ is regular of dimension $j$ at $f(y)$,
then $f^{-1}N$ is regular of dimension $j+m-n$ at $y$.
Consequently a closed original submanifold has a closed submanifold
preimage under a genuine global holomorphic submersion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original regular chart cuts out $N$ by exactly the last $n-j$
coordinates. The preceding simultaneous coordinate dictionary cuts out
its preimage by the injective image of those same $n-j$ coordinates.
An actual finite coordinate permutation puts precisely those cutting
coordinates last. Its induced complex linear isometry is a global
holomorphic equivalence, with holomorphic inverse; compose it with the
actual lifted chart. This is a true regular flattening of dimension
$m-(n-j)=j+(m-n)$. The actual submersion internally implies $n\le m$,
and the original regularity implies $j\le n$, so the equality and dimension
bound hold also when either dimension is zero. For the global statement,
continuity gives closedness and apply this actual regular flattening
at every preimage point to its original regular dimension.
\end{proof}
\leanok

\begin{theorem}[Entire original normal crossings and transversality]
\label{hir:submersion-relative-nc-transversality}
\uses{hir:submersion-relative-coordinate-families}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRelativeGeometry.hasNormalCrossings_preimage_submersion,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRelativeGeometry.isTransverse_preimage_submersion}
The whole preimage of an original centre with normal crossings with
the original ordered boundary again has normal crossings with the
entire pulled boundary. The entire pulled boundary is transverse
to the whole preimage of an original transverse underlying set.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At each actual preimage point apply the preceding family construction
to the original simultaneous family consisting of the underlying set
or centre and every indexed original boundary member. Identify the
pulled list index with the same original index; list length and order
are unchanged. Images under the single injective coordinate map retain
the cardinality one of each actual through-member cutting set.
For transversality, two mapped index sets intersect only when their
original index sets intersect, by injectivity. Hence every original
disjointness relation with the underlying cutting set is retained.
These are exactly the original definitions on the entire preimage;
empty and repeated historical list entries remain literal.
\end{proof}
\leanok

\begin{theorem}[Actual original nonvanishing along the whole preimage]
\label{hir:submersion-relative-nonzero}
\uses{hir:submersion-relative-coordinate-families,hir:submersion-local-section}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRelativeGeometry.isNonzeroAlongAt_preimage_submersion}
A coherent original ideal which is nonzero along the original underlying
set at $f(y)$ has an actual nonzero germ along the entire preimage at $y$.
The original underlying set is arbitrary.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the actual original ideal germ nonzero at points of the original
set arbitrarily close to $f(y)$. Its composition with the genuine smooth
map belongs to the whole original pulled ideal. The genuine local
holomorphic section through $y$ gives exactly
$f_*(\mathcal N_y)=\mathcal N_{f(y)}$: continuity gives one inclusion
and composition with the section gives the other. Transport the original
frequent nonzero predicate through this equality. It says precisely that
the actual composed germ is nonzero at points of the entire original
preimage set arbitrarily close to $y$. No caller witness on the preimage
is used and no analyticity of that set is required.
\end{proof}
\leanok

\begin{theorem}[Whole original marked geometry and invariant domain]
\label{hir:submersion-relative-invariant-domain}
\uses{hir:submersion-relative-regular-geometry,hir:submersion-relative-nc-transversality,hir:submersion-relative-nonzero,hir:submersion-nc-entire-snc-preimage}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRelativeGeometry.isWellFormed_markedPullback_submersion,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRelativeGeometry.isInvDomain_markedPullback_submersion}
The entire actual marked preimage of an original well-formed marked
ideal is well formed. An original invariant domain of underlying
dimension at most $k$ gives an actual preimage invariant domain of
underlying dimension at most $k+m-n$, with the same original marking.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual marked pullback retains the entire preimage underlying set,
whole ideal pullback, original ordered preimage boundary and original
mark. The preceding producers give closed submanifold geometry,
transversality and nonvanishing. Genuine holomorphic pullback gives
coherence and the genuine whole SNC producer gives the entire boundary
condition. At each original point select its actual regular dimension
$j\le k$ from the original domain. The preceding regular producer gives
the preimage dimension $j+(m-n)\le k+(m-n)$. The original positive mark
is unchanged. These are all fields of the true original invariant-domain
definition; no preimage well-formedness, dimension or nonvanishing
certificate is supplied. Higher invariant equality, genuine blowup
base change and complete canonical smooth functoriality remain open.
\end{proof}
\leanok
