\section{Actual coefficient cosupport and finite reduced strict generators}

This chapter connects the actual original coefficient-centre producer to
whole division by the canonical reduced basis. Its strict ideal is the
existing genuine formal strict transform, generated by all nonzero original
members normalized at their actual maximal centre orders. This is the
finite strict-generation component of the presentation argument in
\cite[Section 7, p.~268; Theorem 9.4, pp.~277--278]{bierstone-milman97-invent-math}.
Full semicoherent-presentation construction, transformed derivative/Jacobian
properties, analytic strict-transform equality and Bennett are not asserted.

\begin{lemma}[Actual centre orders of the same canonical reduced members]
\label{hir:actual-reduced-vertex-centre-orders}
\uses{hir:actual-reduced-formal-vertex-members,hir:actual-formal-center-order-from-support}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientReducedStrictGenerators.actualFormalCenterOrder_reduced_vertex,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientReducedStrictGenerators.actualNormalizedReducedFormalVertex}
\leanok
Let $G_a$ be the actual canonical reduced vertex members of $I$ and let
$P_A$ be a genuine selected-coordinate centre ideal. If $G_a\in
P_A^{w_A(a)}$, its actual maximal centre order is exactly $w_A(a)$.
For every real chart $e\in A$ and every direction $b$, actual exceptional
division therefore constructs the same normalized member $G'_a$ with
$X_e^{w_A(a)}G'_a=\sigma_b(G_a)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The genuine coefficient of $G_a$ at $a$ is one, giving the upper bound
for its actual least nonzero coefficient weight. The proved full
coordinate-power characterization and the assumed actual membership give
the lower bound. Actual blowup divisibility constructs the quotient and
formal-domain cancellation proves its uniqueness. No arbitrary original
monic representative is identified with this reduced member.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The entire genuine formal strict ideal from reduced generators]
\label{hir:actual-reduced-basis-entire-formal-strict-generation}
\uses{hir:actual-reduced-vertex-centre-orders,hir:actual-reduced-basis-entire-filtered-division,hir:actual-formal-strict-ideal-normalized-member-identification}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientReducedStrictGenerators.actualNormalizedFormalMember_mem_reduced_vertex_span,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientReducedStrictGenerators.actualFormalStrictTransform_eq_reduced_vertex_span}
\leanok
With actual reduced-centre compatibility as above, the entire genuine formal
strict transform in every chart is $(G'_a:a\text{ an actual vertex})$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine strict ideal is already identified with the ideal of all
normalized original members of all actual centre powers. For an actual
$F\in I\cap P_A^q$, true reduced-basis division gives
$F=\sum_a Q_aG_a$ with $Q_a\in P_A^{q-w_A(a)}$. Normalize every actual
quotient at that genuine power. Applying the actual chart homomorphism to
the whole division identity, and factoring the true exceptional powers,
gives
\[
 [F]_q=\sum_a X_e^{w_A(a)-q}[Q_a]_{q-w_A(a)}G'_a.
\]
The equality follows by multiplying both sides by $X_e^q$ and using
formal-domain cancellation; the natural truncated-exponent identity
$(q-w)+w=q+(w-q)$ handles both inequalities. Thus all normalized original
members lie in the finite reduced span. Conversely each $G_a$ is an actual
original member with its proved centre power, so its normalization belongs
to the genuine strict ideal. This proves both whole ideal inclusions,
including the zero ideal and weight-zero cases.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original coefficient cosupport internally supplies finite strict generators]
\label{hir:actual-original-coefficient-cosupport-reduced-strict-generators}
\uses{hir:actual-coefficient-presentation-cosupport-centre-power,hir:actual-reduced-basis-entire-formal-strict-generation}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientReducedStrictGenerators.actualCoefficientCentreVariables,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientReducedStrictGenerators.actualCoefficientCentreVertexWeight,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientReducedStrictGenerators.ActualReducedCoefficientPresentation,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientReducedStrictGenerators.actualReducedCoefficientPresentation_centre_powers,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientReducedStrictGenerators.exists_actual_reduced_strict_generators_of_original_coefficients}
\leanok
Write the actual coordinates as $(u,y,z)$ and let $C=\{y=0\}$ in the
presentation carrier. For every actual reduced member $G_a$ and every low
normal multiindex $\gamma$ with $|\gamma|<w_A(a)$, assume genuine original
holomorphic coefficient representatives $c_{a\gamma}$ in actual original
coherent ideals $\mathfrak a_{a\gamma}$, with the explicit literal formal
coefficient identity for $G_a$. Assume the actual smooth coordinate centre
lies in $\operatorname{cosupp}(\mathfrak a_{a\gamma},w_A(a)-|\gamma|)$.
Then in every genuine chart of the actual ambient centre $(y,z)$ there is
an internally constructed family $G'_a$ generating the ENTIRE genuine formal
strict ideal and satisfying all its literal exceptional-product identities.
The actual centre powers of the reduced members are not additional inputs.
Here the coefficient ideals are coherent on the whole coordinate model,
and the cosupport hypothesis concerns the whole coordinate plane. Producing
such data from arbitrary local presentation germs, without assuming global
extendability, remains a separate local-domain construction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each vertex the actual original coefficient-centre theorem applies to
these real holomorphic representatives, actual coherent ideals, genuine
cosupport containment and literal coefficient identities. It produces
$G_a\in(y,z)^{w_A(a)}$ internally by Hadamard, actual functional and germ
powers, genuine Taylor expansion and the whole coefficient kernel.
The preceding theorem now constructs the actual normalized reduced members
and proves both whole strict-ideal inclusions. Its actual product identities
are the original chart identities. The coefficient data contain no quotient,
centre-power, strict-generation or Hilbert--Samuel certificate. Existence of
all source presentation data and subsequent transformed stability remain
separate goals.
\end{proof}
