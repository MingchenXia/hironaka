\begin{lemma}[A run reaching the base of a concatenated tail]
\label{hir:original-crossing-run-base-regime}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCrossingLowerHistory.regime_at_base_of_reaching_run,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCrossingLowerHistory.split_append_above_tail}
\leanok
\uses{hir:tower-split}
For an actual regime $P$, actual histories $H,S$ and final point $y$, if
$P(HS,y)$ holds and $|S|\leq\operatorname{run}_P(HS,y)$, then
$P(H,S.\operatorname{proj}(y))$ holds.  Moreover, for every $r\geq|S|$, the
entire actual split of $HS$ at $r$ has stage and head those of the split of
$H$ at $r-|S|$, and tail $\operatorname{tail}(H,r-|S|)S$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For regime membership induct on $S$.  The empty tail uses current membership.
At a nonempty last step, a run reaching the positive length of $S$ cannot
have stopped at that step.  The actual predecessor therefore satisfies $P$,
and the run recursion gives a predecessor run reaching the predecessor
tail length.  Induction gives the original base membership.  For the full
split formula, remove the last actual steps of $S$ recursively.  Once the
tail is exhausted, the remaining $r-|S|$ steps are removed from $H$.
The actual split constructors retain precisely the stated original head
and append $S$ to its actual tail.  This applies also to saturated split
indices beyond the whole length.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original crossing run and its whole selected splits]
\label{hir:original-interior-crossing-selected-splits}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCrossingLowerHistory.actual_crossing_selected_splits}
\leanok
\uses{hir:original-crossing-run-base-regime,hir:original-interior-empty-regime-run,hir:original-interior-empty-step-one-dictionary}
Let $M$ be originally well formed, $H$ an actual test tower of $\overline M$,
$S$ any actual tail, and $y$ an actual nonspecial final point of $HS$.
Put $\ell=\operatorname{level}(M,HS,y)$ and
$r=\operatorname{run}_{M,\ell}(HS,y)$, and assume $|S|\leq r$.
The actual current-level run in $H\epsilon S$ is $r+1$.  Write
$a=r-|S|$, $D=\operatorname{stage}(H,a)$, $K=\operatorname{head}(H,a)$ and
$B=\operatorname{tail}(H,a)$.  The two actual selected full split records
are precisely $(D,K,BS)$ and $(D,K,B\epsilon S)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual nonspecial point satisfies the regime of its own actual level.
The first lemma therefore supplies membership at the original $H$ point.
The exact original run formula, with $r\geq|S|$, gives $r+1$ after insertion;
the original level dictionary also identifies the new actual current level.
The preceding whole split formula gives the old split.  Apply it to the new
prefix $H\epsilon$ at $r+1$.  Because $r\geq|S|$, its remaining split count
is $a+1$.  The last step of this prefix is the identity empty blowup, so
its split at $a+1$ has stage/head $(D,K)$ and tail $B\epsilon$ by the actual
split recursion.  Appending $S$ gives precisely the new whole split record.
This includes level zero and an empty $S$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual restriction of an interior identity empty step]
\label{hir:original-crossing-restricted-empty-history}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCrossingLowerHistory.restrict_identity_empty_step,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCrossingLowerHistory.actual_restricted_empty_history}
\leanok
\uses{hir:original-interior-empty-history,bcalc:tower-restrict}
For any actual open $W$ and histories $B,S$, the restriction of $B\epsilon S$
to $W$ is genuinely isomorphic over the identity of $W$ to the restriction
of $B$ followed by the genuine identity empty blowup and the restriction of
$S$ to the actual open preimage of $W$ under $B$.  The final identification
is the natural equality of the actual open preimages, with the same points.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The empty centre restricts to the empty centre, the identity projection
restricts to the identity on the same actual open preimage, and the actual
restricted blowup has the true empty blowup universal property.  Thus the
whole restricted prefix is exactly the restricted $B$ followed by that
identity empty step.  The genuine restriction-of-concatenation dictionary
then gives the asserted whole tower isomorphism and its natural final
open identification.  No restricted-tail isomorphism is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual crossing lower assignment and restricted history]
\label{hir:original-interior-crossing-lower-history}

restricted lower tail is the genuine interior identity empty insertion
in the actual restricted old tail, as in the preceding restriction diagram.

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two actual tails are $BS$ and $B\epsilon S$, whose composite projections
are equal by the actual identity-step projection dictionary.  Their images

the same original head $K$.  The actual lower mask and lowerMarkedM depend
on the same $M,\ell,K,p$, so their equality follows by substitution, also
for the original chosen contact direction at level zero.  Apply the actual
restriction producer to this literal common lower open.  It supplies the
new whole restricted tail diagram internally.
\end{proof}
