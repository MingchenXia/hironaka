\begin{theorem}[Both actual curve centres correspond through genuine stage isomorphisms]
\label{hir:sharp-original-curve-defects-stage-isomorphism}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-curve-first-defect-original-second-cut,hir:sharp-original-curve-actual-tauD-exact-support,mc:tower-isomorphism-transform,mc:tower-isomorphism-basic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStageComparison.original_curve_first_defect_isTowerIso,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStageComparison.original_curve_actual_difference_isTowerIso}
\leanok

hypotheses. Let $T$ be ANY original product TEST, and let $T'$ be a tower
with an actual stagewise tower isomorphism over the identity of the SAME
base, whose final genuine biholomorphism is $\chi:Y'\to Y$. Then the true
original TEST of $T'$ is supplied internally, and
\[
 Z_{T'}=\chi^{-1}(Z_T),\qquad
 \Delta_{T'}=\chi^{-1}(\Delta_T).
\]
No centre, residual, current-record or member-germ comparison is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original product is coherent and its actual identity pullback is the
same complete marked record. The genuine tower isomorphism transports
TEST histories, hence internally makes $T'$ an original TEST. The
actual original curve domain supplies well-formedness and nonzero-along
at every prefix. The preceding chapter identifies each FIRST defect
with the second nonmonomial cut of the SAME original current record.
Actual tower-record transport and genuine biholomorphic cut locality
therefore give the first equality at every ambient point.

For the second equality, first use the independent literal exceptional
difference formula on both actual TEST histories. The coherent unit
geometric template is its own identity pullback. Transform transport
identifies its ENTIRE current record through $\chi$, hence identifies its
carrier and every ordered exceptional member by true preimage. Actual
tower-isomorphism transport separately identifies the strict transform
of EVERY original member. Preimages preserve the displayed unions and
intersections in the literal difference formula. This proves the second
equality without any inference from FIRST $Z$, product order or an
indexed substitute for distinct germ classes.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual corresponding blowups have a unique holomorphic lift]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-stage-matched-blowup-unique-lift}
\uses{mc:tower-isomorphism-basic,hir:geo-transform-basic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStageComparison.exists_unique_stage_blowup_comparison}
\leanok
Let $\chi:Y'\to Y$ be a genuine biholomorphism, let $C\subset Y$,
and let $\pi':Z'\to Y'$ and $\pi:Z\to Y$ be actual blowups of
$\chi^{-1}(C)$ and $C$. Internally construct a genuine biholomorphism
$e:Z'\to Z$ with
\[
                  \pi\circ e=\chi\circ\pi'.
\]
EVERY holomorphic $k:Z'\to Z$ satisfying this actual square equals $e$.
This includes an empty centre as a genuine length-one blowup.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose $\pi$ with $\chi^{-1}$. Actual base-biholomorphism transport
makes this an actual blowup of the SAME centre $\chi^{-1}(C)$ as
$\pi'$. The actual blowup universal property supplies a unique
holomorphic map $e$ from $Z'$ to $Z$ over $Y'$. Apply it in the reverse
direction to obtain a holomorphic map $f$ over $Y'$. Their composites
commute with the real projections, so uniqueness in the two universal
properties makes both composites the identity. Thus these maps are a
genuine biholomorphism with both inverse laws. Applying $\chi$ to the
original square gives the stated square over $Y$.

If $k$ satisfies that square, composing with $\chi^{-1}$ gives the SAME
square over $Y'$ as for $e$. Uniqueness in the actual universal property
therefore gives $k=e$. No arbitrary terminal-map uniqueness over a
composite projection is inferred.
\end{proof}
