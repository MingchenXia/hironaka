\section{The intrinsic geometric point-chart Hilbert--Samuel comparison}

\begin{theorem}[The unchanged whole vanishing origin ideal has its actual vanishing germ meaning]
\label{hir:actual-whole-vanishing-origin-ideal-meaning}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointGeometricBennett.mk_mem_original_vanishing_origin_ideal_iff,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointGeometricBennett.originalVanishingOriginIdeal_closure}
\leanok
For every actual subset $A$ of the original model and every holomorphic
representative $f$ near zero, its original germ belongs to the unchanged
whole vanishing origin ideal exactly when $f$ vanishes on $A$ near zero.
The whole original vanishing origin ideal is unchanged on replacing $A$
by its actual closure. No coherence or global analytic extension is a premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every defining generator has a genuine holomorphic representative
vanishing on $A$ near zero. The zero germ has such a representative;
addition and multiplication by arbitrary original holomorphic germs
preserve that property. Induction on the unchanged whole ideal span
therefore gives such a representative for every member. Equality with
the given original representative is equality near zero, proving the
forward implication. For the reverse implication the same representative
is literally one of the defining original vanishing generators.
Local holomorphicity supplies continuity near zero, so actual vanishing
on $A$ extends to its closure near zero. The reverse inclusion follows
from $A\subseteq\overline A$, proving the entire closure ideal equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original intrinsic model-point function is the unchanged whole origin-ideal function]
\label{hir:actual-model-zero-whole-stalk-hs-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointGeometricBennett.wholeChartStalkIdeal_model_zero,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointGeometricBennett.hilbertSamuelAt_model_zero}
\leanok
For every original germ ideal sheaf on the actual model, its entire chart
stalk ideal at zero is the same unchanged whole origin ideal. Thus the
existing intrinsic point Hilbert--Samuel function at zero equals the
whole origin-ideal quotient function at every order.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual model chart is the identity and the original point is zero.
Consequently the actual translated chart germ of each original member
is precisely its original whole germ. Original model differentiability
and manifold differentiability agree. Each defining generator therefore
belongs to the other defining generator set, with its genuine membership
preserved. Their entire spans coincide. Apply the existing definition
of the intrinsic point function to these identical actual ideals.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual geometric strict vanishing contains the whole normalized ideal in dimension at least two]
\label{hir:actual-point-geometric-strict-normalized-inclusion}
\uses{hir:actual-whole-vanishing-origin-ideal-meaning,hir:actual-point-quotient-vanishing-exceptional-complement-closure,hir:actual-point-blowup-hyperplane-exceptional}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointGeometricBennett.actual_point_normalized_ideal_le_geometric_strict_vanishing}
\leanok
For $n\ge2$ and the actual point-chart map $\pi$, the whole analytic
normalized member ideal of the original vanishing origin ideal of $A$
is contained in the unchanged vanishing origin ideal of the library's
actual geometric strict transform of $A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take an actual defining normalized quotient $H$ with original member $g$
and true whole exceptional product. The first theorem gives genuine
vanishing of a representative of $g$ on $A$ near zero. The actual
quotient-closure theorem makes $H$ vanish on
$\overline{\pi^{-1}A\setminus\{z_e=0\}}$ near zero.
The true differential calculation in dimension at least two puts every
point with $z_e=0$ in the actual differential exceptional locus: a
different standard coordinate vector is a nonzero kernel vector.
Therefore the original geometric strict transform
$\overline{\pi^{-1}A\setminus\operatorname{Exc}(\pi)}$ is contained
in that actual closure. The same representative of $H$ vanishes on
this strict transform near zero. It is thus an original target vanishing
generator, and span gives the whole inclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual geometric point-chart blowup does not increase the existing intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel function]
\label{hir:actual-geometric-point-bennett-all-orders}
\uses{hir:actual-point-geometric-strict-normalized-inclusion,hir:actual-model-zero-whole-stalk-hs-dictionary,hir:actual-whole-analytic-ideal-hs-antitone,hir:actual-analytic-point-normalized-bennett-all-orders,hir:actual-point-blowup-one-dimensional-identity}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointGeometricBennett.hilbertSamuelValue_actual_geometric_point_strict_le,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointGeometricBennett.hilbertSamuelAt_actual_geometric_point_strict_le}
\leanok
For every original subset $A$, actual point-chart map $\pi$, and order
$\ell$, the actual geometric strict transform has the existing intrinsic
Hilbert--Samuel value at zero at most the original value at zero.
The assertion also holds for the unchanged whole origin-ideal values.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The existence of the actual coordinate implies $n\ge1$. If $n=1$, the
actual point-chart map is literally the identity and its differential
exceptional locus is empty. Its geometric strict transform is therefore
$\overline A$, whose entire original vanishing origin ideal equals
that of $A$ by the first theorem. Hence the values are equal.
If $n\ge2$, the preceding entire ideal inclusion and genuine ideal
antitonicity bound the geometric strict value by the actual analytic
normalized member value. The proved all-order original point-normalized
comparison bounds that by the unchanged original value. Finally the
actual model-zero whole-stalk dictionary identifies these same quotient
values with the unchanged intrinsic point function on both sides.
\end{proof}

This concerns the genuine original point-chart maps and the original
differential-exceptional definition of geometric strict transform.
Transport to arbitrary actual manifold blowup charts and the
positive-dimensional permissible-centre theorem remain separate steps.
