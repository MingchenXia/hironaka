\section{Actual point-chart coordinates and homogeneous unit factors}

Fix a coordinate $e$ of $\mathbb C^n$ and complex numbers $b_i$.
Put $p_e=1$ and $p_i=b_i$ for $i\ne e$. All local algebras in this
chapter are the original holomorphic germ algebras at the origin.
Only finite polynomials are translated; no translation of arbitrary
formal series is used.

\begin{definition}[The actual origin-fixing point-chart maps]
\label{hir:actual-point-unit-coordinate-maps}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnitForward,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnitInverse,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnitDomain,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnitBasePoint}
\leanok
Set $U=\{z:1+z_e\ne0\}$ and define the actual maps
\[
 F_e(z)=z_e,\quad F_i(z)=\frac{b_i+z_i}{1+z_e}-b_i\quad(i\ne e),
 \qquad
 G_e(w)=w_e,\quad G_i(w)=(1+w_e)(b_i+w_i)-b_i\quad(i\ne e).
\]
The actual point $p$ has the coordinates specified above.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine local biholomorphism on the true open domain]
\label{hir:actual-point-unit-local-biholomorphism}
\uses{hir:actual-point-unit-coordinate-maps}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnitLocalEquiv}
\leanok
The set $U$ is an open neighbourhood of zero. Both maps fix zero,
preserve the exceptional coordinate, map $U$ into itself, and satisfy
$G\circ F=\mathrm{id}$ and $F\circ G=\mathrm{id}$ on $U$.
The map $F$ is holomorphic on $U$ and $G$ is holomorphic everywhere.
They therefore construct a genuine original local holomorphic equivalence
with source and target $U$ and centre zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The coordinate function $1+z_e$ is continuous, so its nonzero locus
is open; its value at zero is one. The displayed formulas give the
exceptional coordinates and the origin identities directly. For $i\ne e$,
\[
 G_i(F(z))=(1+z_e)\frac{b_i+z_i}{1+z_e}-b_i=z_i,
 \qquad
 F_i(G(w))=\frac{(1+w_e)(b_i+w_i)}{1+w_e}-b_i=w_i.
\]
The denominators are nonzero on $U$. The exceptional coordinate is
unchanged, so both maps preserve $U$. Each coordinate of $G$ is a
polynomial. Each coordinate of $F$ is a quotient of holomorphic
functions with nonvanishing denominator on $U$. The finite coordinate
criterion for holomorphic maps gives the asserted holomorphicity.
These actual functions, open sets, inverse identities and holomorphicity
proofs supply every field of the original local equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole original germ equivalence and all HS orders]
\label{hir:actual-point-unit-whole-germ-equivalence}
\uses{hir:actual-point-unit-local-biholomorphism,hir:actual-nonlinear-local-germ-algebra-isomorphism,hir:actual-nonlinear-full-quotient-hilbert-samuel-invariance}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnitGermEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.hilbertSamuelValue_actualPointUnitGermEquiv}
\leanok
The actual pullback $E(g)=g\circ F$ is an algebra automorphism of the
entire original holomorphic germ algebra. Its inverse is pullback by $G$.
For every whole ideal $I$ and every $\ell\in\mathbb N$,
\[
 H_{E(I)}(\ell)=H_I(\ell).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the original local-germ algebra construction to the preceding
actual equivalence and its proved origin identity. Composition on
representatives is holomorphic on a neighbourhood of zero; the two
actual local inverse identities prove both inverse algebra identities.
This automorphism preserves the actual maximal ideal and all its powers.
It consequently induces an isomorphism of the entire quotients by
$I+\mathfrak m^{\ell+1}$ and $E(I)+\mathfrak m^{\ell+1}$.
Their complex dimensions, hence the original HS values, are equal.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite homogeneous polynomial dictionary with the actual unit]
\label{hir:actual-point-unit-homogeneous-polynomial-dictionary}
\uses{hir:actual-point-unit-coordinate-maps,hir:actual-point-polynomial-dehomogenization}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnitForward_dehomogenization,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnitReciprocal,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnit_mul_reciprocal,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnit_reciprocal_mul,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.isUnit_actualPointUnit,Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.isUnit_actualPointUnitReciprocal}
\leanok
Let $P$ be any genuine homogeneous complex polynomial of degree $d$.
For every $z\in U$, the actual finite point-chart dehomogenization obeys
\[
 \delta_{e,b}P(F(z))=(1+z_e)^{-d}P(p+z).
\]
The factor is a genuine nonvanishing holomorphic unit near zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $\lambda=(1+z_e)^{-1}$. The arguments of $P$ on the left are
$1$ in coordinate $e$ and $b_i+F_i(z)$ otherwise. The exceptional
argument is $\lambda(1+z_e)=\lambda(p_e+z_e)$, and every other argument
is $(b_i+z_i)/(1+z_e)=\lambda(p_i+z_i)$.
Expand the genuine finite polynomial as its finite sum of supported
monomials. Each supported exponent has total degree $d$, so each
monomial contributes the same scalar $\lambda^d$. Factoring it out
of the finite sum proves the identity. The function $1+z_e$ and its
reciprocal are holomorphic and mutually inverse on the actual
neighbourhood $U$; their germs are therefore actual inverse units.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same identity on entire original germs]
\label{hir:actual-point-unit-entire-germ-polynomial-dictionary}
\uses{hir:actual-point-unit-whole-germ-equivalence,hir:actual-point-unit-homogeneous-polynomial-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointUnitCoordinateChange.actualPointUnitGermEquiv_dehomogenization}
\leanok
For the same finite homogeneous $P$ of degree $d$, let $v$ be the
original germ of $(1+z_e)^{-1}$ and let $p$ be the actual point above.
Then $v$ is a unit in the original germ algebra and
\[
 E\bigl((\delta_{e,b}P)(z)\bigr)=v^d P(p+z)
\]
as equality of entire original holomorphic germs, not just finite jets
or restrictions to the exceptional hyperplane.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original germ pullback acts on every holomorphic representative
by composition with $F$. Apply the pointwise polynomial identity
on the actual neighbourhood $U$ of zero. Equality on this neighbourhood
is equality in the original germ algebra. Polynomial evaluation commutes
with the actual representative-to-germ algebra map, and the germ of
the scalar factor is the stated power of $v$. Its genuine inverse germ
is represented by $1+z_e$.
\end{proof}

The coordinate equivalence and unit identity concern actual finite
homogeneous polynomials and entire original analytic germs. The later
point-centre HS comparison combines them with genuine initial-ideal
inclusions and specialization; no general permissible-centre Bennett
inequality is asserted here.
