\section{A genuine all-level lower preparation on the SAME old history}

Recursive preparation needs a genuine old lower CAN prefix and an actual
appended lower TEST above its entire original invariant, not just a current
candidate with a point. Here both original member and contact branches supply
that precise preparation input. The actual centre trace may be empty or a
proper subset of the maximum; no next lower CAN is inferred.

\begin{theorem}[The same original blowup appends the actual old lower TEST in both branches]
\label{hir:original-stepone-cut-all-level-frozen-lower-test}
\uses{hir:original-positive-stepone-cut-actual-lower-test,hir:original-frozen-recursive-lower-domain-whole-cosupport,hir:step-one-compare,hir:step-one-value-masked-basic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStepOnePreparedLower.original_stepone_cut_frozen_lower_test}
\leanok
Let $n>0$, $M\in\mathrm{DOMAIN}(k+1)$ have maximal order, and let $T$ be
CAN for the unchanged original Step One invariant built from original depth
$k$. Fix an actual nonspecial old upper cosupport point $y$. Compute its
actual original level $\ell$, regime head $H$, tail $S$, mask $W$ and masked
lower record $J$. Take a genuine next ORIGINAL-admissible centre $C$ and
actual blowup $\pi$, whose value at EVERY point of $C$ is at least the
original Step One value at $y$. Then the SAME actual restricted appended
history $(S\pi)|_W$ is a genuine TEST of $J$, for BOTH member and contact
branches. No lower TEST, selected-member centre-containment, tangent or next CAN is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Decide the actual old reference level, using a full dependent level
proposition for its exact head, tail, mask and record. If it is positive,
write it as $s+1$ by the real natural-number predecessor theorem. The proved
original positive-cut theorem constructs the TEST on precisely this SAME
old head and appended tail, with actual selected-member containments and
lower NC internally supplied.

If the actual level is zero, old original canonicality supplies the actual
upper/bar TEST and cosupport equality; actual old nonspeciality supplies the
true zero-regime head cosupport. Original next admissibility appends the
actual upper TEST, and genuine coefficient-homogenized equivalence gives the
extended bar TEST. Literal head/tail append identifies this with $H(S\pi)$.
The proved contact-tail geometry theorem internally constructs the original
chosen tangent at that real head cosupport point and the actual masked
contact lower TEST, with the precise retained divisor. It applies to this
SAME original appended tail and actual mask, without a desired lower TEST or
hypothesis that the reference lies over the centre. The dependent level
proposition transports either genuine proof to the actual old level, with
its exact manifold instances. All real empty positions are retained.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Only original cuts construct actual complete old lower preparation data]
\label{hir:original-stepone-cut-genuine-all-level-lower-preparation}
\uses{hir:original-stepone-cut-all-level-frozen-lower-test,hir:original-stepone-whole-cut-inherited-lower-value,hir:original-frozen-recursive-lower-domain-whole-cosupport,bcalc:inv-domain-stable}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStepOnePreparedLower.original_stepone_cut_prepared_lower_data}
\leanok
Under those original hypotheses, suppose moreover that the actual centre
lies above the original Step One value of EVERY old upper cosupport
reference. Internally construct, for the SAME old member/contact lower
record $J$, all of
\[
 J\in\mathrm{DOMAIN}(k),\quad S|_W\text{ is an actual old lower CAN prefix},\quad
 (S\pi)|_W\text{ is a genuine TEST},\quad
 J_{(S\pi)|_W}\in\mathrm{DOMAIN}(k).
\]
The ENTIRE actual centre trace lies in the whole original lower maximum
locus on $\operatorname{cosupp}J_{S|_W}$. These are genuine original lower
preparation outputs for every actual reference level; no LOWER numerical cut,
lower domain/canonicality or history certificate is a caller input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original canonical lower-domain producer supplies the actual initial
lower DOMAIN and SAME original lower CAN prefix, including the original
contact choice when the level is zero. The first theorem, with the original
cut at $y$, constructs its actual appended lower TEST. Genuine domain
transport now gives its actual current DOMAIN before any recursive value
consumer is invoked.

For each real point of the actual centre trace, the proved inherited-cut
producer first constructs its membership in the actual old lower cosupport
and then proves inequality from EVERY old lower cosupport value to its
value, using TWO genuine same-record original value formulas. These are
exactly the two defining conditions of the whole lower maximum locus. Thus
actual trace containment holds on the same old canonical lower history.
No claim that this trace equals the entire maximum, that the new lower
history is CAN, that independent contact choices already have the same
numerical value after preparation, or that general preparation terminates
is made. This supplies the true geometric/cut input for that further
recursive comparison.
\end{proof}
