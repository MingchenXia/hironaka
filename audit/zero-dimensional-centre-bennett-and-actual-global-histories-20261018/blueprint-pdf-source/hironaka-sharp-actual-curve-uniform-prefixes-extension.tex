\section{Actual uniform prefixes of retained curve histories}

The terminal geometric endpoint does not itself specify global intermediate
blowup stages. Here the first $k$ actual positions of each retained finite
history are constructed, with genuine identity blowups of the empty centre
when that history has already terminated. No empty position is compressed.

\begin{theorem}[Actual uniform prefix and exact independence of extra empty tails]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-padding}
\uses{hir:tower-split,hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding-comparison}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixes.headPacket,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixes.uniformPrefix,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixes.uniformPrefix_length,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixes.uniformPrefix_append_empty}
\leanok
For every actual finite blowup tower $T:X\leftarrow Y$ and natural $k$,
construct an actual endpoint and a tower of exactly $k$ blowups over $X$.
It retains the first $k$ positions of $T$, continuing with real empty-centre
identity blowups if necessary. Appending ANY number of such actual empty
blowups before this construction gives exactly the same pair of endpoint
and tower, not merely the same numerical length or a compressed history.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Package the actual endpoint and head of an existing split as a dependent
pair. Append $k$ real identity-empty blowups to $T$, then remove the last
$|T|$ positions by the actual split operation. The head length is
$(|T|+k)-|T|=k$, including $k=0$ and an originally empty tower.

First induct on $r$: the endpoint/head pair of $T$ followed by $r$ real
empty steps, split before its last $j+r$ positions, equals the endpoint/head
pair of $T$ split before its last $j$ positions. At a successor, the split
definition removes precisely the last actual empty blowup, so the induction
keeps every preceding arrow and centre. For extra padding $r$ in the
uniform-prefix construction, associativity and the actual empty-tail
concatenation identity rewrite the two consecutive tails of sizes $r,k$
as tails of sizes $k,r$. Apply the preceding equality with $j=|T|$.
This is equality of the whole dependent pair; no terminal-isomorphism
certificate or erased step is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Unique uniform-stage comparison from genuine padded histories]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-comparison}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-padding,hir:tower-split-iso,hir:sharp-actual-stagewise-tower-comparison-unique,mc:tower-isomorphism-append}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixes.uniformPrefix_comparison,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixes.exists_unique_uniformPrefix_of_padded_comparison,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixes.uniformPrefix_comparison_cocycle}
\leanok
Any genuine stagewise isomorphism between two actually empty-padded full
histories over a fixed actual base biholomorphism supplies a unique
stagewise isomorphism between their uniform $k$ prefixes. The prefix
endpoints and towers depend only on the original histories and $k$,
so every real larger-cut comparison induces the same map there.
Three such prefix maps satisfy the actual cocycle over the composed base
map. All projection squares and every retained centre are those of
the genuine tower isomorphisms.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For unpadded isomorphic histories, their lengths are equal. Append $k$
actual identity-empty blowups on both sides; the existing genuine
empty-tail comparison extends the given stagewise isomorphism. Split
both full towers before their last common original length. The existing
split-isomorphism theorem supplies the actual head biholomorphism and
the entire head-stage isomorphism, hence the desired uniform-prefix map.

If the given histories were first padded by unequal actual empty-tail
lengths, perform the construction on those real towers. Rewrite both
whole dependent endpoint/tower pairs by the exact padding-independence
identity. This gives the requested map on the original prefix types.
For any two candidates, the genuine fixed-tower universal uniqueness
theorem makes their terminal maps equal. Thus different larger terminal
cuts and different genuine full comparisons cannot change the prefix map.
For three maps, their true stagewise composition and the direct map have
the same source tower, target tower and composed base map; the same
uniqueness proves the cocycle. No scalar, numerical or endpoint-only
comparison is substituted for a tower isomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual original two-phase curve recipes compare at every uniform stage]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-uniform-prefix-comparison}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-comparison,hir:sharp-actual-curve-biholo-two-phase-padded-recipes}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixes.exists_unique_original_uniformPrefix_comparison}
\leanok
Under the original positive ambient dimension, closed smooth pure-curve,
ordered SNC and boundary-complement density inputs, let two genuine FIRST
blowups and literal terminal Delta tails lie over any actual base
biholomorphism, with the original carrier and EVERY ordered member pulled
back on its source. Their full histories have a unique actual comparison
at every uniform stage $k$. No prefix map, source geometric input, common
padding bound or desired centre-matching certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose internally the common terminal tail cut to be the sum of the two
actual tail lengths. Both bounds follow from natural arithmetic. The
existing original two-phase comparison producer first supplies the real
original/source product TEST geometry and actual FIRST/Delta centre
naturality, then constructs a genuine stagewise isomorphism between the
two histories padded to that common tail length. Reassociate each actual
full concatenation as its unpadded full history followed by the real
remaining empty steps. Apply the preceding uniform-prefix theorem and
its uniqueness. The final prefix types therefore use precisely the
original full histories and $k$, independently of that internal cut.

This is a genuine finite-stage producer for later overlap descent. A
global stage atlas, compatibility with restriction to smaller opens,
global next blowup universal property and locally stationary global
sequence are separate remaining constructions; none is asserted here.
\end{proof}
