\section{Actual flattening-coordinate biholomorphisms and intrinsic vanishing HS}

Let $x_0$ be a point of an original complex manifold $M$, write $e$ for
its original preferred chart, and let $\Phi$ be an existing genuine
local holomorphic equivalence centred at $e(x_0)$. Assume its actual
source lies in the actual target of $e$. The original flattening domain
is $D=e.\mathrm{source}\cap e^{-1}(\Phi.\mathrm{source})$.
The actual model target $T=\Phi.\mathrm{target}$ remains an ordinary
open subset of the original complex model; no universe restriction on
$M$ and no replacement local algebra are imposed.

\begin{definition}[The actual flattening-coordinate biholomorphism]
\label{hir:actual-flat-coordinate-open-biholomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFlatCoordinateIntrinsicHS.actualFlatCoordinateDiffeomorph,Hironaka.HironakaActualFlatCoordinateIntrinsicHS.actualFlatCoordinateDiffeomorph_apply,Hironaka.HironakaActualFlatCoordinateIntrinsicHS.actualFlatCoordinateDiffeomorph_symm_apply}
\leanok
The actual maps
\[
 F:D\longrightarrow T,\quad F(x)=\Phi(e(x)),\qquad
 G:T\longrightarrow D,\quad G(u)=e^{-1}(\Phi^{-1}(u))
\]
construct a genuine biholomorphism of the original open subtypes.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Both actual domains are open. The original local equivalence maps its
source into its target and its target back into its source. The source
containment in the chart target ensures that the displayed inverse
lands in the original flattening domain. The true inverse identities of
$\Phi$ and the actual chart prove $G(F(x))=x$ and $F(G(u))=u$.
On the actual flattening domain, the chart is holomorphic and $\Phi$
is holomorphic at each actual chart image. On the actual target, the
inverse $\Phi^{-1}$ is holomorphic and its values lie in the true chart
target, where the chart inverse is holomorphic. These actual composite
maps are holomorphic near every point, hence complex smooth of all
orders. True open-subtype restriction and lifting preserve that
holomorphicity, supplying both smoothness fields of the actual
biholomorphism with precisely the displayed forward and inverse functions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The inverse flat-coordinate map is a genuine holomorphic open embedding]
\label{hir:actual-flat-coordinate-inverse-open-embedding}
\uses{hir:actual-flat-coordinate-open-biholomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFlatCoordinateIntrinsicHS.contMDiff_actual_flat_inverse,Hironaka.HironakaActualFlatCoordinateIntrinsicHS.isOpenEmbedding_actual_flat_inverse}
\leanok
The actual function $u\mapsto e^{-1}(\Phi^{-1}(u))$ from $T$ to the
original manifold $M$ is holomorphic and is an open embedding onto the
actual original flattening domain.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
It is exactly the genuine inverse biholomorphism from the preceding
node followed by the actual inclusion of the original open subtype $D$.
Both maps are holomorphic, and both underlying maps are open embeddings.
Composition gives the asserted actual properties. Surjectivity of the
inverse biholomorphism onto $D$ identifies its image with that unchanged
domain. No same-universe open-embedding packaging or supplied Jacobian
is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original intrinsic vanishing HS value is transported by the actual inverse coordinates]
\label{hir:actual-flat-coordinate-inverse-any-subset-intrinsic-hs}
\uses{hir:actual-flat-coordinate-open-biholomorphism,hir:actual-any-subset-biholomorphic-intrinsic-hs,hir:actual-any-subset-open-intrinsic-hs}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFlatCoordinateIntrinsicHS.hilbertSamuelAt_vanishing_actual_flat_inverse}
\leanok
For every original subset $W\subseteq M$, every actual point $u\in T$
and every order $\ell$, the existing intrinsic vanishing-ideal
Hilbert--Samuel value of $G^{-1}W$ at $u$ equals the unchanged original
value of $W$ at $G(u)$. No analyticity, closedness, coherence or global
analytic extension is a premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Restrict the original subset to the actual open domain $D$. The proved
arbitrary-subset intrinsic transport for the genuine inverse
biholomorphism identifies its unchanged original HS value at $G(u)$
with that of the actual inverse image in $T$ at $u$. The proved arbitrary-
subset open restriction theorem identifies the former with the original
value on $M$. Their composition gives the equality at every arbitrary
order. All functions, ideal sheaves, whole chart stalks, complex quotient
actions and intrinsic functions are the original ones.
\end{proof}

The local equivalence is actual coordinate geometry, not a desired
ideal or HS certificate. Construction of the original point-blowup
normal-form coordinates is a separate geometric producer; the present
actual inverse-coordinate embedding is the transport interface for that
producer. Positive-dimensional permissible-centre Bennett remains open.
