\section{True uniqueness and cocycles of original terminal comparison maps}

Keep the actual recorded finite blowup tower and every original marked input.
The finite canonical-choice route of
\cite{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality} is extended
from existence of true original-final comparisons to their intrinsic

no stage lift, invertibility or dense-isomorphism certificate is a caller input.

\begin{lemma}[An actual positive-length tower has a genuine first-step factorization]
\label{hir:original-actual-first-step-whole-tail-factorization}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:blowup-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProjectionUniqueness.exists_actual_first_step_split}
\leanok
Every actual positive-length recorded tower has a literal first genuine
blowup followed by its ENTIRE actual remaining tail. The tail has strictly
smaller actual length. This includes true empty first centres and actual
nonidentity empty projections.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use actual tower recursion. An empty tower contradicts positive length.
For a next step, if its preceding tower is empty, this step is the first
and its tail is empty. Otherwise the actual preceding tower has positive
length and its genuine first step and entire tail are internally supplied
by recursion. Append the given actual last step to THIS tail; the actual
append recursion identifies the whole result with the original tower.
True length append gives one plus tail length, hence strict decrease.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual final biholomorphisms over a whole blowup projection are intrinsically unique]
\label{hir:original-actual-whole-projection-biholomorphic-map-uniqueness}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-actual-first-step-whole-tail-factorization,hir:general-nc-natural-whole-total-divisor,hir:tower-isomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProjectionUniqueness.biholomorphisms_eq_of_actual_proj_eq}
\leanok
For ANY actual finite tower $T:X\leftarrow Y$ and two genuine
biholomorphisms $a,b:Z\to Y$, equality $p_Ta=p_Tb$ INTERNALLY implies
$a=b$. No source canonical history, chosen stage lift, first-centre
pullback invertibility or generic-density certificate is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use strong induction on ACTUAL target-tower length, uniformly over all
original source manifolds and actual final biholomorphisms. For the empty
tower its projection is identity, so the given equality is exactly $a=b$.
Otherwise the first lemma internally supplies a genuine first blowup
$\pi:A\to X$ and its entire remaining tower $S:A\leftarrow Y$ with
$p_T=\pi p_S$. The actual first blowup's universal property includes
invertibility of the ENTIRE actual pullback of its actual centre ideal
$J$ on $A$. Genuine pullback along an ACTUAL recorded tower preserves an
invertible ideal: each true blowup has exceptional locus of empty interior,
which preserves nonzero local holomorphic generators, and actual coherent
ideal pullback composition gives the whole tower assertion. Apply this
established intrinsic producer to THIS entire $S$ and THIS internally
supplied $\pi^*J$. Pull back further along the genuine biholomorphism $a$;
its inverse differential makes its exceptional locus empty. Thus the
ENTIRE original pullback of $J$ along $\pi p_Sa$ is internally invertible.
Actual coherent ideal pullback composition supplies exactly this original
map's ideal, not a substitute denominator or generation certificate.

The genuine universal property of $\pi$, applied to THIS actual holomorphic
map $\pi p_Sa$, gives uniqueness of its actual holomorphic lift into $A$.
Both $p_Sa$ and $p_Sb$ are real lifts: the former by definition and the
latter by the GIVEN entire projection equality. Hence $p_Sa=p_Sb$.
The target-tail length is strictly smaller, so the induction on THIS
actual tail and SAME genuine final biholomorphisms gives $a=b$. All
invertibility and lift conditions were supplied from the actual original
blowups; no arbitrary holomorphic pullback preservation or maximum
existence was assumed. Empty manifolds, empty steps and dimension zero
are included.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original ambient canonical terminal comparisons are internally unique]
\label{hir:original-ambient-terminal-choice-intrinsic-unique-comparison}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProjectionUniqueness.original_ambient_terminal_choice_existsUnique,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProjectionUniqueness.ambientTerminalComparison,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProjectionUniqueness.ambientTerminalComparison_proj}
\leanok
At EVERY ambient dimension, ONLY the original marked-domain input and TWO
GIVEN whole actual canonical terminal histories internally supply EXACTLY
ONE original-final biholomorphism commuting with their WHOLE original
projections. Let their canonical comparison be this INTERNALLY constructed
unique actual map. All original empty steps/counts remain allowed; no
compressed, nonempty, invertibility, comparison or cocycle certificate
is supplied by a caller.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The completed genuine all-ambient original terminal-choice producer
internally gives an actual original-final biholomorphism with the whole
projection formula; at dimension zero this comes from true original whole
projection biholomorphisms. For any other actual original-final map with
the same whole formula, their target-tower projections are equal at every
original source point. Apply the preceding actual projection uniqueness
theorem to THIS genuine target tower and THESE genuine final maps. Thus
the original-final comparison is unique. Select the actual map supplied
by this proved existence and uniqueness. Its original projection formula
is retained directly from that producer; no desired map is an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual original canonical terminal comparisons satisfy identity, inverse and triple cocycle]
\label{hir:original-ambient-terminal-comparison-identity-inverse-cocycle}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-ambient-terminal-choice-intrinsic-unique-comparison}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProjectionUniqueness.ambientTerminalComparison_self,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProjectionUniqueness.ambientTerminalComparison_symm,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalTerminalProjectionUniqueness.ambientTerminalComparison_trans}
\leanok
For original marked-domain input and GIVEN actual canonical terminal
histories, the internally constructed comparison of a history with itself
is the actual identity. Reversing two histories gives the actual inverse
comparison. For THREE independently given actual canonical terminal
histories, the actual comparison from the first to the second followed
by the actual comparison from the second to the third equals the actual
comparison from the first to the third. These are equalities of genuine
original-final biholomorphisms, including differing actual empty-step
counts, dimension zero and empty final spaces.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual identity map satisfies the same whole original projection
formula as the constructed self-comparison, so intrinsic uniqueness
identifies them. The true inverse of the first-to-second comparison
satisfies the reversed formula: substitute its actual inverse point into
the original whole projection equality and use the genuine right inverse.
Intrinsic uniqueness identifies it with the internally constructed
reverse comparison. For three histories compose their actual first-to-second
and second-to-third biholomorphisms. Their TWO retained whole projection
formulas compose to the original first-to-third formula at EVERY original
source point. Intrinsic uniqueness identifies this actual composite with
the actual directly constructed first-to-third map. No uncompressed tower
length equality or equality of indexed exceptional lists is asserted.
This supplies finite original comparison cocycles; noncompact global
scheduling and global resolution gluing remain distinct source obligations.
\end{proof}
