\section{Actual holomorphic cones lift locally even entirely in the exceptional divisor}

\begin{lemma}[The actual matching-chart lift of every whole compatible cone]
\label{hir:submersion-blowup-local-cone-entire-join}
\leanok
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionBlowupLocalCones.exists_matching_chart_cone,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionBlowupLocalCones.matching_chart_joint_point_unique}
\uses{hir:submersion-adapted-blowup-entire-monomial-dictionary,hir:submersion-adapted-blowup-actual-smooth-chart-map}
Consider actual source/target blowup charts $c',c$, with true block normal-index and pivot identities and the whole source-to-target flattening coordinate identity on the source flat domain. Genuine matching adapted charts are a special case. On an actual manifold $P$, let $u:P\to Y$ and $v:P\to\mathrm{Bl}_C X$
be holomorphic, with $fu=\pi v$. On the genuine open where $u$ lies in the
source flat domain and $v$ lies in the target monomial chart, internally
construct a holomorphic map into the actual source blowup chart, whose
projection is $u$ and whose flat-chart map is $v$. If the actual parameter projection maps the source chart target into the target chart target, two points in that SAME source chart with equal base and flat-chart image are equal. Thus the lift is unique among maps with image in that SAME chart and these whole identities.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $U=\Psi e_y u$ and $V=c.\mathrm{toFun}\,v$ on this true open. Both are
holomorphic there, by their genuine chart and flattening domains. The actual
whole source-coordinate dictionary and the original compatible square give
$\operatorname{drop}U=\sigma_j V$ at every point. Form the literal parameter
map $w=\mathrm{split}^{-1}(\operatorname{fibre}U,V)$. The actual block monomial identities and original square give $\sigma'_j w=U$. The original $u$ lies in the source flat domain, so $U$ belongs to its genuine flattening target; therefore the entire image of $w$ lies in the actual source monomial-chart target. Genuine continuous-linear maps make $w$ holomorphic. The actual
block/monomial identities give $\sigma'_j w=U$ and
$\operatorname{drop}w=V$. Compose with the genuine entire holomorphic inverse
of the source blowup chart to obtain $g$. The actual flattening inverse
identities prove $\pi'g=u$; the true target chart inverse gives
$\mathrm{flatChartMap}\,g=v$. This uses no division by the exceptional
parameter. For two points in the SAME chart, equal source projections give equal monomial parameters. The fibre projection commutes with this genuine block monomial, so their fibre parameters agree. The parameter-projection target containment and true target chart right-inverse turn equality of the flat-chart images into equality of the dropped parameters. The actual linear split is injective, so these two parameter vectors are equal. The genuine source-chart left-inverse gives equality of the actual points, hence uniqueness of maps with these whole identities. This is uniqueness inside an actual chosen chart, not yet global
joint-point uniqueness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original geometry internally lifts every cone near an exceptional point]
\label{hir:submersion-blowup-local-cone-original-exceptional}
\leanok
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionBlowupLocalCones.exists_canonical_exceptional_local_cone}
\uses{hir:submersion-blowup-local-cone-entire-join,hir:submersion-adapted-actual-product-shrink,hir:submersion-global-lift-whole-chart-equality}
ONLY original global submersion $f:Y^{n+r}\to X^n$, closed smooth nowhere-dense
centre $C$, and genuine holomorphic compatible cone $u:P\to Y$,
$v:P\to\mathrm{Bl}_C X$ internally give a local holomorphic lift near any
actual $p_0\in P$ with $f(u p_0)\in C$. The neighbourhood is a true open
containing $p_0$, its lift goes into the actual source blowup, and BOTH whole
projection identities hold on that entire neighbourhood.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set $y=u p_0$ and $z=v p_0$. The genuine original square identifies the actual
base of $z$ with $fy$. Internally choose the genuine centre flattening at $fy$.
The actual non-tangent direction of $z$ supplies its true target pivot. The
actual submersion chart producer supplies the SAME matching source chart and
product target; restricting the target flattening preserves the selected
pivot coordinate, so the target monomial chart contains $z$. The source
flattening contains $y$ by its true central inverse identity. The intersection
of the inverse images of these two actual open domains under the original
holomorphic $u,v$ is therefore a true open neighbourhood of $p_0$.
Apply the preceding actual entire join construction on this SAME open. Its
source projection is $u$. Entire universal-chart equality identifies the
actual canonical lift $G_0$ with the flat-chart map on the entire source
chart, hence $G_0g=v$ throughout this SAME neighbourhood, including when the
ENTIRE original cone maps into the exceptional divisor. No chosen lift,
product-domain, chart-coverage, local-lift or Cartier output certificate is a
caller hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original off-centre cone has its actual local lift]
\label{hir:submersion-blowup-local-cone-original-off}
\leanok
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionBlowupLocalCones.exists_canonical_off_local_cone}
\uses{hir:submersion-blowup-actual-complement-biholomorphism,hir:submersion-blowup-lift-actual-lift}
For the same original data and an actual point with $f(u p_0)\notin C$, a true
open neighbourhood of that point has a holomorphic source blowup lift
satisfying BOTH entire original cone projection identities.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual open inverse image of the source centre complement under $u$.
It contains $p_0$ by the original off-centre assumption. The source primitive
blowup internally gives the true complement biholomorphism, holomorphic in
both directions on its whole actual complement. Compose its genuine inverse
with the corestricted original $u$ to construct the actual holomorphic lift
$g$. Its projection is $u$ on the entire open. The true universal square and
the original compatible cone square give equal bases for $G_0g$ and $v$.
Those bases avoid $C$, so actual off-centre point uniqueness gives $G_0g=v$
on the entire open. Empty complements require no nonexistent witness: the
specified actual point supplies the true nonempty neighbourhood when needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual cone is locally lifted for arbitrary genuine choices]
\label{hir:submersion-blowup-local-cone-arbitrary-choices}
\leanok
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionBlowupLocalCones.exists_actual_local_cone}
\uses{hir:submersion-blowup-local-cone-original-exceptional,hir:submersion-blowup-local-cone-original-off,hir:submersion-blowup-choice-actual-conjugate,hir:submersion-monomial-dimension}
ONLY original submersion $f$ and ANY primitive genuine target/source blowup
choices, together with genuine holomorphic compatible cone $u,v$, internally
give a true local holomorphic source lift at EVERY actual point of $P$, with
both whole original projection identities. The cone may lie entirely in the
exceptional divisor; its pulled-back centre ideal is not required invertible.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For arbitrary original dimensions $m,n$, the specified actual reference
point gives $y=u p_0$. The actual original submersion at $y$ internally
proves $n\leq m$, hence $m=n+r$ for a genuine natural $r$. No caller
dimension bound is assumed, and an empty test manifold has no reference
point to lift. Internally construct the actual identity-base comparisons $a,b$ with the
canonical blowups and their whole lift formula $G=a^{-1}G_0b$. The actual
canonical cone is $u,av$ and is holomorphic; its whole compatible square
follows from the original square and the actual comparison projection.
At each original point, centre membership decides the preceding exceptional
or off-centre producer. Let $g_0$ be its actual local canonical lift on its
actual produced neighbourhood. Then $g=b^{-1}g_0$ is holomorphic there, and
the genuine comparison projection and whole conjugation identities give
$\pi'g=u$ and $Gg=v$ on that entire neighbourhood. Every chart, neighbourhood,
comparison and lift is internally generated. This local existence theorem
does NOT assume or claim global joint-point uniqueness, overlap equality for
arbitrary chosen lifts, a glued global lift, or the full Cartesian universal
property. Those are the next independent mathematical obligations; higher
invariant/canonical-driver functoriality is also still OPEN.
\end{proof}
