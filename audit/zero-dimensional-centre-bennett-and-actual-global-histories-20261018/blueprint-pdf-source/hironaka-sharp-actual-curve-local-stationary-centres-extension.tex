\section{Actual local stationarity of the global blowup centres}

\begin{lemma}[The literal successor centre after a genuine local history is empty]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-local-successor-centre-empty}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-eventual-empty,hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-successor-data}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveLocalStationaryCentres.actualUniformSuccessor_centre_empty}
\leanok
Let $T$ be a genuine finite blowup history. At EVERY natural stage $k$
at or beyond its literal length, the actual chosen successor centre of
its uniform prefix is empty. This concerns the actual centre supplied
by the original last-step constructor, not a replacement empty history.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $k=\operatorname{length}(T)+r$. The actual whole endpoint-and-history identity at $k+1$ identifies
its entire history with $T$ followed by $r$ genuine empty identity steps
and one further identity blowup at the empty centre. The actual chosen
successor is an original last-step constructor of precisely this SAME
whole history. Consider the property of a whole endpoint-history packet
that its last constructor has empty centre (true on a reflexive history).
It holds for the literal displayed empty successor. Transport this
property through the genuine whole packet identity, then read the actual
chosen last constructor. Its centre must therefore be empty. This includes $r=0$ and keeps every prior empty position.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual global successor centres eventually avoid every original chart]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-global-centres-locally-stationary}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-local-successor-centre-empty,hir:sharp-actual-curve-successor-centre-transition,hir:sharp-actual-endpoint-saturated-local-range,hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-uniform-stage-atlas}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveLocalStationaryCentres.globalUniformCentre_disjoint_preimage_open,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveLocalStationaryCentres.originalUniformCentres_locally_stationary}
\leanok
For actual retained histories covering the original space, fix one actual
original chart and let $L$ be the literal length of its SAME retained full
local history. At EVERY $k\ge L$, the entire global successor centre is
disjoint from the entire stage-projection preimage of that original
chart. In particular only original positive-dimensional smooth pure
curve, ordered SNC and density data construct, around EVERY original
point, an actual open neighbourhood and an actual finite bound with this
property. No cover, local stopping bound, empty-centre or stationarity
certificate is supplied to this original-only conclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At each such stage the first lemma makes the actual local successor
centre on this chart empty. The entire preimage of the global centre
under the actual chart inclusion is exactly that local centre. If a
global centre point projected into the chart, the genuine saturated-range
identity would place it in that inclusion range. Choosing its actual
local representative would put that representative in the empty local
centre, a contradiction. Thus the two entire sets are disjoint.

For each original point take its actual original chart from the internally
constructed original retained-history atlas. It is genuinely open and
contains that point. Its actual full history has an ordinary finite
literal length; choose exactly this length as the bound and apply the
preceding argument to every later stage. No maximum over an infinite
family is formed. This proves actual local stationarity of centres;
a uniform global stopping time, identification with the retained sharp
terminal endpoint, and compact finite termination are distinct subsequent
conclusions.
\end{proof}
