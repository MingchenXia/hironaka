\section{Internally constructed local coherent coefficient ideals of actual analytic families}

We retain literal Taylor coefficients of actual holomorphic germs. The coefficient
ideals in this chapter are genuine principal germ ideals on their real common
holomorphic domain. No analytic preimage of an arbitrary formal reduced basis or
semicoherent presentation is assumed. The construction supplies the actual local
coefficient objects used in the coefficient-centre step of
\cite[Theorem 9.4]{bierstone-milman97-invent-math}.

\begin{lemma}[A real holomorphic function generates an actual locally coherent principal family]
\label{hir:actual-local-principal-coefficient-ideal}
\uses{hir:actual-local-coefficient-germ-propagation}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFiniteCoefficientIdeals.actualPrincipalCoefficientIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaActualFiniteCoefficientIdeals.coherentOn_actualPrincipalCoefficientIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaActualFiniteCoefficientIdeals.mem_actualPrincipalCoefficientIdeal}
\leanok
For any actual function $c$ holomorphic on a real open set $U$, define its
principal family at a point $x$ by the genuine holomorphic germ span of $c$.
This family is coherent on $U$, with the single actual generator $c$.
At every $x\in U$ the original function $c$ belongs to its genuine stalk.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At each $x\in U$ use the same real open set $U$ and the one-element family
whose value is $c$. Its range is exactly the singleton consisting of $c$,
so the defining original germ membership is precisely the original
finite holomorphic span membership for that one-element family. Holomorphicity
on $U$ supplies the required generator holomorphicity. The function $c$
belongs to its own germ span by its one-term expression $1\cdot c$ and its
actual holomorphicity near $x$. No statement is made about coherence outside $U$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every finite actual analytic family internally supplies its low coherent coefficient ideals]
\label{hir:actual-finite-local-coherent-coefficient-ideals}
\uses{hir:actual-local-principal-coefficient-ideal,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-literal-formal-taylor-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFiniteCoefficientIdeals.exists_actual_finite_local_coefficient_ideals}
\leanok
Fix a finite family of actual analytic germs $g_i(u,z)$ and a degree bound
$q$. There exist a real open neighbourhood $U$ of the origin and actual
representatives $c_{i,\gamma}(u)$ of all of their original analytic relative
coefficient germs. For every $|\gamma|<q$ these representatives are
holomorphic on $U$, their actual principal germ ideals are coherent on $U$,
and $c_{i,\gamma}$ belongs to the original ideal stalk at the origin.
For every exponent $\gamma$, the entire normal coefficient series of the
literal Taylor series of $g_i$ is the literal Taylor series of the chosen
original germ of $c_{i,\gamma}$. Every later finite relative Taylor polynomial
uses these same original coefficient germs. A common domain for infinitely
many high coefficients is not asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The proved finite relative coefficient construction chooses actual representatives
of the intrinsic original coefficient germs and a single real open neighbourhood
on which all low coefficients of this finite family are holomorphic. Its
whole Taylor coefficient dictionary gives the stated entire-series identity
for every exponent, and its whole-germ stability gives the same coefficients
in every later finite relative Taylor polynomial. For each low coefficient
apply the preceding lemma to its actual representative on this same $U$.
It constructs the genuine principal ideal family and proves its local coherence
and original stalk membership internally. Thus the output consists of actual
functions and actual ideals, without supplied coefficient identities, coherent
ideal certificates or global extensions of local holomorphic functions.
\end{proof}
