\section{Whole original germ algebra maps and genuine Taylor coordinate naturality}

The coefficient-presentation dictionary needs Taylor expansion to commute
with the actual tangential coordinate inclusion. The proof uses genuine
finite original coordinate representatives, not an assumed continuity or
coefficient identity. This supplies the coordinate part of the relative
coefficient route; the entire literal normal-coefficient dictionary remains
separate until proved.

\begin{theorem}[Actual coordinate images determine the whole original germ map]
\label{hir:actual-formal-germ-hom-coordinate-uniqueness}
\uses{hir:actual-whole-germ-finite-monomial-representative,hir:actual-formal-coordinate-power-coefficient-kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualTaylorCoordinateNaturality.map_actualGerm_maximalIdeal_le_formal_coordinates,Hironaka.HironakaActualTaylorCoordinateNaturality.actualGermAlgHom_coefficient_zero_of_mem_maximal_pow,Hironaka.HironakaActualTaylorCoordinateNaturality.actualGermAlgHom_eq_of_coordinate_images}
\leanok
Let $f,h:\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}\to\mathbb C[[X_1,\ldots,X_m]]$
be actual complex algebra homomorphisms. Suppose their images of every
original coordinate germ agree and lie in the genuine formal maximal ideal
$(X_1,\ldots,X_m)$. Then $f=h$ on the ENTIRE original germ ring.
No topological-continuity certificate or desired Taylor identity is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original holomorphic Hadamard decomposition writes every germ with
zero value as a sum of actual coordinate germs times actual holomorphic
germs. Thus each homomorphism maps the entire original maximal ideal into
the formal maximal ideal, and genuine multiplication gives the same
inclusion at every power. Actual formal coordinate-power membership
annihilates every coefficient below that power.

For any original $g$ and any formal exponent $a$, choose the actual finite
original coordinate-word representative $p$ modulo the original maximal
power of degree $|a|+1$. Both image remainders have zero $a$ coefficient.
The two algebra maps agree on $p$ because it lies in the genuine finite
complex span of products of the original coordinate germs: use their exact
coordinate identities, multiplication, addition and complex linearity.
Hence the $a$ coefficients of $f(g)$ and $h(g)$ agree. This holds for every
actual exponent, proving equality of the entire formal series and then
of the whole algebra maps, also when either coordinate block is empty.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine Taylor expansion commutes with the actual tangential embedding]
\label{hir:actual-original-taylor-tangent-coordinate-naturality}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-germ-hom-coordinate-uniqueness,hir:actual-original-formal-taylor-algebra-embedding,hir:relative-normal-basis-actual-entire-quotient-basis}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualTaylorCoordinateNaturality.tangentPullback_coordinateGerm,Hironaka.HironakaActualTaylorCoordinateNaturality.actualUnshiftedTaylor_tangentPullback}
\leanok
For every actual tangential holomorphic germ $g$, its genuine pullback by
the actual first-coordinate projection has original Taylor series equal
to the actual variable-renaming of the original Taylor series of $g$,
using precisely the first $d$ variables inside $d+t$ variables.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The two sides are independently defined actual complex algebra maps on the
entire original tangential germ ring. The real coordinate projection pulls
each original tangential coordinate germ to the corresponding ambient
coordinate germ. The genuine Taylor homomorphism maps that same coordinate
to the corresponding formal variable. The actual formal variable-renaming
homomorphism has exactly the same images. These true coordinate images
belong to the formal maximal ideal, so the preceding whole-map uniqueness
applies. This proves the exact original Taylor identity without replacing
either independently defined map by a proxy.
\end{proof}
