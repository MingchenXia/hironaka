\section{Genuine analytic monic vertices retaining literal centre powers}

Let $R$ be the original analytic germ ring on $\mathbb C^n$, $T:R\to S$
its actual unshifted Taylor map and $I\subseteq R$ any original ideal.
For a coordinate centre set $A$, let $C_A$ be its genuine original coordinate
ideal. Let $V$ be the actual degree-lex vertices of $T(I)S$, and let
$F_\alpha$ be its existing canonical reduced formal vertex series.

\begin{theorem}[Actual analytic vertex generators preserve every required whole centre power]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-centre-compatible-monic-vertex-family}
\uses{hir:actual-original-analytic-monic-vertex-basis,hir:actual-formal-extension-whole-ideal-intersections,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticCentreVertexFamily.exists_actual_analytic_centre_vertex_family}
\leanok
Suppose the literal existing reduced formal members satisfy
$F_\alpha\in\widehat C_A^{w_A(\alpha)}$ for every $\alpha\in V$,
where $w_A(\alpha)=\sum_{i\in A}\alpha_i$.
Then there exists an internally constructed finite actual analytic family
$(g_\alpha)_{\alpha\in V}$ such that
\[
 g_\alpha\in I\cap C_A^{w_A(\alpha)},\qquad
 \operatorname{in}_{\deglex} T(g_\alpha)=\alpha,\qquad
 [x^\alpha]T(g_\alpha)=1,\qquad I=(g_\alpha)_{\alpha\in V}.
\]
No analytic standard family or convergence certificate is supplied. The
formal centre-power hypothesis is explicit; its derivation for general
permissible centres remains a separate source-facing goal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine coordinate Taylor dictionary identifies the extension of every
whole analytic centre power with the corresponding whole formal centre power.
Whole ideal intersection compatibility therefore puts the actual formal
$F_\alpha$ in the extension of $I\cap C_A^{w_A(\alpha)}$.
Apply the original-ideal jet representative construction to this actual
intersection, through degree $|\alpha|$. This internally gives an analytic
$g_\alpha$ in the genuine intersection, preserving the coefficient $1$ at
$\alpha$ and every lower degree-lex coefficient. The latter exponents all
have total degree at most $|\alpha|$, so their coefficients are zero.
Thus its true Taylor initial exponent is exactly $\alpha$ and it is monic.

These actual Taylor series lie in $T(I)S$ and internally supply every field
of the existing monic vertex family: genuine ideal membership, coefficient
$1$ and vanishing below the actual vertex. The existing whole family division
then proves that their entire formal span is $T(I)S$. Extending the original
span of the $g_\alpha$ gives exactly that same formal span; injectivity of
whole original ideal extension yields $I=(g_\alpha)$. The dimension equality
$0+n=n$ merely transports the already proved original Taylor statements to
the existing unshifted map. Zero/unit ideals, empty families, an empty centre
set and dimension zero are included. The higher formal coefficients of this
analytic family are not asserted to equal the canonical reduced family.
\end{proof}
