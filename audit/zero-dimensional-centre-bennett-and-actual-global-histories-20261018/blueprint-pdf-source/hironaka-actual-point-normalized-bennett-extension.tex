\section{The all-order point-centre comparison for the actual analytic normalized ideal}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine unit coordinates identify the entire finite initial ideals]
\label{hir:actual-point-unit-map-whole-initial-ideals}
\uses{hir:actual-point-unit-entire-germ-polynomial-dictionary,hir:actual-point-dehomogenized-initial-ideal,hir:actual-unshifted-translated-initial-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointNormalizedBennett.map_actualDehomogenizedInitialIdeal_unit_coordinates}
\leanok
For every actual formal ideal $K$ and point chart $(e,b)$, the whole
image of its actual analytic dehomogenized finite initial ideal under the
genuine original unit-coordinate germ algebra equivalence is the whole
actual translated initial ideal at $p_e=1$, $p_i=b_i$ for $i\ne e$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The unchanged actual vertex polynomial $P_a$ is genuinely homogeneous of
its actual degree $d_a$. The proved whole analytic polynomial dictionary
sends its dehomogenized generator to $v^{d_a}P_a(p+x)$, where the original
germ $v=(1+x_e)^{-1}$ has the genuine whole inverse $u=1+x_e$.
Each image generator therefore belongs to the translated initial ideal.
Conversely multiply the same image generator by $u^{d_a}$; the actual
whole inverse identity gives exactly the translated generator $P_a(p+x)$.
Both statements hold for every member of the unchanged actual finite
vertex family. Ideal image commutes with span, so span induction proves
both entire ideal inclusions. The degrees may vary with the generator;
each individual multiplying factor is an actual unit in the same ring.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All actual dehomogenized and translated initial values coincide]
\label{hir:actual-point-unit-initial-all-hs-equality}
\uses{hir:actual-point-unit-map-whole-initial-ideals,hir:actual-point-unit-whole-germ-equivalence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointNormalizedBennett.hilbertSamuelValue_actualDehomogenizedInitialIdeal_eq_translated}
\leanok
At every order, the actual analytic dehomogenized finite initial ideal
has precisely the intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel value of the translated
initial ideal in the preceding theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The coordinate change and its genuine inverse give an actual original
local holomorphic equivalence and the resulting whole complex germ
algebra equivalence. The actual finite maximal-ideal quotients retain
their complex dimensions under this equivalence. Use the preceding
entire ideal identity to identify the image quotient with the translated
initial quotient at every order.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual analytic point-normalized member ideal does not increase any Hilbert--Samuel value]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-point-normalized-bennett-all-orders}
\uses{hir:actual-point-normalized-hs-le-dehomogenized-initial,hir:actual-point-unit-initial-all-hs-equality,hir:actual-unshifted-translated-initial-all-hs}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointNormalizedBennett.hilbertSamuelValue_actual_point_normalized_le_original}
\leanok
For every original analytic ideal $I$, every actual point-blowup chart
$(e,b)$ and every $\ell\ge0$,
\[
 H_{I^{\mathrm{norm}}_{e,b}}(\ell)\le H_I(\ell).
\]
The normalized member ideal is the unchanged actual whole analytic
ideal generated by genuine quotients of all original point-power members.
There is no supplied basis, convergence, numerical comparison or ideal
dictionary hypothesis.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual analytic axis specialization and whole finite initial ideal
inclusion already bound $H_{I^{\mathrm{norm}}_{e,b}}(\ell)$ by the value
of the dehomogenized finite initial ideal of $T(I)$. The preceding
genuine unit-coordinate equivalence identifies this with the value of
the actual translated finite initial ideal of $T(I)$ at the true point
$p$. The original translated-initial comparison bounds the latter value
by the unchanged original $H_I(\ell)$. Transitivity proves the assertion
at the arbitrary chosen order, and thus at every order.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same actual point comparison for the entire formal strict ideal of an analytic extension]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-extension-point-formal-strict-bennett}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-point-normalized-bennett-all-orders,hir:actual-unshifted-original-formal-hs-transfer,hir:actual-point-centre-whole-analytic-strict-generators}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointNormalizedBennett.genuineFormalHilbertSamuelValue_actual_point_strict_le_original}
\leanok
For every original analytic ideal $I$, the entire actual point formal
strict ideal of its whole Taylor extension has every genuine formal
Hilbert--Samuel value bounded by that of the unchanged whole extension.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The internally constructed actual analytic point-normalized generators
identify their entire Taylor extension with the existing actual formal
strict ideal. Whole original Taylor Hilbert--Samuel transport identifies
the corresponding actual quotient dimensions on both sides. The
preceding actual analytic inequality therefore gives the stated formal
inequality at every order for this original analytic extension.
\end{proof}

These statements concern the unchanged analytic normalized member ideal
and its proved entire formal strict extension. A statement about the
original geometric strict transform still needs the actual geometric
comparison. In complex dimension one the actual point blowup is the
identity, while the normalized member ideal can differ; that case must
be handled by its genuine geometric identity rather than an asserted
general ideal equality. Positive-dimensional permissible centres also
require their separate centre-compatibility theorem.
