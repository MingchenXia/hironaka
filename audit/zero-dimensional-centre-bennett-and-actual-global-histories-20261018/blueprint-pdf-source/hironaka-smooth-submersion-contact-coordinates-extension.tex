\section{One actual nested contact-coordinate flag and its genuine smooth lift}

\begin{lemma}[The genuine contact coordinate replacement preserves the whole original carrier]
\label{hir:submersion-contact-coordinates-original-flag}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionContactCoordinates.contactCoords_mem_original_plane,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionContactCoordinates.contactCoords_contact_slot}
\uses{mc:contact-hypersurface-along,hir:order-along-flat}
\leanok
In genuine original coordinates flattening $N$ onto the plane of dimension
$k$, replace one tangential coordinate $j<k$ by a true function $u$, moving
that coordinate to slot $l=k-1$. The actual contact-coordinate map lies in
the plane of dimension $k$ exactly on $N$, and its slot $l$ is the entire
original function $u$. These are whole equations on the actual flat domain,
not prescribed chart-output certificates.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original coordinate replacement swaps only $j$ and $l$, both strictly
below $k$. Every normal index $i\geq k$ is therefore fixed by that actual
permutation and is distinct from $l$. At each such index, the literal new
coordinate equals the original normal coordinate. Conversely every original
normal coordinate occurs at that same new normal index. Thus vanishing of
all actual new normal coordinates is precisely vanishing of all original
normal coordinates, equivalent to $N$ by the original genuine flattening.
The replaced slot itself is $u$ by the literal coordinate-map definition.
The already proved contact-coordinate equation gives plane dimension $k-1$
exactly on $N\cap V(u)$; this calculation retains EVERY other original slot.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ONLY original direction geometry internally creates one nested whole coordinate flag]
\label{hir:submersion-contact-coordinates-internal-original-chart}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionContactCoordinates.exists_original_contact_coordinates}

whole tangent direction $u$ on its open $U$ and an actual contact point,
internally produce $k>0$, $l=k-1$ and ONE true centred holomorphic coordinate
chart whose ENTIRE domain lies in $U$. On that domain the actual original

the plane of dimension $k-1$, and the literal coordinate $l$ equals $u$.
No caller chart, index, positive dimension, normal form or desired equality

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual original smoothness chooses true flattening coordinates at the
contact point. Coherence of the genuine tangent iterate gives holomorphicity
of $u$ throughout its actual open. Its real tangent-direction witness at the
zero gives a true nonzero tangent derivative. The actual field decomposition
internally chooses a tangential partial $j<k$ with nonzero value, so $k>0$
and $l=k-1$ exists. Form the genuine contact-coordinate map of node one.
Its real differential is injective by the original coordinate replacement

inverse-function producer therefore constructs a true local coordinate
inverse and recentres its value, with domain contained in this intersection
and an ENTIRE coordinate equality to the contact-coordinate map minus its
value at the point. The original chart was centred and $u$ vanishes at the
contact point, so that value is genuinely zero. Node one's whole original
carrier equation, its contact equation and its literal $u$ coordinate
therefore hold on the ENTIRE new domain. This is an internally constructed
joint flag, rather than separate independently chosen regularity charts.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The SAME nested flag lifts in one genuine fibre-first source chart]

\begin{proof}

original coordinate at its literal shifted index $t+j$, while the $t$ fibre

coordinate dictionary also gives image-domain containment and EVERY
retained original coordinate, not merely a stalk assertion. The actual
linear projection and inverse-point equality are the already proved true
adapted-chart producers. No points in an empty source demand fictitious
indices; an actual point itself supplies the only required dimension
comparison. Dimension zero is excluded only when the true nonzero tangent
derivative produces the genuine positive $k$.
\end{proof}
