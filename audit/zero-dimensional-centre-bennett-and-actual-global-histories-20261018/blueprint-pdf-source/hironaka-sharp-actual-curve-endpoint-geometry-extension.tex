\begin{lemma}[The actual terminal projection determines its true overlap open]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-endpoint-projection-overlap}
\uses{bcalc:tower-restrict,hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding,hir:sharp-actual-chosen-overlap-map-restriction}
\leanok

actual open $V$ equals the literal endpoint open used by the retained pairwise

open has this same underlying projection, at every padding cut.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}

Membership of an endpoint in the genuine inverse image of $V$ means that
its actual base image belongs to $V$. It already belongs to $U$ because the
first projection is a point of $U$. This is equivalent in both directions

of the pairwise history. For its padded projection, use the genuine append
projection equations, the actual identity projection of every real empty
tail, and both original restricted-projection point identities. Every
underlying point is unchanged; no step or ordered position is removed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual diagonal overlap is the whole endpoint and its transition is identity]

projection is asserted unique, and no deletion of empty positions is used.
\end{proof}
