\begin{lemma}[Actual normalized transverse member geometry supplies true order one]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-normalized-transverse-member-geometry-order-one}

\uses{hir:sharp-actual-transverse-boundary-coordinate-not-second-order,hir:sharp-original-member-product-actual-normal-form}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstAmbientStrictExponent.actual_normalized_transverse_member_geometry}

SNC member $H$, its TRUE normalized member ideal
$A=\operatorname{Res}_N\mathcal I_H$ is coherent, nonzero along EVERY
carrier point and genuinely relatively principal there. At EVERY
$x\in N\cap H$ its actual ordinary trace order is exactly one. No
generator, nonzero, order or transversal model certificate is supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}

ambient SNC coordinates supply a genuine local generator of
$\mathcal I_H$; BOTH relative-span substitutions give the SAME
normalized generator dictionary. At an incident point the actual free
coordinate and real carrier retraction give a genuine member germ which

the actual zero germ would witness second-order vanishing; thus it has
genuine frequent nonzero carrier values and supplies nonzero restriction.
At a nonincident point the true member-complement open supplies its unit
germ and actual nonzero carrier value. The real order-one cosupport
contains every incident point, while the internally constructed member
germ excludes the second-order cosupport. The genuine least-failing-order
definition therefore gives actual order one, without a global bound
premise or an assumed ordinary-order identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A true transverse member increases an actual relative principal factor exponent by one]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-transverse-member-product-exponent-add-one}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0087}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-normalized-transverse-member-geometry-order-one,hir:sharp-actual-relative-product-order-additivity-nonzero,hir:exponent-along}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstAmbientStrictExponent.actual_transverse_member_product_exponent_add_one}
\leanok
Let $B$ be any genuine coherent nonzero relatively principal ideal along
the same actual $N$. For the independently defined actual normalized
member $A$, at EVERY $x\in N\cap H$,
\[
 \nu_{AB}(x)=1+\nu_B(x),\qquad
 e_H^N(AB,x)=1+e_H^N(B,x).
\]
No product-order, exponent-shift or desired factor identity is a premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding producer internally supplies all actual normalized member
geometry. Genuine relative product-order additivity and actual member
order one give the first equality at EVERY incident carrier point; true
relative product geometry also constructs product nonzero witnesses.
For every integer $q$, apply the actual ordinary-order/cosupport
dictionary at each nearby point of $N\cap H$. The first equality gives
the genuine successor shift
$\operatorname{IsDivNear}_N(AB,H,q+1,x)$ iff
$\operatorname{IsDivNear}_N(B,H,q,x)$ in BOTH directions.
Choose a real carrier retraction at $x$. Genuine nonzero witnesses make
both actual exceptional exponents finite, and the independently proved
nearby-divisibility/actual-exponent equivalence applies. Divisibility at
the actual $B$ exponent gives the lower bound on the actual product
exponent. A product exponent larger than that exponent plus one would,
by the same genuine successor shift, give divisibility one above the
actual $B$ exponent, contradicting its true least-failing definition.
These are both numerical bounds; their equality proves the second
formula.
\end{proof}
