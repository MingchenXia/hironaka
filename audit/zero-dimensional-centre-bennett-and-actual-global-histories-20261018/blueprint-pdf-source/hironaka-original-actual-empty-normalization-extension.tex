Keep the original marked record, its entire indexed boundary and original head
$H:X\leftarrow A$. An actual empty blowup $\pi:B\to A$ may have a different
source manifold and need not be an identity. Keep its entire original suffix
$S:B\leftarrow Y$. The ordered-boundary route of
\cite{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality} is adapted by
internally normalizing the actual suffix through the empty blowup's genuine
biholomorphism, before deleting only that empty step. The original marked
record is never rebased and all actual later blowups remain recorded.

\begin{theorem}[The actual empty blowup supplies a whole suffix normalization]
\label{hir:original-actual-empty-whole-suffix-normalization}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:general-nc-open-actual-empty-blowup-isomorphism,hir:tower-isomorphism}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalActualEmptyNormalization.exists_actual_empty_step_normalization}
\leanok

original suffix $S$, there are a genuine normalized suffix $S'$ over $A$ and
an actual final biholomorphism $\chi:Y'\to Y$ such that $|S'|=|S|$ and the
ENTIRE normalized identity-inserted history $H\epsilon S'$ is isomorphic to
$H\pi S$ over the identity of the ORIGINAL base $X$, with final map $\chi$.
No biholomorphism, normalized history or tower comparison is a caller input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual empty blowup's universal uniqueness against the genuine identity
empty blowup constructs a biholomorphism $\varphi:B\to A$ whose underlying
map is exactly $\pi$. Transport the SAME entire $S$ through $\varphi^{-1}$.
At the empty suffix the normalized suffix is empty over $A$ and the final
map is $\varphi^{-1}$. At a next original centre and projection, pull back
the centre through the preceding final biholomorphism and compose the
projection with its inverse. The genuine blowup universal property gives
the normalized blowup and a commuting next-stage square. Its new source is
exactly the original source and its final biholomorphism is the identity.
Consequently every source stage after the first, and every later original
centre and projection, remain literal; the first centre and projection are
conjugated by the actual empty blowup's biholomorphism. This constructs a
whole suffix tower isomorphism and preserves its number of actual steps.
The original head is isomorphic to itself over identities. Extend this by
the identity empty step on the normalized side and the actual $\pi$ on the
original side, with intermediate map $\varphi^{-1}$. The square commutes
because $\pi\varphi^{-1}=\mathrm{id}$ and both centres are empty. Concatenate
with the constructed WHOLE suffix isomorphism. This proves the stated
whole-history comparison over the unchanged original base, including empty
suffixes and empty source manifolds.
\end{proof}
