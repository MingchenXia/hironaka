\begin{lemma}[Genuine nonzero on an actual zero-dimensional carrier forces the whole normalized ideal to be a unit]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-zero-dimensional-normalized-ideal-unit}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0088}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-first-good-trace-pure-lower-dimension}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstGoodCurveBase.actual_zero_dimensional_normalized_ideal_eq_unit}
\leanok
Let $S$ be an actual closed smooth carrier of pure dimension zero and
$I$ an actual coherent ideal. If its genuine carrier normalization is
nonzero along $S$ at EVERY actual point, then that ENTIRE normalized
ideal is the actual ambient unit ideal. Both full germ directions hold
at EVERY ambient point; no actual unit, nonzero value or selected
isolated neighborhood is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Actual regularity of dimension zero gives a true open neighborhood whose
intersection with $S$ is the single original point. At a point of $S$,
choose the genuine nonzero restricted member supplied by the hypothesis.
Its frequent nonzero points on $S$ must meet this true isolating
neighborhood and hence equal the original point. Thus that SAME member
has a nonzero actual value there. Its genuine holomorphic inverse makes
the constant one a member of the actual coherent normalized ideal.
Coherent multiplication then gives EVERY actual holomorphic germ. The
reverse direction is its true holomorphicity clause. Outside the closed
carrier the normalization is already a unit on a genuine open complement.
These two real cases prove equality of the WHOLE ambient ideals, retaining
all normal germs and empty carriers.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
