\section{Actual blowup fibres on a smooth carrier of dimension at most one}

We use the actual monomial charts of the analytic blowup and the actual
strict-transform equations, as in Bierstone--Milman\cite{bierstone-milman08-functoriality},
Section 2. The genuine fibre theorem and the actual constructed invariant's
per-sequence finiteness internally produce a globally locally finite and
locally resolving whole-cosupport iteration in carrier dimension at most one.
General higher-dimensional G3 and an analytic final direct-limit space remain open.

\begin{lemma}[Actual singleton strict-transform fibre in nested flat coordinates]
\label{hir:curve-strict-flat-fibre-subsingleton}
\uses{bcalc:strict-submanifold}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.subsingleton_fibre_of_flat_dimension_le_one}
\leanok
Suppose an actual blowup along $C$ has actual centred nested coordinates at
$x$, flattening $C$ to the zero plane and $N$ to the $k$-plane with $k\le1$.
Then the actual fibre over $x$ in the actual strict transform of $N$ has at
most one point.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If two points lie in that actual strict-transform fibre, apply the genuine
local monomial strict-transform theorem to each point. Each has a monomial
index $j$ strictly below $k$ and a local inverse $W$ taking its value in the
$k$-plane. Hence $k=1$ and $j=0$ for both points. Choose one actual holomorphic
lift of that common monomial chart, retaining its true projection equality.
The actual local inverse theorem recovers each point as the lift of its
actual $W$-value. Since the projection is $x$ and the original coordinates
are centred, the $j$-coordinate of this value is zero: the true monomial
projection leaves that coordinate unchanged. All other coordinates vanish
by membership in the $k$-plane. Thus both local inverse values are zero,
and both points equal the same actual monomial lift of zero. If $k=0$,
the required inequality $j<k$ is impossible, so the fibre is empty.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual blowup injectivity on a smooth curve carrier]
\label{hir:curve-strict-transform-projection-injective}
\uses{hir:curve-strict-flat-fibre-subsingleton,bcalc:nested-flattening}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.injOn_strictTransform_of_zero_dimensional_centre}
\leanok
Let $C\subseteq N$ be an actual centre whose every point is genuinely regular
of dimension zero. Let $N$ be an actual smooth carrier whose every
point has genuine dimension at most one. For any actual blowup along $C$,
its actual projection is injective on the actual strict transform of $N$.
The centre may be infinite and disconnected and the carrier may have mixed
zero- and one-dimensional components.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For two strict-transform points with the same projection, first consider a
projection outside $C$. The actual isomorphism of the blowup off its centre
makes the points equal. Over $C$, the common projection is a point of $N$.
Use its genuine regular dimension, at most one, and the genuine regular
dimension zero of $C$ to internally produce actual centred nested flattening
coordinates for $C\subseteq N$. Apply the preceding actual fibre theorem.
All choices are generated by the genuine submanifold geometry; no fibre
injectivity or compatibility certificate is an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine dimension-one invariant domains produce strict-carrier injectivity]
\label{hir:curve-domain-projection-injectivity}
\uses{hir:curve-strict-transform-projection-injective,hir:one-dimensional-actual-cosupport-dimension,hir:analytic-dimzero-all-points-regular}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.regularAtDim_zero_of_subset_cosupport_of_isInvDomain_one,Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.injOn_geoTransform_N_of_isInvDomain_one,Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.injOn_proj_N_of_isInvDomain_one}
\leanok
For a genuine dimension-at-most-one invariant domain, every subset of the
actual cosupport is genuinely regular of dimension zero at its points.
Every actual admissible blowup is therefore injective on its actual strict
carrier, and every actual geometric test-tower projection is injective on
its actual transformed carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The internally produced zero-dimensional analytic cosupport has a genuine
singleton neighbourhood at each of its points. Any actual subset containing
that point has the same germ, so transfer the actual regular dimension zero.
The genuine invariant-domain smooth carrier has pointwise dimension at most
one, and an admissible centre is a subset of this actual cosupport. The
preceding monomial producer gives injectivity for one actual blowup.
For a finite actual test tower, induct on its recorded blowups. Internally
produce the genuine domain data at its preceding stage. The last blowup is
injective on the new actual strict carrier. Its projection carries that
strict carrier into the preceding actual carrier, by continuity and closedness
of the latter. Apply the induction hypothesis to the equal composite
projections and then the last-blowup injectivity. The empty tower is the identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual restricted cosupport stays a subsingleton]
\label{hir:curve-singleton-open-cosupport-all-test-towers}
\uses{hir:curve-domain-projection-injectivity}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.subsingleton_cosupp_restrict_of_isInvDomain_one}
\leanok
If an open subset of the initial genuine dimension-at-most-one domain meets
its actual cosupport in at most one point, the actual cosupport along the
restriction of every actual test tower to that open set is a subsingleton.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine restriction dictionary identifies the restricted cosupport with
the actual trace of the transformed cosupport. Its projection lies in the
initial cosupport and in the given open subset. Thus any two of its points
have equal projected initial points by the actual initial subsingleton property.
They lie in the actual transformed carrier, where the preceding internally
produced tower-projection injectivity makes them equal. Equality of their
open-subset representatives follows from subtype injectivity. Neither a
canonical-prefix assumption nor a singleton-fibre certificate is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole-cosupport prefixes are genuinely canonical on each isolated branch]
\label{hir:curve-whole-cosupport-restriction-canonical}
\uses{hir:curve-singleton-open-cosupport-all-test-towers}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.WholeCosupportTower,Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.isCanonical_restrict_of_whole_cosupport_tower}
\leanok
For a genuine dimension-at-most-one domain, a finite actual test tower whose
every centre is the entire preceding actual cosupport restricts to a genuinely
canonical tower on each initial open singleton-cosupport neighbourhood. This
holds for any actual value function on those restricted towers.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At every restricted prefix the preceding theorem gives a true subsingleton
cosupport. Every function attains its maximum at every point of a subsingleton
set, so its actual maximum locus equals that entire set, including the empty
case. The actual restriction dictionary identifies the next actual centre
trace with this entire restricted cosupport. Induct on the recorded tower:
the empty tower is canonical, and every successor centre is the actual
maximum locus of the actual restricted value function. No comparison of
global and restricted invariant values or empty-step deletion is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual whole-cosupport iteration is finite over each compact set]
\label{hir:curve-whole-cosupport-compact-count-bound}
\uses{hir:curve-whole-cosupport-restriction-canonical,hir:one-dimensional-global-cosupport-growing-sequence,hir:analytic-dimzero-subsets-and-constancy,bcalc:growing-seq-tail,hir:canonical-centres}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.exists_compact_count_bound_of_whole_cosupport_sequence}
\leanok
Along any actual whole-cosupport growing sequence of genuine test towers of
a dimension-at-most-one domain, the actual numbers of blowup centres meeting
the inverse image of a given compact set are bounded.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The initial actual cosupport is closed and discrete. Its intersection with
the given compact set is therefore compact and discrete, hence finite.
For each of these finitely many actual points choose its genuine open
singleton-cosupport neighbourhood. The actual restriction of the growing
sequence is a growing sequence of genuine test towers, and the preceding
theorem proves all of its prefixes genuinely canonical for the actual
constructed dimension-one invariant of the restricted marked ideal.
The real per-sequence local-finiteness theorem of that invariant bounds
the centres meeting the compact singleton in this restricted sequence.
The genuine finite-tail restriction count theorem, at the initial stage,
sums these finitely many actual bounds and covers precisely the initial
cosupport over the compact set. Every later transformed cosupport lies
over that initial cosupport, so this gives the stated bound for all actual
prefixes. No global maximum and no global canonical-prefix property are
assumed for the whole-cosupport sequence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An actual locally resolving global whole-cosupport sequence in carrier dimension one]
\label{hir:curve-global-locally-resolving-sequence}
\uses{hir:curve-whole-cosupport-compact-count-bound,hir:one-dimensional-global-cosupport-growing-sequence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.exists_global_locally_resolving_sequence_of_isInvDomain_one,Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.exists_global_locally_finite_resolving_sequence_of_isInvDomain_one}
\leanok
In every ambient dimension every genuine dimension-at-most-one invariant
domain internally produces an actual global growing sequence of test towers
blowing up their entire preceding actual cosupport. For every compact subset
of the initial manifold, all sufficiently late actual transformed cosupports
miss its inverse image. Thus this sequence really resolves locally and its
actual centres meet each compact initial subset only finitely often.
This is a carrier-dimension-one result; general higher-dimensional G3 and the
construction of an analytic final direct-limit space remain open.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Internally construct the actual whole-cosupport growing sequence from the
genuine domain data. For each compact set the preceding producer bounds
its actual prefix centre counts. These natural counts are nondecreasing,
so their finite range has a maximum attained at some stage. All later counts
are equal to that maximum. Their actual one-step recurrence increments by
one exactly when the actual whole-cosupport centre at that stage meets
the inverse image of the compact set. Therefore no such intersection can
occur at a later stage. Since the actual next centre is the entire actual
transformed cosupport, every later cosupport itself misses that inverse
image. Around each base point choose a genuine compact neighbourhood. Its
eventual cosupport emptiness bounds every meeting index of the actual centre
image family by the corresponding stage number, giving indexed local
finiteness even with repetitions or empty steps. This supplies actual local resolving behaviour, rather than merely
a locally finite family or a preconstructed tower certificate. In ambient
dimension zero, the genuine regular carrier dimensions are at most the ambient
dimension, so the domain is genuinely dimension zero. Its actual cosupport
is empty throughout every test tower. The actual whole-cosupport iteration
therefore has empty centre images and resolves every compact set at stage zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One actual open resolving tail around every compact set]
\label{hir:curve-global-open-resolving-tail}
\uses{hir:curve-global-locally-resolving-sequence,hir:principalization-local}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCurveStrictFibres.exists_global_open_resolving_sequence_of_isInvDomain_one}
\leanok
The genuine dimension-at-most-one domain internally produces an actual global
whole-cosupport growing sequence such that every compact initial subset has
one actual open neighbourhood on which every sufficiently late actual prefix
is a geometric resolution of the actual restricted marked ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the internally constructed indexed locally finite, genuinely locally
resolving sequence. Each actual centre image is closed: its centre is the
actual analytic cosupport, and its actual prefix projection is proper.
For a compact initial subset, take a stage after which its inverse image
misses every actual cosupport. The union of all later actual centre images
is closed, because it is a subfamily of the actual indexed locally finite
closed family. Its complement is one actual open neighbourhood containing
that compact subset. No later actual cosupport projects into this complement.
The genuine transformed-cosupport restriction dictionary therefore makes
the cosupport of each sufficiently late actual restricted prefix empty.
The actual test-tower restriction theorem supplies its genuine geometric
test-tower proof, so each such actual finite prefix is a real resolution
over this one neighbourhood. No open-tail compatibility or final-limit
manifold certificate is assumed.
\end{proof}
