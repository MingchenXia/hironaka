\section{True earlier-mask avoidance along finite original Step One cuts}

\begin{definition}[Literal finite original whole Step One cut history]
\label{hir:original-finite-stepone-cut-history}
\uses{hir:original-finite-level-cut-history,hir:step-one-value-masked}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFiniteStepOneCutMaskAvoidance.IsStepOneCutTower,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFiniteStepOneCutMaskAvoidance.stepOneCutTower_levelCut}
\leanok
Fix positive ambient dimension $n$, a depth $k$ and the original marked ideal.
The reflexive tower is a whole Step One cut history. An actual successor
is one if its prefix is one, its centre is actually original-admissible,
and EVERY old cosupport reference is at most EVERY centre point in the
UNCHANGED original Step One value. This literal recipe has no lower TEST,
centre-maximal-locus equality, next canonicality or desired-value field.
Its actual natural-level recipe is internally obtained at every prefix
by the true first coordinate of Step One.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual finite history. The reflexive recipe is literal.
At each actual successor retain the old level recipe and the real supplied
centre admissibility. The unchanged Step One first coordinate is its
original level, so lexicographic projection of the supplied whole cut gives
the actual level cut. Append the SAME genuine blowup, with every empty
position retained. This proves the conversion without any lower data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual earlier-mask strict value comparison needs no canonical prefix]
\label{hir:original-finite-stepone-cut-earlier-mask-strict-value}
\uses{hir:original-finite-level-cut-current-lower-tests,hir:step-one-value-masked-basic,hir:step-one-mask-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFiniteStepOneCutMaskAvoidance.actual_stepone_lt_of_earlier_mask}
\leanok
For positive ambient dimension and original DOMAIN$(k+1)$ of actual
maximal order, take a genuine whole Step One cut history written as
$T_0\cdot S$, an actual base point of level $s$ at $T_0$, and an actual
current cosupport point over its computed earlier lower mask. Its
unchanged Step One value is strictly below the value of ANY actual point
out of the level-$s$ regime whose level is at least $s$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual level-cut lower-geometry producer supplies the real current
mask membership at each nonspecial original cosupport point. The original
finite-branch definition therefore puts its Step One value strictly below
its own level with outer top; no lower CAN or validity assertion is needed.
The earlier mask bounds its level by $s$. At strictly smaller level,
lexicographic order gives the result. At equal level the genuine actual
bar TEST and mask-regime dictionary put the point in the level-$s$ regime,
so it is nonspecial and has the just-proved strict outer-top bound. Any
out-of-regime point of level at least $s$ has Step One value at least
$(s,\mathrm{top})$ by the literal original SPECIAL/level branches.
Transitivity proves the asserted strict comparison. All current lower
membership used here was internally constructed from the finite recipe.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The real intermediate centres avoid the entire earlier mask]
\label{hir:original-finite-stepone-cut-centres-avoid-earlier-mask}
\uses{hir:original-finite-stepone-cut-earlier-mask-strict-value,hir:step-one-value-masked-basic,hir:step-one-nearby}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFiniteStepOneCutMaskAvoidance.stepOneCutTower_disjoint_earlier_mask,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFiniteStepOneCutMaskAvoidance.stepOneCutTower_centresAvoid_earlier_mask}
\leanok
In the preceding original setting, suppose a genuine whole Step One cut
history is $T_0\cdot(S\cdot\sigma)$ and an actual cosupport reference
before $\sigma$ lies out of the level-$s$ regime with level at least $s$.
Then EVERY actual centre of $S\cdot\sigma$ avoids the genuine earlier mask
of the given level-$s$ point at $T_0$. All empty positions are retained.
No canonicality, avoidance, lower TEST or comparison certificate is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If the last centre met the earlier mask, real original admissibility would
put that trace point in the actual old cosupport. The preceding strict
comparison makes its value less than the given out-of-regime reference.
The literal whole Step One cut of this actual successor gives the reverse
weak inequality. Thus the last centre is disjoint from the entire earlier
mask; exact equality with a whole maximum locus was never used.

Induct on the actual finite intermediate history. For a reflexive prefix,
this disjointness gives the one genuine successor. For a longer prefix,
the actual original/bar TEST and genuine projection theorem take the real
out-of-regime cosupport reference to the preceding stage, retaining its
level lower bound and out-of-regime property. The original cut recipe
restricts literally to this true prefix. The induction hypothesis gives
avoidance of all its centres; the last-centre result gives the remaining
one. This is the genuine CentresAvoid predicate for the full intermediate
history, with every empty centre still present.
The result supplies actual between-run geometry; full fixed-reference
numerical lower identities and a terminating global driver remain separate.
\end{proof}
