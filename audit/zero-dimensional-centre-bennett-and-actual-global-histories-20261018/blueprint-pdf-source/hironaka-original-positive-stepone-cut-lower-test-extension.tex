\section{A genuine positive Step One cut constructs the next lower TEST}

A current lower point without a lower TEST does not suffice for recursive
resolution-invariant consumers. Here old canonicality supplies the actual
old lower TEST, and a genuine next upper centre above the ENTIRE old Step
One reference value constructs its actual next lower admissibility. The
next upper or lower history is not assumed canonical. All actual history positions, including empty centres, are retained; the
lower divisor is exactly the original selected-member construction.

\begin{theorem}[The original positive Step One cut constructs the frozen lower TEST]
\label{hir:original-positive-stepone-cut-actual-lower-test}
\uses{hir:original-frozen-recursive-lower-domain-whole-cosupport,hir:step-one-canonical-tail,hir:step-one-value-masked-basic,bcalc:member-restrict-admissible,hir:step-one-compare,hir:open-restriction-tower,hir:step-one-seq}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPositiveStepOneCutLowerTest.original_positive_stepone_cut_lower_test}
\leanok
Let $n>0$, let $M$ have original DOMAIN$(k+1)$ and maximal order, and let
$T$ be a CAN prefix for the unchanged original Step One invariant built
from the original depth-$k$ invariant. Let $y$ be a nonspecial current
cosupport point with actual positive level $s+1$. Split $T=HS$ at the
actual start of this original level regime, and retain its actual original
positive mask $W$ and selected lower record $J$ at the old reference head.
Take a genuine next ORIGINAL-admissible centre $C$ and actual blowup $\pi$.
Assume the unchanged old Step One value at $y$ is at most its value at
EVERY point of $C$. Internally construct
\[
 J\in\mathrm{DOMAIN}(k),\qquad
 (S\pi)|_W\text{ is an actual TEST of }J,\qquad
 J_{(S\pi)|_W}\in\mathrm{DOMAIN}(k).
\]
No lower TEST, desired centre containment or lower admissibility is supplied.
These are the SAME old reference mask and marked record, not a newly chosen
post-step reference.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original canonical Step One bridge first constructs the actual upper
TEST and its actual coefficient-homogenized TEST and cosupport equality.
The original canonical-tail producer supplies the old masked lower DOMAIN,
CAN and hence its actual TEST, together with its exact entire current
cosupport dictionary. The actual start-of-regime theorem gives the real
head reference point in its mask, head cosupport and level $s+1$.

Take an arbitrary point of the actual trace of $C$ in the old endpoint
mask. Actual original admissibility puts it in the upper cosupport. The
whole Step One cut and its literal lexicographic first coordinate imply
that its level is at least $s+1$. The proved actual mask bound gives level
at most $s+1$. Thus its actual level equals $s+1$, and the old lower
cosupport dictionary places this point in the genuine old lower cosupport.
The exact positive-member cosupport dictionary then puts it on EVERY
strict transform of the originally selected members. This is actual
geometric centre containment, proved before lower admissibility.

The genuine upper TEST restricted to $W$ supplies the actual closed smooth
trace centre and normal crossings with the original restricted whole
boundary. Apply the proved positive-member admissibility equivalence to
this actual centre and the just proved selected strict-member containments.
The equivalence uses the actual coefficient ideal and the actual retained
boundary: omitted selected members contain the centre, so it proves the
precise lower normal-crossings condition as well as the whole cosupport
condition. Consequently this SAME restricted actual blowup appends the old
lower TEST. The initial actual lower DOMAIN and this actual TEST then give
the current lower DOMAIN by genuine domain transport. Nothing asserts that
the appended centre is the lower maximum or that the new history is CAN.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Both next lower cosupport directions retain the old positive level]
\label{hir:original-positive-stepone-cut-next-lower-cosupport}
\uses{hir:original-positive-stepone-cut-actual-lower-test,hir:step-one-canonical-tail,hir:step-one-compare,hir:open-restriction-tower,hir:regime-run}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPositiveStepOneCutLowerTest.original_positive_stepone_cut_next_cosupport}
\leanok
Under exactly the preceding original hypotheses, at EVERY actual point
of the new endpoint mask,
\[
 z\in\operatorname{cosupp}J_{(S\pi)|_W}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 z\in\operatorname{cosupp}M_{T\pi}
 \ \text{and}\ \operatorname{level}(M,T\pi,z)=s+1.
\]
The old level $s+1$, old head and old mask are fixed. There is no assertion
that a new actual regime has not reset, and no new lower membership is an
input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First construct the actual extended lower TEST by the preceding theorem.
Old canonicality and actual original next admissibility give the actual
extended upper TEST; coefficient-homogenized equivalence transports that
TEST and its entire current cosupport. Apply the proved masked lower
cosupport theorem to the SAME old head and the literal tail with the new
actual step. Its hypotheses are now genuine constructed TESTs, and the old
head reference still has its proved cosupport, mask membership and level
$s+1$. The literal tower head/tail equation identifies their append with
$T\pi$. The resulting equivalence supplies BOTH directions for every
actual new point, followed by the actual coefficient-homogenized
cosupport equality back to the original record. It does not assume any
new point membership or regime equation. General noncanonical full-value
naturality, zero-contact comparison and global preparation termination
remain separate producers.
\end{proof}
