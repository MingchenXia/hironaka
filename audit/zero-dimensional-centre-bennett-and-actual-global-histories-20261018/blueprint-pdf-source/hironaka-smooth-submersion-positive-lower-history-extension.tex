\section{Actual positive Step One lower histories under arbitrary smooth maps}

Fix an original well-formed marked ideal $M$ on $X^n$, its actual
coefficient-homogenized test history $T$, and an actual smooth Cartesian
history $Q$ over $f:Y^m\to X^n$, with actual final map $g$.
No surjectivity, positive source compactness, injectivity on global boundary
sets, desired lower-record comparison or restricted-tail certificate is
assumed. Every empty indexed boundary entry and original ordering is retained.

\begin{lemma}[Reflection of original-member membership through a point]
\label{hir:smooth-positive-lower-through-member-reflection}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionPositiveLowerHistory.mem_of_preimage_mem_map_through}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:submersion-nc-entire-snc-preimage,bcalc:snc-simultaneous}
Let $E$ be an actual SNC divisor and $E_0$ a list of its actual members.
For a smooth map $g$, a member $H\in E$ through $g(z)$ whose whole preimage
belongs to $E_0$'s preimage list already belongs to $E_0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose $H_0\in E_0$ with $g^{-1}H_0=g^{-1}H$ and retain their actual
indices $i,j$ in $E$. Smooth pullback supplies the SNC structure of the
entire indexed list $g^{-1}E$. Its indexed members $i,j$ pass through $z$
and agree as whole sets, hence as germs. The actual SNC germ separation
theorem gives $i=j$. Thus $H=H_0\in E_0$. No reflection is claimed for
members missing $z$; coincident empty preimages remain literal entries.
\end{proof}

\label{hir:smooth-positive-lower-whole-member-record}

filter-map theorem identifies the entire surviving indexed divisor in
its original order, with multiplicities and empty entries. These four
field equalities identify the entire original marked record.
\end{proof}
