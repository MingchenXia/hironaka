\section{Actual analytic gluing over saturated Hausdorff base windows}

The following extra hypotheses describe gluing over the same original base.
They are not consequences of arbitrary analytic open overlap data: gluing two
lines along their punctured lines gives the line with two origins and fails
these saturated-window hypotheses. No canonical centre or invariant comparison
is assumed, and no original Hironaka goal is closed here.

\begin{definition}[Original saturated base windows]
\label{hir:disjoint-analytic-saturated-base-windows}
\uses{hir:actual-two-model-overlap-isomorphisms}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.SaturatedBase}
\leanok
Let $A$ be the original two-model holomorphic overlap datum, with its actual
closed analytic carriers $C_i$, $i\in\{0,1\}$. Let $X$ be a Hausdorff space.
An original saturated base datum consists of open sets $W_i\subset X$ and
continuous maps $p_i:C_i\to X$ landing in $W_i$, such that for $i\ne j$ the
actual carrier overlap is exactly $p_i^{-1}(W_j)$ and its actual original
transition preserves $p_i$. These are ordinary maps, opens and subset equations;
there is no glued object, separation certificate or sheaf morphism in the input.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Actual continuous projection and entire chart ranges]
\label{hir:disjoint-analytic-saturated-glued-projection}
\uses{hir:actual-two-model-native-glue-data,hir:disjoint-analytic-saturated-base-windows}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.topData}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.gluedTop}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.chartMap}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.gluedProjection}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.projection_chart}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.chart_range_saturated}
\leanok
On the original native topological gluing of $A$, the maps $p_i$ give a genuine
continuous map $p$ with $p\circ\iota_i=p_i$. Moreover the entire original chart
range satisfies $\operatorname{range}(\iota_i)=p^{-1}(W_i)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Reuse the existing original two-model locally-ringed-space gluing datum and
forget to topological spaces. On diagonal overlaps compatibility is identity.
On off-diagonal overlaps it is exactly the given ordinary transition/base
compatibility, so the actual multicoequalizer universal property constructs
$p$ and gives its stated equations. Every chart point projects into its own
window, proving one range inclusion. For the converse, represent an arbitrary
glued point by a point of chart $j$, using genuine joint surjectivity. If
$j=i$ it is already in the range. Otherwise its projection lying in $W_i$
places that original point in the actual $j,i$ overlap by saturation. Its
actual transition is an original point of chart $i$, and the original gluing
relation identifies its image with the same glued point. This proves the
other inclusion on the whole chart range, not merely near a chosen point.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine separation and second countability]
\label{hir:disjoint-analytic-saturated-glued-separation}
\uses{hir:disjoint-analytic-saturated-glued-projection}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.gluedTop_t2}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.gluedTop_secondCountable}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.overlapGraph}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.overlapGraph_eq_equalizer}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.overlapGraph_isClosed}
\leanok
The actual topological gluing is Hausdorff and second countable. In particular
the actual off-diagonal original overlap graph is closed in $C_0\times C_1$.
No separation or second-countability property of the glued target is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For distinct glued points with distinct $p$-images, choose disjoint open
neighbourhoods of their images in the original Hausdorff base and pull them
back by the actual continuous $p$. For two distinct points with the same
$p$-image, choose an actual chart containing the first point. The entire
range equation just proved puts the second point in the same actual chart.
That chart is a closed analytic subset of an original coordinate open, hence
Hausdorff. Separate the two original representatives there. The canonical
chart inclusion is a genuine open embedding, so the images of these two
open sets are disjoint open neighbourhoods of the two glued points. This
constructs Hausdorff separation in both cases.

The actual map from the disjoint union of the two original carriers to the
gluing is continuous, open and surjective: each canonical inclusion is a
continuous open embedding, and actual joint surjectivity supplies surjectivity.
Each original carrier is second countable, hence so is their finite disjoint
union. The images of a countable source basis under an open quotient map
form a countable basis of the target, proving genuine second countability.
Finally identify the off-diagonal transition graph with the equalizer of the
two canonical chart maps on $C_0\times C_1$. A genuine transition gives equal
glued images by the native gluing condition. Conversely equal glued images
have equal images under the actual continuous projection. Saturation and
the second point's base-window membership place the first point in the
actual overlap. Its genuine transition has that same glued image, and the
second canonical chart map is injective, so the transition is the original
second point. This proves both directions of the actual graph equality. Their maps are continuous and the derived target is Hausdorff,
so this graph is closed. The original transition and its inverse are used
only on the actual analytic carriers; no ambient inverse chart is asserted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual glued reduced complex analytic space]
\label{hir:disjoint-analytic-saturated-glued-space}
\uses{hir:disjoint-analytic-saturated-glued-separation,hir:actual-two-model-complex-structural-compatibility,hir:reduced-analytic-original-two-model-gluing}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.gluedNative}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.underlyingGluingIso}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.underlyingGluingHomeomorph}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.gluedNative_t2}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.gluedNative_secondCountable}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.actualSaturatedAnalyticSpace}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.actualSaturatedChartMap}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointAnalyticSaturatedGluing.actualSaturatedChartMap_properties}
\leanok
The original native locally-ringed-space gluing, with its actual descended
complex structural map, is an object of the repository's category of reduced
complex analytic spaces. Its original chart maps are open immersions over the
same complex point and jointly cover the entire glued object.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Reuse the existing native reduced-analytic gluing producer, whose explicit
Hausdorff hypothesis is now discharged by the preceding genuine theorem.
The existing original overlap maps already preserve the actual structural
morphisms to the complex point. Their proved compatibility is the exact
multicoequalizer compatibility condition, so the actual native universal
property constructs a structural morphism from the unchanged locally ringed
gluing, with the genuine chart/structural-map equations. The canonical
forgetful gluing isomorphism identifies its underlying space with the actual
topological gluing considered above. Transport the proved Hausdorffness and
second countability through this genuine canonical homeomorphism. The native
canonical chart maps are actual open immersions and jointly surjective by
the existing gluing theorem. Each source is its original closed analytic
coordinate model, and its structural equation is the one just constructed.
At every glued point choose its actual chart representative. These facts give
all fields of the original local-analytic-model predicate and therefore the
native reduced complex analytic space object, without a desired-object or
isomorphism certificate supplied by the caller.
\end{proof}
