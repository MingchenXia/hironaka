\section{Actual prescribed blowups and the entire mark-one controlled split}

Every controlled transform below has its ORIGINAL mark one. The old ideal
is the actual source restriction of a coherent ideal and may be nonradical.
Only the regular full normal plane is identified with its vanishing ideal;
the old controlled ideal is never replaced by a radical strict-support ideal.

\begin{lemma}[Whole controlled transport over prescribed genuine blowups]
\label{hir:disjoint-actual-controlled-transport}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointActualControlledSplit.controlled_pullback_over}
\leanok
For genuine blowups $\sigma:P\to N$ and $\rho:Z\to N$ of the same original
centre and a genuine biholomorphism $E:Z\to P$ with $\sigma E=\rho$, the whole
pullback by $E$ of the original coherent admissible ideal's mark-one controlled
transform equals its actual mark-one controlled transform by $\rho$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Actual total transform composition and $\sigma E=\rho$ identify the whole
total ideals. The exceptional ideals are the total pullbacks of the SAME
original centre ideal; the same composition therefore identifies them, and
hence their first powers. Original admissibility gives the actual factorization
of each total ideal by its invertible exceptional ideal and its coherent
controlled ideal. Pull the $\sigma$ factorization through $E$; whole coherent
product pullback and the actual exceptional equality yield the factorization
for $\rho$ with the pulled-back controlled ideal as remaining factor. Genuine
invertible-factor uniqueness identifies that coherent factor with the actual
controlled transform by the prescribed $\rho$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual current cosupport lies on the genuine strict full contact]
\label{hir:disjoint-actual-controlled-strict-contact}
\uses{hir:disjoint-actual-controlled-transport}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointActualControlledSplit.current_cosupport_mem_strict_contact}
\leanok
Let $N$ be closed regular and contain the actual ambient blowup centre $C$.
If the original coherent ambient ideal $J$ contains $\mathcal I_N$, every
actual mark-one controlled-cosupport point lies in the entire geometric
strict transform of $N$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Pullback and the original colon are monotone in their numerator. Thus the
controlled transform of $\mathcal I_N$ is contained in the controlled transform
of $J$. The actual mark-one controlled transform of the regular normal ideal
is precisely the vanishing ideal of the actual geometric strict $N$.
Every member of the ambient controlled ideal vanishes at a point of its
mark-one cosupport. Hence all members of the strict-contact vanishing ideal
vanish there. The strict contact is closed, so its genuine vanishing ideal's
zero locus is exactly that contact. This proves membership without a
strict-contact certificate or radicalization of the old ideal.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual whole nonradical controlled trace and reconstruction]
\label{hir:disjoint-actual-whole-controlled-split}
\uses{hir:disjoint-actual-controlled-strict-contact,hir:disjoint-actual-controlled-transport,hir:disjoint-matched-whole-coherent-trace,hir:actual-closed-contact-full-restricted-blowup,hir:relative-normal-whole-hadamard,hir:disjoint-same-flattening-normal-kernel}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointActualControlledSplit.exists_actual_controlled_split}
\leanok
Let $N\subset A$ be closed regular of dimension $d$ in dimension $d+t$.
Let $C=\operatorname{val}_N(D)$, let $\pi$ be its genuine ambient blowup, and
let $\rho$ be a prescribed genuine blowup of the original intrinsic centre
$D$ on the SAME native $N$. Let $J$ be coherent with
$\mathcal I_N\subset J\subset\mathcal I_C$, and set
$I=\operatorname{val}_N^*J$, without any radicality hypothesis.
At an actual controlled-cosupport point $y$, suppose the ordinary source
joint flattening at $\pi(y)$ identifies $C,N$ with the coordinate $k,d$ planes
and is centered at zero. Internally construct the actual strict-contact
biholomorphism $E$ over $\rho$, the genuine ambient chart $\Theta$ and a
matched original intrinsic chart $\Psi$ at $z=E^{-1}(y)$. Then
\[
 \operatorname{map}(\operatorname{planeTrace})(J'_\Theta)=I'_\Psi,
 \qquad
 J'_\Theta=\operatorname{comap}(\operatorname{planeTrace})(I'_\Psi),
\]
where $J'$ and $I'$ are the ENTIRE actual mark-one controlled ideals of the
prescribed $\pi$ and $\rho$. No target chart, trace, ideal split or comparison
is an input. This is the local producer over a source centre point, not a
complete canonical-history comparison.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding lemma places $y$ on the actual strict contact. Its canonical
native regular atlas and the two genuine blowup universal properties construct
$E$ over $\rho$. Apply actual strict-contact blowup coordinates to the original
source joint flattening and the derived contact membership. This constructs
$\Theta$ with its ENTIRE local strict-contact plane equation. The matched
coherent trace theorem constructs $\Psi$ from this same $\Theta$ and this
actual $E$, and identifies the whole normal trace of every coherent ambient
ideal with its actual intrinsic pullback.

Apply actual whole closed-contact controlled restriction to $J$ with its
original admissibility, retaining mark one. The intrinsic original ideal is
exactly the source restriction $I$ by definition. Apply the preceding genuine
controlled transport through $E$. Thus the actual pullback used by the matched
trace theorem is precisely the controlled ideal for the prescribed $\rho$,
including all nonradical structure. This proves the first equality.

The controlled ideal of $J$ contains the vanishing ideal of the strict full
contact, as in the membership argument. Every normal coordinate of $\Theta$
is an actual local vanishing germ of this contact, and its entire adapted
germ is the corresponding original normal coordinate germ. Thus the whole
normal ideal lies in $J'_\Theta$. Relative Hadamard division identifies that
ideal with the entire kernel of actual plane trace. The trace is surjective,
so the comap of the map of $J'_\Theta$ is $J'_\Theta$ plus that kernel, hence
is $J'_\Theta$ itself. Substitute the proved entire old-controlled trace to
obtain the second equality. No all-orders saturation or vanishing ideal of
the old singular support is used.
\end{proof}
