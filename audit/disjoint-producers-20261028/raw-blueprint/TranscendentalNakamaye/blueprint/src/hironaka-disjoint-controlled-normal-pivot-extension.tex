\section{The actual mark-one controlled normal generator}

All controlled transforms in this section have the fixed mark one. This is
not exceptional saturation over all orders: for $I=(x^2)$ and $J=(x^2,t)$,
the $x$-pivot transform is $(x,t')$, whereas division over all orders would
include $x^2/x^2=1$. No radicalization of the original ideal is used.

\begin{lemma}[The whole actual normal quotient]
\label{hir:disjoint-controlled-normal-quotient}
\uses{hir:actual-centre-weighted-chart-stalk}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointControlledNormalPivot.normal_quotient_mem}
\leanok
Let $U\subset\mathbb C^{d+1}$ contain zero, let the original coordinate centre
be given by $x_k=\cdots=x_d=0$, and let $\pi$ be a genuine blowup of this
centre. Let $J$ be any coherent original ideal on $U$ containing the last
coordinate germ at $\pi(y)=0$. In any actual native blowup chart at $y$,
with owner $e$, direction $b$ and centered coordinates, the entire adapted
mark-one controlled ideal contains $1$ if $e=d$, and contains $t'+b_d$
if $e\ne d$. The chart is required to have its actual original blowup square;
no transformed-ideal comparison is supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual chart square identifies the adapted germ of the total transform
of the last source coordinate with the literal polynomial blowup pullback.
Source membership gives membership of this whole germ in the actual total
ideal. If the owner is $d$, this pullback is the exceptional coordinate $a$.
Otherwise it is $a(t'+b_d)$. The genuine exceptional-divisor stalk theorem
identifies the entire exceptional ideal with the principal ideal $(a)$ in
this same chart. Whole coherent adapted-colon formation identifies the
actual mark-one controlled stalk with the colon of its actual total stalk
by $(a)$. Thus $1$, respectively $t'+b_d$, belongs to that entire colon.
The coherent source ideal and actual exceptional ideal suffice for this
argument; no admissibility or equality of target ideals is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Native normal-owner exclusion and centered normal containment]
\label{hir:disjoint-controlled-normal-pivot-exclusion}
\uses{hir:disjoint-controlled-normal-quotient,hir:relative-normal-whole-hadamard}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointControlledNormalPivot.exists_actual_normal_free_controlled_chart}
\leanok
Assume $k\le d$. At every point $y$ over zero in the actual mark-one controlled
cosupport of $J$, the genuine global blowup internally supplies an actual
native chart whose owner satisfies $k\le e<d$, whose last direction is zero,
and whose entire adapted controlled ideal contains the centered last normal
coordinate. In particular the whole kernel of restriction to the centered normal plane
lies in this controlled ideal. At a point represented by a normal-owner chart the adapted controlled
stalk is the unit ideal; that point therefore cannot lie in the actual
controlled cosupport.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine coordinate-centre chart theorem chooses a native owner, direction
and centered holomorphic chart, retaining the original blowup map square.
Every member of the actual mark-one controlled ideal vanishes at $y$, by the
original definition of cosupport. Every germ of its entire adapted ideal
therefore has zero evaluation: its generators have this property under the
actual inverse chart at zero, and evaluation preserves finite sums and
products. The preceding normal-quotient theorem excludes the last coordinate
as owner, since its unit would evaluate to one. Together with the actual
owner bound and $k\le d$, this gives $k\le e<d$. The same theorem places
$t'+b_d$ in the actual controlled ideal. Evaluation at zero is $b_d$, so the
actual cosupport forces $b_d=0$. Thus the centered normal generator itself
belongs to the entire controlled ideal; closure under holomorphic multiples
then gives containment of its whole principal ideal. This principal ideal
is exactly the full kernel of the actual normal-plane trace, by relative
Hadamard division, giving the asserted entire kernel containment.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
For the natural full ambient ideal $J=I+(t)$ the original normal-membership
hypothesis is automatic, for arbitrary coherent, possibly nonradical $I$.
This result does not identify the remaining trace with the controlled ideal
on a prescribed original base blowup. That matching native-chart trace and
whole reconstruction remain a separate obligation; no original ambient
independence or canonical-history theorem is concluded here.
\end{remark}
