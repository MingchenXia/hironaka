\section{Empty-boundary canonical values on an actual closed section}
The target is equality of the actual recursively defined invariant families,
with the same index, on the genuine closed-section carrier and its native base.
The original source domain internally gives the base domain; no invariant
value, selected lower value or comparison history is an input. The conclusion concerns empty boundaries and empty histories; comparison of born-boundary histories and intrinsic descent remain separate obligations.

\begin{lemma}[Original regularity through a native subspace isomorphism]
\label{hir:actual-section-regularity-native-iso}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualSectionInitialValues.regularAtDim_of_actual_subspace_iso}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualSectionInitialValues.actualSectionSubspaceIso}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualSectionInitialValues.regularAtDim_actual_section_iff}
\leanok
An isomorphism over the complex point between the actual reduced subspaces
of open vector models preserves every original ambient regularity dimension.
In particular, $A\subseteq U$ is regular of dimension $k$ at $x$ if and only
if its actual closed-section image is regular of dimension $k$ at $s(x)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An original regular point of dimension $k$ supplies an actual native manifold
open chart from an open set in $\mathbb C^k$, whose image contains that point.
Compose this chart with the given native isomorphism. An isomorphism is an
open immersion, and composition with the original open immersion is an open
immersion. The composite still lies over the complex point, by the original
chart's scalar square and the given scalar square. Its base map carries the
chosen chart point to the specified image point. The genuine native-chart
regularity converse therefore gives original ambient regularity of dimension
$k$ at that image. Apply the same argument to the inverse isomorphism for the
reverse implication. For a section, its actual holomorphic section and projection
restrict to mutually inverse subset maps, hence give the native isomorphism;
there is no ambient biholomorphism assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual special-locus dictionary in the successor induction]
\label{hir:actual-section-codimension-one-dictionary}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualSectionInitialValues.source_cosupport_eq_lifted}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualSectionInitialValues.section_codimOnePart_iff}
\leanok
For an original whole ideal $J$ with holomorphic members, every actual support
$\operatorname{cosupp}_{s(A)}(J,d)$ is exactly the closed-section image of
$\operatorname{cosupp}_{A}(s^*J,d)$. Their actual codimension-one parts relative
to the respective carriers correspond under the same section.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Composition by the genuine section pulls back each ambient point-ideal power.
Thus an ambient order-$q$ representative of a function along $s(A)$ pulls back
to an order-$q$ representative along $A$. Conversely compose an order-$q$
representative on the base with the true projection. On the entire same lifted
carrier its equality with the source function follows from $s(p(z))=z$.
For whole ideals, every original member supplies its genuine pullback member;
in the reverse direction substitute this scalar order dictionary into each
finite-span generator of the whole pullback. This identifies every order
threshold, hence every support. Every point of a source support lies on the
lifted carrier and equals $s(p(z))$, giving equality of the entire sets.
The actual codimension-one part consists precisely of a carrier regularity
of dimension $k+1$ together with support regularity of dimension $k$.
Apply the preceding native regularity equivalence separately to these two
sets, for each $k$, and retain the same existential $k$ in both directions.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Tangent descent on the genuine open carrier]
\label{hir:actual-open-section-tangent-descent}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualSectionInitialValues.open_section_tangent_descent}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualSectionInitialValues.open_section_regular_contact}
\leanok
Let $A$ be closed regular in the original open $U$, and let $J$ be coherent on
$p^{-1}U$. A genuine tangent direction $u$ along $s(A)$, vanishing at $s(x)$,
restricts to a genuine tangent direction $u\circ s$ along $A$ on some actual
open neighbourhood $W$ of $x$. No closed regularity in the whole vector space
is imposed on $A$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual flattening $\Phi$ for $A$ in $U$ at $x$. Its true product
flattening is $(\Phi\circ p,q)$; its source is contained in the actual open
ambient chart target, because $\Phi$'s source is contained in the chart target
of $U$. Its carrier plane has exactly the original dimension $k$ of $A$.
The source direction gives an actual tangent field with nonzero derivative at
$s(x)$. In this product flattening some partial with index $j<k$ is nonzero.
Since $k\le n$, this index is an actual base index. The genuine open
closed-section partial identity identifies it with the same base partial of
$u\circ s$. This identity is used only on the actual flattening target.
Holomorphy makes this base partial continuous near $x$, so it stays nonzero on
an actual open neighbourhood. Intersect that neighbourhood with the section
preimage of the source tangent open and the true domain of $\Phi$.
On this intersection the whole iterated-derivative closed-section equality
puts $u\circ s$ in the actual tangent ideal at every point. The actual flat
coordinate field in the chosen base direction is tangent to $A$ and has the
nonzero derivative just retained. These are precisely all fields of a genuine
base tangent direction. Its zero carrier is closed regular in the constructed
open neighbourhood by the original tangent-contact regularity theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The projected representative stays in the original domain]
\label{hir:actual-section-projected-domain}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualSectionInitialValues.section_projected_domain}
\leanok
For an empty-boundary original domain record $(A,[],I,d)$ in $U$, the
record $(s(A),[],p^*I,d)$ on the full genuine product open belongs to the
same index domain.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The source carrier is closed regular by the actual product flattening of the
original base carrier. Coherence pulls back under the actual holomorphic
projection. The empty list is SNC and transverse to every closed regular
carrier. At $z\in s(A)$ write $z=s(p(z))$. The base dimension bound and the
actual section regularity equivalence give the same bound at $z$.
For nonzero restricted ideals choose a genuine base ideal member $g$ which is
nonzero at points of $A$ arbitrarily near $p(z)$. The actual projection
pullback contains $g\circ p$. If this member vanished on the lifted carrier
throughout some neighbourhood of $s(p(z))$, pulling that neighbourhood back
by the continuous actual section would make $g$ vanish on $A$ near $p(z)$,
contradicting the chosen base member. Thus the required source member and
frequent nonvanishing are internally produced. The mark remains positive and
unchanged. These are all four fields of the ordinary invariant domain.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original source domain internally gives the native base domain]
\label{hir:actual-section-original-source-domain}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualSectionInitialValues.section_domain_of_original_source}
\leanok
If the original empty-boundary record $(s(A),[],J,d)$ is in the ordinary
index-$q$ domain, then $(A,[],s^*J,d)$ is in that same original domain.
No base domain or comparison certificate is an input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The inverse image of the actual closed source carrier under the continuous
section is exactly $A$, hence $A$ is closed. At each $x\in A$, source
regularity at $s(x)$ and the native section regularity equivalence give the
same base regularity dimension, so they also give the source dimension bound
on the base. Coherence pulls back under the actual holomorphic section, and
the empty list is SNC and transverse to this closed regular carrier.
For nonzero restricted ideals take a genuine source member $g$ nonzero
arbitrarily near $s(x)$ on $s(A)$. Its actual section composition belongs to
$s^*J$. If that composition vanished near $x$ on $A$, the continuous projection
would pull this neighbourhood back to one near $s(x)$. On $s(A)$ the actual
identity $s(p(z))=z$ would then force $g$ to vanish there near $s(x)$, a
contradiction. Thus the entire nonzero-domain field is internally produced.
The original positive mark is unchanged. These constructions give all fields
of the ordinary base domain.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original empty-prefix cross-ambient values]
\label{hir:actual-closed-section-all-index-initial-values}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualSectionInitialValues.actual_initial_family_values}
\leanok
Let $U\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ with $n>0$, and let $s:U\to p^{-1}U$ be the actual
closed section. For an original ideal $J$ on $p^{-1}U$, an original subset
$A\subseteq U$ and mark $d$, put $M=(s(A),[],J,d)$ and
$B=(A,[],s^*J,d)$. If the original source record belongs to the ordinary invariant
domain at index $q$, then at every $x\in\operatorname{cosupp}(B)$ the actual index-$q$ invariant of
$M$ at $s(x)$ along the empty history equals that of $B$ at $x$ along the empty
history. The comparison uses the same value type, with no dimension shift.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First construct the actual base domain from the preceding original-source
domain theorem. Prove the comparison with both natural domains by induction
on $q$, generalizing the original open set, ambient dimension, carrier,
ideal, mark and point. At $q=0$ both actual functions are the defining constant
zero function. For the successor, use the actual general-step definition.
The whole closed-retraction order dictionary identifies every scalar threshold
of the two restricted original ideals. With empty boundary, the monomial part
is the unit ideal; the nonmonomial part represents the original restricted
ideal. Thus the two original nonmonomial orders are equal. Their whole order masks have the same section preimage: on the ordinary
domain the higher-order loci are closed, so the complement-of-closure mask
definition reduces to the complement of the actual order locus; the preceding
whole order dictionary applies at every base point. Their empty histories
have the same empty regime split. If this common order is zero, the empty-list
monomial invariants are identical, completing this branch.

At an original cosupport point this common order is at least the original
positive mark. Thus the companion takes its nonmonomial-only branch. Its
nonmonomial ideal is the actual restricted extension of the original ideal,
which is restricted equivalent to the original representative. Actual StepOne
representative invariance therefore replaces it by that original ideal with
the common order as mark, on the true order mask. If the common order is positive, form the two actual companions on their genuine
order masks. Their whole restricted ideals correspond: the empty monomial parts
are units, and the companion's two defining branches are the same finite sums,
products and powers after the whole restriction dictionary. The actual section
order masks may differ off the carrier; use their genuine section-mask overlap,
not a supposed cylinder identity. On that overlap the whole source ideal is
restricted equivalent to the true projection pullback of its section restriction.
Same-ambient representative invariance and actual open restriction compare these
records with the product-open representatives to which the induction applies.

The masked induction comparison used here is established as follows. 
Flatten the genuine nested open $W$ to its affine open image $W_0$ by the
actual subtype-coordinate biholomorphism $e$. Transport the whole base record
by $e^{-1}$, obtaining a genuine ordinary domain record $B_0$. Lift its entire
ideal by the genuine projection on $p^{-1}W_0$, retaining its closed-section
carrier. The projected-domain lemma internally gives its original domain.
The literal projection-section identity and whole pullback composition identify
its section pullback with the entire ideal of $B_0$. Thus the actual product
induction hypothesis applies. Base tower-isomorphism invariance identifies the
resulting base value with the original value on $W$.

The actual product-coordinate biholomorphism carries this source record to
$C=p_U^{-1}W$, inside $p^{-1}U$. Its entire ideal is $p_C^*(J|_s)$:
compose the actual projection square with $e^{-1}$ and use whole pullback
composition. Its carrier is exactly the same section carrier, by the actual
whole-carrier coordinate identity. Restrict this record and the original
source record to the genuine intersection $V=O\cap C$. The actual mask
representative theorem internally identifies their restricted whole ideals,
with the same carrier, empty list and mark. Ordinary open restriction and
biholomorphic transport construct their domains; the whole cosupport dictionary
places the comparison point in both of their cosupports.

At the empty prefix both canonicality predicates are the defining trivial
predicate. Same-ambient representative invariance therefore identifies the
two actual restricted invariant values. The genuine open-restriction field
identifies each of them with the value of its original record; no history
restriction is silently identified with a head. The actual product and base
biholomorphism fields, followed by the product induction comparison already
proved above, give the required masked equality. All carrier, ideal, domain,
cosupport and point identifications are internally obtained from the original
mask and genuine maps; none is an incoming comparison certificate.


The companions have maximal order and are in the ordinary domain, by the actual
companion-domain and order-bound theorems on the order masks. Their levels are
zero because the original lists are empty. Whole closed-section derivatives,
holomorphic sums and powers identify their actual homogenized and coefficient
ideals. Their whole cosupports correspond. The preceding regularity equivalence,
applied to their original carriers and their whole cosupports, identifies the
actual codimension-one special parts. At special points both StepOne values
are the defining pair $(0,\infty)$.

At a nonspecial point take the source's actual chosen tangent direction. The
whole closed-section differential identity and the actual contact-neighbourhood
construction restrict this same equation to a genuine base tangent direction,
on an actual open neighbourhood with closed regular contact carrier. The
source and base lower contact records are in index-$q$ domain: their carrier
dimensions drop by one, their marks are positive, and the actual lower-domain
construction derives nonzero restricted ideals and the point's cosupport.
Both lower tails are empty, so their canonicality is the defining trivial
predicate. The actual level-zero tangent-change anchor computes the two lower
values through this same equation, with internally derived lower data; no lower
canonicality or value equality is accepted as input. The whole differential,
homogenized and coefficient dictionaries identify the base contact record with
the section restriction of the source contact record. Apply the genuine mask
representative/open comparison above and the induction hypothesis at index $q$
to those two actual lower records. Their lower values agree; hence their entire
StepOne pairs agree. Their actual companion values and the original general
lexicographic pairs therefore agree. This proves the successor case.
\end{proof}
