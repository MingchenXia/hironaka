\section{The actual controlled weighted sheaf stalk in a genuine point blowup chart}

Let $P$ be the prescribed original analytic vertex coefficient data, with its
real coefficient open $U\subset\mathbf C^s$ containing zero. Its powered
coefficient functions define the actual coherent ideal $J_P$ on $U$, with
the unchanged positive common mark $D$. Let $\pi:Y\to U$ be a genuine global
blowup along the actual point $0_U$. At a point $y\in Y$, retain a genuine
centred source chart $\Theta$ whose actual affine square is
$\pi(\Theta^{-1}u)=\pi_{e,b}(u)$ in the literal base coordinates.

qualified point-chart assertion and does not assert the affine map itself

\begin{lemma}[Whole finite original generators in the actual affine square]
\label{hir:actual-point-chart-whole-weighted-total-stalk}
\uses{trace:actual-finite-germ-ideal-pullback-generation,hir:submersion-germ-colon-adapted-stalk,hir:actual-original-coherent-weighted-coefficient-marked}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointWeightedControlledStalk.actual_weighted_total_stalk}
\leanok
For any finite actual holomorphic coefficient family on $U$, the whole
adapted origin stalk of its genuine sheaf total pullback is the literal
ring pullback of the entire powered coefficient germ ideal.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original functions, restricted to their actual open subtype, are
holomorphic and generate their whole finite-function ideal at every point.
The actual holomorphic map $\pi$ pulls this full finite presentation back
to the literal functions $c_i\circ\pi$, including the original powers.
The true inverse source chart transports both directions of every finite
holomorphic relation to the origin. In that chart the actual affine square
identifies each pulled-back function germ with the composition of the same
original coefficient function and $\pi_{e,b}$. This identity holds on a
neighbourhood of zero inside the real chart target; it is not a global
holomorphic extension. Germ composition and its power law therefore identify
each actual generator with the ring image of its original powered germ.
Both whole ideals are their complete finite spans, proving equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The real exceptional denominator has the literal pivot power stalk]
\label{hir:actual-point-chart-exceptional-power-stalk}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-geometric-transform-full-relative-saturation-dictionary,bcalc:hyperplane-ideal,hir:sharp-actual-relative-product-exceptional-power-sum-algebra}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointWeightedControlledStalk.actual_exceptional_power_stalk}
\leanok
In this same genuine point blowup and same source chart, the entire stalk
of every actual exceptional ideal power is $(e^q)$, including $q=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Actual blowup geometry identifies the full exceptional ideal with the
vanishing ideal of $\pi^{-1}\{0_U\}$. In the stated real source domain,
the affine square makes membership in this inverse image equivalent to
$\pi_{e,b}(u)=0$. Every component of the point chart map is divisible by
$u_e$, and its pivot component equals $u_e$; hence this condition is exactly
$u_e=0$. Genuine coordinate Hadamard division identifies the entire original
vanishing ideal at $y$ with the single actual coordinate function
$h=\Theta_e$. This derives the principal denominator from the original
geometry rather than assuming a stalk equation. Finite holomorphic product
span substitution gives the exact generator $h^q$ for every ideal power;
the unit denominator is the case $q=0$. The true inverse chart sends the
same actual $h$ to the literal coordinate $e$, so its whole power stalk is
precisely $(e^q)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole actual controlled sheaf stalk is the genuine ring colon]
\label{hir:actual-point-weighted-controlled-stalk-colon}
\uses{hir:actual-point-chart-whole-weighted-total-stalk,hir:actual-point-chart-exceptional-power-stalk,hir:submersion-germ-colon-original-stalk-quotient,bcalc:controlled-transform}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointWeightedControlledStalk.actual_weighted_controlled_stalk}
\leanok
The whole adapted origin stalk of the actual sheaf controlled transform
$\sigma^c_\pi(J_P,D)$ equals
\[
 (\pi_{e,b}^*\operatorname{Compress}(c(P)):e^D).
\]
No total-stalk, exceptional-stalk or controlled-stalk equality is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original finite powered functions give coherence of $J_P$. Genuine
blowup pullback gives coherence of its whole total transform, and the
actual exceptional ideal and all its powers are coherent. The entire
adapted-stalk colon theorem thus applies directly to the defining actual
sheaf controlled transform. It identifies that stalk with the colon of the
whole total-transform stalk by the whole exceptional-power stalk. Substitute
the first two lemmas. A ring colon by the principal ideal $(e^D)$ is the
colon tested by its same generator $e^D$: closure under every multiplier
gives both directions. This is the displayed whole-ideal equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Same canonical weighted data track the true controlled sheaf stalk]
\label{hir:actual-original-point-weighted-controlled-stalk-tracking}

\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointWeightedControlledStalk.exists_original_point_weighted_controlled_stalk_tracking}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

genuine blowup with the supplied real affine square falls under the
