\section{Intrinsic admissible centres and the entire ambient boundary}

\begin{theorem}[Native intrinsic normal crossings lift across a transverse embedding]
\label{hir:disjoint-embedded-whole-boundary-nc}
\uses{bcalc:snc-simultaneous,lag:regular-point-basics,hir:disjoint-matched-intrinsic-chart}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointEmbeddedBoundaryLift.hasNormalCrossings_native_image}
\leanok
Let $N$ be a regular $d$-dimensional submanifold of a complex manifold
$X$ of positive dimension $d+t$, equipped with its existing native complex atlas.
Global closedness is not needed for this local NC conclusion.
Let $E$ be the entire ordered SNC boundary of $X$, transverse to $N$. If
$D\subset N$ has normal crossings with the literal ordered trace
$[\operatorname{val}^{-1}E_i]$, then its literal ambient image
$\operatorname{val}(D)$ has normal crossings with the entire original $E$.
No ambient centre-NC, joint centre chart, or history lift is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $x\in D$. First apply the existing simultaneous-coordinate theorem to
the centre $N$ and carrier $N$ themselves: its needed ambient NC is precisely
the already supplied transversality of $N$, not the desired NC of $D$.
This gives one actual chart straightening $N$ to a standard coordinate plane
and all active boundary members to distinct tangent coordinate hyperplanes.
Subtract its value at $x$. This preserves the carrier plane and every active
boundary equation, because those coordinates vanish at $x$. The resulting
chart $\Theta$ is centered. If its plane index is $b$, its local regular
dimension is $\min(n,b)$, where $n=d+t$. Regular-point dimension uniqueness
identifies this with $d$. Thus its actual plane is precisely the $d$-plane,
and each active boundary slot is below $d$ (if $b>n$, then $d=n$, so this
still follows). Ambient positive dimension is explicitly assumed, exactly
as required by the stock simultaneous-coordinate theorem.

Apply the matched-native-coordinate construction to THIS $\Theta$ and the
identity diffeomorphism of the existing native $N$. It gives genuine native
coordinates $\Psi$, centered at zero, such that on a true neighbourhood of
zero the inverse native coordinates, followed by inclusion in $X$, agree
with the inverse ambient $\Theta$ on the zero plane. Unpack the intrinsic
normal-crossings hypothesis at $x$, obtaining one centered intrinsic chart
$\Phi$ for $D$ and all literal traced boundary members. The actual transition
$F=\Phi\circ\Psi^{-1}$ is a genuine local biholomorphism at zero, with source
the intersection of the true target of $\Psi$ and the inverse image of the
true source of $\Phi$. Extend $F$ by the identity in the normal directions,
using the actual product equivalence. This is a map with actual inverse and
actual open source and target, not a formal extension of coordinate germs.

All equations just obtained hold on neighbourhoods of zero. Intersect their
finitely many neighbourhoods, the true domains of the inverse charts, the
ambient $\Theta$ target, and the inverse projection of the transition source.
Choose an open neighbourhood of zero inside this intersection. Restrict the
actual composition of $\Theta$ and the product transition to its inverse
image, with inverse target restricted correspondingly. This is the required
true shrinking: an arbitrary ambient source open is never declared a full
product. On this source, $N$ is the zero plane. For any ambient coordinate
point $z$, its zero-plane projection corresponds to the actual native point
in the matching $\Psi$ coordinates. The old transverse boundary equations
depend only on tangent coordinates, so membership in each active ambient
$E_i$ is equivalent to membership of that projected native point in the
literal trace. The intrinsic $\Phi$ equations then give its lifted singleton
mask after the extended transition. The same calculation for $D$, together
with all unchanged normal coordinates being zero, gives its ambient image
mask. One actual chart therefore straightens the image centre and EVERY
original active boundary member simultaneously. Each lifted singleton has
cardinality one, proving exactly the ambient NC definition. All original
positions and empty members are retained.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope.} This theorem is a geometric NC producer. Actual ambient
blowup construction, empty-interior and source cosupport transport, prescribed
intrinsic blowup comparison, and finite-history recursion are subsequent
consumers. They are not additional hypotheses or asserted outputs here.
