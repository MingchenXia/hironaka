\section{Actual finite controlled histories over a closed contact}

\begin{definition}[The contact along an ambient tower]
\label{hir:disjoint-finite-contact-along}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointFiniteControlledHistory.contactAlong}
\leanok
For a closed contact $N\subset X$ and an actual ambient blowup tower $U$, let
$N_U$ be $N$ at the empty tower and the centre-relative strict transform of
$N_V$ when the last step extends $V$. Every physical step, including a blowup
along the empty centre, is retained.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Compatible physical prefixes]
\label{hir:disjoint-finite-coupling}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointFiniteControlledHistory.fullMarked,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointFiniteControlledHistory.Endpoint,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointFiniteControlledHistory.CoupledHistory}
\uses{hir:disjoint-finite-contact-along}
\leanok
The full marked data associated with an ordered boundary $B$ and ideal $J$
have carrier the whole ambient space and mark one. An endpoint coupling is a
map $\eta$ from an original lower endpoint to an upper endpoint which is a
biholomorphism onto the actual native contact $N_U$, satisfies the whole
functional ideal and ordered boundary dictionaries, and commutes with the
original total projections. It also records closed regularity of $N_U$,
coherence and normal containment of the upper ideal, and simple normal
crossings of the entire upper boundary transverse to $N_U$.
A coupled history starts with the original inclusion at both empty towers.
At each physical successor it retains its preceding coupling, the new
endpoint coupling, the equality $C'=\eta(C)$ of the upper and lower centres,
and the exact square $\pi\circ\eta'=\eta\circ\rho$. Thus every prefix uses
the same endpoint map in its preceding and following step squares.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Constructed finite controlled history]
\label{hir:disjoint-finite-controlled-history}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointFiniteControlledHistory.exists_actual_finite_controlled_history}
\uses{hir:disjoint-finite-contact-along,hir:disjoint-finite-coupling,hir:disjoint-functional-controlled-successor,hir:geo-transform-ambient,hir:bridge-consumers,hir:biholomorphism-blowup,hir:biholomorphism-transform,hir:biholomorphism-invariance,hir:disjoint-native-mark-one-source-cosupport,hir:disjoint-embedded-whole-boundary-nc}
\leanok
Let $X$ be a complex manifold of dimension $d+t$, where $t>0$, let $N$ be a
closed regular submanifold of constant dimension $d$, and let $B$ be an
ordered simple normal crossings divisor transverse to $N$. Let $J$ be a
coherent ideal containing the entire vanishing ideal of $N$. On the native
manifold $N$, set $M=(N,N,B|_N,J|_N,1)$, and on $X$ set
$A=(X,X,B,J,1)$. For every prescribed finite multiple test blowup $T$ of $M$,
there exists an actual ambient multiple test blowup $U$ of $A$, with exactly
the same length. Its contact $N_U$ is closed and regular of dimension $d$;
its entire ordered boundary is simple normal crossings and transverse to
$N_U$; its transformed ideal is coherent and contains $I_{N_U}$.
There is one biholomorphism $F$ from the original final space of $T$ onto
$N_U$ such that the projection square commutes, the whole functional
pullback of the transformed ambient ideal is the original transformed ideal
$M_T$, and the whole ordered boundary pulled back by the same $F$ is
$E(M_T)$. The constructed pair carries a coupled history: every physical
prefix has these endpoint assertions, the upper centre is exactly the image
of the prescribed lower centre, and successive endpoint embeddings commute
with the actual original step maps. Empty old boundary members and every born exceptional member are
retained in their original order. Neither an ambient lift nor any equality
at a sought successor is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the prescribed tower, extending at its last physical step. At the
empty tower take the empty ambient tower, the given contact, and the identity
of its native manifold. Coherence, normal containment, and divisor properties
are precisely the source assumptions; both dictionaries and the projection
square are identities.

Suppose the preceding ambient tower and its comparison $F$ have been
constructed, and write $D$ and $\rho$ for the prescribed next original
centre and map. Transport this centre to the native current contact by
$F(D)$, and rebase its actual blowup map to $F\circ\rho$. This is a genuine
blowup: apply the universal-property invariance under the inverse
biholomorphism $F^{-1}$. Pull back the original current marked ideal by
$F^{-1}$. Its entire ideal equals the literal restriction of the current
ambient ideal, by the preceding whole functional equality and composition
with $F^{-1}$. Its ordered divisor equals the literal traced ambient divisor,
by composing the preceding whole ordered-list equality with $F^{-1}$.
Normal crossings and admissibility transfer through this biholomorphism.
The original current carrier is the whole native space and its mark remains
one. The Hadamard inclusion therefore gives the full lower ideal inclusion
in the first power of the centre ideal.

Apply the functional controlled successor theorem to these derived source
data. It internally constructs the actual next ambient blowup, the next
regular closed contact, its coherent ideal containing the entire new normal
ideal, and one comparison over $F\circ\rho$ carrying the whole ideal and
ordered boundary. The ambient image centre is admissible: its cosupport
inclusion follows from the lower mark-one inclusion, and its normal crossings
follow from the native normal crossings and transversality of the old ambient
boundary. Append this actual blowup to the constructed ambient tower. Both
lengths increase by one. The full ambient carrier remains the whole space,
so its marked ideal transform is its controlled transform. Naturality of the
lower marked transform under $F^{-1}$ identifies the rebased successor ideal
and whole boundary with the original prescribed successor. The two commuting
projection squares compose to the required original projection square.
The new ambient centre is precisely the image of $F(D)$ under the native
inclusion, equivalently the image of the original $D$ under the preceding
endpoint embedding; this follows by applying $F^{-1}$ and $F$. Retain this
centre equality, the new step square, and the previous coupled history in
the successor history. The
successor's other geometric and normal-ideal conclusions give exactly the
induction assertions. No step is deleted, so the argument also retains empty
centres and empty boundary slots.
\end{proof}
