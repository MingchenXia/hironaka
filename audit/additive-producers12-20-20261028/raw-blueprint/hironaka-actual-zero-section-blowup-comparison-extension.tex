\chapter{The actual zero section and prescribed genuine blowups}

Fix $d,t\geq0$. Throughout, $X=\mathbb C^d$, $M=\mathbb C^{d+t}$,
$i=\operatorname{tangentInclusion}(d,t)$ and
$P=\operatorname{tangentProjection}(d,t)$ are the actual globally
holomorphic coordinate maps, with $Pi=\mathrm{id}_X$. Let
$N=\mathbb C^d\times\{0\}\subset M$, $C\subset X$, and $\bar C=i(C)$.
The geometric comparison starts with two prescribed genuine blowups
$p:B\to X$ along $C$ and $\pi:Y\to M$ along $\bar C$, in the original
\texttt{IsBlowupAlong} sense. Thus smoothness, closedness and nowhere
density of each actual centre are already part of the original blowup
data. The theorem does not take a desired comparison map, strict-carrier
smoothness, sheaf identity or equivalence as an extra hypothesis.

Write $S=\overline{\pi^{-1}(N\setminus\bar C)}$, literally the existing
\texttt{geoStrictTransform}. This is not replaced by the different
definition using the differential exceptional locus, including at Cartier
centres. The algebraic zero-section normalized-ideal dictionaries and this
whole geometric comparison have distinct roles; neither stands in for the
other. No canonical choice of centre or independence of a resolution
algorithm is asserted here.

\begin{lemma}[The whole actual zero-section carrier is smooth]
\label{hir:actual-zero-section-whole-carrier-regular}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-section-whole-centre-sheaf}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionBlowupLift.actual_zeroSection_plane_closed,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionBlowupLift.actual_zeroSection_plane_regular,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionBlowupLift.actual_zeroSection_range}
\leanok
The actual coordinate plane $N$ is closed, is exactly the range of $i$,
and is regular of complex dimension $d$ at every one of its actual points.
These assertions hold also when either coordinate block is empty.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The plane is the intersection of the zero sets of the continuous actual
normal coordinate functions, so it is closed. The actual coordinate
retraction identities give $i(X)\subset N$ and $iPz=z$ for every
$z\in N$, proving the whole range equality. At $z\in N$ the actual
translation $w\mapsto w-z$ with inverse $v\mapsto v+z$ is a global
holomorphic change of coordinates centred at $z$. Since every normal
coordinate of $z$ is zero, $w\in N$ if and only if $w-z\in N$.
This is precisely the original pointwise \texttt{RegularAtDim} condition
of dimension $d$ in the actual model chart. No smoothness certificate for
the strict transform is involved.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine ambient lift and its whole exceptional ideal]
\label{hir:actual-zero-section-genuine-blowup-lift}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-section-whole-centre-sheaf,hir:submersion-choice-image-trace-whole-pullback,bex:lift-unique,bcalc:exceptional-divisor}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionBlowupLift.existsUnique_actual_zeroSection_blowup_lift,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionBlowupLift.actual_zeroSection_lift_exceptional_ideal}
\leanok
The original pair of genuine blowups internally produces one unique
holomorphic map $f:B\to Y$ with $\pi f=ip$. For every actual such lift,
the entire ideal sheaves satisfy
\[
 f^*(\pi^*\mathcal I_{\bar C})=p^*\mathcal I_C.
\]
These are whole sheaves at all points, not only origin ideals.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The accepted actual zero-section retraction theorem proves
$i^*\mathcal I_{\bar C}=\mathcal I_C$ at every point for every subset
$C$. Actual holomorphic pullback composition across the two actual
dimensions therefore gives
$(ip)^*\mathcal I_{\bar C}=p^*\mathcal I_C$. The right side is
invertible by the genuine base blowup. The universal property of the
ambient blowup tests source manifolds of every dimension, so it applies
to the actual $d$-dimensional $B$ and the actual holomorphic map $ip$.
It gives existence and uniqueness of $f$. For any actual lift, whole
pullback composition and $\pi f=ip$ give the displayed exceptional
ideal equality by the same calculation. The only member holomorphy needed
by composition is present in the literal vanishing ideal definition.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual lift lands in the entire strict carrier]
\label{hir:actual-zero-section-lift-in-whole-strict-carrier}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-section-genuine-blowup-lift,hir:actual-zero-section-whole-carrier-regular,bex:lift-unique}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionBlowupLift.actual_zeroSection_lift_mem_geoStrictTransform}
\leanok
Every actual holomorphic lift $f$ of the preceding square maps every
point of $B$ into $S$. The density needed for this conclusion is produced
internally from the original base blowup.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The true invertibility of $p^*\mathcal I_C$ implies that no nonempty
open subset of $B$ maps entirely into $C$: a local invertible generator
would then vanish identically on a nonempty open, contradicting its
actual nonvanishing clause. This is the existing
\texttt{BlowupConstruction.exists\_not\_mem\_of\_isOpen} theorem.
Thus $p^{-1}(X\setminus C)$ meets every nonempty open in $B$.
For any point $b\in B$ and open neighbourhood $V$ of $f(b)$, the actual
continuous inverse image $f^{-1}V$ is a nonempty open. Choose $b'$ in it
with $p(b')\notin C$. The commuting square gives
$\pi f(b')=i p(b')\in N\setminus\bar C$, where the nonmembership uses
the genuine all-point inverse-image identity $i^{-1}\bar C=C$.
Consequently $V$ meets $\pi^{-1}(N\setminus\bar C)$. The actual closure
criterion proves $f(b)\in S$ for every $b$. No closed-image or properness
argument, and no supplied density or range-equality certificate, is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual strict carrier receives its genuine manifold structure]
\label{hir:actual-zero-section-strict-carrier-manifold}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-section-whole-carrier-regular,bcalc:strict-submanifold,ana:submanifold-corestriction}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionBlowupComparison.actual_zeroSection_strict_regular}
\leanok
Every point of the actual whole strict carrier $S$ is regular of dimension
$d$. In particular the existing flattening atlas supplies a genuine
$d$-dimensional complex manifold on its actual subspace topology, with
holomorphic subtype inclusion into $Y$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original carrier $N$ is closed and regular of dimension $d$ at every
point by the first lemma, and $\bar C\subset N$ holds from the actual
zero-section inclusion. Apply the original
\texttt{regularAtDim\_geoStrictTransform} theorem to the genuine ambient
blowup. It proves regularity at every point of the entire $S$, including
points over the centre. The actual
\texttt{SubmanifoldChartedSpace.chartedSpace} construction and its proved
complex-manifold and inclusion-holomorphy theorems now supply the stated
structure; they do not require a caller atlas, smooth strict carrier or
equivalence. The accepted corestriction theorem applies to maps with values
in this exact atlas.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine biholomorphism with the whole ambient strict carrier]
\label{hir:actual-zero-section-blowup-whole-geometric-comparison}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-section-genuine-blowup-lift,hir:actual-zero-section-lift-in-whole-strict-carrier,hir:actual-zero-section-strict-carrier-manifold,ana:submanifold-corestriction,bcalc:exceptional-divisor}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionBlowupComparison.actualZeroSectionComparisonFor,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionBlowupComparison.exists_actual_zeroSection_blowup_comparison}
\leanok
From the original genuine pair of blowups, internally construct the genuine
manifold structure on $S$ and a biholomorphism $E:B\simeq S$ with
\[
 \pi(E(b))=i(p(b)),\qquad
 p(E^{-1}(s))=P(\pi(s)).
\]
The entire ambient range of its forward inclusion is exactly $S$, and its
whole exceptional-ideal pullback is $p^*\mathcal I_C$ as above.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the unique ambient lift $f$ once, and use the preceding two results
to corestrict this same actual map to a holomorphic $f_S:B\to S$ in the
genuine flattening atlas. Define $q:S\to X$ by
$q=P\pi\operatorname{incl}$; all three actual maps are holomorphic.
The whole closure is contained in $\pi^{-1}N$, since $N$ is closed and
$\pi$ is continuous. The real retraction identity on $N$ therefore gives
$iq=\pi\operatorname{incl}$ pointwise, as whole functions.

The existing actual exceptional ideal is invertible \emph{along} $S$.
Indeed a local exceptional generator cannot vanish on a nonempty
relatively open subset of this closure, because that subset meets its
defining off-centre set, where the ideal contains one. This is the proved
\texttt{MarkedIdeals.MarkedIdeal.isInvertibleAlong\_geoStrictTransform}
at power one; the power-one identification follows from the actual ideal
power recursion and coherence. The actual restriction theorem
\texttt{SubmanifoldIdealRestriction.isInvertible\_restrictIdeal} converts
this along-$S$ property into genuine invertibility of
$\operatorname{incl}^*(\pi^*\mathcal I_{\bar C})$ on the constructed
manifold $S$. An ambient invertibility claim alone would not suffice here.
The whole sheaf identities already proved give
\[
 q^*\mathcal I_C=q^*i^*\mathcal I_{\bar C}
 =(iq)^*\mathcal I_{\bar C}
 =(\pi\operatorname{incl})^*\mathcal I_{\bar C}
 =\operatorname{incl}^*(\pi^*\mathcal I_{\bar C}).
\]
Thus the true base universal property supplies a holomorphic
$g:S\to B$ with $pg=q$.

Now $qf_S=Pip=p$, so $pgf_S=p$. The genuine base universal uniqueness
compares $gf_S$ with $\mathrm{id}_B$ and gives $gf_S=\mathrm{id}_B$.
For the other inverse, the two actual holomorphic ambient maps
$\operatorname{incl}f_Sg$ and $\operatorname{incl}$ lift the same map
$iq=\pi\operatorname{incl}$; its centre pullback has already been proved
invertible. Ambient universal uniqueness gives their equality, and subtype
injectivity gives $f_Sg=\mathrm{id}_S$. These two genuine inverse identities
construct $E$ with inverse $g$. They also prove its entire ambient range
is exactly $S$. The whole exceptional-ideal assertion follows from the
earlier all-point sheaf calculation for this same lift.

No connectivity, compactness or positive-dimensional-block hypothesis is
used. Empty centres and $d=0$ or $t=0$ are covered by the genuine blowup
data. A centre equal to the nonempty entire base cannot satisfy the original
nowhere-density clause. This is a comparison for prescribed actual centres;
it does not assert that two embeddings produce the same canonical centres,
an arbitrary compatible tower, or abstract analytic resolution descent.
\end{proof}
