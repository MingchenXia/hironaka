\chapter{Constructing the ambient blowup from the original zero-section data}

Let $X=\mathbb C^d$, $M=\mathbb C^{d+t}$, and let $i:X\to M$ and
$P:M\to X$ be the actual first-coordinate inclusion and projection. Write
$N=\mathbb C^d\times\{0\}$ and $Q=\mathrm{id}_M-iP$. All dimensions
$d,t\geq0$ are allowed. The original datum in the final theorem is a
genuine blowup $p:B\to X$ along $C$. An ambient blowup, a comparison map,
smoothness of an image centre or of a strict carrier, and a desired sheaf
identity are not additional inputs.

We use the existing geometric blowup construction of a nowhere dense
closed submanifold, adapted here by explicitly constructing the centre's
local product flattenings before invoking its universal property. This
is the zero-section geometric step, not independence of a canonical
resolution algorithm. The strict carrier is literally
$\overline{\pi^{-1}(N\setminus i(C))}$, the existing
\texttt{geoStrictTransform}, including when $C$ is a Cartier centre.

\begin{lemma}[Actual product extension of a local flattening]
\label{hir:actual-zero-section-product-flattening}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-section-whole-carrier-regular}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionCentreGeometry.actual_zeroSection_localEquiv,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionCentreGeometry.actual_zeroSection_localEquiv_plane_iff}
\leanok
Let $\Phi$ be an actual local holomorphic equivalence at $x\in X$,
with source $U$ and target $V$. On the actual open sets $P^{-1}U$ and
$P^{-1}V$, the formulas
\[
 \bar\Phi(z)=i\Phi(Pz)+Qz,\qquad
 \bar\Phi^{-1}(w)=i\Phi^{-1}(Pw)+Qw
\]
give a local holomorphic equivalence at $ix$. For every $k\leq d$,
\[
 \bar\Phi(z)\in\mathbb C^k\times\{0\}\subset M
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 z\in N\ \text{and}\ \Phi(Pz)\in\mathbb C^k\times\{0\}\subset X.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The identity $Pi=\mathrm{id}_X$ gives $PQ=0$, $Qi=0$ and $Q^2=Q$.
Consequently $P\bar\Phi(z)=\Phi(Pz)$ and $Q\bar\Phi(z)=Qz$.
The preimages defining the two domains are open by continuity of $P$;
the base point $ix$ belongs to the first because $P(ix)=x$. Actual
holomorphic composition with the complex linear maps $i,P$, followed by
addition and subtraction, proves holomorphy on the indicated domains.
The displayed projection identity and the original domain mapping
properties give both new domain mapping properties. On $P^{-1}U$,
\[
 \bar\Phi^{-1}\bar\Phi(z)
 =i\Phi^{-1}(\Phi(Pz))+Qz=iPz+Qz=z.
\]
On $P^{-1}V$ the other composition equals $iPz+Qz=z$ by the other
original inverse identity. These establish all clauses of an actual
local holomorphic equivalence.

At the actual index $j<d$, the $j$th coordinate of $\bar\Phi(z)$ is
$\Phi(Pz)_j$. At the actual normal index $d+j$, it is exactly $z_{d+j}$.
If $\bar\Phi(z)$ lies in the ambient $k$-plane, $k\leq d$ makes every
normal coordinate of $z$ zero, and its tangent coordinates with index
at least $k$ give the base $k$-plane condition on $\Phi(Pz)$.
Conversely, these two conditions set every ambient coordinate of index
at least $k$ to zero: use the base condition for indices below $d$ and
the normal condition for the remaining indices. This proves the whole
equivalence without a permutation of coordinates.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual image of every original closed submanifold]
\label{hir:actual-zero-section-image-closed-submanifold}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-section-product-flattening,hir:actual-zero-section-whole-carrier-regular}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionCentreGeometry.actual_zeroSection_image_eq,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionCentreGeometry.actual_zeroSection_image_regular,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionCentreGeometry.actual_zeroSection_image_closedSubmanifold}
\leanok
For every subset $C\subset X$,
\[
 i(C)=P^{-1}C\cap N.
\]
If $C$ is closed, its image is closed. If $C$ is regular of dimension
$k$ at $x$, $i(C)$ is regular of that same dimension at $ix$.
In particular every actual closed submanifold $C$ has an actual closed
submanifold $i(C)$ in $M$; its dimension may vary across components.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $z=ix$ for $x\in C$, then $Pz=x$ and $z\in N$. If $Pz\in C$ and
$z\in N$, the actual identity $iPz=z$ proves $z\in i(C)$.
The projection is continuous and $N$ is closed, so the intersection
formula proves the closedness assertion.

At an actual regular point $x$, choose the original local flattening
$\Phi$, of dimension $k\leq d$, with $\Phi(x)=0$. Preferred charts on
the actual model spaces are identities. Extend this actual flattening
by the preceding lemma. Its value at $ix$ is zero, because $Q(ix)=0$.
On its source $P^{-1}U$, the base flattening and the intersection formula
give
\[
 z\in i(C)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ z\in N\text{ and }Pz\in C
 \ \Longleftrightarrow\ z\in N\text{ and }\Phi(Pz)\in\mathbb C^k\times\{0\}.
\]
The final condition is exactly the ambient $k$-plane condition by the
preceding lemma. Also $k\leq d\leq d+t$. These are all clauses of the
original \texttt{RegularAtDim} definition. Applying this at every point
of $C$, with its own original regular dimension, proves that $i(C)$ is
a closed submanifold. No whole-space ideal or origin germ replaces the
actual subsets in this argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual image centre has no interior]
\label{hir:actual-zero-section-image-empty-interior}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-section-image-closed-submanifold}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionCentreGeometry.actual_zeroSection_image_interior_eq_empty}
\leanok
For every subset $C\subset X$, if $\operatorname{int}(C)=\varnothing$,
then $\operatorname{int}(i(C))=\varnothing$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Suppose $z$ belongs to the interior of $i(C)$. Then $z=ix$ for some
$x\in C$. The actual continuous map $i$ makes
$U=i^{-1}(\operatorname{int}(i(C)))$ open, and $x\in U$.
Every $y\in U$ has $iy\in i(C)$; applying $P$ and using $Pi=\mathrm{id}$
gives $y\in C$. Thus $U$ is an open subset of $C$ containing $x$,
contradicting $\operatorname{int}(C)=\varnothing$.
This proof also applies when $t=0$, so it does not use the existence of
a normal coordinate. When $d=0$, the same proof forces the original
centre to be empty, as required. An empty centre introduces no special
choice of a point or of a flattening.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Construct the actual ambient blowup from the base blowup]
\label{hir:actual-zero-section-constructed-blowup-comparison}
\source{demailly-cadg:VII.12.2,demailly-cadg:VII.12.3}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-section-image-closed-submanifold,hir:actual-zero-section-image-empty-interior,bex:blowup-exists,hir:actual-zero-section-blowup-whole-geometric-comparison}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionConstructedBlowup.actualConstructedZeroSectionComparisonFor,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroSectionConstructedBlowup.exists_actual_constructed_zeroSection_blowup_comparison}
\leanok
Input only an actual $d$-dimensional complex manifold $B$ and a genuine
blowup $p:B\to X$ along $C$. Set
\[
 Y=\operatorname{BlowupSpace}(d+t,i(C)),\qquad
 \pi=\operatorname{blowupProj}(d+t,i(C)).
\]
Equip this actual existing construction with its actual topology,
flattening atlas and complex manifold structure. Then $\pi$ is a
genuine blowup along $i(C)$. The whole actual strict carrier
$S=\overline{\pi^{-1}(N\setminus i(C))}$ receives its actual
$d$-dimensional complex manifold structure and a genuine biholomorphism
$E:B\simeq S$, with
\[
 \pi(Eb)=i(pb),\qquad p(E^{-1}y)=P(\pi y),\qquad
 \operatorname{range}(\mathrm{incl}\circ E)=S,
\]
and the entire ideal sheaves satisfy
\[
 (\mathrm{incl}\circ E)^*(\pi^*\mathcal I_{i(C)})=p^*\mathcal I_C.
\]
The existing geometric construction, not a caller-supplied ambient
blowup, is used in this conclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original genuine blowup implies that $C$ is an actual closed
submanifold and has empty interior. The two preceding lemmas prove the
same statements for its actual image. Use this internally produced
closed-submanifold fact to instantiate the existing
\texttt{BlowupSpace} topology and its genuine atlas and complex manifold.
The existing blowup construction's universal-property theorem, applied
to the internally proved empty-interior statement, proves that this
literal $\pi$ is an \texttt{IsBlowupAlong} map.

Apply the genuine prescribed-pair comparison to the original $p$ and
this newly constructed $\pi$. That theorem internally constructs the
strict carrier's atlas, both holomorphic maps, and their inverse
identities by the two genuine universal uniqueness clauses. Its entire
commuting, range and exceptional-sheaf conclusions give exactly the
ones stated here. No new smoothness, invertibility, comparison or ideal
certificate is required from the caller. All input and constructed
centres remain the actual whole subsets. The product flattening and
empty-interior arguments cover $t=0$, $d=0$, and the empty centre, so
the same construction applies in those cases.
\end{proof}
