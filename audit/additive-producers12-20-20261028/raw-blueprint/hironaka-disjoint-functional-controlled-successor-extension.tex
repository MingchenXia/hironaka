\begin{theorem}[Constructed whole functional successor]
\label{hir:disjoint-functional-controlled-successor}
\uses{hir:disjoint-constructed-controlled-lift,hir:actual-closed-contact-whole-controlled-ideal-restriction,hir:disjoint-actual-controlled-transport,bcalc:strict-ideal,bcalc:controlled-transform,hir:disjoint-whole-old-born-boundary-trace}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaDisjointFunctionalControlledSuccessor.exists_functional_controlled_successor}
\leanok
Assume an actual closed regular native $N$ of dimension $d$ in ambient
dimension $d+t$ with $t>0$, an entire ordered ambient SNC boundary $B$
transverse to $N$, and a coherent ambient ideal $J$ containing the entire
normal ideal $\mathcal I_N$. For an intrinsic centre $D$ with NC against the
literal entire trace of $B$, take a prescribed genuine intrinsic blowup
$\rho:Z\to N$ and ONLY the lower original mark-one admissibility of the
literal whole ideal $I=\operatorname{val}_N^*J$.

Internally construct an actual ambient global blowup $\pi:Y\to X$ of the
literal image of $D$, with its source upper admissibility proved internally,
and ONE actual native comparison $F:Z\simeq N'$ over $\rho$. Write
$J'=\operatorname{controlled}_{\pi}(J,1)$. Prove WHOLE coherence of $J'$, the
persistent entire normal containment $\mathcal I_{N'}\subset J'$, and the
ENTIRE FUNCTIONAL identity on the prescribed whole $Z$
\[
 (\operatorname{val}_{N'}\circ F)^*J'
    =\operatorname{controlled}_{\rho}(I,1).
\]
Also prove closed regularity of $N'$ of the same original dimension $d$,
SNC and transversality of the literal full strict-old-plus-born ambient
boundary, EVERY original indexed strict trace including empty members,
the born preimage identity, and the entire ordered old-plus-born list
identity through THIS SAME $F$. No ambient $\pi$, upper admissibility,
target functional ideal equality, persistent normal containment, boundary
dictionary or history Lift is an input. The original lower ideal and its
controlled transform retain their entire nonradical structures.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the actual constructed controlled-lift producer to the original lower
step and original source data. It constructs the actual ambient manifold and
blowup, internally proves upper source admissibility, and produces one whole
native strict-contact comparison $F$ over the prescribed $\rho$. Retain the
whole chronological old-plus-born SNC/transversality outputs. The actual
controlled-transform coherence theorem applies to the coherent original $J$
and this internally proved upper source admissibility, giving coherence of
all of $J'$. Actual strict-contact regularity supplies the entire closed
regular $N'$ of the same dimension $d$, independent of any current point.

For the whole functional identity, let $\sigma:N'\to N$ be the literal
restricted projection. It is a genuine intrinsic blowup along $D$, by the
actual closed-contact restriction theorem and the prescribed lower centre
geometry. Whole functional controlled restriction identifies
$\operatorname{val}_{N'}^*J'$ with
$\operatorname{controlled}_{\sigma}(I,1)$. Actual exceptional-factor
transport through the SAME $F$, whose literal square is $\sigma F=\rho$,
identifies its entire pullback with $\operatorname{controlled}_{\rho}(I,1)$.
The actual cross-dimensional coherent-pullback composition theorem identifies
this composite pullback with $(\operatorname{val}_{N'}F)^*J'$. These are
entire functional ideal equalities, not only adapted-stalk comparisons at
points of the current cosupport; no closed pullback/colon flatness is assumed.

Monotonicity of actual total pullback and the defining controlled colon sends
$\mathcal I_N\subset J$ to
$\operatorname{controlled}_{\pi}(\mathcal I_N,1)\subset J'$. Since the normal
contact $N$ is REGULAR, the actual controlled-normal formula identifies the
left side with the entire $\mathcal I_{N'}$. This is the persistent normal
containment needed at the next physical step. Only this regular full normal
ideal uses a vanishing-ideal formula; the lower $I$ is never replaced by the
vanishing ideal of its support.

Finally apply the exact whole strict-boundary trace producer to THIS same
actual $\pi$ and SAME $F$. Its source hypotheses are the original geometry
and intrinsic centre NC, already available. It gives every original indexed
strict trace, born exceptional preimage, and entire chronological list
identity, including every empty member. The same actual comparison therefore
carries BOTH the whole functional old controlled ideal and the complete
boundary dictionary, while the constructed ambient successor retains whole
coherence, normal containment and its true native contact geometry. No
finite-history existence is inferred merely from this one-step result.
\end{proof}
