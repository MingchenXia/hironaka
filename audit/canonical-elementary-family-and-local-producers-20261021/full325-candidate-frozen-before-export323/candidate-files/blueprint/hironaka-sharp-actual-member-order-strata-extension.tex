\section{Finite weighted pieces of an actual adapted centre}

Throughout, the ambient complex manifold has positive dimension. All
ideals below are whole coherent germ ideals, and all orders are actual
ordinary orders along the indicated smooth carrier.

\begin{theorem}[Locally constant profiles cut admissible closed centres]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-locally-constant-admissible-fibres}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualMemberOrderStrata.admissible_fibre_of_eventually_constant}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-family-member-orders-on-product-stratum}
Let $C$ be an admissible centre for a coherent marked ideal $M$ and let
$f$ be a function on the ambient manifold. Suppose that near every
$x\in C$, the restriction of $f$ to $C$ equals $f(x)$. Then every entire
fibre $C\cap f^{-1}(a)$ is again an admissible closed centre for $M$.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Admissibility is local on an ambient open cover. At a point outside the
closed set $C$, use its open complement; the fibre is empty there. At a
point of $C$, choose an ambient open neighbourhood on which $f$ is
constant along $C$. If its value is $a$, the fibre agrees with the whole
of $C$ on this open; otherwise it is empty there. Restrict the original
admissibility in the first case and use empty-centre admissibility in the
second. The local admissibility theorem gives global closed smoothness,
cosupport containment and normal crossings with the unchanged boundary.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual product order gives a finite admissible partition]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-finite-member-order-centre-partition}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualMemberOrderStrata.finite_member_order_strata}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-locally-constant-admissible-fibres,hir:sharp-actual-family-member-orders-on-product-stratum,hir:sharp-actual-finite-relative-product-geometry-order-sum}
Let $F_i$, $i\in\{0,\ldots,m-1\}$, be relatively principal nonzero
coherent ideals on a closed smooth carrier $N$. Let $C\subseteq N$ be
admissible for $M$. Suppose the actual relative product has order $q$
at every point of $C$. For each vector
$\alpha\in\{0,\ldots,q\}^{m}$, put
\[
 C_\alpha=\{x\in C:\operatorname{ord}_{N,x}F_i=\alpha_i
                      \text{ for every }i\}.
\]
These finitely many entire pieces cover $C$, are pairwise disjoint,
and are admissible closed centres for the unchanged $M$.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Product-order additivity makes the sum of the nonnegative member orders
equal $q$, so every member order is at most $q$. This gives a vector in
the stated finite index set at each point of $C$, proving coverage.
Equality of two such vectors follows position by position at a common
point, giving pairwise disjointness. The preceding constant-product
theorem makes the whole member-order vector locally constant along $C$.
Apply the admissible-fibre theorem to that actual vector. Empty families,
empty pieces and $q=0$ require no exceptions to this argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Each piece is adapted to the actual individual marks]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-finite-member-order-weight-admissibility}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualMemberOrderStrata.member_order_strata_weighted_admissible}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-finite-member-order-centre-partition,hir:sharp-original-empty-boundary-ordinary-trace-order-dictionary}
Under the preceding hypotheses, $C_\alpha$ is simultaneously admissible
for every actual marked ideal $(N,E_M,F_i,\alpha_i)$, with the same
unchanged boundary list $E_M$. Its actual member orders are precisely
the specified marks at every point of the entire piece.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding partition gives closed smoothness and normal crossings
with $E_M$ for the entire piece. Its definition gives the exact ordinary
order of each member. The genuine order/cosupport equivalence therefore
places the piece in the cosupport of that member at its specified mark.
These three assertions are exactly admissibility. Marks equal to zero
are retained as literal marked ideals; no positive-mark well-formedness
claim is inferred for them. These are the actual normalised members,
not a replacement for the original ambient strict traces or a proof of
strict decrease of the modified invariant.
\end{proof}
