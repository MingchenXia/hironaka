\section{Actual centre-order reflection and exact weighted admissibility}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-weighted-coordinate-power-reflection}

\begin{theorem}[Actual selected-coordinate order is multiplicative]
\label{hir:actual-selected-coordinate-order-multiplicative}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-center-order-from-support}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedCoordinatePowerReflection.actualFormalCenterOrder_mul,Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedCoordinatePowerReflection.actualFormalCenterOrder_pow}
\leanok
For any coordinate set $A$ and nonzero original complex formal series $F,G$,
the actual $A$-centre order satisfies
$\nu_A(FG)=\nu_A(F)+\nu_A(G)$ and $\nu_A(F^r)=r\nu_A(F)$ for every natural $r$.
The coordinate set may be empty or contain all coordinates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $p=\nu_A(F)$ and $q=\nu_A(G)$. Actual whole centre-power membership gives
$F\in I_A^p$ and $G\in I_A^q$, hence $FG\in I_A^{p+q}$ and
$\nu_A(FG)\ge p+q$. The degree-$p$ selected-weight component of $F$ is nonzero:
an actual nonzero coefficient attains the defining minimum $p$ and remains
unchanged in this component. Likewise the degree-$q$ component of $G$ is nonzero.
All lower selected-weight coefficients vanish. The degree-$(p+q)$ component of
the product is therefore the product of those two components, by actual
finite-antidiagonal multiplication. The formal series ring is a domain, so
this component is nonzero. An order greater than $p+q$ would make every one of
its coefficients zero, a contradiction. Thus equality holds. The unit has
centre order zero, as witnessed by its nonzero constant coefficient. Induction
on $r$, starting with this unit and applying multiplicativity, proves the power
identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual normal powers reflect positive powers of the original germ]
\label{hir:actual-normal-coordinate-power-reflection}
\uses{hir:actual-selected-coordinate-order-multiplicative,hir:actual-formal-normal-power-contracts-exact-original-analytic-power}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedCoordinatePowerReflection.mem_formal_coordinate_power_mul_iff,Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedCoordinatePowerReflection.mem_normal_power_mul_iff}
\leanok
For $r>0$ and every genuine original analytic germ $f$,
\[
 f^r\in N^{wr}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f\in N^w,
\]
where $N$ is the whole original normal-coordinate ideal. The corresponding
statement holds for every formal coordinate-centre ideal $I_A$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If the series is zero both memberships hold. Otherwise the proved actual
maximal-power dictionary changes formal membership into the numerical
inequality $wr\le\nu_A(F^r)=r\nu_A(F)$. Since $r>0$, this is equivalent to
$w\le\nu_A(F)$, hence to $F\in I_A^w$. For an original analytic germ, apply
the actual Taylor homomorphism, which preserves powers. Its entire normal-power
contraction identifies both analytic memberships with these exact formal
memberships. This proves both directions without assuming a power-reflection
or order certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Common-mark compression has exactly the original weighted centre conditions]
\label{hir:actual-weighted-compression-exact-centre-admissibility}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-coordinate-power-reflection,hir:actual-weighted-coefficient-common-mark}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualWeightedCoordinatePowerReflection.weightedCompression_le_normal_power_iff}
\leanok
Let $c_i$ be finitely many original analytic germs with positive integer weights
$w_i$, and let $D=\prod_iw_i$. For their actual compressed ideal
$J=(c_i^{D/w_i}:i)$ one has
\[
 J\subseteq N^D\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \text{for every }i,\ c_i\in N^{w_i}.
\]
In particular the equivalence applies to the literal finite coefficient family
internally constructed from an original analytic vertex ideal, and to its
actual transformed coefficient family with the same weights.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An entire span lies in an ideal exactly when every actual generator does.
Thus the left side is equivalent to $c_i^{D/w_i}\in N^D$ for every $i$.
Each positive weight divides $D$, and its quotient is positive, with
$w_i(D/w_i)=D$. Substitute this equality and apply positive-power reflection
to each unchanged original germ. Both implications follow. With an empty
family the span is zero and the right side is vacuous, so the equivalence still
holds.
\end{proof}

This proves exact weighted coordinate-centre conditions, not an identification
with a current Hilbert--Samuel locus. A persistent weighted presentation and its
iterated geometric admissibility and strict progress require further results.
