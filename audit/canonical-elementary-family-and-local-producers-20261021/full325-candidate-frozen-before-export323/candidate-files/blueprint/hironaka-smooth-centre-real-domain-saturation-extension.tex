\section{Exact original saturation on the real nonlinear domain}

The original flattened ideal is coherent on its real target. The actual
Oka and Noetherian saturation arguments require finite generation near
zero, rather than coherence on the whole ambient model. Thus they apply
to this true local domain without any extension assumption.

\begin{lemma}[Real local coherence supplies the actual finite analytic presentation]
\label{hir:real-domain-coherence-actual-finite-analytic-presentation}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-centre-domain-original-pullback-coherence,hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-finite-span}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainSaturation.idealFGAt_of_coherentOn}
\leanok
If an actual germ ideal $F$ is coherent on a real open $W$ and $y\in W$,
then its literal membership predicate has an actual finite holomorphic
presentation near $y$. No coherence outside $W$ is needed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The real local coherence gives an actual open $V$ containing $y$,
contained in $W$, with finitely many actual holomorphic generators and
complete membership at every point of $V$. On a complex Euclidean
model, holomorphic manifold derivatives and the corresponding complex
Fr\'echet derivatives agree. Hence those actual generators and
coefficient germs give precisely a finite analytic span presentation
on $V$. Both implications of the membership dictionary are the same
actual finite equality with the same coefficient functions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original input supplies one real domain for exact all-power saturation]
\label{hir:real-domain-original-exact-uniform-saturation}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-coherence-actual-finite-analytic-presentation,hir:real-centre-domain-actual-flattened-coherent-family,hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-neighborhood-stability}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainSaturation.exists_original_regular_real_domain_exact_uniform_saturation}
\leanok
Let $A$ be originally coherent, let $C$ be originally regular at $x$,
and let $h$ be an actual holomorphic function near zero in the ambient
model. Original data internally supply a genuine flattening $\Phi$,
an integer $N$, and ONE actual open $U$ containing zero in the real
$\Phi.\mathrm{target}$, such that for $F=f^*A$ and EVERY $y\in U$:
all actual repeated colons $F:h^{N+q}$ equal $F:h^N$; multiplication
by every power $h^q$ for $q\geq N$ has exactly the same membership
condition as multiplication by $h^N$; and the whole saturation union
is exactly $F:h^N$. One internally selected finite actual holomorphic
family presents this same fixed colon at every point of the SAME $U$.
The caller supplies no stable index, nearby equality, finite colon
family or saturation certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original regularity and original coherence give the genuine flattening
and coherence of $F$ on its real target, with zero in that target.
The preceding lemma gives actual finite analytic generation near zero.
The ambient holomorphic germ ring is Noetherian, so its ascending chain
of actual repeated colons by the actual germ of $h$ has two equal
successive ideals at an internally selected index $N$. The actual
whole finite span dictionary identifies those ideals with the genuine
origin membership conditions. Oka's theorem supplies actual local
finite presentations of both successive analytic colons. Spread the
finite functional membership witnesses of each presentation in the
other to one open neighbourhood; the two inclusions give equality of
the whole actual colon membership conditions at every nearby point.
Iterating the literal colon definition gives equality of every later
colon with that fixed colon at those same points. The original finite
analytic presentation gives holomorphic ideal membership and closure
under multiplication by $h$. Repeated colons are therefore exactly
colon by actual powers, proving the stated all-power dictionary, and
the increasing-chain property together with later stabilization proves
that the entire saturation union equals the fixed colon.
Oka's theorem also supplies finite actual holomorphic generators of
that fixed colon near zero. Intersect their presentation open with the
stability open and the actual real flattening target. The resulting
single open still contains zero and carries all stated equalities and
the same fixed finite presentation. No property outside the real
domain has been assumed or inferred.
\end{proof}
