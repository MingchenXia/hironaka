\section{Actual intrinsic holomorphic germs extend through true flattening charts}

All intrinsic structures below are the genuine native flattening atlas
constructed from original regularity. No ambient extension or ideal
comparison certificate is assumed.

\begin{theorem}[Every actual intrinsic holomorphic germ has a genuine local ambient lift]
\label{hir:actual-submanifold-holomorphic-germ-extension}
\uses{ana:submanifold-complex-structure}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualSubmanifoldGermExtension.exists_ambient_holomorphic_germ}
\leanok
Let $W\subset X^n$ be an original subset regular of dimension $k\le n$
at every one of its points, equipped with its actually constructed native
complex manifold atlas. For every $p\in W$ and every actual intrinsic
holomorphic scalar germ $b$ at $p$, there is an actual ambient scalar
function $B$, holomorphic near $p$, whose restriction has the same entire
germ as $b$. No global holomorphic extension is claimed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the actual original flattening datum $F$ at $p$, and its native
intrinsic chart $e$. Write $G(y)=\operatorname{pr}_k(F_{\rm flat}(F_{\rm amb}(y)))$
and use the literal total map $R(y)=e^{-1}(G(y))$ into $W$. At $p$ the
coordinate $G(p)$ lies in the true target of $e$. Actual ambient chart
holomorphy, the true flattening source and the linear projection make
$G$ holomorphic on the real ambient source. Shrink an actual neighbourhood
of $p$ so that every $G(y)$ lies in the actual target of $e$. The genuine
native atlas inverse theorem then makes $R$ holomorphic at every point
of this neighbourhood. The actual chart inverse identity gives
$R(p)=p$ and $R(y)=y$ for all nearby $y\in W$. Thus $B=b\circ R$ is
holomorphic near $p$ by the actual germ composition theorem and agrees
with $b$ on all nearby intrinsic points. Values chosen by the total chart
inverse outside its real target are never asserted to be holomorphic.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The entire vanishing ideal of every singular intrinsic subset is a genuine restriction]
\label{hir:actual-submanifold-whole-singular-vanishing-restriction}
\uses{hir:actual-submanifold-holomorphic-germ-extension}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualSubmanifoldGermExtension.pullback_vanishing_image}
\leanok
For the same genuine native $W$ and its actual inclusion $j:W\to X$,
every original subset $V\subset W$ satisfies the whole independent
functional ideal equality
\[
 j^*\mathcal I_{j(V)}=\mathcal I_V.
\]
No smoothness, closedness, analyticity or coherence of $V$ is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual continuity of $j$ pulls each genuine ambient vanishing germ back
to a holomorphic germ vanishing on $V$ near the intrinsic point. A finite
actual holomorphic span preserves this property, proving one inclusion
for the entire functional pullback ideal. Conversely take an actual
$b\in\mathcal I_{V,p}$ and lift it by the preceding theorem to a genuine
ambient holomorphic $B$. The entire germ equality and actual vanishing
of $b$ hold simultaneously on a neighbourhood in $W$. The genuine subtype
neighbourhood formula supplies an ambient neighbourhood whose intersection
with $W$ lies in that whole intrinsic neighbourhood. Thus $B$ vanishes
on every point of $j(V)$ in this actual ambient neighbourhood, so
$B\in\mathcal I_{j(V),j(p)}$. Its one-term actual pullback span, together
with the true germ equality, contains $b$. This is equality of entire
functional ideals, rather than a point-value or radical substitute.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual intrinsic restriction reflects the whole ambient restricted comparison]
\label{hir:actual-submanifold-intrinsic-restrict-comparison-iff}
\uses{hir:actual-submanifold-holomorphic-germ-extension}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualSubmanifoldGermExtension.restrictLe_iff_restrictIdeal_le}
\leanok
For the same original regular $W$, let $\mathfrak a,\mathfrak b$ be
original ambient functional ideals and let $\mathfrak a$ be coherent.
Then the original ambient restricted comparison
$\operatorname{RestrictLe}(W,\mathfrak a,\mathfrak b)$ holds if and only if
the entire intrinsic ideals satisfy
$\mathfrak a\mathcal O_W\subseteq\mathfrak b\mathcal O_W$.
No coherence of $\mathfrak b$ or coefficient-extension premise is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The proved ambient-to-intrinsic comparison theorem restricts every
actual ambient coefficient and gives the forward implication. For the
converse, take an original ambient member $g\in\mathfrak a_{j(p)}$.
Coherence supplies its genuine holomorphy, so its restriction lies in
the intrinsic extension of $\mathfrak a$. The given entire intrinsic
ideal inclusion writes this actual restriction as a finite holomorphic
combination of restrictions of true original members of $\mathfrak b$.
For each of these finitely many actual intrinsic coefficients, use the
first theorem to construct a true ambient holomorphic lift. Intersect
the finitely many actual germ equalities with the original finite span
identity. The subtype neighbourhood formula turns this simultaneous
identity into an actual ambient neighbourhood on whose intersection
with $W$ the required whole finite relation holds. These literal ambient
coefficients and original generators prove the ambient restricted
comparison at every original point and for every original member.
\end{proof}
