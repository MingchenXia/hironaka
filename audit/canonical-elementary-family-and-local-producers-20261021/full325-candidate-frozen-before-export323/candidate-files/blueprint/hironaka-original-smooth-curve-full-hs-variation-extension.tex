\begin{proof}

family of its existing intrinsic HS values, indexed by the genuine
position/order pairs. Apply the previously independent actual function
variation predicate to THIS function. Equality of these actual functions
means equality at EVERY position and EVERY order. Thus the definition is
well defined without selecting presentations, ranks, denominators or a
finite-order surrogate.
\end{proof}
