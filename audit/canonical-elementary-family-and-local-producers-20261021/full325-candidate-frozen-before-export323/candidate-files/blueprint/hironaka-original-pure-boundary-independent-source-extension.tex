\section{An independently stopped intrinsic source with its own boundary}

Retain an original compact analytic carrier $W\subseteq X^n$, pure of
dimension $d<n$, an original ordered SNC family $E_0$ and
$W\subseteq\overline{W\setminus|E_0|}$.  For an arbitrary holomorphic
submersion $b:Y^m\to X$, put
\[
 W'=b^{-1}W,\quad E'_0=b^{-1}E_0,\quad d'=d+(m-n),\quad
 M=(X,X,E_0,I_W,1),\quad M'=(Y,Y,E'_0,I_{W'},1).
\]
The actual whole vanishing-ideal pullback theorem identifies $b^*M=M'$.
The original intrinsic good-open and bad-set dictionaries identify
$O'=b^{-1}O$ and $F'=b^{-1}F$ with the source's own dimension-$d'$
definitions.  There is no global dimension inequality imposed on an
empty source.

\begin{lemma}[Empty histories transport the original boundary and only discard empty slots]
\label{hir:original-empty-histories-filtered-boundary-comparison}
\uses{hir:original-all-empty-whole-boundary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureBoundaryIndependentSource.empty_towers_filtered_boundary_transport}
\leanok
Let $A$ be an original marked record with closed initial boundary
members.  For actual all-empty towers $S,Q$ of $A$ and any endpoint map
$a$ satisfying $p_Qa=p_S$, the ordered nonempty boundary lists satisfy
\[
 E_S^{\ne\varnothing}=((a^{-1}E_Q))^{\ne\varnothing}.
\]
The actual lengths may differ; the initial boundary need not be empty.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The whole ordered empty-history formula gives
$E_S=p_S^{-1}E_A$ followed by exactly $\operatorname{length}S$ empty
members, and the corresponding formula for $Q$.  Taking inverse images
of the latter by $a$ replaces its original prefix by
$(p_Qa)^{-1}E_A=p_S^{-1}E_A$ and leaves its entire empty suffix empty.
Filtering deletes both empty suffixes.  The two remaining ordered
prefix filters are literally the same.  No equality of the two
unfiltered lists or of their lengths follows or is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Any intrinsic source first-meeting prefix compares with the same target]
\label{hir:original-pure-boundary-independent-intrinsic-comparison}
\uses{hir:actual-singular-vanishing-ideal-pullback-submersion,hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-singular-boundary-good,hir:original-pure-boundary-prefix-smooth-comparison,hir:original-first-meeting-prefix-map-unique,hir:original-first-meeting-filtered-boundary,hir:original-empty-histories-filtered-boundary-comparison,hir:normal-crossings-delete-empty-boundary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureBoundaryIndependentSource.independent_intrinsic_source_prefix_comparison}
\leanok
For this finite-history comparison, compactness of $W$ is unnecessary.
Let $T$ be an actual original target canonical prefix avoiding $O$,
whose next whole maximum meets $O$ when $W$ is nonempty, and let $Q$ be
its actual smooth pullback.  Let $S$ be any independent finite canonical
history of the intrinsic $M'$ avoiding its own $O'$; when $W'$ is
nonempty, suppose its next whole maximum meets $O'$.
There is a unique endpoint biholomorphism $\eta$ from $S$ to $Q$ over
$Y$.  With $G=g_Q\eta$, the projection square for $p_S,p_T,b,G$ is
holomorphic Cartesian and $G$ is a submersion.  Their conventional strict
carriers correspond as whole sets, and
\[
 E_S^{\ne\varnothing}=((G^{-1}E_T))^{\ne\varnothing}.
\]
The source has its own actual SNC boundary.  Target smoothness,
dimension $d$ and normal crossings imply source smoothness, dimension
$d'$ and normal crossings with its own $E_S$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The whole singular vanishing-ideal pullback equality gives the literal
initial record equality $b^*M=M'$, including its entire ordered original
boundary.  Transport source canonicality along this actual equality.
The proved intrinsic good-open equality identifies source avoidance and
its stipulated encounter with those over $b^{-1}O$.

If $W'$ is nonempty, openness of $b$ and density of $W\cap O$ give a
point of $W'$ over $O$.  Lift it through the surjective actual $Q$.
The target pure-carrier maximum dictionary puts its image in the target
whole maximum; the actual whole-value ambient dimension embedding
makes this source lift a whole source maximum over $O'$.  Thus both
$S$ and $Q$ satisfy the same original source first-meeting conditions.
Actual first-meeting uniqueness supplies $\eta$, and its filtered
boundary theorem gives the asserted source comparison.

If $W'$ is empty, its actual original cosupport is empty.  Every actual
canonical centre in $S$ and $Q$ is empty, and both histories are
terminal.  Genuine terminal endpoint uniqueness supplies $\eta$.
The preceding all-empty comparison supplies the same filtered boundary
equality, retaining the possibly nonempty initial ordered boundary.

In either case compose the actual upper map of $Q$ with $\eta$.
Biholomorphism and actual blowup universal properties give the stated
submersion and Cartesian square.  The projection identity transports
the entire conventional strict carrier through $\eta$; the actual
smooth strict-transform theorem for $Q$ identifies it with the inverse
image of the target strict carrier.  Smooth submersion pullback of
target regular coordinates gives source smoothness and dimension.
Pull back target normal-crossings coordinates, then use the filtered
boundary equality and invariance under deleting empty members to
obtain normal crossings with the source's own actual boundary.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original data internally select an independent intrinsic stopped source]
\label{hir:original-pure-boundary-independent-intrinsic-source}
\uses{hir:original-pure-boundary-canonical-first-prefix,hir:original-pure-boundary-independent-intrinsic-comparison,hir:original-global-finite-smooth-source-history,hir:original-cosupport-persistence-avoided-open,hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-pure-boundary-density,hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-singular-boundary-good}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureBoundaryIndependentSource.exists_original_independently_stopped_intrinsic_source}
\leanok
Only the preceding original $W,X,E_0$ data and the actual smooth map
$b$ internally construct the actual target canonical stopped prefix
$T$ and its tail, a separately stopped canonical source prefix $S$ and
its tail in the actual smooth pullback of the full target terminal
history, and an upper submersion $G$ with the preceding Cartesian,
strict-carrier and filtered own-boundary comparisons.

The source is canonical for its literal intrinsic $M'$, avoids its own
$O'$, and has its next whole maximum encounter there when $W'$ is
nonempty.  If $W'$ is empty its selected prefix is the identity and
the full source history has only empty centres.  Its actual accumulated
boundary is SNC, its own strict carrier is regular of dimension $d'$
and has normal crossings with this same boundary.  It is admissible
relative to $E'_0$ with centres over its intrinsic $F'$, and its whole
new-boundary union contains its exceptional locus and lies over $F'$.
The inverse image of its intrinsic singular set lies in its exceptional
locus.  Its projection is holomorphic, proper, surjective and an
isomorphism over $Y\setminus F'$.  The complete target elementary
dictionary and both actual history factorizations are retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual original target prefix construction once, retaining its
full canonical terminal history $P$, stopped prefix $T$, tail and
complete elementary boundary geometry.  Construct the actual smooth
pullback $B$ of this full $P$.  The original finite-source theorem gives
canonicality and terminality for $b^*M$, hence for the intrinsic $M'$
by the whole initial record equality.

If $W'$ is nonempty, openness and the original density give a point over
$W\cap O$.  If all centres of $B$ avoided $O'$, lift this point by
surjectivity.  Actual cosupport persistence would put its lift in the
terminal empty cosupport, a contradiction.  The genuine first-centre
splitting of this same $B$ therefore supplies an independently stopped
$S$, its literal tail, canonicality, avoidance and the source whole
maximum encounter.  If $W'$ is empty, choose the identity prefix and
$B$ as its tail; empty original cosupport forces every actual centre
of $B$ to be empty.

Apply the preceding comparison to this $S$ and the actual pullback of
the already selected $T$.  It gives the asserted same-map Cartesian,
strict-carrier, own-boundary and regularity comparisons.  Source
canonicality gives actual testness and its own SNC boundary.  Its
centres lie over $W'$ and over the complement of its intrinsic $O'$,
whose intersection is contained in $F'$.  This proves admissibility
and the new-boundary support over $F'$; the actual exceptional support
formula supplies its full exceptional containments.  Source strict
regularity gives the intrinsic singular-preimage containment.  Finally
the actual finite source tower supplies holomorphicity, properness
and surjectivity, and the exceptional image containment gives the
isomorphism off $F'$.  No source compactness, source history, chart,
ideal equality or comparison witness was a caller input.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
These conclusions concern the stated compact original pure-dimensional
carrier, including reducible and empty cases, and arbitrary initial
SNC boundary.  They do not equate actual stopping indices or unfiltered
accumulated boundary lists of independently stopped histories.
Arbitrary non-pure dimension-part composites and separate relatively
compact elementary variants require their own compatibility statements.
\end{remark}
