\section{Actual global canonical resolution of original normal-crossing carriers}

\begin{theorem}[Finite entire coordinate maximum candidates at every actual length]
\label{hir:general-nc-finite-entire-prefix-maximum-candidates}
\uses{hir:canonical-word-finite-attained-maxima}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsGlobalCanonicalResolution.finite_coordinate_maximum_candidates}
\leanok
For each fixed actual prefix length $j$, only finitely many entire
attained maximum values occur among all weak canonical histories of
all original coordinate normal-crossing marking-one models in the
given dimension. No geometric finite-orbit assertion or full value-image
finiteness is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
There are finitely many subsets of the finite set of ambient coordinate
indices. Each such subset fixes one whole original coordinate marked
record. The genuine recipe-history theorem gives finitely many entire
attained maximum candidates for this record at length $j$, retaining
every empty and full-maximum historical slot. Take the finite union of
these finite candidate sets over the actual coordinate subsets.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original general NC prefix attains its entire global maximum]
\label{hir:general-nc-actual-entire-global-maximum}
\uses{hir:general-nc-finite-entire-prefix-maximum-candidates,hir:general-nc-every-actual-canonical-prefix-coordinate-history,hir:general-nc-actual-chart-history-whole-value-and-cosupport,hir:homogeneous-every-whole-value-over-origin-neighbourhood,hir:homogeneous-model-every-prefix-whole-maximum}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsGlobalCanonicalResolution.nc_maxLocus_nonempty}
\leanok
For an original general normal-crossing carrier $H$, its unchanged
whole datum $M_H=(X,X,\varnothing,I_H,1)$, and any actual weak canonical
prefix with nonempty cosupport, the entire global invariant maximum
is attained. No compactness, selected model histories, proposed maximum,
finite value-image or termination certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix an actual cosupport point and choose the internally produced
original chart history at its image in $X$. It gives an entire
coordinate canonical history of exactly the same length, with genuine
whole cosupport and invariant comparisons on the literal restrictions.
Its cosupport is nonempty, hence its entire maximum is attained by the
genuine homogeneous model theorem. That maximum value is among the
finite candidates of the first theorem.

Every entire model value, including this maximum, is represented over
the actual origin-neighbourhood chart target. Carry such a maximizing
representative back through the actual chart-history biholomorphism.
It is an actual point of the original cosupport with exactly that same
candidate value. The original point's value is at most this value,
by the actual whole model maximum and the genuine value comparison.
Thus every original value is bounded above by an actually attained
candidate value in one fixed finite set.

Intersect that finite candidate set with the actual image of the
original cosupport. This intersection is nonempty by the preceding
construction. Its largest element is the value of an actual original
cosupport point. Every original value is bounded by a member of the
intersection, hence by this largest element. Its actual preimage is
therefore a point of the entire original maximum locus.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine global full-maximum successor for original general NC data]
\label{hir:general-nc-actual-global-canonical-successor}
\uses{hir:general-nc-actual-entire-global-maximum}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsGlobalCanonicalResolution.exists_nc_canonical_successor}
\leanok
Every nonterminal actual canonical prefix of that same original
general NC datum internally supplies a genuine blowup of its nonempty
entire global maximum, and an actual canonical test successor.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding theorem supplies nonemptiness of the actual whole maximum.
The original NC geometry puts the unchanged original marked record in
the invariant domain. The actual invariant theorem makes its whole
maximum a closed smooth admissible centre, and the original positive
mark and domain imply its interior is empty. Genuine smooth-centre
blowup existence now produces an actual complex manifold and actual
blowup along this centre. Append it to the same original prefix; actual
admissibility gives testness and the literal full-maximum equation gives
canonicality. No successor space or centre is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A literal finite canonical suffix and direct original canonical resolution]
\label{hir:general-nc-actual-global-canonical-finite-resolution}
\uses{hir:general-nc-actual-global-canonical-successor,hir:general-nc-actual-entire-global-maximum,hir:homogeneous-actual-finite-canonical-resolution-suffix}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsGlobalCanonicalResolution.exists_nc_resolution_suffix,Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsGlobalCanonicalResolution.exists_canonicalInvariant_nc_resolution}
\leanok
For a proved resolution invariant with genuinely well-founded value
order, every nonempty-centre canonical segment of the original datum
of a general NC carrier has a literal finite appended suffix resolving
that same datum. In particular the actual original
$\operatorname{canonicalInvariant}(n)$ gives such a finite canonical
resolution directly from $H$ alone, using its existing well-founded
value-type instance. The ambient manifold and carrier need not be compact.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on an upper bound in the genuine well-founded value order,
retaining the actual original prefix. If its cosupport is empty, the
identity suffix suffices. Otherwise the second theorem gives its entire
maximum and the third theorem constructs a true next blowup of that
whole nonempty maximum. Every new cosupport value is strictly below
the old entire maximum, by the actual on-centre strict decrease and
off-centre invariance law of the proved resolution invariant. If the
new cosupport is empty, this one step is the suffix. Otherwise its
entire maximum is attained again by the second theorem, hence is
strictly below the preceding bound. Apply the well-founded induction
hypothesis to this same literal extended history. Append the one-step
suffix to its returned actual suffix; associativity gives the required
literal appended history and retains all nonempty whole-maximum centres.
Start at the identity segment and specialize to the genuine existing
well-founded value type of the actual canonical invariant in dimension
$n$. No external numerical bound or terminal-history certificate is used.
\end{proof}

These results concern $I_H$ with marking one and empty original boundary.
They do not yet permit an arbitrary merely normal-crossing initial
boundary for an arbitrary marked datum; that original G9 endpoint and
its joint strict-trace/admissibility/good-locus dictionary remain separate.
