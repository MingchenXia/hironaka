\section{Actual global histories for discrete original cosupport}
\label{sec:hironaka-discrete-cosupport-canonical-history}

\begin{theorem}[Original discrete cosupport internally gives compact isolating opens]
\label{hir:original-discrete-cosupport-isolating-opens}
\uses{hir:open-restriction-marked,hir:marked-ideal,hir:invariant-driver}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaDiscreteCosupportCanonicalHistory.isolatingOpen,Hironaka.HironakaDiscreteCosupportCanonicalHistory.isolatingOpen_inter_cosupp,Hironaka.HironakaDiscreteCosupportCanonicalHistory.isolatingOpen_cover,Hironaka.HironakaDiscreteCosupportCanonicalHistory.isolatingOpen_support_disjoint,Hironaka.HironakaDiscreteCosupportCanonicalHistory.isCompact_isolating_cosupp}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an original marked ideal with coherent ideal and
discrete original cosupport $S$. From these original data
one can internally choose opens $U_a$, indexed by $a\in S$,
such that $a\in U_a$, $U_a\cap S=\{a\}$, and the entire
original restricted cosupport of $M|_{U_a}$ is a compact
singleton. These opens cover $S$ and are pairwise disjoint
on $S$. They need not be disjoint in the ambient manifold.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Discreteness of the subspace $S$ makes each singleton open
in $S$. By the literal subspace-topology definition choose
an ambient open $U_a$ whose intersection with $S$ is precisely
$\{a\}$. This also gives $a\in U_a$, the cover property,
and disjointness on $S$ of opens indexed by distinct points.
The coherent original marked-record restriction dictionary
identifies the entire cosupport of $M|_{U_a}$ with the
inverse image of $S$ under the true open inclusion. The
displayed intersection equality identifies that actual
inverse image with the singleton represented by $a$ inside
$U_a$. Singletons are compact, which proves compactness
of the genuine restricted original cosupport.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Support-disjoint refinement constructs an actual growing history]
\label{hir:support-disjoint-canonical-growing-history}
\uses{hir:support-disjoint-canonical-global-refinement,hir:componentwise-canonical-growing-history,hir:support-disjoint-whole-canonical-centre}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaDiscreteCosupportCanonicalHistory.exists_support_disjoint_canonical_sequence,Hironaka.HironakaDiscreteCosupportCanonicalHistory.restricted_steps_are_support_maxima}
\leanok
Let original opens $U_i$ cover the original cosupport $S$,
with pairwise disjoint intersections $U_i\cap S$, and let
$M$ belong to the domain of a proved invariant $R$.
Then the original input internally constructs a genuine
global growing test history, blowing up the whole union
of the current canonical maximum centres on the true
restrictions to the fixed opens $U_i$. All those actual
restrictions remain canonical. Every next restricted
centre is its entire current maximum, including empty
centres, and every recorded absolute stage is retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use as states the actual global test prefixes equipped
with canonical restrictions to the fixed original opens.
The original reflexive tower is a state. The genuine
support-disjoint global refinement supplies a next state,
including its actual blowup map and whole parallel centre.
Choose a next state for each state and iterate from the
reflexive one. The exact recorded next-step equalities
give a true growing sequence. Restrict each actual blowup
to $U_i$; its true centre is the inverse image of the whole
parallel centre. The proved support-disjoint trace formula
identifies it with the entire local current maximum.
Thus the same restricted sequence has actual whole-maximum
steps, without deleting empty stages.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original discrete cosupport gives a locally terminal noncompact elementary history]
\label{hir:original-discrete-cosupport-locally-terminal-history}
\uses{hir:original-discrete-cosupport-isolating-opens,hir:support-disjoint-canonical-growing-history,hir:compact-cosupport-maximum-sequence-terminates,hir:growing-canonical-sequence-closed-images,hir:growing-canonical-sequence-isomorphism-tail,hir:growing-sequence-empty-restricted-tails}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaDiscreteCosupportCanonicalHistory.locally_finite_empty_tails_support_disjoint,Hironaka.HironakaDiscreteCosupportCanonicalHistory.exists_original_discrete_cosupport_locally_terminal_history}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an original marked ideal of the actual canonical
invariant's domain, and suppose only the additional geometric
hypothesis that its original cosupport is discrete. Then
the original input internally constructs an actual growing
global test history, with globally closed smooth admissible
centres and proper actual blowups, whose restrictions to
the internally chosen isolating opens are canonical and
eventually resolutions. The actual centre images are
closed and locally finite. Every point of the original
ambient manifold has a genuine open neighbourhood over
which all sufficiently late actual step maps are
isomorphisms and every entire finite later tail restricts
to an empty-centre tower. The ambient manifold may be
connected and noncompact; the terminal times may be unbounded.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The invariant-domain condition makes the original cosupport
$S$ closed. Choose the isolating opens above internally.
Apply the constructed support-disjoint growing history to
them. The same actual restricted history on each $U_a$
has whole maximum steps and compact original cosupport,
the literal singleton above. Per-sequence finiteness and
properness therefore give an eventual resolution for this
same restriction by the compact-original-cosupport theorem.

For sufficiently late indices on $U_a$, its current
cosupport and hence its whole maximum centre are empty.
The exact whole-centre trace formula implies that the
actual next global centre misses the full preimage of
$U_a$. Outside the closed original cosupport, every
current cosupport and every constructed centre are absent
at every stage, so the fixed genuine open $X\setminus S$
is avoided from the start. These opens cover the ambient
manifold and each meets actual centre images at only
finitely many indices. This proves local finiteness.
Actual centre images are closed because each actual
centre is closed and its entire prefix projection is proper.

Every actual step missing such an open preimage is an
isomorphism over it, by the genuine off-centre blowup
isomorphism. The actual prefix--tail projection equality
then shows inductively that every centre of any entire
later finite tail restricts to the empty set on that same
open. This gives the asserted actual empty and isomorphic
tails. No centres are cut off and no independently chosen
terminal histories or stage-alignment certificates are used.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Discreteness is a hypothesis on the ORIGINAL cosupport,
not on any selected stage centre. It includes empty
cosupport, when the internally chosen family is empty
and the whole ambient open has empty tails from the start.
An arbitrary positive-dimensional coherent cosupport need
not be discrete. The unrestricted connected noncompact
elementary scheduler remains a separate obligation.
\end{remark}
