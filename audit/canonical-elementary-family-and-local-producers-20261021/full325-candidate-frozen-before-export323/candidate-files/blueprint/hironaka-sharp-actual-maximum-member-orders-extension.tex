\section{Actual original-member order profiles on one full maximum centre}

Assume the ambient dimension $n$ is positive throughout.
Let $N$ be a closed smooth carrier and $F_i$ a finite family of coherent,
relatively principal, nonzero whole germ ideals. All ordinary orders below
are the actual orders along $N$, with the original finite positions retained.

\begin{theorem}[A constant product order fixes every individual order locally]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-family-member-orders-on-product-stratum}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualMaximumMemberOrders.eventually_member_orders_of_constant_product}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-actual-member-trace-orders-finite-usc,hir:sharp-actual-finite-relative-product-geometry-order-sum}
Suppose $C\subseteq N$ and the whole relative product $\prod_i F_i$ has
constant actual ordinary order on $C$. At every $x\in C$, there is an
ambient neighbourhood such that all $y\in C$ in it satisfy simultaneously
\[
 \operatorname{ord}_{N,y}(F_i)=\operatorname{ord}_{N,x}(F_i)
 \quad\text{for every original position }i.
\]
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Coherence, relative principality and actual nonzeroness give the whole
product geometry and the literal equality of its order with the finite
sum of the member orders. Upper semicontinuity gives an ambient
neighbourhood on which each member order is at most its value at $x$.
Intersect these finitely many neighbourhoods. For $y\in C$ there, the
product-order hypothesis makes the two finite sums equal. Equality of
finite sums of natural numbers, with every summand bounded above by the
corresponding summand at $x$, forces equality at every position. Empty
families are covered by the same finite-sum argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The complete proper-position profile is fixed on the same neighbourhood]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-family-proper-profile-on-product-stratum}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualMaximumMemberOrders.eventually_member_orders_and_proper_positions}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-family-member-orders-on-product-stratum,hir:sharp-original-empty-boundary-ordinary-trace-order-dictionary}
Under the preceding hypotheses, the same neighbourhood can be chosen
so that the entire actual finite set of proper ideal positions at $y$
equals that at $x$. This concerns the unchanged whole germ predicates;
no indexed count replaces the actual proper-position set.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a coherent ideal, the actual germ is proper exactly when the point
belongs to its whole zero locus. On $N$, the genuine order/cosupport
dictionary identifies this condition with ordinary order at least one.
Thus equality of every individual order on the preceding neighbourhood
gives both directions of membership for every finite position. Finite-set
extensionality proves the asserted equality, simultaneously with all
order equalities. No equality of distinct ideal-germ classes is inferred.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original geometry internally supplies the adapted full maximum centre]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-full-maximum-original-member-orders}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualMaximumMemberOrders.exists_actual_dynamic_centre_member_order_profile}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-dynamic-first-full-born-incidence,hir:sharp-original-chronological-full-product-geometry-order-sum,hir:sharp-actual-family-proper-profile-on-product-stratum}
Let $W$ be a compact smooth positive-pure-dimensional carrier, let $E$
be an actual ambient SNC list, and suppose $W$ is dense away from its
union. At every genuine finite test prefix for the empty-boundary
carrier template, if the actual chronological FIRST defect is nonempty,
there is one nonempty actual FULL maximum centre $C$ in that defect,
admissible for both the actual current product and the carrier template.
For each $x\in C$, all original normalised chronological member orders,
the whole proper-position set and every born-boundary incidence are
constant near $x$ along that same $C$.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the original full-maximum/born-incidence producer once and retain its
chosen $C$, both admissibility assertions and its actual maximum equality.
Any two points of this maximum have equal whole current canonical values.
Taking the genuine first coordinate and using the actual normalised-product
order dictionary shows that the entire chronological product has equal
ordinary order at the two points. The whole product is the actual finite
relative product of the original chronological member family.

The actual test-prefix geometry internally supplies coherence, relative
principality and nonzeroness of every such member on the same current
carrier. The carrier is smooth by the actual template test tower. Apply
the preceding two theorems to this very family and centre. Intersect their
neighbourhood with the retained born-incidence neighbourhood, leaving
$C$ unchanged. This yields all conclusions simultaneously. No order,
incidence or terminal-history certificate is an original input. This
proves local adaptation of the actual normalised member profile only;
strict original ambient trace-factor compatibility, full modified-history
decrease, later joint boundary admissibility and sharp termination remain
separate obligations.
\end{proof}
