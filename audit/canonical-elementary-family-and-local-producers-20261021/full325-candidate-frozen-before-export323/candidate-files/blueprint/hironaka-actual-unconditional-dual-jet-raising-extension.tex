\section{Unconditional holomorphic raising for original jet annihilators}

\begin{theorem}[An actual nonzero generator gives a genuine open unit-ideal domain]
\label{hir:actual-nonzero-generator-open-unit-ideal-domain}
\uses{hir:actual-original-hs-zero-constant-centre-generators-vanish,hir:actual-every-original-ideal-finite-jet-columns}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualUnconditionalDualJetRaising.actual_translatedIdeal_eq_top_of_generator_ne_zero,Hironaka.HironakaActualUnconditionalDualJetRaising.exists_actual_tangent_unit_ideal_neighborhood}
\leanok
Let $G$ be an actual holomorphic family on a real open $U$, and let
$V$ be real open with $T(V)\subset U$. If $c\in V$ and some actual
$G_i(Tc)$ is nonzero, then there is a real open $Z$ with
$c\in Z\subset V$ such that the entire original translated ideal
$I_b$ is the unit ideal for every $b\in Z$. This is an actual open
domain assertion, not merely a unit-ideal equality at one point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First, at any actual point where $G_i$ is nonzero, suppose the entire
translated ideal were proper. It is contained in the local maximal
ideal. Its actual translated generator belongs to it, so evaluating
that germ at zero gives $G_i$ at the actual point equal to zero,
a contradiction. Thus the translated ideal is the unit ideal.

The actual composite $b\mapsto G_i(Tb)$ is holomorphic, hence
continuous, on $V$. Let $Z$ be its nonzero locus inside $V$. The
relative preimage of the open complement of zero is open in the
ambient tangent space because $V$ is open. The chosen point belongs
to $Z$, and $Z\subset V$. At every point of $Z$ the preceding
argument applied to the same actual generator gives equality of the
entire same translated ideal with the unit ideal.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine constructed prolongation always annihilates the entire original next ideal jet]
\label{hir:actual-unconditional-dual-jet-prolongation-whole-original-ideal}
\uses{hir:actual-nonzero-generator-open-unit-ideal-domain,hir:actual-dual-jet-prolongation-whole-original-ideal-zero,hir:actual-dual-jet-prolongation-original-unit-ideal-zero}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualUnconditionalDualJetRaising.actualDualJetProlongation_whole_ideal_zero_unconditional}
\leanok
Let $G$ be actual holomorphic on $U$, let $V$ be actual open with
$T(V)\subset U$, and let $\eta$ be an actual holomorphic moving
old-order row annihilating every original Macaulay column on $V$.
Then at every $c\in V$ and every tangent direction $a$, the actual
constructed $\nabla_a\eta$ annihilates $J_{q+1}(g)$ for every
$g\in I_c$. No generator-vanishing, positivity, higher-annihilator,
or unit-domain certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $c$. If every $G_i(Tc)$ is zero, the actual next-column theorem
and its entire-ideal consequence apply directly to the same family,
old row and actual point. They include all new maximal-length words.

Otherwise choose an actual nonzero generator at $Tc$. The preceding
theorem constructs a real open $Z$ through $c$ inside $V$ on which
the entire same original ideals are all unit ideals. Restrict the
actual holomorphic coefficient functions and the old column equations
to $Z$. The genuine unit-ideal prolongation theorem differentiates
the whole fixed-jet pairing on this real open and identifies each
successor with the original directional derivative germ. Consequently
the same constructed prolongation at $c$ is zero on every actual
next true jet, in particular on $J_{q+1}(g)$. The construction of the
functional does not depend on the choice of its domain, so the
restricted argument proves the claimed original value.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Unconditional explicit holomorphic raising on the entire true jet image]
\label{hir:actual-unconditional-holomorphic-dual-jet-raising}
\uses{hir:actual-unconditional-dual-jet-prolongation-whole-original-ideal,hir:actual-dual-jet-prolongation-holomorphic-fixed-true-jet,hir:actual-holomorphic-true-jet-annihilator-generation,hir:actual-dual-jet-raising-coefficients}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualUnconditionalDualJetRaising.actual_dual_jet_holomorphic_raising_unconditional}
\leanok
Let $D\subset U$ be real open and put $V=T^{-1}(D)$. Use an actual
next-order pivot with nonzero determinant on $D$ and size equal to
the actual centre rank there. Suppose $\eta$ and its first derivative
functions in direction $a$ are holomorphic on $V$ and $\eta$ kills
every old original Macaulay column there. Then the same specified
raising coefficient functions $B_s(c,a)$ are holomorphic on $V$ and
\[
 (\nabla_a\eta)_c(v)=\sum_s B_s(c,a)\theta_s(c)(v)
 \qquad(c\in V,\ v\in\operatorname{im}J_{q+1}).
\]
No generator-vanishing or properness hypothesis is needed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual constructed moving functional is holomorphic on every
fixed true next jet because its defining finite sum uses only the
given coefficient functions and their stated first derivatives. The
preceding theorem supplies annihilation of the entire same original
ideal jet without an extra properness hypothesis. Apply the genuine
holomorphic true-jet generation theorem for the stated actual next
pivot. Its coefficients evaluate this same moving functional on the
one fixed original true-jet vector $S(R e_s)$, so they are exactly
the previously specified functions $B_s$. That theorem gives their
holomorphicity and equality on the entire true jet image, retaining
all original relations between scalar coordinate words.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original HS-constant centre geometry supplies unconditional all-order raising]
\label{hir:actual-original-hs-constant-centre-unconditional-dual-jet-raising}
\uses{hir:actual-unconditional-dual-jet-prolongation-whole-original-ideal,hir:actual-original-hs-centre-holomorphic-true-jet-dual-generation,hir:actual-dual-jet-prolongation-holomorphic-fixed-true-jet,hir:actual-dual-jet-raising-coefficients}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualUnconditionalDualJetRaising.exists_original_hs_constant_centre_unconditional_dual_jet_raising}
\leanok
Only original coherent ideal data, an actual regular centre, and the
entire intrinsic HS constancy on that centre internally supply one
original flattening, one original finite generating family and real
open $U$ before all orders. At every positive old order $q$ there
are internally chosen actual next-order pivots and a real open
$D\subset U$ through zero. On $V=T^{-1}(D)$, every actual moving
old-order annihilating row with holomorphic coefficients and first
derivative functions has the specified holomorphic raising
coefficients and the displayed equality on the entire true next jet
image. No positivity, generator-vanishing, properness, pivot, rank,
or higher-annihilator certificate is a geometric caller input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the original HS-constant-centre holomorphic true-jet generation
producer. It internally chooses the same original flattening and
coherent finite generating family and real open before all orders,
with the entire translated-ideal dictionary. At each old positive
order $q$, use its internally chosen actual pivot at order $q+1$.
Its domain $D\subset U$ is real open through zero, and its true
whole-ideal generation theorem holds on $V=T^{-1}(D)$.

For an actual old moving row satisfying the stated ordinary
holomorphic and column equations, form its genuine differential
prolongation restricted to the actual next true-jet image. Fixed-vector
evaluation is holomorphic by the accepted prolongation calculation.
The unconditional entire-ideal theorem above proves the required
next-ideal annihilation on this same $V$, including the unit-ideal
case. Apply the internally supplied true-jet generation theorem to
this constructed functional. The specified coefficients are exactly
its evaluations on the original fixed representative vectors, which
proves both asserted conclusions for all points and true jets.
The order-dependent domains need not have a common all-order open;
no identification with the normal-adic filtration or normal-flatness
conclusion is asserted here.
\end{proof}
