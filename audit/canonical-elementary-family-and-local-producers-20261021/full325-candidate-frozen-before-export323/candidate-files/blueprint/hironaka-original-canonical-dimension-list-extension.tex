\section{Intrinsic dimension parts in the ordered smooth recursion}

Let $b:Y^m\to X^n$ be an actual holomorphic submersion and put
$r=m-n$ in natural-number arithmetic.  The $k$-dimensional part is the
actual closure of the intrinsic dimension-$k$ regular locus.  No
global inequality $n\le m$ is imposed when $Y$ is empty.

\begin{lemma}[Whole dimension parts commute with smooth inverse image]
\label{hir:original-dimension-part-smooth-shift}
\uses{hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-regular-locus}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalDimensionList.dimensionPart_preimage_eq_shift}
\leanok
For every original subset $W\subseteq X$ and every $k\in\mathbb N$,
\[
 (b^{-1}W)^{(k+r)}=b^{-1}(W^{(k)}).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual intrinsic regular-locus equality identifies the source
dimension-$(k+r)$ locus with the inverse image of the target
dimension-$k$ locus.  A holomorphic submersion is continuous and open,
so inverse image commutes with closure.  Taking closures of this
literal regular-locus equality gives the claimed whole-set equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Every low source dimension part is actually empty]
\label{hir:original-dimension-part-below-smooth-fibre-empty}
\uses{hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-regular-locus}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalDimensionList.dimensionPart_preimage_eq_empty_of_lt_fibre}
\leanok
For every original $W\subseteq X$ and every $j<r$, the intrinsic
source part $(b^{-1}W)^{(j)}$ is empty.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If a source point were regular of dimension $j$, genuine regularity
reflection at that point would imply $r\le j$, contrary to $j<r$.
Thus the entire dimension-$j$ regular locus is empty, and so is its
closure.  The argument is pointwise and includes an empty source.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The entire non-pure singular set is an actual inverse image]
\label{hir:original-nonpure-singular-set-smooth-pullback}
\uses{hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-regular-locus}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalDimensionList.singularSet_preimage_eq}
\leanok
The singular set obtained by removing \emph{all} intrinsic regular
dimensions satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{Sing}(b^{-1}W)=b^{-1}\operatorname{Sing}(W).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A singular source point lies in $b^{-1}W$ and cannot lie over a target
regular point, since smooth pullback would make it regular in the
target dimension plus $r$.  Conversely, any regular source point of
any dimension reflects to a regular target point of its dimension
minus $r$.  These two actual implications identify the complements
of the unions of all regular strata inside the two whole carriers.
No purity, analyticity or ideal-equality hypothesis is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The increasing intrinsic dimension list is the shifted pulled list]
\label{hir:original-increasing-dimension-list-smooth-pullback}
\uses{hir:original-dimension-part-smooth-shift,hir:original-dimension-part-below-smooth-fibre-empty}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalDimensionList.nonempty_dimension_list_preimage}
\leanok
List the source pairs $((b^{-1}W)^{(j)},j)$ for $0\le j<m$ in
increasing order and delete exactly those pairs with empty carrier.
The resulting ordered list is literally the list of
$(b^{-1}(W^{(k)}),k+r)$ for $0\le k<n$, again deleting exactly empty
carriers.  This is equality of whole ordered labelled sets, rather
than only equality of their unions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If the source has a point, the actual submersion at that point gives
$n\le m$, hence $m=r+n$.  Split its increasing range into $0\le j<r$
and the shifted range $j=r+k$, $0\le k<n$.  Every part in the first
range is empty by the preceding lemma.  The preceding whole-part
equality identifies every ordered pair in the second range with its
pulled target pair, including its shifted dimension label.  Filtering
removes the first range and agrees on the second range.

If the source is empty, every subset of it is empty.  Both filtered
lists are therefore empty, independently of their unfiltered lengths.
Thus the literal filtered-list equality holds without a false global
dimension inequality for an empty source.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This chapter supplies the actual ordered input dictionary for the
mixed-dimensional finite recursion.  These lemmas alone do not
construct its canonical separation or resolution stages, identify its
composite projection, or close the non-pure elementary functoriality
contract.
\end{remark}
