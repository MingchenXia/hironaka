\section{The literal pure embedded prefix of any actual canonical terminal history}

\begin{theorem}[First meeting cuts the same terminal history without a compactness premise]
\label{hir:original-pure-boundary-given-terminal-first-prefix}
\uses{hir:original-pure-boundary-canonical-first-prefix}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureBoundaryTerminalPrefix.exists_pure_boundary_prefix_of_terminal}
\leanok
Let $W\subset X^n$ be an actual analytic subset of pure dimension $d<n$,
let $E_0$ be an actual ordered SNC boundary, and assume
$W\subset\overline{W\setminus|E_0|}$. Let $P:X\leftarrow Z$ be any
actual canonical terminal history of the original unchanged datum
$(X,E_0,\mathcal I_W,1)$ for the actual canonical invariant. No
compactness of $W$ is imposed. Put
$O=X\setminus((W\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_dW)\cup|E_0|)$ and
$F=(W\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_dW)\cup(W\cap|E_0|)$.
Then internally cut this very $P=T\mathbin{+}R$ so that $T$ is canonical,
its centres avoid $O$, and for nonempty $W$ its next whole maximum meets
$O$. Its actual entire boundary $E_T$ is SNC and its strict $W_T$ is a
closed dimension-$d$ submanifold with normal crossings with $E_T$.
The same projection is admissible over $F$, proper, surjective and an
isomorphism off $F$, with
\[
 \operatorname{Exc}(T)\subset\bigcup E_T^{\mathrm{new}}
 \subset T^{-1}F,\qquad
 T^{-1}(W\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_dW)\subset\operatorname{Exc}(T).
\]
If $W$ is empty, choose the genuine identity prefix and retain the entire
same $P$ as its tail; that whole $P$ has only empty centres. This is a
cutting theorem for a supplied actual terminal history. A local
original-only resolution producer must still construct that history from
its natural local input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual analytic pure subset and SNC boundary make its original
marked datum an invariant-domain datum, without compactness. Its actual
cosupport is exactly $W$. Purity and boundary density make $W\cap O$
dense in $W$, all its points regular of dimension $d$, and every old
boundary member disjoint from $O$.

Suppose $W$ is nonempty. If all centres of the actual terminal $P$
avoided $O$, density would supply an original point of $W\cap O$.
Surjectivity of the actual blowup projection supplies a point above it.
The true cosupport formula on the preserved open would put that point
in the final cosupport, contradicting actual terminality. Thus a genuine
first meeting exists. Cut the unchanged $P$ at its first centre meeting
$O$, retaining its literal tail and the canonical prefix property.
If $W$ is empty, original empty cosupport forces every centre of its
actual canonical history to be empty; use the literal identity and the
unchanged $P$ as the desired split.

For nonempty $W$, the first meeting point and the genuine constant
regular-locus value formula identify the whole maximum over the
preserved open with the actual strict carrier. Its actual admissibility
makes that carrier a closed dimension-$d$ submanifold and gives genuine
normal crossings with the actual current boundary. For empty $W$ these
same geometric claims hold for its actual empty strict transform.
The canonical prefix is a genuine test history. Its centres lie both
over the original cosupport $W$ and over the complement of $O$, hence
over exactly the permitted set $F$. Actual test geometry gives its
admissible sequence and SNC boundary. The entire total exceptional
support is the union of its new boundary members; confinement of
centres puts that support in $T^{-1}F$, and the actual exceptional locus
lies in this support. Conversely the genuine regularity reflection for
the smooth strict carrier puts the inverse image of the original
singular set in the actual exceptional locus. Actual finite blowup
geometry gives holomorphy, properness and surjectivity, and confinement
of its entire exceptional image to $F$ gives the isomorphism off $F$.
Every assertion concerns this same literal prefix of the supplied $P$;
compactness was only needed by a separate producer of $P$.
\end{proof}
