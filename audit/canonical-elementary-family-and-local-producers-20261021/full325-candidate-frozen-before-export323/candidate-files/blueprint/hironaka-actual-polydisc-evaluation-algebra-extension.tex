\section{Actual holomorphic division on one internally constructed polydisc}

\begin{theorem}[Actual convergent evaluation preserves monomials, addition and finite sums]
\label{hir:actual-polydisc-evaluation-additive-algebra}
\uses{hir:actual-coefficient-polydisc-uniform-evaluation}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPolydiscEvaluationAlgebra.evaluation_monomial,Hironaka.HironakaActualPolydiscEvaluationAlgebra.evaluation_add,Hironaka.HironakaActualPolydiscEvaluationAlgebra.evaluation_finset_sum}
Literal actual series evaluation sends $cX^a$ to $cz^a$. On the
actual absolute coefficient domain it preserves addition and finite
sums, throughout the closed original polydisc.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a monomial, all coefficients except the actual exponent are zero,
so the actual infinite sum has its single stated value. For addition,
the true formal coefficient map is additive. Distribute the actual
monomial factor and use the already proved absolute summability to
interchange the two actual sums. For a finite sum, coefficient
linearity followed by the finite-sum interchange for genuinely
summable families proves the assertion without any formal evaluation
topology assumption.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual convergent evaluation preserves true formal convolution]
\label{hir:actual-polydisc-evaluation-multiplicative-algebra}
\uses{hir:actual-coefficient-polydisc-uniform-evaluation,hir:actual-polydisc-evaluation-additive-algebra}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPolydiscEvaluationAlgebra.evaluation_mul}
If $F,G$ have finite actual absolute coefficient mass, their literal
evaluation satisfies $(FG)(z)=F(z)G(z)$ at every point of the entire
closed polydisc, where $FG$ is the genuine formal convolution product.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both actual evaluated monomial families are absolutely summable.
The product family indexed by two actual exponent vectors is therefore
summable. The actual Cauchy-product theorem rewrites the product of
the two actual sums as the sum over genuine finite antidiagonals.
On an antidiagonal $a+b=c$, the literal monomials satisfy
$z^az^b=z^c$. Thus its finite inner sum is precisely the genuine
formal convolution coefficient multiplied by $z^c$. Summing gives
the actual evaluation of the entire true formal product.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One internally chosen radius gives actual holomorphic vertex division]
\label{hir:actual-common-radius-holomorphic-vertex-division}
\uses{hir:actual-polydisc-evaluation-additive-algebra,hir:actual-polydisc-evaluation-multiplicative-algebra,hir:actual-coefficient-polydisc-holomorphy,hir:actual-deg-lex-vertex-small-radius,hir:actual-vertex-convergent-whole-division}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPolydiscEvaluationAlgebra.exists_actual_holomorphic_vertex_division}
Let $I$ be the actual whole formal ideal and $B$ its actual monic
deg-lex vertex family. Suppose the actual finite generator tails have
finite absolute mass at a positive radius $\eta$. There is one positive
$\rho\leq\eta$, chosen before every numerator, such that every actual
$F$ of finite mass at $\eta$ has actual convergent quotients $H_a$
and the unchanged canonical whole-ideal remainder $S$. They satisfy
both the literal whole formal division identity and, on the entire
closed $\rho$-polydisc, the genuine function identity
\[
 F(z)=\sum_a B_a(z)H_a(z)+S(z).
\]
All the displayed generator, quotient and remainder evaluations are
complex differentiable on the open $\rho$-polydisc. Neither weights,
a strict norm bound, quotients, a canonical remainder convergence
certificate, nor a holomorphic function identity is supplied.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine bounded deg-lex weight construction and anisotropic
shrink theorem internally choose one positive $\rho\leq\eta$ with
the computed actual tail constant strictly less than one. This
choice depends on the same original vertex family, before the
numerator. Decreasing the radius preserves its actual coefficient
mass. Apply the genuine complete complex coefficient-space inverse
to obtain the actual owner quotients, canonical whole-ideal
remainder and literal whole formal identity. The actual generator
is its convergent tail plus its genuine single monomial, so it too
has finite mass at this same radius. All quotients, generators and
the remainder therefore give actual holomorphic functions by the
proved polydisc theorem. Apply actual additive, finite-sum and
multiplicative evaluation to the unchanged whole formal identity.
The result is the asserted literal function identity on the entire
closed polydisc, with holomorphy asserted on its open part.
\end{proof}

The initial fixed-radius coefficient-mass premise is the genuine
analytic-series domain. Identifying arbitrary original holomorphic
germs with these convergent series, persistent HS tracking and
resolution termination remain separate obligations.
