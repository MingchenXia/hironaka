\section{Actual elementary final Cartesian arrows satisfy identity and composition}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-elementary-cartesian-naturality}

The genuine whole holomorphic square uniqueness and pasting theorems
already apply to arbitrary finite actual blowup projections, without a
canonical or terminal marked history. The following final-arrow laws
therefore apply to the actual elementary ordered algorithms, even when
their distinct ideal stages are not one CAN history of a mixed ideal.

\begin{theorem}[The actual Cartesian arrow over the identity is the identity]
\label{hir:actual-elementary-cartesian-identity}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualElementaryCartesianNaturality.upperMap_identity}
For any actual finite blowup tower $T:D\to X$, let $g:D\to D$ be
holomorphic, commute with this same actual projection, and make the
whole projection square over $\mathrm{id}_X$ holomorphically Cartesian.
Then $g=\mathrm{id}_D$. No marked ideal, CAN, terminality or common
history certificate is required.
\uses{hir:smooth-terminal-whole-pullback-map-intrinsic-uniqueness}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The literal identity on $D$ is holomorphic and commutes with $T$.
Its identity projection square is genuinely universal: for a
holomorphic cone $(u,v)$ with $u=T v$, take the actual lift $v$;
its two components are the cone equalities, and any other lift is
$v$ by its identity upper component. Compare the given whole Cartesian
arrow with this genuine identity square using the proved actual finite
source projection uniqueness theorem. That theorem constructs the two
actual inverse universal lifts before invoking genuine blowup
projection rigidity; it imposes no marked-history premise. It gives
$g=\mathrm{id}_D$ for the same actual tower.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual independently chosen elementary Cartesian arrows compose]
\label{hir:actual-elementary-cartesian-composition}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualElementaryCartesianNaturality.upperMap_composition}
Let $T:D\to X$, $S:E\to Y$ and $Q:F\to Z$ be arbitrary actual
finite blowup towers. Let $f:Y\to X$ and holomorphic $h:Z\to Y$
be the actual base maps. Suppose actual holomorphic arrows $g:E\to D$
and $k:F\to E$ commute with the respective projections and give
whole holomorphic Cartesian squares over $f,h$. If an independently
chosen actual holomorphic $c:F\to D$ gives the whole Cartesian square
over $f\circ h$, then $c=g\circ k$.
No common choice of individual stages, globally scheduled history or
canonical/terminal marked-history assumption is added.
\uses{hir:smooth-terminal-whole-pullback-map-intrinsic-uniqueness,hir:smooth-terminal-whole-holomorphic-pullback-pasting}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Genuine projection commutation gives
$T(g(k(z)))=f(S(k(z)))=f(h(Q(z)))$. The composite is holomorphic.
Apply the proved genuine whole-square pasting theorem: for any actual
holomorphic cone over $f\circ h$, first use the square for $g$ to
lift to $E$, then use the square for $k$ to lift to $F$. Successive
universal uniqueness proves existence and uniqueness for the same
composite $g\circ k$. Thus both $c$ and $g\circ k$ are actual whole
holomorphic Cartesian arrows with the same actual finite source
projection $Q$ and target projection $T$. The proved genuine finite
source projection uniqueness theorem makes them equal. These are
literal laws for the independently constructed final arrows; they do
not assume those laws or any equality of their individual histories.
\end{proof}
