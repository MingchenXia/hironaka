\section{An independently stopped smooth source for the same embedded target map}

The original target record is $M=(X,X,\varnothing,I_V,1)$, and the source
record is literally $N=b^*M$.  No identification of its whole ideal
$b^*I_V$ with a separately reduced vanishing ideal is made.  The good open
is $O=X\setminus\operatorname{Sing}V$ and $U=b^{-1}O$.  Empty pulled-back
carriers and actual empty source steps are included.

\begin{lemma}[Cosupport persists over an original avoided open]
\label{hir:original-cosupport-persistence-avoided-open}
\uses{bcalc:restrict-empty-tower,hir:open-restriction-marked,bcalc:transform-off-centre,hir:smooth-centre}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalIndependentStoppedSource.mem_cosupp_iff_of_centresAvoid}
\leanok
For an original well-formed marked record $A$, an actual test tower $S$
and an original open $U$ avoided by all its actual centres, a point $z$
over $U$ belongs to the transformed cosupport if and only if its original
projection belongs to the original cosupport of $A$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual tower.  For the identity tower the assertion is
literal equality.  At an added blowup, avoidance says that a point whose
original projection lies in $U$ maps outside the actual centre.  The
whole off-centre controlled-transform dictionary identifies membership
in the new cosupport with membership of its image in the preceding
cosupport.  The preceding transform has a closed smooth ambient carrier
and a coherent ideal by actual testness, so this dictionary applies.
The induction hypothesis gives original cosupport membership, and the
literal composite projection formula completes the equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual pulled stopped prefix has a next source encounter, or is empty]
\label{hir:original-pulled-stopped-source-next-encounter}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-embedded-stopped-smooth-comparison,hir:smooth-original-ambient-canonical-history-values,hir:submersion-relative-control-joint-tower,hir:original-cosupport-persistence-avoided-open}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalIndependentStoppedSource.source_maximum_meets_pulled_regular_open,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalIndependentStoppedSource.canonical_histories_empty_of_original_cosupp_empty}
\leanok
Let $V$ be the original irreducible target, let $b$ be any holomorphic
submersion, and let $Q$ be an actual smooth pullback of a canonical target
prefix $T$ avoiding $O$, whose next whole maximum meets $O$.
If $b^{-1}V$ is nonempty, the next whole maximum of the actual source
prefix for $N$ meets $U$.  If $b^{-1}V$ is empty, every actual finite
canonical history for $N$ has only empty centres and is already a
geometric resolution.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The submersion $b$ is open.  Pulling back the density
$V\subseteq\overline{V\cap O}$ shows that a nonempty $b^{-1}V$ contains a
point over $V\cap O$.  Surjectivity of the actual source tower projection
lifts that point to its endpoint.  The actual square puts its target
image over $V\cap O$.  The target first-meeting maximum contains exactly
the preimage of $V$ over $O$, so this target image belongs to the whole
target maximum.  The source cosupport is the true preimage cosupport.
Its original ambient invariant values are the images of the target
values under one order embedding with the genuine fibre dimension.
Thus this source point dominates every source cosupport point and is
itself a whole source maximum over $U$.

If $b^{-1}V$ is empty, the original source cosupport is empty by the
actual original cosupport pullback formula.  Every transformed
cosupport of an actual test tower lies over that original empty set.
Hence every current maximum is empty.  A canonical centre is empty or
that whole current maximum, so induction proves that every actual
centre is empty.  The transformed cosupport is empty as well, giving
genuine geometric terminality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An independent source first-meeting map compares with the same target]
\label{hir:original-independent-source-stopped-cartesian-comparison}
\uses{hir:original-pulled-stopped-source-next-encounter,hir:original-first-meeting-prefix-map-unique,hir:original-ambient-terminal-choice-intrinsic-unique-comparison,hir:original-stopped-strict-transform-smooth-square,hir:submersion-relative-regular-geometry,hir:submersion-relative-nc-transversality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalIndependentStoppedSource.independent_source_prefix_comparison}
\leanok
Keep the preceding actual target prefix $T$ and its actual pulled-back
source $Q$.  Let $S$ be any independently chosen actual finite canonical
history for the literal $N$.  When $b^{-1}V$ is nonempty, suppose $S$
avoids $U$ and its next whole maximum meets $U$.  Then there is a unique
biholomorphism from the endpoint of $S$ to that of $Q$ over $Y$.
Composing it with the actual upper map of $Q$ gives a holomorphic
submersion $g$ on the independent endpoint, with
\[
 p_T g=b p_S
\]
and the genuine holomorphic Cartesian universal property.  The whole
conventional strict transform is exactly
\[
 \operatorname{Str}_{p_S}(b^{-1}V)
       =g^{-1}\operatorname{Str}_{p_T}(V).
\]
If the target strict carrier is smooth and has normal crossings with
its actual boundary, its pullback through $g$ has the corresponding
regularity and normal crossings with the literal pulled target boundary.
The actual independent source boundary is SNC by canonical testness.
No equality of the two full indexed boundary lists is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $b^{-1}V$ is nonempty, the preceding theorem supplies the next maximum
encounter for $Q$.  Both actual source prefixes are canonical for the
same literal $N$, avoid the same $U$, and their next whole maxima meet
$U$.  First-meeting-prefix uniqueness therefore supplies the unique
over-$Y$ biholomorphism.  If $b^{-1}V$ is empty, both source histories are
genuine canonical terminal histories by the preceding theorem, so
original terminal comparison supplies that unique biholomorphism.

In either case conjugating the actual upper map by this source
biholomorphism gives a submersion, the original projection equality, and
the holomorphic Cartesian property: transfer a cone through the inverse
biholomorphism and use the actual Cartesian lift and its uniqueness.
A biholomorphic square over the same original source transports the
conventional strict transform; combine this with the actual pulled
strict-transform equality.  Genuine smooth pullback gives the stated
carrier regularity and normal crossings with the pulled target
boundary.  Independent source canonicality gives actual testness, whose
well-formed transform gives SNC for its own actual boundary.  Different
empty-step counts may insert different empty indexed members, so these
two boundary statements are kept distinct.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original input internally supplies an independently stopped smooth source]
\label{hir:original-independent-stopped-smooth-source-producer}
\uses{hir:original-independent-source-stopped-cartesian-comparison,hir:original-cosupport-persistence-avoided-open,hir:original-canonical-embedded-stopped-smooth-comparison,hir:original-global-finite-smooth-source-history}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalIndependentStoppedSource.exists_original_independently_stopped_smooth_source}
\leanok
For an original compact target $X$, an original irreducible $V$ of
dimension less than $n$ with coherent whole vanishing ideal, and any
holomorphic submersion $b:Y\to X$, internally construct the target
canonical first-meeting prefix and an independent source prefix as in
the preceding theorem.  The source is chosen as a prefix of an
internally constructed whole finite canonical terminal source history,
with the exact remaining tail retained.  If $b^{-1}V$ is empty choose the
identity prefix; otherwise its next whole maximum meets $U$.
The same original target stopped projection admits the resulting genuine
smooth Cartesian upper map and the whole strict-transform equality.
The target's actual SNC boundary, strict-carrier regularity and normal
crossings and good-open isomorphism are retained.  The independent source
projection is a proper holomorphic surjective admissible blowup sequence
with centres over $b^{-1}\operatorname{Sing}V$, is an isomorphism over $U$,
and its actual boundary is SNC.  Its whole strict carrier is closed
smooth of dimension $\dim V+(m-n)$ at every point, with normal crossings
with the literal pulled target boundary.  In the empty-carrier branch
the identity prefix has length zero and every actual centre of the
constructed whole source history is proved empty.
No source compactness, source history, comparison map or stopping index
is supplied by the caller.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The compact target producer constructs its actual whole canonical
terminal history and the genuine target first-meeting prefix.  Pull
back the entire terminal history by the actual smooth-tower producer;
this independently constructs a whole finite canonical geometric
terminal history for $N$ on all of $Y$.

If the pulled carrier is empty, choose the identity source prefix and
the entire constructed source tower as its remaining tail.  Otherwise
openness and original regular-locus density supply an original
cosupport point in $U$.  If the whole terminal source history avoided
$U$, lift that point by its surjective projection.  The first lemma
would keep it in final cosupport, contradicting genuine terminality.
The actual first-centre theorem therefore selects an independent
canonical source prefix avoiding $U$ with a next whole maximum over
$U$, together with its exact remaining tail and factorization into the
constructed full source history.  Apply the preceding comparison
theorem to this internally selected source prefix and the unchanged
target prefix.  The whole strict-transform equality and genuine upper
submersion transfer target closed smoothness and its exact dimension,
and normal crossings with the pulled target boundary.  Actual source
canonical testness gives its own SNC boundary, and actual source
avoidance gives centres over the inverse singular set, admissibility,
exceptional-image containment and the good-open isomorphism.  Its actual
finite blowup projection is proper, holomorphic and surjective.  All
source choices and both necessary branches are therefore internal
original-input constructions.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The theorem compares genuine independent endpoint maps for the literal
pulled marked record.  It does not claim equality of actual stopping
indices, equality of indexed boundary lists with different empty
positions, or $b^*I_V=I_{b^{-1}V}$ for singular $V$.  Normal crossings
with the independent source's own indexed boundary requires its
empty-member boundary transport, beyond the map comparison here.
\end{remark}
