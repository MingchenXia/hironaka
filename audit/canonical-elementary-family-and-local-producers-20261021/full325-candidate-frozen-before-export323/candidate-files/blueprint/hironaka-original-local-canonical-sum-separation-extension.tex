\section{Original canonical individual-sum separation on a prescribed relatively compact open}
\label{sec:hironaka-original-local-canonical-sum-separation}

The original analytic parts and their intersection need not be compact.
The actual stage remains the canonical terminal stage of the individual
sum $I_A+I_B$ with the entire original ordered boundary. It is not
replaced by a sum with the union of all later parts.

\begin{theorem}[Whole intrinsic sum data and mutual density on the same open]
\label{hir:original-local-canonical-sum-whole-open-data}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalSumSeparation.local_sum_record,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalSumSeparation.local_sum_density}
For original analytic parts $A,B$ and actual ordered boundary $E_0$,
the whole marked pullback to an actual open $U$ is the independently
intrinsic sum datum of $A_U,B_U$ and the literal boundary $E_0|_U$.
If each original part is dense away from the other, then each whole
local part has that same genuine mutual density on $U$.
\uses{hir:original-canonical-sum-intrinsic-source-record,hir:actual-singular-vanishing-ideal-pullback-submersion}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual open inclusion is a holomorphic submersion. The genuine
whole singular-ideal pullback theorem identifies each intrinsic
vanishing ideal and the whole sum-pullback formula identifies their
sum. Whole inverse images identify the carrier, original ordered
boundary list and mark, proving equality of the entire records.
The open inclusion commutes with closure. Pull back each original
mutual-density inclusion, commute the whole closure and actual
inverse image, and use inverse image of difference. This proves both
whole local density statements without compactness of either part.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original local data construct the same actual canonical terminal individual-sum separator]
\label{hir:original-local-canonical-sum-prescribed-open-producer}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalCanonicalSumSeparation.exists_local_canonical_sum_separation}
Let $A,B\subset X^n$ be original analytic parts, with $A$ of empty
interior, each dense away from the other, and let $E_0$ be the actual
original ordered SNC boundary. For any prescribed open $U$ contained
in a given compact set, these data internally construct an actual
canonical terminal history $T$ of the intrinsic local individual-sum
datum $(U,E_0|_U,I_{A_U}+I_{B_U},1)$. This same $T$ separates the
whole conventional strict transforms of $A_U$ and $B_U$. It is
admissible over $A_U\cap B_U$, its whole boundary is SNC, its whole
sum pullback is the actual exceptional divisor ideal, and that
exceptional support is the inverse image of $A_U\cap B_U$. Its
projection is holomorphic, proper, surjective and an isomorphism off
that intersection.

Fix this target before every smooth $b:Y\to U$. Internally construct
an actual source tower $Q$ and upper submersion $g$ with a true
holomorphic Cartesian projection square. Its marked input is the
whole intrinsic sum of the two source parts and literal source
boundary. It is canonical and terminal, has the whole corresponding
principalization and admissible geometry, and its entire ordered
boundary is exactly the $g$-inverse-image list of the target boundary.
The whole source strict parts are the $g$-inverse images of the two
target strict parts, hence disjoint. No source compactness, incoming
history or ideal/comparison certificate is assumed.
\uses{hir:original-local-canonical-sum-whole-open-data,hir:original-local-canonical-principalization-prescribed-open,hir:original-local-canonical-principalization-terminal-geometry,hir:original-stopped-strict-transform-smooth-square}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original coherence of both vanishing ideals gives coherence of their
whole sum. Empty interior of $A$ makes its vanishing ideal nonzero at
every point, hence makes the larger sum ideal nonzero. Apply the
proved original-only local canonical principalization producer to
this actual sum and the original SNC boundary. It constructs a
finite actual canonical terminal history on the prescribed open.
The preceding whole-record identity rewrites its actual input as the
intrinsic local sum datum. Its principalization identity and exact
exceptional support, together with the preceding actual local mutual
density, force disjointness of the two entire strict transforms by
the genuine sum-separation theorem. All remaining target geometry
comes from that same canonical terminal history.

For a given $b$, take the actual universal smooth blowup pullback of
this same $T$. The genuine whole sum-record pullback identity gives
its independently intrinsic source input, and true canonical-history
and cosupport pullback make it canonical and terminal. Apply the
proved whole terminal principalization geometry to this actual source
record. Its ordered boundary is literally the inverse image of the
entire target list by the actual marked-history dictionary.

All target centres lie over the actual local intersection. Each
part's mutual density says it is dense in the complement of that
intersection. The genuine preserved-open strict-square theorem
therefore identifies both whole source strict transforms with the
inverse images of their corresponding target strict transforms,
including exceptional points. Target disjointness then gives source
disjointness. True finite blowup geometry gives the source's
holomorphy, properness and surjectivity. This constructs an actual
individual-sum stage of the same ordered algorithm without compact
intersection or source compactness assumptions.
\end{proof}
