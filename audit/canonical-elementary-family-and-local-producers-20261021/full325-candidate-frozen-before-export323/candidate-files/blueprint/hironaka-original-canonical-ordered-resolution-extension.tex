\section{The actual canonical head-and-tail resolution tree}

\begin{definition}[Canonical ordered resolution provenance]
\label{hir:original-canonical-ordered-resolution-tree}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-ordered-separator-chain,hir:original-pure-boundary-canonical-first-prefix}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalOrderedResolution.CanonicalOrderedResolution}
\leanok
A pure head stage for $(E,A,d)$ is a genuine canonical prefix of the
literal marked datum $(X,X,E,I_A,1)$ avoiding the intrinsic good open
$O=(A\setminus\operatorname{Reg}_d A\cup|E|)^c$, with its next whole
maximum meeting $O$ if $A$ is nonempty. Its original analytic,
pure-dimensional, boundary-density and invariant-domain properties are
retained. Its dimension is $<n$ whenever $A$ is nonempty.

Define an actual ordered resolution tree of $(E,G,L)$ recursively.
The empty list uses the identity. For $(A,d)::L_0$, take an actual
ordered canonical separator chain, then an actual pure head stage
for its transformed head and whole accumulated boundary. Let $L_2$
be the ordered strict transforms of $L_0$ under these two stages and
let $K_2$ be the union of their carriers. Recurse on $L_2$ with the
whole new boundary and bad set
$G_2=p_{\rm head}^{-1}p_{\rm sep}^{-1}G\cap K_2$. The actual tower
is the literal append of the separator, head stage and recursive tail.
Every boundary and empty stage position is retained.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Original good parts construct the actual ordered resolution and its same smooth source]
\label{hir:original-canonical-ordered-resolution-producer}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-ordered-resolution-tree,hir:original-canonical-ordered-separator-producer,hir:original-pure-boundary-canonical-first-prefix,hir:original-stopped-strict-transform-smooth-square,hir:actual-smooth-cartesian-tower-append,hir:actual-singular-vanishing-ideal-pullback-submersion,hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-singular-boundary-good}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalOrderedResolution.exists_canonical_ordered_resolution}
\leanok
An original finite ordered list of compact good parts relative to a
whole SNC boundary $E$ and a closed bad set $G$, with pairwise
intersections contained in $G$, internally constructs an actual
canonical ordered resolution tree $T$ and its whole ordered boundary.
Its projection is admissible over $G$, the final boundary is SNC,
each original part has a strict transform regular of its labelled
dimension and in normal crossings with this boundary, and the
different listed strict transforms are pairwise disjoint.

After this same target tree is fixed, every original holomorphic
submersion $b:Y^m\to X^n$ internally supplies an actual source tree
for the pulled list with dimension labels shifted by $m-n$. It is a
literal stagewise pullback of the same target append, with upper
submersion and holomorphic Cartesian square. Its whole ordered
boundary is the literal inverse image of the target boundary. Every
whole source strict part is the inverse image of the corresponding
target strict part. The source is admissible over $b^{-1}G$ and has
the corresponding own-boundary regularity, normal crossings and
pairwise disjointness. No source compactness or incoming algorithm
history is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the original ordered list length. The empty case is the
actual identity on each side. For $(A,d)::L_0$, construct the actual
ordered separator chain for $A$ and $L_0$ using the preceding
producer. All transformed original good parts remain compact good
parts relative to its whole accumulated SNC boundary and the inverse
image of $G$.

Apply the original pure-boundary driver to the transformed head,
retaining the actual canonical first-meeting prefix and its complete
elementary geometry. Its intrinsic bad set is contained in the
current inverse image of $G$, by the good-part hypotheses. The strict
tail parts remain good parts. Cut their new bad set down to its
intersection with their whole finite union $K_2$ and apply induction.
The resolved head misses $K_2$: it was separated from every tail part,
and strict transforms preserve these disjointness inclusions. Thus
it misses every later centre image. Its later strict transform is its
literal inverse image through a map isomorphic along it, preserving
its resolved dimension and normal crossings with the actual final
boundary. The tail properties follow by induction. Literal strict
transform composition identifies these actual sets with strict
transforms under the same full appended projection. Admissibility
composes over $G$, and disjointness persists under the later strict
transforms. The retained stages give exactly the stated provenance.

For a given $b$, construct the actual smooth source separator chain
for that fixed target separator, then construct the actual smooth
pullback of the fixed target head prefix. The whole singular
vanishing-ideal theorem identifies its source marked datum with its
intrinsic source datum. Intrinsic pure regular loci, boundary density
and good opens pull back exactly. If the pulled head is nonempty,
openness and the original dense good part give a genuine source
whole maximum over its own good open, by the actual ambient-dimension
order embedding of the complete invariant. If it is empty that
encounter condition is vacuous. Hence the source head is an actual
pure canonical head stage; the dimension bound is needed only when
it has a point, where the actual submersion supplies it.

Apply the induction hypothesis to the actual head upper submersion.
The literal projection equalities identify the two recursive bad
sets, and the preserved-open strict-square theorem identifies their
whole labelled tail inputs. Entire ordered boundary equalities
identify the true initial boundary of every next source stage.
Therefore append the actual source separator, head and tail towers;
actual pullback append gives the same full target and its true
Cartesian square. Every original part is dense away from $G$ and
every actual target centre lies over $G$. The genuine preserved-open
strict-square theorem therefore identifies all final source strict
parts, including exceptional points. Smooth pullback of the target
regular and normal-crossings coordinates gives the asserted source
geometry with its own entire boundary. Disjointness follows by
inverse image. All choices and all source stages were constructed
internally from the stated original inputs.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The source tree here retains all raw pulled labels, actual empty
blowups and whole boundary positions. The intrinsic nonempty
dimension-list dictionary identifies its nonempty input labels, but
does not by itself identify an independently filtered source
algorithm: deletion of empty blocks and recursive uniqueness require
their own genuine proofs. The appended resolution is not asserted to
be one canonical history for a different whole mixed-dimensional
ideal. This chapter does not assert the unrestricted noncompact
global scheduler.
\end{remark}
