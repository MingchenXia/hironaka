\section{Internally stopped intrinsic smooth sources of the same original local pure target}
\label{sec:hironaka-original-local-pure-boundary-independent-source}

The open set and target history are chosen before every smooth source.
The original carrier need not be compact. The only local size condition
is that the prescribed open lies in a given compact set; the final
neighbourhood form chooses such an open around any given compact set.
The original boundary remains ordered SNC.

\begin{definition}[A local original pure target with internally stopped intrinsic sources]
\label{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-internal-source-data}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalPureBoundaryIndependentSource.HasIndependentLocalPureBoundaryResolution}
Fix original analytic pure data and a prescribed open $U$. The stated
local resolution data consist of an actual canonical terminal history
$P$ for the whole intrinsic original ideal on $U$, a literal
first-meeting factorization $P=T+R$, and the complete pure embedded
geometry of this same $T$. For every smooth $b:Y\to U$, they further
include an actual whole pullback $B$ of $P$, an actual pullback $Q$ of
$T$, and an internally chosen canonical prefix $S$ of $B$ stopped by
its own intrinsic boundary-free regular open. The source prefix keeps
its literal tail, canonicality, next maximum when nonempty, identity
choice when empty, own testness and SNC boundary, and its full
original singular-plus-boundary admissible and exceptional geometry.
A unique biholomorphism over $Y$ compares $S$ with $Q$, giving the same
upper Cartesian submersion, whole strict carrier and exact boundary
identity after filtering only empty members. All target choices precede
the quantification over $b$; no source compactness is imposed.
\uses{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-same-target-independent-source}
\leanok
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The original local pure data internally choose every intrinsic stopped source]
\label{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-internal-source-producer}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalPureBoundaryIndependentSource.exists_local_original_independently_stopped_intrinsic_source}
An original globally analytic carrier $W$ of pure dimension $d<n$,
original ordered SNC boundary $E_0$ with density of $W$ away from it,
and a prescribed open $U$ contained in a given compact set internally
produce the preceding local target and source data. No whole local
canonical terminal history, source prefix, first-meeting maximum,
ideal identity or comparison certificate is an input.
\uses{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-internal-source-data,hir:original-local-pure-boundary-canonical-prefix-producer,hir:original-pure-boundary-given-terminal-first-prefix,hir:original-pure-boundary-independent-intrinsic-comparison}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose once the actual local canonical terminal $P$, its literal
embedded prefix $T$ and tail, with the full target geometry, using the
original-only local producer. For a given $b$, take its genuine actual
whole pullback $B$ of this same $P$. The whole original vanishing-ideal
pullback theorem identifies its source datum with the intrinsic source
carrier and its own literal pulled boundary. Actual canonical-history
and cosupport pullback prove that $B$ is canonical and terminal for
this source datum. The preceding same-target source theorem also
constructs an actual $Q$ over the same $T$ and supplies the unique
comparison law for every independently stopped intrinsic source.

If $Y$ has a point, the actual submersion gives $n\le m$; hence the
true shifted dimension $d+(m-n)$ is strictly below $m$. Openness pulls
back the original pure regular density and boundary density. Apply
the genuine terminal-prefix theorem to the intrinsic source carrier
and the actual source terminal $B$. It internally chooses $S$ and its
literal tail in $B$, with canonicality, avoidance of its own good
open and the actual next whole maximum when its carrier is nonempty.
If $Y$ is empty, use its literal identity prefix and all of $B$ as
tail. Its intrinsic carrier and cosupport are empty; the real canonical
empty-cosupport theorem makes $B$ all-empty. Every asserted source
encounter condition is vacuous, with no false global dimension bound.

Apply the actual unique comparison law to this internally selected
$S$. It gives its own testness, whole strict-carrier square, exact
filtered boundary, shifted regular dimension and normal crossings,
using the full geometry of the same target $T$. Its centres lie both
over its own original cosupport and over the complement of its own
good open, hence over exactly its own original singular-plus-boundary
bad set. Actual test geometry gives its admissible sequence and SNC
boundary. The literal new-boundary support contains its entire
exceptional locus and lies over that same bad set. Its resolved
strict carrier puts the inverse image of its original singular set
in its actual exceptional locus. True finite blowup geometry gives
holomorphy, properness and surjectivity, and confinement of the actual
exceptional image gives the isomorphism off its own bad set. Every
source choice is internal and its literal factorization in the actual
pullback of the same target terminal history is retained.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One relatively compact neighbourhood of a given compact set works before every smooth source]
\label{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-compact-neighbourhood-internal-source}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalLocalPureBoundaryIndependentSource.exists_original_pure_boundary_canonical_local}
For the same original globally analytic pure carrier and ordered SNC
boundary data and any compact $K\subset X$, internally choose an open
neighbourhood $U$ of $K$ with compact closure and all the preceding
actual target and internally stopped source data on this same $U$.
The neighbourhood and target are fixed before every smooth source;
no functorial choice of neighbourhoods is asserted.
\uses{hir:original-local-pure-boundary-internal-source-producer,hir:resolution-relatively-compact}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The finite-dimensional complex manifold is locally compact. A genuine
compact-neighbourhood theorem gives one actual open $U$ containing
$K$ with compact closure. Apply the preceding original-only producer
to this very open, using its compact closure as the prescribed compact
container and $U\subset\overline U$. Both the neighbourhood and the
resulting target are therefore chosen before quantifying over sources.
All source factorization, canonical stopping, actual Cartesian and
original good-locus conclusions are those of the same chosen target.
\end{proof}
