\section{Actual whole homogeneous histories over every origin neighbourhood}
\label{sec:hironaka-homogeneous-canonical-neighbourhoods}

Retain the entire lifted coordinate model, a selected coordinate union,
and its whole original marked datum $M_S=(X,X,\varnothing,I_{H_S},1)$.
Let $R$ be a proved invariant whose dimension bound is at least the
ambient dimension. All neighbourhoods below are genuine open subsets
of the original coordinate base containing its origin. All prefixes,
open restrictions and centres are the actual ones.

\begin{theorem}[Every whole prefix value occurs over every origin neighbourhood]
\label{hir:homogeneous-every-whole-value-over-origin-neighbourhood}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalNeighbourhoods.exists_value_representative_over_neighbourhood}
For any actual finite canonical prefix and any actual origin
neighbourhood, every whole value on its entire transformed cosupport
is represented by an actual cosupport point whose original projection
belongs to that neighbourhood. Empty historical positions are allowed.
\leanok
\uses{hir:homogeneous-exact-canonical-prefix-automorphism-lift,hir:homogeneous-model-entire-record-and-prefix-value}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Pull the actual open neighbourhood back under the lifted-model
coordinate homeomorphism. It contains a genuine ball of radius
$\epsilon>0$ about zero. For a current cosupport point with original
coordinate image $v$, put
$c=(\epsilon/2)/(\lVert v\rVert+1)>0$, regarded as a nonzero complex
scalar. The actual whole-record homothety lift along the exact given
canonical prefix preserves the entire current value and current
cosupport. Its exact projection equation sends the actual original
image to $cv$. The inequality
$\lVert cv\rVert=(\epsilon/2)\lVert v\rVert/(\lVert v\rVert+1)
\leq\epsilon/2<\epsilon$ places this actual image in the supplied open
neighbourhood. Thus the lifted actual point is the required
representative. This uses no invariance of the neighbourhood under
scaling and no equality inferred from current incidence data.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The exact entire full maximum restricts to the exact local full maximum]
\label{hir:homogeneous-origin-neighbourhood-whole-maximum-preimage}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalNeighbourhoods.maxLocus_restrict_eq}
For every such actual prefix and neighbourhood, the actual full maximum
of the restricted original datum along the literal restricted history
is exactly the preimage of the entire original full maximum.
\leanok
\uses{hir:homogeneous-every-whole-value-over-origin-neighbourhood,hir:invariant-order}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual whole-transform naturality identifies the restricted cosupport
with the preimage of the entire current cosupport. The invariant's
actual open-history law identifies their whole values pointwise.
An entire maximum point lying in that preimage consequently dominates
all restricted cosupport values, so lies in the restricted maximum.
Conversely, take a restricted maximum point and any entire cosupport
point. The preceding theorem represents the latter's entire value at
an actual cosupport point over the neighbourhood. The restricted
maximum dominates that representative value. Pointwise whole-value
compatibility therefore makes it dominate the original arbitrary
entire value. Thus it is an entire maximum point. Both inclusions
hold even when the cosupport is empty.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A genuine canonical centre loses no nonemptiness on restriction]
\label{hir:homogeneous-origin-neighbourhood-exact-empty-centre-slots}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalNeighbourhoods.centreTrace_nonempty_iff}
For any actual next step of an actual canonical prefix, its literal
centre trace over an origin neighbourhood is nonempty if and only if
the actual entire centre is nonempty. Thus literal empty slots and
nonempty full-maximum slots are preserved.
\leanok
\uses{hir:homogeneous-every-whole-value-over-origin-neighbourhood}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A point of the literal centre trace is an actual point of the entire
centre, giving one implication. For the other, the actual canonical
step says its centre is empty or the entire full maximum. A nonempty
centre excludes the empty case. Choose an actual point in that full
maximum and represent its whole value over the neighbourhood by the
preceding theorem. The representative is in the actual cosupport and
has the same whole maximum value, hence is in that very entire
centre. It defines an actual point of the literal centre trace.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Literal restriction preserves every nonempty-centre canonical history]
\label{hir:homogeneous-origin-neighbourhood-nonempty-canonical-history}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalNeighbourhoods.isCanonicalSegment_restrict}
The literal restriction to an origin neighbourhood of every actual
nonempty-centre canonical segment of $M_S$ is a nonempty-centre
canonical segment of the literal restricted original datum.
\leanok
\uses{hir:homogeneous-origin-neighbourhood-whole-maximum-preimage,hir:homogeneous-origin-neighbourhood-exact-empty-centre-slots}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual given history. Restriction of the reflexive
history is reflexive. At a genuine next step, restriction gives
exactly the already restricted preceding history followed by the
actual blowup of the literal centre trace. The induction hypothesis
gives canonicality of the preceding restricted segment. The preceding
lemma makes its new centre trace nonempty. Its actual original centre
is the entire full maximum, and the exact full-maximum preimage theorem
identifies that literal trace with the entire full maximum of the
restricted history. These are precisely the nonempty-centre canonical
segment conditions for the actual restricted next step. No historical
slot is discarded or inserted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One actual entire coordinate resolution restricts canonically over every origin neighbourhood]
\label{hir:homogeneous-common-coordinate-resolution-origin-neighbourhood}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaHomogeneousCanonicalNeighbourhoods.exists_common_coordinate_resolution_on_neighbourhood}
For the actual ambient-dimensional canonical invariant and every
selected coordinate union, every genuine origin neighbourhood has an
internally constructed actual entire coordinate canonical resolution
whose literal restriction is also a nonempty-centre canonical
resolution of the literal restricted original datum.
\leanok
\uses{hir:homogeneous-actual-canonicalInvariant-coordinate-resolution,hir:homogeneous-origin-neighbourhood-nonempty-canonical-history}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Internally construct an actual entire coordinate resolution by the
proved ambient-dimensional canonical invariant. Its literal centre
history is a nonempty-centre canonical segment. The preceding theorem
makes its literal restriction another nonempty-centre canonical
segment. The actual restriction theorem for test towers preserves
admissibility, and the genuine cosupport restriction identity turns
the entire final empty cosupport into the local empty cosupport.
Hence this same entire actual tower and its literal restriction give
the claimed two resolutions. No incoming resolution, common history,
local maximum, or centre comparison is assumed. This is a statement
about coordinate original models and their actual open restrictions;
arbitrary original normal-crossing chart globalization remains a
separate construction.
\end{proof}
