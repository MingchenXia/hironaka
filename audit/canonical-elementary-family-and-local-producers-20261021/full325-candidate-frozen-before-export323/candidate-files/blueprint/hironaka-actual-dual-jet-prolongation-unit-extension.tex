\section{The genuine differential prolongation vanishes for the original unit-ideal complement}

\begin{theorem}[Canonical actual directional derivative germ gives precisely the original successor scalar jet]
\label{hir:actual-canonical-directional-germ-successor-scalar-jet}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-actual-iterated-derivative-germs,hir:actual-analytic-directional-jet-commutation}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualDualJetProlongationUnit.actual_directional_derivative_germ_scalar_jet}
\leanok
For every whole original holomorphic germ $g$, actual direction $w$,
order $q$ and original coordinate word $r$ of degree less than $q$,
the old scalar-jet coordinate of the canonical existing derivative
germ $\operatorname{iteratedDerivativeGerm}[w](g)$ is exactly the
successor original derivative component of $J_{q+1}g$ evaluated with
$w$ first and the same original row tuple afterward. The canonical
derivative operation is fixed before every jet order; no new
replacement derivative germ is chosen.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Represent the original whole germ by an actual function $f$
holomorphic on a true neighborhood of zero. The existing canonical
derivative-germ dictionary identifies the derivative with the germ
of $z\mapsto Df(z)(w)$, which is genuinely holomorphic near zero.
Actual holomorphicity supplies an analytic germ at zero. The accepted
analytic directional-jet identity says that the $|r|$-th derivative
of this same directional derivative function, on the original row
tuple, equals the $(|r|+1)$-st original derivative of $f$ with $w$
first. The unchanged finite-jet and scalar-coordinate definitions
are precisely those evaluations. Thus the equality holds for the
same original germ, canonical derivative, direction and row.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Zero on the entire actual old jet image implies zero prolongation on the entire true next jet image]
\label{hir:actual-dual-jet-prolongation-zero-old-image-zero-next-image}
\uses{hir:actual-dual-jet-differential-prolongation,hir:actual-canonical-directional-germ-successor-scalar-jet}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualDualJetProlongationUnit.actualDualJetProlongation_zero_of_old_true_jet_zero}
\leanok
Let the actual original row coefficients $\eta_r(c)$ be holomorphic
on one true open $V\subset\mathbb C^d$. Suppose that at every
$c\in V$ their actual pairing with the old jet of every whole
original holomorphic germ is zero. Then their genuine differential
prolongation vanishes on every actual next-order jet at every
$c\in V$, in every tangent direction. Only genuine old and next
finite-jet images are used; no full multilinear-product realization
or individual redundant-row coefficient vanishing is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix an actual next jet and choose one original whole germ $g$
representing it. For this fixed germ, the actual scalar function
$c\mapsto\sum_r\eta_r(c)(J_qg)_r$ is zero on the actual open.
Differentiate this true finite sum. The old jet coordinates are fixed
complex scalars, so the finite product rule says precisely that the
sum of the derivative-coefficient terms in the genuine prolongation
is zero. No individual redundant row coefficient is concluded zero.

Choose an actual representative $f$ of the same germ $g$ that is
holomorphic near zero. Its analytic germ at zero follows from actual
complex holomorphicity on a genuine open neighborhood. For the fixed
actual direction $Ta$, the function $z\mapsto Df(z)(Ta)$ is
holomorphic on a genuine neighborhood of zero, by analytic Fr\'echet
differentiation and existing continuous linear direction evaluation.
It therefore gives the existing canonical original holomorphic germ
$h=\operatorname{iteratedDerivativeGerm}[Ta](g)$.
The accepted actual analytic directional-jet identity identifies each
old derivative coordinate of $h$ with the corresponding successor
coordinate of the same original next jet of $g$, with $Ta$ first.
The original zero-on-all-old-germs hypothesis applied to this actual
$h$ makes the whole successor term sum zero. Adding the two zero
sums gives exactly the genuine prolongation evaluated at the chosen
original next jet. The empty sum $q=0$ is included. This proof uses
only genuine holomorphic representatives and actual jet identities.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An actual original unit ideal needs no generator-vanishing premise]
\label{hir:actual-dual-jet-prolongation-original-unit-ideal-zero}
\uses{hir:actual-dual-jet-prolongation-zero-old-image-zero-next-image,hir:actual-macaulay-annihilator-whole-ideal-jet}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualDualJetProlongationUnit.actualDualJetProlongation_zero_of_translatedIdeal_eq_top}
\leanok
Use the same original holomorphic family on a true open $U$ and
actual parameter open $V$ with $T(V)\subset U$. Suppose actual
holomorphic moving rows annihilate every original old Macaulay column
on $V$, and the unchanged entire original translated ideal is the
unit ideal at every actual $Tc$ in this domain. Then their actual
differential prolongation vanishes on every true next jet there, in
every tangent direction. No original generator value is assumed zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At each actual parameter point, every whole original germ belongs to
the unchanged original translated ideal because that ideal is the
whole ring. The accepted entire-original-ideal annihilator theorem
turns actual old-column annihilation into zero pairing with the old
jet of every such germ. This is exactly the hypothesis of the
preceding genuine old-image-to-next-image theorem on the same actual
open and original row functions. Apply it. The conclusion kills the
entire actual next jet image, so it also kills the jet of every germ
of the original unit ideal. This is the true unit-ideal complement
to the proper-case generator-vanishing argument; it does not derive
vanishing of generators of a unit ideal.
\end{proof}
