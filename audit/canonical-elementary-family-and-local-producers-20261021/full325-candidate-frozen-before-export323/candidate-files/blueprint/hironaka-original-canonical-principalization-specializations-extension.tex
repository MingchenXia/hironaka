\begin{theorem}[The actual original canonical producers give the full result]
\label{hir:original-principalization-public-canonical-producer}

\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One nonzero germ on a connected manifold is nonzero everywhere]
\label{hir:original-principalization-connected-nonzero-germ-reduction}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalPrincipalizationSpecializations.nonzeroAlong_of_nonzero_germ}
On a connected complex manifold, a coherent original ideal with one
germ $g\in I_x$ nonzero frequently near $x$ is nonzero along the whole
ambient manifold at every point.
\uses{mc:factorization-domain,hir:public-statements-complete}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
That original germ prevents the whole ideal from being contained in
the vanishing ideal of the entire ambient manifold: such containment
would make $g$ zero on an actual neighbourhood, contradicting its
frequent nonvanishing. The connected ambient manifold is a closed
connected submanifold of itself. Apply the proved coherent
identity-principle theorem
\texttt{FactorizationDomain.isNonzeroAlongAt\_of\_not\_le} to that
whole actual submanifold and this genuine noncontainment. It gives
nonzero-along at every ambient point, without an everywhere-nonzero
input or a desired principalization certificate.
\end{proof}
