\section{Actual projection neutrality of an empty recursive head}

\begin{lemma}[Every actual all-empty tower has its own biholomorphic projection]
\label{hir:original-all-empty-tower-projection}
\uses{hir:original-empty-filtered-parts-tree-projection-neutrality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalEmptyHeadPrefix.exists_empty_tower_projection}
\leanok
An actual finite blowup tower whose every actual centre is empty has a
genuine biholomorphism to its original base whose underlying map is
literally that tower's entire projection.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual tower. The identity tower uses the identity
biholomorphism. At an actual step, the empty-tower hypothesis gives
both an empty preceding history and an empty final centre. The genuine
empty-blowup theorem makes the final blowup map a biholomorphism.
Compose it with the inductively constructed preceding projection.
The actual projection formula for an appended step proves equality
of the whole underlying map. No chosen terminal-resolution certificate
is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual separator and pure-head prefix of an empty head]
\label{hir:original-empty-head-prefix-projection}
\uses{hir:original-all-empty-tower-projection,hir:original-empty-head-separation-chain-all-empty,hir:original-pure-head-unique-empty-base-comparison}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalEmptyHeadPrefix.exists_empty_head_prefix_projection}
\leanok
Let an actual ordered separator chain start with head $A=\varnothing$,
original boundary $E$, arbitrary raw labelled tail and actual tower
$T_0$. Let $T_1$ be an actual pure-head stage for the actual strict
transform of this head and the separator's recorded boundary. Write
$T=T_0\mathbin{+}T_1$ and let $E_f$ be the whole boundary recorded by
this actual pure-head stage. Then every actual centre of $T$ is empty,
and its projection is a genuine biholomorphism $\beta$ to the original
base. The entire chronological boundary is exactly
\[
 E_f=E.map(\beta^{-1})+ [\varnothing]^{T.length}.
\]
Consequently its filtered nonempty list is exactly the filtered list
of $E.map(\beta^{-1})$. For every original subset $P$, including
nonclosed raw tails, its conventional strict transform is exactly
$\beta^{-1}(\overline P)$. No closedness assumption on those raw
subsets or on the raw tail is imposed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine empty-head separator theorem forces every actual centre
of $T_0$ empty. Its actual strict head is empty. The whole original
cosupport of the following pure-head marked ideal is therefore empty,
so its genuine canonical history $T_1$ also has only empty centres.
The actual append formula gives this for the same whole prefix $T$.
Apply the preceding lemma to construct its actual projection $\beta$.

The original boundary is closed: a nonempty separator supplies its
actual invariant-domain SNC boundary; when the chain is nil, its
boundary is unchanged and the actual pure-head SNC record supplies
it. The genuine separator boundary identity and independence of
geometric boundary transport from the auxiliary ideal identify $E_f$
with the boundary of the same whole $T$ starting at $E$. The actual
all-empty boundary theorem therefore gives precisely the full list
of original inverse images followed by one empty entry for every
actual centre. Filtering the latter entries gives the stated exact
ordered-list equality.

A genuine biholomorphism has empty differential exceptional locus.
The definition of the conventional strict transform thus reduces to
the closure of its inverse image of $P$. The actual homeomorphism is
open and continuous, so this is the inverse image of the closure of
$P$. This proves the whole-set formula for arbitrary raw subsets,
without replacing them by closed inputs as a premise.
\end{proof}
