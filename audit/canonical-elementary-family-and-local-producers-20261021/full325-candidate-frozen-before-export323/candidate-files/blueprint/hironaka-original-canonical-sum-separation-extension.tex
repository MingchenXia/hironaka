\section{Canonical sum-ideal separation with the same actual smooth Cartesian stages}

Let $A,B\subset X^n$ be original analytic subsets, with $A$ of empty
interior, $A\cap B$ compact, and
\[
 A\subset\overline{A\setminus B},\qquad
 B\subset\overline{B\setminus A}.
\]
Let $E_0$ be an actual ordered SNC boundary. Define the unchanged
auxiliary datum
$M=(X,X,E_0,\mathcal I_A+\mathcal I_B,1)$.
All sums and vanishing ideals below are their independently defined
original functional ideals. No separator or future history is input.

\begin{lemma}[The intrinsic sum datum is the exact whole smooth pullback]
\label{hir:original-canonical-sum-intrinsic-source-record}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSumSeparation.sumMarkedIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSumSeparation.isInvDomain_sumMarkedIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSumSeparation.pullback_sumMarkedIdeal_eq}
\uses{hir:actual-singular-vanishing-ideal-pullback-submersion,hir:submersion-relative-ideal-sums}
\leanok
For every genuine submersion $b:Y^m\to X^n$, the actual entire pulled
record $b^*M$ is literally
\[
 N=(Y,Y,b^{-1}E_0,\mathcal I_{b^{-1}A}+\mathcal I_{b^{-1}B},1),
\]
retaining every original ordered boundary position. The source subsets
and source ideal are intrinsic, and no source compactness is assumed.
The stated original analytic, empty-interior and SNC hypotheses internally
make $M$ an actual invariant-domain datum.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual ambient preimage of $X$ is $Y$ and the marking stays one.
The ordered boundary is the literal inverse-image list by the original
marked pullback definition. Analyticity of $A,B$ gives coherence of both
original vanishing ideals. The proved whole sum-pullback identity
therefore writes the actual pulled ideal as the sum of their two actual
pullbacks. Genuine singular-submersion vanishing-ideal equality identifies
these with the independently defined whole source vanishing ideals of
$b^{-1}A$ and $b^{-1}B$. Equality of every original record field proves
the literal entire marked-record equality.
For the last assertion, Cartan coherence gives coherence of the sum.
The empty interior of $A$ makes its vanishing ideal nonzero along every
point, and inclusion in the sum preserves this property. Together with
the stated SNC boundary, these are exactly the ambient invariant-domain
conditions for marking one.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original analytic geometry internally selects a true canonical separator]
\label{hir:original-canonical-sum-selected-terminal-separator}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSumSeparation.exists_canonical_sum_separation}
\uses{hir:separation,hir:original-canonical-boundary-principalization-compact-cosupport}
\leanok
The original data internally produce an actual manifold $D$, a literal
finite tower $T:X\leftarrow D$, and its whole ordered boundary
$E_T=(M_T).E$, such that $T$ is a genuine canonical terminal resolution
of the same auxiliary sum datum for the actual
$\operatorname{canonicalInvariant}(n)$. Its centres lie over $A\cap B$,
it is admissible relative to $E_0$, $E_T$ is SNC, and
\[
 T^*(\mathcal I_A+\mathcal I_B)=\mathcal O(-D_T),\qquad
 |D_T|=T^{-1}(A\cap B),\qquad A_T\cap B_T=\varnothing.
\]
Here $D_T$ is the actual total exceptional divisor and $A_T,B_T$ are
the whole original geometric strict transforms. The same selected map
is proper, surjective, holomorphic and an isomorphism off $A\cap B$.
The choice of $T$ precedes every smooth source in the next theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Cartan coherence and the original sum construction make
$J=\mathcal I_A+\mathcal I_B$ coherent. Empty interior of the original
analytic $A$ makes its vanishing ideal nonzero along every point; its
inclusion in $J$ gives the same property for $J$. Its whole zero locus
is exactly $A\cap B$. Thus the genuine compact-cosupport canonical
driver applies to the unchanged original marked sum datum, with its
SNC boundary and positive marking one. Choose the actual finite terminal
tower it supplies, before choosing any smooth source.

Actual canonical testness makes every centre admissible and confines its
image to the original cosupport $A\cap B$. The genuine terminal
principalization identity gives $T^*J=\mathcal O(-D_T)$; the actual zero
locus pullback formula gives $|D_T|=T^{-1}(A\cap B)$. The accumulated
whole boundary is SNC by true marked-transform geometry. The genuine
separation lemma now applies to these actual principal ideal and support
identities and the two stated original density conditions. It makes the
whole conventional strict transforms $A_T,B_T$ disjoint. Finite actual
blowup towers are holomorphic, proper and surjective. Their actual
exceptional locus lies in $|D_T|$, so the same selected map is an
isomorphism over the complement of $A\cap B$. No separation tower,
principal ideal equality or terminal certificate was assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every genuine smooth source retains that same selected separator and whole geometry]
\label{hir:original-canonical-sum-same-separator-smooth-square}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalSumSeparation.exists_canonical_sum_separation}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-sum-intrinsic-source-record,hir:original-canonical-sum-selected-terminal-separator,hir:original-canonical-boundary-principalization-smooth,hir:original-stopped-strict-transform-smooth-square}
\leanok
For the very $D,T$ chosen above, \emph{every} genuine submersion
$b:Y^m\to X^n$ internally produces an actual finite tower $Q:Y\leftarrow C$
and actual upper submersion $g:C\to D$. Their square is genuinely
holomorphic Cartesian, $Tg=bQ$, and their literal lengths agree,
including every empty pulled centre. The source tower is canonical and
terminal for the intrinsic original sum datum $N$ above, using the
actual $\operatorname{canonicalInvariant}(m)$. It is admissible with
centres over $b^{-1}(A\cap B)$, its \emph{entire unfiltered} boundary
satisfies
\[
 E_Q=g^{-1}E_T,
\]
and is SNC. Its total pullback of the whole intrinsic source sum ideal
is its actual total exceptional divisor ideal. Its whole strict sets
satisfy
\[
 (b^{-1}A)_Q=g^{-1}A_T,\qquad (b^{-1}B)_Q=g^{-1}B_T,
\]
and are disjoint. The source projection is holomorphic, proper,
surjective and an isomorphism off $b^{-1}(A\cap B)$. No source
compactness, source resolving tower or comparison certificate is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply genuine stepwise smooth Cartesian blowup construction to the same
selected actual $T$ and original $b$. It internally produces $Q,g$,
including every empty inverse-image centre, with the actual universal
property, submersion property, commuting projections and equal lengths.
The existing actual ambient canonical comparison makes this same source
history canonical for its original marked pullback. Actual whole marked
transform comparison and target terminality make it terminal. The first
lemma identifies this whole pulled source record with the literal
intrinsic sum datum $N$. The same actual whole-transform equality gives
$E_Q=E_T.\mathrm{map}(g^{-1})$, with no deletion or reindexing of empty
or repeated old entries. Source canonical testness gives genuine SNC and
admissibility. The whole zero-locus pullback formula identifies the
source cosupport with $b^{-1}(A\cap B)$; actual canonical testness then
confines every source centre over this original source set.
Source terminal principalization gives the displayed
whole intrinsic source sum-ideal equation. Properness, surjectivity and
holomorphy follow from the actual source blowups; confinement of the
actual exceptional locus gives the claimed source isomorphism.

Put $O=X\setminus(A\cap B)$. Every actual target centre avoids this
original open by its just-proved confinement. The original density
conditions imply $A\subset\overline{A\cap O}$ and
$B\subset\overline{B\cap O}$. The genuine preserved-open strict-transform
formula writes each target strict set as the closure of its actual
preimage on $O$. Actual submersions are continuous and open, so inverse
image commutes with these closures. Apply the actual Cartesian strict-set
theorem to this same $T,Q,g,O$ to obtain both asserted \emph{whole}
strict-transform equalities, including exceptional points. Their
intersection is empty because $A_T,B_T$ are disjoint. All source data
were constructed after fixing the same target witness, so the target
map has not been replaced for a different smooth source.
\end{proof}

This is a canonical functorial separation stage for finite mixed-dimension
recursion. Its auxiliary sum history is canonical; no assertion requires
its eventual composite with head resolution and tail recursion to be one
canonical history of the vanishing ideal of the entire mixed-dimensional
set. That finite ordered recursion remains a separate construction.
