\section{A category with actual reduced complex analytic local models}

\begin{definition}[The complex point and actual embedded models over it]
\label{hir:reduced-analytic-complex-point-models}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.complexPoint,Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.modelOver}
Let the complex point be the genuine locally ringed space of the
zero-dimensional complex manifold $\mathbb C^0$. Every actual subspace
model has its structural morphism to this point, obtained by composing
the actual ambient inclusion with the original constant holomorphic map.
For an open $U\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ and $C\subseteq U$, denote this
actual object over the complex point by $\operatorname{Model}(U,C)$.
The slice morphisms retain the complex structural map.
\leanok
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Reduced complex analytic spaces]
\label{hir:reduced-analytic-space-category}
\uses{hir:reduced-analytic-complex-point-models,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-structure-sheaf}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.hasAnalyticCharts,Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.ReducedComplexAnalyticSpace}
A reduced complex analytic space is a Hausdorff, second countable
locally ringed space over the complex point such that every point lies
in the image of an actual open immersion
\[
 \operatorname{Model}(U,C)\longrightarrow X
\]
over the complex point, with $U$ open in some $\mathbb C^n$ and $C$ a
closed complex analytic subset of $U$. Use the full subcategory of the
native slice category on this local-model property. This definition
contains no resolution, centre, atlas-descent or terminal-map data.
\leanok
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual closed analytic model is an object]
\label{hir:reduced-analytic-embedded-model-object}
\uses{hir:reduced-analytic-space-category}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.hasAnalyticCharts_model,Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.embeddedModel}
Every closed analytic subset of an actual open $U\subseteq\mathbb C^n$,
with its already constructed quotient structure sheaf, is an object of
this category.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine underlying subset of $U$ is Hausdorff and second countable
by the inherited topologies. At any point use this same original
analytic model and its identity morphism as the local chart. The identity
is an actual open immersion, covers the point, and commutes with its
structural morphism to the complex point. Thus every part of the
local-model property follows from the original analytic subset.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Reducedness of every actual abstract stalk]
\label{hir:reduced-analytic-abstract-stalks-reduced}
\uses{hir:reduced-analytic-space-category,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-reduced-stalks}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.isReduced_stalk}
Every stalk of every object of this category is a reduced local ring.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Locality is part of the native locally ringed space. At a given point
take an actual analytic model chart covering it and a genuine preimage
point. The open immersion induces an actual isomorphism from the
abstract stalk to that model stalk. The latter is the previously proved
whole reduced vanishing-quotient local ring. If an element of the
abstract stalk is nilpotent, its image in the model stalk is nilpotent
and hence zero. Injectivity of the actual stalk isomorphism makes the
original element zero. Reducedness is therefore proved from the local
models, rather than inserted as an additional stalk certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original holomorphic maps give genuine complex-structural morphisms]
\label{hir:reduced-analytic-model-holomorphic-morphisms}
\uses{hir:reduced-analytic-embedded-model-object,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-holomorphic-map,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-map-functor-laws}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.actualSubspaceMap_point,Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.embeddedModelMap,Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.embeddedModelMap_id,Hironaka.HironakaReducedComplexAnalyticSpaces.embeddedModelMap_comp}
An actual holomorphic map between original open coordinate domains,
carrying their specified analytic subsets into one another, induces a
genuine morphism of these objects. It retains the literal quotient
subspace map, commutes with the structural morphisms to the complex
point, and preserves identities and composition.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual subspace-map inclusion square reduces structural commutation
to the native composition law for original holomorphic ambient maps.
Composing any such map with the original constant map to $\mathbb C^0$
is precisely the original source constant map. The resulting equality
is the actual commutative triangle defining a slice morphism; the full
subcategory then supplies the asserted analytic-space morphism.
The underlying slice arrows of identity and composition are the actual
subspace maps. Their previously proved functor laws give equality of
these arrows. Native slice and full-subcategory extensionality give
the exact analytic-space identity and composition laws.
\end{proof}

This supplies a concrete abstract reduced analytic-space category and its
embedded generators. Intrinsic smooth loci, effective atlas gluing,
change between unrelated presentations and resolution descent still
require their actual geometric producers.
