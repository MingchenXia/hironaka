\section{Actual general normal-crossing canonical histories in whole coordinate models}
\label{sec:hironaka-normal-crossings-canonical-chart-histories}

Let $H$ be a general normal-crossing carrier in an actual complex
manifold $A$. Its datum throughout is the whole original
$M_H=(A,A,\varnothing,I_H,1)$. Let $R$ be a proved resolution invariant
whose dimension bound is at least the ambient dimension. No global
component labels, global compactness, or proposed future history is
part of the hypotheses.

\begin{theorem}[An actual original chart identifies the whole original marked records]
\label{hir:general-nc-actual-original-coordinate-chart-record}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCanonicalChartHistories.exists_original_coordinate_chart}
Every original point has a genuine open chart $U$ onto an actual open
coordinate neighbourhood $V$ of the origin, and an actual selected
coordinate union $H_S$, such that the chart pullback of the literal
restricted whole original coordinate record equals the literal
restricted whole original record of $H$.
\leanok
\uses{hir:general-normal-crossings-carrier,hir:actual-flat-domain-biholomorphism,hir:general-nc-one-step-history-universal-comparison}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the actual normal-crossing chart from the definition at the
given original point. Its genuine flattening map and inverse give an
actual biholomorphism between its open source and its lifted open
target. The chart centre maps to zero, so the actual target contains
the origin. The defining coordinate equation identifies the entire
carrier trace with the chart preimage of the whole selected coordinate
union trace. Actual restriction transports the whole holomorphic
vanishing ideal, and the genuine chart and inverse transport that
entire ideal predicate. Both original records have full ambient
carrier, empty boundary and marking one. The carrier trace identity
therefore gives equality of their whole marked records, rather than
only equality of zero sets or current strict transforms.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Whole actual value and cosupport comparison in an actual chart history]
\label{hir:general-nc-actual-chart-history-whole-value-and-cosupport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCanonicalChartHistories.chart_history_cosupp_value}
Suppose an actual original canonical prefix and an actual entire
coordinate canonical prefix have a genuine whole history isomorphism
between their literal restrictions over the chosen original chart.
The isomorphism identifies their actual whole cosupports and whole
values at the actual corresponding points of those restrictions.
\leanok
\uses{hir:general-nc-actual-original-coordinate-chart-record,hir:homogeneous-origin-neighbourhood-whole-maximum-preimage}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual coordinate prefix restricts canonically over the genuine
origin neighbourhood. Actual whole-transform naturality for the given
history isomorphism, together with the original whole-record identity,
identifies the two restricted cosupports. The genuine restriction
identities identify these restricted cosupports with the traces of
the two entire current cosupports. On these actual cosupport points,
the invariant's genuine tower-isomorphism law identifies the entire
entries of the restricted history values. Its original and coordinate
open-history laws identify those values with the respective entire
prefix values. Combining these three equalities proves the whole-value
comparison at the actual corresponding points. No current branch or
boundary-incidence description is substituted for the histories.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[An actual original next step constructs its actual whole model next step]
\label{hir:general-nc-actual-chart-history-next-model-step}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCanonicalChartHistories.exists_next_model_chart_history}
Given such an actual chart history comparison, every actual canonical
next step of the original prefix internally supplies an actual
canonical next step of the entire coordinate prefix and a genuine
whole history isomorphism of the literal extended chart histories.
\leanok
\uses{hir:general-nc-actual-chart-history-whole-value-and-cosupport,hir:homogeneous-every-whole-value-over-origin-neighbourhood,hir:general-nc-one-step-history-universal-comparison}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Inspect the literal trace of the actual original next centre in the
chosen chart history. If that trace is empty, take an actual
blowup of the empty centre as the next entire model step. Its literal
restricted centre and the original centre trace agree under the
already constructed history biholomorphism.

Otherwise the actual original centre is its entire full maximum:
the empty-centre canonical alternative is impossible. Choose an
actual centre point in the trace. The whole-history comparison carries
its cosupport and whole value to the actual coordinate chart history.
Every whole value of the entire model prefix is represented over the
same genuine target neighbourhood. Carrying these representatives
back by the actual history isomorphism shows that the chosen original
maximum value dominates every entire model value. Consequently its
model image is an entire model maximum.

Conversely, every entire model maximum in the target trace has the
same whole value as that image and carries back to an actual original
cosupport point with the original entire maximum value. Thus the
actual original centre trace is exactly the history preimage of the
entire model maximum trace. Genuine homogeneous maximum admissibility
and smooth-centre blowup existence construct a real blowup of that
entire model maximum. In both cases actual open restriction gives
genuine blowups of the corresponding literal centre traces. The
actual one-step universal comparison constructs their lifted
biholomorphism. Its exact projection equation appends the preceding
whole history isomorphism. The new model centre is exactly empty or
the entire actual model full maximum, so the entire model prefix
remains canonical, with exactly one new actual position.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual general NC canonical prefix has an actual whole coordinate chart history]
\label{hir:general-nc-every-actual-canonical-prefix-coordinate-history}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCanonicalChartHistories.exists_original_nc_chart_model_history}
For every actual finite canonical prefix of the original datum and
every original point, an actual original chart is internally chosen,
and an actual entire coordinate canonical prefix of the same literal
length is internally constructed, together with a genuine whole
history isomorphism of their literal open restrictions. Empty and
full-maximum model positions retain the actual chronological slots.
\leanok
\uses{hir:general-nc-actual-original-coordinate-chart-record,hir:general-nc-actual-chart-history-next-model-step}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First choose the actual original chart by the first theorem, and keep
that same chart fixed. Induct on the actual given original tower. For
the reflexive tower use the actual reflexive coordinate tower; the
genuine chosen base chart is its whole history isomorphism, and both
lengths are zero. At a genuine original next step, the induction
hypothesis supplies the actual entire model prefix and actual
restricted whole history comparison for the preceding literal
history. The next-model-step producer constructs a genuine next
entire model step and the appended actual whole history comparison.
Both lengths increase by exactly one, preserving their equality.
This constructs the claimed actual entire model history for the
whole given tower. The final producer assumes no selected model,
chart-history isomorphism, maximum image, or later canonical-history
certificate. It does not yet assert a global maximum or termination
for the arbitrary original NC space.
\end{proof}
