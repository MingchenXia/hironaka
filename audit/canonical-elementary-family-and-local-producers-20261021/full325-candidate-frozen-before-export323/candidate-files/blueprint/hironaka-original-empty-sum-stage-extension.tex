\section{Genuine projection neutrality of empty sum-separation stages}

\begin{lemma}[Disjoint original parts force every canonical sum centre empty]
\label{hir:original-disjoint-sum-all-empty-canonical-stage}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-sum-intrinsic-source-record}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalEmptySumStage.canonical_sum_stage_empty_of_disjoint}
\leanok
Let $A,B\subset X^n$ be actual analytic subsets, with $A$ of empty
interior, disjoint $A,B$, and actual ordered SNC boundary $E_0$.
For the unchanged original sum datum
$M=(X,E_0,\mathcal I_A+\mathcal I_B,1)$, every actual canonical history
$T$ has only empty centres and is already terminal. No compactness,
terminality or stage-neutrality certificate is supplied. In particular
this applies when a pulled dimension part is actually empty.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Original analyticity gives coherence of the whole sum ideal; empty
interior of $A$ and the original SNC boundary make the actual datum an
invariant-domain datum. Its marking-one cosupport is exactly its whole
zero locus $A\cap B$, which is empty by the actual disjointness.
The genuine original empty-cosupport theorem applies to this very actual
canonical history: every transformed cosupport is empty, so each whole
maximum centre is empty, as is every permitted empty centre. Its actual
canonical test history therefore has empty final cosupport and is
terminal. No new history has replaced the supplied $T$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole empty sum stage is uniquely projection-neutral]
\label{hir:original-empty-sum-stage-unique-projection-neutrality}
\uses{hir:original-disjoint-sum-all-empty-canonical-stage,hir:original-ambient-terminal-choice-intrinsic-unique-comparison,hir:original-all-empty-whole-boundary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalEmptySumStage.existsUnique_projection_neutral_sum_stage}
\leanok
For the same original inputs and any actual canonical sum history
$T:X\leftarrow D$, there is a unique actual biholomorphism
$\eta:D\simeq X$ equal pointwise to its actual projection. Every original
closed subset $P$ has literal strict transform $\eta^{-1}P$. The actual
entire boundary is
\[
 E_T=E_0.\operatorname{map}(\eta^{-1})
       \mathbin{+}[\varnothing]^{\operatorname{length}(T)}.
\]
After filtering exactly empty entries it is therefore the filtered
inverse image of $E_0$. The original actual history is all-empty and
terminal. This retains its actual number of empty boundary positions;
comparison of subsequent recursive stages after deleting those positions
is a separate invariant-transport statement.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding lemma makes $T$ all-empty and terminal for the unchanged
sum datum. The identity history is also canonical and terminal for that
same empty-cosupport datum. Genuine actual terminal-history comparison,
including ambient dimension zero, gives an actual biholomorphism from
$D$ to $X$ commuting with these two projections. Since the second
projection is the literal identity, its underlying function equals the
actual $T$ projection pointwise. Any other such biholomorphism has that
same underlying function, hence equals it.
A genuine biholomorphism has no differential exceptional points and is a
homeomorphism. Its strict transform of any original closed subset is
therefore exactly its inverse image. The actual all-empty tower boundary
formula retains every old member as the inverse image under this same
projection and appends one actual empty entry for each performed blowup.
Substitute the proved pointwise projection identity. Filtering removes
exactly the appended empty entries, giving the displayed neutral
filtered boundary while retaining the entire unfiltered identity.
\end{proof}
