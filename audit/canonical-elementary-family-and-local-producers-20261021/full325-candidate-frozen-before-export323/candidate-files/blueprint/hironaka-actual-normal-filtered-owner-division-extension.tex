\section{Actual normal-filtered analytic owner division and relative initial relations}

Let $R=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^{d+t},0}$ be the actual original analytic
germ ring, let $N=(z_1,\ldots,z_t)$ be the original normal-coordinate
ideal, and write $\tau$ for its actual whole Taylor map. Put
$K=\tau(I)\mathbb C[[u,z]]$ for an original ideal $I\subset R$.
The finite actual degree-lex vertex labels are denoted by $a$, their
literal normal weights by $w_a=\sum_{j=1}^t a_{d+j}$, with weight
zero on every tangent coordinate and one on every normal coordinate,
and their canonical reduced formal series by
$F_a$. Throughout, a prescribed actual analytic family $G_a$ satisfies
$\tau(G_a)=F_a$. Thus no independently chosen generating family replaces it.

\begin{theorem}[Prescribed canonical analytic generators retain their literal recursive owner quotients]
\label{hir:actual-prescribed-canonical-analytic-owner-division}
\uses{hir:actual-original-canonical-reduced-vertex-basis,hir:actual-original-canonical-reduced-analytic-division,hir:actual-owner-division-formal-quotient-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalFilteredOwnerDivision.exists_prescribed_canonical_owner_division}
\leanok
For every actual $g\in R$, there are actual analytic germs $Q_a,s$ with
\[
 g=\sum_aG_aQ_a+s,\qquad
 \tau(s)=\operatorname{Rem}_K(\tau(g)),\qquad
 \tau(Q_a)=\operatorname{OwnerQuot}_{K,a}(\tau(g)).
\]
The last expression is the literal recursive quotient for the actual
canonical reduced family and the unchanged whole ideal $K$.
No analytic quotient, convergence or owner-support certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine analytic canonical reduced basis of $I$ has precisely the
same whole Taylor images as the prescribed $G_a$. Injectivity of the
actual Taylor map therefore identifies each of its analytic members
with the corresponding prescribed $G_a$. Apply the proved whole analytic
owner division for the canonical basis to $g$, and retain its actual
analytic quotients and canonical remainder. Its whole owner-support
condition and actual analytic identity identify the quotient Taylor
series with the exact recursive formal quotients by the proved
owner-supported relation uniqueness theorem. These identities concern
the same prescribed members just identified, the same ideal $K$ and
the same numerator $g$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual normal powers produce whole shifted normal powers of these same analytic quotients]
\label{hir:actual-prescribed-canonical-normal-filtered-division}
\uses{hir:actual-prescribed-canonical-analytic-owner-division,hir:actual-formal-normal-power-contracts-exact-original-analytic-power,hir:actual-monic-family-centre-power-division}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalFilteredOwnerDivision.exists_normal_filtered_canonical_division}
\leanok
Suppose additionally that every prescribed actual generator belongs to
the genuine whole original power $G_a\in N^{w_a}$. For every
$g\in N^q$, the preceding actual division has
$Q_a\in N^{q-w_a}$. All whole analytic identities and literal recursive
Taylor quotients above remain true for these same $Q_a$.
The generator power hypotheses are the actual centre-compatible powers,
not assumptions on any output quotient or relation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the preceding theorem without choosing a new family. Actual Taylor
normal-power naturality puts $\tau(g)$ in the whole formal normal power
$\widehat N^q$ and every $F_a$ in $\widehat N^{w_a}$. The genuine
coefficient recursion for this exact canonical formal family preserves
all coordinate weights compatible with its coefficients. Its proved
coordinate-power quotient bound therefore places the very same recursive
quotient in $\widehat N^{q-w_a}$. The preceding exact Taylor identity
identifies that series with $\tau(Q_a)$. The actual whole formal normal
power contracts to the original analytic normal power, so
$Q_a\in N^{q-w_a}$. Subtraction is truncated in the natural numbers,
so the assertion includes $q<w_a$, $q=0$ and an empty normal block.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A whole normal-order relation error has a genuine same-family analytic syzygy correction]
\label{hir:actual-prescribed-canonical-normal-syzygy-correction}
\uses{hir:actual-prescribed-canonical-normal-filtered-division}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalFilteredOwnerDivision.exists_normal_filtered_canonical_syzygy}
\leanok
Under the same prescribed-family and genuine generator-power hypotheses,
if actual analytic $P_a$ satisfy $E=\sum_aG_aP_a\in N^q$, there are
actual analytic $C_a$ with
\[
 \sum_aG_aC_a=0,\qquad P_a-C_a\in N^{q-w_a}.
\]
No exact syzygy or desired correction bound is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The entire error belongs to $I$, since each $G_a$ does: injectivity of
the actual Taylor map identifies each prescribed member with the genuine
original canonical basis, whose whole analytic span is $I$.
Apply the preceding normal-filtered division to this actual error $E$.
Its canonical formal remainder vanishes because $\tau(E)\in K$.
Actual Taylor injectivity makes the analytic remainder zero. Thus
$E=\sum_aG_aQ_a$ with each $Q_a\in N^{q-w_a}$. Set
$C_a=P_a-Q_a$. Distributing the finite sum gives
$\sum_aG_aC_a=E-E=0$, and the literal difference
$P_a-C_a=Q_a$ gives the asserted whole analytic correction power.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A literal relative homogeneous initial relation lifts to an actual analytic relation]
\label{hir:actual-prescribed-canonical-relative-initial-syzygy}
\uses{hir:actual-prescribed-canonical-normal-syzygy-correction,hir:actual-normal-homogeneous-polynomial,hir:actual-normal-homogeneous-entire-taylor-realization,hir:relative-normal-polynomial-jets-literal-variable-powers}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalFilteredOwnerDivision.exists_normal_homogeneous_initial_syzygy}
\leanok
Under the same prescribed-family and genuine generator-power hypotheses
$G_a\in N^{w_a}$, let $f_a\in B[z]$,
$B=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$, be the actual
normal homogeneous polynomial of degree $w_a$ of the same $G_a$.
Suppose $D\ge w_a$ for every label, and let literal polynomials
$p_a\in B[z]$ be homogeneous of degree $D-w_a$ with the whole
polynomial identity $\sum_ap_af_a=0$. There are actual analytic
$C_a$ such that
\[
 \sum_aG_aC_a=0,\qquad
 \operatorname{ev}(p_a)-C_a\in N^{D+1-w_a}.
\]
The next-order analytic relation error is derived from the displayed
whole polynomial relation, rather than supplied as a premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual Taylor normal-power coefficient criterion gives
$G_a-\operatorname{ev}(f_a)\in N^{w_a+1}$: all its coefficients of
normal degree below $w_a$ vanish by the genuine generator-power
hypothesis, and its degree $w_a$ coefficients cancel the actual
homogeneous component. The literal homogeneous polynomial $p_a$ has no
coefficient of normal degree below $D-w_a$, so actual normal polynomial
evaluation puts it in $N^{D-w_a}$. Hence
$\operatorname{ev}(p_a)(G_a-\operatorname{ev}(f_a))\in N^{D+1}$,
using $w_a\le D$ to identify the sum of these two exponents.
The whole polynomial relation, evaluated by the genuine ring map,
makes $\sum_a\operatorname{ev}(p_a)\operatorname{ev}(f_a)=0$.
Consequently the actual finite sum
$\sum_aG_a\operatorname{ev}(p_a)$ lies in $N^{D+1}$. Apply the
preceding analytic syzygy correction to this actual sum and that order.
It gives exactly the claimed same-family relation and shifted powers.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Original Hilbert--Samuel constant regular-centre geometry supplies the prescribed normal-filtered analytic family]
\label{hir:actual-original-hs-centre-normal-filtered-owner-division}
\uses{hir:actual-original-hs-centre-canonical-coefficient-data,hir:actual-prescribed-canonical-normal-filtered-division}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalFilteredOwnerDivision.exists_original_hs_centre_normal_filtered_division}
\leanok
An original coherent analytic ideal and a regular centre on which all
original Hilbert--Samuel values are constant supply one actual flattening
and one canonical analytic coefficient datum. For the literal original
ideal in that same flattening, its unchanged canonical generators satisfy
all the normal-filtered analytic division conclusions above, before
quantifying over every numerator and every normal order. No incoming
flattening, canonical family, normal-flatness or quotient bound is supplied.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual original regular-centre canonical coefficient producer
once. It fixes one flattening, the same original translated ideal and
one actual coefficient datum whose generator Taylor images are exactly
the canonical reduced series and whose original analytic generators
belong to their whole normal powers. Retain all these same witnesses.
For each numerator and order apply the second theorem to these exact
generators, their actual Taylor identities and the internally produced
original normal-power memberships. This constructs the asserted actual
analytic quotients and remainder without making any new chart or
family choice. The flattening and coefficient datum were fixed before
all the numerators and orders.
\end{proof}
