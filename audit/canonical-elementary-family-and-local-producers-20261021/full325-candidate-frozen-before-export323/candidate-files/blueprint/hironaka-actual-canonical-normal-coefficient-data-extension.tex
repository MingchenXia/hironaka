\begin{theorem}[A pure normal reduced vertex has genuine constant coefficients on the whole diagram]
\label{hir:actual-pure-normal-reduced-diagram-coefficients}

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCanonicalNormalCoefficientData.normalCoefficient_reduced_vertex_of_pure_normal}

$F_a$ its unchanged canonical reduced vertex series. Whenever the pure normal

coefficient series is the constant
\[

\]

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

asserted constant base series. Equality at every unchanged base exponent
proves the entire relative coefficient identity.
\end{proof}
