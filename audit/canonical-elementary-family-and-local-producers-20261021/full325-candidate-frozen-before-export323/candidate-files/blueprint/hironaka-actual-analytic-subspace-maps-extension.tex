\section{Actual presentation maps of reduced analytic subspace models}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-analytic-subspace-maps}

The locally ringed models in this section are the actual vanishing-quotient
sheaves on the genuine topological subspaces constructed previously. No
structure-sheaf isomorphism is part of the input. Let $f:M\to N$ be an actual
holomorphic map of complex manifolds, with $f(C)\subset D$.

\begin{theorem}[The actual subspace inclusion is a monomorphism]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-inclusion-mono}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-inclusion-stalk}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceMaps.actualSubspaceInclusion_mono}
\leanok
The constructed inclusion of each actual subspace model into its original
ambient manifold is a monomorphism of locally ringed spaces.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Its underlying map is the injective actual subtype inclusion, and every
stalk map is the surjective original quotient homomorphism, hence an
epimorphism of rings. Suppose two locally ringed morphisms become equal
after composing with this inclusion. Underlying injectivity makes their
continuous maps equal. At every source point, the two stalk maps agree
after precomposition by the inclusion stalk epimorphism, so they agree.
A morphism of ring sheaves is determined by all of its stalk maps, hence
the two locally ringed morphisms coincide. This proves the monomorphism
assertion without a closedness or smoothness assumption on the subspace.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual holomorphic maps descend to the whole quotient structure sheaves]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-holomorphic-map}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-structure-sheaf,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-reduced-stalks,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-inclusion-stalk}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceMaps.actualSubspaceMap,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceMaps.actualSubspaceMap_inclusion,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceMaps.actualSubspaceMap_stalk}
\leanok
The actual restriction $g:C\to D$, $g(x)=f(x)$, underlies an internally
constructed morphism of the actual locally ringed subspace models. Its
composition with the target inclusion equals the source inclusion followed
by the actual ambient holomorphic morphism. On whole original stalk
numerators its pullback is exactly
\[
 [r]\longmapsto[f^*r],
\]
with each class taken modulo the entire original vanishing ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On an original target open $U$, compose each genuine holomorphic section
with $f$ on $f^{-1}U$. A section vanishing on $D\cap U$ pulls back to one
vanishing on $C\cap f^{-1}U$, since $f(C)\subset D$. The actual section
vanishing criterion therefore proves that this ring homomorphism sends
the entire target section ideal into the entire source section ideal.
It descends to the full quotient rings. Composition of actual section
restrictions commutes with pullback, so these maps form a genuine
quotient-presheaf morphism. The sheafification universal property extends
it to the actual ambient quotient sheaves, retaining every original
quotient numerator.

Let $i_C,i_D$ be the subtype inclusions. Compose this quotient-sheaf map
with the original inverse-image adjunction unit for $i_C$ and its actual
sheafification unit. The equality $f i_C=i_D g$ identifies the resulting
map with a map from the target ambient quotient sheaf to $i_{D*}g_*\mathcal
O_C$. The genuine inverse-image adjunction for $i_D$, followed by its
sheafification universal property, gives $\mathcal O_D\to g_*\mathcal O_C$.
The two universal-property unit equations show that its composite with
the target inclusion map is exactly the original ambient pullback
followed by the source inclusion map. This proves the claimed equality
of actual ringed morphisms, rather than assuming it.

Apply the stalk functor to this equality. The two inclusion stalk maps
are the actual whole quotient maps, giving the displayed numerator
formula. To prove the descended stalk map is local, lift any of its
input elements through the surjective target inclusion stalk map. If its
image is a unit, the composite ambient/source-inclusion stalk map is a
unit on that lift. Both actual constituent stalk maps are local, so the
lift is a unit. Its quotient class is therefore a unit. Thus every
constructed stalk map is local, giving the genuine locally ringed morphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Identity and composition retain the actual subspace models]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-map-functor-laws}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-inclusion-mono,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-holomorphic-map}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceMaps.actualSubspaceMap_id,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceMaps.actualSubspaceMap_comp}
\leanok
The internally constructed subspace maps preserve actual identities and
composition of original holomorphic maps carrying the specified subsets.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose the proposed identity equality with the actual target inclusion.
Both sides equal that inclusion, by the preceding commutative square
and the actual ambient identity law. Cancel the inclusion monomorphism.
For two composable original holomorphic maps, compose the proposed
composition equality with the final target inclusion. The two proved
commutative squares make both sides the source inclusion followed by the
same composite ambient holomorphic map. The ambient composition law
identifies these maps exactly. Cancel the final inclusion monomorphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual ambient biholomorphisms induce actual subspace isomorphisms]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-biholomorphic-comparison}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-map-functor-laws,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-holomorphic-map}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceMaps.actualSubspaceBiholomorphicIso}
\leanok
Let $e:M\simeq N$ be an actual ambient biholomorphism satisfying
$x\in C$ if and only if $e(x)\in D$. Then it internally constructs an
actual isomorphism of the two reduced locally ringed subspace models.
Its underlying map is the literal restriction of $e$, its inverse is the
literal restriction of $e^{-1}$, and its forward and inverse morphisms
commute with the actual ambient inclusions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The subset equivalence implies that $e$ carries $C$ into $D$. Applying
it to $e^{-1}(y)$ proves that $e^{-1}$ carries $D$ into $C$. Both maps are
actual holomorphic maps, so the preceding construction gives two genuine
locally ringed subspace morphisms, each with its actual inclusion square.
Their two composites are the constructed restrictions of $e^{-1}e$ and
$ee^{-1}$ by the proved composition law. The genuine inverse identities
and the actual identity law make both composites identity morphisms.
These morphisms therefore form the asserted locally ringed isomorphism.
No isomorphism of quotient sheaves or resolution data was supplied.
\end{proof}

This comparison is induced by an actual ambient biholomorphism. Comparison
between unrelated local embeddings of the same abstract analytic space,
effective atlas gluing and resolution descent remain further obligations.
