\begin{theorem}[The actual finite-family FIRST defect has dense complement and no carrier-open piece]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-family-first-defect-thin-dense}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-finite-relative-product-geometry-order-sum,hir:sharp-actual-chronological-distinct-defect-objects,bcalc:nonmonomial-order-local}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstDefectDimensionDrop.actual_family_first_thin_dense}
\leanok
For any genuine finite coherent nonzero relatively principal ideal family on
an actual closed smooth carrier N, the whole literal FIRST defect is contained
in the genuine full product zero locus. Its complement is dense in N, and
EVERY ambient open whose carrier intersection lies in that defect has empty
carrier intersection. No desired dense/thin/branch certificate is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Genuine finite-product geometry supplies actual coherence and nonzero of the
SAME literal product. At a defect point, its true product order strictly
exceeds a nonnegative actual quotient count, hence is at least one. The real
ordinary-order/cosupport and mark-one dictionaries put the point in the actual
product zero locus. At any carrier point, actual nonzero of the product gives
a genuine ideal member with nonzero values frequently on the carrier.
Coherence propagates membership of THIS actual germ to a genuine neighborhood.
Every such frequent nonzero point therefore lies outside the product zero locus,
so it lies outside the defect. This proves the whole relative complement density.
If an actual open carrier piece lay in the defect, those same propagated member
values would vanish throughout that piece, contradicting their genuine frequent
nonzero values. Hence the actual open carrier piece is empty. No equality of
proper quotient classes with original positions is used.
\end{proof}
