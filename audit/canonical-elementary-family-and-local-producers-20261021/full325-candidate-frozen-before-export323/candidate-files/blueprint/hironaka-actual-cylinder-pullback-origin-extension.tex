\section{Actual singular product pullback in the original functional ideal language}

The product germ-ideal equality is now transported to the independently
defined original functional pullback. Every span below consists of finite
holomorphic combinations, even when the generating function family is
infinite. No coherence of that family or of its vanishing set is assumed.

\begin{lemma}[Actual functional spans equal the entire literal germ span]
\label{hir:actual-functional-span-origin-germ-span}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCylinderPullbackOrigin.actualFunctionGermSpan,Hironaka.HironakaActualCylinderPullbackOrigin.isInSpanAt_zero_iff_mem_actual_germ_span}
\leanok
Let $\mathcal S$ be any family of functions on $\mathbb C^n$, every member
holomorphic near zero, and let $F$ be holomorphic near zero. Then $F$ is
an actual finite holomorphic combination of functions in $\mathcal S$
near zero if and only if its genuine holomorphic germ belongs to the
entire ideal generated by the literal germs of those same functions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An actual finite functional relation $F=\sum a_i s_i$ becomes the exact
finite relation $[F]=\sum[a_i][s_i]$ in the holomorphic germ ring, proving
one implication. For the reverse implication, use ideal-span induction.
Every generator has its original function representative and its
one-term functional span relation. Zero has the empty relation. The sum
of two represented germs has the sum of the original representatives;
concatenating their finite relations gives its actual span relation.
For multiplication by an arbitrary holomorphic germ, choose an actual
holomorphic representative of that multiplier and multiply the finite
relation by it. Thus each element of the whole ideal has some genuine
holomorphic representative in the original functional span. Equality
of its germ with $[F]$ gives equality on a genuine neighbourhood.
Functional span membership is invariant under this local equality,
so the same original $F$ belongs to that span. No finite generating
family or coherence certificate has been supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The original composed-function family has exactly the actual whole ideal extension]
\label{hir:actual-cylinder-pullback-functional-family-span}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCylinderPullbackOrigin.actualPullbackFunctionFamily,Hironaka.HironakaActualCylinderPullbackOrigin.actual_pullback_function_germ_span_eq}
\uses{hir:actual-cylinder-base-extension-trace}
\leanok
Let $W\subset\mathbb C^k$, and let $p:\mathbb C^{k+t}\to\mathbb C^k$
be the actual projection. Consider the literal functions $a\circ p$,
where $a$ is an original holomorphic function germ vanishing near zero
on $W$. The ideal generated by all their actual germs is exactly the
extension of the independently defined entire original ideal $I_W$
under the actual holomorphic germ map $p^*$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each original function $a$ in this family represents a member of $I_W$.
The true composition formula for the actual germ map identifies
$[a\circ p]$ with $p^*[a]$, so every functional-family generator lies
in the actual extended ideal. Conversely, every member of $I_W$ has
an original holomorphic representative $a$ vanishing near zero on $W$,
by the actual whole vanishing-ideal definition. Its image under $p^*$
is exactly $[a\circ p]$, one of the displayed actual generators.
The ideal image is generated by these images, so the two whole ideals
are equal. All composed functions are holomorphic near zero because
the original projection is holomorphic and fixes zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original singular product vanishing ideal is the genuine functional pullback at the origin]
\label{hir:actual-cylinder-functional-pullback-origin}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCylinderPullbackOrigin.pullback_vanishingIdeal_mem_zero_iff}
\uses{hir:actual-functional-span-origin-germ-span,hir:actual-cylinder-pullback-functional-family-span,hir:actual-cylinder-whole-vanishing-ideal-extension}
\leanok
For every original subset $W\subset\mathbb C^k$ and every original
function $F$ on $\mathbb C^{k+t}$, membership of $F$ at zero in the
actual functional ideal pullback $p^*\mathcal I_W$ is equivalent to
membership at zero in the actual vanishing ideal of the literal product
$W\times\mathbb C^t$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By its independent original definition, functional pullback membership
is actual finite holomorphic span membership in precisely the family
of original functions $a\circ p$ from the preceding lemma. It implies
holomorphy of $F$ near zero. The first lemma identifies this finite
functional relation with membership of $[F]$ in the entire germ span
of that family. The second lemma identifies that whole span with the
actual extension $p^*I_W$. The already proved Noetherian and relative
Hadamard argument identifies this extension with the entire product
vanishing-germ ideal. Membership in that independently defined ideal
is exactly local pointwise vanishing of the same holomorphic
representative $F$ on the literal product. This is the original
functional vanishing-ideal condition. Reading these equivalences in
reverse proves the other implication. No desired pullback equality,
coherent-ideal certificate, or chosen function representation is input.
\end{proof}

This is the original functional membership dictionary at the actual origin
of a literal product chart. Its translation to every point and then to
arbitrary genuine submersion coordinates remains a distinct local-geometry
transport. No resolution termination is asserted.
