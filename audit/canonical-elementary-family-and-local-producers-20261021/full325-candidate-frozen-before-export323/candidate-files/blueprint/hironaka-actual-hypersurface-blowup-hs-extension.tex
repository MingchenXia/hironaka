\section{Actual all-order HS nonincrease for genuine maximal-order hypersurface blowups}

The HS here is the existing intrinsic dimension of the entire original
local quotient. Original admissibility and genuine blowup geometry supply
the comparison; no HS or transformed-ideal equality is an input. This
chapter covers the hypersurface case, including its singular exceptional
points. General nonprincipal Bennett comparison remains a separate problem.

\begin{lemma}[The computed principal jet dimension increases with actual order]
\label{hir:actual-principal-binomial-hs-monotone-order}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHypersurfaceBlowupHS.actual_principal_binomial_hs_mono}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-principal-entire-hilbert-samuel-formula}
For $\mu\le\nu$ and $n,\ell\ge0$,
\[
 \binom{n+\ell}{n}-\mathbf1_{\mu\le\ell}\binom{n+\ell-\mu}{n}
 \ \le\ 
 \binom{n+\ell}{n}-\mathbf1_{\nu\le\ell}\binom{n+\ell-\nu}{n}.
\]
Each subtracted term is understood to be zero when its indicated condition
fails, as in the genuine principal quotient formula.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $\nu\le\ell$, then also $\mu\le\ell$ and
$n+\ell-\nu\le n+\ell-\mu$. Monotonicity of binomial coefficients in
the upper index gives that the term subtracted on the right is at most
the one subtracted on the left; natural subtraction reverses this
inequality. If $\ell<\nu$, the right side is the full ambient jet
dimension, which bounds either the ambient dimension or the corresponding
natural subtraction on the left. These cases include $n=0$ and zero orders.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original invertible-ideal order comparison gives every actual intrinsic HS comparison]
\label{hir:actual-invertible-order-comparison-entire-hs}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHypersurfaceBlowupHS.actual_invertible_intrinsic_hs_le_of_original_order_le}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-invertible-original-order-entire-intrinsic-hs,hir:actual-principal-binomial-hs-monotone-order}
Let $A,B$ be original invertible ideals on complex manifolds of the same
dimension $n$, at actual points $y,x$. If their EXISTING original ambient
orders satisfy $\operatorname{ord}_y A\le\operatorname{ord}_x B$, then
$H_A(y,\ell)\le H_B(x,\ell)$ for every $\ell\ge0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each original invertible ideal the preceding intrinsic producer chooses
internally one finite integer order, identifies it with the EXISTING
original order, and computes all of the actual intrinsic HS values from
that same integer. Substitute these two genuine order identities in the
assumed original order comparison. Their finite integer orders are
therefore ordered, so the preceding binomial inequality compares the two
computed EXISTING profiles at every order. No principal generator, whole
stalk equality or HS formula is required from the caller.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every point of a genuine maximal-order hypersurface blowup has nonincreasing intrinsic HS]
\label{hir:actual-hypersurface-blowup-all-order-hs-nonincreasing}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHypersurfaceBlowupHS.actual_hypersurface_blowup_intrinsic_hs_nonincreasing}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-invertible-order-comparison-entire-hs,hir:actual-blowup-off-centre-intrinsic-reduced-hs,hir:actual-max-order-hypersurface-strict-ideal,hir:maximum-phase-entire-current-ideal}
Let $W$ be an original analytic hypersurface in a complex manifold of
dimension $m+1$, with original invertible vanishing ideal, dense regular
$m$-dimensional locus, and original SNC boundary $E$. Suppose its original
order on $W$ is bounded by $\mu\ge2$. For ANY original admissible centre
of $(X,E,\mathcal I_W,\mu)$ and ANY genuine blowup $\pi:Y\to X$ at that
centre, the actual reduced strict transform $W'$ satisfies
\[
 H_{W'}(y,\ell)\le H_W(\pi(y),\ell)
 \qquad (y\in Y,\ \ell\ge0).
\]
In particular the statement includes all singular exceptional strict points.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original coherence and admissibility give the actual centre-power inclusion
by the genuine Hadamard theorem. The original global order bound gives the
actual maximal-order derivative-unit condition. The genuine controlled
transform preserves that condition, hence its actual original order is
bounded by $\mu$ at every new point. The proved hypersurface strict-ideal
theorem, using only the hypotheses just stated, identifies the ENTIRE
controlled ideal with the ENTIRE reduced strict-transform vanishing ideal;
its genuine invertibility follows from actual invertible ideal division.
If $\pi(y)$ is in the centre, original admissibility gives source order at
least $\mu$, whereas maximal order bounds it above by $\mu$. Thus the
new original order is at most the original source order. Apply the preceding
invertible-ideal intrinsic HS comparison at every order. If $\pi(y)$ is
outside the centre, the independently constructed genuine complement
biholomorphism and actual whole strict-ideal dictionary give equality of
the existing intrinsic HS profiles, hence the required inequality. No
regularity assumption on $y$, no replacement principal ideal and no HS
comparison certificate occurs in either case.
\end{proof}
