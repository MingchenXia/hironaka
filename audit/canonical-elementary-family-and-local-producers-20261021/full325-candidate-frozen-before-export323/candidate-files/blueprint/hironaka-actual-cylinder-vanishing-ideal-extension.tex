\section{The whole original singular product ideal is the actual base extension}

For an arbitrary subset $W\subset\mathbb C^k$, let $I_W$ be its entire
holomorphic vanishing-germ ideal at zero. Put $S=W\times\mathbb C^t$,
$J=I_S$, and let $p:\mathbb C^{k+t}\to\mathbb C^k$ and
$s:\mathbb C^k\to\mathbb C^{k+t}$ be the literal projection and zero
section. All ideal extensions below use the actual holomorphic germ map
$p^*$. No smoothness, analytic presentation, coherent-ideal certificate,
or desired equality is assumed for $W$ or $S$.

\begin{lemma}[The actual product projection and zero section retain the whole vanishing ideals]
\label{hir:actual-cylinder-base-extension-trace}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCylinderVanishingIdealExtension.map_base_vanishingIdeal_le_cylinder,Hironaka.HironakaActualCylinderVanishingIdealExtension.trace_cylinder_vanishingIdeal_le_base}
\uses{hir:actual-singular-cylinder-relative-difference}
\leanok
The actual whole germ maps satisfy $p^*I_W\subset J$ and $s^*J\subset I_W$.
The identity $s\circ p(z,v)=(z,0)$ is literal on the whole coordinate model.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A representative vanishing on $W$ near zero pulls back to a holomorphic
representative vanishing on the entire product near zero, because $p$
is continuous and sends zero to zero. Thus every actual extended ideal
generator belongs to $J$, which proves the first inclusion by ideal
closure. Conversely, $s$ is continuous, fixes zero and sends every
point of $W$ into $S$. Composing a representative vanishing on $S$ near
zero with $s$ therefore gives a representative in $I_W$. The asserted
coordinate retraction identity follows by reading the first $k$ and
last $t$ coordinates separately.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The entire original cylinder ideal is generated modulo its own fibre multiples]
\label{hir:actual-cylinder-extension-mod-normal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCylinderVanishingIdealExtension.cylinder_vanishingIdeal_le_extension_sup_normal_mul}
\uses{hir:actual-cylinder-base-extension-trace,hir:actual-singular-cylinder-relative-difference}
\leanok
Let $N$ be the ideal generated by all actual fibre-coordinate germs.
Then
\[
 J\subset p^*I_W+NJ.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take any actual germ $F\in J$ and choose a holomorphic representative.
Its restriction $F(z,0)$ is in $I_W$ by the genuine zero-section
statement above. Pulling that restriction back puts the germ
$F\circ s\circ p$ in $p^*I_W$. Actual relative Hadamard division gives
\[
 F-F\circ s\circ p=\sum_{i\ge k}z_iH_i
\]
with every $H_i$ in the same whole original ideal $J$. Reading this
actual local equation in the holomorphic germ ring gives a finite
sum in $NJ$: each displayed coordinate belongs to $N$, and each
coefficient belongs to $J$. The coefficients below $k$ are zero.
Adding the two parts gives the claimed inclusion for every original
member of the entire ideal, not only a finite jet or initial ideal.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual whole singular cylinder vanishing ideal equals the actual original base extension]
\label{hir:actual-cylinder-whole-vanishing-ideal-extension}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCylinderVanishingIdealExtension.cylinder_vanishingIdeal_eq_base_extension}
\uses{hir:actual-cylinder-extension-mod-normal,lag:germ-noetherian}
\leanok
For every actual subset $W\subset\mathbb C^k$ and every $t$, the entire
original product vanishing-germ ideal satisfies
\[
 I_{W\times\mathbb C^t}=p^*I_W
\]
in the genuine holomorphic germ ring at zero of $\mathbb C^{k+t}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine holomorphic germ ring is Noetherian, so its original ideal
$J$ is a finite module. Every actual fibre-coordinate germ has value
zero at the origin and therefore lies in the actual maximal ideal.
This local ring's maximal ideal is its Jacobson radical, so
$N\subset\operatorname{Jac}(0)$. Apply the finite-module form of
Nakayama's lemma to the actual inclusion
$J\subset p^*I_W+NJ$ just proved. It gives $J\subset p^*I_W$.
The reverse inclusion is the actual projection inclusion from the first
lemma. Their conjunction proves equality of the entire original ideals.
Neither finite generation nor the desired equality was assumed: finite
generation follows from the independently proved Noetherian germ ring,
and the crucial self-multiple inclusion came from actual quotients
inside $J$.
\end{proof}

This is an equality of the independently defined entire holomorphic germ
ideals. Its transport to genuine sheaf pullback under an arbitrary
submersion is a separate local-coordinate dictionary; it is not asserted
without that transport. No resolution driver or termination follows here.
