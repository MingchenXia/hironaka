\section{The actual intrinsic regular locus is open and dense}

For an actual locally ringed space $A$ over our actual complex point, call
$x$ intrinsically regular of dimension $d$ if an actual native holomorphic
manifold on a coordinate open in $\mathbf C^d$ admits a genuine open
immersion into $A$, over that same complex point, whose actual image
contains $x$. This definition contains only the usual local manifold
structure; it includes no resolution tower or desired regularity equality.
Write $A_{\mathrm{reg}}$ for the union over all $d$ of these actual loci.

\begin{theorem}[The intrinsic predicate is the original embedded regularity]
\label{hir:intrinsic-complex-analytic-regularity-original-dictionary}
\uses{hir:actual-intrinsic-manifold-chart-original-regularity,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-regular-native-manifold-chart}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaIntrinsicComplexAnalyticRegularLocus.intrinsicRegularAtDim,Hironaka.HironakaIntrinsicComplexAnalyticRegularLocus.intrinsicRegularLocus,Hironaka.HironakaIntrinsicComplexAnalyticRegularLocus.intrinsicRegularAtDim_model_iff}
\leanok
For every original subset $C\subset U\subset\mathbf C^n$, every actual
$x\in C$, and every dimension $d$, intrinsic regularity of its constructed
quotient model at $x$ is equivalent to the existing original
$\operatorname{RegularAtDim}(C,x,d)$. No analyticity or coherence is needed
for this local equivalence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An intrinsic regularity witness is an actual over-point native manifold
open chart. Choose its actual source point over $x$ and apply the genuine
native-chart-to-original-regularity theorem. Its conclusion concerns the
same original subset $C$; the actual equality of image points identifies
it with $x$. Conversely original regularity includes the genuine inequality
$d\le n$. Write $n=d+t$ and use the original-regularity-to-native-chart
construction. It internally constructs a genuine native manifold open chart,
its structural triangle, and an actual preimage of $x$. These are precisely
the intrinsic predicate witnesses. Neither implication assumes the other
regularity assertion as a certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual intrinsic regular locus is open]
\label{hir:intrinsic-complex-analytic-regular-locus-open}
\uses{hir:intrinsic-complex-analytic-regularity-original-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaIntrinsicComplexAnalyticRegularLocus.isOpen_intrinsicRegularAtDim,Hironaka.HironakaIntrinsicComplexAnalyticRegularLocus.isOpen_intrinsicRegularLocus,Hironaka.HironakaIntrinsicComplexAnalyticRegularLocus.intrinsicSingularLocus,Hironaka.HironakaIntrinsicComplexAnalyticRegularLocus.isClosed_intrinsicSingularLocus}
\leanok
On every actual locally ringed space over the complex point, the intrinsic
regular locus of each fixed dimension is open. Its union
$A_{\mathrm{reg}}$ is therefore open. In particular its complement is
an actual closed singular locus on every constructed abstract reduced
complex analytic space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose an intrinsic chart witnessing regularity at a point. Its actual
base map is an open embedding, so its whole image is an open neighbourhood
of that point. The very same chart witnesses regularity at every point
of this whole image: simply retain each actual source preimage. Thus every
point of the fixed-dimension locus has an actual open neighbourhood
contained in it. This proves openness. For the union over dimensions,
retain the dimension of a witness at the given point and use its same
open neighbourhood. The complement of an open set is closed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual abstract analytic space has a dense intrinsic regular locus]
\label{hir:intrinsic-complex-analytic-regular-locus-dense}
\uses{hir:intrinsic-complex-analytic-regularity-original-dictionary,hir:intrinsic-complex-analytic-regular-locus-open,hir:reduced-analytic-space-category,lag:regular-locus-dense}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaIntrinsicComplexAnalyticRegularLocus.exists_intrinsicRegularAtDim_mem_nhds,Hironaka.HironakaIntrinsicComplexAnalyticRegularLocus.dense_intrinsicRegularLocus}
\leanok
For every actual object $A$ of the constructed reduced complex analytic
category, every actual neighbourhood of every point contains an intrinsic
regular point of some dimension. Consequently $A_{\mathrm{reg}}$ is dense.
The actual analytic atlas is the ordinary input; no local or global
resolution data are supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual analytic model chart covering the given point and a true
source point over it. Continuity pulls the given neighbourhood back to
a genuine neighbourhood of this source point in the actual analytic subset.
Choose an actual open subset of that neighbourhood. Subtype topology
writes it as the inverse image of a true open in its original ambient
coordinate manifold. Original density of regular points for the genuine
analytic subset supplies an original regular point in this ambient open.
The first theorem gives a genuine over-point native manifold chart there.
Compose that actual open immersion with the chosen analytic model chart.
Composition of open immersions is an open immersion, and the two genuine
structural triangles compose over the same complex point. The chosen
actual source preimage proves that the resulting native manifold chart
covers the actual image point. The original neighbourhood pullback puts
this image point in the initially prescribed neighbourhood. Thus every
neighbourhood meets $A_{\mathrm{reg}}$, exactly its density.
\end{proof}

This identifies the actual regular and singular loci needed for a future
intrinsic resolution contract. Constructing resolution towers and proving
their descent and termination remain distinct geometric tasks.
