\begin{theorem}[Every whole original trace germ relation and actual DISTINCT count restrict both ways]
\label{hir:sharp-original-first-trace-quotient-open-dictionary}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-trace-ideal-open-restriction-relation,hir:sharp-original-member-order-proper-incidence-all-points,hir:open-restriction-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalFirstDefectOpenNaturality.original_traceGerm_rel_open_iff,Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalFirstDefectOpenNaturality.original_distinct_count_open}
\leanok
For any actual original open U and point x in its carrier, retain every
original list position through its literal length equality. Two restricted
member trace germ ideals are equal if and only if their SAME original trace
germ ideals are equal at the original point. The actual number of DISTINCT
proper trace classes is therefore exactly the original count. No injectivity
of the quotient on incident positions or good-NC condition is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original SNC gives coherent actual member ideals. The genuine restricted-span
open dictionary identifies every holomorphic germ on U with its extension
near the original point. This gives the forward transport. Conversely restrict
an arbitrary original holomorphic germ to U and apply the actual restricted
relation; extension of this restriction agrees with the original germ on the
whole open neighbourhood of the point. Restricted spans are unchanged by
that genuine germ equality, giving the reverse implication.

The true finite position equivalence is the literal list-length cast. The
relation equivalence gives a genuine equivalence of the two quotient spaces,
not an identification of quotient and index cardinalities. Actual proper
positions equal the incident positions for closed original members, on both
manifolds. Exact member preimage therefore carries the finite proper set to
the actual restricted proper set. The quotient equivalence takes the whole
finite image of proper classes bijectively to its restricted counterpart.
Its genuine finite cardinality gives equality of the two DISTINCT counts,
including the empty list and every empty original member position.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The literal original FIRST-Z is the entire preimage on every actual open]
\label{hir:sharp-original-first-distinct-defect-open-naturality}

\begin{proof}

inequality defining FIRST-Z is identical on both manifolds. Away from the
carrier both literal sets are empty. No quotient collision is erased.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The true restricted maximum matches exactly on active opens and drops on silent opens]
\label{hir:sharp-original-first-restricted-maximum-open-dichotomy}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-first-distinct-defect-open-naturality,hir:sharp-original-first-distinct-defect-restricted-maximum-centre,hir:invariant-order}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalFirstDefectOpenNaturality.original_first_maximum_open_of_meeting,Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalFirstDefectOpenNaturality.original_first_value_lt_of_maximum_trace_empty}
\leanok

at most the ambient dimension n, consider the
actual original full product invariant at the reflexive history and its
maximum on the literal FIRST-Z. On an actual open meeting this true maximum,
the whole maximum of the independently restricted original invariant on its

misses it, its centre trace is empty and every actual local FIRST-Z value is
strictly below any actual attained original maximum value. No equality of
global and local maxima is asserted for such a silent open.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original positive pure dimension and genuine product geometry supply the
actual original invariant domain. The reflexive tower is genuinely canonical,
so its proved open-restriction law gives equality of the whole original and
restricted full invariant at each actual FIRST-Z point. The preceding literal
FIRST-Z dictionary gives precisely the preimage domain.

If the true global maximum has a point in U, its actual preimage witnesses
attainment there. A local maximum dominates that true witness, while the
original maximum dominates every local value; their equality identifies both
whole maximum loci in both directions. If U misses the true original maximum,
the literal centre preimage is empty. Every local value is at most the attained
original maximum by its genuine maximality; equality would put that local
point in the omitted actual maximum. Linear order therefore makes it strictly
smaller. This is the real active/silent obstruction before any attempted
history synchronization. Full modified-history decrease and termination,
recursive intersection phases and general locally finite scheduling remain
separate unmet producers.
\end{proof}
