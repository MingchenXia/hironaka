\section{Original order-zero HS constancy forces genuine centre vanishing}

\begin{theorem}[The unchanged order-zero HS value detects properness of the original germ ideal]
\label{hir:actual-original-hs-zero-positive-iff-proper}
\uses{hir:actual-point-quotient-finite-dimensional}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSZeroCentreVanishing.hilbertSamuelValue_zero_pos_iff_ne_top}
\leanok
For every ideal $I$ of the original ambient holomorphic germ ring,
its unchanged original Hilbert--Samuel value at order zero is positive
if and only if $I$ is proper.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $I$ is the unit ideal, its original quotient by $I+\mathfrak m$ is
zero, so its complex dimension and order-zero HS value are zero.
If $I$ is proper, the original local-ring property places $I$ inside
its original maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$. Hence $I+\mathfrak m$ is
exactly $\mathfrak m$. The original residue quotient is nontrivial
because the original maximal ideal is proper. It is finite-dimensional
by the genuine point-power quotient theorem at power one. A nontrivial
finite-dimensional complex vector space has positive dimension. This
is exactly the original order-zero HS value by its unchanged definition.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Proper original family ideals and original HS constancy force the actual generators to vanish on the tangent centre]
\label{hir:actual-original-hs-zero-constant-centre-generators-vanish}
\uses{hir:actual-original-hs-zero-positive-iff-proper,hir:actual-holomorphic-family-whole-fibre-macaulay-dictionary,hir:relative-normal-entire-germ-retraction}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSZeroCentreVanishing.actual_family_holomorphicAt,Hironaka.HironakaActualHSZeroCentreVanishing.actual_hs_zero_constant_tangent_generators_vanish}
\leanok
Fix one original holomorphic family $G_i$ on a genuine open
$U\subset\mathbb C^{d+t}$ containing zero. Use the unchanged original
translated ideals generated by its actual germs. Suppose its ideal at
zero is proper, and its unchanged original order-zero HS value at
every actual tangent point $Tc\in U$ equals the value at zero.
Then every actual generator satisfies $G_i(Tc)=0$ at every such point.
Only ordinary order-zero HS constancy is used; vanishing and normal
flatness are not hypotheses.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At every actual point of $U$, openness and actual holomorphicity on
$U$ give a genuine neighborhood on which each original $G_i$ is
complex differentiable. These are the actual holomorphic germs used
by the unchanged original translated-ideal construction; no global
extension or new generator family is chosen.

Properness of the original ideal at zero and the preceding theorem
make its order-zero value positive. The assumed equality of that same
original value at $Tc$ therefore makes the unchanged translated ideal
there proper as well. The original local-ring property places this
whole ideal inside the same original maximal ideal of the ambient
germ ring. Each original translated generator belongs to that entire
ideal by the original span definition, hence to this maximal ideal.
The original holomorphic-germ maximal-ideal dictionary says precisely
that its actual value at the origin is zero. Evaluating its literal
representative $z\mapsto G_i(z+Tc)$ at zero gives $G_i(Tc)=0$.
This works for every index and actual tangent point in the same real
open domain. Zero lies in this parameter domain because $T0=0\in U$.
\end{proof}

This is a genuine consequence of the original proper-ideal and ordinary
HS-constant-centre hypotheses. It supplies actual generator vanishing
needed for the highest-word boundary terms in adjacent jet orders.
It does not assert full adjoint prolongation, compatible vertex-family
selection, normal flatness or the general Bennett theorem.

\begin{theorem}[The new maximal-length original word columns vanish in the actual next jet space]
\label{hir:actual-new-maximal-word-generator-next-jet-zero}
\uses{hir:actual-original-hs-zero-constant-centre-generators-vanish,hir:whole-point-power-derivative-kernel,hir:actual-finite-linear-jet-kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSZeroCentreVanishing.actual_wordMonomial_mem_maximalIdeal_pow,Hironaka.HironakaActualHSZeroCentreVanishing.actual_new_maxword_generator_finiteJet_zero}
\leanok
Every genuine original coordinate word of length $m$ belongs to the
$m$-th power of the original maximal ideal. In particular, let $w$ be
an unchanged original word in the actual $(q+1)$-jet word index whose
length is exactly $q$, and let the original function $g$ be holomorphic
near an actual point $p$, with $g(p)=0$. Then
\[
 \operatorname{finiteJet}_{q+1}
   (\operatorname{wordMonomial}(w)\,[z\mapsto g(z+p)])=0.
\]
This holds for every $q\ge0$ in the same original jet space, with no
annihilator or prolongation certificate assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each actual coordinate germ evaluates to zero at the origin, hence
belongs to the original maximal ideal. The genuine product of $m$
such original germs therefore belongs to its $m$-th power, by the
finite ideal-product membership rule; the empty product belongs to
the zeroth power, the whole ring. The original translated germ of
$g$ evaluates to $g(p)=0$, so it belongs to the same original maximal
ideal. Multiplying this membership by the word's membership in
$\mathfrak m^q$ gives membership in
$\mathfrak m^q\mathfrak m=\mathfrak m^{q+1}$. The unchanged actual
finite-jet kernel is precisely this entire original maximal-ideal
power, so its actual $(q+1)$-jet is zero. When $q=0$, this is exactly
the order-zero vanishing of the original translated generator, with
the genuine empty word equal to one.
\end{proof}
