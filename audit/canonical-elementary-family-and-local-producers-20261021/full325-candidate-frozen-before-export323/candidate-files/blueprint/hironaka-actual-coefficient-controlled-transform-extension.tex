\section{Actual coefficient extraction commutes with controlled base-chart normalization}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-coefficient-controlled-transform}
Use genuine two-block coordinates $(u,z)\in\mathbf C^d\times\mathbf C^t$ and a centre generated by the selected base coordinates indexed by $A$ together with every normal coordinate. Fix an exceptional pivot $e\in A$. The actual chart directions in the normal block are literally zero; all base directions remain arbitrary.

\begin{theorem}[The actual base-pivot chart preserves normal coefficients]
\label{hir:actual-coefficient-controlled-chart-equations}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-polynomial-truncations,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-germ-stability}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientControlledTransform.blowup_tangentPullback,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientControlledTransform.blowup_normalCoordinate,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientControlledTransform.blowup_normal_power}
\leanok
The actual full chart pullback restricts on every entire base germ to the true base-chart pullback. It sends each normal coordinate $z_i$ to $e z_i$ and sends every entire normal power $N^q$ into itself.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Projection to the base block commutes with the two actual polynomial chart maps, coordinate by coordinate. Representing each base germ by a holomorphic function proves the whole pullback identity. Every normal coordinate belongs to the centre, is distinct from the base pivot, and has zero chosen direction, so the defining polynomial chart formula is exactly $ez_i$. Hence the image of the whole normal ideal is contained in that ideal; functoriality and monotonicity of ideal powers give the assertion for every power, including zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Literal coefficient formula in the original analytic germ rings]
\label{hir:actual-coefficient-controlled-pullback-coefficients}
\uses{hir:actual-coefficient-controlled-chart-equations,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-polynomial-truncations,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-germ-stability}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientControlledTransform.coefficient_tangent_mul,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientControlledTransform.coefficient_blowup}
\leanok
For every original analytic germ $g$ and every normal exponent $\gamma$, the entire actual base coefficient satisfies
\[
 c_\gamma(B^*g)=e^{|\gamma|}B_0^*c_\gamma(g).
\]
Also $c_\gamma(a g)=a c_\gamma(g)$ for every entire base germ $a$. These are equalities of analytic base germs, with their entire coefficients.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At any finite normal order $q$, replace $g$ by its genuine original finite normal Taylor polynomial; the remainder belongs to the entire $N^q$. The first theorem keeps that remainder in $N^q$. Pullback of each polynomial monomial $a_\gamma z^\gamma$ is $B_0^*a_\gamma\, e^{|\gamma|}z^\gamma$. Thus its finite polynomial has exactly these coefficients, no inserted high terms, and represents the actual pulled-back numerator modulo $N^q$. Uniqueness of the actual normal-polynomial representative identifies it with the actual Taylor polynomial of $B^*g$. Extracting any prescribed coefficient at $q>|\gamma|$ gives the stated whole analytic equality. The same finite-remainder and uniqueness argument for multiplication by a base germ proves base linearity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual exceptional division gives the exact retained coefficient marks]
\label{hir:actual-coefficient-controlled-quotient-coefficients}
\uses{hir:actual-coefficient-controlled-pullback-coefficients}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientControlledTransform.coefficient_controlled_quotient,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientControlledTransform.coefficient_controlled_quotient_high}
\leanok
For the same actual numerator $g$ and actual analytic quotient $H$ satisfying $e^mH=B^*g$, every $|\gamma|\le m$ obeys
\[
 e^{m-|\gamma|}c_\gamma(H)=B_0^*c_\gamma(g).
\]
For $m\le|\gamma|$ the exact formula is $c_\gamma(H)=e^{|\gamma|-m}B_0^*c_\gamma(g)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply whole actual coefficient extraction to the genuine quotient equation. Base linearity and the preceding actual pullback formula give $e^m c_\gamma(H)=e^{|\gamma|}B_0^*c_\gamma(g)$. In the first case factor $e^m=e^{|\gamma|}e^{m-|\gamma|}$ and cancel the nonzero analytic base germ $e^{|\gamma|}$. In the second case factor the right side and cancel $e^m$. Nonzeroness follows from the genuine base coordinate germ and the original analytic germ domain. The formulas include equal degrees and mark zero; no replacement formal-series coefficients are used.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}
Apply the proved actual analytic exceptional-division theorem to each original numerator and its proved whole centre-power membership. Choose those actual quotients once. The resulting genuine product equations satisfy the hypotheses of the preceding theorem, which produces every coefficient identity for this identical family. \begin{theorem}[The same actual transformed generators supply the whole controlled coefficient ideal]
\label{hir:actual-coefficient-controlled-same-family-ideals}
\uses{hir:actual-coefficient-controlled-family-quotients,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-literal-formal-taylor-dictionary,hir:actual-original-weighted-coefficient-compression}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientControlledTransform.vertex_coefficient_eq_analytic_germ,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientControlledTransform.exists_actual_vertex_controlled_coefficient_ideals}

In three-block coordinates $(u,y;z)$ retain a prescribed actual analytic vertex family $P$ and its actual coefficient functions on its genuine common domain. Suppose the original base centre $y=0$ lies locally in all literal low-coefficient cosupports at their genuine difference marks. For each actual base-pivot chart with literal zero normal direction, the actual quotients $H_a$ of this same $P_a$ satisfy the preceding coefficient equations with the very original function germs $[P_{a,\gamma}]$.
The whole image of the compressed weighted coefficient ideal is $(e^D)$ times the compressed ideal of the literal coefficients $c_\gamma(H_a)$; its colon by $(e^D)$ equals that latter whole ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The entire Taylor-coefficient dictionary and faithful original Taylor embedding identify each original coefficient function germ of $P$ with its actual analytic relative coefficient germ. Original local coefficient cosupport produces the genuine whole centre-power membership of each same $P_a$, hence the preceding internal actual quotient construction gives one $H_a$ and the exact difference-mark equations for all low exponents.
The same original local cosupports also put the finite original coefficient germs in their true base centre powers. Apply the already proved actual compressed-ideal transform theorem to these very germs. Its constructed quotient coefficient at each finite position has the same product equation as the actual extracted coefficient $c_\gamma(H_a)$. The nonzero exceptional power cancels in the genuine analytic base domain, identifying them position by position.
Substitute that actual family equality in the whole compressed-ideal image and colon identities. Thus the transformed coefficients are the literal coefficients of the actual transformed generators, with no independently selected substitute family or desired tracking equation as a hypothesis.
\end{proof}
