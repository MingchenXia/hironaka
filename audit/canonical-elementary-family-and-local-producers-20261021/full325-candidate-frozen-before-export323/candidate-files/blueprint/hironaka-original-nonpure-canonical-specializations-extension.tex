\[

\]

are precisely the three genuine containments
$\operatorname{Exc}(T)\subseteq\bigcup E_f
\subseteq T^{-1}(W_{\rm sing})\subseteq\operatorname{Exc}(T)$.
Antisymmetry closes this actual cycle and proves both equalities.

$\eta^{-1}(\bigcup E_f)$. Substitute the proved target equalities and
the actual projection square. This proves the same two exact source
formulas, without asking for a source support or ideal certificate.
\end{proof}
