\section{The whole geometric strict ideal is the radical exceptional saturation}

\begin{theorem}[The actual differential exceptional set is the coordinate hyperplane]
\label{hir:actual-normal-blowup-exceptional-locus-exact}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-blowup-differential-coordinates,hir:actual-normal-blowup-hyperplane-exceptional}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictRadicalPullback.mfderiv_actual_selected_blowup_bijective_of_coordinate_ne_zero,Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictRadicalPullback.exceptionalLocus_actual_normal_blowup_eq}
\leanok
For an actual normal-coordinate blowup chart with normal dimension
$t\ge2$, pivot $e=d+j$ and any actual chart direction, its entire original
differential exceptional locus is exactly the hyperplane $z_e=0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The already constructed nonzero normal differential kernel gives the
inclusion of that hyperplane in the exceptional locus. At any actual
point with $z_e\ne0$, use the genuine coordinate differential formula.
For an arbitrary output $w$, set $v_e=w_e$, set $v_i=w_i$ at every
unselected coordinate, and at a selected nonpivot coordinate set
$v_i=(w_i-w_e(b_i+z_i))/z_e$. Substitution in every actual differential
row gives $w$, so the differential is surjective. Conversely a zero
output has $v_e=0$ and every unselected $v_i=0$; every other row is then
$z_ev_i=0$, so $v_i=0$. Thus its kernel is zero and it is injective. The
differential is bijective at every such point, proving the reverse
inclusion and the equality of the entire actual sets.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole off-hyperplane zero-locus ideals are radical colons]
\label{hir:actual-off-hyperplane-zero-ideal-radical-colon}
\uses{hir:actual-whole-vanishing-origin-ideal-meaning,hir:actual-local-continuous-vanishing-closure,lag:prime-nullstellensatz}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictRadicalPullback.original_closure_zero_locus_diff_ideal_eq_radical_colon}
\leanok
For any finite family of actual functions $F_i$ holomorphic near the
origin, let $K$ be the whole original germ ideal generated by their germs.
Then the whole original vanishing germ ideal of
$\overline{\{F_i=0\ \forall i\}\setminus\{z_e=0\}}$ equals
\[
 (\sqrt K):(z_e).
\]
No radicality, saturation or desired ideal equality is supplied as input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $h$ have actual holomorphic representative $H$. If it vanishes on
the indicated closure near the origin, the actual product $z_eH$ vanishes
on the entire finite zero locus near the origin. Indeed at $z_e=0$ the
product is zero, and at $z_e\ne0$ its point belongs to the defining subset
of that closure. The genuine analytic Nullstellensatz applied to the
actual equations $F_i$ says the germ $z_eh$ belongs to $\sqrt K$.
Commutativity identifies this with membership in the stated whole colon.
Conversely if $z_eh\in\sqrt K$, some whole power $(z_eh)^N$ belongs
to $K$. Every actual equation generator vanishes on the entire common
zero locus, so this power vanishes there near the origin. At points with
$z_e\ne0$, cancellation of its nonzero coordinate factor implies
$H^N=0$, hence $H=0$. Continuity of the actual holomorphic representative
extends this local vanishing to the whole closure. This proves equality
of the whole ideals, rather than only equality of their zero sets.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual finite equations give the whole radical strict-pullback dictionary]
\label{hir:actual-geometric-strict-whole-radical-pullback}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-blowup-exceptional-locus-exact,hir:actual-off-hyperplane-zero-ideal-radical-colon,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictRadicalPullback.original_strict_zero_locus_ideal_eq_radical_pullback_colon}
\leanok
For any finite actual functions $F_i$ holomorphic near the origin, their
literal common zero set $W$, and an actual normal-coordinate blowup chart
with normal dimension at least two, the whole original vanishing germ
ideal of its genuine geometric strict transform is
\[
 \sqrt{\pi^*((F_i)_i)}:(z_e).
\]
Here $\pi^*((F_i)_i)$ is the entire actual analytic pullback ideal of
the original equation germs, and the geometric strict transform uses the
original differential exceptional locus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The exact differential theorem replaces the original exceptional locus
by the entire coordinate hyperplane. The inverse image of the literal
source common zero set is exactly the common zero set of the actual
composed functions $F_i\circ\pi$, pointwise. The actual polynomial blowup
map is holomorphic and fixes the origin, so each such composition is an
actual holomorphic representative of the whole pullback of its original
germ. Apply the preceding radical-colon equality to these actual composed
functions. Mapping an entire finitely generated ideal gives exactly the
whole ideal generated by the images of the original generators, which
identifies its $K$ with the displayed actual pullback ideal.
\end{proof}

Dropping the radical requires an additional reducedness argument for the
saturated pullback. Identifying a normalized canonical standard family and
proving a reverse Hilbert--Samuel persistence criterion remain separate.

\begin{theorem}[The original analytic subset internally supplies the whole radical pullback ideal]
\label{hir:actual-original-analytic-strict-whole-radical-pullback}
\uses{hir:actual-geometric-strict-whole-radical-pullback,lag:prime-nullstellensatz}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualStrictRadicalPullback.original_analytic_strict_ideal_eq_radical_pullback_colon}
\leanok
Let $W$ be any original complex analytic subset of $\mathbf C^{d+t}$,
and let $I(W)$ be its whole original holomorphic vanishing germ ideal at
the origin. For every actual normal-coordinate blowup chart with $t\ge2$,
\[
 I(W')=\sqrt{\pi^* I(W)}:(z_e),
\]
where $W'$ is its genuine geometric strict transform. The original
analytic subset and actual chart are the only geometric inputs; a finite
presentation, radicality or desired strict-ideal equality is not supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original analytic presentation at the origin, read in the actual
self-model chart, internally supplies finitely many actual holomorphic
functions $F_i$ whose common zero set agrees with $W$ throughout a true
neighbourhood of the origin. The analytic Nullstellensatz and pointwise
vanishing show $I(W)=\sqrt K$, where $K$ is the whole ideal of these
actual equation germs: every member of $I(W)$ belongs to $\sqrt K$,
and every member whose whole power belongs to $K$ vanishes on their common
zero set, hence on $W$, near the origin. The actual chart fixes the origin
and is continuous, so the same local set equality holds after pullback.
Removing the exceptional hyperplane preserves this local equality, and
local vanishing on the closure is equivalent to local vanishing on the
set itself by continuity. Thus the preceding theorem computes the whole
strict ideal using the internally chosen $F_i$. Finally for any ring
map $f$, $\sqrt{f(\sqrt K)}=\sqrt{f(K)}$: the inclusion from right to
left follows from $K\subset\sqrt K$, while for the reverse inclusion
$f(\sqrt K)\subset\sqrt{f(K)}$ follows by applying $f$ to a whole
power witnessing radical membership and then taking radicals. Substitute
$I(W)=\sqrt K$ in this equality. The resulting radical-colon formula is
exactly the displayed whole original ideal identity.
\end{proof}
