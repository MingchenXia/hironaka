\section{Literal composition of actual smooth Cartesian blowup stages}

\begin{lemma}[Actual pulled histories append with every literal centre retained]
\label{hir:actual-smooth-cartesian-tower-append}
\uses{hir:submersion-cartesian-tower-original-production}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionCartesianTower.IsActualTowerPullback.append}
\leanok
Let $T:X\leftarrow D$ and $S:D\leftarrow Z$ be actual finite blowup
towers. Suppose actual stepwise smooth pullbacks along $b:Y\to X$
and the resulting upper submersion $g:D'\to D$ give towers $Q$ and
$R$ with upper map $k:Z'\to Z$. Then the literal appended source
$Q\mathbin{+}R$ is the actual stepwise pullback of the literal appended
target $T\mathbin{+}S$, with the unchanged upper map $k$. Every empty
centre and its position are retained.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual pullback history of $S$. At its identity stage,
appending the identity on each side gives the original actual history
of $T$. At a successor stage, the source centre is literally the
inverse image of the same target centre under the current actual upper
submersion, and its upper arrow is the actual smooth Cartesian blowup
lift. Append recursively to the preceding histories and use that very
same geometric blowup, source centre and actual lift to extend their
actual pullback history. The definition of tower append preserves each
successor blowup literally. No centre deletion or replacement is made.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original smooth geometry internally constructs the actual composite square]
\label{hir:actual-selected-stage-composite-smooth-pullback}
\uses{hir:actual-smooth-cartesian-tower-append,hir:submersion-cartesian-tower-original-production}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualTowerPullbackAppend.exists_actual_stagewise_composite}
\leanok
For any actual towers $T,S$ as above and genuine original submersion
$b:Y\to X$, internally construct actual source towers $Q,R$ and actual
upper arrows $g,k$ giving both stepwise pullbacks and their appended
actual pullback. The composite square is genuinely holomorphic
Cartesian, $k$ is a submersion, its projections commute, and its literal
length is $\operatorname{length}(T)+\operatorname{length}(S)$.
There is no source tower, source comparison, surjectivity or source
compactness premise. No assertion makes stages chosen for distinct
auxiliary ideals into one canonical history of a different ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the genuine actual smooth blowup-tower construction to the fixed
original $T$ and $b$, producing $Q,g$ and the actual first history.
Its actual upper arrow $g$ is a submersion. Apply the same genuine
construction to the unchanged $S$ along this actual $g$, producing
$R,k$ and the actual second history. The preceding lemma appends these
actual histories with the same upper $k$. The actual history theorem
gives its genuine holomorphic Cartesian universal property, submersion
property and commuting projections. Its literal length equals the
length of the appended target. The original tower append length identity
is exactly the sum of the two unchanged target lengths. All source
towers and arrows have thus been constructed from the stated original
geometry, before asserting the resulting composite properties.
\end{proof}
