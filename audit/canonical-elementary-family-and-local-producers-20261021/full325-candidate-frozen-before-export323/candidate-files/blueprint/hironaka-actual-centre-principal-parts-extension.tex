\section{Whole original principal parts over genuine tangent germs}

\begin{theorem}[The origin-unit pivot gives the whole tangent-germ projection kernel]
\label{hir:actual-centre-whole-germ-pivot-kernel}
\uses{hir:actual-centre-joint-whole-macaulay-image,hir:actual-hs-constant-centre-origin-pivot-whole-frames}
A genuine original pivot nonzero at the original point is a UNIT
pivot over $B$. If the actual original matrix has that SAME rank on
the actual centre near zero, the genuine original pivot projection,
lifted entrywise to tangent germs, has kernel exactly the ENTIRE
$B$-image of the SAME original Macaulay columns. It preserves the
true joint-jet image $S_B$ and fixes its actual selected-row kernel.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreMacaulayGerm_eval}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreMacaulayGerm_pivot_unit}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreProjectionEntry_holAt}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreGermProjection}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreGermProjection_representatives}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreGermProjection_fixes_row_kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreGermProjection_kills_columns}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreGermProjection_row_zero}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreGermProjection_mem_model}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreGermProjection_kernel}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Evaluation of the actual determinant germ is the nonzero original
pivot determinant at zero, so the original local-ring unit criterion
gives a true unit. The actual inverse-pivot projection has holomorphic
entries on its real pivot open. Its identities killing the selected
columns and fixing the row kernel lift to equalities of ENTIRE tangent
germs. The unit pivot splits an arbitrary germ vector into a genuine
selected-column vector and a selected-row-kernel vector. Those
identities identify the entire projection kernel with the selected
column image. On the actual centre, source-derived rank constancy
makes the original projection kill every original column; equality on
a real tangent neighbourhood makes the SAME lifted whole germ product
zero. Thus the selected image equals the full actual Macaulay image.
That image lies in $S_B$ by the preceding true joint dictionary, so
subtracting it preserves $S_B$ and the original projection fixes its
row kernel. No generic denominator away from the original point occurs.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original HS-constant centres supply whole principal-parts quotient frames]
\label{hir:actual-hs-constant-centre-whole-principal-parts}
\uses{hir:actual-centre-whole-germ-pivot-kernel,hir:actual-centre-joint-original-word-jet,hir:actual-hs-constant-centre-orderwise-jet-system}
ONLY the original coherent ideal, actual regular centre and constancy
of ALL intrinsic HS values on that centre internally supply the SAME
original finite generators and ambient neighbourhood before every
order. For each order, on a genuine tangent neighbourhood containing
zero, the ENTIRE original quotient $R/(J+N_z^q)$ is $B$-linearly
isomorphic to the true joint-jet image intersected with its selected-row
kernel, by the genuine original pivot projection.

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentreJointIdeal_image}

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.actualCentrePrincipalPartsModel}

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCentrePrincipalParts.exists_original_hs_constant_centre_whole_principal_parts}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the accepted original HS-constant common-generator and origin-pivot
producer once, keeping the SAME real generators and ambient domain
before all orders. For a chosen order its actual pivot is nonzero at
zero and its real domain contains zero; its true tangent preimage does
too. The original intrinsic HS/rank dictionary and source HS constancy
prove exactly the required centre rank equality internally. The three
preceding results identify the whole original ideal image and the
whole projection kernel. Restrict the original projection to the true
joint-jet image and its selected-row kernel. It is surjective because
it fixes every vector of that kernel, and its entire original germ
kernel is precisely $J+N_z^q$: one direction follows from the genuine
ideal image and normal-power kernel, while the other subtracts an
actual ideal preimage and uses that same exact whole kernel. The
ordinary original module quotient theorem gives the asserted entire
$B$-linear equivalence. No caller rank, quotient frame, output diagram
or normal-flatness certificate is supplied.
\end{proof}

The original word arrays may have repetitions. Every assertion above
keeps their TRUE joint-jet image, and never identifies its quotient with
the full word-array cokernel. Neighbourhoods depend on the finite order.
This moving principal-parts construction does not yet identify the
NORMAL-adic graded algebra of the original ideal or prove tangent
Spencer compatibility, normal-flatness, compatible analytic vertices,
or general positive-centre Bennett.
