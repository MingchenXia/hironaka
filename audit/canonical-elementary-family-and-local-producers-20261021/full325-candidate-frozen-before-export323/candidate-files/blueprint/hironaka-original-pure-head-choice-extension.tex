\section{Actual pure-head choices over a genuine base change and empty boundary positions}

\begin{lemma}[The intrinsic pure good open ignores only empty boundary entries]
\label{hir:original-pure-head-good-open-empty-base}
\uses{hir:original-smooth-intrinsic-regular-locus,hir:original-boundary-empty-filter-geometry}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureHeadChoice.pure_good_open_over_base}
\leanok
Let $\Phi:X'\simeq X$ be a genuine biholomorphism, let $A$ be an original
analytic subset, and let $E,E'$ be original ordered SNC boundaries whose
filtered nonempty lists agree under actual $\Phi$ inverse image. For the
same dimension label $d$, the source intrinsic boundary-free regular open
of $\Phi^{-1}A$ and $E'$ is exactly the inverse image of the target
intrinsic boundary-free regular open of $A$ and $E$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Filtering only empty members does not change a list's whole union: each
original point in a member proves that member nonempty. Equality of the
filtered original lists therefore identifies the union of $E'$ with the
inverse image under $\Phi$ of the union of $E$. The actual biholomorphism
is a holomorphic submersion of relative dimension zero, so its entire
intrinsic dimension-$d$ regular locus is the inverse image of the target
regular locus. Substitute these two actual set equalities into the
definition of the boundary-free regular open, and commute inverse image
with difference, union and complement. This proves equality of the
actual opens, not a supplied good-locus certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Independent actual pure-head stages have one map over the prescribed base]
\label{hir:original-pure-head-unique-empty-base-comparison}
\uses{hir:original-pure-head-good-open-empty-base,hir:original-empty-boundary-terminal-base-comparison,hir:original-empty-boundary-first-meeting-base-comparison,hir:original-canonical-ordered-resolution-tree}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPureHeadChoice.existsUnique_pure_head_choice_over_base}
\leanok
For the same actual original inputs, let $T$ and $S$ be actual pure-head
stages of the ordered recursion for $(A,d,E)$ and
$(\Phi^{-1}A,d,E')$. Then there is a unique actual final biholomorphism
$\eta$ over $\Phi$. It transports their entire ordered final boundaries
after filtering precisely empty members. For every original closed set
$P\subset X$ it also satisfies
\[
 P_S(\Phi^{-1}P)=\eta^{-1}P_T(P).
\]
No common stopping index, comparison map or terminality premise is added.
The empty head case and ambient dimension zero are included.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual whole vanishing-ideal pullback for the genuine submersion
$\Phi$ identifies the source pure-head ideal with the actual pullback
of the target whole ideal. Whole ambient carriers and marking one
agree, and the assumed filtered boundary equality is exactly the
remaining initial-record condition for the genuine stage comparison.

If $A$ is nonempty, the actual base bijection makes $\Phi^{-1}A$
nonempty. Both genuine pure-head stage records therefore supply actual
next whole maximum points and avoidance of their own intrinsic good
opens. The preceding lemma proves those actual opens are the required
corresponding opens. Apply the genuine first-meeting comparison over
$\Phi$ and empty initial positions. It constructs the unique actual
map and the whole ordered filtered-boundary identity, with no common
index or terminality assumption.

If $A$ is empty, both whole original cosupports are empty. Each actual
canonical head history is then genuinely terminal, by the original
empty-cosupport theorem. Apply the genuine terminal comparison over
$\Phi$ and empty initial positions instead. It gives the same form of
unique map and whole ordered filtered-boundary identity, including in
ambient dimension zero; no next-maximum point is invented.

Finally the genuine whole strict-transform comparison for a
biholomorphic projection square applies to the constructed actual
$\Phi,\eta$, the two actual blowup projections and every original
closed $P$. It gives the stated entire strict-transform identity.
Uniqueness of the already constructed map also gives uniqueness with
this additional whole-set property. These are comparisons of the actual
canonical pure stages supplied by the recursive provenance, not a
claim of uniqueness for an arbitrary unrelated resolution.
\end{proof}
