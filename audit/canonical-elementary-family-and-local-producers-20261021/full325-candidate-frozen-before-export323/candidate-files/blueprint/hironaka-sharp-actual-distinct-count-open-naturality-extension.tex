\begin{theorem}[True open restriction preserves BOTH directions of the entire ideal-germ relation]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-ideal-germ-relation-open}

underlying point. No equality of generators or desired germ relation is input.

original ideal member. Coherence first makes that member holomorphic. Its true
restriction belongs to the actual pulled-back ideal and so to the other

membership gives the original extended restricted germ in the other ideal.

point. The actual local finite generating presentation of that coherent ideal
transports membership through this germ equality. Repeat in the other direction.
Both implications apply to every actual queried germ, rather than only a chosen
set of generators.
\end{proof}
