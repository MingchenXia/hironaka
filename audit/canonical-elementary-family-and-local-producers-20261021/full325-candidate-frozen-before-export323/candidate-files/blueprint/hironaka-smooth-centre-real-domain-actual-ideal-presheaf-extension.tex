\section{Actual ideal section presheaves and their whole colimit stalks}

Keep the literal complex structure sheaf and the entire original germ
membership ideal. On an actual open, take ALL sections whose actual germ
at EVERY included point lies in the original whole ideal there. This gives
the actual ideal section presheaf, independently of a chosen local finite
family. Coherence is used to prove the whole stalk comparison, rather than
supplied as an ideal-stalk or generation certificate.

\begin{lemma}[Original coherent membership spreads on true original opens]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-original-coherent-membership-spread}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:actual-coherent-whole-point-quotient-column-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.coherent_original_membership_eventually,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.coherent_original_membership_exists_open}
\leanok
If the original ideal $A$ is coherent and $A_y$ contains the original
function germ $f$, there is a true original open neighbourhood $V$ of $y$
on which $A_z$ contains the SAME original function $f$ at EVERY $z\in V$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the original finite presentation on an actual open $W$ containing $y$.
Membership of $f$ at $y$ gives a finite holomorphic combination of that
presentation, equal to $f$ on a genuine neighbourhood of $y$. Choose an
open $V\subseteq W$ on which this equality holds and on which $f$ and all
finitely many coefficients are holomorphic. At each $z\in V$ the SAME
coefficients and finite family give the original local presentation of
$f$: the identity holds on the open $V$, hence near $z$. The original
presentation at $z$ therefore gives $A_z$ membership. No extension away
from this real open or global finite presentation is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The actual ideal section presheaf]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-ideal-section-presheaf}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-complex-sheaf-original-section-germs,hir:real-domain-actual-complex-sheaf-stalk-whole-original-ideals}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualSectionIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualSectionIdeal_restrict,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealSectionRestrict,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealSectionPresheaf,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealSectionStalk,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealSectionGermHom,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealSectionGermCocone,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealStalkToWholeHom,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealStalkToWholeHom_germ}
\leanok
For every actual open $V$, the ideal section module consists of ALL
literal complex structure-sheaf sections $s$ with $\operatorname{germ}_z(s)$
in the whole original ideal at EVERY $z\in V$. Actual open restrictions
make these modules a genuine additive ideal section presheaf. Each actual
section germ maps to its SAME original whole ideal germ, and these maps
form a cocone on the actual neighbourhood diagram at every original point.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The section ideal is the intersection, over all $z\in V$, of the inverse
images of the original whole stalk ideal under the actual section-to-stalk
ring homomorphism. Hence it is an ideal, with the literal section ring
operations. For $V\subseteq W$, the actual structure-sheaf germ identity
identifies the germ of a restricted section at each $z\in V$ with its
original germ on $W$. Thus restriction preserves this section ideal.
Restriction identity and composition are inherited from the literal
structure sheaf on all section values, so they define the stated additive
presheaf. At a neighbourhood containing $y$, its actual section-to-stalk
map lands in the whole original ideal at $y$ by the defining pointwise
condition. The actual restriction germ identity proves compatibility of
all these maps, giving the genuine colimit cocone without any stalk map
or ideal comparison as an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual ideal section colimit is the entire original stalk ideal]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-ideal-section-whole-stalk-equivalence}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-original-coherent-membership-spread,hir:real-domain-actual-ideal-section-presheaf}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealStalkToWholeHom_injective,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.sectionExtension_restrict_eventuallyEq,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.exists_actual_ideal_section_of_original_member,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealSectionGermImage,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealSectionGermImage_eq_whole,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealStalkToWholeHom_surjective,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealStalkWholeEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualIdealStalkWholeEquiv_germ}
\leanok
Original coherence internally gives a genuine additive equivalence from
the literal colimit stalk of the actual ideal section presheaf to the
ENTIRE original ideal in the literal structure-sheaf stalk. Its value on
EVERY actual ideal section germ is its original structure-sheaf germ.
No whole-ideal, stalk-injectivity, representative or generation certificate
is a caller premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding cocone gives the actual colimit homomorphism. For injectivity
represent each actual ideal presheaf stalk element by a true ideal section.
Equality of their images gives equality of original structure-sheaf germs.
The actual filtered neighbourhood colimit equality gives a true common
original neighbourhood on which both structure-sheaf restrictions are
literally equal. Their ideal subtype restrictions are therefore equal on
that SAME actual open containing the point. The actual ideal presheaf
colimit restriction identity gives the original ideal-presheaf stalk equality.

For surjectivity first treat a generator of the entire original stalk
ideal: it is the actual germ of an original membership function $f$.
The first lemma spreads membership of the SAME $f$ to a true neighbourhood,
and the literal structure-sheaf producer represents $f$ by an actual section.
Shrink to the common open where that section extension equals $f$. At
EVERY point of this open its actual germ is an original membership germ,
so it is an actual ideal section with the prescribed germ. Now the set
of such genuine ideal-section germs is an ideal in the actual ring stalk.
Zero is represented by the zero section. To add two represented elements,
restrict both actual sections to their true open intersection and add.
To multiply a represented element by an arbitrary ring stalk element,
represent the latter by an actual structure-sheaf section, restrict to
the common open and multiply; the pointwise ideal property retains
membership. Thus ideal-span induction gives EVERY element of the entire
original ideal a true ideal-section germ preimage. This proves bijectivity
and the stated whole equivalence, retaining all representative formulas.
It does not yet identify quotient layers, the whole normal-cone sheaf,
or its scalar-action stalks; those remain subsequent comparisons.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[The actual centre vanishing ideal has the same entire ideal-presheaf stalk]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ideal-section-whole-stalk-equivalence}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-ideal-section-presheaf,hir:real-domain-actual-ideal-section-whole-stalk-equivalence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.vanishing_original_membership_eventually,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualCentreIdealSectionGermImage_eq_whole,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualCentreIdealStalkToWholeHom_surjective,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualCentreIdealStalkWholeEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualIdealPresheaf.actualCentreIdealStalkWholeEquiv_germ}
\leanok
For EVERY original subset $C$ and original point $y$, the actual additive
colimit stalk of the ideal section presheaf of the original vanishing ideal
is internally equivalent to its ENTIRE ideal in the literal complex
structure-sheaf stalk. The equivalence retains EVERY actual centre ideal
section germ. No global coherence, regularity or desired centre-stalk
certificate is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An original vanishing-ideal member $f$ at $y$ is holomorphic and vanishes
on $C$ in a true neighbourhood of $y$. Shrink to an original open on
which both properties hold. At EVERY point of that open the SAME $f$
is holomorphic near the point and vanishes on $C$ near it. Thus original
vanishing membership spreads on actual original points, irrespective of
any global analytic property of $C$. The preceding whole colimit proof
uses coherence only to obtain this membership spreading for each original
generator. Use the just proved vanishing membership spreading instead:
EVERY original vanishing generator has a genuine actual ideal section
preimage, the actual common-open addition and scalar multiplication close
these germs to an ideal, and entire span induction supplies surjectivity.
Injectivity uses only literal actual colimit equality and common open
restrictions, independently of coherence. This internally proves the
same entire additive stalk equivalence and all representative formulas
for the actual centre vanishing ideal, without a global coherence
extension or a chosen coordinate-plane certificate.
\end{proof}
