\section{A genuine finite permitted boundary prephase at every marking}
\label{sec:hironaka-normal-crossings-any-mark-prephase}

Let $H$ be an intrinsic normal-crossing carrier, and let $E$ be a
finite actual SNC boundary admitting simultaneous adapted coordinates
with $H$. The original double locus must lie in the original marked
cosupport. This is a qualified permitted prephase: it does not replace
the case of an arbitrary merely-NC initial boundary whose selfcrossings
can lie outside the original cosupport.

\begin{theorem}[Every actual higher-old-branch history is a test history at the unchanged marking]
\label{hir:general-nc-any-mark-higher-history-test-and-double-cosupport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsAnyMarkPrephase.higherSeparationCentres_isGeoTestTower_and_double_subset_cosupp}
Let $M$ be a well-formed whole-ambient marked datum, and suppose the
original double locus of an actual NC carrier $H$ lies in $M$'s
cosupport. A genuine finite history whose every centre is a nonempty
highest old-branch superlevel of count at least two, with actual
adapted coordinates for the current literal boundary, is an actual
geometric test history for this same datum. The entire final strict
old-carrier double locus lies in the actual final cosupport. The
original marking is arbitrary and unchanged at every step.
\uses{hir:general-nc-actual-highest-step-any-mark-double-cosupport,hir:general-nc-intrinsic-higher-separation-step}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the literal finite history. At length zero the differential
strict transform by the identity is the original closed carrier, so
the original double-locus inclusion is precisely the required one;
the empty history is a test history.

For a final step, its genuine higher-centre record supplies actual
adapted coordinates for the previous strict old carrier and its
literal accumulated boundary, a global upper bound $r\geq2$ on the
old-branch count, and equality of the actual centre with that highest
superlevel. Induction supplies a true previous test history and its
entire double-locus cosupport inclusion. Actual admissible-transform
well-formedness gives coherence of the current ideal and the actual
current smooth carrier; the whole-ambient transform dictionary keeps
that carrier equal to the whole current manifold.

Apply the any-mark highest-step theorem to this very current datum.
It internally gives admissibility of the actual next centre and
places the entire new old-carrier double locus in the literal
controlled cosupport at its unchanged mark. The genuine whole-ambient
transform dictionary identifies that controlled cosupport with the
actual next marked datum. Composition of actual differential strict
transforms identifies the new old carrier with the strict transform
along the whole appended history. Thus append exactly this admissible
step to the previous test history and transport the proved inclusion
along that actual carrier equality. No intermediate cosupport or
admissibility certificate is supplied by the caller.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The internally constructed finite separation is permitted for the original ideal at every marking]
\label{hir:general-nc-any-mark-original-permitted-finite-prephase}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsAnyMarkPrephase.naturalSeparation_isGeoTestTower_of_doubleLocus_subset_cosupp}
For every original coherent ideal $I$, natural marking $d$ and actual
adapted pair $(H,E)$, suppose the original double locus lies in the
literal whole-ambient cosupport of $(I,d)$. Form the actual original
marked datum $(X,E,I,d)$ and its internally constructed natural
old-branch separation. This same finite history is a genuine test
history. Every recorded prefix has its entire current old-carrier
double locus in its actual current cosupport. Its length is at most
$n-1$; the terminal strict old carrier is closed and smooth, is
transverse to the literal accumulated boundary, and adjoining it to
that boundary is SNC. Every centre lies over the original cosupport,
the history is an actual admissible blowup sequence permitted there,
and its projection is an isomorphism on the whole complement of that
original cosupport.
\uses{hir:general-nc-any-mark-higher-history-test-and-double-cosupport,hir:general-nc-permitted-double-locus-prephase,hir:general-nc-bounded-actual-separation-tower}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the original coherent ideal, original boundary and original mark
to form the literal whole-ambient marked datum. Its boundary is
actually SNC by the given simultaneous adapted coordinates, and the
whole ambient carrier is closed, smooth and transverse to that
boundary. Thus this original datum is well formed.

Choose its genuine natural old-branch separation. The construction
already supplies an actual higher-centre record at every performed
step, the strict old-carrier bound by one at the end, and length at
most $n-1$. Apply the preceding theorem to these actual centre records
to obtain its test-history property and every actual prefix
cosupport inclusion. The prefix statement follows by the same
induction, taking at each step the actual adapted old carrier supplied
by that very step's centre record.

The existing actual separation geometry makes the entire terminal
strict old carrier closed and smooth, gives its genuine transversality
to the literal accumulated boundary, and makes their appended list
SNC. The original marked cosupport is closed by coherence. Apply the
actual permitted-set separation geometry with this same closed set:
the assumed original double-locus inclusion places every actual
centre over it and makes the literal tower an admissible blowup
sequence permitted there. The centres avoid its entire complement;
the actual blowup projection is therefore an isomorphism on that
whole complement. All these conclusions concern the same internally
chosen tower and the unchanged original ideal and marking.
\end{proof}
