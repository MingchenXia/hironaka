\section{Whole controlled ideals on the same actual closed contact blowup}

Let $N\subset A^n$ be an original closed subset regular everywhere of
dimension $k\le n$, with its actual native flattening atlas. Let
$D\subset N$ be an original smooth closed nowhere dense centre, put
$C=j(D)$ for the literal inclusion $j:N\hookrightarrow A$, and let
$\pi:Y^n\to A$ be an actual blowup along $C$. The preceding genuine
closed-contact construction gives the same native strict contact
$S=\overline{\pi^{-1}(N\setminus C)}$, its inclusion $j_S:S\hookrightarrow Y$
and the literal actual blowup $\sigma:S\to N$ with
$\pi j_S=j\sigma$. No global retraction, supplied comparison map or
desired ideal restriction equality is an input.

\begin{theorem}[Original admissibility and the whole total ideal square]
\label{hir:actual-closed-contact-original-admissibility-total-square}
\uses{hir:actual-closed-contact-native-geometry,hir:actual-closed-contact-full-restricted-blowup,hir:crossdim-entire-unit-power-pullback,hir:submersion-choice-image-trace-whole-pullback}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualClosedContactControlledRestriction.restricted_original_admissibility,Hironaka.HironakaActualClosedContactControlledRestriction.restricted_totalTransform}
\leanok
For any original coherent ideal $I$ on $A$ and any mark $\mu\in\mathbb N$,
original admissibility $I\subseteq\mathcal I_C^\mu$ gives actual lower
admissibility $j^*I\subseteq\mathcal I_D^\mu$. The whole ideal square is
\[
 j_S^*(\pi^*I)=\sigma^*(j^*I).
\]
Both are equalities or inclusions of the independently defined entire
functional germ ideals, rather than identities only on a chosen finite
generating family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply actual whole-ideal pullback monotonicity to the original
admissibility inclusion. The genuine holomorphic pullback theorem for
powers applies to $j$: the actual ambient centre ideal is coherent
because $\pi$ is an actual blowup. It identifies the upper ideal with
$(j^*\mathcal I_C)^\mu$. The genuine whole singular-subset germ
extension theorem already proved $j^*\mathcal I_C=\mathcal I_D$, so
this gives precisely the asserted lower admissibility. No submersion
property of the inclusion is used.

For the square, apply genuine whole pullback composition first to
$\pi\circ j_S$ and then to $j\circ\sigma$. All members of $I$ are
holomorphic by its original coherence. The actual literal function
identity $\pi j_S=j\sigma$ identifies the intermediate pullbacks,
proving the whole ideal equality on the same actual $S$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same entire exceptional denominator restricts exactly]
\label{hir:actual-closed-contact-exceptional-denominator-restriction}
\uses{hir:actual-closed-contact-original-admissibility-total-square,hir:actual-closed-contact-full-restricted-blowup,hir:crossdim-entire-unit-power-pullback,bcalc:exceptional-divisor}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualClosedContactControlledRestriction.restricted_exceptionalIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaActualClosedContactControlledRestriction.restricted_exceptionalIdeal_power}
\leanok
Write $L_\pi=\pi^*\mathcal I_C$ and
$L_\sigma=\sigma^*\mathcal I_D$ for the actual exceptional divisor
ideals of these same two blowups. Then, for every $q\in\mathbb N$,
\[
 j_S^*L_\pi=L_\sigma,\qquad j_S^*(L_\pi^q)=L_\sigma^q.
\]
The lower blowup and its denominator are internally produced from the
original closed regular contact, original smooth nowhere dense centre
and the actual ambient blowup.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine contact restriction theorem proves that the literal
$\sigma$ is a full actual blowup along $D$, using both genuine
universal properties. By definition the actual exceptional ideal of
each blowup is the entire pullback of its original centre ideal.
Apply the preceding whole total square with $I=\mathcal I_C$, which
is coherent by the actual ambient blowup. The actual centre identity
$j^*\mathcal I_C=\mathcal I_D$ identifies the resulting entire ideal
with the actual $L_\sigma$.

The actual ambient exceptional ideal is coherent because it is
invertible. Generic holomorphic whole pullback of powers along the
actual inclusion $j_S$ therefore gives
$j_S^*(L_\pi^q)=(j_S^*L_\pi)^q$. Substitute the just-proved actual
exceptional ideal identity. This also covers $q=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole controlled ideal restricts on this same actual blowup]
\label{hir:actual-closed-contact-whole-controlled-ideal-restriction}
\uses{hir:actual-closed-contact-original-admissibility-total-square,hir:actual-closed-contact-exceptional-denominator-restriction,hir:crossdim-entire-product-pullback,bcalc:controlled-transform}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualClosedContactControlledRestriction.restricted_controlledTransform}
\leanok
For an original coherent ideal $I$ and original admissibility
$I\subseteq\mathcal I_C^\mu$, the same literal $\sigma$ satisfies the
whole independently defined controlled-ideal equality
\[
 j_S^*\bigl((\pi^*I:L_\pi^\mu)\bigr)
   =\bigl(\sigma^*(j^*I):L_\sigma^\mu\bigr).
\]
The original mark $\mu$ is unchanged. No colon-pullback compatibility,
controlled-ideal equality or output factorisation is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $Q=(\pi^*I:L_\pi^\mu)$, the actual ambient controlled
transform. Original coherence and original admissibility give its
genuine coherence and the whole actual factorisation
$\pi^*I=L_\pi^\mu Q$. Pull this equality back along the genuine
holomorphic $j_S$. Generic holomorphic whole pullback of products
applies because both factors are coherent; it does not require an
inclusion to be a submersion. Use the first theorem for its left side
and the second theorem for its first factor. This gives the actual
whole factorisation
\[
 \sigma^*(j^*I)=L_\sigma^\mu\,j_S^*Q.
\]
The entire ideal $j_S^*Q$ is genuinely coherent by holomorphic
pullback of the proved ambient coherent controlled ideal. The actual
exceptional power $L_\sigma^\mu$ is invertible for this same lower
blowup. Cancellation of an invertible ideal identifies the displayed
coherent quotient uniquely with the independently defined lower colon
ideal. This proves the stated equality without commuting a colon
through a general closed immersion.
\end{proof}

These are one-step whole-ideal results for the same actual contact and
the same actual original admissible blowup. The strict contact above
uses the centre-relative \texttt{geoStrictTransform} closure. No ordinary
exceptional-locus strict-transform equality, canonical invariant tail,
maximal-centre equality, ordered boundary comparison or canonical
resolution descent is inferred. A tower statement still needs actual
compatible contact and centre data at every stage.
