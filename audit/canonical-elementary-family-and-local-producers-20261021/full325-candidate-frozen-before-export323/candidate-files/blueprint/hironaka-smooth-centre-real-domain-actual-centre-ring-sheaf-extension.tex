\section{The original centre quotient ring sheaf acts globally}

On every genuine original open $U$, let $J(U)$ be all actual holomorphic
sections whose germs belong to the whole original vanishing ideal of $C$
at every point of $U$. Use the literal quotient $\mathcal O(U)/J(U)$;
its restriction maps descend from actual holomorphic restrictions.

\begin{definition}[The genuine original centre quotient ring sheaf]

\leanok
The literal section quotients form a ring presheaf on the original
space. Its ring-valued sheafification is the actual centre quotient ring
sheaf. The original structure ring sheaf has a genuine quotient morphism
to this sheaf, given on every original section by its literal quotient
class followed by the sheafification unit.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Actual restriction preserves every pointwise centre germ condition.
The quotient-ring universal property therefore descends restriction to
the section quotients. On every numerator it is the quotient class of
the same restricted holomorphic section. This proves identity and
composition, hence the presheaf laws. Apply genuine ring-valued
sheafification. The numerator quotient maps are natural by their
restriction formula; compose them with the unit to obtain the original
structure ring sheaf morphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual associated ring sheaf is a global centre algebra]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-sheaf-associated-map}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}

\leanok
For every original ideal $A$ and set $C$, there is a genuine ring sheaf
morphism from the actual centre quotient ring sheaf to the actual
associated ring sheaf. Composing it with the original quotient morphism
gives exactly the existing ambient ring sheaf morphism. Every original
numerator retains the same degree-zero section class followed by the
associated-ring sheafification unit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual ambient section algebra map is the degree-zero class in
$\mathcal O(U)/(A(U)+J(U))$, inserted in the direct sum. A member of
$J(U)$ has zero class in this quotient, hence zero image in the actual
associated section ring. The quotient universal property gives a ring
map from $\mathcal O(U)/J(U)$ to the actual associated section ring.
Every numerator keeps its original ambient algebra image. The already
proved naturality of that image gives a natural ring presheaf map.
Compose with the associated-ring unit and extend uniquely through the
centre ring sheafification. The extension identity proves the numerator
formula on every true original open. Extensionality on every original
section then proves the whole ambient factorization. No coherent-ideal
assumption or stalk comparison supplies this global morphism.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The global factorization retains actual ambient stalk scalars]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-sheaf-stalk-factorization}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-centre-ring-sheaf-associated-map,hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-ring-sheaf-ambient-scalars}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheaf.actualCentreAmbientRingSheafStalkHom,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheaf.actualCentreRingAssociatedSheafStalkHom,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualCentreRingSheaf.actualCentreRingAssociatedSheafStalkHom_factorization}

Applying genuine ring stalk functoriality to the global quotient
factorization gives two actual ring stalk maps whose composition is the
existing actual ambient stalk ring homomorphism. Thus every actual
ambient stalk scalar keeps its original image under this global centre
ring-sheaf action.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine ring stalk functor preserves composition. Apply it to the
proved equality of global ring sheaf morphisms and then to an arbitrary
actual ambient stalk element. This uses the already constructed maps;
it does not transport operations through the whole ring dictionary.
\end{proof}
