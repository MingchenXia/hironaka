\section{Actual singular centres have normal codimension at least two}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-singular-centre-codimension}

\begin{theorem}[The true singular-locus bound forces centre codimension]
\label{hir:actual-singular-centre-normal-codimension-two}
\uses{rem:pure-singular-locus,rem:dimension-monotone,hir:analytic-dimzero-all-points-regular}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualSingularCentreCodimension.regular_singular_centre_add_two_le,Hironaka.HironakaActualSingularCentreCodimension.normal_codimension_two_of_regular_singular_centre}
\leanok
Let $W$ be an analytic pure $r$-dimensional subset of an $n$-manifold, with $r<n$, and let $C\subset W\setminus W_{\rm reg}$.
At every genuine regular point of $C$ of dimension $k$, one has $k+2\le n$.
In particular, if $n=k+t$, the actual normal codimension satisfies $t\ge2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The regular point belongs to $C$ and therefore to the singular locus of $W$.
If $r=0$, the analytic dimension-zero regularity theorem makes every point of $W$ regular of dimension zero, contradicting this singular membership. Thus $r>0$.
The proved analytic singular-locus dimension bound is $\dim W_{\rm sing}\le r-1$.
Monotonicity of actual analytic dimension under inclusion gives $\dim C\le r-1$, so its specified regular dimension satisfies $k\le r-1$.
Since $r>0$ and $r<n$, natural-number arithmetic gives $k+2\le n$.
Substitution of $n=k+t$ gives $2\le t$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same internally chosen singular blowup has this codimension]
\label{hir:actual-original-chosen-singular-centre-codimension-two}
\uses{hir:actual-singular-centre-normal-codimension-two,hir:actual-original-hs-constant-singular-centre-proper-blowup}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualSingularCentreCodimension.exists_actual_original_singular_centre_proper_blowup_codimension_two}
\leanok
Original compact nonsmooth pure analytic geometry of dimension $r<n$, with its original SNC boundary, internally chooses an actual admissible HS-constant singular centre and a proper blowup.
For this same centre $C$, every actual regular dimension $k$ at every point satisfies $k+2\le n$.
The whole chosen centre, original HS-constancy, actual blowup, properness, original regular-open isomorphism and the unchanged boundary conclusions are retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the genuine proper singular-centre blowup from the existing original-only producer, retaining its centre, blowup and every stated geometric field.
That centre is contained in the actual singular locus of the same original pure analytic set.
Apply the preceding dimension theorem to that inclusion for each original regular centre point and dimension.
No new centre or target chart is chosen, and no codimension premise is inserted.
\end{proof}
