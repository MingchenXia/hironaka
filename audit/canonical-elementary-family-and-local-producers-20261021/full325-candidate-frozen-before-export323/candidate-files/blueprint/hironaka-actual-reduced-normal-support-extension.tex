\section{Literal normal monomial independence forces reduced vertex support}

Write $S=\mathbb C[[u_1,\ldots,u_d,z_1,\ldots,z_t]]$,
$\widehat B=\mathbb C[[u]]$ and $N=(z_1,\ldots,z_t)$.
Let $K\subset S$ be an actual ideal, let $m$ be a monomial order,
and let $\Gamma$ be its actual initial diagram. These statements concern
the actual canonical reduced vertex series in $K$. They do not assume
the desired normal-power membership.

\begin{lemma}[Reconstruction from the entire low normal coefficient family]
\label{hir:actual-reduced-normal-support-reconstruction}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualReducedNormalSupport.sub_formalNormalCombination_mem_normal_power}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-formal-normal-truncation-coordinates}
For every $F\in S$ and $q\geq0$, let $c_\gamma(u)$ be its entire formal
coefficient of $z^\gamma$. Then
\[
 F-\sum_{|\gamma|<q}c_\gamma(u)z^\gamma\in N^q.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For every exponent $(\beta,\gamma)$ with $|\gamma|<q$, the actual
coefficient formula for the finite normal monomial combination extracts
exactly the $u^\beta$ coefficient of $c_\gamma$. This is, by definition,
the $(\beta,\gamma)$ coefficient of $F$. Every coefficient of the
difference of normal weight below $q$ therefore vanishes. The proved
whole coordinate-power coefficient criterion places the difference in
the actual ideal $N^q$. The argument also covers $q=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Reduced tails give literal normal-standard coefficient families]
\label{hir:actual-reduced-normal-support-standard-coefficients}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualReducedNormalSupport.reducedNormalCoefficients_mem_standard}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-reduced-formal-vertex-members,hir:actual-formal-ideal-initial-diagram-upper}
Let $a$ be an actual initial vertex and let $G_a$ be the canonical
reduced vertex series. If $q\leq |a_z|$, then its low normal coefficient
family $(c_\gamma)_{|\gamma|<q}$ has $c_\gamma=0$ whenever
$(0,\gamma)\in\Gamma$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix such a normal exponent $\gamma$. Upperness of the actual initial
diagram gives $(\beta,\gamma)\in\Gamma$ for every tangent exponent
$\beta$. Its normal weight is $|\gamma|<q\leq|a_z|$, so it cannot
equal the vertex $a$. The actual reduced series has coefficient zero
at every exponent of $\Gamma$ other than $a$. Thus every tangent
coefficient of $c_\gamma$ is zero, which proves the asserted equality
of entire formal coefficient series.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Literal standard normal independence gives true vertex powers]
\label{hir:actual-reduced-normal-support-from-monomial-injectivity}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualReducedNormalSupport.actualReducedVertexSeries_mem_normal_power_of_standardNormalMonomialMap_injective,Hironaka.HironakaActualReducedNormalSupport.all_actualReducedVertexSeries_mem_normal_power_of_standardNormalMonomialMap_injective}
\leanok
\uses{hir:actual-reduced-normal-support-reconstruction,hir:actual-reduced-normal-support-standard-coefficients}
For each $q$, let $V_q$ be the actual $\widehat B$-submodule of finite
normal coefficient arrays $(b_\gamma)_{|\gamma|<q}$ for which
$b_\gamma=0$ whenever $(0,\gamma)\in\Gamma$. Consider the literal
normal monomial map
\[
 V_q\longrightarrow S/(K+N^q),\qquad
 (b_\gamma)_\gamma\longmapsto
 \left[\sum_{|\gamma|<q} b_\gamma(u)z^\gamma\right].
\]
If this named map is injective for $q=|a_z|$, then the canonical
reduced vertex series $G_a$ belongs to the entire actual ideal
$N^{|a_z|}$. Consequently, injectivity of these literal maps at
every order gives this true normal-power membership at every actual
vertex of the same ideal $K$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take $q=|a_z|$. By the preceding lemma, the actual low normal
coefficient family of $G_a$ belongs to $V_q$. The reconstruction lemma
shows that its finite normal combination is congruent to $G_a$ modulo
$N^q$. The actual reduced vertex series belongs to the original ideal
$K$, so this literal coefficient family maps to zero in $S/(K+N^q)$.
Injectivity of the stated monomial map makes the entire coefficient
family zero. Thus every coefficient of $G_a$ of normal weight below
$q$ vanishes. The actual whole coordinate-power coefficient criterion
gives $G_a\in N^q$. Applying this argument independently at every
actual vertex proves the all-vertex conclusion.
\end{proof}

The hypothesis is genuine linear independence of specified monomial
classes in an actual formal normal truncation. Producing it from an
original Hilbert--Samuel constant centre, including comparison of the
fibre-standard and original-diagram indices, is a separate geometric
producer. No such geometric producer or general Bennett theorem is
asserted by this chapter.
