\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The true global divisor restricts to exactly this SNC support]

whose ideal is the true restricted global pullback ideal and whose
support is exactly the union of the actual ordered SNC family of the

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The already proved range ideal dictionary says that the true restricted
global pullback is $(e_i^{-1})^*\mathcal O(-E_{T_i})$ and is invertible.

restricted effective divisor; it is literally the restriction of the

biholomorphism.  Thus the restricted divisor support is the inverse image
of that union.  Inverse image commutes with union, and the transported
whole list is exactly the family of those inverse images.  This proves
the asserted equality for the actual global divisor restriction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual restricted global ideal generators are units times coordinate monomials]
\label{hir:original-global-principalization-range-unit-monomial-generator}

\leanok

at every point of every actual original global range, the true restricted
global pullback ideal has a genuine local holomorphic generator.  There
are centered holomorphic coordinates adapted to the actual transported
terminal boundary in which this generator is a nonvanishing holomorphic

The principal stalk identity is for the same actual restricted global
ideal, not for a replacement local model.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding theorem constructs the actual restricted effective divisor,
its entire genuine ordered SNC family, and their true support equality.
The proved local SNC divisor generator theorem applies to exactly these
objects.  Invertibility supplies a holomorphic local generator and all
principal stalk identities.  SNC supplies centered holomorphic adapted
coordinates with distinct hyperplanes for the members through the point.
The actual support equality confines every zero of the coordinate
generator germ to these coordinate hyperplanes.  Holomorphic coordinate
factorization then gives nonnegative integer powers of coordinates and
a holomorphic residual factor nonzero at the origin.  These are the
asserted genuine unit and monomial.  The divisor ideal is literally the
true restricted global pullback, so its principal stalk identities are
those of that same global ideal.
\end{proof}
