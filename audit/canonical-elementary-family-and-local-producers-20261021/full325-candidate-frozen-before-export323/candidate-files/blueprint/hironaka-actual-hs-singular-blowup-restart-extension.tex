\begin{lemma}[Actual geometric purity and density of the same-dimensional regular locus]
\label{hir:actual-current-purity-regular-density-dictionary}
\uses{lag:regular-locus-dense,lag:regular-point-basics}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHSSingularBlowupRestart.actualPure_subset_closure_regularLocus,Hironaka.HironakaActualHSSingularBlowupRestart.actualPure_of_subset_closure_regularLocus}

$W\subseteq\overline{\operatorname{Reg}_dW}$. Conversely, for any actual set,
this same density forces every one of its regular points to have dimension $d$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}

Conversely, at an actual regular point of dimension $j$, regular coordinates

The assumed true closure supplies a $d$-regular point in that neighborhood.
Uniqueness of actual local manifold dimension gives $j=d$. No caller dimension
or regular-density certificate replaces the original analyticity/purity producer.
\end{proof}
