\section{Actual coefficient algebras for all original nearby ideals on real domains}

Only original regular-centre geometry and original ideal coherence are
inputs. One genuine flattening and real base neighbourhood are retained
for all nearby points. The coefficient algebra consists of actual functions
holomorphic on their stated real domains. Its nearby maps translate these
functions before taking germs; no arbitrary origin-germ evaluation is used.

\begin{theorem}[Original real domains supply one entire coefficient-function initial family]
\label{hir:real-domain-original-coefficient-function-initial-family}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-original-all-nearby-initial-polynomial-kernels,hir:relative-normal-coefficient-function-polynomial-lift,hir:relative-normal-graded-internal-finite-relations}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainCoefficientAlgebra.exists_original_regular_real_coefficient_algebra_initial_family}
\leanok
Original inputs internally supply ONE genuine flattening and ONE real base
neighbourhood $B$ of zero whose tangent inclusion lies in the actual target
and coordinate centre plane. The SAME finite original homogeneous relation
family lifts to homogeneous polynomials over the algebra $A_B$ of actual
functions holomorphic near every point of $B$. Every lifted polynomial maps
back to its entire original polynomial at zero. The image of the whole
lifted ideal at zero is the entire original normal initial ideal, and its
image at EVERY $a\in B$ is the entire initial ideal of the actual translated
original flattened family, all using this same flattening and finite family.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original all-nearby polynomial-kernel producer supplies the actual
flattening, one real base open, each original coefficient function holomorphic
near every base point, and the WHOLE nearby initial-ideal equality at every
point. For each selected original relation, form the finite polynomial sum
with its supported coefficients replaced by their actual coefficient functions
in $A_B$. The true coefficient-function lift formulas identify all coefficients,
retain the original homogeneous degree, and identify its image under each
actual translated germ map with the whole nearby coefficient polynomial.
At zero the actual chosen coefficient function has its prescribed original
germ, so the whole polynomial image is the original relation. The image of
an ideal generated by a family is the ideal generated by the images of that
family: this follows in each direction by applying the ring homomorphism to
finite coefficient sums and then taking their ideal span. At zero the actual
finite original relations generate the entire original initial ideal.
At each other included point, the supplied all-nearby equality identifies
the full ideal span of these polynomial images with the entire original
translated initial ideal. The actual geometric domains and single flattening
are retained, including empty relation families and zero normal dimension.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One actual finite type coefficient algebra presents all nearby original ideals]
\label{hir:real-domain-original-finite-type-coefficient-initial-family}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-original-coefficient-function-initial-family,hir:relative-normal-finite-type-coefficient-algebra}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainCoefficientAlgebra.exists_original_regular_real_finiteType_coefficient_initial_family}
\leanok
For the SAME genuine flattening and real base $B$, original inputs internally
supply a finite type complex subalgebra $C\subset A_B$ and ONE finite homogeneous
relation family over $C$. The entire origin ideal image is the original
normal initial ideal. At EVERY point of $B$, the entire ideal image under the
actual coefficient-function germ map restricted to $C$ is the original
nearby initial ideal. No coefficient algebra or finite-family witness is an
original-input premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the actual finite lifted family from the preceding theorem. The union
of its supported coefficient sets is finite, since the family and every
polynomial support are finite. Let $C$ be the complex subalgebra generated by
that finite set. The finite type coefficient lemma gives its finite type
property and a whole coefficient lift of each polynomial to $C$. Coefficient
inclusion recovers every polynomial exactly, and injectivity of that
inclusion retains each original homogeneous degree. Compose each actual
origin or nearby coefficient germ map with this inclusion. Its polynomial
map of each lifted relation is exactly the preceding polynomial image,
by composition of coefficient maps. Taking the full ideal spans in both
directions therefore preserves every entire initial-ideal equality on the
same real base. No open is changed in this finite coefficient construction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine common coefficient domain retains every entire nearby ideal]
\label{hir:real-domain-original-common-domain-coefficient-initial-family}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-original-finite-type-coefficient-initial-family,hir:relative-normal-coefficient-algebra-kernel-spread,hir:relative-normal-coefficient-algebra-domain-nearby-homs}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainCoefficientAlgebra.exists_original_regular_real_common_domain_initial_family}
\leanok
Original inputs internally supply the SAME genuine flattening and actual
finite type coefficient-function subalgebra $C$, its quotient domain
$D=C/\ker(\rho_0|_C)$, and one smaller real neighbourhood $W\subset B$ of zero.
The quotient remains finite type over $\mathbb C$. At EVERY $a\in W$ an actual
complex algebra map from $D$ to the translated base germ ring composes with
the quotient map to give exactly the true coefficient-function germ map.
The image of one finite homogeneous ideal over $D$ is the ENTIRE original
nearby initial ideal at every point of $W$. All actual points still map into
the same genuine flattening target and coordinate centre plane.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Finite type over $\mathbb C$ makes the internally supplied $C$ Noetherian.
Its whole origin coefficient kernel is consequently finitely generated.
Each selected generator is an actual function with zero origin germ; it
vanishes near zero. Intersect these finitely many vanishing neighbourhoods
with $B$, and choose an open neighbourhood $W$ of zero inside that
intersection. At every $a\in W$ each generator vanishes on a neighbourhood
of $a$, so its actual translated germ is zero. The actual nearby kernel is
an ideal and therefore contains their whole span, which is the full origin
kernel. Every genuine nearby coefficient map thus factors through the
quotient, with its actual-function composition identity preserved.
The origin coefficient map takes values in the domain of holomorphic germs,
so its kernel is prime and $D$ is a domain. Quotients preserve finite type.
Map each actual relation over $C$ through the quotient coefficient map;
homogeneous degree is retained. At every $a\in W$, composition of polynomial
maps and the defining quotient factorisation give exactly the preceding
actual nearby relation images. The image of their complete ideal span is
therefore the entire original nearby initial ideal. Restrict the preceding
actual geometric domain dictionary along $W\subset B$. This gives a genuine
common domain for subsequent uniform localisation; no generic free open,
origin flatness, or sheaf normal-flatness is concluded here.
\end{proof}
