\section{Actual quotient-subspace models commute with genuine open restriction}
\label{sec:hironaka-actual-analytic-subspace-open-restriction}

Let $C\subset M$ be a subset of a complex manifold and let $U\subset M$
be an original open. Write $C_U=\{x\in U:x\in C\}$. Each subspace model
has its independently constructed actual whole vanishing-quotient sheaf.
No comparison of structure sheaves or stalks is assumed.

\begin{theorem}[The native sheaf stalk pullback is the actual whole chart-germ pullback]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-native-chart-pullback}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-complex-sheaf-stalk-whole-germ-equivalence,hir:actual-original-holomorphic-map-whole-germ-pullback}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceOpenRestriction.actualNativeStalk_chart_pullback}
\leanok
For an actual holomorphic map $f:M\to N$, the original native locally
ringed stalk pullback agrees, under the actual whole stalk/chart ring
equivalences, with the independently constructed actual holomorphic
chart-germ pullback. This equality holds for every original target
stalk element, with the original scalar and numerator maps.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Represent an arbitrary original target stalk element by a genuine
holomorphic section $s$ on an original open $V$ containing $f(x)$.
The native stalk-pullback germ formula sends it to the germ of the
literal section pullback on $f^{-1}V$. The actual source chart map sends
this germ to the translated chart germ of that pulled-back section's
original extension. The actual target chart map sends the original
germ to the translated chart germ of the original extension of $s$.
The independently proved chart-germ pullback formula sends that
numerator to its actual composition with $f$. On the genuine open
$f^{-1}V$, this composition equals the original extension of the
literal pulled-back section pointwise. Their original function germs,
and hence their translated chart germs, are equal. Thus both actual
maps agree on every genuine section representative and on the entire
target stalk.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual open-subspace inclusion has isomorphic whole quotient stalks]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-open-stalk-iso}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-native-chart-pullback,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-holomorphic-map,hir:actual-any-subset-open-whole-vanishing-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceOpenRestriction.actualOpenSubspaceMap,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceOpenRestriction.actualOpenSubspaceStalkEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceOpenRestriction.actualOpenSubspaceMap_stalk_apply,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceOpenRestriction.actualOpenSubspaceMap_stalk_isIso}
\leanok
The genuine holomorphic original open inclusion $U\hookrightarrow M$
constructs an actual locally ringed map from the model of $C_U$ to the
model of $C$. Every stalk map of this same actual subspace morphism is
an isomorphism of the entire original quotient local rings.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual open-inclusion chart-germ pullback is the identity on the
same original preferred-chart germ ring. The preceding compatibility
therefore identifies its native ambient stalk map, in both actual chart
equivalences, with that identity. The existing genuine open vanishing
transport theorem, proved by actual restriction and actual local zero
extension, identifies the entire original chart vanishing ideal for
$C_U$ with the entire original chart ideal for $C$. Thus the identity
induces an internally constructed isomorphism of their whole chart
quotients. Compose it with the two actual quotient-subspace stalk/chart
equivalences to obtain a bijection between the actual subspace stalks.
The proved actual subspace pullback formula on all original ambient
numerators and the native-chart compatibility identify the actual
subspace stalk map with this bijection. The original target inclusion
stalk is surjective, so these numerator identities cover every target
subspace stalk element. Hence the actual stalk map itself is an
isomorphism; no stalk-isomorphism certificate was an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual open quotient models are the genuine open restriction]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-open-restriction-iso}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-subspace-open-stalk-iso,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-holomorphic-map}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceOpenRestriction.actualOpenSubspaceMap_base_isOpenEmbedding,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceOpenRestriction.actualOpenSubspaceMap_isOpenImmersion,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceOpenRestriction.actualOpenSubspaceRestrictionIso,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceOpenRestriction.actualOpenSubspaceRestrictionIso_inclusion}
\leanok
The same actual map from the model of $C_U$ to the model of $C$ is a
genuine open immersion. It internally constructs an actual isomorphism
from the independently constructed model of $C_U$ to the actual open
restriction of the model of $C$. Composing its isomorphism with the
literal restriction inclusion recovers the same actual holomorphic
subspace map, hence also its proved original ambient inclusion square.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The underlying map sends $\langle\langle x,x\in U\rangle,x\in C\rangle$
to $\langle x,x\in C\rangle$. It is the literal homeomorphic
identification with the open subset $\{x\in C:x\in U\}$ of the actual
subtype $C$, so it is an open embedding. Every actual stalk map is an
isomorphism by the preceding theorem. The genuine locally ringed open
immersion criterion therefore proves that this same actual map is an
open immersion. Its original universal restriction construction gives
the asserted isomorphism with the actual restriction along this open
embedding. The universal construction's actual inclusion identity
identifies its composite with the restriction inclusion with the
original actual subspace map. No overlap structure-sheaf map was supplied.
\end{proof}

These statements apply in particular to actual analytic subsets $C$ and
their original open pieces. They supply genuine open model maps needed
for an analytic atlas. Effective compatible atlas gluing and descent of
resolution centres and blowups remain further obligations.
