\section{Actual native manifold charts imply original ambient regularity}

The native chart is a genuine open immersion of locally ringed spaces over
our actual complex point. No derivative rank, ambient flattening, or equality
of the original subset with a parametrized image is supplied.

\begin{theorem}[Actual stalk surjectivity yields a regular local image]
\label{hir:actual-native-stalk-surjection-regular-local-image}
\uses{hir:actual-holomorphic-stalk-surjection-injective-derivative,rem:constant-rank-submanifold}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceRegularityConverse.exists_regular_local_image_of_stalk_surjective}
\leanok
Let $f:O\to V$ be holomorphic, for actual coordinate opens
$O\subset\mathbf C^d$ and $V\subset\mathbf C^n$. If its actual native
holomorphic stalk pullback is surjective at $x$, there is an actual open
$A\subset O$ containing $x$ such that every point of $f(A)$ is an original
regular point of dimension $d$ of this same image in $\mathbf C^n$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual open extension of the coordinate expression of $f$; it is
holomorphic at every point of the actual source open. The preceding genuine
local-left-inverse theorem makes its actual derivative injective at $x$.
The set of injective continuous linear maps from a finite-dimensional space
is open. Holomorphy on the actual source open gives continuous derivative
there. Intersecting with the inverse image of that open set gives a genuine
source neighbourhood on which every derivative is injective. Its actual
range dimension is therefore $d$ at every point, by the injective linear
range dimension theorem. Apply the genuine constant-rank image theorem to
this actual original map and open set. That theorem chooses complementary
linear coordinates, constructs its actual graph parametrization, and uses
the holomorphic inverse function theorem to flatten the image. It supplies
an actual smaller source open $A$ containing $x$ and original regularity
at every point of its same actual image. All values agree with the original
$f$ on $A$; no global holomorphic extension is asserted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine holomorphic model chart forces original subset regularity]
\label{hir:actual-holomorphic-model-chart-original-regularity}
\uses{hir:actual-native-stalk-surjection-regular-local-image,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-holomorphic-map,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-univ-native-manifold}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceRegularityConverse.regularAtDim_of_holomorphic_model_open_immersion}
\leanok
Let $C\subset V$, and let $f:O\to V$ be holomorphic with $f(O)\subset C$.
If the actual induced whole quotient-subspace map from the whole model on
$O$ to the actual model on $C$ is an open immersion, then $C$ is an original
regular subset of dimension $d$ at $f(x)$ for every $x\in O$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual whole-manifold model inclusion is an isomorphism, and an actual
open immersion induces isomorphisms on its genuine stalks. The actual
inclusion of the model on $C$ has surjective stalk map. Its whole commuting
stalk square with the same $f$ therefore makes the native stalk map of
$f$ surjective: lift a source germ through the open-immersion stalk
isomorphism and the genuine ambient inclusion, and cancel the injective
whole-manifold inclusion stalk. The preceding theorem gives an actual
smaller parameter open $A$ and original regularity of its actual image.
The actual model map is an open topological embedding, so the image of this
same open $A$ is open in the actual subtype $C$. Subtype topology supplies
an actual ambient open whose intersection with $C$ is exactly this same
whole image. Including the coordinate open $V$ in $\mathbf C^n$ preserves
openness and this exact equality. Original regularity is local under true
intersection equality on an ambient open neighbourhood. Transfer the image
regularity across that equality and then through the actual open subtype
$V$. This proves regularity of the original $C$, rather than merely of a
chosen image contained in $C$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Any intrinsic native manifold chart implies original regularity]
\label{hir:actual-intrinsic-manifold-chart-original-regularity}
\uses{hir:actual-holomorphic-model-chart-original-regularity,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-intrinsic-local-holomorphic-lift,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-univ-native-manifold,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-open-restriction-iso}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticSubspaceRegularityConverse.regularAtDim_of_native_manifold_chart}
\leanok
Let $j$ be any actual native locally ringed open immersion from the native
holomorphic manifold on $O\subset\mathbf C^d$ to the constructed model of
$C\subset V\subset\mathbf C^n$. Assume only its genuine structural triangle
over the actual complex point. Then $C$ is an original regular subset of
dimension $d$ at the original point $j(x)$ for every $x\in O$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Precompose $j$ with the internally constructed whole-manifold model
isomorphism. The structural triangle makes the composite an actual
complex-point-compatible morphism between our genuine quotient models.
The intrinsic local ambient lifting theorem constructs, near $x$, a true
holomorphic $f:T\to V$ carrying the whole source into $C$, and proves the
entire locally ringed map square with this same original $j$. The actual
open restriction of the source model, the whole-manifold isomorphism, and
$j$ are open immersions; their composite is one. The whole proved map
square therefore makes the induced model map of the constructed $f$ an
open immersion. If this true source open is nested inside $O$, identify it with its actual
open image in $\mathbf C^d$: the literal inclusion and the unique subtype
representative are inverse holomorphic maps. Their genuine whole-model
isomorphism transports the same model map to that actual coordinate open.
Apply the preceding theorem there. Evaluating the same whole
square on the actual original source point identifies $f(x)$ with $j(x)$,
so its conclusion is exactly the asserted original regularity. No ambient
extension, chart square, rank or flattening certificate is input.
\end{proof}

Together with the original-regularity-to-native-chart construction this is
a presentation-independent local regularity dictionary. Resolution towers,
their descent, and a terminal resolution driver are separate results.
