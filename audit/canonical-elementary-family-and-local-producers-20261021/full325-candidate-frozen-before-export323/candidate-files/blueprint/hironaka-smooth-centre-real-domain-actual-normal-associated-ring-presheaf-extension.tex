\section{The literal section graded ring and its actual whole stalk map}

Let $A$ be the original germ ideal and $C$ any subset of the original complex
manifold. On a true open $U$ write $R(U)$ for the actual holomorphic section
ring, $I(U)$ for all actual ideal sections of $A$, and $J(U)$ for all actual
sections vanishing on $C$. Put
\[
 L_q(U)=\operatorname{im}\bigl(J(U)^q\longrightarrow
 R(U)/(I(U)+J(U)^{q+1})\bigr),\qquad
 G(U)=\bigoplus_{q\geq0} L_q(U).
\]
These literal section rings and powers define the operations before any
stalk comparison. This chapter constructs a ring presheaf and its entire
actual stalk homomorphism; ring sheafification and its comparison with the
previous module sheaf are subsequent constructions.

\begin{lemma}[True layer products commute with original open restrictions]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-section-layer-product-restriction}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-intrinsic-layer-product-independent,hir:real-domain-actual-normal-layer-section-presheaf,hir:real-domain-actual-centre-power-section-presheaf}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalLayerRestrict_mul}
\leanok
For every true inclusion $U\subseteq V$, every $p,q\geq0$, and arbitrary
$x\in L_p(V)$, $z\in L_q(V)$, the actual restriction sends the genuine
product $xz\in L_{p+q}(V)$ to the product of the actual restrictions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose actual representatives $s\in J(V)^p$ and $t\in J(V)^q$ of $x$ and
$z$. The previously constructed layer product is the class of $st$ in
$L_{p+q}(V)$: its representative independence follows from
$(I+J^{p+1})J^q\subseteq I+J^{p+q+1}$ and the corresponding containment
with $p,q$ interchanged. True restriction is a ring homomorphism and sends
each actual power into the same power on $U$. Thus restriction of $st$ is
exactly $s|_U t|_U$. The actual layer restriction class formulas identify
its class with the product of the two restricted classes. Every layer
has such an actual representative, so this proves the assertion for all
$x,z$, including degree zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The genuine graded ring presheaf on all original opens]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-section-ring-presheaf}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-section-layer-product-restriction,hir:relative-normal-intrinsic-graded-algebra,hir:real-domain-actual-normal-layer-section-presheaf}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSection,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSectionCommRing,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSectionAlgebra,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingRestrict,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingRestrict_of,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSection_of_mul_of}
\leanok
The genuine finite-support section direct sum $G(U)$ carries its intrinsic
commutative graded ring and $R(U)$-algebra structures. The actual degree
restrictions extend to a genuine ring restriction $G(V)\to G(U)$, keeping
every degree inclusion. These rings and restrictions form a genuine ring
presheaf on the original manifold. Its multiplication on actual degree
classes is $\iota_p[s]\,\iota_q[t]=\iota_{p+q}[st]$.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Use the existing intrinsic representative product on each actual section
layer. Representative independence supplies the graded associative,
commutative and distributive laws; its degree-zero class of $1$ is the
unit. The finite-support direct sum therefore has the intrinsic graded
commutative ring structure. Send each degree summand by its actual layer
restriction followed by the same degree inclusion. The finite-support
additive universal property gives one additive map on the entire direct
sum. The first lemma proves its multiplicative law on two arbitrary
homogeneous elements. Distributing finite sums extends this law to every
pair of direct-sum elements. The actual degree-zero representative $1$
restricts to $1$, so the map is unital. Equality of two additive maps out
of a direct sum is determined on every degree inclusion. Actual layer
restriction identity and composition therefore give whole ring
restriction identity and composition, proving the presheaf laws. The
intrinsic original homogeneous product formula gives the stated class
formula in every degree.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual ambient sections act through the degree-zero ring map]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-section-ambient-ring-map}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-section-ring-presheaf,hir:relative-normal-intrinsic-graded-algebra}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingRestrict_algebraMap,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedAmbientRingMap}
\leanok
The true section algebra maps $R(U)\to G(U)$ form a morphism of ring
presheaves from the literal original structure-ring presheaf. Each map
sends $r$ to its true degree-zero class, and actual ring restrictions
commute with these maps.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The intrinsic section algebra map is exactly the degree-zero inclusion
of the quotient class of $r\in J(U)^0=R(U)$. The whole ring restriction
preserves this inclusion, and the actual power-class restriction sends
$r$ to its literal structure-ring restriction. Hence the two composites
are equal on every $r$, which is precisely naturality. This also proves
the actual degree-zero formula without defining the algebra through a
stalk equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole actual ring stalk has its genuine original graded ring map]
\label{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-ring-stalk-whole-map}

\uses{hir:real-domain-actual-normal-associated-section-ring-presheaf,hir:real-domain-actual-normal-layer-whole-germ-map,hir:relative-normal-intrinsic-graded-algebra}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualWholeNormalAssociatedRing,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualWholeNormalAssociatedRingCommRing,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalLayerSectionGermHom_mul,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSectionGermHom,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingSectionGermHom_of,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingGermCocone,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingStalkToWholeHom,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingStalkToWholeHom_germ,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainActualNormalAssociatedRingPresheaf.actualNormalAssociatedRingStalkToWholeHom_power_class}
\leanok
For every original point $y$, the genuine neighbourhood colimit ring
stalk of $G$ has an internally constructed unital ring homomorphism to
$\bigoplus_q L_q(R_y,I_y,J_y)$, where $R_y$ is the actual original
structure-sheaf stalk and $I_y,J_y$ are the whole original stalk ideals.
Every actual homogeneous section germ maps to its SAME original power
germ class. No coherence, prescribed stalk map, bijection, or global
extension is an input to this ring homomorphism construction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual section germ of the original structure ring is a ring
homomorphism and sends each true section power into its whole original
stalk power. For arbitrary section layer classes choose actual power
representatives $s,t$. The previously proved whole layer germ formulas
send them to the classes of the actual germs of $s,t$. Since the actual
structure-ring germ sends $st$ to the product of these germs, the layer
germ maps preserve the genuine homogeneous products. The degree-zero
class of $1$ maps to the whole original degree-zero class of $1$.
Extend these additive layer germ maps to the genuine entire direct sum.
The same homogeneous multiplication and finite-sum argument gives a
unital ring homomorphism for every actual neighbourhood of $y$.
Restriction followed by this homomorphism equals the original larger
neighbourhood homomorphism on every homogeneous section class by the
true section-germ restriction identity. Whole direct-sum equality
follows, giving an actual ring cocone. The genuine neighbourhood-colimit
universal property supplies the claimed whole stalk ring homomorphism.
Its actual colimit-inclusion identity gives the formula for every
original section germ, hence for every homogeneous power class.
\end{proof}
