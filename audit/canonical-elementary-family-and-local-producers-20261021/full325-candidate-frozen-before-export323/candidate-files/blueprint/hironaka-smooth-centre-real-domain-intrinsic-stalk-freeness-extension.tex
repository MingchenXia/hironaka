\section{Intrinsic original relative stalk freeness through actual chart overlaps}

The entire original membership ideal is used in each actual translated
chart. Whole centre quotients act only on the genuine associated layers,
by their already independently descended ambient multiplication. Actual

\begin{lemma}[Entire canonical relative modules transport through true ring equivalences]
\label{hir:real-domain-all-relative-degrees-free-ring-equivalence}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:nonlinear-centre-canonical-scalars-and-whole-sum}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainIntrinsicStalkFreeness.allRelativeIdealDegreesFree_of_ringEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainIntrinsicStalkFreeness.allRelativeIdealDegreesFree_ringEquiv_iff}
\leanok
Let $R,S$ be commutative rings, $I,J\subset R$, and let $E:R\simeq S$
be a ring equivalence with $I'=E(I)$ and $J'=E(J)$. Then the ENTIRE
associated sum is free and flat over $R/J$, and EVERY genuine relative
power layer is finite free and flat over $R/J$, if and only if the same
properties hold over $S/J'$ for $I',J'$. All actions are the original
independently descended centre quotient actions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual ring map gives the centre quotient equivalence $R/J\simeq S/J'$.
On the whole truncation quotient $R/(I+J^{q+1})$ it gives the true quotient
ring equivalence to $S/(I'+J'^{q+1})$. Its restriction to the image of $J^q$
gives the actual additive equivalence of the genuine relative degree $q$
layers. The established representative formula says that the image of an
original class of $g\in J^q$ is the class of $E(g)$. Therefore the image of
the original centre scalar class of $r$ acting on that class is the class
of $E(rg)=E(r)E(g)$; this is precisely the independently descended target
action of its centre scalar class. It is a semilinear equivalence for the
true centre quotient ring equivalence. Assemble these maps componentwise
on the entire direct sum; every finite-support inclusion is retained and
the same scalar formula holds in each degree. These genuine semilinear
equivalences transport module freeness and finite generation; finite
generation is claimed degreewise only. Free modules are flat under the
same exact additive and scalar instances. Thus the forward properties
hold for ALL degrees and the whole sum. Apply the identical argument to
$E^{-1}$, using the actual inverse ideal-map identities, for the reverse
implication. No centre quotient action is asserted on a higher truncation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual original centre-stalk freeness is independent of the translated chart]
\label{hir:real-domain-original-relative-stalk-freeness-chart-independent}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-domain-all-relative-degrees-free-ring-equivalence,hir:actual-translated-chart-whole-stalk-transition}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainIntrinsicStalkFreeness.originalRelativeStalkFreeAt,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainIntrinsicStalkFreeness.original_relative_stalk_degrees_free_chart_iff,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainIntrinsicStalkFreeness.originalRelativeStalkFreeAt_iff_fixedChart}
\leanok
For ANY original germ ideal $A$ and centre $C$, at an actual common original
point $y$ of two preferred chart domains based at $x$ and $x'$, the entire
relative associated sum is free and flat and ALL its genuine layers are
finite free and flat over the original fixed-chart centre quotient in
one chart if and only if these SAME properties hold in the other chart.
In particular the statement computed in the chart based at $y$ is intrinsic
to the original point and has this exact meaning in every actual chart
containing that point. No whole-ideal or scalar-action dictionary is
supplied by the caller.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual translated original overlap map whose local coordinates
are $e_{x'}(e_x^{-1}(z+e_x(y)))-e_{x'}(y)$ and its genuine inverse. Its
source and target are the true translated overlaps; its original chart
inverse identities make it fix zero and produce the whole holomorphic
germ algebra equivalence. The existing entire original membership-germ
formula maps each germ of each original holomorphic function in $A$ to
the germ of that SAME function in the other chart. The corresponding
whole ideal image equality therefore holds. Apply the same formula to
all original functions in the actual centre vanishing ideal to obtain
the whole centre ideal image equality, without a coordinate plane premise.
These TWO actual whole dictionaries supply both hypotheses of the preceding
canonical relative-module transport theorem. Apply it in each direction.
The chart based at $y$ always contains $y$, so its computed predicate has
exactly the same truth value in every actual chart containing $y$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original regular-centre inputs supply intrinsic all-degree stalk freeness on one real centre open]

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreRealDomainIntrinsicStalkFreeness.exists_original_regular_intrinsic_all_stalk_degrees_free_centre_open}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual original centre-open producer internally gives one true fixed

\end{proof}
