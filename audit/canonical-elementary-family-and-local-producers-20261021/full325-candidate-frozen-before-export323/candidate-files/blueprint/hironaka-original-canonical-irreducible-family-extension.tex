\begin{definition}[The actual connected proper branch and full-dimensional identity branch]

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.ConnectedPureCanonicalFamily,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.FullDimensionCanonicalFamily}
\leanok

\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The same proper-dimensional canonical prefix has the original connected carrier]
\label{hir:original-canonical-irreducible-proper-connected}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-irreducible-family-results,hir:original-compact-pure-fixed-target-independent-family}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.connected_pure_canonical_family}
\leanok
For an original compact closed irreducible analytic subvariety with
$\dim V<n$, the full connected proper-dimensional result holds on
any ambient complex manifold. No compactness of the ambient or any
smooth source is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the genuine fixed-target compact pure canonical producer on the
original carrier, its actual pure regular density and the empty
original boundary. Retain exactly its chosen terminal history,
stopped prefix, tail and entire later source family. Its actual
exceptional image lies in $V_{\rm sing}$, by the proved whole
exceptional and born-boundary inclusions. The genuine irreducible
strict-transform connectedness theorem for finite actual blowup
sequences applies on arbitrary ambient manifolds. Combining it with
the SAME prefix's closed strict carrier and actual pointwise
$\dim V$ regularity gives the original closed connected submanifold.
The producer's empty-boundary equalities, with the literal definition
of the irreducible singular locus, give the two exact exceptional
identities for this SAME prefix. All original source fields are
retained unchanged, so the target precedes every smooth source.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The full-dimensional original canonical branch is the actual identity]

exceptional locus, empty boundary union and inverse singular locus
literally. No zero vanishing-ideal domain or nonempty source
assumption is introduced.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All-dimensional original irreducible canonical elementary resolution]
\label{hir:original-canonical-irreducible-all-dimensions}
\uses{hir:original-canonical-irreducible-proper-connected,hir:original-canonical-irreducible-full-identity}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.exists_original_irreducible_canonical_family,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalIrreducibleFamily.exists_original_irreducible_canonical_family_compactSpace}
\leanok
Every original compact irreducible analytic subvariety has its actual
canonical elementary family, in the proper-dimensional or the
full-dimensional branch as appropriate. Both branches retain all
original geometric fields, including connectedness and exact
empty-boundary exceptional equalities, and arbitrary smooth-source
compatibility. On a compact ambient manifold no carrier compactness
premise is needed. No proper-dimension hypothesis replaces the
original all-dimensional statement.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The intrinsic subvariety dimension bound gives either $\dim V<n$
or $\dim V=n$. In the first case apply the genuine compact pure
canonical connected producer; in the second apply the literal actual
identity producer. These construct the stated branch from the original
data and retain their entire source families. On a compact ambient
manifold the original closed carrier is compact, supplying the sole
compactness input of the proper branch internally.
\end{proof}
