\section{Original geometry supplies a weighted partition of the same full maximum}

\begin{theorem}[An internally chosen full centre has actual finite member marks]
\label{hir:sharp-original-full-maximum-weighted-partition}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFullMaximumWeightedPartition.exists_actual_full_maximum_weighted_partition}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-full-maximum-original-member-orders,hir:sharp-actual-finite-member-order-centre-partition,hir:sharp-actual-finite-member-order-weight-admissibility}
Let $W$ be a compact smooth carrier of positive pure dimension in a
positive-dimensional complex manifold. Let $E$ be an ambient SNC list,
with $W$ dense away from its union. At every actual finite test prefix
for the empty-boundary carrier template, suppose the actual
chronological FIRST defect is nonempty. Original geometry then supplies
one nonempty entire FULL maximum centre $C$ and a natural number $q$.
For the unchanged actual normalised chronological members $F_i$ on
the current carrier $N$, the relative product has order $q$ everywhere
on $C$. The entire pieces
\[
 C_\alpha=\{x\in C: \operatorname{ord}_{N,x}F_i=\alpha_i\ (\forall i)\},
 \qquad \alpha\in\{0,\ldots,q\}^{|E|},
\]
form a finite disjoint cover of $C$. Every piece is simultaneously
admissible for the actual current product, the carrier template, and
each actual member $F_i$ marked by $\alpha_i$ with the entire unchanged
current born boundary. All member orders on a piece equal these literal
marks.
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the actual FULL maximum centre once using the original geometric
producer, retaining its nonemptiness and both admissibilities. Choose a
point $c\in C$ and let $q$ be the ordinary order of the actual relative
product at $c$. Any two points of the FULL maximum have the same full
canonical value. Its true first coordinate, followed by the actual
nonmonomial/ordinary product dictionary, shows that the product order
at every $x\in C$ equals $q$.

The actual test-prefix geometry supplies smoothness of the current
carrier and coherence, relative principality and nonzeroness of every
unchanged chronological member. It also supplies coherence of the
actual current product and the carrier-template ideal. Apply the finite
order-strata theorem to this very family and $C$ for each of the two
marked ideals. Both applications define the same literal pieces, so
their admissibilities coexist with one cover and disjointness proof.
Apply the weighted-member theorem to the current product's unchanged
boundary; it gives simultaneous individual admissibility on every
whole piece. Exact member orders are their defining equations.

The entire canonical centre remains $C$. This partition justifies local
weighted calculations on its closed pieces; it does not replace the
canonical step by arbitrarily chosen sequential partial blowups. No
original ambient strict-trace comparison or modified-invariant descent
is asserted.
\end{proof}
