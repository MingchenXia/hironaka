\begin{theorem}[A true original normal power spreads actual principal cosupport]
\label{hir:actual-coefficient-normal-power-cosupport-open}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-power-spread-actual-recursive-open,hir:order-taylor,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-polynomial-truncations}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientCosupportSpread.exists_actual_principal_cosupport_open,Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientCosupportSpread.exists_actual_finite_principal_cosupport_open}
\leanok
If the genuine germ of a holomorphic function $c$ lies in the entire normal ideal power $(y)^q$ at zero, some genuine open neighbourhood $U$ of zero has $U\cap\{y=0\}$ contained in the cosupport of the actual principal germ ideal $(c)$ marked by $q$.
Any finite family with its own individual true powers admits one such common open domain for every member and mark.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine normal-power dictionary gives a recursive expression of $c$ in products of $q$ actual normal coordinates and holomorphic germs near zero. The proved recursive normal-power spread supplies a true open neighbourhood on which this same expression is valid at every point. At a point of the normal plane, each actual normal coordinate lies in that point's actual maximal ideal, so the proved functional normal-power to ideal-power theorem puts $c$ in its $q$th power. Every member of the actual principal ideal is a local holomorphic combination of $c$ and therefore lies in this same maximal power; this is the genuine whole principal cosupport condition. For finitely many members intersect these actual open neighbourhoods. The resulting finite intersection is open, contains zero, and retains every individual mark, including zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same actual vertex coefficients inherit their true difference powers]
\label{hir:actual-coefficient-vertex-difference-powers}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual Taylor map sends each genuine whole centre power into the true formal coordinate centre power. The literal formal coefficient criterion for that centre gives precisely the stated difference power for every low normal coefficient. The prescribed family's actual coefficient-function Taylor identity identifies that series with the Taylor expansion of the identical original coefficient germ. Genuine whole formal/analytic normal-power contraction returns membership in the original analytic base ring. No point-value test or coefficient order assumption replaces that whole membership.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One common shrink retains every original generator and coefficient function]
\label{hir:actual-coefficient-same-family-cosupport-shrink}

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Index the actual finite low coefficients by the genuine finite vertex labels and their low normal exponent indices. Retain the original generator and coefficient functions verbatim; restrict their actual holomorphy to this smaller open. Their genuine principal ideals are coherent there by the actual one-generator coherence theorem, and every remaining original vertex datum and Taylor identity is unchanged. Thus the record with only its domain shrunk has the asserted same-family and all-point cosupport properties.
\end{proof}
