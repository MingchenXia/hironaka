\section{A finite actual deep-product phase preserving every original good point}

The ambient dimension is positive. Let $W$ be a closed smooth carrier of fixed
pure dimension at most the ambient dimension, let $E$ be the entire original
ordered SNC boundary, and assume $W\setminus\bigcup E$ is dense in $W$.
The auxiliary original product has empty initial exceptional boundary. The
statements below retain its genuine original test history, without resetting
the full invariant at the current object. They concern a finite deep-product
phase, not the complete sharp normal-crossings resolution.

\begin{theorem}[Terminal original marking bounds the actual chronological product]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-terminal-marking-chronological-order}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-original-controlled-history-all-exceptional-residual}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFiniteDeepPhase.terminal_original_marking_bounds_actual_product}
For every positive mark $q$ and every genuine geometric resolution of the
same original marking-$q$ product, the actual chronological member product
has ordinary trace order strictly less than $q$ at every point of the final
carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine original test history induces the same-map geometric template
history. The exact whole controlled-history residual identity identifies the
actual chronological member product with the nonmonomial part of the actual
controlled product, along the same final carrier and its entire born boundary.
A coherent ideal is contained in this nonmonomial part: multiply any original
germ by a monomial-part germ and retain its original ideal membership before
passing to the restricted span. Therefore the marking-$q$ cosupport of the
nonmonomial part lies in that of the controlled product. The latter is empty
at a geometric resolution. Coherence and nonzero restriction of the actual
chronological product identify its marking-$q$ cosupport with the ordinary
trace-order superlevel. Hence its ordinary trace order is strictly below $q$.
\leanok
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite deep phase with all original good points preserved]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-finite-deep-product-phase}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-terminal-marking-chronological-order,hir:principalization-compact-support}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFiniteDeepPhase.exists_actual_finite_deep_phase}
If the original marking-$(|E|+1)$ cosupport is compact, there is a finite
canonical resolution of this same original marked product. Its actual
chronological product has trace order at most $|E|$ everywhere on the final
carrier. Every centre lies over the original sharp bad locus. The composite
is proper and is an isomorphism over the entire original good locus. The
actual final template carrier is a closed smooth submanifold transverse to
its entire born SNC boundary.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original coherence, nonzero restriction, pure carrier dimension and positive
mark put this original product in the canonical invariant domain. The
compact-cosupport canonical driver constructs a finite original test history
with empty terminal controlled cosupport. Apply the previous theorem with
mark $|E|+1$. Every centre of an original coherent test history lies over its
original cosupport. The actual original deep-product inclusion places that
cosupport in the original first defect, which lies in the original sharp bad
locus. Thus the sequence has all centres over that same fixed bad locus.
Its admissible-sequence calculus places its exceptional locus over that bad
locus and makes its composite an isomorphism over the complement, which is
exactly the original good locus. A finite blowup sequence is proper.
The same-map template history is well formed; its final carrier is smooth,
its full born boundary is SNC and the carrier is transverse to that boundary.
These are born-boundary assertions; admissibility against every strict
original boundary member at later stages is a separate obligation.
\leanok
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Terminal literal first-defect erasure for at most one original member]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-finite-one-member-first-erasure}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-finite-deep-product-phase}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFiniteDeepPhase.deep_phase_erases_first_defect_of_length_le_one,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFiniteDeepPhase.exists_actual_finite_one_member_first_erasure}
If $|E|\leq1$, every terminal deep-phase history above has empty literal
actual chronological distinct defect. In particular the compact original
deep cosupport produces such a finite history, preserving every original
good point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The terminal actual product order is at most $|E|\leq1$. Each actual
chronological factor is coherent, nonzero on the actual carrier and locally
principal there, as supplied by the true original chronological geometry.
The actual small-order dictionary applies to this entire finite family:
additivity forces at most one proper position; the quotient map is injective
on such a set, so its genuine proper-class image count equals the actual
product order. The strict inequality defining the actual distinct defect is
therefore impossible at every carrier point. Outside the carrier the defect
has no points by definition. The preceding finite-phase existence theorem
now gives the claimed history. Joint normal crossings of the strict original
boundary with the born boundary, recursive higher intersection phases and
the general sharp terminal endpoint are not inferred from this first-defect
erasure.
\leanok
\end{proof}
