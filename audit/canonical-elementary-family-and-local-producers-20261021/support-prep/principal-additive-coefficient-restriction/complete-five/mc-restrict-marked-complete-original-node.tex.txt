\begin{definition}[Restriction of a marked ideal to a set]
\label{mc:restrict-marked}
\uses{hir:marked-ideal,hir:order,mc:strict-along,mc:coefficient-ideal}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.withN,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.withN_N,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.withN_E,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.withN_ideal,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.withN_d,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.cosupp_withN,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.CentresIn,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.centresIn_refl,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.centresIn_step_iff,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.N_geoTransformAlong_withN,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.N_coeffMarked,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.CoefficientIdealRestriction.isCoherent_coeffMarked}
\leanok
For a set \(S\subset M\), the \emph{restriction} \(\underline{\mathcal B}|_S=(M,S,E,\mathfrak b,d)\) has the same \(E\), ideal and mark and \(N=S\); its cosupport is
\(\{x\in S\mid \operatorname{ord}_x(\mathfrak b|_S)\ge d\}\) (Definition~\ref{hir:order}). A tower \(T\) has its \emph{centres in the strict transforms of \(S\)} if
each centre \(C_i\) lies in the strict transform \(S_i\) of \(S\) (Definition~\ref{mc:strict-along}). The coefficient ideal \(\mathcal C(\underline{\mathcal I})\)
has \(N=M\) and a coherent ideal.
\end{definition}
