\section{Actual point-normalized coefficients retain their literal source bands}

Every series below belongs to the actual full formal ring. The point chart
$(e,b)$ is arbitrary, and the exceptional quotient equation uses its literal
blowup substitution. Write $P_d(F)$ for the actual finite degree-$d$
source polynomial.

\begin{theorem}[Every actual pivot coefficient is the literal dehomogeneous source-band coefficient]
\label{hir:actual-point-normalized-all-pivot-coefficient-bands}
\uses{hir:actual-point-exceptional-restriction-dehomogenized-polynomial,hir:actual-point-homogeneous-remainder-next-power,hir:actual-formal-blowup-normalized-member}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointNormalizedCoefficientBands.normalized_point_coefficient_band}
\leanok
Let $F\in\mathfrak m^w$ and let the genuine quotient satisfy
$e^wH=\pi_b^*F$. For every $r\ge0$ and every pivot-free exponent
$\gamma$,
\[
 [X^{\gamma+r\mathbf1_e}]H
   =[X^\gamma]\operatorname{dehom}_{e,b} P_{w+r}(F).
\]
The source numerator, actual quotient and chart are retained for all labels.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual source component and remainder theorem gives
$F=P_w(F)+F_1$ with $F_1\in\mathfrak m^{w+1}$.
Its genuine exceptional quotient $Q$ exists internally. Actual homogeneous
blowup factorization gives
$\pi_b^*P_w(F)=e^w\operatorname{dehom}_{e,b}P_w(F)$.
Apply the literal substitution to the whole source decomposition and cancel
the nonzero actual exceptional power in the formal domain. This yields
\[
 H=\operatorname{dehom}_{e,b}P_w(F)+eQ.
\]
The finite dehomogeneous polynomial is pivot free: every substituted
variable is either $1$ or $b_i+X_i$ with $i\ne e$.

Induct on $r$. At $r=0$, the $eQ$ coefficient at a pivot-free exponent is
zero, so the displayed decomposition gives the assertion. At $r+1$, its
finite polynomial coefficient is zero because that exponent has positive
pivot value. The genuine monomial coefficient shift leaves the coefficient
at $\gamma+r\mathbf1_e$ of $Q$. Apply the induction hypothesis to
the actual numerator $F_1$, its power $w+1$ and its SAME quotient $Q$.
The removed degree-$w$ source component has no coefficient in degree
$w+1+r$, so its actual degree polynomial is exactly $P_{w+1+r}(F)$.
This proves the formula at $r+1$. All cancellations and component
equalities occur in the actual whole series ring.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual current powers and factors identify whole projected leading bands]
\label{hir:actual-current-point-factor-source-band-dictionary}
\uses{hir:actual-point-normalized-all-pivot-coefficient-bands,hir:actual-exceptional-pivot-projection-kernel-powers,hir:actual-formal-coordinate-power-coefficient-kernel}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointNormalizedCoefficientBands.dehom_band_coefficients_zero_of_current_power,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointNormalizedCoefficientBands.projected_current_factor_eq_source_band}
\leanok
Retain the same actual quotient and let $r\le q$. If $H\in\mathfrak m^q$,
every band of $\operatorname{dehom}_{e,b}P_{w+r}(F)$ below degree
$q-r$ vanishes. Independently, if its actual current degree-$q$ component factors as
$H_q=e^rL$, then
\[
 \rho_e(L)=
 \bigl(\operatorname{dehom}_{e,b}P_{w+r}(F)\bigr)_{q-r}
\]
as whole actual series.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a pivot-free exponent $\gamma$ of degree below $q-r$, the preceding
formula identifies the source-band coefficient with the actual coefficient
of $H$ at $\gamma+r\mathbf1_e$, whose degree is below $q$.
The genuine maximal-power coefficient characterization makes it zero.
At an exponent with positive pivot value, every dehomogeneous polynomial
coefficient is already zero. This proves all lower-band vanishing.

For the factor identity, genuine monomial multiplication in
$H_q=e^rL$ implies that $L$ is homogeneous of degree $q-r$.
Check every coefficient. At positive pivot exponent both sides vanish by
literal pivot projection and dehomogeneous pivot freeness. At pivot-free
degree unequal to $q-r$, both vanish by homogeneity. At pivot-free degree
$q-r$, the actual coefficient shift identifies the coefficient of $L$
with that of $H_q$, hence of $H$, at the shifted exponent of degree $q$.
The preceding theorem identifies it with the literal source-band
coefficient. Thus every genuine coefficient agrees, proving equality of
the whole projected factor and the whole source band.
\end{proof}

These are actual coefficient and factor dictionaries. Whole current initial
membership and genuine source directional tangent stability are separate
geometric or algebraic producers; no desired order-retention conclusion is
assumed or asserted here.
