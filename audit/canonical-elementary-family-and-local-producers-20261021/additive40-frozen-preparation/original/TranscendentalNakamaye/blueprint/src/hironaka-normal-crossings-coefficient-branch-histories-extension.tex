\section{Actual old-branch and born-boundary histories of the coefficient phase}
\label{sec:nc-coefficient-branch-histories}

The old NC carrier is represented by genuine local branches; no global branch
labelling is imposed. The coefficient phase retains the original marked ideal
and every actual chronological step. Its old local branches can be put back
into its genuine marked test histories because every actual centre lies in
their common strict transform. The comparison is a test-history statement;
it does not assert equality with the old ordered canonical invariant.

\begin{theorem}[Literal entire old and born boundary decomposition]
\label{hir:nc-history-entire-old-born-boundary}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsCoefficientBranchHistories.entire_old_born_boundary}
\leanok
For an original marked record $M$ with empty boundary, any actual finite
boundary list $B$, and EVERY actual finite tower $T$, the WHOLE transformed
boundary of the record obtained by inserting $B$ is
\[
 (M[B])_T.E=\bigl(\operatorname{strict}_T(A)\bigr)_{A\in B}\mathbin{+\!+}M_T.E.
\]
This retains all original indices and all chronological empty entries.
\uses{hir:geo-transform}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At the empty tower the original boundary of $M$ is empty, so the displayed
identity is literal. At one actual blowup, transform every entry of the
previous whole list by its actual centre-relative strict transform and append
exactly the new exceptional inverse image. Mapping the inductive concatenation
gives the transformed original branch block followed by the transformed
born block; the appended new member belongs to the latter. Composition of
the strict transforms is precisely the tower strict transform. This proves
the literal ordered equality by induction, including empty old members and
empty centres.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual original NC charts give entire marked branch histories]
\label{hir:nc-highest-stratum-actual-local-branch-histories}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsCoefficientBranchHistories.exists_highest_stratum_local_branch_histories}
\leanok
Let $M=(X,\varnothing,\mathcal I,d)$ with coherent original ideal, and let an
actual NC carrier $H$ have branch multiplicity everywhere at most $r$.
Put $Z=\{\operatorname{mult}_H\ge r\}$. For ANY genuine $M$-test tower $T$
whose actual centres lie in the strict transforms of $Z$, every original
$x\in Z$ has an actual open chart $U$ and genuine SNC branch list $B$ with
$H|_U=\bigcup B$, such that the SAME actual restricted tower is a genuine test
tower of $(U,B,\mathcal I|_U,d)$. At EVERY prefix, the ENTIRE marked record is
well formed and its WHOLE boundary is exactly the old-branch strict block
followed by the SAME born-only block of the restricted original history.
No local history, local centre condition, or current SNC certificate is supplied.
\uses{hir:nc-history-entire-old-born-boundary,hir:nc-highest-subcentre-actual-branches,hir:general-restrict-open,hir:drop-containing-members,hir:wellformed-tower}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine original highest-stratum flattening supplies $U$ and its full
coordinate branch list $B$. It proves both $H|_U=\bigcup B$ and containment
of the ENTIRE original $Z|_U$ in every member of $B$. Restrict the actual
original test tower to $U$. Restricting the actual centre containments gives
centres in the literal strict transforms of $Z|_U$.

The original record with boundary $B$ is well formed: its carrier is the whole
open manifold, the original ideal pullback is coherent, and the actual branch
list is SNC. Apply the proved whole-history drop-containing-members equivalence
in the direction that ADDS the branch list to the restricted empty-boundary
history. Every required dropped-member containment was just derived from the
actual original chart. It follows that the SAME restricted tower, with its
SAME centres and maps, is a genuine branch-marked test history. Every prefix
inherits actual testness. The actual well-formed-transform theorem supplies
its complete current geometry, including SNC of the WHOLE boundary. The
preceding literal list identity identifies that boundary with exactly its
original branch strict transforms followed by ALL actual born members.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An original coefficient remainder phase has genuine local old-and-born SNC histories]
\label{hir:nc-original-coefficient-phase-local-branch-histories}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsCoefficientBranchHistories.exists_original_nc_coefficient_phase_branch_histories}
\leanok
For original coherent $\mathcal I$, positive mark, compact original cosupport,
and an actual NC carrier with the same highest-count bound, one internally
chosen finite coefficient nonzero-remainder phase has ALL the original
properties of the finite phase and also the actual local branch histories
of the preceding theorem at every original point of $Z$.
\uses{hir:nc-original-coefficient-remainder-finite-phase,hir:nc-highest-stratum-actual-local-branch-histories}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the original-only finite coefficient phase producer. It internally
constructs one actual finite remainder-canonical terminal tower and proves
its actual ORIGINAL marked testness and centres in the strict transforms of
$Z$, besides its entire final cosupport avoidance. Apply the preceding
actual chart-history construction to that SAME tower, at each original
point of $Z$. Retain its original canonical remainder, coefficient and
original test histories, whole controlled cosupport dictionary and terminal
remainder avoidance unchanged. Every additional local boundary and history
field has therefore been derived internally from the original data.
No selected-zero-component phase or strict global decrease is asserted.
\end{proof}
