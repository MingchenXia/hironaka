\section{The actual coefficient phase retains joint old and born histories}
\label{sec:boundary-coefficient-branch-histories}

The retained SNC boundary is the entire current born block. It is present in
both the canonical remainder input and the branch histories below. No old
boundary index is removed, and no global old multiplicity decrease is claimed.

\begin{lemma}[Literal old and retained-boundary concatenation at every position]
\label{hir:nc-entire-old-retained-born-boundary}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaBoundaryCoefficientBranchHistories.entire_old_retained_boundary}
\leanok
For any original marked record $M$, any finite old branch list $B$, and any
actual finite tower $T$, the entire boundary of $(M[E:=B+\!+M.E])_T$ is
$\operatorname{strict}_T(B)+\!+(M_T).E$. This includes every empty position
and requires no testness assumption.
\uses{hir:nc-history-entire-old-born-boundary}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At the empty tower this is the literal original concatenation. At an actual
next blowup, each entire previous block is mapped by the same geometric
strict transform, and the same exceptional member is appended. Mapping a
concatenation and associating the final append proves the identity. Induction
on the actual tower retains all ordered positions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original joint geometry yields actual branch histories with the whole retained boundary]
\label{hir:nc-highest-joint-retained-boundary-histories}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaBoundaryCoefficientBranchHistories.exists_highest_joint_branch_histories}
\leanok
Let $H$ be an original NC carrier adapted to an entire SNC boundary $E$,
with old branch count bounded by $r$. Let $M=(X,E,I,d)$ have coherent $I$.
For every actual original marked test tower $T$ whose centres lie in the
successive strict transforms of its highest stratum $Z$, and every $x\in Z$,
there are an internally chosen genuine neighbourhood $U$ and old branch list
$B$ with $H|_U=\bigcup B$ and $B+\!+E|_U$ SNC. The SAME restricted tower is
an actual test history for $(U,B+\!+E|_U,I|_U,d)$. Every actual finite prefix
is well formed and its ENTIRE boundary is the strict old block followed by
the SAME retained original-and-born boundary of $M|_U$ at that prefix.
\uses{hir:nc-original-highest-joint-branch-chart,hir:nc-entire-old-retained-born-boundary,hir:drop-containing-members}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the internally constructed original joint chart at $x$. Its entire
concatenation $B+\!+E|_U$ is genuinely SNC, its union of old branches is the
literal $H|_U$, and every old branch contains the whole $Z|_U$. The augmented
marked record is well formed: its carrier is the entire ambient open,
its ideal is the actual coherent pullback, and its whole boundary is the
proved joint SNC list. Restrict the given genuine $M$ history to $U$; its
centres remain in the successive strict transforms of $Z|_U$.

Apply the genuine whole-history drop-member equivalence with retained list
$E|_U$, rather than the empty list. Every retained member belongs to the
whole concatenation. A member of that concatenation absent from the retained
list must come from $B$ and therefore contains $Z|_U$. Thus all equivalence
hypotheses are derived from the actual original chart. Its ADD direction
proves the augmented history's testness from the already true restricted
original history. Actual testness passes to every prefix, whose well
formedness follows from the original augmented well-formed record. The
preceding literal concatenation identity gives every asserted whole boundary.
No joint-history certificate or special empty-boundary assumption is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Only original data produce the whole boundary phase with all joint histories]
\label{hir:nc-original-boundary-stratum-joint-histories}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaBoundaryCoefficientBranchHistories.exists_original_boundary_stratum_joint_histories}
\leanok
Let the original ideal be coherent with positive marking and compact original
cosupport. Let the actual old NC carrier be adapted to the whole retained
boundary, with positive highest old count $r$. Original data alone supply one
finite coefficient-stratum resolution, a genuine SAME original marked test
tower, all centres in strict $Z$, and original final cosupport disjoint from
strict $Z$. This SAME tower has all the preceding genuine joint branch
histories and whole-prefix identities at every original highest point.
\uses{hir:original-boundary-nc-whole-coefficient-stratum-resolution,hir:nc-highest-joint-retained-boundary-histories}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the actual original whole-boundary coefficient-stratum producer. It
internally supplies the finite tower, whole coefficient terminality, original
testness, true strict-$Z$ centre containment, and final original cosupport
clearance. Its canonical remainder input already contains the entire retained
boundary. Apply the preceding genuine joint-history construction to this SAME
tower at each original highest point. Every local branch list and every
whole-prefix property is then an output. The caller supplies no phase,
chosen centre, maximum, local history or terminality certificate. This does
not identify an older ordered invariant or lower the old count outside the
current permitted cosupport; open-support extension and iteration remain
separate obligations for general initial-NC resolution.
\end{proof}
