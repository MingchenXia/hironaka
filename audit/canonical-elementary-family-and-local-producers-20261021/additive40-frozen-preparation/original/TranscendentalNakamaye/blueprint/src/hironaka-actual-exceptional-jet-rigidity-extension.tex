\begin{theorem}[Actual whole jet ranks detect exceptional multiplication injectivity]
\label{hir:actual-exceptional-jet-rank-injectivity-converse}
\uses{hir:actual-exceptional-jet-exact-sequence-rank}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalJetRigidity.exceptionalJetMul_injective_of_dimension_add,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalJetRigidity.exceptional_point_colon_eq_of_jetMul_injective}
\leanok
For a whole actual formal ideal $I$, let $V_q=R/(I+\mathfrak m^q)$
and $W_{q+1}=R/(I+(x_e)+\mathfrak m^{q+1})$. If their genuine finite
ranks satisfy $\dim V_{q+1}=\dim V_q+\dim W_{q+1}$, then actual
multiplication by $x_e$ from $V_q$ to $V_{q+1}$ is injective and
$(I+\mathfrak m^{q+1}):x_e=I+\mathfrak m^q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual quotient onto $W_{q+1}$ is surjective and its entire kernel
is the multiplication range, independently of injectivity. Its genuine
rank-nullity equation therefore expresses $\dim V_{q+1}$ as
$\dim W_{q+1}$ plus the rank of that multiplication range. The disclosed
rank equality makes the latter rank equal to $\dim V_q$. Rank-nullity
for multiplication gives zero kernel dimension, and finite-dimensionality
makes its actual kernel zero. Hence the genuine map is injective.
If $x_eF\in I+\mathfrak m^{q+1}$, the actual image of $[F]$ is zero,
so injectivity puts $F$ in the original whole denominator
$I+\mathfrak m^q$. This proves the nontrivial colon inclusion. The
reverse inclusion follows directly by multiplying the ideal and power
summands by the actual coordinate. Thus the entire ideal equality holds.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The entire literal same-ring shadow profile characterizes all exceptional initial colon identities]
\label{hir:actual-exceptional-entire-shadow-hs-colon-rigidity}
\uses{hir:actual-exceptional-jet-rank-injectivity-converse,hir:actual-pivot-fixed-fibre-whole-colons,hir:actual-exceptional-regular-symbol-fibre-hs-profile}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalJetRigidity.exceptional_colons_iff_entire_shadow_hs}
\leanok
For a finite actual formal family $H_i$, put $I=(H_i)$ and let
$J=(\rho_eH_i)$ be its literal whole shadow extension in $R$.
Then $H_I(\ell)=H_J(\ell)$ at every finite order if and only if
$(I+\mathfrak m^{q+1}):x_e=I+\mathfrak m^q$ at every $q$.
In particular the natural entire-profile equality internally forces
all actual exceptional jet multiplications to be injective.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Pivot idempotence makes every generator of $J$ pivot fixed, so the
proved whole family theorem internally gives all its colon identities
and exact rank recurrences. The two whole ideals satisfy
$I+(x_e)=J+(x_e)$. Entire HS equality gives equality of the two genuine
jet ranks at each positive $q$, and their order-zero jet spaces are
both zero. For each $q$, substitute those equalities and the common
fibre denominator into the rank recurrence for $J$. This gives the
rank addition required in the preceding theorem for $I$, so its
actual multiplication is injective and its whole colon equality
follows at that $q$. Every $q$ is obtained without a regularity input.
Conversely all whole colons give the entire shadow profile equality
by the already proved genuine finite-jet rank induction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual original point HS persistence forces its entire analytic axis-specialization profile]
\label{hir:actual-original-point-persistent-hs-axis-profile}

\leanok

independently defined actual point-normalized ideal. If the actual

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

The whole dehomogenized source-initial ideal lies in the actual
axis image, so genuine quotient dimensions give
$H_{\alpha_e^*N}\le H_{D}$ for that entire dehomogenized ideal.

with the translated homogeneous source-initial profile, which is
bounded above by $H_A$ by the actual holomorphic ray specialization
argument. At each finite order the stated persistence therefore puts
$H_N$ at both ends of this chain. Antisymmetry identifies its value
with the actual analytic axis-image value. This retains the entire
independently defined ideals and the actual same chosen point chart.
\end{proof}
