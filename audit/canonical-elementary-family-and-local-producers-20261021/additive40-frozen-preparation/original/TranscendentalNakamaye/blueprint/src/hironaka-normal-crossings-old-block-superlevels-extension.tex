\section{Actual old-block superlevels lie in their original strict transforms}
\label{sec:nc-old-block-superlevels}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual current old high locus comes from its original strict high locus]
\label{hir:nc-old-block-current-high-subset-strict-original-high}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsOldBlockSuperlevels.exists_current_old_carrier_superlevel_control}
\leanok
Let $B+\!+E$ be an actual SNC list, $I$ coherent, and let $T$ be a genuine
marked test tower for $(X,B+\!+E,I,d)$. The actual old union at the end is
NC and adapted to the ENTIRE current retained/born boundary. For EVERY
natural reference count $q$, its whole current old-count superlevel is
contained in the strict transform of the original old-count superlevel:
\[
 \{y:q\le\operatorname{mult}_{B_T}(y)\}
 \subseteq \operatorname{strict}_T\{x:q\le\operatorname{mult}_{B}(x)\}.
\]
No high-locus identity, branch bound, chosen maximum or output support
certificate is a premise.
\uses{hir:nc-snc-old-block-boundary-adapted-union,hir:nc-snc-old-block-intrinsic-count,hir:nc-entire-old-retained-born-boundary,bcalc:member-intersection-tower}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual testness and the original well-formed record give the whole current
SNC boundary. Its literal identity is $B_T+\!+E_T$, retaining all positions,
so the genuine block-carrier construction supplies the current old carrier.
At a point $y$, let $J$ be precisely the original OLD indices whose strict
members pass through $y$. The exact old-block count theorem, and the literal
length-preserving map of old indices, give current multiplicity $|J|$.

Let $K$ consist of ALL original boundary indices whose strict members pass
through $y$; this includes every old index in $J$. The ambient marked carrier
remains the whole ambient space under actual blowups. The genuine
member-intersection theorem therefore puts $y$ in the strict transform of
the original intersection of the members indexed by $K$. At every original
point $z$ of that intersection, all old members indexed by $J$ pass through
$z$. Its intrinsic old count is consequently at least $|J|$. If the current
count is at least $q$, the entire original intersection is contained in the
original $q$-superlevel. Actual monotonicity of strict transform gives the
asserted inclusion at $y$. This works equally for $q=0$, empty lists and
empty members; no global count decrease outside a permitted cosupport is
inferred.
\end{proof}
