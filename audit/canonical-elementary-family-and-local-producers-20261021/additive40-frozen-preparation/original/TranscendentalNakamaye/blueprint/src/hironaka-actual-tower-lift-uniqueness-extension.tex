\section{Unique holomorphic lifts along an actual finite blowup extension}

\begin{theorem}[The actual tail projection is the unique whole holomorphic lift]
\label{hir:actual-tower-tail-holomorphic-lift-uniqueness}
\uses{hir:blowup-tower,hir:original-actual-whole-projection-biholomorphic-map-uniqueness}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualTowerLiftUniqueness.actual_lift_unique}
\leanok
Let $T:Y\to X$ and $R:Z\to Y$ be actual finite towers of genuine blowups.
Every holomorphic $f:Z\to Y$ satisfying $T\circ f=(T\circ R)$ is exactly
the whole actual tail projection $R$. No invertibility, canonicality,
terminal datum or inverse for $f$ is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual last step of $T$. For the empty tower its projection
is the identity, and the given equation is the desired equality.
For $T=T_0\circ\pi$, retain the SAME genuine extension from the endpoint
of $T_0$ obtained by appending the actual first step $\pi$ and then $R$.
The induction hypothesis applies to the holomorphic map $\pi\circ f$ and
this actual extension. Associativity of the true tower composition gives
its required projection equation. Consequently
$\pi\circ f=\pi\circ R$ on the entire endpoint.

The actual blowup property makes $\pi^*I_C$ invertible. Pulling this very
invertible ideal through the actual finite tower $R$ remains invertible;
the genuine whole pullback composition formula identifies it with
$(\pi\circ R)^*I_C$. Thus the actual universal property of $\pi$ applies
to the SAME base map $\pi\circ R$, with its required invertibility proved
internally. Both $f$ and the actual projection $R$ are holomorphic lifts
of this base map, by the induction equality and by definition. Genuine
universal uniqueness identifies them on the whole endpoint, including
exceptional points. Every finite empty position remains in the induction.
\end{proof}
