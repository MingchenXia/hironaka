\section{Actual exceptional saturation and homogeneous fibres give strict filtered division}

Work in the genuine ring $R=\mathbb C[[x_1,\ldots,x_n]]$, with its
actual coordinate maximal ideal $\mathfrak m=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$.
All coefficients, ideal powers and products below are the original
formal-series operations. No comparison of diagrams in fixed coordinates
is assumed.

\begin{definition}[The literal pivot-zero projection]
\label{hir:actual-exceptional-pivot-zero-projection}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.pivotZeroProjection}
\leanok
For a coordinate $e$, let $\rho_e:R\to R$ first discard that coordinate
and then include the remaining series literally. Thus its coefficient
at $\alpha$ is the original coefficient when $\alpha_e=0$, and zero
otherwise. It is an actual complex algebra endomorphism.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Homogeneous generators give exact shifted filtered representations]
\label{hir:actual-homogeneous-family-shifted-filtered-division}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-coordinate-power-coefficient-kernel}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.exists_homogeneous_filtered_representation}
\leanok
Let $(p_i)_{i\in A}$ be a finite family of genuine series homogeneous
of degrees $d_i$. If $F\in(p_i)\cap\mathfrak m^q$, then there are
actual whole series $Q_i\in\mathfrak m^{q-d_i}$ with
$F=\sum_i p_iQ_i$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose any whole finite span representation $F=\sum_i p_iA_i$.
Define $Q_i$ by retaining exactly the coefficients of $A_i$ of total
degree at least $q-d_i$. These are genuine whole series and have the
stated maximal-power membership by the true coefficient criterion.
For a coefficient indexed by $\alpha$, every nonzero coefficient of
$p_i$ in its finite product antidiagonal has degree exactly $d_i$.
Consequently the corresponding coefficient of $Q_i$ is retained
exactly when $|\alpha|\ge q$. The genuine convolution therefore gives
$[x^\alpha](p_iQ_i)=[x^\alpha](p_iA_i)$ above that degree and zero below
it. Summing gives the coefficients of $F$ in both cases, because its
coefficients below $q$ vanish. Whole coefficient extensionality gives
the exact identity, including zero degrees and empty families.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole actual projection, kernel and maximal powers]
\label{hir:actual-exceptional-pivot-projection-kernel-powers}
\uses{hir:actual-exceptional-pivot-zero-projection,hir:actual-formal-coordinate-power-coefficient-kernel}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.pivotZeroProjection_coefficient,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.pivotZeroProjection_idempotent,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.pivotZeroProjection_eq_zero_iff,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.pivotZeroProjection_mem_point_power,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.point_power_of_X_mul_mem}
\leanok
The literal projection is idempotent, preserves every actual maximal
power, and $\rho_e(F)=0$ if and only if the actual variable $x_e$
divides $F$. In addition, $x_eF\in\mathfrak m^{q+1}$ implies
$F\in\mathfrak m^q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine inclusion and complementary-coordinate restriction give
the displayed coefficient formula: precisely exponent vectors with
pivot exponent zero are in the image of the complementary exponent
embedding. Applying the formula twice proves idempotence. The same
formula preserves every coefficient vanishing condition of total
degree less than $q$, proving maximal-power preservation.
The projection vanishes exactly when all coefficients with pivot
exponent zero vanish. The genuine formal-variable divisibility
criterion identifies this with $x_e\mid F$. Finally multiplication
by the literal monomial $x_e$ shifts each coefficient index by its
unit exponent and increases the total degree by exactly one. The
coefficient criterion for $\mathfrak m^{q+1}$ therefore annihilates
every coefficient of $F$ of degree less than $q$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual exceptional saturation produces whole strict maximal filtration]
\label{hir:actual-exceptional-saturated-homogeneous-fibre-filtered-division}
\uses{hir:actual-homogeneous-family-shifted-filtered-division,hir:actual-exceptional-pivot-projection-kernel-powers}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.exists_exceptional_filtered_representation}
\leanok
Let $I=(H_i)_{i\in A}$ be an actual whole finite span. Suppose
$x_eF\in I\Rightarrow F\in I$ for every whole series $F$,
$H_i\in\mathfrak m^{d_i}$, and each literal shadow
$\rho_e(H_i)$ is homogeneous of degree $d_i$.
Then every $F\in I\cap\mathfrak m^q$ admits an exact whole identity
$F=\sum_iH_iQ_i$ with $Q_i\in\mathfrak m^{q-d_i}$.
These are natural saturation, generator-order and literal fibre
conditions; no filtered representation or standardness is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on $q$. For $q=0$ any actual finite-span representation works,
since all the required coefficient powers are the whole ring.
Suppose the assertion holds at $q$, and let
$F\in I\cap\mathfrak m^{q+1}$.
Apply the literal projection to any whole span representation of $F$.
Its image lies in the span of the homogeneous shadows and in
$\mathfrak m^{q+1}$. The homogeneous-family theorem gives
$\rho_e(F)=\sum_i\rho_e(H_i)A_i$ with
$A_i\in\mathfrak m^{q+1-d_i}$.
Apply the projection once more. Its idempotence replaces these
coefficients by $\rho_e(A_i)$ without changing the identity or their
power bounds. Hence
$D=F-\sum_iH_i\rho_e(A_i)$ has zero projection, so $D=x_eK$.
Every summand subtracted from $F$ belongs to
$\mathfrak m^{q+1}$, since
$d_i+(q+1-d_i)\ge q+1$; thus $K\in\mathfrak m^q$ by the true
one-variable coefficient shift. Also $D\in I$, and the actual
exceptional saturation gives $K\in I$.

The induction hypothesis writes $K=\sum_iH_iB_i$ with
$B_i\in\mathfrak m^{q-d_i}$. Set
$Q_i=\rho_e(A_i)+x_eB_i$. The first term has the required power;
the second does too because $1+(q-d_i)\ge q+1-d_i$.
Substituting the exact identity for $K$ proves the exact whole
identity for $F$. This is a finite induction, with no convergence,
incoming lifting certificate or change of generators.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual exceptional initial symbol is regular in every order]
\label{hir:actual-exceptional-homogeneous-fibre-regular-initial-symbol}
\uses{hir:actual-homogeneous-family-shifted-filtered-division,hir:actual-exceptional-pivot-projection-kernel-powers}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.exceptional_point_power_colon_eq}
\leanok
Under the same natural span, generator-order, homogeneous-shadow and
whole exceptional saturation conditions,
\[
 (I+\mathfrak m^{q+1}):x_e=I+\mathfrak m^q
 \qquad(q\ge0).
\]
Thus regularity of the actual exceptional initial symbol is produced;
it is not an incoming filtered regularity condition.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $x_eF=A+B$ with $A\in I$ and $B\in\mathfrak m^{q+1}$.
Projection kills $x_e$ and gives $\rho_e(A)=-\rho_e(B)$, so this
shadow belongs to the span of the homogeneous shadows and to
$\mathfrak m^{q+1}$. The homogeneous-family result and a second
projection give coefficients $C_i$ fixed by the projection, of orders
at least $q+1-d_i$, with $\rho_e(A)=\sum_i\rho_e(H_i)C_i$.
Then $A'=\sum_iH_iC_i$ belongs to $I\cap\mathfrak m^{q+1}$ and has
exactly the same shadow as $A$. Write $A-A'=x_eK$ using the actual
projection kernel. Actual whole saturation gives $K\in I$.
The exact two identities give $x_e(F-K)=B+A'\in\mathfrak m^{q+1}$;
the genuine coefficient shift therefore puts $F-K$ in
$\mathfrak m^q$. Hence $F\in I+\mathfrak m^q$.
Conversely multiplication by $x_e$ preserves $I$ and sends
$\mathfrak m^q$ into $\mathfrak m^{q+1}$, proving the reverse
inclusion and the whole colon equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same original canonical normalized family has strict formal maximal filtration]
\label{hir:actual-canonical-point-normalized-strict-filtered-division}
\uses{hir:actual-exceptional-saturated-homogeneous-fibre-filtered-division,hir:actual-exceptional-homogeneous-fibre-regular-initial-symbol,hir:actual-original-point-normalized-exceptional-regularity,hir:actual-original-point-normalized-total-saturation,hir:actual-formal-relations-original-analytic-syzygies,hir:actual-point-canonical-quotient-pure-coefficient}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.exists_original_point_canonical_filtered_family,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualExceptionalFilteredDivision.exists_original_point_canonical_strict_symbol_family}
\leanok
An original analytic germ ideal $J$ and a genuine point chart internally
produce ONE analytic normalized family $H_a$ of the original canonical
reduced generators $G_a$. Their whole span is the independently defined
normalized ideal. The family is chosen before all order hypotheses and
finite jets. If every $H_a$ retains its own original vertex order $d_a$,
then every $F\in(\widehat H_a)\cap\mathfrak m^q$ has an exact whole formal
representation $F=\sum_a\widehat H_a Q_a$ with
$Q_a\in\mathfrak m^{q-d_a}$. No target filtered division, homogeneous
shadow, exceptional regularity or HS certificate is supplied. The SAME
chosen family also has the whole colon identities above at every order.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the genuine same-generator canonical normalized family with
actual exceptional regularity. The whole point-normalized ideal is
the actual total-pullback saturation. The image of the original ideal
lies in the span of these same $H_a$, since the original canonical
$G_a$ span the original ideal and $\pi^*G_a=e^{d_a}H_a$.
Repeated actual exceptional cancellation therefore puts every member
of that saturation in the same span. Conversely every $H_a$ is a
literal normalized member, using the original canonical generator's
true point power. Thus the whole spans agree without changing family.

Exceptional regularity also holds for the entire Taylor extension.
Indeed a formal relation between the $\widehat H_a$ and the actual
$\widehat e=x_e$ is generated by genuine analytic syzygies, by actual
Taylor flatness. In every such analytic syzygy, the coefficient of
$e$ lies in the analytic span of $H_a$ by its actual exceptional
regularity. Hence its whole Taylor image lies in the formal span.
The expression for the coefficient of $x_e$ in the original formal
relation then places that coefficient itself in this formal span.
This proves the required whole formal exceptional cancellation.

For each same quotient, its pure coefficients are exactly those of
the literal dehomogenized degree-$d_a$ original polynomial. The latter
is pivot independent: substitution removes the pivot variable. Its
total degree is at most $d_a$, because each source monomial has that
degree and each substituted variable has total degree at most one.
The assumed actual order of $H_a$ makes all its Taylor coefficients
below $d_a$ vanish. The actual pivot-zero shadow is therefore precisely
a genuine homogeneous series of degree $d_a$, including the possible
zero shadow. The preceding whole filtered-division theorem applies to
this same formal family, its internally proved exceptional regularity,
these homogeneous shadows and the true Taylor order bounds. It yields
the stated exact shifted representation at every finite order. Apply
also the preceding regular-initial-symbol theorem to these same internally
proved conditions; this gives every whole colon identity for this SAME
family, without selecting a second normalized family.
\end{proof}

The point-order hypothesis now gives actual target maximal-filtration
control and actual exceptional initial-symbol regularity for the same

entire current HS persistence, a complete HS identification from this
