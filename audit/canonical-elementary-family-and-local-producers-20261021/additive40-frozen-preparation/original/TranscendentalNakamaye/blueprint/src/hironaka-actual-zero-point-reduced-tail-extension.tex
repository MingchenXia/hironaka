\section{Literal point-normalization coefficients retain the canonical reduced tail}

This chapter concerns the genuine zero-direction point chart. The
source diagram is always the ACTUAL diagram of the original whole
ideal in its original coordinates. It is not asserted to lie in the
current diagram.

\begin{definition}[The actual source exponent of a low current point term]
\label{hir:actual-zero-point-low-source-exponent}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-monomial-blowup-coefficient-reflection}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroPointReducedTail.zeroPointLowSourceExponent}
\leanok
For a pivot $e$, weight $w$ and current exponent $\beta$, define
$L_{e,w}(\beta)=\beta+(\beta_e+w-|\beta|)\mathbf1_e$,
where the natural subtraction is evaluated as
$\beta_e+(w-|\beta|)$. In the stated low-degree range $|\beta|<w$
this has ordinary integer meaning, is above $\beta$, and has strictly
larger pivot component.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The actual whole normalized coefficient has its literal source exponent]
\label{hir:actual-zero-point-low-coefficient-source-dictionary}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-point-low-source-exponent,hir:actual-formal-monomial-blowup-coefficient-reflection}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroPointReducedTail.zeroPointLowSourceExponent_blowup,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroPointReducedTail.normalized_zero_point_low_coefficient}
\leanok
For $|\beta|<w$, the actual monomial blowup exponent of
$L_{e,w}(\beta)$ is exactly $\beta+w\mathbf1_e$.
Consequently every actual whole quotient $x_e^wH=\pi_0^*F$
satisfies $H_\beta=F_{L_{e,w}(\beta)}$. No coefficient formula or
source-support certificate is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $k=w-|\beta|>0$. The lifted exponent equals
$\beta+(\beta_e+k)\mathbf1_e$, so its nonpivot components are those
of $\beta$ and its total degree is $w+\beta_e$.
The genuine zero point blowup retains all nonpivot components and
replaces the pivot component by that total degree. Its actual image
is therefore $\beta+w\mathbf1_e$ at every coordinate. Take that
actual coefficient in the defining whole quotient equation. Genuine
multiplication by $x_e^w$ shifts the coefficient by exactly
$w\mathbf1_e$, giving $H_\beta$ on one side. The actual injective
monomial blowup coefficient theorem gives precisely the indicated
source coefficient on the other. This proves the entire numerical
coefficient equality for each low exponent.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every low normalized canonical coefficient vanishes on the entire original diagram]
\label{hir:actual-zero-point-canonical-low-tail-normality}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-point-low-coefficient-source-dictionary,hir:actual-reduced-formal-vertex-members}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroPointReducedTail.canonical_zero_point_low_coefficient_zero_on_source_diagram}
\leanok
Let $I$ be an actual original formal ideal, let $G_a$ be its SAME
canonical reduced vertex series, and put $w=|a|$. For any genuine
whole quotient $x_e^wH_a=\pi_0^*G_a$, every coefficient of $H_a$
at a source-diagram exponent $\beta\in\Gamma(I)$ with
$|\beta|<|a|$ is zero. Thus each homogeneous component of degree
below $|a|$ is normal outside the ENTIRE original diagram.
The literal source reduced-support condition is produced by the
canonical family itself, and no desired target obstruction is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For such a $\beta$, put $\alpha=L_{e,w}(\beta)$. The preceding
actual coefficient dictionary gives $(H_a)_\beta=(G_a)_\alpha$.
Since $\alpha\ge\beta$ and the original diagram is upward closed,
$\alpha\in\Gamma(I)$. On its entire original diagram the actual
canonical reduced series has coefficient zero except at its single
vertex $a$. If $\alpha=a$, then $\beta\le a$ and
$\beta\in\Gamma(I)$; actual vertex minimality gives $a\le\beta$.
But the low-degree lift has $\alpha_e>\beta_e$, contradicting these
two inequalities. Hence $\alpha\ne a$ and the genuine reduced
coefficient is zero. The homogeneous-component coefficient formula
then gives the same entire source-diagram normality at every degree
below $|a|$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original data internally choose one whole normalized family with finite low-jet normality]
\label{hir:actual-canonical-zero-point-low-jet-normality-producer}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-point-canonical-low-tail-normality,hir:actual-point-centre-canonical-vertex-powers,hir:actual-formal-blowup-normalized-member}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroPointReducedTail.exists_canonical_zero_point_normalized_low_jet}
\leanok
An actual original formal ideal and actual pivot internally select ONE
whole canonical normalized family before every coefficient and finite
order. It satisfies its actual quotient equations and all the preceding
source-diagram low-coefficient vanishings. Its literal finite jet
through degrees below each original vertex degree, and each such
homogeneous component, have zero coefficient on the ENTIRE original
diagram. No selected family, source power or obstruction certificate
is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The degree-first canonical leading coefficient and its lower coefficient
vanishing internally place each source reduced series in the true
point-power of its original vertex degree. The genuine formal blowup
division theorem therefore constructs the normalized quotient of each
of these SAME series. Choose that entire family once. Its actual
quotient equations hold by the defining division theorem, so the
preceding result gives every low coefficient vanishing. The actual
finite truncation retains exactly coefficients below the stated degree
and kills all others; hence every source-diagram coefficient of the
whole finite jet is zero. The homogeneous-component formula likewise
retains a coefficient only at its stated low degree, where the same
vanishing applies. These are exact actual finite polynomials and
whole homogeneous series, not an assumed current normal-form model.
\end{proof}

Turning a nonzero such component into a strict current-jet rank drop
still requires an actual comparison with the current lower-vertex
initial span. No source/current diagram inclusion, arbitrary-direction
extension, all-vertex HS converse or history termination is inferred.
