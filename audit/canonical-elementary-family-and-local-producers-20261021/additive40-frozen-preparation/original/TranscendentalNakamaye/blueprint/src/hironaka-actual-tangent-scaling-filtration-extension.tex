\begin{theorem}[The genuine whole chart contracts every normal power to the point power]
\label{hir:actual-tangent-scaling-formal-filtration}

\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualTangentScalingFiltration.actualTangentScalingExponent_normal_weight,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualTangentScalingFiltration.mem_normal_pow_tangentScaling_iff_point_pow,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualTangentScalingFiltration.comap_normal_pow_tangentScaling}
\leanok

normal weight $|a|$. Consequently, for every entire formal $F$ and every $q$,
\[
 \sigma(F)\in N^q\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad F\in\mathfrak m^q.
\]
This is a whole ideal inverse-image equality for every finite power.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The selected nonpivot coordinates of $\sigma$ are precisely $S^c$.
Inserting their sum in the pivot, whose normal weight is one, gives
$\operatorname{wt}_S(T(a))=\operatorname{wt}_S(a)+\operatorname{wt}_{S^c}(a)=|a|$.

normal pivot. The whole point ideal therefore maps into $N$, and actual
ideal-map powers give the forward implication at every $q$.
Conversely, normal-power membership kills all target coefficients below
normal weight $q$. At $T(a)$ this kills the coefficient whenever $|a|<q$.
Genuine whole coefficient reflection identifies it with the actual source
coefficient at $a$. The actual whole point-power coefficient criterion

inverse-image equality, not a selected-generator comparison.
\end{proof}
