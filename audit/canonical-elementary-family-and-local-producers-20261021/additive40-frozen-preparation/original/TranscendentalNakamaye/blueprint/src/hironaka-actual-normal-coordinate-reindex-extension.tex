\section{Actual normal-coordinate reindexing and chart conjugacy}

All reindexings below are genuine coordinate permutations. The polynomial,
formal and original analytic germ rings are independently reindexed by the
same permutation. A fixed degree-lex order in the reindexed ring is not
identified with the original degree-lex order. No transport of a canonical
owner division or of reduced vertices is asserted.

\begin{theorem}[The actual whole germ reindexing has the literal Taylor square]
\label{hir:actual-normal-reindex-whole-taylor}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-germ-hom-coordinate-uniqueness}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.coordinateLinearEquiv,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.coordinateGermEquiv,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.coordinateGermEquiv_coordinate,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.actualTaylor_coordinateGermEquiv,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.coordinateGermEquiv_polynomial}
\leanok
For every coordinate permutation $r$, there is a genuine complex-linear
coordinate equivalence and its original germ algebra equivalence $P_r$, with
$P_r(x_i)=x_{r(i)}$. The whole original Taylor map satisfies
$\tau P_r=\operatorname{rename}_r\tau$. The polynomial renaming has precisely
this same literal analytic evaluation, for every entire polynomial.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the linear isometry $z\mapsto(i\mapsto z_{r(i)})$ and pull back actual
holomorphic representatives. Composition with its inverse gives a genuine
whole germ equivalence, preserving every constant. Applying it to coordinate
representatives gives $x_{r(i)}$. The two maps from the original germ ring
to formal series agree on these actual coordinates, whose images have zero
constant term. Actual Hadamard decomposition and finite Taylor remainders
therefore make the two maps agree on every entire germ. Polynomial
universality gives the evaluation square from the same coordinate identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A prescribed normal pivot is carried to the first normal coordinate]
\label{hir:actual-normal-reindex-pivot-powers}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-reindex-whole-taylor,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.firstNormalIndex,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.normalPivotPermutation,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.normalPivotPermutation_pivot,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.normalPivotPermutation_tangent,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.normalPivotPermutation_normal_set,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.map_analyticCoordinateIdeal_coordinateGermEquiv,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.map_formalCoordinateIdeal_rename,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.map_normalIdeal_pow_normalPivotPermutation,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.map_formal_normal_pow_normalPivotPermutation,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.map_maximalIdeal_pow_coordinateGermEquiv,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.normalPivotPermutation_tangentPullback,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.normalPivotPermutation_tangent_mul}
\leanok
In $d+t$ coordinates, any prescribed normal pivot $j$ admits an actual
permutation fixing every tangent coordinate, preserving the whole normal
coordinate set, and carrying $j$ to the first normal coordinate. Its whole
analytic and formal normal ideals, all their powers, and every genuine
analytic maximal power are preserved. The whole original tangent pullback
is fixed, so the tangent algebra structure is retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The normal pivot itself supplies $t>0$. Swap its ambient index with the
first normal ambient index. Both indices belong to the normal block, and
every tangent index differs from both. Thus the swap fixes the tangent
block and preserves exactly the normal block, including when the pivot
already is first. Mapping a coordinate-ideal span by either genuine
coordinate equivalence maps its generators exactly to the coordinates of
the image set. This proves whole ideal equalities; ideal-map powers give
all powers, including the zeroth. Evaluation at the origin is unchanged
by the linear equivalence, so the true maximal ideal is preserved and
again so are all powers. For tangent pullback, apply the entire Taylor
square and its injectivity: both maps have the same actual tangent
coordinate images and hence agree on every original tangent germ.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine whole blowup charts are conjugate under reindexing]
\label{hir:actual-normal-reindex-chart-conjugacy}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-reindex-whole-taylor,hir:actual-normal-reindex-pivot-powers,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.rename_actualFormalBlowup,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.coordinateGermEquiv_actualAnalyticBlowupPullback,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.coordinateGermEquiv_analytic_chart_conjugacy,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualNormalCoordinateReindex.image_tangentScalingVariables}
\leanok
For any selected coordinate set $A$, pivot $e$, and actual chart constants
$b$, the same whole formal and analytic charts satisfy
\[
 P_r\,\beta_{A,e,b}=\beta_{rA,r(e),b\circ r^{-1}}P_r.
\]
Consequently the genuine analytic conjugate $P_r\beta P_r^{-1}$ is precisely
the displayed reindexed chart. The selected tangent-scaling set
$S^c\cup\{e\}$ becomes $(rS)^c\cup\{r(e)\}$, so both the relative normal
chart and the tangent-scaling chart have this literal square.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Renaming is the genuine formal substitution $X_i\mapsto X_{r(i)}$.
Composition of actual formal substitutions gives, on the two sides,
substitution by the corresponding reindexed chart coordinates. For an
index outside $A$ both are $X_{r(i)}$; the pivot gives $X_{r(e)}$; every
other selected index gives
$X_{r(e)}(b_i+X_{r(i)})$. Thus the coordinate families are identical and
the substitutions agree on every entire formal series. The whole Taylor
square for reindexing and the actual analytic blowup square identify the
Taylor series of both analytic compositions with this same formal series.
Injectivity of the unchanged original Taylor map proves their whole germ
equality. Substitute $P_r^{-1}g$ to get the conjugacy formula. Finally a
bijection carries complements and singletons to their literal complements
and singletons, proving the tangent-scaling selected-set identity.
\end{proof}

These equalities permit working with a fixed monomial order in the permuted
ring and transporting the resulting whole analytic division back through
$P_r$. They do not assert that fixed degree-lex canonical vertices or owner
recursions commute with an arbitrary permutation.
