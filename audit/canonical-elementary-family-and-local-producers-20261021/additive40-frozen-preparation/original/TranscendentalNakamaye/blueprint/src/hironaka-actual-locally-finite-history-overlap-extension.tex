\section{Internally constructed overlap maps of one actual locally finite history}

The only global-history inputs are the actual growing blowup sequence and
local finiteness of its actual centre images. The existence of the general
noncompact scheduler is not inferred. All windows, local finite endpoints,
whole overlap maps and their cocycle are constructed from these inputs.

\begin{lemma}[Whole actual prefix comparisons restrict to smaller windows]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-prefix-comparison-restriction}
\uses{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-late-prefix-comparison}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryOverlap.restrictPrefixComparison,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryOverlap.restrictPrefixComparison_proj}
\leanok
Any genuine endpoint biholomorphism between two actual finite prefixes
restricted to an open $U$, commuting with their whole base projections,
restricts to a genuine biholomorphism over every smaller open $V\subseteq U$.
Its whole restricted projection equation holds on $V$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original projection equation implies that the image of a point over $V$
is again over $V$. Applying that equation to the inverse point gives the
same fact for the genuine inverse. Restrict both actual maps using these
membership facts. Their true inverse laws remain literal, and holomorphicity
is inherited by the actual open inclusions. The original whole projection
equation, followed by the defining restricted projection equations, gives
the entire projection equation on $V$. No comparison is assumed on $V$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

Actual finite blowup projections and their genuine restrictions supply
the local holomorphicity and properness; the retained avoidance field
concerns every actual later centre. No local history or cover is supplied
by the caller.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual pairwise overlap biholomorphisms are internally constructed]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-whole-overlap-maps}
\uses{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-internal-window-atlas,hir:actual-locally-finite-history-late-prefix-comparison,hir:actual-locally-finite-prefix-comparison-restriction}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryOverlap.transition,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryOverlap.transition_projection}
\leanok
The actual inverse images of $U_x\cap U_y$ in the stage-$N_x$ and
stage-$N_y$ finite endpoints have a genuine whole biholomorphism over the
original base. It induces a biholomorphism between the actual saturated
overlap opens of the two local endpoint manifolds. Its projection equation
holds on the entire overlap, including exceptional points.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The intersection is stabilizing after $\min(N_x,N_y)$: whichever of the
two indices is smaller already gives avoidance on this smaller open.
Both actual prefix indices are at least this minimum. The genuine late-prefix
comparison therefore supplies their actual whole biholomorphism over the
intersection. Reversing the order of the two open factors is the literal
identity on points. The actual nested-open biholomorphisms identify these
direct finite-prefix inverse images with the saturated overlap opens in the
local endpoints. Compose these genuine maps to obtain the overlap map.
Their actual projection formulas give the whole asserted square.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The constructed actual overlaps supply genuine gluing data]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-overlap-cocycle-gluing}
\uses{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-whole-overlap-maps,hir:original-actual-whole-projection-biholomorphic-map-uniqueness}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryOverlap.transition_self,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryOverlap.transition_inter,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryOverlap.transition_cocycle,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryOverlap.actualHistoryGlueCore}
\leanok
These same internally chosen overlap maps have diagonal identity, preserve
the actual third-window intersections and satisfy the genuine triple cocycle.
Consequently the actual local finite endpoints determine topological gluing
data, with no incoming overlap comparison or cocycle certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The diagonal comparison is the proved actual late-prefix identity; the
nested-open identifications are mutually inverse. Its induced overlap map
is therefore the literal identity. The whole projection equation preserves
membership in every third original window, proving the true intersection
condition.

On a triple intersection restrict the three already constructed direct
finite-prefix comparisons to the same smaller actual open. Their genuine
projection equations show that the composite of the first two and the third
are biholomorphisms between the same actual finite restricted endpoints
over the same base. Actual whole-projection uniqueness forces equality.
Evaluating this equality at the original triple-overlap point and undoing
the literal nested-open identifications yields the cocycle for the same
chosen maps, even though their original pairwise cuts differ. The actual
open cover, diagonal, intersection condition and cocycle now populate every
field of the genuine topological gluing construction.
\end{proof}
