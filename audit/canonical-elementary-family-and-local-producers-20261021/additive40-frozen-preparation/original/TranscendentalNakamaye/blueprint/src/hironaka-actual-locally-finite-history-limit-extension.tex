\section{The actual complex limit of one locally finite blowup history}

This construction takes an actual growing sequence and local finiteness of
its actual centre images. It constructs its genuine complex endpoint and
proper holomorphic composite. It does not assert existence of such a sequence
for arbitrary original noncompact analytic data.

\begin{theorem}[The internally constructed limit has its actual continuous projection]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-limit-projection}
\uses{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-overlap-cocycle-gluing}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.gluedProjection,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.gluedProjection_local,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.localInclusion_range,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.embeddingTransition,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.embeddingTransition_inclusion}
\leanok
The quotient of the internally constructed local finite endpoints has a
continuous projection to the original base. On every local endpoint it is
literally the projection of that actual recorded finite prefix. The entire
range of each local inclusion is the inverse image of its original window.
The actual embedding-overlap transitions are genuine biholomorphisms.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The whole projection equations of the already constructed overlap maps give
a compatible family of continuous finite-prefix projections. The genuine
topological quotient universal property supplies the global projection and
its whole local equations. Each local endpoint projects into its own
window. Conversely represent an arbitrary point by another local endpoint.
If its projection lies in the first window, it is in the actual overlap;
its already constructed transition gives a representative in the first
endpoint, with equality supplied by the actual gluing relation. This proves
both directions of the saturated-range equation. These equations identify
the embedding overlaps with the actual saturated overlaps. Conjugating the
constructed holomorphic overlap map by the literal equal-open
biholomorphisms gives the asserted true embedding transition and its
inclusion equation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine locally finite complex endpoint]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-limit-complex-manifold}
\uses{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-limit-projection,hir:actual-locally-finite-history-internal-window-atlas}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.limitManifold,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.localInclusion_contMDiff,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.gluedProjection_contMDiff}
\leanok
The actual glued space, with charts lifted from its actual finite endpoints,
is a Hausdorff second-countable complex manifold of the original ambient
dimension. Every local inclusion and the actual global projection are
holomorphic. There is no incoming limit manifold or atlas certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The internally chosen windows cover the original Hausdorff second-countable
manifold. The proved saturated-range equations and actual open embeddings
therefore make the glued space Hausdorff: points with distinct base images
are separated downstairs, and points above one window are separated in its
actual finite endpoint. A countable subcover of the original windows, with
countable bases of their actual finite endpoints, supplies second
countability. Lift the genuine endpoint charts through the actual open
embeddings. Their true holomorphic overlap maps prove the complex atlas
compatibility. The same lifted charts make every local inclusion
holomorphic. On any such chart, the global projection is literally the
holomorphic actual finite-prefix projection; hence it is holomorphic on the
whole limit. These are applications of the generic actual saturated-atlas
producers to data proved above, not assumptions about a desired atlas.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole limit projection is genuinely proper]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-limit-proper}
\uses{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-limit-projection,hir:actual-locally-finite-history-limit-complex-manifold}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.gluedProjection_fibre,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.gluedProjection_isClosedMap,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryLimit.gluedProjection_isProperMap}
\leanok
The actual holomorphic limit projection is proper. Its entire fibre at an
original point is the image, under that point's actual endpoint inclusion,
of the compact fibre of its genuine finite restricted prefix.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The saturated-range equation places every point of the whole global fibre
inside the endpoint attached to the original point. The whole local
projection identity then proves the literal stated fibre equality. The
actual finite blowup projection is proper, so that fibre is compact, and
its actual continuous inclusion preserves compactness.

To prove closedness, let $C$ be closed in the limit and let $x$ lie outside
its projected image. Pull $C$ back to the actual endpoint attached to $x$.
Its image under the proper finite restricted prefix is closed in the local
base window. The complement is an open neighbourhood of $x$ there. Its
image in the original base is open; the entire saturated-range equation
shows that it misses the global image of $C$. Thus the global projection is
closed. It is continuous and has compact fibres, so the actual closed-map
criterion proves properness. No global maximum or uniform finite stopping
time is used.
\end{proof}
