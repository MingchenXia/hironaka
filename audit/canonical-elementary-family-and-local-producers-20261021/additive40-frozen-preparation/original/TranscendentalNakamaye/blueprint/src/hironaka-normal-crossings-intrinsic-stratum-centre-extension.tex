\begin{theorem}[The whole derivative-stratum ideal detects the original permitted locus exactly]
\label{hir:nc-intrinsic-stratum-detection-ideal}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsIntrinsicStratumCentre.stratumDetectionIdeal,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsIntrinsicStratumCentre.stratumDetectionMarked,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsIntrinsicStratumCentre.stratumDetectionIdeal_geometry}
Let $Z=\{x:\operatorname{mult}_H(x)\ge r\}$ be an intrinsic highest branch
level, with $r>0$ a global multiplicity bound. For original coherent $I$ set
\[
 K=I_Z+\mathcal D^j I.
\]
This is the actual coherent sum ideal, and its WHOLE zero locus is
$V(K)=Z\cap\operatorname{cosupp}(I,j+1)$. No desired support ideal is input.
\uses{hir:general-nc-highest-multiplicity-smooth-centre,hir:order-taylor}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The intrinsic highest-level theorem makes $Z$ a genuine closed complex
submanifold. Its whole vanishing ideal is coherent. The actual derivative
ideal $\mathcal D^jI$ is coherent by repeated coherent differentiation;
concatenating their local finite generators proves coherence of their
actual sum. A germ lies in this sum precisely when it is a finite
holomorphic combination of germs from the two ideals. Its value vanishes
whenever both original ideals vanish. Conversely inclusion of each summand
shows that vanishing of the entire sum forces vanishing of both summands.
Thus its WHOLE zero locus is the intersection of their zero loci. The
actual vanishing-ideal dictionary gives $V(I_Z)=Z$, and the proved exact
Taylor-order dictionary gives
$V(\mathcal D^jI)=\operatorname{cosupp}(I,j+1)$. Substitute these two
actual equalities to obtain the displayed equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original stratum internally supplies the actual auxiliary invariant domain]
\label{hir:nc-intrinsic-stratum-detection-domain}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsIntrinsicStratumCentre.stratumDetectionMarked_isInvDomain}
The actual marking-one datum $(X,K,1,[])$ lies in the domain of the genuine
ambient-dimensional canonical invariant. No nonzero, flatness, maximum,
history or invariant-domain certificate is incoming.
\uses{hir:nc-intrinsic-stratum-detection-ideal,hir:general-nc-actual-highest-stratum-exists,hir:embedded-resolution-pure-dimensional}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine positive-codimension highest-stratum coordinates give empty
interior of $Z$. Its actual analytic local equations therefore include,
at every ambient point, a germ of $I_Z$ which is frequently nonzero:
otherwise all finitely many local equations would vanish identically on a
neighbourhood and make $Z$ have interior. The real inclusion $I_Z\subseteq K$
retains this very germ and its frequent nonvanishing, proving the whole
ambient nonzero condition for $K$ internally. The preceding coherence,
smooth whole ambient carrier and empty SNC boundary give actual
wellformedness. The ambient carrier has dimension $n$ and the auxiliary
mark is literally one. These are every actual invariant-domain field.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original data internally select a nonempty intrinsic locally permitted NC blowup]
\label{hir:nc-intrinsic-original-marked-first-centre}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsIntrinsicStratumCentre.exists_intrinsic_original_marked_nc_blowup}
Assume the ORIGINAL cosupport is compact and its intersection with $Z$ is
nonempty. From only these original data there are a nonempty smooth centre
$C$ and a genuine universal blowup $\pi:Y\to X$. The centre is the actual
WHOLE initial canonical maximum for the internally constructed datum $K$,
and it lies in $Z\cap\operatorname{cosupp}(I,j+1)$. The same map is an
isomorphism off the ORIGINAL cosupport. On actual neighbourhoods of all
centre points the whole old branch list is SNC, its centre is admissible
for the ORIGINAL ideal at its unchanged mark, and the actual restricted
blowup has the complete old strict branches plus true new exceptional as
SNC. Whole-ambient original local wellformedness is internally proved.
No centre, maximum, local branch or history is supplied by a caller.
\uses{hir:nc-intrinsic-stratum-detection-domain,hir:nc-highest-subcentre-actual-blowup,hir:centre-function}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual marking-one datum just constructed. Its cosupport is its
whole zero locus, hence exactly the ORIGINAL intersection already proved.
It is genuinely compact as a closed subset of the original compact
cosupport, and is nonempty by hypothesis. Genuine upper semicontinuity of
the actual canonical invariant on the reflexive history therefore attains
its WHOLE maximum on this very set. Take its entire maximum locus as $C$;
it is nonempty and the actual canonical maximum-admissibility theorem
makes it a closed smooth centre. Its cosupport containment gives BOTH
$C\subseteq Z$ and $C\subseteq\operatorname{cosupp}(I,j+1)$ internally.

Apply the proved highest-stratum subcentre construction to this actual
$C$ and the ORIGINAL ideal and mark. It constructs a real universal
blowup, gives its original off-cosupport isomorphism, and internally
constructs the genuine entire local branch lists and actual restricted
blowups. The local SNC/marked admissibility, unchanged original
wellformedness and complete transformed old-branch/new-exceptional SNC
fields all belong to that SAME map. The auxiliary mark-one datum selects
only the first centre; no equality of auxiliary and old invariants or
controlled transforms is used, and no further progress or termination is
inferred.
\end{proof}
