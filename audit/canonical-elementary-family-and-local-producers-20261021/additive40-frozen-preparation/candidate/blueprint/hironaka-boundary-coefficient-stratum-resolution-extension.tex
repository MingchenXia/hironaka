\begin{theorem}[The genuine component remainder retains its whole boundary domain]
\label{hir:boundary-coefficient-remainder-original-domain}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaBoundaryCoefficientStratumResolution.contactRemainder_boundary_domain}

invariant domain, $A.\mathrm{cosupp}=L.\mathrm{cosupp}\cap R$, and the entire
original marked records of $A$ and $L$ restrict equally to the actual open

\uses{hir:original-all-level-contact-remainder-nonzero-geometry,hir:smooth-selected-components,hir:dimtwo-unselected-smooth-components}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

the WHOLE $L.E$ across this germ equality. The boundary itself is unchanged

identifies the two entire carrier restrictions there. The ideal, marking and
ENTIRE ordered boundary were kept literally, so the whole original records
restrict equally to $G$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An actual finite remainder phase includes the same whole retained boundary]
\label{hir:boundary-coefficient-remainder-finite-parent-phase}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaBoundaryCoefficientStratumResolution.exists_boundary_remainder_parent_phase}

is an internally constructed finite canonical terminal tower of $A$ which

of $R$, and $L_T.\mathrm{cosupp}\cap R_T=\varnothing$. The actual canonical

\uses{hir:boundary-coefficient-remainder-original-domain,hir:principalization-compact-support,hir:original-open-carrier-test-history-extension,hir:original-open-equal-current-test-records}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

from the preceding theorem. This constructs a finite canonical terminal

exceptional component, with no deleted boundary entry or parent-history input.

\begin{theorem}[The actual selected-zero append resolves the whole retained-boundary datum]
\label{hir:boundary-coefficient-positive-codimension-resolution}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaBoundaryCoefficientStratumResolution.exists_boundary_resolution_of_positive_codimension_carrier}
\leanok
For well-formed $L$ with positive mark, compact original cosupport and a
regular carrier dimension bound $k<n$, there is an actual finite whole
$L$-resolution. Every centre is in the literal strict carrier and has normal
crossings with the WHOLE current boundary. No nonzero or phase certificate
is an input.
\uses{hir:boundary-coefficient-remainder-finite-parent-phase,hir:nc-coefficient-selected-strict-geometry,ana:blowups,bcalc:transform-off-centre}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the preceding actual remainder phase $P$. The entire selected
strict union $C=F_P$ is closed smooth, transverse to the WHOLE current boundary
and contained in the SAME parent cosupport, by the general selected-strict
geometry theorem. Its interior is empty: an interior point would have regular
dimension $n$, whereas inclusion into the actual current carrier, whose
true test-tower dimension bound is still $k<n$, would force $n\le k$.
Thus its real blowup exists. Append it to the SAME parent history; every
actual centre-admissibility field, including the whole retained boundary,
has been proved.

The current carrier is the disjoint union $R_P\cup C$. Blowing up its whole
component $C$ removes that component's strict transform, so the new carrier
is the strict transform of $R_P$. Any new cosupport point therefore projects
into $R_P$ and is off $C$. The genuine reverse off-centre cosupport law
places its image in the preceding parent cosupport, contradicting the
already proved remainder avoidance. This proves entire terminality.
Actual testness gives every strict-carrier centre containment. No boundary
was removed from the auxiliary driver or from the parent histories.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual highest old stratum is transverse to the entire born boundary]
\label{hir:boundary-adapted-highest-stratum-transverse}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaBoundaryCoefficientStratumResolution.highest_stratum_isTransverse}
\leanok
If $H$ is an actual NC carrier adapted to an entire retained SNC boundary $E$,
and its old branch multiplicity is everywhere at most $r$, its true highest
stratum $Z=\{\operatorname{mult}_H\ge r\}$ is transverse to the WHOLE $E$.
\uses{hir:general-nc-highest-multiplicity-smooth-centre,hir:general-nc-simultaneous-original-boundary}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a point of $Z$, take the actual common flattening of the old carrier and
the whole boundary. The highest-stratum theorem identifies $Z$ there with
simultaneous vanishing of exactly the old branch coordinates $S$. Each
boundary member through the point has one genuine coordinate, disjoint from
$S$, by actual boundary adaptation. The SAME flattening therefore supplies
the entire required transverse coordinate family, with all original boundary
indices retained. This proves transversality at every point of $Z$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original data produce a whole coefficient phase with every born member retained]
