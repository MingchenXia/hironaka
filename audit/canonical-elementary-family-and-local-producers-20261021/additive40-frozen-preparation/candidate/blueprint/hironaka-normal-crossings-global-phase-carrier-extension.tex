\section{The same actual coefficient phase has a global old/born NC carrier}
\label{sec:nc-global-phase-carrier}

\begin{lemma}[Actual open traces glue the whole boundary-adapted carrier]
\label{hir:nc-boundary-adapted-of-actual-open-traces}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsGlobalPhaseCarrier.boundaryAdapted_of_open_traces}
\leanok
Let $H$ and every member of a finite retained list $E$ be closed. If every
point has a genuine open neighbourhood whose literal $H,E$ traces are
boundary-adapted, then the whole original pair $(H,E)$ is boundary-adapted.
\uses{hir:general-nc-simultaneous-original-boundary}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At a point take its actual open trace and its genuine centred joint
flattening. Its source lies in the open-submanifold chart target, hence also
in the ambient chart target. Every ambient point of its flattening domain
therefore belongs to that very open neighbourhood. The subtype chart and
ambient chart have the same coordinate function there, so the same local
holomorphic equivalence gives the whole original $H$ equation and every
through-$E$ member equation. The finite map of preimage indices is a genuine
length-preserving cast; singleton, old/born disjointness and through-index
injectivity pass through it. Original closedness supplies the remaining
fields. No gluing or atlas certificate is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Every actual empty tower preserves the whole old/born carrier]
\label{hir:nc-boundary-adapted-actual-empty-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsGlobalPhaseCarrier.boundaryAdapted_empty_tower}
\leanok
For an actual boundary-adapted pair $(H,M.E)$, every actual tower with empty
centres has boundary-adapted current pair $(H_T,(M_T).E)$, including every
literal appended empty exceptional position. No canonicality is assumed.
\uses{hir:general-nc-boundary-transform-original-count-step}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual tower. For an empty next centre the current NC count is
at most the ambient dimension $n$. Thus its actual superlevel at $n+1$ is
empty. The genuine whole highest-centre blowup transform theorem applies to
this SAME actual empty blowup at reference count $n+1$. Its exact resulting
old strict carrier and entire mapped boundary with appended empty
exceptional member are precisely the next current marked record. The
induction preserves every empty position, rather than dropping them.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original data produce the same phase with a genuine global current old/born carrier]
\label{hir:nc-original-coefficient-phase-global-old-born-carrier}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsGlobalPhaseCarrier.exists_original_phase_global_old_born_carrier}
\leanok
Only the original coherent positive-mark compact-cosupport ideal and actual
boundary-adapted old NC carrier with positive highest count are inputs.
They internally produce the same true coefficient-stratum resolution,
original marked test history, strict-highest centre containment, and final
original cosupport clearance. The literal final old strict carrier is a
GLOBAL NC carrier adapted to the ENTIRE actual current retained/born list.
\uses{hir:nc-original-boundary-phase-current-local-adapted-carriers,hir:nc-boundary-adapted-of-actual-open-traces,hir:nc-boundary-adapted-actual-empty-tower}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Internally choose the actual phase and every current local old carrier from
the preceding producer. The global final old strict carrier is closed and
every current boundary member is closed by genuine original testness and
well formedness. At a point mapping to the original highest stratum, use
its internally chosen genuine original branch neighbourhood. At the actual
final prefix, the produced local pair is exactly the trace of the global
old strict carrier and current boundary: actual strict transforms and whole
marked transforms commute with open restriction. Its open preimage contains
the point and gives the required local witness.

At a point whose original image lies outside the closed highest stratum,
use its genuine open complement. All centres lie in successive strict
transforms of that original stratum, hence lie above it. The actual
restricted tower therefore has only empty centres. Pull back the original
boundary-adapted pair to this open and apply the preceding true empty-tower
transport. Again actual strict/whole-record restriction identifies its pair
with the literal final global traces. The open-trace gluing lemma proves
the entire global final pair. No global smaller old count is inferred:
highest old crossings outside permitted original cosupport are retained,
and open-support count transition and terminal iteration remain separate.
\end{proof}
