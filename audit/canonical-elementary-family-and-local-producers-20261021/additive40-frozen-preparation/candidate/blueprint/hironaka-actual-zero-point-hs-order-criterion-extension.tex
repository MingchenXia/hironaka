\begin{theorem}[Original zero-point persistence retains all canonical formal orders]
\label{hir:actual-original-zero-point-persistent-hs-all-canonical-orders}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-point-tangent-stable-persistent-hs-order-converse,hir:actual-persistent-point-source-cone-pderiv}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualZeroPointHSOrderCriterion.original_zero_point_persistent_hs_orders}
\leanok

the entire Hilbert--Samuel values of its genuine zero-point normalized
ideal equal those of $I$. Every actual quotient of a SAME canonical
reduced source series $G_a$ by its prescribed exceptional power then
belongs to the whole current maximal power $\mathfrak m^{|a|}$.
There is no tangent-stability, selected-family or desired order input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual persistent homogeneous-cone theorem, applied to this original
ideal and this pivot, proves that the entire homogeneous initial ideal
of its genuine Taylor extension is closed under the pivot derivative.
It constructs its finite coherent cone and actual translated stalks,
derives Hilbert--Samuel constancy on the whole pivot line from genuine
Bennett comparisons, and obtains the derivative closure from the actual
constant-rank tangent calculation. Apply the qualified zero-point
order theorem with precisely this internally obtained closure and the
given original persistence. That theorem uses the whole current colons,
literal source-initial shadow and canonical reduced-tail normality of
this SAME source ideal. It therefore applies to every genuine quotient
in the statement and gives the asserted whole maximal power.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One actual canonical analytic family has the full zero-point criterion]
\label{hir:actual-original-zero-point-entire-hs-iff-canonical-orders}
\uses{hir:actual-original-zero-point-persistent-hs-all-canonical-orders,hir:actual-original-point-order-retention-entire-hs,hir:actual-original-formal-taylor-maximal-power-kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualZeroPointHSOrderCriterion.exists_original_zero_point_hs_iff_orders_family}
\leanok

chart, there is ONE canonical analytic normalized family, chosen before
any order or Hilbert--Samuel premise. Its members satisfy their genuine
exceptional quotient equations and span the entire independently defined
normalized ideal. The whole original and current Hilbert--Samuel profiles

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the SAME canonical analytic family from the original point
order-retention theorem. That choice already supplies every true
exceptional quotient equation and its entire normalized-ideal span;
it precedes all current-order hypotheses and all finite-jet labels.
If every member retains its vertex order, that theorem proves equality
of all original and current Hilbert--Samuel values.

Conversely suppose the entire profiles are equal. Apply the first theorem
to the genuine Taylor series of each already chosen analytic member.
The actual Taylor blowup dictionary carries its exact analytic quotient
equation to the literal formal quotient equation for the SAME reduced
source series. Thus its whole Taylor series lies in
$\mathfrak m^{|a|}$. The genuine Taylor coefficient characterization
of analytic maximal powers reflects this membership to the original
analytic germ. Every member therefore retains its original vertex order.
Literal reassociation of the ambient dimension changes neither the
original ideal, the pivot nor its Taylor coefficients. No new family is
chosen in the reverse direction.
\end{proof}

The assertion concerns genuine zero-direction point charts. Arbitrary
directions, positive-dimensional centres, their full mixed maximal
filtration and the current-history resolution driver remain separate.
