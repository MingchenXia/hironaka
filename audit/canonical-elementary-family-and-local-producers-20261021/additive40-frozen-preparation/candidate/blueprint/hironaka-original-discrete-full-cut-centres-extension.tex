\section{Whole actual centres above an initially discrete full reference cut}

The original cosupport may be connected, positive-dimensional and
noncompact. Only its actual initial strict full-value upper cut is assumed
discrete. The cut and every current compact high-value locus are literal
sets of the original invariant; no auxiliary ideal replaces them.

\begin{lemma}[Actual canonical histories bound every current value by its original value]
\label{hir:original-full-cut-canonical-initial-bound}
\uses{hir:centre-function-seq}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.canonical_value_le_initial,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.canonical_step_value_le}
\leanok
For any proved resolution invariant and actual canonical finite prefix,
each current cosupport point has value at most the value of its original
projected point at the empty prefix. Every actual empty position is retained.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual prefix. The empty case is the literal identity. For a
last nonempty whole-maximum centre, the genuine decrease field gives a
strict decrease above the centre and equality away from it. For an empty
centre use the genuine empty-step field. The actual test transform places
each new cosupport point above an old cosupport point, so the induction
bound applies. Compose these inequalities and the actual projection formula.
No canonicality is inferred for a globally parallel history.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The whole current high cut is a true compact subset of an isolated proper fibre]
\label{hir:original-discrete-full-cut-compact-current-high-locus}
\uses{hir:original-full-cut-canonical-initial-bound,hir:centre-function-seq}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.strictCut,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.isClosed_strictCut,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.cutWindow,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.current_high_cut_subset_fibre,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.isCompact_current_high_cut}
\leanok
Let $F=\{x\in\operatorname{cosupp}(M):\lambda<\operatorname{inv}(M,\mathrm{id},x)\}$
be the actual initial strict cut and assume its subtype is discrete.
Internally choose an original isolating open $U_a$ for each $a\in F$.
For every actual canonical prefix of the literal restricted original record,
its whole current strict cut is closed and contained in the inverse image
of the single original point $a$. It is compact. The entire original
cosupport in $U_a$ need not be compact.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The true local upper-bound form of upper semicontinuity and closedness of
the current cosupport prove that every strict upper cut is closed: outside
the cosupport use its open complement, and at a point whose value is at most
$\lambda$ use the actual upper-bound neighbourhood. Discreteness supplies
an isolating ambient open with literal trace $U_a\cap F=\{a\}$.
The proved canonical initial bound and actual empty-prefix open naturality
put every current high point above $F\cap U_a$, hence over $a$.
The genuine finite restricted prefix projection is proper. The inverse
image of its actual singleton base point is therefore compact; its closed
subset given by the whole strict high cut is compact as well. No desired
compactness premise enters this argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[True high-cut maxima give an actual global refinement]
\label{hir:original-discrete-full-cut-global-refinement}
\uses{hir:original-discrete-full-cut-compact-current-high-locus,hir:invariant-driver-seq}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.selectedMaximum,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.parallelCutCentre,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.preimage_parallelCutCentre,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.parallelCutCentre_subset_initial_cut,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.selectedMaximum_nonempty_of_compact_high_cut,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalDiscreteFullCutCentres.exists_discrete_cut_refinement}
\leanok
Suppose an actual original test prefix has actual canonical restrictions
on these internally chosen isolating opens. In each window use its entire
local maximum if the whole current strict high cut is nonempty, and the
empty centre otherwise. Their whole union is a globally closed smooth
original-admissible centre lying over $F$. Its actual proper blowup advances
every same restricted canonical prefix, padding empty when its high cut is
already empty. If a current high cut is nonempty, its selected centre is
nonempty and meets the same isolated proper fibre.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A maximum attained on the compact nonempty high cut dominates the whole
local cosupport: points outside the high cut have value at most $\lambda$,
strictly below its maximum. Thus the selected whole maximum is nonempty;
every one of its points is itself high. The actual admissible-maximum
producer applies to the same restricted original canonical prefix. Empty
selected centres are genuinely admissible. The initial bound places every
chosen centre over the same original $F$.

Two isolating windows overlap only off $F$. Consequently a centre from one
window has empty trace in any different window. This proves the literal
whole local trace equation for their union. Over $F$ the actual pullback
window gives a genuine admissible local centre; off the closed initial cut
the whole union is empty on the inverse image of its open complement.
Locality proves global closedness, smoothness, cosupport inclusion and
normal crossings with the entire current ordered boundary. The original
nonzero-domain condition gives empty interior, so actual blowup existence
supplies a genuine proper global blowup. Its exact restricted centre traces
are either the whole local maximum or empty, and therefore extend the same
actual original canonical histories. No whole-space maximum is used.
\end{proof}
