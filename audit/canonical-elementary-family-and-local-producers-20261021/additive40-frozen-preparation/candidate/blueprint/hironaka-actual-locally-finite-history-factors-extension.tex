\begin{lemma}[Genuine lift uniqueness on an actual restricted continuation]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-restricted-lift-uniqueness}
\uses{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-limit-complex-manifold,hir:actual-tower-tail-holomorphic-lift-uniqueness}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryFactors.restricted_lift_unique}
\leanok
Fix an original open $U$ and actual indices $k\leq k+r$. Two holomorphic
maps from the stage-$(k+r)$ inverse image of $U$ to the stage-$k$ inverse
image of $U$ which commute with the actual whole base projections are equal.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual recorded tail from $k$ to $k+r$ is an actual finite blowup tower.
Restrict it to the actual stage-$k$ inverse image of $U$. The actual append
identity identifies its final open with the stage-$(k+r)$ inverse image of
$U$, and its composite projection with the actual later restricted prefix.
Precompose the proposed maps by this genuine equal-open biholomorphism.
The actual-tower lift uniqueness theorem, applied to the restricted
stage-$k$ prefix and this genuine restricted tail, identifies each map with
the same actual tail projection. Undoing the equal-open map proves equality.
The invertibility required for universal lift uniqueness is internally
provided by the actual first blowup and actual continuation; it is not an
additional desired-map assumption.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual local finite-stage factors and their whole overlap equality]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-local-finite-stage-factors}

append identity proves the whole original projection equation.

On the intersection of two original windows pass to the actual common
late prefix of index $k+(N_x+N_y)$. Its genuine late-prefix maps to the two
chosen endpoints have their actual whole projection equations. Actual
finite-prefix biholomorphic uniqueness identifies their comparison with
the same already chosen overlap map. The two proposed finite-stage
factors, after this precomposition, are holomorphic maps over the same
actual restricted stage-$k$ prefix. The restricted lift uniqueness lemma
makes them equal. Undoing the genuine common late-prefix biholomorphism
proves whole overlap equality, including exceptional points.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual finite prefix receives a proper holomorphic factor]
