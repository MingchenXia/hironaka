\section{Actual late-prefix comparisons for locally finite histories}

The history here is an actual growing sequence of global blowups, whose
actual centre images are locally finite. No canonical invariant, original
marked record, compatible finite-history atlas or overlap comparison is an
input. These are coherence producers for the composite of a genuine locally
finite history, rather than an existence assumption for such a history.

\begin{lemma}[Actual local finiteness internally produces stabilizing windows]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-stabilizing-windows}
\uses{hir:growing-sequence-actual-centres,hir:growing-sequence-actual-tail-tower,hir:growing-sequence-empty-restricted-tails}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryComparison.exists_actual_stabilizing_window}
\leanok
For an actual growing sequence with locally finite actual centre images,
every original base point has an actual open neighbourhood $U$ and an index
$N$ such that every actual centre after $N$ misses its prefix inverse image
of $U$. Every finite actual tail from $N$, restricted above $U$, is an
all-empty tower. No invariant or global maximum is needed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Local finiteness supplies a neighbourhood meeting only finitely many actual
centre images. Choose an actual open neighbourhood inside it and choose
$N$ strictly above the finitely many indices meeting the larger neighbourhood.
A later centre over this open would put its actual image among those finitely
many indices, a contradiction. The actual tail tower is constructed from the
recorded successor maps. Restricting every genuine step above this avoided
open makes its centre empty, proving the all-empty assertion for every finite
tail, including the zero tail.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual late prefixes have unique whole comparisons]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-late-prefix-comparison}
\uses{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-stabilizing-windows,hir:original-all-empty-tower-projection,hir:original-actual-whole-projection-biholomorphic-map-uniqueness}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryComparison.exists_unique_late_prefix_comparison,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryComparison.latePrefixComparison,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryComparison.latePrefixComparison_proj}
\leanok
On an actual stabilizing window $U$ from index $N$, any two late actual
restricted prefixes $T_i|_U$ and $T_j|_U$, with $i,j\ge N$, have a unique
actual endpoint biholomorphism $\beta_{ij}$ such that
$T_i|_U\circ\beta_{ij}=T_j|_U$. The comparison is constructed from the same
recorded history, without an incoming endpoint map or comparison certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First compare any actual prefix $T_{N+r}|_U$ to $T_N|_U$. The literal
constructed tail from $N$ to $N+r$ restricts to an all-empty tower. Its
whole actual projection is a biholomorphism. The exact equality obtained by
appending this actual tail to $T_N$ identifies its domain with the actual
inverse image of $U$ at stage $N+r$. The real projection formula therefore
constructs a biholomorphism to the stage-$N$ inverse image, commuting with the
whole original restricted prefix projections. Compose the stage-$j$ map
with the inverse of the stage-$i$ map to obtain the desired comparison.
Two such endpoint biholomorphisms have equal composites with the actual
finite restricted tower projection. Genuine whole-projection uniqueness for
actual blowup towers forces them to agree. It requires no canonicity or
terminal ideal assumption.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual late-prefix identity, inverse and cocycle]
\label{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-comparison-cocycle}
\uses{hir:actual-locally-finite-history-late-prefix-comparison}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryComparison.latePrefixComparison_self,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryComparison.latePrefixComparison_symm,Hironaka.HironakaActualLocallyFiniteHistoryComparison.latePrefixComparison_trans}
\leanok
These same constructed comparisons satisfy $\beta_{ii}=\mathrm{id}$,
$\beta_{ij}^{-1}=\beta_{ji}$ and
$\beta_{ij}\circ\beta_{jk}=\beta_{ik}$ on every actual stabilizing window.
Every recorded empty position remains part of the underlying prefixes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual identity map commutes with the same prefix projection, so the
proved uniqueness identifies it with $\beta_{ii}$. The genuine inverse
commutes with the two projections by applying the original equation at its
inverse point; uniqueness identifies it with $\beta_{ji}$. The actual
composite of two comparisons commutes with the original projection by their
two proved whole projection equations, so uniqueness identifies it with the
third comparison. All maps act on the actual restricted endpoint manifolds;
no deletion of empty positions or common raw stopping index is used.
\end{proof}
