\begin{theorem}[Every whole relative homogeneous initial relation lifts for the same prescribed normalized family]
\label{hir:actual-relative-normalized-polynomial-relation-lifts}
\uses{hir:actual-prescribed-canonical-relative-initial-syzygy,hir:actual-normal-polynomial-blowup-factorization,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-exceptional-quotient-formal-dictionary,hir:actual-relative-homogeneous-dehomogenization-injective}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeNormalizedSaturation.relative_normalized_polynomial_relation_lift}
\leanok
Under the prescribed canonical-family and genuine generator-power hypotheses,
let $f_a$ be the actual normal homogeneous polynomial of $G_a$ of degree $w_a$.
For $D\ge w_a$ and actual $p_a\in B[z]$ homogeneous of degree $D-w_a$,
any whole polynomial relation $\sum_ap_af_a=0$ produces actual analytic $L_a$
with $\sum_aH_aL_a=0$ and $L_a-\operatorname{ev}(\delta p_a)\in(e)$.
The normalized family $H$ is unchanged before every such relation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Genuine normal-filtered canonical owner division lifts the given whole polynomial
relation to an actual source syzygy $\sum_aG_aC_a=0$ with
$\operatorname{ev}(p_a)-C_a\in N^{D+1-w_a}$. Homogeneity gives
$\operatorname{ev}(p_a)\in N^{D-w_a}$; descending one power then gives
$C_a\in N^{D-w_a}$. Actual analytic exceptional division produces $L_a$ with
$e^{D-w_a}L_a=\pi^*C_a$. Multiply $\sum_aH_aL_a$ by $e^D$, use the
prescribed quotient equations and $w_a+(D-w_a)=D$, and substitute the true
source syzygy. The genuine nonzero coordinate germ and analytic domain cancel
$e^D$, giving the whole target relation.

Apply actual exceptional division to $C_a-\operatorname{ev}(p_a)$ at order
$D+1-w_a$, obtaining $R_a$. Genuine homogeneous normal-polynomial
factorization gives $\pi^*\operatorname{ev}(p_a)=e^{D-w_a}
\operatorname{ev}(\delta p_a)$, retaining every tangent coefficient.
Subtracting the two displayed quotient equations and cancelling the genuine
nonzero $e^{D-w_a}$ gives $L_a=\operatorname{ev}(\delta p_a)+eR_a$.
This proves the actual principal-ideal congruence without a desired target
relation or remainder-order hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole prescribed relative normalized quotient is exceptional-regular]
\label{hir:actual-relative-normalized-family-exceptional-regular}
\uses{hir:actual-relative-normalized-polynomial-relation-lifts,hir:actual-relative-exceptional-fibre-complete-polynomial-syzygies,hir:actual-relative-finite-polynomial-relation-rehomogenization,hir:actual-normal-quotient-canonical-exceptional-restriction}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeNormalizedSaturation.relative_normalized_family_exceptional_regular}
\leanok
For the same prescribed family and genuine powers, let $J=(H_a)$ be its whole
actual analytic ideal. Then $eg\in J$ if and only if $g\in J$, for every
whole original analytic germ $g$. In particular every power of $e$ cancels
in this actual quotient, including the zero quotient and empty family cases.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine initial quotient restriction gives
$H_a-\operatorname{ev}(\delta f_a)\in(e)$ for each member of this exact $H$.
The polynomials $\delta f_a$ have no pivot variable. If
$eg=\sum_aH_aA_a$, subtract the actual initial congruences to obtain the
whole analytic fibre relation $\sum_a\operatorname{ev}(\delta f_a)A_a\in(e)$.
Complete actual relative fibre flatness produces finitely many entire
pivot-free polynomial syzygies $c_{al}$ with
$A_a-\sum_l\operatorname{ev}(c_{al})B_l\in(e)$ and
$\sum_a(\delta f_a)c_{al}=0$.

For each finite column, genuine rehomogenization over the original analytic
coefficient ring constructs one degree and actual homogeneous polynomials
$P_{al}$ with $\delta P_{al}=c_{al}$ and $\sum_aP_{al}f_a=0$.
Apply the preceding genuine analytic relation lift to this column. It produces
$L_{al}$ with $\sum_aH_aL_{al}=0$ and
$L_{al}-\operatorname{ev}(c_{al})\in(e)$, for this same $H$.
Thus $A_a-\sum_lL_{al}B_l=eD_a$ for actual $D_a$.
Substituting in the original expression for $eg$, the finite column syzygies
cancel and give $eg=e\sum_aH_aD_a$. Actual domain cancellation yields
$g\in J$. The reverse direction is genuine ideal multiplication. Induction
then cancels every power of the exceptional germ; no completed-relation or
exceptional-regularity certificate has entered the proof.
\end{proof}
