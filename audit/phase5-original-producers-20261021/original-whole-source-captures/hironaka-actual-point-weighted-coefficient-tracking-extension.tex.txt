\section{The same canonical weighted coefficients through an actual point chart}

Fix the literal splitting $\mathbb C^{s+t}=\mathbb C^s_y\times\mathbb C^t_z$.
The point chart has a pivot in the $y$ block and arbitrary base directions,
with the actual normal directions equal to zero. This is an explicit chart
qualification. No maximal-contact or boundary compatibility for arbitrary
chart directions is inferred. All coefficient weights are the original
positive differences $w_a-|\gamma|$, and their common positive mark is their
literal product $D$. No radical or mark-one replacement is made.

\begin{theorem}[Actual point powers are exactly the finite weighted coefficient powers]
\label{hir:actual-point-power-finite-weighted-coefficient-iff}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-centre-power-relative-coefficients,hir:actual-analytic-formal-tail-coordinate-power-contraction,hir:actual-weighted-compression-exact-centre-admissibility}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointWeightedCoefficientTracking.mem_point_power_iff_low_normal_coefficients,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointWeightedCoefficientTracking.compressed_coefficients_point_power_iff}
\leanok
For every actual analytic germ $g(y,z)$ and natural $q$,
\[
 g\in\mathfrak m_{y,z}^q\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \forall\gamma,\ |\gamma|<q\Longrightarrow
 c_\gamma(g)\in\mathfrak m_y^{q-|\gamma|}.
\]
Consequently for a finite family $H_a$ and original weights $w_a$, its
finite low-normal-coefficient compression belongs wholly to
$\mathfrak m_y^D$ exactly when every $H_a\in\mathfrak m_{y,z}^{w_a}$.
The low coefficient positions retain their original vertex labels.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The literal joined exponent $(\beta,\gamma)$ has total point degree
$|\beta|+|\gamma|$. The whole formal coefficient centre-power theorem with
all base variables selected therefore identifies membership in the entire
point power with the displayed low-normal-coefficient conditions on the
whole base series. The actual Taylor map commutes with the genuine analytic
normal coefficient germ. Both whole analytic point powers contract from
these formal point powers by the actual coordinate-tail dictionary, giving
both directions for the actual analytic germ. Terms with $|\gamma|\ge q$
need no condition. This includes $q=0$ and empty coordinate blocks.
For the finite compression, genuine positive-power reflection makes
$c_i^{D/w_i}\in\mathfrak m_y^D$ equivalent to
$c_i\in\mathfrak m_y^{w_i}$. Whole span membership is exactly membership
of every displayed generator. Reindex the finite pairs $(a,\gamma)$ and
apply the first equivalence to each $H_a$. The empty family has $D=1$ and
zero compressed ideal, so both sides remain true.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole current weighted coefficient ideal detects the true point HS profile]
\label{hir:actual-same-canonical-point-coefficient-control-hs-iff}
\uses{hir:actual-point-power-finite-weighted-coefficient-iff,hir:actual-original-arbitrary-point-hs-iff-orders-same-family,hir:actual-original-canonical-reduced-vertex-basis}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointWeightedCoefficientTracking.prescribed_canonical_coefficient_compression_hs_iff}
\leanok
Let $P$ be actual original analytic coefficient data whose WHOLE generator
Taylor series are the exact canonical reduced source series. For prescribed
actual point quotients $H_a$ of these SAME generators in the stated chart,
\[
 \operatorname{Compress}_{w_a-|\gamma|}(c_\gamma(H_a))
       \subset\mathfrak m_y^D
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \forall\ell,\operatorname{HS}_{\mathcal N_{e,b}(I)}(\ell)
       =\operatorname{HS}_I(\ell).
\]
The whole independently defined normalized ideal is generated by the
prescribed SAME $H_a$; no family, point-order or HS certificate is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compare the prescribed generators with the original canonical analytic
basis using their ENTIRE equal Taylor series and actual Taylor injectivity.
The original-only point theorem chooses canonical quotients with the whole
normalized span and the entire HS/order equivalence. Each prescribed quotient
has the SAME numerator, pivot and direction as that quotient. The original
coordinate germ is nonzero in the genuine analytic domain; cancellation of
its literal vertex power proves equality of the two actual quotients,
position by position. Thus the whole normalized-ideal span and the point
HS/order equivalence transfer to the prescribed family, without changing
its generators, coefficients or labels. Dimension reassociation transports
the original point coordinates and whole Taylor homomorphism explicitly.
For point centres, the original coefficient vertex weight is exactly the
original total vertex degree, since every source coordinate is selected.
Apply the first theorem to this same family and these exact positive
difference marks. This gives the displayed equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original-only one-chart weighted tracking with unchanged canonical data]
\label{hir:actual-original-point-weighted-coefficient-tracking}
\uses{hir:actual-same-canonical-point-coefficient-control-hs-iff,hir:actual-canonical-reduced-analytic-coefficient-data,hir:actual-coefficient-same-family-cosupport-shrink,hir:actual-coefficient-controlled-same-family-ideals}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualPointWeightedCoefficientTracking.exists_original_point_weighted_coefficient_tracking}
\leanok
An arbitrary original analytic ideal internally supplies ONE exact canonical
coefficient datum $P$, on one genuine original base open domain, before all
base pivots and directions. It has the actual low-coefficient centre cosupport
and a positive common mark. For every such point chart it supplies SAME
analytic normalized generators $H_a$, the exact low coefficient transform
identities, and the whole original powered-coefficient ideal equations
\[
 \pi_y^*\operatorname{Compress}(c(P))
    =(e^D)\operatorname{Compress}(c(H)),\qquad
 (\pi_y^*\operatorname{Compress}(c(P)):e^D)
    =\operatorname{Compress}(c(H)).
\]
This WHOLE controlled germ ideal lies in $\mathfrak m_y^D$ exactly when the
entire actual source/current normalized point HS profile is unchanged.
The whole normalized ideal is generated by these same actual $H_a$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the exact canonical reduced analytic coefficient data, rather than
arbitrary monic finite-jet representatives. Every original canonical series
belongs to its genuine full point power by its initial degree. Actual whole
Taylor contraction gives that point power for each SAME analytic generator.
The prescribed-datum coefficient-cosupport shrinking theorem now restricts
one real base domain while preserving the generators and coefficient
FUNCTIONS exactly. It therefore retains their whole canonical Taylor series
and provides all the actual source coefficient cosupports internally.
The common mark is positive because every retained difference is positive.
For each base pivot, apply the genuine prescribed-family controlled
coefficient theorem to this same datum. The literal zero normal directions
give $c_\gamma(\pi^*G_a)=e^{|\gamma|}\pi_y^*c_\gamma(G_a)$.
Actual analytic exceptional division constructs each quotient, and domain
cancellation gives the difference-mark coefficient equations. Compressing
with the unchanged common mark factors the ENTIRE ideal image by $e^D$;
true cancellation gives the whole colon identity. Apply the preceding
theorem to exactly these quotients, not a newly chosen transformed basis.
It supplies both the whole normalized span and the asserted HS equivalence.
\end{proof}

This is actual ONE-point-chart, whole germ-ideal tracking for the original
weighted presentation. It does not replace the controlled marked SHEAF stalk
dictionary, construct a compatible maximal-contact/boundary chart for every
persistent direction, prove nonzero invariant-domain membership of a possibly
zero coefficient ideal, or provide a current-history termination driver.
For an actual analytic set in ambient dimension at least two, the accepted
point saturation theorem separately identifies the normalized ideal with its
whole geometric strict ideal; that substitution is not assumed here.
