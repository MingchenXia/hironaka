\chapter{Whole zero-coordinate ideals and the specified intrinsic sheaf square}

Keep the original ordered coordinates $\mathbf C^n\oplus\mathbf C^m$.
Write $p,q$ for the two projections. All ideals below are the original
functional vanishing germ ideals, and all locally ringed spaces use their
actual reduced quotient structure sheaves.

\begin{lemma}[The entire ideal after adjoining zero coordinates]
\label{hir:actual-zero-coordinate-whole-functional-ideal}
\uses{hir:actual-graph-functional-ideal-every-point}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualIntrinsicStableIdealSquare.zero_coordinate_functional_mem_iff}
\leanok
For every subset $W\subset\mathbf C^n$, every $x\in\mathbf C^n$, and
every original function $F:\mathbf C^{n+m}\to\mathbf C$, the germ of $F$
at $(x,0)$ belongs to $\mathcal I_{W\times\{0\}}$ if and only if it is a
finite holomorphic linear combination of the entire family
\[
 \{a\circ p:a\in\mathcal I_{W,x}\}\ \cup\ \{y_1,\ldots,y_m\}.
\]
Here each $a$ is an original function representative holomorphic near $x$.
The statement includes all such representatives, not a prescribed finite
generating family, and does not require coherence or analyticity of $W$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the entire original functional graph ideal theorem to the constant
zero map $f:\mathbf C^n\to\mathbf C^m$. It is holomorphic near every
original point, its graph section is exactly $(x,0)$, and its graph carrier
is exactly $W\times\{0\}$. Its original graph equations are
$y_i-f_i(p(z))=y_i$. The base part of its generating family is precisely
all original representatives of $\mathcal I_{W,x}$ composed with $p$.
Substitution in both directions of the graph theorem proves the displayed
equivalence for every original $F$. Thus this is equality of the complete
functional germ ideals, including the finite holomorphic span operation;
it is not merely equality of their zero sets.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Whole ideal and marked-record comparison under the same shears]
\label{hir:actual-stable-plane-whole-ideal}
\uses{hir:actual-stable-ambient-subset-iso,hir:biholomorphism-transform}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualIntrinsicStableIdealSquare.stable_pullback_vanishingIdeal,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualIntrinsicStableIdealSquare.stable_pullback_marked}
\leanok
Use the actual opens and mutually inverse shears $\Psi:\Omega\to\Lambda$
from the stable ambient comparison. Assume the original subset identities
$f(C\cap A)\subset D\cap B$, $g(D\cap B)\subset C\cap A$, $gf=1$ on
$C\cap A$ and $fg=1$ on $D\cap B$. Put
$C_0=\Omega\cap(C\times\{0\})$ and
$D_0=\Lambda\cap(\{0\}\times D)$. Then
\[
 \Psi^*\mathcal I_{D_0}=\mathcal I_{C_0}.
\]
For the actual marked records with whole ambient carrier, empty ordered
boundary and marking one, the same map satisfies
\[
 \Psi^*(\Lambda,\varnothing,\mathcal I_{D_0},1)
   =(\Omega,\varnothing,\mathcal I_{C_0},1).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual subset comparison gives $\Psi^{-1}D_0=C_0$ for this same
biholomorphism. We spell out why this gives the full pullback ideal.
A target function vanishing near $\Psi(z)$ on $D_0$ pulls back to a
holomorphic function vanishing near $z$ on $C_0$. Finite holomorphic
linear combinations of these functions still vanish there. Conversely,
if $F$ vanishes near $z$ on $C_0$, then $F\circ\Psi^{-1}$ is a genuine
target holomorphic germ vanishing on $D_0$ near $\Psi(z)$, and its
pullback is exactly $F$. This is also the original biholomorphic
pullback theorem, with the identity map on the source and its whole
vanishing-ideal identity. Therefore the equality holds for all original
function representatives at every source point. In the marked record,
the inverse image of the whole carrier is whole, the inverse-image map
of the empty list is empty, the ideal field is the equality just proved,
and the positive marking remains one. These four fields give the full
record equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The whole quotient-sheaf square retains the specified isomorphism]
\label{hir:actual-stable-plane-specified-sheaf-square}
\uses{hir:actual-stable-ambient-subset-iso,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-holomorphic-map,hir:actual-analytic-subspace-intrinsic-map-faithful}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualIntrinsicStableIdealSquare.stable_specified_sheaf_square}
\leanok
Let $e:C\simeq D$ be a specified intrinsic isomorphism of the actual
original reduced models over the complex point, with $C\subset U$,
$D\subset V$. Suppose $A\subset U$, $B\subset V$ are the actual opens
above and $f(x)=e(x)$ for every original $x\in C\cap A$.
Let $E:C_0\simeq D_0$ be the actual quotient-subspace isomorphism
induced by the same $\Psi$. The two original coordinate projections
induce actual quotient-model maps $P:C_0\to C$ and $Q:D_0\to D$.
They satisfy the equality of entire locally ringed-space morphisms
\[
                  Q\circ E=e\circ P.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The first projection maps the true open $\Omega$ holomorphically into
$U$, because $p(z)\in A\subset U$. On $C_0$ its value belongs to $C$.
Likewise $q:\Lambda\to V$ is holomorphic and maps $D_0$ into $D$.
The actual quotient-map construction therefore supplies $P,Q$ on
the complete quotient sheaves, and both preserve the complex
structural morphism. The ambient-induced $E$ also preserves that
morphism, as does the given $e$. At every original point $z=(x,0)$ of
$C_0$, the second coordinate of $\Psi(z)$ is $f(x)=e(x)$. Hence the
two displayed composites have identical base maps. Faithfulness of
actual reduced model morphisms over the complex point gives equality
of the entire locally ringed-space maps. Its proof uses values of
all local quotient sections and their actual vanishing ideal, so
the conclusion is equality of the full sheaf maps, not an assumed
stalk comparison or a point-map-only conclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original intrinsic data internally produce the whole ideal/sheaf square]
\label{hir:actual-intrinsic-stable-ideal-square-producer}
\uses{hir:actual-intrinsic-iso-paired-original-windows,hir:actual-stable-plane-whole-ideal,hir:actual-stable-plane-specified-sheaf-square}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualIntrinsicStableIdealSquare.exists_actual_intrinsic_stable_ideal_square}
\leanok
Given only an original intrinsic isomorphism $e$ over the complex point,
original analytic models $C\subset U$, $D\subset V$ and an original
point $c\in C$, there exist genuine opens $A\subset U$, $B\subset V$
containing $c,e(c)$ and actual holomorphic representatives $f,g$ such
that the same internally constructed stable ambient biholomorphism
has both whole vanishing-ideal and marked-record equalities of the
second lemma and the entire specified-$e$ quotient-sheaf square of
the third lemma. No ambient extension, prescribed comparison map,
ideal equality or descent witness is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original paired-window producer applied to $e$ and $c$ constructs
$A,B,f,g$ internally. It gives all four subset inverse identities,
holomorphy on those true opens, and the identity $f(x)=e(x)$ for
every original $x\in C\cap A$. Apply the whole-ideal lemma to
these exact data, giving the ideal equality and the four-field
marked-record equality for their same shears. Apply the sheaf-square
lemma to those same data and to the original $e$. Its coordinate
projections are constructed from $A\subset U$, $B\subset V$;
faithfulness supplies their entire sheaf square. The paired-window
membership statements place $c,e(c)$ in the produced opens. All
required output maps, ideal equalities and sheaf equalities have
therefore been constructed from the original inputs.
\end{proof}

This proves the whole ideal and specified intrinsic sheaf bridge for
added zero-coordinate planes. Invariance of canonical invariant tails,
centres and histories under adjoining the zero-coordinate generators,
and proper resolution descent over an abstract analytic atlas, remain
separate proof obligations. No smooth cylinder base-change statement
is used to discharge either obligation.
