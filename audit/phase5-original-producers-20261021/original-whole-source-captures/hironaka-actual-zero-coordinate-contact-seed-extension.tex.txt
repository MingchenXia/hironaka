\chapter{The actual marking-one zero-coordinate contact seed}

Let $W\subset\mathbf C^n$ be an original analytic subset. In the same
original coordinates $(x,z)\in\mathbf C^{n+1}$ put
$J=\mathcal I_{W\times\{0\}}$, $Z=\{z=0\}$, and
$M_J=(\mathbf C^{n+1},\mathbf C^{n+1},[],J,1)$. Let $p(x,z)=x$
and $s(x)=(x,0)$. No canonical value or comparison history is an input.

\begin{lemma}[The original added coordinate supplies genuine maximal contact]
\label{hir:actual-zero-coordinate-genuine-contact-seed}
\uses{hir:actual-graph-normal-tangent-direction,mc:homogenized-along-maximal-order}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroCoordinateContactSeed.zero_coordinate_tangent,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroCoordinateContactSeed.zero_coordinate_maximal_order}
\leanok
The same coordinate $z$ is a genuine marking-one tangent direction
of $J$ along the whole ambient manifold, on the whole ambient open.
The original ideal has maximal order at most one everywhere.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The zero graph equation is precisely $z$, so the actual graph-normal
contact theorem gives membership in $J$ at every point and normal
derivative exactly one. The whole-ambient equivalence between ordinary
and along-carrier tangent directions therefore gives the claimed
actual tangent direction. In the preferred Euclidean chart, the
normal derivative function of this same $z$ is identically one.
Since $z\in J$, this function belongs to the actual derivative ideal
$\mathcal D J$ at every point. Thus $\mathcal D J=\mathcal O$ in the
marking-one maximal-order sense. Its whole-ambient along-carrier
equivalence gives maximal order at most one. No supplied tangent or
order certificate was used.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual StepOne object retains exactly the same marked ideal]
\label{hir:actual-zero-coordinate-stepone-object}
\uses{mc:homogenized-basic,mc:homogenized-along-basic,mc:coefficient-ideal}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroCoordinateContactSeed.stepOne_zero_marked,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroCoordinateContactSeed.coefficient_stepOne_zero_marked}
\leanok
For the actual definitions used by the canonical construction,
\[
 \operatorname{stepOneMarked}(\mathbf C^{n+1},J,1)=M_J,
 \qquad \mathcal C_{\mathbf C^{n+1}}
     (\operatorname{stepOneMarked}(\mathbf C^{n+1},J,1))=M_J.
\]
These are equalities of the entire marked records, including their
carrier, ordered empty boundary, ideal and marking.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The zero-graph set is the intersection of $p^{-1}W$ with the zero
fibre of the actual normal projection, hence is analytic. Cartan
coherence therefore gives coherence of its original vanishing ideal
$J$. On the whole ambient carrier, along-carrier homogenization
equals ordinary homogenization. At marking one the latter is exactly
$J$. Thus the actual homogenized marked record is $M_J$.

At marking one the coefficient sum has just its index-zero summand.
Its derivative order is zero, and the product of the other marks is
the empty product one. The ideal power $J^1$ is exactly $J$: the
definition gives $\mathcal O\cdot J$, and finite holomorphic span
closure for the coherent ideal gives equality. The one-summand
finite ideal sum is likewise exactly $J$. The other three record
fields are literally unchanged. This proves the first actual
StepOne equality, and applying the same coefficient computation to
it proves the second.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual lower contact object and its entire original trace]
\label{hir:actual-zero-coordinate-whole-contact-lower-object}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-coordinate-whole-functional-ideal,hir:actual-zero-coordinate-stepone-object,hir:actual-graph-section-whole-ideal-pullback,hir:actual-holomorphic-graph-original-quotient}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroCoordinateContactSeed.zero_contact_restrictEquiv,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroCoordinateContactSeed.zero_section_pullback,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroCoordinateContactSeed.zeroQuotientAlgEquiv,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroCoordinateContactSeed.zeroQuotientAlgEquiv_mk}
\leanok
Restrict the actual coefficient object of the preceding lemma to the
same $Z$. Its entire marked record is restricted-equivalent to
$(\mathbf C^{n+1},Z,[],p^*\mathcal I_W,1)$. Moreover
$s^*J=\mathcal I_W$ as whole functional germ ideals, and the literal
zero-graph trace gives an actual algebra isomorphism
\[
 \mathcal O_{\mathbf C^{n+1},0}/J_0
       \simeq_{\mathbf C}\mathcal O_{\mathbf C^n,0}/\mathcal I_{W,0}.
\]
Every original numerator class is taken to its actual zero-section
restriction class.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At each point of $Z$, the entire zero-coordinate ideal theorem writes
every member of $J$ as a finite holomorphic sum of original base
vanishing pullbacks and the same literal normal coordinate. Each
base term belongs to $p^*\mathcal I_W$. On $Z$, each normal-coordinate
term vanishes, and hence has the empty restricted-span expression.
Substitution in the finite sum gives the required restricted ideal
inclusion. Conversely every base pullback vanishes on
$W\times\{0\}$, and finite holomorphic sums preserve this vanishing,
so the opposite inclusion holds even in the ambient ideal. The
carrier $Z$, boundary $[]$ and marking one agree literally, giving
the full restricted equivalence of marked records.

The actual graph-section pullback theorem for the zero graph proves
$s^*J=\mathcal I_W$ for every original representative at every base
point. At the origin, the entire graph trace theorem identifies
$J_0$ with the inverse image of $\mathcal I_{W,0}$ under the actual
surjective trace. Its quotient algebra isomorphism therefore gives
the displayed full quotient. The original graph quotient numerator
formula, specialized to the zero graph, proves the assertion for
every numerator. All these constructions use this same $J,z,Z,s$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original analytic data produce the actual contact-tail seed]
\label{hir:actual-original-zero-coordinate-contact-tail-seed}
\uses{hir:actual-zero-coordinate-genuine-contact-seed,hir:actual-zero-coordinate-stepone-object,hir:actual-zero-coordinate-whole-contact-lower-object}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualZeroCoordinateContactSeed.original_zero_coordinate_contact_tail_seed}
\leanok
The original analytic $W$ internally supplies all the preceding
conclusions for the same actual $J$ and $z$. For every genuine open
$A\subset\mathbf C^{n+1}$, the actual canonical-construction
\texttt{contactMarked} at the empty prefix, using this $z$, is
exactly the open restriction to $A$ of $(Z,[],J,1)$. Thus its full
marked record, restricted ideal and quotient seed are established
before any comparison of target values or histories is requested.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the analytic zero graph and its coherence internally as
above, and apply the first three lemmas. Unfold the actual
\texttt{contactMarked} definition at the empty prefix. Its
transformed StepOne record is its original record, its second
coefficient operation is exactly the one in the second lemma, and
the strict transform of $\mathbf C^{n+1}\cap\{z=0\}$ is the same
$Z$. It is therefore literally the open pullback to $A$ of
$(Z,[],J,1)$. This uses no chosen lower value, canonical prefix,
comparison history or descent certificate. The full ideal and
quotient conclusions are those already constructed for this same
original family.
\end{proof}

This is a contact-tail seed in the actual canonical construction.
It does not yet identify the inductive invariant on this closed
contact embedding with the lower ambient invariant, prove stability
of canonical centres or histories under adding zero-coordinate
generators, or prove proper resolution descent. In particular it
does not substitute smooth cylinder base change for those claims.
