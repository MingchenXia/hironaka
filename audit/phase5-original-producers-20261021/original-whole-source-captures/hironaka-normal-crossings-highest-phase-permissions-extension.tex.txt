\section{Genuine local old-branch permissions at every highest-phase centre}
\label{sec:nc-highest-phase-permissions}

The original marked carrier in the producer below is the whole ambient
manifold. Its ideal and positive mark are unchanged. The retained and
born boundary remains an ordered SNC list, while the old carrier may
have several local branches. The condition defined here records actual
local centre admissibility and does not infer that condition from a
final NC pair. This chapter does not assert the still separate finite
phase-composition, arbitrary smooth-carrier or canonical naturality
endpoint.

\begin{definition}[Actual local joint old-branch centre permission]
\label{hir:nc-local-joint-branch-centre-permission}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestPhasePermissions.HasLocalJointBranchCentre}
\leanok
For a current marked record $A$, old carrier $H$ and centre $C$, require
that every $y\in C$ has a genuine open neighbourhood $V$ and a finite
list $B$ of its actual old branches such that $H|_V=\bigcup B$ and
$B\mathbin{+\!+}E_A|_V$ is SNC. Require moreover that, for \emph{every}
permutation $F$ of $B$, the actual centre $C|_V$ is an admissible centre
of the \emph{same} restricted marked record with boundary
$F\mathbin{+\!+}E_A|_V$. Its carrier, ideal and marking remain the
literal restricted original fields. The entire retained and born list
keeps its order and every empty position.
\uses{hir:marked-ideal}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Whole actual old-branch permission history]
\label{hir:nc-joint-branch-permission-history}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestPhasePermissions.HasJointBranchPermissions}
\leanok
The identity history has this property. An actual appended blowup has
this property precisely when its preceding history has it and its last
centre has actual local joint old-branch permission for the literal
strict old carrier and the entire transformed marked record at that
stage. Thus the condition retains permission at every genuine centre,
including empty-centre positions; it is not only an endpoint condition.
\uses{hir:nc-local-joint-branch-centre-permission}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real highest-stratum test histories derive all old-branch permissions]
\label{hir:nc-highest-test-history-all-joint-permissions}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestPhasePermissions.permissions_of_test_highest,TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestPhasePermissions.HasJointBranchPermissions.head}
\leanok
Let $(H,E)$ be an original adapted pair with original global old-count
bound $r$. Any \emph{actual} original marked test history whose actual
centres lie in the strict transforms of the original highest $r$-cut
has a whole actual old-branch permission history. Every actual prefix
of this same history has that property as well. No old-branch
permission, branch matching or local test-history certificate is an
assumption.
\uses{hir:nc-joint-branch-permission-history,hir:nc-highest-joint-retained-boundary-histories,hir:nc-entire-old-retained-born-boundary,hir:tower-split}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Consider an actual last step with preceding prefix $P$ and centre $C$.
For $y\in C$, real highest-cut centre containment places $y$ in the
strict transform of the original highest cut $Z$. The actual strict
transform is contained in $P^{-1}Z$, since $Z$ is closed. Hence
$x=P(y)$ lies in $Z$. The actual joint-branch history theorem supplies
an original neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ and its genuine old branch list
$B$, with SNC of $B$ followed by the entire original boundary trace,
and a true joint marked test history for the restriction of the
\emph{whole} given history. This is derived from its real parent
testness and real highest-cut centre containment.

Set $V=P^{-1}U$. At the stage immediately preceding the last step, take
the literal list $B_P$ of strict transforms of the original $B$ along
$P|_U$. Exact finite strict-union compatibility and actual restriction
identify the trace of $\operatorname{strict}_P H$ with $\bigcup B_P$.
The genuine joint test history is well formed at that stage, and its
whole boundary is exactly $B_P$ followed by the entire current parent
boundary trace, retaining every original and born position. Thus this
whole joint list is SNC. The actual last-step test condition gives NC
of the actual centre trace with this very list.

The parent last-step admissibility pulls back to $V$: its actual centre
is smooth and lies in the actual same restricted controlled cosupport.
Replacing only its boundary by $B_P$ followed by the whole current list
does not change this carrier, ideal, marking or cosupport. Combined
with the genuine joint NC condition, this proves actual admissibility
for the augmented local record. For any permutation of $B_P$, the
augmented boundary has the same members; transporting its NC charts
through that actual member correspondence preserves the centre's NC
condition. Smoothness and cosupport are unchanged. This proves all
local permissions, including every permutation, at the last centre.

Structural induction on the actual tower proves the history condition:
the identity has no centres, and the preceding argument supplies the
last permission after the induction hypothesis supplies all earlier
ones. For an actual prefix cut, induction on the same tower and the
number of removed final steps gives its permission history: removing
zero steps retains the full history, and removing a further last step
uses the genuine preceding history condition. Thus no permission is
recovered merely from endpoint geometry.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original data internally produce an attained highest phase with all actual permissions]
\label{hir:nc-original-highest-phase-all-joint-permissions}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestPhasePermissions.exists_original_highest_phase_with_permissions}
\leanok
Let the original coherent positive-mark ideal have compact whole
controlled cosupport, and let the actual adapted old carrier $H$ be
nonempty. The original data internally select its attained positive
highest intrinsic branch count $r$, its whole highest stratum and a
genuine coefficient-stratum resolving phase. This same phase is a true
original marked test tower, has centres in the literal highest-stratum
strict transforms, and has the entire actual old-branch permission
history. It retains a global adapted old/current-boundary pair and
supplies an actual open containing the whole current original
cosupport on which old count is at most $r-1$. No maximum, phase,
permission history or desired centre geometry is an input.
\uses{hir:nc-highest-test-history-all-joint-permissions,hir:nc-original-phase-whole-cosupport-count-drop,hir:general-nc-actual-highest-stratum-exists}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine intrinsic old branch counts lie in the finite set
$\{0,\ldots,n\}$. The nonempty original carrier supplies a positive
count. The largest actual count is therefore positive, bounded by the
ambient dimension and attained; every original point has count at
most this very $r$. Apply the actual original coefficient-stratum phase
producer to the same ideal, positive marking, compact cosupport,
adapted pair and this internally obtained bound. It produces the real
whole coefficient resolution, original parent testness, actual
highest-cut centre containment, global adapted current pair and the
literal whole-cosupport open count drop. Apply the preceding theorem
to this \emph{same} internally constructed tower, its real testness and
its real centre containment. This derives the entire old-branch
permission history. The chosen $r$, its attainment and every history
field are outputs of the actual construction; no permission or
progress certificate is substituted for a producer.
\end{proof}
