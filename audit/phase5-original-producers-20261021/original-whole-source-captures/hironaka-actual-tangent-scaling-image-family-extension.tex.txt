\section{Actual tangent-scaling image vertex families}

Write $R=\mathbb C[[u_1,\ldots,u_d,z_1,\ldots,z_t]]$,
$\mathfrak m=(u,z)$ and $N=(z)$. For a normal pivot $e$, the actual
tangent-scaling map $\sigma$ replaces $u_i$ by $eu_i$ and fixes every
normal variable, including $e$. Let $\rho$ be the actual normal retraction
which sets all $u_i$ equal to zero. Every polynomial in $(e,z)$ is fixed by
$\sigma$; no independence of $e$ is asserted.

\begin{lemma}[Actual normal powers make the matching total component tangent-free]
\label{hir:actual-normal-order-total-component-tangent-free}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-coordinate-power-coefficient-kernel,hir:actual-homogeneous-initial-component-derivative-dictionaries}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualTangentScalingImageFamily.homogeneousComponent_normal_only}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualTangentScalingImageFamily.current_vertex_tangent_zero_of_normal_components}
\leanok
If $F\in N^w$, then its total degree-$w$ component $C=F_w$ has zero
coefficient at every exponent with a nonzero tangent coordinate. Thus
$\rho C=C$, and every tangent partial derivative of $C$ vanishes. If the
whole initial ideal of $K$ is generated by such components, it is stable
under every tangent partial derivative, and every actual degreeLex minimal
vertex of $K$ has zero tangent block.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A possibly nonzero coefficient of $C$ has total degree $w$. If a tangent
exponent is positive, its normal weight is strictly smaller than $w$, so
its coefficient in $F$ vanishes by the whole normal-power coefficient
criterion. This proves the support assertion and the retraction identity.
A coefficient of a tangent derivative is an integer multiple of precisely
such an excluded coefficient, hence is zero. Leibniz and whole ideal-span
induction show that the span of these components is stable under tangent
partials. At a minimal vertex with a positive tangent exponent, differentiating
a monic leading witness gives the strictly smaller leading exponent obtained
by subtracting the corresponding tangent unit; its coefficient is nonzero
over $\mathbb C$. This contradicts actual diagram minimality. The genuine
homogeneous-initial diagram equality transfers this conclusion to $K$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The first normal pivot is absent from genuine current minimal vertices]
\label{hir:actual-first-normal-pivot-minimal-vertices}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-order-total-component-tangent-free,hir:actual-current-colons-homogeneous-initial-pivot-cancellation}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualTangentScalingImageFamily.current_vertex_first_normal_pivot_zero}
\leanok
Suppose $\operatorname{in}_{\mathfrak m}K=(C_i)$ with every $C_i$
tangent-free, and
$(K+\mathfrak m^{q+1}):e=K+\mathfrak m^q$ for all $q$.
Use the genuine degreeLex order with $e$ the first normal coordinate.
Then every actual minimal vertex of $K$ has zero $e$ exponent as well as
zero tangent block.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The preceding lemma gives the zero tangent block. Choose a monic witness
in the whole initial ideal at a minimal vertex $c$, retain its component
of degree $|c|$ and apply $\rho$. The initial ideal is preserved by $\rho$,
because it is generated by the fixed $C_i$. The result remains homogeneous,
monic at $c$, and zero at every earlier exponent, since $c$ is tangent-free.
If $c_e>0$, every coefficient whose pivot exponent is zero vanishes: a
nonzero coefficient must be tangent-free and have degree $|c|$, so it
would be earlier than $c$ at the first normal coordinate. The whole witness
is therefore $eQ$. The genuine coefficient shift makes $Q$ homogeneous of
degree $|c|-1$. Actual homogeneous initial cancellation, supplied by the
whole current jet colons, puts $Q$ in the same initial ideal. Its leading
exponent plus the pivot unit equals $c$, by the true product-leading theorem.
This is a strictly smaller componentwise diagram member below the actual
minimal vertex, a contradiction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite homogeneous multiplier bands give actual analytic image witnesses]
\label{hir:actual-tangent-scaling-analytic-image-witnesses}
\uses{hir:actual-exceptional-family-homogeneous-multiplier-bands,hir:actual-normal-order-total-component-tangent-free,hir:actual-first-normal-pivot-minimal-vertices,hir:actual-tangent-scaling-analytic-filtration}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualTangentScalingImageFamily.actualTangentScaledTaylorFamily}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualTangentScalingImageFamily.exists_tangentScaling_analytic_image_family}
\leanok
Let $h_i$ be a finite actual analytic family, set $H_i=\sigma h_i$, and
assume $H_i\in N^{d_i}$. Suppose the actual whole initial ideal of
$K=(\widehat H_i)$ is generated by $C_i=(\widehat H_i)_{d_i}$, and actual current jet colons as above. For each genuine minimal vertex
$c$ there are actual finite homogeneous polynomials $q_{ic}(e,z)$, zero
when $d_i>|c|$, such that
\[
 B_c=\sigma\Bigl(\sum_i q_{ic}(e,z)h_i\Bigr)
      =\sum_i q_{ic}(e,z)H_i
\]
is in the whole analytic family span, belongs to $N^{|c|}$ and has monic
Taylor coefficient at $c$ and zero Taylor coefficients at every earlier
exponent. The vertices have zero tangent and pivot exponents. Consequently
the Taylor series of the $B_c$ form a genuine monic vertex family for $K$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the tangent-free homogeneous monic initial witness $P_c$ from the
preceding proof. Actual homogeneous filtered representation in the whole
component span writes $P_c=\sum_i C_i A_{ic}$, with $A_{ic}$ homogeneous
of degree $|c|-d_i$ and zero for $d_i>|c|$. Apply $\rho$ to this entire
finite identity; it fixes $P_c$ and the $C_i$. Each $\rho A_{ic}$ is a
homogeneous finite series in the normal variables, hence is its literal
finite polynomial $q_{ic}(e,z)$. This produces the coefficients rather
than supplying a polynomial relation. Since $\sigma$ fixes every such
polynomial, the two displayed whole analytic expressions agree.

Each nonzero summand of $B_c$ lies in $N^{|c|}$: its normal polynomial
has degree $|c|-d_i$ and $H_i\in N^{d_i}$. The total degree-$|c|$
component of its Taylor series is exactly $\sum_i C_iq_{ic}=P_c$.
The normal power kills every lower total degree, and $P_c$ kills every
earlier exponent of the same degree; a higher degree cannot be earlier
in degreeLex. Its coefficient at $c$ is one. Whole span membership follows
from the displayed finite analytic combination. Choosing these actual
witnesses for each genuine minimal vertex gives the stated monic family.
No assertion that this family is the canonical reduced family is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same original relative quotients internally supply the image family]
\label{hir:actual-original-relative-current-image-monic-family}
\uses{hir:actual-relative-point-prescribed-normalized-family,hir:actual-relative-point-same-family-current-initial-generation,hir:actual-canonical-point-normalized-strict-filtered-division,hir:actual-normal-reindex-chart-conjugacy,hir:actual-tangent-scaling-analytic-image-witnesses}
\lean{TranscendentalNakamaye.Pending.HironakaActualTangentScalingImageFamily.exists_original_relative_current_image_family}
\leanok
Let $I$ be an arbitrary actual original analytic germ ideal, and fix
actual original germs $G_a$ whose literal Taylor series are the canonical
reduced vertices of $\widehat I$. Fix prescribed
relative quotients $e^{d_a}h_a=\beta_{\mathrm{relative}}^*G_a$, where
$d_a$ is the original total vertex degree. If each current $h_a$ has
actual maximal order at least $d_a$, the literal tangent-scaled family,
after the genuine tangent-fixing normal permutation carrying $e$ to the
first normal coordinate, internally has the preceding analytic image monic
family. No whole initial-generation, current-colon or desired image-family
certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The exact relative-to-point chart square identifies $\sigma h_a$ with
the point quotients of the same original $G_a$ and direction. Equality
holds for each quotient by cancellation of the same nonzero pivot power.
The actual point-family theorem supplies whole shifted representations,
initial generation and every current jet colon, under precisely these
current order bounds. The actual scaling filtration sends the maximal
order of each $h_a$ to normal order $d_a$ of $\sigma h_a$.

Apply the true normal permutation. Its analytic and formal equivalences
preserve whole normal and maximal powers and give the literal chart square;
transport each whole shifted representation and current colon through the
actual equivalence. Obtain initial generation in the permuted ring by taking
components of these transported representations. No old degreeLex vertex
or canonical basis is identified with a new one; use the permuted ring's
own degreeLex minimal vertices. The preceding construction then produces
its actual analytic image witnesses and monic family, with all auxiliary
conditions internally discharged from the same original quotients.
\end{proof}

This construction produces an image monic family for division. Descent of the
whole owner support, relative maximal-adic standardness, both directions of the
current Hilbert--Samuel criterion and the uniform history presentation remain
separate mathematical obligations.
