# Independent review of upstream-only blueprint attachment scope

The proposed distinction is mathematically honest provided the final byteguard verifies an exhaustive, disjoint partition of all original attachment names and preserves every original statement, complete proof, hypothesis and frontier paragraph. An upstream declaration which is not present in the standalone environment must retain its exact original qualified name and fixed source revision, with actual upstream compiled-owner, complete-body and standard-axiom evidence. It must not be counted as a standalone kernel-checked declaration. Neither a new mathematical premise nor an extra copy of an unused upstream theorem is needed merely to preserve an inherited full chapter.

The initial 243 missing-local names are **not** all verified upstream facts: the actual native check found 241 existing compiled declarations and TWO historical attachment names which do not resolve. Those two must remain explicitly unresolved and cannot receive a verified-upstream status or contribute to any verified-root count. Preserving their complete historical text and recording them as unresolved is preferable to silently dropping the attachments or inventing local declarations. The intended final partition is 1,991 local, 241 verified upstream-only, and 2 unresolved historical names, out of 2,234 distinct original references. These are scope categories of references, not a claim to have completed the open Hironaka theorem.

I have read the exporter scope helper and rendered preamble, the external/unbound name maps, original-qualified-name provenance, and the parent's actual 241-native-owner/standard-three audit log. The mechanism changes only attachment and status macros. Final acceptance will independently pin the exact maps/helper/exporter, reconstruct every complete fixed-source chapter, verify every original attachment is retained in exactly one category, compare mathematical prose after removing only these explicit metadata macros, and check node-level statuses do not describe an unresolved-only attachment as verified. This report presently records the bounded design review; final immutable hashes and replay results are recorded by the final export byteguard after the parent freezes all metadata. It does not rerun a build, root axiom probe, or mathematical self-review.
