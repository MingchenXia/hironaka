/-!
# The ambient extension of a restricted ideal (`𝔣̃_B`)

Source: Copy-4, Step 1 of `prop:principalization`
(`transcendental-nakamaye-copy4-20260911`, `source/main-Nulllocus.tex:1810-1823`):

> Let `𝔣̃_B ⊆ 𝒪_{Y_1}` be the inverse image of `𝔣_B` under the surjection
> `𝒪_{Y_1} → 𝒪_{W_1}`.  This is a coherent ideal containing `𝓘_{W_1}`, with
> `Res_{W_1} 𝔣̃_B = 𝔣_B`, and it is not contained in `𝓘_{W_1}` because `𝔣_B ≠ 0`.

That sentence is what makes the third application of `lem:relative-principalization`
legal: relative principalization takes an ideal on the *ambient* manifold, whereas
`𝔣_B` is an ideal of `𝒪_{W_1}`.  This module supplies the construction and all four
properties, for an arbitrary subset `W`.

`GermIdeal.restrictOn W 𝔞` is the preimage of `Res_W 𝔞` under `𝒪_M → 𝒪_W`: the
holomorphic germs that agree **on `W`, near the point** with a germ of `𝔞`.  The
substance is

* `restrictOn_eq_sum_vanishingIdeal`: it is `𝔞 + 𝓘_W` — the remainder
  `g - ∑ aᵢ sᵢ` of the restricted span relation is holomorphic and vanishes on `W`,
  which is exactly a germ of `𝓘_W`; and
* `isCoherent_restrictOn`: hence coherent, by concatenating the two generating
  families, `𝓘_W` being coherent for a closed submanifold
  (`Pending/VanishingIdealCoherent`).

The remaining three properties are immediate from that description, and the last one
is stated in the manuscript's own form: `Res_W 𝔞 ≠ 0` — one germ of `𝔞` not vanishing
at one point of `W` — already forces `𝔣̃ ⊄ 𝓘_W`.

`GermIdeal.sum` (the ideal generated by `𝔞 ∪ 𝔟`) is introduced here because
`FirstPass/HolomorphicIdealSheaves` has products and pullbacks but no sums; it is
modelled on `GermIdeal.mul` and its coherence proof on
`IdealWeightPowers.isCoherent_mul`.

Everything here is sorry-free.
-/

/-! ### Sums of ideal sheaves -/


theorem le_sumIdeal_left {𝔞 𝔟 : GermIdeal n M} (h𝔞 : 𝔞.IsCoherent) :
    𝔞.Le (sumIdeal 𝔞 𝔟) := by
  intro x g hg
  exact isInSpanAt_of_eq_mul (S := fun s => 𝔞.mem x s ∨ 𝔟.mem x s) (Or.inl hg)
    (GermIdeal.IsCoherent.holomorphicAt_mem h𝔞 hg) (holomorphicAt_const 1)
    (Eventually.of_forall fun y => (one_mul _).symm)

theorem le_sumIdeal_right {𝔞 𝔟 : GermIdeal n M} (h𝔟 : 𝔟.IsCoherent) :
    𝔟.Le (sumIdeal 𝔞 𝔟) := by
  intro x g hg
  exact isInSpanAt_of_eq_mul (S := fun s => 𝔞.mem x s ∨ 𝔟.mem x s) (Or.inr hg)
    (GermIdeal.IsCoherent.holomorphicAt_mem h𝔟 hg) (holomorphicAt_const 1)
    (Eventually.of_forall fun y => (one_mul _).symm)

end
end Hironaka.RestrictedIdealExtension
