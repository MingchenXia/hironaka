\section{All-order Hilbert--Samuel comparison for the genuine analytic axis contraction}

\begin{theorem}[Nonzero axis parameters preserve every original Hilbert--Samuel value]
\label{hir:actual-axis-nonzero-hs}
\uses{hir:actual-axis-contraction,hir:smooth-centre-actual-whole-ambient-jet-transport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAxisSpecializationHS.hilbertSamuelValue_actual_axis_nonzero}
\leanok
For every ideal $I$ of the original analytic germ ring at the origin, every
nonzero $c\in\mathbb C$, and every $\ell\ge0$, the actual whole image
$L_c^*I$ has Hilbert--Samuel value $H_I(\ell)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual inverse coordinate map $L_{c^{-1}}$ gives the genuine complex
germ algebra equivalence already constructed for $L_c$. The intrinsic
finite-jet quotient of the whole ideal is preserved by this genuine
equivalence, so its complex dimension is unchanged at every order.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The genuine axis-contraction family on its actual domain]
\label{hir:actual-axis-family-domain}
\uses{hir:actual-axis-contraction-joint-holomorphic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAxisSpecializationHS.actualAxisContractionDomain,Hironaka.HironakaActualAxisSpecializationHS.actualAxisContractionFamily}
\leanok
Given original holomorphic functions $G_1,\ldots,G_s$ on one original open
neighbourhood $U$ of zero, put
$D=\{(t,z):L_{t_0}(z)\in U\}$ and
$F_i(t,z)=G_i(L_{t_0}(z))$. These are the actual original functions and
the true preimage domain, with one complex parameter independent of the
original ambient dimension.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Real joint holomorphicity and the entire original fibre-ideal dictionary]
\label{hir:actual-axis-family-whole-ideal-dictionary}
\uses{hir:actual-axis-family-domain}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAxisSpecializationHS.isOpen_actualAxisContractionDomain,Hironaka.HironakaActualAxisSpecializationHS.axis_mem_actualAxisContractionDomain,Hironaka.HironakaActualAxisSpecializationHS.differentiableOn_actualAxisContractionFamily,Hironaka.HironakaActualAxisSpecializationHS.actualAxisContractionFibreIdeal_eq_map_original_span}
\leanok
The domain $D$ is open and contains every parameter-axis point $(t,0)$.
Every $F_i$ is jointly holomorphic on $D$. At each actual parameter $t$,
the entire actual fibre ideal is the whole ideal image under $L_{t_0}^*$
of the ideal spanned by the original germs of the $G_i$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine joint contraction is complex smooth and hence continuous,
so the true inverse image of $U$ is open. Its value at every $(t,0)$ is
zero, proving the axis inclusion. The chain rule on the open domain gives
joint holomorphicity of each original composition. The actual fibre germs
are precisely $z\mapsto G_i(L_{t_0}(z))$, which are the whole pullbacks of
the original generator germs. Ideal image commutes with span, proving
both inclusions for the entire ideals, rather than just finite jets.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original ideal has all Hilbert--Samuel values bounded by its true axis-zero specialization]
\label{hir:actual-axis-zero-all-hs}
\uses{hir:actual-axis-nonzero-hs,hir:actual-axis-family-whole-ideal-dictionary,hir:actual-holomorphic-family-fibre-hs-closed-superlevels}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAxisSpecializationHS.hilbertSamuelValue_actual_axis_specialization}
\leanok
For every ideal $I$ of the original analytic germ ring, every original
coordinate $e$, and every $\ell\ge0$,
\[
 H_I(\ell)\le H_{L_0^*I}(\ell).
\]
There is no supplied finite generating system, family, or numerical
comparison hypothesis.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual original analytic ring is Noetherian. Choose a finite generating
family of the given whole ideal, original holomorphic representatives,
and the finite intersection of their true holomorphic neighbourhoods.
This internally supplies one open neighbourhood $U$, with the original
generator germs still spanning exactly $I$. Construct the preceding real
joint family on its actual domain. Its entire fibre ideal at the genuine
parameter point with coordinate $c$ is exactly $L_c^*I$.

Fix $\ell$. Actual holomorphic-fibre Hilbert--Samuel semicontinuity makes
the superlevel set $\{c:H_I(\ell)\le H_{L_c^*I}(\ell)\}$ closed in
the true complex parameter line. Every nonzero parameter belongs to it
by the genuine inverse-coordinate equivalence. The complement of the
origin is dense, so the same closed superlevel set contains zero. This
gives the claimed inequality for the unchanged original whole ideal at
the arbitrary chosen order, and hence at every order.
\end{proof}

The specialized ideal is the whole image under the actual map setting
coordinate $e$ to zero. A comparison with a finite dehomogenized initial
ideal, and the resulting point-blowup inequality, are subsequent steps.
