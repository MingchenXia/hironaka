\section{The literal genuine relative Taylor coefficient dictionary}

The actual relative coefficients were constructed by inversion of the true
normal-power quotient basis, and already have a common holomorphic domain for
any finite family and finite degree bound. We now identify their entire
original Taylor series with the literal normal coefficients of the original
ambient Taylor expansion. This uses the actual coefficient convention of
formal power series and introduces no unproved raw-derivative convention.

\begin{lemma}[The entire genuine formal coefficient of an actual normal polynomial]
\label{hir:actual-relative-polynomial-formal-taylor-coefficient}
\uses{hir:actual-original-taylor-tangent-coordinate-naturality,hir:relative-normal-basis-actual-entire-quotient-basis}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.actualTangentFormalEmbedding,Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.actualNormalFormalEmbedding,Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.actualJoinedExponent_tangent_embedding,Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.actualJoinedExponent_normal_embedding,Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.actualUnshiftedTaylor_normalExponentMonomial,Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.coefficient_tangent_rename_joined,Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.coefficient_tangent_rename_mul_normal_monomial,Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.coefficient_actualTaylor_normalPolynomial_monomial,Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.actualFormalNormalCoefficient_normalPolynomialEval}
\leanok
Let $P$ be any finite polynomial in the normal variables with coefficients in
$\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$. Evaluate $P$ using the actual tangent pullback
and actual ambient normal coordinate germs. The literal formal normal
coefficient at every normal exponent $\gamma$ of the genuine ambient Taylor
series of that evaluation is the genuine tangential Taylor series of the
entire original polynomial coefficient $P_\gamma$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For one actual monomial $a Z^\alpha$, the genuine ambient Taylor
homomorphism sends the actual tangent pullback of $a$ to the literal variable
renaming of its genuine tangential Taylor series, by whole-map coordinate
naturality. It sends every actual normal coordinate to the corresponding
original formal variable. Multiplicativity therefore gives the product of
that renamed series with the true normal monomial. The coefficient at the
joined original exponent $(\beta,\gamma)$ equals the $\beta$ coefficient of
Taylor$(a)$ if $\gamma=\alpha$, and is zero otherwise: the true renamed
series has no nonzero normal exponent, and genuine monomial multiplication
shifts exponents exactly. This is coefficient equality with the original
numerical coefficients, including zero coefficients and empty coordinate
blocks. Add the finitely many actual polynomial monomials. Additivity of
the actual Taylor homomorphisms and coefficient maps gives the statement
for the entire original polynomial and every tangential exponent.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every genuine relative coefficient has exactly its original formal Taylor series]
\label{hir:actual-relative-coefficient-literal-formal-taylor-dictionary}
\uses{hir:actual-relative-polynomial-formal-taylor-coefficient,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-polynomial-truncations,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-germ-stability,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-common-finite-domain}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.actualJoinedExponent_normal_weight,Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.actualFormalNormalCoefficient_actualTaylor,Hironaka.HironakaActualRelativeTaylorCoefficients.exists_actual_finite_coefficient_common_domain_with_Taylor}
\leanok
For any actual holomorphic ambient germ $g$ and any normal exponent
$\gamma$, the literal $\gamma$ normal coefficient of the genuine entire
ambient Taylor series equals the genuine tangential Taylor series of the
actual stable relative coefficient germ of $g$ at $\gamma$.
Consequently every finite family of actual analytic ambient germs has
actual holomorphic coefficient representatives on one genuine common open
for every prescribed finite low-degree range, satisfying these literal
entire formal-series identities.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take $q=|\gamma|+1$ and the genuine canonical relative polynomial $P_q(g)$
constructed from the actual normal-power quotient. Its actual remainder
$g-P_q(g)$ belongs to the whole original normal ideal power of order $q$.
The genuine ambient Taylor homomorphism maps this entire ideal into the
actual formal normal ideal power, by its true normal-coordinate generator
dictionary and the actual ideal-map power identity. Every coefficient with
normal degree below $q$ vanishes in that ideal power, regardless of its
tangential exponent. Thus the literal normal coefficient at $\gamma$ of
Taylor$(g)$ equals that of Taylor$(P_q(g))$. Apply the preceding polynomial
identity. By construction, the entire $\gamma$ coefficient of $P_q(g)$ is
the actual stable relative coefficient germ. This proves equality of the
whole formal tangential series, with no convergence assertion for an
arbitrary prescribed formal series. Finally use the already constructed
actual finite coefficient representatives and their genuine common
holomorphic domain, and substitute the whole-series equality just proved.
This does not construct the full general reduced coefficient presentation
or prove transformed stability or Bennett inequalities.
\end{proof}
