\section{A genuine global analytic extension of a regular order stratum}

Bierstone--Milman\cite{bierstone-milman97-invent-math}, Section 13,
proof of Theorem 13.4 (printed page 300), extends a local centre by taking
appropriate dimensional components of genuine global analytic superlevel
sets. The following producer proves that extension step for an actual ideal
order superlevel already regular of one dimension on the selected open set.
It does not assume a global extension, or assert that this global extension
is already smooth away from that open set.

\begin{lemma}[Dimension selection preserves a regular trace]
\label{hir:dimension-part-regular-trace}
\uses{hir:embedded-resolution-dimension-parts}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaAnalyticCentreExtension.dimensionPart_inter_eq_of_regularOn}
\leanok
Let $A$ be closed, and suppose every point of $A\cap U$ is regular of
complex dimension $q$ in $A$. Then $A^{(q)}\cap U=A\cap U$, where
$A^{(q)}=\overline{\operatorname{Reg}_q A}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Closedness gives $A^{(q)}\subset A$. Conversely, each point of $A\cap U$
belongs to $\operatorname{Reg}_q A$ by the assumed actual regularity,
and therefore to its closure. Intersect these two containments with $U$.
No compactness or global pure-dimensionality of $A$ is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Produced global analytic extension of an actual order stratum]
\label{hir:actual-order-stratum-global-analytic-extension}
\uses{hir:dimension-part-regular-trace,hir:embedded-resolution-dimension-parts,hir:dimension-part-global-component-union}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaAnalyticCentreExtension.exists_pure_global_extension_of_regular_order_superlevel}
\leanok
Let $I$ be a coherent holomorphic germ ideal on a complex manifold, $N$ a
closed smooth submanifold, and $\mu\ge1$. Put $A=\operatorname{cosupp}_N(I,\mu)$.
Suppose $A\cap U$ is regular of complex dimension $q$ at every point, where
$U$ is open. There is a genuine global analytic pure-$q$ subset $T\subset A$
with $T\cap U=A\cap U$. It is exactly the union of the actual $q$-dimensional global components
selected from $A$, and every point
of its trace on $U$ is regular of dimension $q$ in $T$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The derivative-ideal order theorem identifies the actual superlevel with
$N\cap V(\mathcal D_N^{\mu-1} I)$. Coherence of the derivative ideal and
analyticity of the closed smooth $N$ make $A$ a global analytic set. Take
$T=A^{(q)}$. Actual dimension-part analyticity and purity give the first
assertions, and closedness of $A$ gives $T\subset A$. The preceding lemma
gives the exact trace equality. A regular point of $A$ is regular of the
same dimension in its actual dimension part: the regular coordinate
neighbourhood agrees with that part. Finally the global dimension-part
identification proved from genuine global components gives
$T=\bigcup\{B:B\text{ is a global component of }A\text{ of pure dimension }q\}$.
Thus this is an internally constructed analytic extension; no extension or
component labels are supplied by the caller. Its singularities away from
$U$ remain for the separate recursive smoothification step of Lemma 13.7.
\end{proof}
