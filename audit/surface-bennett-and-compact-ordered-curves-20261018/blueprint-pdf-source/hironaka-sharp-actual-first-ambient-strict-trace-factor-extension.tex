\begin{lemma}[Genuine relative invertible-product cancellation gives the whole normalized quotient]

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstAmbientStrictTraceFactor.actual_relative_invertible_product_colon}
\leanok

\[
 (\operatorname{Res}_N(AB):A)=\operatorname{Res}_N B
\]
as WHOLE ambient germ ideals. No product quotient, desired equality or

off-carrier units are retained.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}

closure. Real quotient coherence and the two actual invertible-along

real products and cancel the actual nonzero-along generator of $A$ in

$\operatorname{Res}_N(AB:A)\subseteq\operatorname{Res}_N B$.
Conversely each true $b\in B$ multiplies every actual $a\in A$ into the
actual product $AB$, giving $B\subseteq(AB:A)$ before relative-span
closure. These give BOTH normalized ideal directions. The true colon
normalization theorem removes its outer restriction, by actual finite
relative-span substitution. Closed-carrier complements supply the real

an on-carrier membership statement, follows.
\end{proof}
