\section{The whole original fibre Hilbert--Samuel dictionary}

Write $B=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$, $R=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^{d+t},0}$,
$J$ for the actual normal-coordinate ideal, and $M$ for the whole image of
$\mathfrak m_B$ under the actual tangent pullback. The genuine original
fibre ideal of an original ambient germ ideal $I$ is $I+M$. The objective
is the existing whole local-ring Hilbert--Samuel quotient in
Bierstone--Milman's ``Use of the Hilbert--Samuel function''. The argument
below identifies its entire sequence on the one original-input open from
the uniform relative ranks; it does not assert descent after blowing up.

\begin{definition}[The genuine whole original base-fibre ideal]
\label{hir:relative-normal-original-base-fibre-ideal}

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalFibreHilbertSamuel.originalBaseMaximalIdeal}

Let $M$ be the whole ideal image of $\mathfrak m_B$ under the original
tangent pullback. The fibre ideal of an arbitrary original $I$ is $I+M$.
All quotients use the original whole germ ring, its actual multiplication,
and its original complex scalar structure.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[The actual split-coordinate maximal ideal]
\label{hir:relative-normal-original-fibre-maximal-ideal}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalFibreHilbertSamuel.baseMaximalIdeal_sup_normalIdeal}
\leanok
\uses{hir:relative-normal-original-base-fibre-ideal,hir:relative-normal-whole-hadamard}
The literal maximal ideal of $R$ is exactly $M+J$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every base germ in $\mathfrak m_B$ evaluates to zero at the original base
origin, and its true tangent pullback has the same value at the ambient
origin. Therefore $M\subseteq\mathfrak m_R$. Every normal coordinate
also evaluates to zero, so $J\subseteq\mathfrak m_R$.
Conversely, for $g\in\mathfrak m_R$, the actual relative holomorphic
Hadamard decomposition writes
$g=\operatorname{tangentPullback}(\operatorname{planeTrace}(g))+
\sum_i z_i h_i$, with the sum over the original normal coordinates.
The actual plane trace has the same origin value as $g$, hence belongs
to $\mathfrak m_B$. Its true tangent pullback therefore belongs to $M$.
Every normal-coordinate summand belongs to $J$. Thus $g\in M+J$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[All actual fibre maximal-ideal powers are the same normal-power quotients]
\label{hir:relative-normal-original-fibre-maximal-power}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalFibreHilbertSamuel.fibreMaximalIdeal_power_eq}
\leanok
\uses{hir:relative-normal-original-fibre-maximal-ideal}
For every original whole $I$ and every $s\in\mathbb N$,
\[
 I+M+\mathfrak m_R^s=I+M+J^s.
\]
Thus the existing whole Hilbert--Samuel quotient of the genuine fibre
ideal $I+M$ at order $\ell$ is literally the original quotient
$R/(I+M+J^{\ell+1})$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $\pi:R\to R/M$ be the genuine whole quotient map. The images
of $M+J$ and $J$ under $\pi$ agree, because the image of $M$ is zero.
Ideal image commutes with taking powers, so the images of $(M+J)^s$
and $J^s$ agree for every $s$, including zero. The inverse image of
the image of any whole ideal $K$ under this surjective quotient map is
exactly $K+M$. Taking inverse images therefore gives
$M+(M+J)^s=M+J^s$. Substitute the preceding actual identity
$\mathfrak m_R=M+J$ and add the same whole original ideal $I$.
This proves precisely the asserted equality, with $M$ retained on both
sides. For positive base dimension it does not identify the ambient
quotient by $I+\mathfrak m_R^s$ with a normal-power or fibre quotient;
that quotient retains mixed tangent degrees.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual residue tensor is the whole original fibre-power algebra]
\label{hir:relative-normal-original-fibre-tensor-algebra}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalFibreHilbertSamuel.originalNormalFibreTruncationAlgEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalFibreHilbertSamuel.originalNormalFibreTruncationAlgEquiv_tmul_mk,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalFibreHilbertSamuel.originalNormalFibreTruncationAlgEquiv_symm_mk}
\leanok
\uses{hir:relative-normal-original-base-fibre-ideal,hir:relative-normal-nearby-complete-fibre-power-dimensions}
Using the genuine base residue evaluation $B\to\mathbb C$, there is a
$\mathbb C$-algebra isomorphism
\[
 \mathbb C\otimes_B R/(I+J^s)\ \simeq_{\mathbb C}\ R/(I+M+J^s).
\]
It maps every pure original numerator $c\otimes[g]$ to the actual class
$[cg]$, where $c$ is the original constant germ. Its inverse maps every
original class $[g]$ to $1\otimes[g]$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The proposed forward map is induced by multiplication of the two actual
maps into the whole original quotient: complex constant germs and the
original quotient of $R$. It is balanced over $B$. Indeed, for $b\in B$,
$b-b(0)$ belongs to $\mathfrak m_B$; its true tangent pullback therefore
belongs to $M$ and is zero in the target. Thus the base action in that
quotient agrees with actual origin evaluation. The tensor universal
property gives the forward unital algebra map.
For the inverse, first map $g\in R$ to $1\otimes[g]$. The map kills
$I+J^s$ by the actual original quotient, and it kills every $bg$ with
$b\in\mathfrak m_B$ by tensor balancing and $b(0)=0$. It therefore
kills the whole generated image ideal $M$ and descends to the asserted
whole fibre-power quotient. Both maps preserve the actual complex constants.
Their composition on every original quotient numerator is the identity.
On a pure tensor $c\otimes[g]$, the other composition is
$1\otimes[cg]=c\otimes[g]$, because evaluation of the constant germ is
$c$. Pure tensors generate the tensor algebra additively, so this second
composition is also the identity. These two identities prove the stated
algebra isomorphism and both numerator formulas.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One original-input open gives the entire whole fibre Hilbert--Samuel sequence]
\label{hir:relative-normal-original-nearby-whole-fibre-hilbert-samuel}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalFibreHilbertSamuel.exists_original_nearby_whole_fibre_hilbertSamuel_open}
\leanok
\uses{hir:relative-normal-original-fibre-maximal-power,hir:relative-normal-original-fibre-tensor-algebra,hir:relative-normal-nearby-complete-fibre-power-dimensions}
Original coherence alone supplies one actual open $U$ meeting every origin
neighbourhood and one sequence $r(q)$, such that for every $a\in U$ and
every $\ell$, the EXISTING whole Hilbert--Samuel value of the genuine
original fibre ideal $I_a+M$ equals
\[
 H_{R/(I_a+M)}(\ell)=\sum_{q<\ell+1}r(q).
\]
The same $U$ works for the entire sequence. No geometric descent or
singular-resolution driver is a conclusion of this theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original-input uniform-rank producer gives the true finite complex
dimension of every residue tensor fibre at every normal order, all on
its same actual open. Apply the preceding genuine fibre tensor algebra
isomorphism at $s=\ell+1$. Its underlying complex linear isomorphism
retains that dimension. The preceding whole ideal-power equality then
identifies its target with the existing whole maximal-ideal quotient
that defines the original fibre ideal's Hilbert--Samuel value. Taking
complex dimensions proves the equality for every $\ell$, with no new
open, denominator, branch, or rank certificate.
\end{proof}
