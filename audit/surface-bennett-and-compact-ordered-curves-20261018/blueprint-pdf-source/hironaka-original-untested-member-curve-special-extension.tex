\section{Actual current curve geometry supplies Step One special leaves without a lower TEST}

The positive-member dimension induction in
\cite[Section~5, Step~I.A]{bierstone-milman08-functoriality} has a genuine
scope issue after an arbitrary original TEST: the actual lower member
carrier is smooth and its whole controlled ideals are coherent/nonzero,
but the original centres need not lie in that smaller carrier. A lower
TEST must therefore not be assumed. The next producer instead proves
the required special leaf from the actual CURRENT coefficient curve
geometry, and supplies that geometry internally along the real original
member history.

\begin{lemma}[A genuine bound-one cosupport point has actual carrier dimension one and cosupport dimension zero]
\label{hir:original-curve-current-cosupport-regular-dimensions}
\uses{hir:one-dimensional-actual-cosupport-dimension,hir:analytic-dimzero-all-points-regular}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalUntestedMemberCurveSpecial.actual_curve_cosupport_regular_dimensions}
\leanok
For ANY actual marked bound-one domain and actual point of its positive
cosupport, the real carrier is regular of dimension one at that point,
and the real cosupport is regular of dimension zero there. No maximal
order, history or regularity certificate is supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Actual coherence/nonzero and the positive mark make the genuine
cosupport analytic and of dimension at most zero, so the proved analytic
zero-dimensional regularity theorem gives its actual dimension zero.
The original domain gives a real carrier dimension at most one at the
point. If it were zero, its genuine isolating neighbourhood and actual
nonzero-along witness would force some actual ideal germ to have nonzero
value at that point. Positive cosupport forces EVERY actual ideal germ
to vanish there, a contradiction. Thus the carrier dimension is one.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual current coefficient curve geometry forces the original special branch on ANY history]
\label{hir:original-current-bar-curve-special-without-lower-test}
\uses{hir:original-curve-current-cosupport-regular-dimensions,hir:step-one-lower-domain,hir:step-one-value-masked-basic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalUntestedMemberCurveSpecial.isSpecial_of_current_bar_curve_domain}
\leanok
Let $A$ be ANY original marked record and $T$ ANY actual tower. If its
ACTUAL current coefficient record $\overline A_T$ is a genuine bound-one
domain, then EVERY point of its actual positive cosupport is special
for the ORIGINAL $A,T$. No TEST of $A$ or $\overline A$, no canonicality,
maximal-order or desired special-locus certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At such a point the first lemma gives actual carrier dimension one and
actual cosupport dimension zero. If its original level is zero, these
two genuine regularity witnesses are EXACTLY the defining original
codimension-one-part membership, so it is special.

If the level is positive, its actual original member list is a prefix
of the CURRENT ordered SNC boundary. The true finite indexed
through-point set is nonempty. Actual current transversality and the
genuine SNC member-intersection dimension theorem make the intersection
of the real carrier with ALL these actual through-point members regular
of dimension strictly below one, hence zero. Choose its genuine
isolating neighbourhood. At every point there lying in the carrier and
all original members through the reference point, the actual
through-index/member-intersection dictionary gives membership in this
isolated intersection, hence equality with the reference point. It
therefore lies in the actual cosupport. This is BOTH fields of the
ORIGINAL member-locus definition, proving specialness at positive
level. Every member is the actual original indexed entry, not a
distinct-branch replacement.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Genuine original member geometry supplies entire curve domains for arbitrary same-geometry lower ideals]
\label{hir:original-member-same-geometry-untested-curve-domains}
\uses{hir:member-tail-actual-geometry,hir:member-tail-actual-coherent-nonzero}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalUntestedMemberCurveSpecial.actual_member_same_geometry_curve_domains}
\leanok
Let $m$ be an actual original bound-two domain, $S$ a genuine nonempty
set of its actual ordered member positions, and $B$ ANY genuine initial
bound-one domain whose carrier and ordered boundary are EXACTLY those
of the actual member remainder record. Along EVERY genuine original

bound-one domain. No TEST or admissible centres for $B$, later
coherence/nonzero or later domain certificate is supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The independently proved member-remainder geometric theorem supplies
the actual strict carrier smoothness, whole ordered SNC boundary,
transversality and dimension bound one at EVERY true original stage.
Carrier and boundary transform depend only on their literal initial
sets and ordered lists, so the assumed initial equalities transport
these actual geometric assertions to $B$ without assuming its centres
are admissible.

Induct on the ACTUAL original tower to construct coherence and nonzero
of the ENTIRE actual $B$ ideal. Initially they are genuine initial
domain fields. At a step use the real original-stage and next-stage
smooth carrier geometry already supplied above. Genuine coherent
pullback and relative colon produce the actual controlled ideal
coherence even without an admissibility inclusion. Actual strict
accumulation from the off-centre carrier and genuine nonzero there
produce nonzero at EVERY true next carrier point. This proves the
induction with the real controlled ideal and mark. Combine these
independently constructed ideal assertions with the actual geometric
fields and unchanged positive mark, obtaining every current domain
field. This is not an assertion that the lower tower is a TEST.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ONLY actual original positive-member history gives every untested coefficient curve special leaf]
\label{hir:original-positive-member-untested-curve-stepone-leaves}

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalUntestedMemberCurveSpecial.original_positive_member_curve_step_one_values}
\leanok
ONLY an original bound-two domain, ANY genuine split original TEST and
an actual positive-level point at its head internally construct the
ACTUAL original member-lower extension. At EVERY genuine prefix of the
same original tail, internally prove true bound-one domains for its
ENTIRE actual lower transform AND the ENTIRE actual transform of its

SAME prefix and its unchanged sequence-aware Step One value is
$(\mathrm{level},\infty)$ at EVERY recursive depth. No lower TEST,
canonicality, coefficient model or desired special/value certificate
is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Genuine original coefficient equivalence supplies the actual coefficient
TEST and its real head/tail TEST decomposition. Original domain
stability supplies the genuine head coefficient bound-two domain. The
actual original head level is positive, so the finite through-point
member subset in the ORIGINAL definition is internally nonempty.
The actual member-remainder domain theorem therefore constructs the
actual member-lower extension in bound one. Its genuine original
coefficient-of-homogenization has the same ENTIRE carrier/boundary and
is independently a bound-one domain, by true coherent operations and
nonzero original summand.

At EVERY real tail prefix, the genuine original coefficient tail TEST
supplies the independently proved member geometry. The preceding lemma
applied to the actual original coefficient record, with its defining
carrier/boundary equalities, internally constructs its WHOLE transformed
bound-one domain. The actual lower domain is independently supplied
by the genuine member-remainder transform theorem. Apply the second
theorem to this ACTUAL current coefficient geometry and each actual
cosupport point: it is special for the unchanged ORIGINAL lower record
and same prefix. Only then unfold the original sequence-aware Step One
definition, whose true special branch is precisely
$(\mathrm{level},\infty)$, independently of the lower recursive depth.

This supplies an actual previously missing NON-TEST lower-history

comparison, exceptional/infinitesimal semicoherence or general global
scheduler is asserted.
\end{proof}
