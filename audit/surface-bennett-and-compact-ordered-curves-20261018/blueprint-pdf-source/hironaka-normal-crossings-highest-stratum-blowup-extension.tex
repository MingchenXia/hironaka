\section{The actual blowup of the highest original branch stratum}
\label{sec:hironaka-normal-crossings-highest-stratum-blowup}

These statements concern the strict transform of the original carrier.
They do not count the new exceptional component as an original branch.
They construct the next original-branch value by actual monomial charts,
without identifying an ordered invariant under arbitrary permutations.

\begin{lemma}[Coordinates adapted to the whole intrinsic highest stratum]
\label{hir:general-nc-highest-stratum-branch-adapted-coordinates}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestStratumBlowup.exists_highest_stratum_branch_adapted_coordinates}
\uses{hir:general-nc-highest-multiplicity-smooth-centre,hir:general-nc-exact-nearby-branch-count}
\leanok
Let $H$ be an actual normal-crossing carrier with all original local
branch counts at most $r$. At a point $x$ of its highest stratum $C_r$,
there are actual centred coordinates in which $C_r$ is the plane of
dimension $n-r$ and $H$ is the union of precisely the last $r$
coordinate hyperplanes. These coordinates are produced from the original
flattening, without a supplied simultaneous-flattening witness.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take an actual original flattening with selected set $S$. The intrinsic
highest-stratum theorem gives $|S|=r$ and expresses the entire centre
on that chart domain as the simultaneous vanishing of the selected
coordinates. Since $|S|\le n$, there is a permutation of the $n$
indices sending $S$ onto the last $r$ indices: choose bijections on $S$
and its complement to these two blocks and combine them. The resulting
linear coordinate permutation is a holomorphic linear isomorphism.
Postcompose the actual flattening with this map, and precompose its
actual inverse with the inverse permutation. The new maps have the
same actual source, a permuted open target and both inverse identities.
Their centre value is zero. The selected-coordinate union is now the
union of the last $r$ coordinate hyperplanes, and the selected-coordinate
intersection is precisely the dimension-$(n-r)$ plane. This produces
both whole-set dictionaries on one actual open domain.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The intrinsic positive highest stratum admits an actual blowup]
\label{hir:general-nc-highest-stratum-actual-blowup}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestStratumBlowup.interior_highest_superlevel_eq_empty,Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestStratumBlowup.exists_highest_stratum_blowup}
\uses{hir:general-nc-highest-multiplicity-smooth-centre}
\leanok
If $r>0$ bounds all original branch counts, the actual closed smooth
highest stratum $C_r$ has empty interior and its complex blowup exists.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The entire centre is closed and regular of dimension $n-r$ at every
one of its points. At any hypothetical interior point, the centre is
the whole ambient manifold on a neighbourhood and hence regular of
dimension $n$ there. Uniqueness of the dimension of an actual regular
flattening would give $n=n-r$. At a point of the centre, the actual
branch bound gives $r\le n$, so this contradicts $r>0$. Thus the centre
has empty interior. The proved complex blowup existence theorem applies
to this actual closed smooth centre, producing its manifold, projection
and genuine blowup-along-centre property. An empty highest level also
has empty interior and is covered by the same existence theorem.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The genuine local strict-original-carrier calculation]
\label{hir:general-nc-highest-stratum-local-strict-branch-drop}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestStratumBlowup.mem_strict_carrier_iff_of_actual_chart}
\uses{hir:general-nc-highest-stratum-branch-adapted-coordinates}
\leanok
Consider a genuine monomial chart of the blowup of $C_r$, with pivot
$j$ among the last $r$ coordinates and its actual lift inverse.
On this chart the strict transform of $H$ is exactly the union of the
remaining last-coordinate hyperplanes, with the pivot omitted. The
exceptional hypersurface is the pivot-coordinate zero set.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose the produced branch-adapted coordinates at the base point.
The actual blowup universal property supplies a monomial local inverse
$W$ with pivot $j\ge n-r$ and an actual holomorphic lift inverse on its
open domain. In those coordinates the monomial projection sends the
pivot coordinate to itself, each other normal coordinate to its
product with the pivot, and each tangential coordinate to itself.
The centre preimage is therefore exactly $W_j=0$.

On the base chart the original carrier is a finite union of the actual
coordinate zero sets. Local equality on a base neighbourhood pulls back
to local equality on the blowup by continuity of the actual projection.
Membership in the closure of the preimage outside the centre depends
only on this local equality. Closure commutes with this finite union,
so its strict transform is the finite union of the branch strict
transforms. The pivot branch has no points outside the centre in this
chart and its strict transform misses the chart. For a remaining normal
coordinate $i$, outside $W_j=0$ its pulled back zero equation is exactly
$W_i=0$. Every point of $W_i=0$ in the chart is a limit of points there
with $W_j\ne0$, because the coordinates $i$ and $j$ are distinct and
the actual chart has an open model domain. Thus its genuine strict
transform is the whole $W_i=0$ set. Conversely any such limit remains
in that zero set by continuity. These are the two directions of the
whole strict-original-carrier dictionary; no transform membership
certificate is an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual centred strict-original coordinates and the lower count]
\label{hir:general-nc-highest-stratum-over-centre-count-decrease}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestStratumBlowup.exists_strict_carrier_coordinates_over_highest}
\uses{hir:general-nc-highest-stratum-local-strict-branch-drop,hir:general-nc-intrinsic-branch-multiplicity}
\leanok
At every blowup point above the centre, the original strict carrier
has an actual centred normal-crossing flattening with at most $r-1$
branches. The exceptional equation is simultaneously a coordinate
hyperplane with a different index from all of these branches.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual monomial local inverse has injective differential, as follows
by differentiating its genuine lift inverse. Express it in the preferred
chart at the blowup point, apply the holomorphic inverse function
theorem and subtract its centre value. This produces actual centred
coordinates. Among the $r-1$ remaining normal coordinates, retain
exactly those vanishing at the point. The finitely many other coordinates
are nonzero on one smaller open neighbourhood, so they contribute
no branch there. For each retained coordinate, subtraction of the
centre value changes nothing. The preceding whole strict-transform
dictionary thus gives a genuine centred NC flattening of the original
strict carrier. Its selected set has at most $r-1$ elements. Since
the point is above the centre, the pivot coordinate vanishes there;
its translated equation is still the actual exceptional equation,
and the pivot is distinct from each remaining selected index.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine global first step with smaller original branch count]
\label{hir:general-nc-highest-stratum-global-strict-count-decrease}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestStratumBlowup.isNormalCrossingsCarrier_strict_highest,Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestStratumBlowup.branchMultiplicity_strict_highest_le,Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsHighestStratumBlowup.exists_highest_stratum_blowup_strict_count_decrease}
\uses{hir:general-nc-highest-stratum-actual-blowup,hir:general-nc-highest-stratum-over-centre-count-decrease,hir:general-nc-actual-highest-stratum-exists}
\leanok
If $H$ is nonempty, its internally produced maximum $r>0$ and the
blowup of its whole intrinsic maximum stratum produce an actual strict
normal-crossing carrier $H'$ with all original branch counts at most
$r-1$. The construction requires neither compactness nor a global
ordering of the original branches.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Produce the attained positive actual maximum and its smooth whole
centre, then its genuine blowup. The strict carrier is closed by its
closure definition. Above the centre use the preceding produced
centred chart and lower count. Away from the centre the actual blowup
is a local biholomorphism and the strict carrier equals the preimage
of $H$: the original carrier is closed, so local closure outside the
centre gives exactly this preimage. Compose an actual original
flattening at the image point with the actual local inverse coordinates
of this biholomorphism. The resulting centred chart gives the whole
strict-carrier union with the same original selected indices, hence
the same intrinsic count. The image point is outside the global
maximum stratum, so that integer count is strictly less than $r$,
equivalently at most $r-1$. These two cases prove actual NC everywhere
and the claimed global strict-original count bound. The chosen maximum
and centre were intrinsic; no assertion about the old ordered algorithm
was used.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The new exceptional branch can restore the total boundary count to $r$;
the decrease asserted here counts only strict original branches.
Compatibility with previously accumulated exceptional boundary requires
a further simultaneous-coordinate producer. For an additional marked
ideal, this centre need not be contained in its cosupport. Consequently
this first geometric step alone does not supply the required marked-ideal
admissibility, cosupport-complement isomorphism or canonical global
sequence descent.
\end{remark}
