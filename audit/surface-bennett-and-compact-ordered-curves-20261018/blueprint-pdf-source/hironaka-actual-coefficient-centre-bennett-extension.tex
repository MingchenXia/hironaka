\section{Bennett comparison for genuine coefficient-compatible coordinate centres}

Let $I\subset\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^{d+t},0}$ be an original analytic
ideal and $P$ genuine analytic monic vertex data for its entire original
Taylor ideal. Write $w_a$ for the normal weight of the same vertex $a$,
and $c_{a\gamma}$ for its actual analytic normal coefficient on the
actual common tangent domain $U$. The coefficient compatibility
condition used here is the original geometric inclusion
\[
 U\cap\mathbb C^d\subset
 \operatorname{cosupp}(c_{a\gamma},w_a-|\gamma|)
 \qquad (|\gamma|<w_a).
\]
It is a condition on these actual coefficient functions and their
original orders, not on a transformed ideal or an HS value.

\begin{theorem}[All original normalized HS orders decrease under actual coefficient compatibility]
\label{hir:actual-coefficient-centre-normalized-bennett}
\uses{hir:actual-vertex-coefficient-cosupport-axis-translated-hs,hir:actual-every-translated-normal-vertex-hs-bound}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientCentreBennett.hilbertSamuelValue_actual_coefficient_centre_normalized_le}
\leanok
Assume the displayed original coefficient compatibility for $P$. For
every actual normal pivot $j$, every actual blowup-chart parameter $b$
and every order $\ell$, the unchanged original HS value of the actual
normalized ideal in that chart is at most the original HS value of $I$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual original coefficient-cosupport theorem first puts every
same vertex generator in its normal ideal power. Its canonical genuine
exceptional quotient and entire zero-axis image then put the entire
dehomogenized normal symbol ideal inside the zero-axis image of the
same actual normalized ideal. Original axis specialization and ideal
antitonicity bound the normalized HS value by the dehomogenized one.
The true normal-unit coordinate equivalence identifies the whole latter
ideal with the whole translated normal vertex ideal at the vector whose
pivot is one and whose other entries are the actual normal entries of
$b$. The preceding translated-vertex HS theorem bounds this last
original HS value by that of $I$. Compose these inequalities at the
arbitrary original order $\ell$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Intrinsic geometric Bennett in normal codimension at least two]
\label{hir:actual-coefficient-centre-geometric-bennett-at-least-two}
\uses{hir:actual-coefficient-centre-normalized-bennett,hir:actual-normal-geometric-strict-normalized-inclusion}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientCentreBennett.hilbertSamuelAt_actual_coefficient_centre_strict_le_of_two_le}
\leanok
Let $t\ge2$, let $W\subset\mathbb C^{d+t}$ be any subset, and let
$I$ be its entire original vanishing germ ideal at zero. Suppose the
same genuine vertex data $P$ for $I$ satisfy the displayed original
coefficient compatibility. For every actual pivot and parameter, the
original intrinsic HS of the original geometric strict transform at
zero is at most the original intrinsic HS of $W$ at zero, at every order.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual normal chart has its exceptional hyperplane inside its true
geometric exceptional locus, because a different genuine normal
direction gives a nonzero kernel vector for the actual differential.
Every actual normalized generator therefore vanishes on the genuine
geometric strict transform by its true exceptional product identity
and continuity on the closure. Entire ideal inclusion and original HS
antitonicity bound the intrinsic strict-transform HS by the normalized
HS. Apply the preceding normalized comparison to the same original
vanishing ideal. The original model-zero whole-stalk dictionary
identifies its HS value with the original intrinsic HS of $W$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Intrinsic coefficient-compatible coordinate Bennett includes the divisor case]
\label{hir:actual-coefficient-centre-geometric-bennett-all-normal-codimensions}
\uses{hir:actual-coefficient-centre-geometric-bennett-at-least-two,hir:actual-normal-blowup-codimension-one-identity}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualCoefficientCentreBennett.hilbertSamuelAt_actual_coefficient_centre_strict_le}
\leanok
The preceding intrinsic geometric inequality holds for every actual
normal pivot, including normal codimension one, with precisely the
same original coefficient compatibility assumptions.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $t=1$, the actual normal chart is the identity on the whole ambient
model and its actual geometric exceptional locus is empty. Its
geometric strict transform is therefore exactly the closure of $W$.
The original entire vanishing ideal is unchanged by closure, so the
two intrinsic HS values are equal at every order. Otherwise an actual
normal pivot implies $t>0$, and hence $t\ge2$. Apply the preceding
intrinsic comparison. The case $t=0$ admits no normal pivot.
\end{proof}

These statements retain genuine original coefficient compatibility.
Constancy of the original HS values along a smooth centre does not
force that condition for an arbitrary chosen monic vertex family.
Producing an actual compatible analytic presentation from the original
centre geometry is a separate assertion in general Bennett.
