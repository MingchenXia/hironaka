\section{Intrinsic vanishing-ideal Hilbert--Samuel transport for arbitrary subsets}

All ideals, chart germs and Hilbert--Samuel functions below are the
unchanged original ones. A subset need not be analytic, closed or have
a supplied coherent vanishing sheaf. Actual inverse maps, rather than
coherence of a general ideal pullback, give both entire ideal directions.

\begin{theorem}[Actual biholomorphic transport of the entire original vanishing stalk ideal]
\label{hir:actual-any-subset-biholomorphic-whole-vanishing-ideal}
\uses{hir:actual-original-whole-germ-pullback-functorial,hir:actual-biholomorphic-whole-original-germ-algebra-equivalence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualVanishingIntrinsicHSTransport.map_wholeChartStalkIdeal_vanishing_actual_biholomorphic}
\leanok
Let $e:Y\to X$ be a genuine global biholomorphism between original
complex manifolds of the same dimension, let $W\subseteq X$ be arbitrary,
and let $y\in Y$. The image of the entire original preferred-chart
vanishing stalk ideal of $W$ at $e(y)$ under the actual original germ
pullback equals the entire original preferred-chart vanishing stalk
ideal of $e^{-1}W$ at $y$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original whole stalk ideal is the span of chart germs of actual
holomorphic functions vanishing on the given subset near the given point.
The actual whole germ pullback sends every such chart germ of $f$ to the
same original chart germ of $f\circ e$. Continuity of $e$ carries the
vanishing neighbourhood to a neighbourhood of $y$. These images are
therefore genuine defining generators of the target ideal, proving the
forward entire inclusion by the ideal image-of-span identity.
Conversely let $h$ be a defining target representative. The actual
function $f=h\circ e^{-1}$ is holomorphic near $e(y)$, and continuity of
the true inverse carries its target vanishing neighbourhood back to a
source vanishing neighbourhood on $W$. Its source chart germ is a
genuine defining source generator. Its actual pullback has representative
$h\circ e^{-1}\circ e=h$, by the true global inverse identity. Thus every
target generator is in the image ideal. Span proves the reverse entire
inclusion, without a finite generating-family or coherence hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original intrinsic vanishing Hilbert--Samuel value is biholomorphically invariant]
\label{hir:actual-any-subset-biholomorphic-intrinsic-hs}
\uses{hir:actual-any-subset-biholomorphic-whole-vanishing-ideal,hir:actual-biholomorphic-whole-original-germ-algebra-equivalence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualVanishingIntrinsicHSTransport.hilbertSamuelAt_vanishing_actual_biholomorphic_any}
\leanok
For the same actual geometry, arbitrary subset $W$, point $y$ and every
$\ell\in\mathbb N$, the existing intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel value of
$e^{-1}W$ at $y$ equals that of $W$ at $e(y)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual biholomorphism induces the existing genuine equivalence of
the entire original complex germ algebras. It preserves the actual
maximal ideal and all its powers, and hence the complex dimensions of
the whole original quotient by the ideal plus its $(\ell+1)$-st maximal
power. Apply the preceding entire ideal image identity. The existing
intrinsic point function is exactly that original quotient value in the
preferred chart, proving equality at the arbitrary chosen order.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine open inclusion preserves the entire original vanishing stalk ideal]
\label{hir:actual-any-subset-open-whole-vanishing-ideal}
\uses{hir:actual-open-inclusion-whole-germ-pullback-identity}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualVanishingIntrinsicHSTransport.map_wholeChartStalkIdeal_vanishing_actual_open,Hironaka.HironakaActualVanishingIntrinsicHSTransport.wholeChartStalkIdeal_vanishing_actual_open}
\leanok
Let $U$ be a true open subset of an original complex manifold $X$, let
$W\subseteq X$ be arbitrary and let $y\in U$. The image of the original
whole vanishing stalk ideal of $W$ under the actual open-inclusion germ
pullback is the whole vanishing stalk ideal of $W\cap U$ in the open
subtype. These two actual preferred-chart ideals are equal under the
existing identity expression for the actual open germ pullback.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A source representative restricts to the same actual function on $U$.
Original holomorphicity and near-point vanishing restrict together, so
its actual pulled-back chart germ is a target generator. Conversely a
target representative $h:U\to\mathbb C$ extends by the existing actual
zero extension outside $U$. This extension is required to be holomorphic
only near the original point, where openness gives a neighbourhood
entirely inside $U$. There it is exactly $h$, and it vanishes on $W$.
It therefore gives an original source generator whose actual pullback
is the original chart germ of $h$. Ideal image and span give both whole
inclusions. Finally the existing original open-inclusion germ pullback
is the identity on the actual preferred-chart germ algebra, so its image
ideal is the unchanged source ideal itself.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original intrinsic vanishing Hilbert--Samuel value survives true open restriction]
\label{hir:actual-any-subset-open-intrinsic-hs}
\uses{hir:actual-any-subset-open-whole-vanishing-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualVanishingIntrinsicHSTransport.hilbertSamuelAt_vanishing_actual_open_any}
\leanok
For every actual open $U$, arbitrary subset $W$, point $y\in U$ and
order $\ell$, the existing intrinsic vanishing-ideal Hilbert--Samuel
value of $W\cap U$ at $y$ equals that of $W$ at the same original point.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Unfold the unchanged intrinsic point function into its original whole
preferred-chart ideal quotient value. The preceding actual entire ideal
equality identifies those same ideals, maximal ideals and every power,
so their whole quotient dimensions are equal at every order.
\end{proof}

These are actual vanishing-ideal transport theorems. They do not remove
coherence from a general original sheaf-pullback theorem. Construction
of actual manifold blowup coordinate charts and the positive-dimensional
permissible-centre Bennett comparison remain separate tasks.
