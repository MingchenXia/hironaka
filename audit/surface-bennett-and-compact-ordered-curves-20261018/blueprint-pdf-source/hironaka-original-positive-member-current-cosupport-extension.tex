\section{Actual current cosupport of a retained positive member lower}

The positive member reduction of
\cite[Sections~5--6]{bierstone-milman08-functoriality} must retain its
original coefficient reference, actual indexed members and literal run.
A centre admissible for the upper record can miss the smaller member
carrier. The argument below proves point membership without declaring
the lower tail admissible or canonical. It uses the controlled
one-sided restriction theorem on the same original history.

\begin{theorem}[The retained positive endpoint belongs to the actual remaining strict carrier]
\label{hir:original-positive-member-actual-endpoint-carrier}
\uses{hir:member-lower-extension-actual-mask-identity,hir:member-tail-actual-clopen-strict,bcalc:member-intersection-tower,hir:step-one-mask-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPositiveMemberCurrentCosupport.original_positive_member_endpoint_mem_carrier}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an original well-formed positive-mark record in positive ambient
dimension. Let $H$ followed by $R$ be a genuine test history of its actual
$\overline M$. Let $y$ be an actual current cosupport point, $y_0=R(y)$,
and $s+1$ its current positive level. Assume that the head point $y_0$
is in that same positive nonspecial regime. Form the ACTUAL member
remainder $K$ from $\overline M_H$ and its original members through
$y_0$. Then $y\in K_R.N$. No lower test or current lower cosupport
membership is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $m=\overline M_H$, $S$ for the actual indexed member positions
through $y_0$, and $V=\operatorname{maskA}(M,H,y_0)$. Genuine positive
nonspecial head data internally gives $y_0\in V$. Projecting the actual
upper cosupport point along the genuine upper tail gives $y_0\in m.N$.
Every selected original member contains $y_0$ by its definition, so
$y_0$ lies in the full member carrier $F=m.N\cap\bigcap_{i\in S}m.E_i$.
The original lower record on $V$ is the literal pullback of the member
restriction. The already proved whole initial-record identity also
identifies it with the pullback of the actual remaining-component
record $K$. Reading their actual carrier fields at $y_0\in V$ therefore
gives $y_0\in K.N$, before any transformed lower membership is asserted.

The exact indexed level dictionary on $V$ applies at $R(y)=y_0$.
Equality of current and head levels forces $y$ to lie on the strict
transform of EVERY original member through $y_0$. Actual upper carrier
membership and the genuine strict-intersection theorem thus give
$y\in\operatorname{strictAlong}_R(F)$.
The remaining and selected components form a disjoint closed partition
$F=K.N\cup F_{\rm sel}$: both are genuine unions of components of the
closed smooth member carrier. Strict transform preserves finite unions,
and the strict transforms of the two closed disjoint parts remain
disjoint since their projections lie in the respective old parts.
Consequently
\[
 \operatorname{strictAlong}_R(K.N)
 =\operatorname{strictAlong}_R(F)\cap R^{-1}(K.N).
\]
The two independently proved endpoint facts, together with
$R(y)=y_0\in K.N$, give membership in this strict transform. It is
literally the actual current carrier $K_R.N$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual computed positive-member current cosupport without a lower test]
\label{hir:original-positive-member-computed-current-cosupport}
\uses{hir:original-positive-member-actual-endpoint-carrier,hir:original-controlled-reference-current-cosupport,mc:homogenized-tower-along,hir:member-tail-actual-mask-transform-identity}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPositiveMemberCurrentCosupport.original_computed_positive_member_current_cosupport}
\leanok
For an original DOMAIN $b$ record $M$, with arbitrary natural bound $b$,
ANY genuine original upper test
history $H R$, an actual upper current cosupport point and an actual
retained positive nonspecial head regime as above, construct the true
endpoint subtype on $V=\operatorname{maskA}(M,H,R(y))$ and prove
\[
 y_V\in\operatorname{cosupp}
   \left((\operatorname{lowerMarked}(M,s+1,H,R(y)))_{R|_V}\right).
\]
The current member lower is the unchanged original computed record.
There is no input lower test or desired endpoint membership.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original homogenized-coefficient weak equivalence supplies the genuine
$\overline M$ upper test history and its same whole current cosupport.
Split that actual history into the head and tail. At the head write
$m=\overline M_H$ and take the full reference $B=\mathcal C(m)$ formed
ONCE there. The true coefficient theorem makes $R$ a test history of
this full $B$ and identifies $B_R$'s cosupport with $m_R$'s cosupport.
Original well-formedness internally supplies the coherent smooth full
reference. The actual member remainder $K$ has a closed carrier
contained in $m.N=B.N$, with LITERALLY the same ideal and marking as
$B$. Its initial restricted comparison is therefore reflexive.

The preceding theorem internally gives $y\in K_R.N$. The controlled
reference history theorem now gives
$B_R.\mathrm{cosupp}\cap K_R.N\subseteq K_R.\mathrm{cosupp}$,
so the actual upper endpoint belongs to $K_R$'s cosupport. Original
DOMAIN $b$ member geometry supplies its real coherent current ideal and
whole actual open controlled-record identity. Pulling the actual
cosupport back through the literal endpoint inclusion, then substituting
that whole identity, gives the displayed current lower membership.
This uses the original lower ideal and ordered boundary, including every
empty step; it never changes the upper history into a lower test.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original run constructs its positive lower current cosupport internally]
\label{hir:original-positive-member-actual-run-current-cosupport}
\uses{hir:original-positive-member-computed-current-cosupport,hir:step-one-mask-tower,hir:tower-split}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalPositiveMemberCurrentCosupport.original_run_positive_member_current_cosupport}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an original DOMAIN $b$ record, with arbitrary natural bound
$b$, and $T$ ANY original upper test
history, and $y$ an actual current upper cosupport point that is
nonspecial and has positive current level $s+1$. Use the UNCHANGED
original regime-run counter to form its head $H$, tail $R$ and base point
$R(y)$. These inputs ALONE construct the actual mask endpoint and prove
its membership in the current original positive member lower cosupport.
No run, head, mask, reference geometry or lower membership certificate
is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original run-head theorem, applied to the true upper test and
cosupport point, internally proves that the projected head point lies
in the same current-level nonspecial regime and in the true head
cosupport. Positivity chooses the actual member mask. The genuine
head--tail theorem retains the SAME original history, all positions and
its literal endpoint, and the actual current level equals the head
regime level by that theorem. Apply the preceding constructed-membership
producer to these exact head and tail objects and that genuine
retained-level equality. Its returned endpoint is precisely the subtype
used in the original run's lower marked ideal, so its actual current
membership is the asserted one.
\end{proof}
