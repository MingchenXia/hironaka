\begin{theorem}[An actual stagewise tower comparison is unique]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-stagewise-tower-comparison-unique}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-stage-matched-blowup-unique-lift,mc:tower-isomorphism-append}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveFiniteRecipeComparison.actual_tower_comparison_unique}
\leanok
For two fixed actual finite towers $T',T$ and a fixed genuine base
biholomorphism $\Phi$, two actual stagewise tower isomorphisms over
$\Phi$ have the SAME terminal biholomorphism. This is uniqueness of
stagewise comparisons, not of arbitrary maps over the composite base.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the first actual stagewise isomorphism. At the reflexive towers
the other isomorphism must also have terminal map $\Phi$, by the
reflexive constructor. At a successor, invert the other isomorphism's
last constructor. It supplies an actual shorter stagewise isomorphism,
a real next projection square and a true centre match. The induction
hypothesis equates its shorter stage map with that of the first
isomorphism. Both actual terminal maps are therefore holomorphic maps
of the SAME two actual blowups satisfying the SAME next projection
square. The preceding genuine same-centre uniqueness makes their
underlying functions equal. Equality of the actual biholomorphisms
follows pointwise. This also treats every empty-centre position.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Literal difference recipe tails of the same length have an actual comparison]

genuine stagewise isomorphism over the identity and actual terminal map

zero, when the tail isomorphism is the actual reflexive one over
$\chi_0$ and the full comparison is the given prefix comparison.
A successor on one side forces a successor on the other; their shorter

and are compared by the same universal property.
\end{proof}
