\begin{theorem}[Actual next arrows and centres respect the already chosen stage maps]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-successor-chosen-map-compatible}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-successor-data,hir:tower-isomorphism,hir:sharp-actual-stagewise-tower-comparison-unique}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformSuccessorComparison.actualUniformSuccessor_projection,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformSuccessorComparison.actualUniformSuccessor_compatible}
\leanok
Given two actual finite histories over a genuine base biholomorphism,
and genuine stagewise isomorphisms of their actual uniform histories at
$k$ and $k+1$, the independently constructed next blowup arrows commute
with THESE actual stage maps. The inverse image of the target centre
under THIS already chosen stage-$k$ map is the source centre. Each actual
next arrow also factors its full projection through the actual previous
projection. The centre
and next arrow themselves are outputs of the actual successor construction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Rewrite both next histories by their literal actual last-step equalities.
Invert the genuine next-stage tower-isomorphism constructor. This yields
an actual isomorphism of the preceding histories, its true centre-preimage
identity and the actual commuting next-blowup square. The preceding-history
universal uniqueness theorem identifies this actual predecessor map with
the already chosen stage-$k$ map. Substitute that equality in the entire
centre identity and square. No commuting square or centre correspondence
is assumed, and no map is identified merely because its projection to the
original base agrees. The absolute projection identity follows from the
literal last-step equality and the actual tower projection recursion.
\end{proof}
