\section{Actual Cartesian maps between independently chosen terminal histories}

Retain the ORIGINAL source and target marked records and their given whole
canonical terminal histories, including different actual empty-step counts.
The smooth functoriality route of \cite{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
is adapted by constructing an actual Cartesian source history internally,
then comparing its genuine final space with the independently given source
terminal history. No source history is presumed to be an actual Cartesian
pullback, and no equality of differently sized indexed exceptional lists
is asserted. The resulting final-space map is holomorphic and its entire
projection square has the genuine holomorphic pullback universal property.

\begin{theorem}[Original terminal inputs internally compare the given source with one actual Cartesian model]
\label{hir:smooth-original-terminal-source-actual-cartesian-comparison}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionOriginalTerminalCartesian.original_terminal_source_actual_cartesian_comparison}
\leanok
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:smooth-original-ambient-canonical-history-values,hir:submersion-cartesian-choice-actual-production,hir:submersion-relative-control-joint-tower,hir:original-ambient-terminal-choices-all-dimensions}
Let $f:Y\to X$ be an actual smooth submersion, $M$ an original bound-$n$
record on $X$, and let $T$ and $S$ be GIVEN whole terminal canonical
histories of $M$ and its actual marked pullback on $Y$ for their actual
ambient canonical invariants. From these original inputs and primitive
individual geometric blowup choices, internally construct the actual
Cartesian history $Q$ over the SAME $T$, its whole source canonicality and
terminality, and a genuine biholomorphism $\eta:S_{\rm final}\to Q_{\rm final}$
with $p_Q\eta=p_S$. No source domain, actual-Cartesian-history, compressed
history, source comparison or source canonicality for $Q$ is supplied.
Every ambient dimension, genuine empty manifold and actual empty step is
included, without source compactness or surjectivity of $f$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compute the actual whole source history from the primitive choices and
the GIVEN original target history. Its real stage witnesses give actual
Cartesian source centres and maps. Original smooth domain pullback supplies
the source ambient domain. The completed entire ambient canonical history
producer supplies whole canonicality of THIS $Q$ internally. Given target
terminality supplies true target geometric testness. The original relative
marked dictionary gives source testness, and its entire cosupport dictionary
is the preimage of the actual empty target terminal cosupport. Therefore
THIS $Q$ is terminal. Now apply the completed original ambient terminal
choice comparison to the GIVEN $S$ and THIS internally constructed canonical
terminal $Q$. In positive dimension it internally removes their actual
empty steps before genuine canonical terminal comparison. In dimension
zero it internally constructs their actual whole projection biholomorphisms.
Both routes give an actual final biholomorphism satisfying $p_Q\eta=p_S$.
No equal recorded length or caller-normalized comparison is required.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original given final spaces admit a holomorphic Cartesian comparison map]
\label{hir:smooth-original-terminal-final-holomorphic-pullback}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionOriginalTerminalCartesian.original_terminal_final_holomorphic_cartesian}
\leanok
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:smooth-original-terminal-source-actual-cartesian-comparison,hir:submersion-cartesian-tower-whole-universal}
With ONLY the same original inputs, internally produce a holomorphic map
$g:S_{\rm final}\to T_{\rm final}$ with $p_Tg=fp_S$. Its ENTIRE square is a
genuine holomorphic pullback: for EVERY actual complex manifold $W$ of any
dimension and compatible holomorphic maps $u:W\to Y$, $v:W\to T_{\rm final}$
with $fu=p_Tv$, there is EXACTLY ONE holomorphic $w:W\to S_{\rm final}$ with
$p_Sw=u$ and $gw=v$. No final map, lift, universal-property or source-history
comparison certificate is a caller premise. This compares GIVEN finite
terminal spaces; arbitrary noncompact resolution existence, global gluing,
uniqueness from the base projection alone and functor cocycles remain
separate obligations.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first theorem internally supplies the actual model $Q$, its real
submersion lift $g_Q$ and actual final comparison $\eta$. Define
$g=g_Q\eta$. Both factors are holomorphic, and the real whole Cartesian
projection identity together with $p_Q\eta=p_S$ gives $p_Tg=fp_S$ at every
point. For an actual compatible holomorphic cone $(u,v)$, the genuine whole
Cartesian universal property of $Q$ internally gives a unique holomorphic
lift $w_Q$ with $p_Qw_Q=u$ and $g_Qw_Q=v$. Choose $w=\eta^{-1}w_Q$.
The true inverse identity and the actual projection comparison give
$p_Sw=u$ and $gw=v$; composition of the genuine holomorphic maps gives
holomorphicity. Any other holomorphic lift $w'$ produces a holomorphic
lift $\eta w'$ into THIS $Q$, with both original cone equations by the
same whole dictionaries. Its genuine universal uniqueness gives
$\eta w'=w_Q$. Injectivity of the actual final biholomorphism gives
$w'=\eta^{-1}w_Q$. This proves the full actual universal property on the
original final spaces, rather than a transported certificate supplied by
the caller. Different empty-step counts and indexed exceptional lists
remain recorded; only genuine final-space geometry is compared.
\end{proof}
