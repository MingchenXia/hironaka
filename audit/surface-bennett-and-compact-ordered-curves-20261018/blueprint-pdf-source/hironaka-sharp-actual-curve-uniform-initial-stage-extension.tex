\section{The actual initial global stage is the original space}

\begin{lemma}[The zero-prefix projection is an actual local biholomorphism]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-zero-prefix-biholomorphism}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-head-successor}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformInitialStage.zeroPrefixBiholomorphism,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformInitialStage.zeroPrefixBiholomorphism_point}
\leanok
For every genuine finite blowup history over a complex manifold, the entire
zero-prefix endpoint is biholomorphic to its original base by its actual
projection. Every position of the original history is retained in the
full-history construction; taking its zero prefix selects the genuine
reflexive history, rather than deleting empty steps from a positive prefix.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The whole dependent endpoint-and-history identity for the zero prefix
identifies it with the original base equipped with its reflexive history.
On that history the identity biholomorphism has exactly the required
projection. Transport the entire assertion, including both its source
manifold and the actual projection formula, along this whole identity.
The resulting actual biholomorphism has point map equal to the zero-prefix
projection on its entire source. No separate identification is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual initial-stage projection is the original-space biholomorphism]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-initial-stage}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-zero-prefix-biholomorphism,hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-uniform-stage-atlas,hir:sharp-actual-endpoint-saturated-local-range,hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-global-uniform-stages}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformInitialStage.initialProjection_bijective,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformInitialStage.initialProjectionBiholomorphism,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformInitialStage.initialProjectionBiholomorphism_point,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformInitialStage.originalInitialBiholomorphism,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformInitialStage.originalInitialBiholomorphism_point}
\leanok
For actual retained local histories covering the original complex manifold,
the SAME actual global zero-stage projection is a biholomorphism onto that
original manifold. Its entire inverse on every original chart is the
actual local zero-prefix inverse followed by the actual gluing inclusion.
Only the original positive-dimensional smooth pure curve, ordered SNC,
and density data internally supply this construction for the original
uniform stages. No initial-stage isomorphism or inverse certificate is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each actual chart, the first lemma identifies its zero-prefix
projection with a genuine local biholomorphism onto the original chart.
The exact glued projection law identifies the global projection on the
actual inclusion with this local projection followed by the original
open inclusion. For any original point choose a chart containing it;
the inverse local biholomorphism followed by the actual inclusion gives
a global preimage. This proves surjectivity.

If two global points have equal original images, choose one original
chart containing their common image. The entire saturated-range identity
places both global points in the SAME actual inclusion range. Choose
their local representatives there. The exact projection law makes their
local projection values equal. The actual local biholomorphism is
injective, so these representatives, and hence the original global points,
are equal. This proves injectivity using actual ranges, rather than an
assumed separation or inverse property.

Take the inverse of this actual bijective projection. On the entire
original chart it equals the actual gluing inclusion composed with the
local zero-prefix inverse: applying the now proved injective projection
to these two values gives the same original point. This composite is
holomorphic by the actual inclusion and local inverse holomorphicity.
Every original point belongs to one such genuine open chart, and the
open-subtype holomorphicity criterion therefore proves holomorphicity
of the entire inverse. The original projection is already holomorphic.
Its actual bijectivity supplies both inverse laws, so it is the stated
biholomorphism, with underlying map precisely the original projection.
Finally the original-only histories and their actual cover supply all
inputs internally. This identifies stage zero; neither a uniform finite
length nor a globally finite scheduler is concluded.
\end{proof}
