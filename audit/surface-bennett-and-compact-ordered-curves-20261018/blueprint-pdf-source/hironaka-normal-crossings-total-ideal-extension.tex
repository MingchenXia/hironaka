\label{sec:hironaka-normal-crossings-total-ideal}

\begin{lemma}[Actual nonzero whole original coordinate product]
\label{hir:general-nc-original-nonzero-chart-product}
\uses{hir:general-nc-actual-local-snc-branches,hir:associated-original-equations-actual-unit}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsTotalIdeal.exists_nonzero_original_coordinate_equation}
\leanok
At every original point of a genuine NC carrier, a real flattening chart
internally gives a nonzero holomorphic product germ whose entire zero germ is
the original carrier chart germ. Every original factor is an actual normal
coordinate of that flattening; an empty branch set gives the unit product.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write e for the actual chart, q=e(x), Phi for the genuine NC flattening and S
for its actual finite set of distinct original coordinates. Its shifted map
F(z)=Phi(z+q) is holomorphic near zero. The genuine holomorphic local inverse
satisfies F composed with its shifted inverse equal to the identity near F(0).
Differentiate that true identity. The differential of F is surjective, since
the derivative of the inverse is its right inverse. Composing each actual
coordinate projection with this surjective differential gives a nonzero
linear map. Each coordinate $F_j$ has value zero at zero and nonzero derivative;
its entire holomorphic germ is therefore prime and nonzero. The whole germ
algebra is an integral domain, so the product over S is nonzero. If S is empty
it is 1. Near zero, the actual inverse chart lands in the actual flat domain,
and the NC definition identifies membership in the original chart germ with
vanishing of at least one precisely indexed original coordinate. The finite
product vanishes exactly under that condition. This proves the whole zero-germ
dictionary, not a supplied equation or a generator certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Internally invertible complete original NC vanishing ideal]
\label{hir:general-nc-whole-original-ideal-invertible}
\uses{hir:general-nc-original-nonzero-chart-product,hir:original-equations-empty-interior-invertible}

\leanok

zero the carrier is empty and its whole ideal is the unit ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

neighbourhood, hence has zero holomorphic germ, contradicting the preceding
nonzero product. Thus the actual interior is empty. In positive dimension,
apply the proved whole original-equation reduction theorem to the internally
produced nonzero equation at every original point and this genuine empty
interior. It produces the reduced local generator and equality with every
nearby stalk of the complete original vanishing ideal; its generator is
nondegenerate. These internally produced neighbourhoods give actual
invertibility. In dimension zero the finite coordinate index type is empty.
The NC membership dictionary therefore excludes every point from the carrier.
Its vanishing ideal is the unit ideal, generated by the holomorphic nonzero
constant 1 on the whole ambient manifold.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Whole original and finite boundary full-preimage dictionary]
\label{hir:general-nc-original-whole-preimage-step}
\uses{hir:general-nc-actual-highest-step-pair-transport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsTotalIdeal.preimage_original_union_boundary_step}
\leanok

closed boundary E and an actual centre C contained in K, the whole preimage of
K together with the boundary union equals the union of geoStrict(pi,C,K),
every geoStrict(pi,C,$E_i$), and the entire new exceptional pi inverse C.
The full preimage of K alone equals its geometric strict transform together
with this whole new exceptional set.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The identity K=(K minus C) union C gives the full-preimage decomposition.
Each nonexceptional preimage point belongs to its closure, which remains in
the closed full preimage of K by actual continuity. All points of the new
exception lie in that full preimage because C is contained in K. This proves
both inclusions for K. For any old $E_i$, a full-preimage point away from C
belongs to its geometric strict transform, and a point above C belongs to the
whole new exceptional set. Conversely its geometric strict closure remains
inside the closed full preimage of $E_i$. Take the actual finite union and use
the already proved new-exception inclusion in the full preimage of K. No
condition C contained in $E_i$ is required.
\end{proof}
