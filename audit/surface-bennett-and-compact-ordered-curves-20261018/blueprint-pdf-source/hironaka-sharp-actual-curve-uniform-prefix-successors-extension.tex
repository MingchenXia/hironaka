\section{Actual successor blowups of uniform local histories}

Here the uniform prefix retains every actual blowup position, including the
identity blowups of the empty centre used after a finite local history ends.
All equalities below are equalities of the WHOLE dependent endpoint--tower
pair. They do not replace a history by a compressed or merely isomorphic one.

\begin{lemma}[True iterated head packets and the original zeroth stage]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-head-successor}
\uses{hir:tower-split,hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-padding}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixSuccessors.headPacket_head,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixSuccessors.uniformPrefix_zero,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixSuccessors.uniformPrefix_head_one}
\leanok
For ANY actual finite blowup history $T$, taking its head after removing $j$
last positions, and then removing $r$, gives literally its head packet after
removing $j+r$. The actual zeroth uniform prefix is the original ambient
with its reflexive history. Removing the last position of the actual
$(k+1)$st uniform prefix gives literally the $k$th uniform prefix.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual tower, with the numbers of removed positions arbitrary.
For a reflexive tower every head packet is reflexive. For an actual last
blowup, removing zero changes nothing; removing a successor reduces to the
previous tower and one fewer removed position, including its actual stage.
The same recursion at the actual entire length proves the zeroth assertion.
Apply the iterated-head identity to the defining head of the tower obtained
by appending $k+1$ actual empty identity blowups. Removing its final position
literally leaves the tower with $k$ such actual empty successors. The complete
endpoint and tower pair is retained throughout.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual next centre and blowup arrow are internally constructed]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-actual-next-blowup}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-head-successor,hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-padding}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixSuccessors.uniformPrefix_successor}
\leanok
For ANY actual finite history $T$ and every $k$, there exist a centre in the
actual $k$th uniform stage and an actual map from stage $k+1$ to that stage,
with genuine \texttt{IsBlowupAlong}, such that the entire stage-$k+1$ history
is literally the actual successor of the stage-$k$ history by this centre
and map. The whole projection is the preceding projection composed with
this actual blowup arrow. No centre, next arrow or blowup certificate is
an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The length of the actual next prefix is $k+1$, hence is positive. Case
analysis on this actual tower therefore rules out the reflexive constructor
and exposes its actual last centre, map and blowup proof. Its entire head
packet is the actual previous uniform prefix by the preceding lemma.
Transport the WHOLE existential proposition on dependent endpoint--tower
pairs along that exact equality. This retains the original step, centre,
map and blowup proof, and proves literal tower equality. The real projection
recursion for this actual last step gives the asserted projection square.
No choice of an unrelated endpoint or external factorization is involved.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Literal terminal histories and genuine eventual empty successors]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-eventual-empty}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-padding,hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding,hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-head-successor}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixSuccessors.uniformPrefix_of_length_le,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixSuccessors.uniformPrefix_after_length,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveUniformPrefixSuccessors.uniformPrefix_after_length_successor}
\leanok
If $k$ is at least the actual length of $T:X\leftarrow Y$, its actual uniform
prefix packet is literally $(Y,T\mathbin{+}\mathrm{Empty}^{k-|T|})$. In
particular its stage at $|T|+r$ is the SAME original terminal ambient and
the original full history followed by exactly $r$ actual empty identity
blowups. Its next step in these identifications has centre empty and map
identity, with the original genuine empty-centre blowup proof.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First induct on the number of positions removed from an actual empty
suffix, keeping its length arbitrary. Removing zero leaves that entire
suffix; removing a successor requires a nonzero suffix and its actual last
empty identity blowup is removed by the defining split recursion. The
induction leaves exactly the original suffix length minus the number
removed, with the SAME terminal ambient. Apply this to the defining
uniform prefix, whose suffix has length $k$ and from which exactly $|T|$
positions are removed. The assumed inequality justifies the recursion.
Substitute $k=|T|+r$ and use actual natural subtraction. At $r+1$ unfold the
last actual empty-tail constructor: it is literally the identity map,
empty centre and genuine original empty-centre blowup. Every empty position
is present. This is local eventual stationarity with the actual local
length; no uniform global bound, overlap-arrow descent or global blowup
sequence is concluded here.
\end{proof}
