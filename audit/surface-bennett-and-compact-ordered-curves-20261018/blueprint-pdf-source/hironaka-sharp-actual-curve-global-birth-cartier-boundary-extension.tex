\begin{theorem}[A locally finite simultaneous hyperplane family has a true NC union]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-locally-finite-coordinate-union-nc}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-global-indexed-boundary-snc-coordinates,hir:general-nc-actual-local-snc-branches}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalBirthCartierBoundary.normalCrossingsCarrier_iUnion_of_locallyFinite,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalBirthCartierBoundary.normalCrossingsCarrier_iUnion_offset}
\leanok
For ANY locally finite family of closed subsets of a complex manifold,
assume actual simultaneous holomorphic coordinates at each point make
every incident member a coordinate hyperplane. Its ENTIRE union is an
original \texttt{IsNormalCrossingsCarrier}. Repeated coordinate labels
need not be identified globally. There is no supplied finite local
union, nonincident-avoidance window or equation certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Local finiteness and closedness give closedness of the whole union.
At a real point $x$, local finiteness gives finitely many incident
indices. The complements of ALL nonincident closed members have a
common neighborhood of $x$: this follows from the actual locally finite
closed-family neighborhood theorem, even if infinitely many labels are
present globally. Choose a genuine open subset of that neighborhood.

Take the supplied actual simultaneous chart. Restrict its source by
the true preferred-chart image of that open subset; its actual inverse
makes this image open and ensures the restricted source still contains
the actual center. The original forward/inverse maps are retained and
all holomorphic identities continue to hold on the smaller domains.
Let $S$ be the finite union of the singleton coordinate sets of the
actual incident indices. At EVERY point of the smaller flat domain,
membership in any family member implies that this member was incident
at $x$. Its actual flattening identity and singleton cardinality give
one coordinate in $S$ which vanishes. Conversely a zero coordinate in
$S$ belongs to a real incident singleton; its full flattening identity
puts the point in that same actual member. Thus the ENTIRE union on
the genuine flat domain is exactly the union of these coordinate
hyperplanes. This is the original NC-carrier definition, not an
assumed finite chart model. Empty incidence gives the empty union and
constant nonzero local equation.
\end{proof}

pure curve, actual ordered SNC family and true boundary-complement
density, the SAME born union $B=\bigcup_j D_{|E|+j}$ on the SAME

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

Each born member is one of its actual closed members. The map
$j\mapsto |E|+j$ is genuinely injective, so the actual locally finite
whole family restricts to a locally finite born family, retaining ALL
empty positions. The original-only global-coordinate theorem gives
one true simultaneous holomorphic chart for ALL natural positions and
singleton cardinality at every incident position. Restrict that real
coordinate family along the literal offset map. The preceding theorem
now constructs the entire born union's NC structure internally. No
caller atlas, local cutoff or NC witness is supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The SAME whole reduced vanishing ideal is an actual effective Cartier divisor]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-original-born-reduced-cartier-divisor}

$\mathcal I_B$. Its support is exactly $B$, that whole ideal is

EVERY positive power. Its actual support is closed analytic, contains
the whole differential exceptional locus, and lies in the preimage of
the original sharp bad locus.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding genuine NC structure is supplied to the existing G6
whole-original-ideal invertibility theorem. That theorem constructs
nonzero actual finite coordinate product germs and uses the original
whole vanishing dictionary; it does not assume a generator or an
invertible substitute. Use this complete ideal and its actual
invertibility in the existing effective-divisor constructor.
Invertibility supplies actual coherence. Closedness of the SAME NC
carrier gives the existing exact zero-locus-of-vanishing-ideal identity,
so the constructed divisor's actual support is exactly $B$, and the

pointwise complex reducedness implies that the function itself
vanishes there; conversely its positive power does. This proves
reducedness of the literal whole ideal, without treating multiplicities
of indexed labels as coefficients or forming an infinite product.
Finally substitute the exact support identity into the two already
proved whole exceptional/birth support inclusions. General global
scheduling and the higher-dimensional sharp driver remain separate.
\end{proof}
