\section{Actual smooth coordinate-plane relative ideal powers}
\label{sec:hironaka-relative-normal-ideal}

This supplies the entire normal ideal and finite relative truncations of an
actual smooth coordinate plane, from genuine holomorphic functions. All scalar
coefficients are whole holomorphic germs on the actual tangential plane. No
kernel, power equality, finite-generation or frame certificate is an input.
Finite generation is not flatness or freeness. Full smooth-centre normal
flatness, all-degree relative ranks and actual higher Hilbert--Samuel blowup
descent remain open.

\begin{definition}[Original tangential projection and inclusion]
\label{hir:relative-normal-original-split}

\leanok
For $n=d+t$, let $\pi:\mathbf C^{d+t}\to\mathbf C^d$ be the actual first-$d$
coordinate projection and $i:\mathbf C^d\to\mathbf C^{d+t}$ the actual first-$d$
coordinate inclusion with the last $t$ coordinates zero. Both are original
continuous complex linear maps. Their actual composite $\pi i$ is identity;
$i\pi$ is identity precisely on the original coordinate plane $\mathbf C^d\times0$.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
Construct $\pi$ from the finite actual coordinate continuous linear functionals
and the genuine finite-product/PiLp linear equivalence. Construct $i$ as the
finite sum of the actual source coordinate functionals times the corresponding
actual ambient coordinate vectors. Evaluate each coordinate to obtain the
projection/inclusion formulas and the two true identities. In the normal
coordinates, $i\pi$ is zero, so its fixed-point condition is exactly the actual
plane equations; in the tangential coordinates it preserves the original value.
Continuous complex linear maps are everywhere holomorphic, including $d=0$,
$t=0$ and empty ambient dimension.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Entire original germ algebra retraction]
\label{hir:relative-normal-entire-germ-retraction}

Actual composition gives $\pi^*:R_d\to R$ and $i^*:R\to R_d$, genuine entire

surjective and the pullback injective. Use $\pi^*$ as the real $R_d$-algebra
structure on $R$. Every original normal-coordinate germ $z_{d+j}$ has trace zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Both actual linear maps fix zero and are holomorphic, so their real nonlinear
composition maps on ALL holomorphic representatives induce entire germ algebra
homomorphisms. On any original holomorphic representative the composite acts
by $F\circ\pi\circ i=F$ at every point, proving the retraction. Any entire

of pullbacks proves injectivity. Each actual normal coordinate vanishes
identically after $i$, giving trace zero as an equality of entire germs.
The algebra action is literally multiplication by this actual pullback.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The entire reduced plane ideal is exactly the whole trace kernel]
\label{hir:relative-normal-entire-plane-kernel}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-whole-hadamard}

\leanok
Let $J$ be the entire ring ideal generated by the actual $t$ normal coordinate
germs. Then $J=\ker i^*$ and membership is exactly vanishing near zero on the
actual coordinate plane, for an original holomorphic representative. The true
quotient $R/J$ is isomorphic as an entire complex germ algebra to $R_d$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every normal coordinate has trace zero; since trace is an actual ring
homomorphism, its kernel contains their whole generated ideal. Conversely if
an ENTIRE germ has trace zero, its actual relative Hadamard decomposition has
zero base term and expresses it as a true finite sum of those coordinate
multiples. This lies in the full ideal $J$ and proves both ideal inclusions.
If an actual representative vanishes on the plane near zero, composing with
$i$ gives zero near zero because $i$ is continuous and fixes zero; hence its
whole trace is zero. Conversely trace zero gives a representative $F$ whose
$F\circ i$ is zero near zero. For ambient points near zero on the plane,
$\pi$ continuity sends them into that true neighbourhood and $i\pi z=z$, so
$F(z)=0$. This proves the genuine all-representative geometric dictionary.
Finally the actual trace is surjective, and its real ring/complex-algebra
first-isomorphism theorem gives the entire plane quotient, using the proved
kernel equality. Thus the ideal is reduced, without assuming reducedness or
supplying an abstract ideal-kernel certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Internally constructed all-degree relative normal-word representatives]
\label{hir:relative-normal-all-power-representatives}

$R_d$-linear combination $p$ of actual normal-coordinate words of length
strictly below $q$, with $g-p\in J^q$. Here the scalar action is actual $\pi^*$;
coefficients are entire tangential holomorphic germs, not point scalars.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual $q$. At $q=0$ choose zero; the real zeroth power is the
entire ring. For $q+1$, apply the actual relative Hadamard decomposition to $g$
and the induction hypothesis to each internally produced ENTIRE coefficient
$h_j$. Write $h_j=p_j+r_j$, with actual $r_j\in J^q$. Set
$p=\pi^*i^*g+\sum_j z_{d+j}p_j$. The base term is its actual $R_d$ coefficient
times the genuine degree-zero word. Prefixing each actual normal word by

required below-$q+1$ span. Multiplication by a normal coordinate commutes with
the true $R_d$ action, so this extends from generating words to their entire
scalar span. The true remainder is $\sum_j z_{d+j}r_j$; actual ideal power
multiplication puts each summand in $J\,J^q=J^{q+1}$ and finite ideal addition
puts the whole remainder there. This is a genuine manufactured all-power
representative, not a caller Taylor or normal-ideal-power equality certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[True entire relative power quotients are finite tangential modules]
\label{hir:relative-normal-entire-power-quotients-finite}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-all-power-representatives}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalIdeal.normalWordSpan_fg,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalIdeal.finite_quotient_sup_normalIdeal_pow,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalIdeal.finite_quotient_normalIdeal_pow}
\leanok
For every $q$, the literal entire ring quotient $R/J^q$ is a finite $R_d$ module.
More generally, for EVERY whole original ring ideal $I$, the literal quotient
$R/(I+J^q)$ is a finite $R_d$ module. No freeness or flatness is inferred.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual normal-word index is finite: a degree in $\{0,\ldots,q-1\}$ and a
word of that degree in the actual $t$ normal coordinates. Its real finite
$R_d$ span is therefore finitely generated. Every ENTIRE quotient member has
an actual whole-germ representative, and the preceding internally constructed
representative differs from that whole germ by an actual $J^q$ member. The
real quotient map sends both to the same class. Thus the actual image of the
finite normal-word span is the ENTIRE quotient module. Taking its finite image
of generators proves genuine module finiteness. The identical representative
works for $I+J^q$, since the actual power is contained in this actual sum ideal.
At $q=0$ both literal quotients are zero; at $t=0$ all positive truncations
have the actual degree-zero word alone and recover the tangential whole germ
ring or its true quotient. At $d=0$ these are genuine finite point truncations.
No relative monomial independence, free graded pieces, normal-flat centre or
all-degree constant rank is presumed or claimed.
\end{proof}
