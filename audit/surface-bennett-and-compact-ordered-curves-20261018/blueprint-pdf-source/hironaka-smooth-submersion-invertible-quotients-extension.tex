\section{Whole invertible ideal quotients under genuine submersions}

Let $f:Y\to X$ be a genuine holomorphic submersion of arbitrary smooth complex manifolds, with source dimension $m$ and target dimension $n$. We use the original germ ideals, original colon ideal, original natural powers and literal marked pullback. No openness, quotient, factorization or controlled-transform certificate is supplied. The general higher-invariant and canonical blowup base-change goals remain open.

\begin{lemma}\label{hir:submersion-actual-nonzero-generator}
\uses{hir:submersion-actual-map-nhds}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionInvertibleQuotients.exists_ne_zero_comp_on_open}
\leanok
Suppose the original function $h$ vanishes identically on no nonempty open subset of $U\subseteq X$. Then $h\circ f$ vanishes identically on no nonempty open subset of $f^{-1}U$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take a nonempty open $W\subseteq f^{-1}U$ and an actual point $w\in W$. The internally proved equality of entire image neighbourhood filters says that $f(W)$ is a neighbourhood of $f(w)$: its preimage contains the genuine neighbourhood $W$. Choose an open neighbourhood $V$ of $f(w)$ contained in this actual image. Then $V\subseteq U$. The original nonvanishing condition supplies $z\in V$ with $h(z)\ne0$. Since $z\in f(W)$, take its actual preimage $y\in W$. This gives $(h\circ f)(y)\ne0$. No image-openness hypothesis or nonvanishing witness is an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:submersion-actual-invertible-pullback}
\uses{hir:submersion-actual-nonzero-generator}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionInvertibleQuotients.isInvertible_pullback_submersion}
\leanok
The whole pullback of an original invertible germ ideal $L$ is invertible, in the genuine source dimension.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At $f(y)$ choose the actual open set $U$ and nonzero local holomorphic generator $h$ from the original invertibility definition. The genuine submersion is holomorphic and continuous. Therefore $f^{-1}U$ is an open neighbourhood of $y$, and $h\circ f$ is holomorphic there. The preceding lemma proves its required nonvanishing on every nonempty open subset. An arbitrary germ in the whole pullback is a finite holomorphic combination of pulled-back original ideal germs. Express each original germ as a multiple of $h$ and compose the actual span expression with $f$. Transitivity of actual holomorphic spans shows that the entire pullback is generated by $h\circ f$. Conversely $h$ is in the original ideal, so its actual pullback generates germs of the pullback ideal. These prove every clause of original invertibility.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:submersion-actual-colon-ideal}
\uses{hir:submersion-actual-invertible-pullback}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionInvertibleQuotients.pullback_colon_eq}
\leanok
For an original coherent ideal $I$ contained in an original invertible ideal $L$, the entire original colon ideal obeys $f^*(I:L)=(f^*I:f^*L)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original coherent colon theorem internally produces coherence of $Q=(I:L)$. Its two genuine inclusions give the full original ideal equality $I=LQ$. Invertibility also gives coherence of $L$. The actual whole product pullback theorem therefore gives $f^*I=(f^*L)(f^*Q)$. The actual pullback of $Q$ is coherent, and the preceding theorem makes $f^*L$ invertible. Cancellation of this genuine invertible local generator in the original colon definition gives $((f^*L)(f^*Q):f^*L)=f^*Q$. Combining these equalities proves the whole colon dictionary. The proof never assumes a caller-produced factorization or quotient.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:submersion-actual-powered-colon}
\uses{hir:submersion-actual-colon-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionInvertibleQuotients.pullback_colon_idealPow_eq}
\leanok
For every natural $q$, if the original coherent ideal $I$ is contained in $L^q$, then $f^*(I:L^q)=(f^*I:(f^*L)^q)$. In particular the power zero and empty-divisor cases use the actual original unit ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original invertible power construction makes $L^q$ invertible for every natural $q$. Apply the preceding full colon theorem to this actual power and the given original inclusion. The whole original coherent-power pullback dictionary identifies $f^*(L^q)$ with $(f^*L)^q$. Rewrite that genuine denominator ideal. The power-zero definition and actual unit pullback are included in the same argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:submersion-actual-quotient-marked-record}
\uses{hir:submersion-actual-colon-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionInvertibleQuotients.markedPullback_colon_record_eq,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionInvertibleQuotients.colon_cosupport_pullback_eq}
\leanok
Replacing an original marked ideal's ideal by its actual colon quotient commutes with the literal entire marked pullback. Its original support, literal ordered boundary list and original mark are retained. For every natural order threshold, the ambient cosupport of the whole pulled-back colon ideal is precisely the preimage of the original ambient cosupport.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The ideal field is exactly the internally proved whole colon equality. The other three original marked fields are unchanged by the quotient, and their actual pullback definitions give the same preimage support, mapped ordered list and original mark on both sides. Destruct the original record to obtain the complete record equality. For the cosupport statement the original quotient is coherent by the genuine coherent-colon theorem. Apply the actual submersion whole-order cosupport dictionary to that entire quotient, then rewrite its pullback by the colon theorem. This proves the statement for every natural threshold, including zero, without an assumed order or cosupport certificate.
\end{proof}
