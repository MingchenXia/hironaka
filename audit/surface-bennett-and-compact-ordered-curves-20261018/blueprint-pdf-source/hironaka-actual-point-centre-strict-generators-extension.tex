\section{Unconditional whole analytic strict generators for a point centre}

The coordinate centre with every variable selected is the original point
at the origin. Its ideal is the whole coordinate maximal ideal. Unlike a
positive-dimensional centre, its coordinate weight is exactly total degree,
so degree-first vertex order itself supplies the required centre filtration.

\begin{lemma}[Canonical reduced vertices automatically respect the point-centre powers]
\label{hir:actual-point-centre-canonical-vertex-powers}
\uses{hir:actual-reduced-formal-vertex-members,hir:actual-formal-coordinate-power-coefficient-kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointCentreStrictGenerators.actualReducedVertexSeries_mem_point_power}
\leanok
For every entire formal ideal $J$ and every genuine degree-lexicographic
vertex $\alpha$, its actual canonical reduced member $F_\alpha$ belongs to
the entire point ideal power $\mathfrak m^{|\alpha|}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The selected-coordinate weight for all variables is their total degree.
Take any exponent $\beta$ of degree less than $|\alpha|$. Degree-first
ordering gives $\beta<_{\deglex}\alpha$. The actual reduced vertex
coefficient theorem then makes its coefficient in $F_\alpha$ zero.
The proved whole coordinate-power coefficient criterion, applied to every
such $\beta$, is exactly membership in $\mathfrak m^{|\alpha|}$. This uses
the actual canonical reduced series and the entire ideal power, with no
compatibility or convergence certificate as an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole point-centre normalized analytic ideals have actual finite generators]
\label{hir:actual-point-centre-whole-analytic-strict-generators}
\uses{hir:actual-point-centre-canonical-vertex-powers,hir:actual-analytic-normalized-member-finite-generation}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualPointCentreStrictGenerators.exists_actual_point_normalized_member_generators}
\leanok
For every original analytic ideal $I$, every actual point-blowup coordinate
chart and every actual chart-direction parameter, internally construct a
finite family of original analytic normalized members $H_\alpha$ such that
the whole normalized-member ideal is $\langle H_\alpha\rangle$, and its
entire genuine Taylor extension is the actual formal strict-transform ideal.
No reduced-formal-vertex centre-power hypothesis is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Form the entire actual Taylor extension $J=T(I)S$. The preceding lemma
supplies the point-centre power condition for every actual canonical
reduced vertex of this same ideal. Apply the proved actual analytic
normalized-member generator theorem with all coordinate variables selected.
It internally chooses original analytic generators in the required whole
ideal intersections and their true analytic exceptional quotients. The
whole Taylor square and formal quotient uniqueness identify their entire
formal normalized members. Both ideal inclusions give the full Taylor
extension equality, and faithful contraction gives generation of the entire
original analytic normalized-member ideal by the same finite family.
The compatibility premise has thus been proved internally from the point
geometry, rather than added to the original input.
\end{proof}

This closes the point-centre case of this local analytic/formal generator
interface for arbitrary original ideals. Identifying the whole geometric
strict closure, proving the general Bennett inequality, and deriving
vertex compatibility from general positive-dimensional permissible centres
remain separate open obligations.
