\section{Actual middle histories between run starts without canonicality}

\begin{lemma}[Genuine original cut recipes persist on actual heads]
\label{hir:original-finite-stepone-cut-head-recipe}
\uses{hir:original-finite-stepone-cut-history,hir:tower-split}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFiniteStepOneCutMiddleHistory.stepOneCutTower_head}
\leanok
Every actual head of a genuine finite original whole Step One cut history
is again that literal cut history. Removing positions retains the real
original centres, maps, cuts and every remaining empty slot.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual tower with the number of removed last positions
arbitrary. At zero remove nothing. At a positive number and an actual
successor, its actual head is the corresponding head of the real preceding
history, whose cut recipe is the literal first field of the successor
recipe. Reflexive heads remain reflexive. This is genuine structural
recursion, with no rebasing or replacement history.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual middle tower and entire earlier-mask avoidance from original finite cuts]
\label{hir:original-finite-stepone-cut-actual-middle-history}
\uses{hir:original-finite-stepone-cut-head-recipe,hir:original-finite-stepone-cut-centres-avoid-earlier-mask,hir:step-one-canonical-tail,hir:step-one-nearby,hir:tower-split}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalFiniteStepOneCutMiddleHistory.exists_actual_middle_of_stepone_cut}
\leanok
For positive ambient dimension and original DOMAIN$(k+1)$ of actual
maximal order, let $T$ be a literal finite original whole Step One cut
history. Let $z$ be an actual current cosupport point of level $s$, with
actual run $r_z$. Let $r_q\geq r_z$ be the actual computed run of $q$ for that same
level-$s$ regime, and suppose its image at its genuine run-start head has
level $s$. Internally construct the actual middle tower $B$ with
\[
 \operatorname{head}_{r_q}T\cdot B=\operatorname{head}_{r_z}T,
 \qquad B\cdot\operatorname{tail}_{r_z}T=\operatorname{tail}_{r_q}T.
\]
EVERY real centre of $B$ avoids the ENTIRE earlier lower mask defined by
the image of $q$ at its actual start. No middle tower, avoidance, lower TEST,
canonicity or desired numerical comparison is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original finite cut geometry gives the genuine entire upper TEST and its
real coefficient/homogenized bar TEST. If the runs are equal, the true split
factorization gives a middle history whose actual length is zero. Thus its
genuine CentresAvoid predicate holds with no deletion of actual steps.

For $r_z<r_q$, both runs are bounded by the actual tower length. Split the
actual history between heads $r_z+1$ and $r_z$. The true length identity
makes this segment exactly one genuine original blowup, exposing its centre,
map and blowup proof. Independently split between heads $r_q$ and $r_z+1$.
Concatenate that actual middle segment with the exposed one-step tower.
The two true split concatenation identities give precisely both required
whole head/tail equations.

One stage before the start of $z$, the literal maximal-run definition says
its image is out of the level-$s$ regime. Genuine cosupport projection
puts that actual image in the old cosupport; the true original ordered
level monotonicity along heads bounds its level below by $s$. Transport
these complete ordinary statements along the actual split equality.
The actual head is itself a genuine cut history by the first lemma.
Its real original/bar cosupport equality supplies the same point in the
original cosupport. Apply the proved finite-cut ALL-centres avoidance
theorem to this true intermediate history and actual out-of-regime point.
It gives the ENTIRE earlier-mask avoidance for the newly constructed
middle tower. Neither a canonical maximum-locus equality nor a lower
numerical certificate was used.

This supplies genuine between-run geometry on finite noncanonical cut
histories. Fixed-reference lower numerical equality still requires a true
source-bound comparison producer: existing canonical-only invariant
rebase/tower-isomorphism interfaces are not silently applied to these
noncanonical lower histories. No all-history FULL, semicoherent stability
or global termination theorem is asserted here.
\end{proof}
