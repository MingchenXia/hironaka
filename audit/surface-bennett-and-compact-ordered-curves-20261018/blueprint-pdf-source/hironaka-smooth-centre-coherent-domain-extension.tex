We use the REAL open target of a genuine original holomorphic flattening.
Its inverse followed by the original inverse chart is a total function
whose holomorphic assertions are made on that open target only. The
original germ ideal is pulled back by the EXISTING literal germ-ideal

\begin{lemma}[Actual local composition of original span relations]
\label{hir:real-centre-domain-local-span-composition}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreCoherentDomain.isInSpanAt_comp_eventually_mdifferentiable}
\leanok
Suppose $f:Y\to M$ is holomorphic on a neighbourhood of an actual point
$y$. Every actual original finite holomorphic span relation at $f(y)$
pulls back to the relation of the composed functions at $y$. Only the
real local holomorphic map and its actual continuity are used.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write the original relation as a finite sum of actual generators times
holomorphic coefficient germs. Compose each actual generator and each
coefficient with $f$. Composition of holomorphic germs is holomorphic
because $f$ is holomorphic near $y$. The original equality holds on a
neighbourhood of $f(y)$; continuity pulls it back to a neighbourhood of
$y$. Its pulled equality is precisely the desired finite sum relation.
No origin-germ-to-nearby-germ map is introduced.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original coherence supplies real local pullback generators]
\label{hir:real-centre-domain-original-pullback-coherence}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-centre-domain-local-span-composition}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreCoherentDomain.exists_local_original_pullback_generators,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreCoherentDomain.coherentOn_actual_pullback}
\leanok
Let $A$ be an ORIGINAL coherent germ ideal on $M$, let $W\subset Y$ be
open and let $f:Y\to M$ be holomorphic at every actual point of $W$.
The EXISTING actual pullback $f^*A$ is coherent ON $W$. At every $y\in W$
the original finite generators at $f(y)$ supply a true open neighbourhood
$V\subset W$, containing $y$, on which their ACTUAL composites with $f$
are holomorphic and generate the pullback at EVERY point of $V$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose actual original coherent generators $s_i$ on an open $O$ containing
$f(y)$. Continuity at $y$ gives a true open $V$ containing $y$, contained
in $W\cap f^{-1}(O)$. Each $s_i\circ f$ is holomorphic on $V$. For a
point $z\in V$, every actual pullback generator $a\circ f$ with
$a\in A_{f(z)}$ is generated by the $s_i\circ f$: apply the original
membership characterization at $f(z)$ and the local composition lemma,
then substitute that finite relation into the actual pullback span.
Conversely each $s_i$ belongs to the original ideal at $f(z)$, so
$s_i\circ f$ is itself a literal pullback generator. Inclusion of the
two actual span families gives the membership equivalence at EVERY $z$.
This is exactly local coherence on $W$, proved from original coherence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine flattening inverse supplies the original real-open family]
\label{hir:real-centre-domain-actual-flattened-coherent-family}
