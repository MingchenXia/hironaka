\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-prefix-terminal-biholomorphism}

projection at every actual point is exactly the projection of that SAME
terminal point. No terminal identification or history-isomorphism
certificate is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine entire endpoint-and-history packet identity identifies the
uniform prefix with the SAME terminal manifold equipped with $T$ and
its literal empty identity tail. On this packet use the actual identity
biholomorphism and reflexive whole-history isomorphism. Transport the
entire existence assertion through the genuine packet identity, keeping

The entire empty identity tail has projection the identity, and the
actual concatenation projection formula therefore identifies this square
with the stated original terminal projection at EVERY point, including
all exceptional and centre points. Every padding position is retained.
\end{proof}
