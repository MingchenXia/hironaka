\section{Padding-independent cocycles on a genuine common open}

Three retained local recipes can be compared on ANY actual open contained
in their three original base opens. Every map below is constructed from
the actual recipes; no arbitrary sharp endpoint is assigned centres.
A cocycle on that fixed common open is distinct from compatibility with
restricting a previously chosen pairwise-intersection map. The latter and
geometric manifold descent remain separate obligations.

\begin{lemma}[The actual common-open base maps compose]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-common-open-base-cocycle}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-overlap-original-data}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCommonOpenCocycle.commonOpenBase_trans}
\leanok
For three actual opens $U_1,U_2,U_3$ and a common open $O\subset U_i$,
let $\Phi_{ij}:A_i\to A_j$ be the genuine common-open base maps between
the nested presentations of $O$. Then
$\Phi_{23}\circ\Phi_{12}=\Phi_{13}$ as genuine biholomorphisms.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each map is the actual nested-open biholomorphism to $O$ followed by the
inverse nested-open biholomorphism for the other chart. The real inverse
laws cancel the two maps for $U_2$. Equivalently, at every actual nested
point both composite and direct map have the same underlying point of $X$;
their subtype membership proofs express the same genuine point. Thus their
forward functions agree, and genuine biholomorphism extensionality gives
the claimed equality. No base cocycle is supplied as a hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[True finite comparisons compose independently of their padding cuts]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-common-cut-padding-cocycle}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-terminal-recipe-padding-independence,hir:sharp-actual-stagewise-tower-comparison-unique,mc:tower-isomorphism-basic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCommonOpenCocycle.paddedHistory,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCommonOpenCocycle.terminal_padding_comparison_cocycle}
\leanok
Fix three actual prefixes and tails, with genuine base biholomorphisms
$\Phi_{12},\Phi_{23}$. Suppose real stagewise comparisons of their actual
identity-empty-padded full histories are given for pairs $12,23,13$,
where the third base map is the literal composite. The three common tail
cuts may differ and each bounds both of its actual tail lengths. Then the
actual terminal maps satisfy $e_{23}\circ e_{12}=e_{13}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If the three cuts are $L_{12},L_{23},L_{13}$, take their genuine common size
$N=L_{12}+L_{23}+L_{13}$. Extend the first comparison by the actual empty
identity towers of length $L_{23}+L_{13}$, the second by length
$L_{12}+L_{13}$, and the third by length $L_{12}+L_{23}$. True empty-tail
concatenation and natural-number subtraction identify all three extended
comparisons with the same fixed histories padded to $N$. Their terminal
maps have not changed. Actual stagewise composition gives a genuine
comparison of the first and third fixed histories over the literal composite
base map. Actual fixed-history comparison uniqueness identifies its terminal
biholomorphism with the independently given third map. No empty position
has been deleted and no canonicality or compressed history is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Retained original local recipes construct the common-open cocycle]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-retained-common-open-cocycle}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-common-open-base-cocycle,hir:sharp-actual-curve-common-cut-padding-cocycle,hir:sharp-actual-curve-overlap-terminal-recipes}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCommonOpenCocycle.exists_actual_common_open_recipe_cocycle}
\leanok
Let the original positive-dimensional ambient manifold have a closed smooth
pure curve $W$, original ordered SNC family $E$, and density of the complement
of that whole family in $W$. On each of three actual base opens choose an
actual computed-FIRST blowup followed by a literal terminating difference
recipe. For ANY genuine common open $O$ and ANY three pairwise common cuts,
construct the real restricted padded pair comparisons independently. Their
actual terminal maps satisfy the cocycle, with all original and empty positions
retained. No matching centres, source TESTs, endpoint maps or cocycle certificate
are additional inputs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the genuine arbitrary-common-open overlap producer separately to the
three pairs, using the same actual $O$ and their respective cuts. It internally
constructs all restricted original data and TESTs, independently computed
centre matches, literal restricted recipes, exact retained lengths, terminal
difference emptiness and genuine padding. Thus each pair has its unique
actual full stagewise comparison on the stated real histories. Rewrite only
the actual base composition by the first lemma. The preceding theorem applies
to these exact three comparisons, even though their padding cuts differ,
and proves the terminal-map cocycle. All three comparison maps were produced
from the original recipes. This assertion holds in particular after choosing
$O=U_1\cap U_2\cap U_3$. It does not yet identify these independently constructed
common-open maps with restrictions of pairwise-intersection maps; that genuine
restriction-and-flattening comparison remains necessary before geometric
descent.
\end{proof}
