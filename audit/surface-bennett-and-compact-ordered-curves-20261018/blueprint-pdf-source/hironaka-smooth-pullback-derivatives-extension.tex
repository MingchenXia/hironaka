\section{Actual derivative ideals under maps of different dimensions}

We prove the genuine chain-rule inclusion on entire holomorphic germ ideals for
arbitrary source and target dimensions. This is a necessary first derivative
layer of smooth functoriality. The converse under a submersion, its coefficient
and homogenization consumers, and the selected canonical tower remain open.

\begin{lemma}\label{hir:crossdim-chart-map}
Let $f:Y^m\to X^n$ be holomorphic and let $e'$ and $e$ be actual atlas charts
at $y$ and $f(y)$. The real chart map $T=e\circ f\circ(e')^{-1}$ is holomorphic
on a neighbourhood of $e'(y)$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothPullbackDerivatives.eventually_differentiableAt_chartMap}
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual chart inverse is continuous and holomorphic near $e'(y)$ and sends
that point to $y$. The original holomorphic map sends $y$ to $f(y)$.
Continuity gives a neighbourhood contained in the true source-chart target
and whose image under $f\circ(e')^{-1}$ stays in the true target-chart source.
On that neighbourhood, compose the two holomorphic atlas maps with the original
map. The ordinary differentiability characterization in the model vector
spaces proves the claimed holomorphicity, with no same-dimension hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}\label{hir:crossdim-chain-coeff}
For $v\in\mathbb C^m$ and $j\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$, set
$c_j(p)=(DT(e'(p))v)_j$ using the actual original chart map.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothPullbackDerivatives.chainCoeff}
\leanok
\uses{hir:crossdim-chart-map}
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
This formula uses the actual Frechet derivative of the genuine map between
the original model spaces and coordinate evaluation in its target. Its source
and target dimensions are independent; no matrix or transport certificate is input.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{hir:crossdim-chain-holomorphic}
Every actual coefficient $c_j$ is a holomorphic germ at $y$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothPullbackDerivatives.holomorphicAt_chainCoeff}
\leanok
\uses{hir:crossdim-chart-map,hir:crossdim-chain-coeff}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose a genuine open neighbourhood on which $T$ is holomorphic, using the
preceding lemma. The holomorphic derivative theorem makes $z\mapsto DT(z)v$
holomorphic there. Compose with complex linear coordinate evaluation in
$\mathbb C^n$ and the original source atlas chart. This proves actual scalar
germ holomorphicity, including an empty target coordinate family when $n=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{hir:crossdim-chain-rule}
For every original holomorphic germ $a$ at $f(y)$ and $v\in\mathbb C^m$,
near $y$ one has
$\partial^{e'}_v(a\circ f)=\sum_{j=1}^n c_j\, (\partial^e_j a)\circ f$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothPullbackDerivatives.chartDeriv_comp_eventuallyEq_sum}
\leanok
\uses{hir:crossdim-chart-map,hir:crossdim-chain-coeff}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On one true neighbourhood all original charts and the original germ are
holomorphic and their arguments stay in the actual chart domains.
The germ $(a\circ f)\circ(e')^{-1}$ equals
$(a\circ e^{-1})\circ T$ there, by the actual inverse identity of $e$.
Apply the genuine Frechet chain rule. Expand the true vector $DT(e'(p))v$
in the standard finite coordinate basis of $\mathbb C^n$ and use complex
linearity of the derivative of $a\circ e^{-1}$. The resulting finite sum
is exactly the stated formula for the original chart derivatives.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{hir:crossdim-pulled-derivative-generator}
Every actual member $a$ of the derivative ideal $\mathcal D(I)_{f(y)}$
pulls back to a genuine germ of $f^*\mathcal D(I)$ at $y$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothPullbackDerivatives.isInSpanAt_comp_of_mem_derivIdeal}
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Derivative-ideal membership itself gives holomorphicity of $a$ at the original
point. Compose with the actual holomorphic map. The defining genuine pullback
family contains $a\circ f$ with coefficient one, which supplies the required
span relation. No generator equality is an input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:crossdim-derivative-inclusion}
For any actual ideal $I$ of holomorphic germs,
$\mathcal D(f^*I)\subseteq f^*\mathcal D(I)$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothPullbackDerivatives.derivIdeal_pullback_le}
\leanok
\uses{hir:crossdim-chain-holomorphic,hir:crossdim-chain-rule,hir:crossdim-pulled-derivative-generator}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original pullback ideal is the span of the actual functions $a\circ f$
with $a\in I$. Those original germs already belong to $\mathcal D(I)$, so
all germs of $f^*I$ satisfy the inclusion. For a chart derivative of an
arbitrary actual pullback member, write that member as a real finite
holomorphic combination of those generators. The actual product rule
separates derivatives of coefficients and derivatives of pulled-back
original generators. The first terms use $I\subseteq\mathcal D(I)$.
For the second terms apply the preceding actual chain rule, whose genuine
coefficients are holomorphic. Each original chart derivative of $a$ is
an actual member of $\mathcal D(I)$. Actual finite-sum, product and germ
locality properties conclude the inclusion on the whole ideal.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}\label{hir:crossdim-iterated-inclusion}
For every natural $r$, $\mathcal D^r(f^*I)\subseteq f^*\mathcal D^r(I)$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothPullbackDerivatives.iterDerivIdeal_pullback_le}
\leanok
\uses{hir:crossdim-derivative-inclusion}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on $r$. At zero both sides are the literal original pullback ideal.
For the successor, derivative monotonicity applies to the actual induction
inclusion. Apply the preceding one-step theorem to the real original
iterated derivative ideal, whose members are holomorphic by the actual
derivative-ideal construction. Compose these inclusions. No higher
invariant or canonical functoriality identity is assumed.
\end{proof}
