\section{Actual power lifts give the entire compatible normal filtration}

For a commutative algebra $B\to R$ and actual original ideals $I,J$, write
$L_q=\operatorname{im}(J^q\to R/(I+J^{q+1}))$ and
$D=\bigoplus_{q\geq0}L_q$. Suppose these genuine original layers are free
over $B$. Use their internally constructed power lifts
$\lambda_q:L_q\to J^q$. Put
\[
 \Lambda(v)=\sum_q\lambda_q(v_q),\quad
 T_s=R/(I+J^s),\quad \Gamma_s(v)=[\Lambda(v)].
\]
Every sum has finite support. The original coherent-ideal consumer below
obtains these free layers internally on the same actual nearby open.

\begin{definition}[The true whole representative and every normal truncation map]
\label{hir:relative-normal-actual-whole-power-lift-truncation-map}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-actual-free-layer-power-lift}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualWholePowerLift,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationMap,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualWholePowerLift_lof,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationMap_lof}
\leanok
The preceding original power representatives define a genuine $B$-linear
whole map $\Lambda:D\to R$ and, for every $s$, a genuine $B$-linear
map $\Gamma_s:D\to T_s$. The degree-$q$ image is the same original
numerator $\lambda_q(x)$ followed by the actual quotient map.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The $q$th whole power lift followed by its original power-submodule
inclusion is $B$-linear. The finite-support direct-sum universal property
therefore constructs one whole linear map whose restriction in every
degree is this composite. The actual whole quotient map to $T_s$ is
$B$-linear. Composing it with the same whole map gives $\Gamma_s$ and
the asserted original numerator formula in every degree.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Every actual power representative has a preimage in the correct tail]
\label{hir:relative-normal-actual-power-lift-truncation-tail-surjectivity}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-actual-whole-power-lift-truncation-map,hir:relative-normal-actual-free-layer-power-lift-numerators}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationMap_power_preimage,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationMap_power_preimage_any,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationMap_surjective}
\leanok
For every $r,s$ and every actual original $g\in J^r$, its class in
$T_s$ is $\Gamma_s(v)$ for some $v\in D$ with $v_q=0$ for all $q<r$.
In particular every $\Gamma_s$ is surjective.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $r\geq s$, then $J^r\subseteq J^s$, so the actual class of $g$ is
zero and $v=0$ works. For $r<s$, put $x_r=p_r(g)$ in the true original
layer. The lift identity gives
$g-\lambda_r(x_r)\in I+J^{r+1}$. Write this difference as
$i_r+g_{r+1}$ with $i_r\in I$ and $g_{r+1}\in J^{r+1}$. Thus its
actual class in $T_s$ is the sum of the class of $\lambda_r(x_r)$
and the class of $g_{r+1}$. Repeat the construction at $r+1$; induction
on $s-r$ terminates at degree $s$, whose whole power is killed in
$T_s$. The resulting finite sum of degree inclusions has all degrees
below $r$ zero and maps to the original class of $g$. For unrestricted
surjectivity take $r=0$, when the actual original power is all of $R$,
and use surjectivity of the original quotient map.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The full kernel is exactly the original high-degree tail]
\label{hir:relative-normal-actual-power-lift-truncation-whole-kernel}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-actual-power-lift-truncation-tail-surjectivity,hir:relative-normal-actual-free-layer-power-lift-numerators}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualWholePowerLift_mem_power,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationMap_eq_zero_iff,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationMap_ker}
\leanok
For every $s$,
\[
 \Gamma_s(v)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 v_q=0\text{ for every }q<s.
\]
Moreover $\Lambda(v)\in J^r$ whenever $v_q=0$ for every $q<r$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In the last assertion each nonzero summand of the finite representative
sum has degree at least $r$ and lies in its actual power $J^q\subseteq
J^r$. The zero lower-degree summands also lie in $J^r$, so closure
under finite sums proves the assertion. If every degree below $s$
vanishes, apply it with $r=s$ to prove $\Gamma_s(v)=0$.

Conversely, suppose $\Lambda(v)\in I+J^s$. Prove $v_q=0$ for $q<s$
by induction on $q$. Assume every lower degree is zero and subtract
the degree-$q$ inclusion $\iota_q(v_q)$ from $v$. This remaining vector
has every degree below $q+1$ zero, so its true representative lies in
$J^{q+1}$. Since $q<s$, $J^s\subseteq J^{q+1}$. Linearity therefore
gives $\lambda_q(v_q)\in I+J^{q+1}$. Its true layer class is zero,
while the power-lift identity identifies that same class with $v_q$.
Hence $v_q=0$, completing the induction and the full kernel identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual maps preserve the entire normal filtration and every truncation comparison]
\label{hir:relative-normal-actual-power-lift-truncation-compatible-filtration}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-actual-power-lift-truncation-whole-kernel}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTail,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalPowerImage,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationMap_tail_image,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationComparison,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationMap_comparison,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationQuotientEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualCompatibleTruncations.actualNormalTruncationQuotientEquiv_mk}
\leanok
Let $D^{\geq r}$ be the original direct-sum tail and let $F_r(s)$ be
the actual image of $J^r$ in $T_s$. Then
$\Gamma_s(D^{\geq r})=F_r(s)$ for every $r,s$. For $s\leq t$, the
true original quotient map $T_t\to T_s$ composed with $\Gamma_t$ is
$\Gamma_s$. In particular $D/D^{\geq s}\simeq_B T_s$, retaining the
whole original normal filtration.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding whole representative containment gives one inclusion
in the image identity. The actual power-tail preimage theorem gives
the reverse inclusion for every actual representative of $F_r(s)$.
The comparison quotient sends the class of every original numerator
to its same class. Thus it sends each degree image of $\Gamma_t$
to the same degree image of $\Gamma_s$. Equality on every direct-sum
degree proves equality of the whole maps. Their previously proved
surjectivity and full kernel identity give the quotient isomorphism.
The image identity proves that this same isomorphism preserves all
original normal filtration levels.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Only original coherence gives the entire actual compatible filtration on one nearby open]
\label{hir:relative-normal-original-nearby-compatible-power-truncations}

\leanok
Original coherence of the ideal on $\mathbb C^{d+t}$ gives one genuine
base open meeting every origin neighborhood, the original uniform
degree ranks, and, at every actual plane point in that open, all the
preceding compatible original truncation maps, whole kernels and
filtration identities. No power lift, filtration splitting or desired
truncation dictionary is a caller premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original-only all-power-lift producer supplies one true open and
all actual original degree modules with their finite freeness and
uniform ranks. Apply the preceding constructions at each original
plane point to its same whole translated original ideal and actual
normal-coordinate ideal. Their proofs apply simultaneously to all
orders using the same internally selected degree lifts. Retain the
previous original open and rank data. This gives every stated map
and identity from the original coherent ideal alone.
\end{proof}
