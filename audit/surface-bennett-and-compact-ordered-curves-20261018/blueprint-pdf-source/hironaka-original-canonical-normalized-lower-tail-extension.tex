\section{Actual canonical normalization of the original crossing lower tail}

The literal original crossing lower data are retained at every recursive
dimension.  Normalizing a restriction of a concatenation is a real tower
isomorphism, whose complete original canonical history and values must be
transported before the positive-order recursive branch can use them.
The proof uses the genuine sequence-form canonical invariant; it does not
replace its local finiteness by a uniform noncompact bound.  This supplies
explicit original-history bookkeeping in W\l odarczyk's Step One and the
trivial-isomorphism convention of Bierstone--Milman
\cite{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}.

\begin{theorem}[Original canonical input supplies the normalized whole lower history]
\label{hir:original-canonical-crossing-normalized-lower-data}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalCanonicalNormalizedLowerTail.original_canonical_crossing_normalized_lower_data}
\leanok
\uses{hir:original-canonical-crossing-lower-data,hir:tower-isomorphism,mc:tower-isomorphism-restrict-append}
Let $M$ be in the original dimension-$(k+1)$ domain and of actual maximal
order.  Let $HS$ be its genuine specific-family Step One canonical
history and $y$ an original nonspecial transformed-cosupport point whose
actual current-level run $r$ reaches $S$.  Retain the true crossing
assignment $K=\operatorname{head}(H,r-|S|)$,
$B=\operatorname{tail}(H,r-|S|)$, $p=(BS).\operatorname{proj}(y)$,
$W=\operatorname{lowerMask}(M,\ell,K,p)$ and
$J=\operatorname{lowerMarkedM}(M,\ell,K,p)$.
These original data internally supply $p\in W$, the true dimension-$k$
domain of $J$, BOTH the canonical whole old restricted history $(BS)|_W$
and the canonical whole normalized history
$B|_W\,S|_{B^{-1}W}$, the corresponding actual cosupport points, and
equality of their actual full $R_k$ values at the same underlying $y$.
This applies to every actual lower nonmonomial order, including its
positive branch, and to an empty or nonempty $S$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original canonical crossing lower-data producer internally supplies
the genuine mask member, lower domain, entire old canonical restricted
tail and real cosupport point.  The true restriction-of-concatenation
isomorphism identifies this old restricted tail with the stated
normalized history.  Apply the actual sequence-form canonical tower-isomorphism theorem to its inverse,
whose base and final identifications are the actual identity maps on
the corresponding equal opens.  The genuine coherent identity-pullback
dictionary identifies its initial marked ideal with the same actual $J$;
thus it gives canonicity of the entire normalized history.  The actual
old canonical tail is a test history.  Its genuine transform cosupport
dictionary across this same inverse isomorphism supplies the normalized
cosupport point.  The established actual-family tower-isomorphism value
property, applied to the true old canonical tail and that internally
supplied cosupport point, gives the full value equality at $y$, without
an order-zero branch assumption.  All lower geometry, canonical histories,
point identifications and values are produced from the original inputs.
\end{proof}

Canonicity of a history with an additional interior empty step, its
positive-order recursive value transport, the outer general companion
history and noncompact scheduling remain further obligations.  Normalizing
the existing actual canonical history does not by itself prove those claims.
