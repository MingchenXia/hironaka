\section{Original intrinsic HS for hypersurface centres and ambient surfaces}

\begin{theorem}[Every original hypersurface-centre blowup preserves all intrinsic HS values at the centre]
\label{hir:actual-hypersurface-centre-blowup-intrinsic-hs-equality}
\uses{hir:actual-smooth-centre-blowup-coordinate-normal-form,hir:actual-normal-blowup-codimension-one-identity,hir:actual-open-coordinate-geometric-strict-square,hir:actual-flat-coordinate-inverse-any-subset-intrinsic-hs,hir:actual-any-subset-open-intrinsic-hs}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHypersurfaceCentreBennett.hilbertSamuelAt_actual_hypersurface_centre_blowup_eq}
\leanok
Let $\pi:Y\to X$ satisfy the original full blowup predicate in complex
ambient dimension $d+1$. Suppose its same original centre is regular
of dimension $d$ at $\pi(z)$. For every original subset $W\subset X$
and every order $\ell$, its original geometric strict transform has
unchanged intrinsic Hilbert--Samuel value:
\[
 H_{\mathcal I_{\operatorname{strict}_{\pi}(W)},z}(\ell)
 =H_{\mathcal I_W,\pi(z)}(\ell).
\]
Only the original blowup and actual centre regularity are hypotheses;
no coordinate normal form or numerical certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine smooth-centre coordinate theorem constructs centred local
holomorphic equivalences $\Phi$ at $\pi(z)$ and $\Theta$ at $z$, an
actual pivot $j\ge d$, and the entire commuting polynomial square for
the same original blowup and same centre. Since $j<d+1$, necessarily
$j=d$. The normal set $\{i\mid d\le i<d+1\}$ is exactly the one-element
last normal block. The actual polynomial normal map is therefore the
codimension-one model, which is the literal identity, in every tangent
dimension and for every parameter. Its exceptional locus is empty;
its original strict transform of an arbitrary subset $A$ is its closure.
The original vanishing germ ideal of a set equals that of its closure,
so all its unchanged intrinsic HS values at zero agree.

Let $U=\Theta.target$ and $V=\Phi.target$ be the actual open coordinate
targets. The entire original coordinate square places the model map of
$U$ inside $V$ and gives its genuine holomorphic map $Q:U\to V$.
The inverse coordinates identify $U$ and $V$ biholomorphically with
the actual original source and target coordinate domains. For the
subset $A$ of the model obtained by imaging the original inverse-target
pullback of $W$, injectivity of the open inclusion identifies its whole
pullback to $V$ with that same original inverse-coordinate pullback.
Apply the original geometric strict-transform square first to the actual
blowup and these inverse coordinates, then to the actual polynomial map
and its true open restrictions. Equality of the original differential
exceptional sets and open-map closure pullback in these squares gives
exact equality of the two whole strict-transform pullbacks on $U$.

The genuine original intrinsic HS transports under both inverse
coordinate equivalences and both actual open inclusions apply to any
subset. They identify the original strict-transform value at $z$ with
the model strict-transform value at zero, and the value of $W$ at
$\pi(z)$ with the model value of $A$ at zero. The model equality already
proved between these middle values yields the asserted equality at
every order. The original centre, map, subsets and whole vanishing
ideals are retained throughout.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original all-point Bennett inequality on every ambient complex surface]
\label{hir:actual-ambient-surface-blowup-bennett}
\uses{hir:actual-hypersurface-centre-blowup-intrinsic-hs-equality,hir:actual-manifold-point-centre-bennett-all-orders,hir:actual-smooth-centre-blowup-coordinate-normal-form,hir:actual-blowup-off-centre-intrinsic-reduced-hs}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualHypersurfaceCentreBennett.hilbertSamuelAt_actual_surface_blowup_le}
\leanok
Let $X$ and $Y$ have complex dimension two and let $\pi:Y\to X$
satisfy the original full blowup predicate. For every original analytic
subset $W\subset X$, every $z\in Y$ and every order $\ell$,
\[
 H_{\mathcal I_{\operatorname{strict}_{\pi}(W)},z}(\ell)
 \le H_{\mathcal I_W,\pi(z)}(\ell).
\]
No compatibility, selected standard basis, HS comparison, or supplied
restriction dictionary is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Off the original centre, the genuine original complement
biholomorphism gives equality of every unchanged intrinsic HS value.
At a point of the centre, the original full blowup predicate itself
supplies original centre regularity of some dimension $k$. The genuine
smooth-centre coordinate producer supplies an actual pivot $j$ with
$k\le j<2$. Hence $k=0$ or $k=1$; a full-dimensional centre at this
point cannot occur. In dimension zero, apply the original point-centre
Bennett theorem, which has no compatibility or numerical hypotheses.
In dimension one, the preceding original hypersurface-centre equality
applies with $d=1$ and implies the required inequality. These cases
exhaust every original point and establish every order.
\end{proof}

This closes the original Bennett inequality in ambient dimension two
and the original equality above hypersurface centres in every ambient
dimension. For higher-dimensional ambient manifolds with positive
centre dimension and normal codimension at least two, genuine
compatible-family selection and the general Bennett comparison remain
separate mathematical requirements.
