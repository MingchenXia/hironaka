\section{Genuine entire analytic normal powers and exact formal contraction}

The literal relative coefficient dictionary now allows genuine membership in
entire analytic normal powers to be recovered from the actual formal Taylor
series. The statement concerns the original coordinate normal ideals, not
faithful flatness for an arbitrary ideal or convergence of prescribed series.

\begin{theorem}[The entire original normal power is the actual analytic coefficient kernel]
\label{hir:actual-entire-analytic-normal-power-relative-coefficient-kernel}
\uses{hir:actual-relative-coefficient-polynomial-truncations,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-germ-stability}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticFormalNormalPowers.mem_normalIdeal_pow_iff_actual_relative_coefficients_zero}
\leanok
For every genuine holomorphic germ $g$, every actual normal degree bound $q$,
and both coordinate blocks, $g$ belongs to the entire original normal ideal
power $N^q$ if and only if all actual stable analytic relative coefficient
germs of normal degree less than $q$ vanish in the whole tangential germ ring.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $P_q(g)$ be the independently constructed original relative quotient
polynomial, with actual remainder $g-P_q(g)$ in $N^q$. If $g\in N^q$, then
the genuine evaluation of $P_q(g)$ also belongs to $N^q$. The original whole
polynomial coefficient separation annihilates every polynomial coefficient
below degree $q$. Each such coefficient is the actual stable analytic
coefficient germ. Conversely, if every actual low coefficient germ vanishes,
every low coefficient of $P_q(g)$ vanishes by its stabilization identity.
Every higher coefficient vanishes by construction of the genuine quotient
polynomial. Thus the entire polynomial is zero. Its genuine whole remainder
then proves $g\in N^q$. This also includes $q=0$ and empty normal or tangent
coordinate blocks.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every genuine formal normal power contracts to exactly the original analytic power]
\label{hir:actual-formal-normal-power-contracts-exact-original-analytic-power}
\uses{hir:actual-entire-analytic-normal-power-relative-coefficient-kernel,hir:actual-relative-coefficient-literal-formal-taylor-dictionary,hir:actual-original-formal-taylor-algebra-embedding}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticFormalNormalPowers.actualUnshiftedTaylorAlgHom_injective,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticFormalNormalPowers.mem_normalIdeal_pow_iff_actualTaylor_mem_formal_normal_pow,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticFormalNormalPowers.comap_actual_formal_normal_pow_eq_original_normal_pow}
\leanok
Under the independently defined genuine original Taylor algebra embedding,
the inverse image of every actual formal normal ideal power is exactly the
entire original analytic normal ideal power. Equivalently, membership of the
Taylor series of an arbitrary actual analytic germ in the formal normal power
is equivalent to genuine membership of that same analytic germ in $N^q$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true Taylor coordinate dictionary identifies the image of the original
normal ideal with the actual formal normal coordinate ideal. The actual
ideal-map power identity sends every original member of $N^q$ into its formal
power. In the reverse direction, genuine formal normal-power membership
annihilates every formal coefficient of normal degree less than $q$,
regardless of the tangent degree. Thus each entire formal normal coefficient
series below degree $q$ is zero. The literal relative Taylor dictionary
identifies it with the genuine tangential Taylor series of the actual analytic
relative coefficient germ. The original Taylor map is injective on the entire
original germ ring, including after the genuine dimension reassociation
$0+d=d$; hence each such actual coefficient germ is zero. Apply the preceding
whole analytic coefficient-kernel criterion. Equality of the two entire ideals
follows by their membership criterion. No existence of an analytic preimage
for any arbitrary formal series is asserted.
\end{proof}
