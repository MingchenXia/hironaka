\begin{lemma}[An actual endpoint biholomorphism transports the whole conventional strict transform]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-finite-endpoint-biholomorphic-strict-identity}
\uses{ana:strict-transform-composition,hir:biholomorphism-invariance}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactAdmissibleHistory.strictTransform_biholomorphic_source}
\leanok
For a genuine endpoint biholomorphism and a holomorphic projection from its
target to the original ambient, the conventional strict transform under
the literal composite equals the ENTIRE inverse image of the target's
conventional strict transform. This applies to every original subset,
without a supplied strict-set comparison certificate.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The proved actual inverse laws and holomorphic maps show that the
biholomorphism has empty differential exceptional locus. The genuine
strict-transform composition theorem writes the composite's strict
transform as the biholomorphism's strict transform of the target strict
transform. That target strict transform is closed by its actual closure
definition. With empty exceptional locus, the latter is closure of its
actual inverse image; continuity makes that inverse image closed. Its
closure therefore equals the same entire inverse image. No arbitrary
projection uniqueness or omitted exceptional points enter this identity.
\end{proof}
