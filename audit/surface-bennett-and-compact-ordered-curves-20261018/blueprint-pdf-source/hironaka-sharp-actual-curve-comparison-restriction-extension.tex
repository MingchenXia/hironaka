\section{Genuine restriction of comparison histories and empty padding}

The common-open cocycle does not by itself identify a direct common-open map
with the restriction of a previously constructed pairwise map. The actual
geometric restriction operations below supply the first needed comparison.
No canonical compression or deletion of empty steps is used.

\begin{lemma}[Actual restrictions of a biholomorphism to matching opens]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-comparison-open-biholomorphism}
\uses{mc:tower-isomorphism-basic,bcalc:restrict-nested}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveComparisonRestriction.preimageOpen,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveComparisonRestriction.openRestriction,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveComparisonRestriction.openRestriction_val}
\leanok
For a genuine biholomorphism $\Phi:X'\to X$ and actual opens $U'\subset X'$,
$U\subset X$ with $U'=\Phi^{-1}U$, construct a genuine restricted
biholomorphism $U'\to U$ whose underlying map is exactly $\Phi$.
Its inverse is the actual restriction of $\Phi^{-1}$. In particular the
source open can be constructed internally as the genuine inverse image of $U$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Send an actual source subtype point $x$ to $\Phi(x)$ with its target membership
supplied by the actual inverse-image equality. Send a target subtype point
$y$ to $\Phi^{-1}(y)$; applying the real inverse equation gives its source
membership. The two genuine inverse laws identify both subtype composites
with their identities. Compose the actual holomorphic maps with open
inclusions and use the genuine open-subtype holomorphic criterion for both
directions. This constructs the claimed biholomorphism and exact underlying
point maps without extending any germ or choosing a new ambient map.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual stagewise comparisons restrict to every genuine base open]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-stagewise-comparison-open-restriction}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-comparison-open-biholomorphism,mc:tower-isomorphism-basic,mc:restrict-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveComparisonRestriction.openPreimage_eq_preimage,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveComparisonRestriction.IsTowerIso.open_restriction}
\leanok
Given an actual stagewise isomorphism of two finite towers over $\Phi$ and
any genuine target base open $U$, restrict the target history to $U$ and
the source history to the actual inverse-image open $\Phi^{-1}U$.
Construct an actual stagewise isomorphism of those exact restricted histories.
The new base map is the genuine restricted $\Phi$, and the terminal map is
exactly the restriction of the original terminal biholomorphism. Every actual
successor, including every centre with empty trace, remains in the history.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the genuine stagewise isomorphism. For reflexive towers the preceding
open biholomorphism is exactly the required reflexive comparison. At a
successor, the induction hypothesis constructs the genuine previous restricted
comparison with its exact underlying original point map. The full projection
commuting equation identifies the actual terminal source open with the
inverse image of the actual terminal target open under the given next
biholomorphism. Construct its genuine restriction by the preceding lemma.

Both original arrows restrict to actual blowups of the traces of their
centres. The true old centre-preimage law and the exact underlying previous
restricted map identify these two trace centres in both directions.
The real old blowup square gives the next restricted square by subtype
extensionality. Extend the genuine restricted stagewise isomorphism with
these actual blowup proofs, centre law and next biholomorphism. Its underlying
terminal map is the given original one by construction. No centre or
restriction compatibility certificate is a new premise, and an empty trace
is retained as a genuine blowup step.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Restricting genuine identity empty tails preserves every actual padding position]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding-open-restriction}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-comparison-open-biholomorphism,hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding,mc:restrict-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveComparisonRestriction.identityEmptyTail_openPreimage,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveComparisonRestriction.identityEmptyRestriction,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveComparisonRestriction.identityEmptyTail_open_restriction,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveComparisonRestriction.identityEmptyRestriction_val}
\leanok
For an actual open $U\subset Y$ and every $r$, the actual restriction to $U$
of the genuine $r$-step identity empty-centre tower on $Y$ is stagewise
isomorphic over the identity of $U$ to the genuine $r$-step identity
empty-centre tower on $U$. The terminal map is the identity on underlying
points. No step is deleted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual composite projection of the identity empty tail is the identity,
so its actual inverse-image terminal open has exactly the points of $U$.
The equality of these opens gives the genuine endpoint biholomorphism by the
preceding open construction. Induct on $r$. The zero case is the actual
reflexive comparison. At a successor, the restricted original arrow is the
actual restriction of the identity blowup of the empty centre. Its source
and target opens have the same underlying points, its map is the true
identity on those points, and the trace centre is genuinely empty.
The target padding arrow is the actual identity blowup of the empty centre
on $U$. The two empty centres match, and both squares commute on every
actual point. Extend the preceding stagewise comparison by this actual
successor. The exact endpoint point map and length $r+1$ are retained.
This proves genuine padding transport; it does not identify arbitrary
ambient ideal representatives after deleting their normalizations.
\end{proof}
