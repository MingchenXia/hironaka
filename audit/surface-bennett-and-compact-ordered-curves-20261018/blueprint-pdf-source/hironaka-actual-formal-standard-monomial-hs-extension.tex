\section{Genuine standard monomials compute the actual formal and original HS functions}

Let $S=\mathbb C[[X_1,\ldots,X_n]]$ with its GENUINE complex action,
let $I\subset S$ be ANY actual ideal, and let $\mathcal N(I)$ be its
internally constructed actual degree-lexicographic initial diagram.
Write $\mathfrak n=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$. The following supplies the genuine
finite quotient bases underlying the diagram Hilbert--Samuel formula in
\cite[\S7, Lemmas~7.5--7.7, pp.~261--264]{bierstone-milman97-invent-math}.
The finite diagram complement, basis, and quotient dimensions are all
constructed from the actual whole ideal, rather than supplied as premises.

\begin{lemma}[Actual diagram-avoiding bounded series are separated in the whole jet quotient]
\label{hir:actual-formal-standard-monomial-jet-separation}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-initial-exponent-coefficient-product,hir:actual-formal-ideal-initial-diagram-upper,hir:actual-formal-variable-power-coefficient-kernel}
Let $q\ge0$ and let $P\in S$ be an ACTUAL series whose every nonzero
coefficient exponent $a$ satisfies $|a|<q$ and $a\notin\mathcal N(I)$.
If $P\in I+\mathfrak n^q$, then $P=0$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actual_degree_le_of_deglex_lt,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.bounded_diagram_avoiding_series_eq_zero}
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $P=G+Z$ in the actual whole ideal sum, with $G\in I$ and
$Z\in\mathfrak n^q$. If $P\ne0$, take its actual lowest nonzero
coefficient exponent $a$. Its genuine support condition gives $|a|<q$
and $a\notin\mathcal N(I)$. The actual power kernel kills every
coefficient of $Z$ below degree $q$. Thus the genuine coefficient of
$G$ at $a$ is the SAME nonzero coefficient of $P$. For any exponent
$b\prec a$, degree-lex compatibility gives $|b|\le|a|<q$, so both
the coefficient of $P$ there and that of $Z$ vanish. Hence the actual
lowest exponent of the genuine nonzero $G$ is precisely $a$. But
$G\in I$ forces $a\in\mathcal N(I)$ by the actual definition of the
diagram, a contradiction. This includes $q=0$ and both extreme ideals.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual finite standard monomial map is a genuine complex linear bijection]
\label{hir:actual-formal-standard-monomial-whole-jet-basis}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-standard-monomial-jet-separation,hir:actual-formal-vertex-division-identity,hir:actual-formal-vertex-division-supports,hir:actual-formal-variable-power-coefficient-kernel}
Construct the actual finite set
$B_q(I)=\{a\in\mathbb N^n:|a|<q,\ a\notin\mathcal N(I)\}$.
The literal complex linear map
\[
 \mathbb C^{B_q(I)}\longrightarrow S/(I+\mathfrak n^q),\qquad
 (c_a)\longmapsto\left[\sum_{a\in B_q(I)}c_aX^a\right]
\]
is bijective, retaining the genuine existing complex scalar action on
the ACTUAL whole quotient.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardIndex,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardIndexFintype,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardLift,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardLift_coefficient,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardLift_coefficient_outside,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardLift_support,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardQuotientMap,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardQuotientMap_apply,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardQuotientMap_injective,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardQuotientMap_surjective,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.actualStandardQuotientLinearEquiv}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual bounded exponent set is finite; its actual diagram complement
therefore defines a true finite coordinate space. The genuine finite sum
of the actual monomials is complex linear, and the actual quotient map
is complex linear for the existing action.
If such a sum maps to zero, it belongs to the SAME actual whole
$I+\mathfrak n^q$. Its true coefficients are zero except at the
displayed distinct bounded diagram-complement exponents, and its
coefficient at each displayed exponent is precisely $c_a$. The preceding
actual separation lemma forces this entire series to be zero, and hence
every $c_a=0$. This proves injectivity.
For any actual formal numerator $F$, genuine formal division gives
$F=\sum Q_af_a+R$, with every actual $f_a\in I$ and every remainder
coefficient on $\mathcal N(I)$ zero. Form the ACTUAL finite sum of the
coefficients of $R$ at the exponents of $B_q(I)$. Its coefficients
agree with those of $R$ at EVERY exponent of degree below $q$: on the
diagram both are zero, and off the diagram the literal matching
monomial contributes the same coefficient. The genuine power kernel
therefore places their difference in $\mathfrak n^q$. The actual
whole finite sum $\sum Q_af_a$ belongs to $I$, so this finite standard
sum has exactly the SAME whole quotient class as $F$. Thus every actual
formal quotient class is reached. No quotient rank, independence,
spanning, or division certificate is a caller hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every genuine formal and existing original HS value is an actual diagram count]
\label{hir:actual-formal-and-original-hs-standard-monomial-count}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-standard-monomial-whole-jet-basis,hir:actual-original-formal-entire-hs-transfer}
For EVERY actual formal ideal $I\subset S$ and EVERY $\ell\ge0$,
\[
 H^{\mathrm{formal}}_I(\ell)
 =\#\{a\notin\mathcal N(I):|a|\le\ell\}.
\]
Consequently, for EVERY original holomorphic germ ideal $J\subset
\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^n,0}$, its EXISTING original HS value satisfies
\[
 H_J(\ell)=\#\{a\notin\mathcal N(S\mathcal T(J)):|a|\le\ell\},
\]
where $\mathcal T$ is the ACTUAL original Taylor complex algebra embedding.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.genuineFormalHilbertSamuelValue_eq_card_standardIndex,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalStandardMonomialHS.hilbertSamuelValue_eq_card_actual_formal_standardIndex}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding internally constructed true complex linear bijection gives
a genuine linear equivalence from the actual finite complex coordinate
space to the whole formal jet quotient. Its true dimension equality,
together with the actual dimension of a finite complex function space,
gives the first formula at $q=\ell+1$. The previously proved equality
of EVERY existing original HS value with that of the actual whole formal
extension gives the second formula for the SAME original $J$. No
scalar action, ideal, or numerator is replaced. These are diagram counts;
general strict Bennett comparison and transformed standard-basis
stability still require their separate genuine transformation proofs.
\end{proof}
