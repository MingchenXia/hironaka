\section{Genuine analytic and formal blowup saturation on the same chart}

Let $R_n$ be the original germ ring on $\mathbb C^n$ and $T_n:R_n\to S_n$
the existing unshifted Taylor map. Let $P$ be the genuine holomorphic
polynomial blowup chart map with its actual original germ pullback $P^*$,
and $\widehat P$ the independently defined formal blowup homomorphism.
The chosen exceptional coordinate is $u=z_e$, with $e$ in the centre set.
All statements use the actual existing origin-colon chain $C_q$.

\begin{theorem}[Whole total-transform ideals have the literal formal Taylor image]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-formal-blowup-whole-total-ideal}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticFormalBlowupSaturation.map_analyticBlowup_totalIdeal}
\leanok
For every entire original ideal $I\subseteq R_n$,
\[
 T_n(P^*(I)R_n)S_n=\widehat P(T_n(I)S_n)S_n.
\]
No generating family, formal comparison certificate or Taylor-square
hypothesis is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The previously proved genuine Taylor naturality identity is the equality
of the two entire algebra homomorphisms $T_n\circ P^*=\widehat P\circ T_n$.
Ideal extension along a composite is the composite of ideal extensions.
Applying that identity to the entire original ideal gives both ideal
inclusions simultaneously. The chart is the actual holomorphic polynomial
map and its independently defined formal substitution.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[One true analytic colon computes the entire formal blowup saturation]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-formal-blowup-whole-saturation}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-formal-blowup-whole-total-ideal,hir:actual-formal-extension-whole-colon-saturation}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticFormalBlowupSaturation.exists_actual_analytic_formal_blowup_saturation}
\leanok
For every original ideal $I$, every actual centre set and exceptional chart,
there is an internally chosen $N\geq0$ for which the actual analytic chain
$C_q(P^*I,u)$ is constant for all $q\geq N$ and, for every formal $F$,
\[
 (\exists q\geq0:\ F\in C_q(\widehat P(T_n(I)S_n),x_e))
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 F\in T_n(C_N(P^*I,u))S_n.
\]
This is equality of the entire formal saturation with extension of the
one true original analytic saturated ideal. Zero/unit ideals and arbitrary
formal $F$ are included. Geometric closure identification and compatibility
with a specified formal reduced vertex basis are not assumed or claimed here.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Reassociate the dimension equality $0+n=n$ in the already proved original
Taylor saturation theorem; this is exactly the definition of the existing
unshifted Taylor map, with no new map or action. Apply the whole saturation
theorem to the actual analytic ideal $P^*I$ and its actual exceptional germ
$u$. It internally supplies one original Noetherian index $N$, actual
analytic stability, and the entire formal membership equivalence for every
formal $F$. The preceding whole total-transform identity identifies the
formal starting ideal. The genuine coordinate Taylor identity sends the
actual exceptional germ $u$ to the formal variable $x_e$. Substituting these
two identities yields exactly the stated formal blowup saturation on the
same chart, with the same original analytic ideal and the same $N$.
No arbitrary formal coefficient is declared convergent.
\end{proof}
