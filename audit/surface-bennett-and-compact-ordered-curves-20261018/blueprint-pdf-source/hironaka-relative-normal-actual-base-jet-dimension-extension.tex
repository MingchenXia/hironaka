\section{Count the genuine original complex base jets}

For $B_d=\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^d,0}$ and its ACTUAL original maximal
ideal $\mathfrak m_d$, the required tangent weight at order $k+1$ has
complex dimension $\binom{d+k}{d}$. We count genuine distinct monomials,
not the full multilinear derivative codomain, whose redundant nonsymmetric
tensors would give an incorrect dimension.

\begin{lemma}[The actual zero-dimensional germ algebra is the original complex field]
\label{hir:relative-normal-actual-zero-base-complex-algebra}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.zeroBaseEval_injective,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.zeroBaseComplexEval,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.zeroBaseComplexAlgEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.zeroBaseMaximalIdeal_eq_bot,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.zeroTangentNormalIdeal_eq_maximalIdeal}
\leanok
Evaluation is a genuine complex-algebra isomorphism $B_0\simeq_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C$.
In particular its actual maximal ideal is zero and the actual normal ideal
$J_{0,d}$ in $B_d$ is precisely $\mathfrak m_d$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual model $\mathbb C^0$ has one point. Every genuine holomorphic
germ representative therefore agrees everywhere with its original constant
value. The original germ equality relation gives injectivity of evaluation.
Every complex constant has its actual constant germ, which gives surjectivity
and the original complex scalar compatibility. This constructs the stated
algebra isomorphism. Membership in the actual zero-dimensional maximal ideal
is exactly zero evaluation, hence that ideal is zero. Its whole image in
$B_d$ is zero. The original relative Hadamard identity
$\mathfrak m_d=\mathfrak m_0B_d+J_{0,d}$ proves the last assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

so these maps preserve the genuine complex action. Identify the same

\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual bounded original exponent families have the binomial cardinality]
\label{hir:relative-normal-actual-base-jet-exponent-binomial-count}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.optionMultisetCard,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.optionMultisetReconstruction,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.normalExponentDegree_eq_multisetCard,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.filterMap_none_replicate,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.normalExponentToSym,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.normalExponentFromSym,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.normalExponentFromSym_toSym,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.normalExponentToSym_fromSym,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.normalExponentSymEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaRelativeNormalActualBaseJetDimension.actualNormalExponentIndex_card}
\leanok
The genuine distinct exponent family $\{\alpha:|\alpha|\leq k\}$ has
cardinality $\binom{d+k}{d}$, including $d=0$ and $k=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An exponent is its actual multiplicity multiset on $\operatorname{Fin}d$.
Map that multiset into $\operatorname{Option}(\operatorname{Fin}d)$ using
$\mathrm{some}$ and fill its missing $k-|\alpha|$ entries with $\mathrm{none}$.
This gives a multiset of exactly $k$ entries on an alphabet of $d+1$ symbols.
Conversely discard the $\mathrm{none}$ entries and retain the actual
multiplicities of every $\mathrm{some}(i)$. The total retained cardinality
is at most $k$, giving a valid original exponent. Counting each original
symbol proves both inverse identities, including the slack symbol count
$k-|\alpha|$. This is a true finite equivalence with
$\operatorname{Sym}(\operatorname{Option}(\operatorname{Fin}d),k)$.
The genuine symmetric-power cardinality is $\binom{d+k}{k}$, equal to
$\binom{d+k}{d}$ by binomial symmetry. This proves the formula without
counting repeated normal words as distinct monomials.
\end{proof}
