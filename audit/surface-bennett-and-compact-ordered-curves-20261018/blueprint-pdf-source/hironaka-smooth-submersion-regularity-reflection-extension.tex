\section{Regularity of actual saturated cylinders}

This chapter proves the geometric reflection step for a genuine smooth
submersion.  Product coordinates are supplied by the original submersion;
no reflected-regularity or chosen-normal-equation certificate is an input.
The local product calculation is stated first and then consumed by those
actual charts.

\begin{lemma}[Actual vertical-preserving normal coordinates]
\label{hir:saturated-cylinder-vertical-preserving-coordinates}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRegularityReflection.exists_vertical_preserving_normal_coordinates}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{bcalc:snc-simultaneous}
Let $\Xi$ be a local holomorphic equivalence at the origin of
$\mathbb C^{n+t}$.  Suppose its inverse image of a specified coordinate
plane contains the vertical plane $\{0\}\times\mathbb C^t$ on its actual
source.  There are new local holomorphic coordinates at the origin, a
normal cutting index set $X$ contained in the first $n$ coordinates, and
an actual source contained in that of $\Xi$, in which the same normal
zero set is the coordinate subspace cut by $X$.  Every vertical coordinate
is retained literally.  The number of cutting indices is the original
normal codimension.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Apply the existing simultaneous model-coordinate construction with the
centre cutting indices equal to the first $n$ coordinates and with the
divisor family empty.  Containment of the vertical plane is exactly its
centre-containment premise.  That construction internally chooses
independent normal differential columns from those first $n$ coordinates,
then applies the holomorphic inverse function theorem.  It gives a normal
index set $X$ contained in this first block and a permutation $\sigma$.
Undo that permutation in the resulting coordinate values.  Its literal
unchanged-coordinate formula then says that every coordinate outside $X$
is retained, hence all vertical coordinates are retained.  The normal
plane dictionary becomes precisely vanishing on $X$.  The permutation
identifies $X$ with the old last normal block, giving the asserted cardinal.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[An actual horizontal slice of vertical-preserving coordinates]
\label{hir:saturated-cylinder-horizontal-slice-coordinates}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRegularityReflection.horizontalSliceCoordinates,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRegularityReflection.horizontalSliceCoordinates_zero}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:saturated-cylinder-vertical-preserving-coordinates}
A local holomorphic equivalence at the origin of $\mathbb C^{n+t}$
which retains every vertical coordinate restricts to a genuine local
holomorphic equivalence at the origin of $\mathbb C^n$.  Its source and
target are the literal preimages of the original source and target under
$u\mapsto(u,0)$; its maps are the first-block projections of the original
maps on that slice.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The source and target are open by continuity of the linear slice inclusion.
The forward map sends a horizontal input to a horizontal output because
all vertical coordinates are retained.  Conversely, the inverse of a
horizontal output is horizontal: applying the forward map to that inverse
shows that its retained vertical coordinates vanish.  The original inverse
identities therefore imply both slice inverse identities and both genuine
source-to-target maps.  Holomorphicity follows by composition with the
linear inclusion and projection.  The origin is retained, so the actual
slice source contains it.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Reflection of regularity of an actual cylinder]
\label{hir:saturated-cylinder-regularity-reflection}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRegularityReflection.regularAtDim_of_cylinder_flattening,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRegularityReflection.regularAtDim_cylinder_reflection}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:saturated-cylinder-horizontal-slice-coordinates}
Let $A\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ contain the origin.  If its actual cylinder
$A\times\mathbb C^t$, read in the fixed finite product coordinates, is
regular of dimension $j$ at the origin, then $t\leq j$ and $A$ is regular
of dimension $j-t$ at the origin.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take the actual flattening supplied by cylinder regularity.  The vertical
plane lies in its normal zero set, since $0\in A$.  The first lemma
supplies genuine vertical-preserving coordinates with normal cutting
indices $X$ in the first block, of cardinal $n+t-j$.  Restrict those
actual coordinates to the horizontal slice using the second lemma.
The cylinder dictionary on that slice says exactly that membership in
$A$ is vanishing on those same first-block indices.  Permute only this
first block to put its $|X|$ cutting indices last.  This is a genuine
flattening of $A$ of dimension $n-|X|$.  Because $|X|\leq n$, we have
$t\leq j$, and the displayed cardinal gives $n-|X|=j-t$.
\end{proof}

The subsequent original-manifold consumer composes these actual slice
coordinates with the actual lifted submersion and original preferred charts.
The cylinder calculation alone does not yet identify the zero-branch contact
choices or complete higher invariant values under a smooth map.


\begin{theorem}[Original regularity reflection under an arbitrary smooth map]
\label{hir:smooth-submersion-original-regularity-reflection}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRegularityReflection.regularAtDim_of_preimage_submersion}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:saturated-cylinder-regularity-reflection,hir:submersion-nc-full-lifted-coordinates}
Let $f:Y^m\to X^n$ be an actual holomorphic submersion at $y$, and let
$A\subseteq X$ be any original subset.  If the actual preimage $f^{-1}A$
is regular of dimension $j$ at $y$, then $m-n\leq j$ and the original
$A$ is regular of dimension $j-(m-n)$ at $f(y)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true submersion supplies $n\leq m$ and its actual lifted holomorphic
coordinates, centred at $y$, with first block equal to the actual centred
preferred coordinates of $f$.  Compose their genuine inverse with the
actual flattening of the whole source preimage.  Its source is the actual
intersection where both coordinate maps and both original preferred charts
apply.  On that source the normal plane dictionary identifies membership
with the literal cylinder over the original $A$ in the centred target
coordinates: the first-block equality and both chart inverse identities
prove this directly.  The source point belongs to the preimage, hence the
original target point belongs to $A$.  Apply the cylinder reflection
producer on this actual local source.  It supplies the fibre-dimension bound
and an actual target flattening of dimension $j-(m-n)$ in the centred target
coordinates.  Compose this flattening with the actual centred preferred
target coordinates.  The literal inverse identities give the original
membership dictionary, and its source lies in the preferred target chart.
This is precisely the original definition of target regularity.
\end{proof}


\begin{corollary}[Actual regular codimension-one pairs]
\label{hir:smooth-submersion-original-codimension-one-pairs}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionRegularityReflection.regular_codim_one_preimage_iff}
\leanok
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:smooth-submersion-original-regularity-reflection,hir:submersion-relative-regular-geometry}
For any original subsets $S,N$ and an actual holomorphic submersion at $y$,
the existence of regular dimensions $k+1,k$ for $f^{-1}N,f^{-1}S$ at $y$
is equivalent to the existence of such consecutive regular dimensions for
$N,S$ at $f(y)$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For reflection, apply the original reflection theorem to both subsets.
The regular dimension $k$ of the smaller source subset is at least the
fibre dimension $t$, so $(k+1)-t=(k-t)+1$.  This supplies the target pair.
Conversely, the original forward regularity producer adds the same actual
fibre dimension to both original dimensions, retaining their difference
of one.  No supplied choice of target regular dimension or coordinates
is needed in either direction.
\end{proof}
