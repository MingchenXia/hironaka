\section{Actual finite terminal smooth maps commute with independent history choices}

The finite smooth functor of \cite{bierstone-milman08-functoriality} must be
compatible with independent canonical terminal histories on both source
and target. Keep the original given histories, including different real
empty-step counts. Construct their actual comparisons internally from
original canonicality and terminality; prove whole-map naturality from
true universal properties. This supplies a finite gluing prerequisite,
not original noncompact resolution existence or a global schedule.

\begin{lemma}[Actual biholomorphic whole projection comparisons transport a full pullback square]
\label{hir:smooth-terminal-full-square-biholomorphic-transport}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionOriginalTerminalChoiceNaturality.holomorphicSquarePullback_biholomorphic_transport,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionOriginalTerminalChoiceNaturality.holomorphicSquarePullback_biholomorphic_identity}
\leanok
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:submersion-cartesian-tower-whole-universal}
Let $g:E\to D$ have the genuine full holomorphic pullback square over
$f:Y\to X$, with actual projections $p:E\to Y$, $t:D\to X$.
Let actual biholomorphisms $\eta:E'\to E$, $\theta:D'\to D$ preserve
the whole original projections: $p\eta=p'$, $t\theta=t'$.
Then $\theta^{-1}g\eta$ has the genuine full holomorphic pullback
property with $p',t'$. In particular, for an actual biholomorphism
$\eta:E'\to E$ with $p\eta=p'$, the whole square of $\eta$ over
base identity is a genuine holomorphic pullback. No transported unique
lift is an assumed certificate.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For an arbitrary holomorphic cone $(a:W\to Y,b:W\to D')$ with
$fa=t'b$, the actual old square applies to $(a,\theta b)$, since
the genuine target comparison gives $t\theta b=t'b$. It constructs
its unique holomorphic lift $w:W\to E$. The actual holomorphic map
$\eta^{-1}w$ has projection $p'$ equal to $a$ by
$p\eta=p'$ and the true inverse identity. Its other equation is
$\theta^{-1}g\eta\eta^{-1}w=\theta^{-1}gw=b$, using
$gw=\theta b$ and both actual inverse equations. A competing lift
$v$ gives the old lift $\eta v$: its projection follows from
$p\eta=p'$, and applying actual $\theta$ to its second equation
gives $g\eta v=\theta b$. Old-square uniqueness gives $\eta v=w$;
actual injectivity identifies the entire lift. For the identity-base
square of $\eta$, construct $\eta^{-1}b$ directly. The actual
projection equation and inverse identity give both cone equations;
actual injectivity gives full uniqueness. This covers every test
manifold and dimension without a projection injectivity assumption.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual identity-base terminal map between independent histories is their intrinsic comparison]
\label{hir:smooth-original-terminal-identity-independent-history}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionOriginalTerminalChoiceNaturality.terminalSmoothMap_identity_independent_histories}
\leanok
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:smooth-original-finite-terminal-intrinsic-smooth-map,hir:smooth-terminal-full-square-biholomorphic-transport,hir:original-ambient-terminal-choice-intrinsic-unique-comparison,hir:smooth-original-finite-terminal-map-identity-composition}
From only an original marked-domain input $M$ and TWO GIVEN canonical
terminal histories $T',T$ of $M$, the actual finite terminal map over
base identity is the actual intrinsic biholomorphic comparison from
$T'$ to $T$. Whole marked identity pullback is constructed internally
before transporting the original source history inputs. In particular
the actual identity-base final map is genuinely biholomorphic even
when the real histories have different empty-step counts.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Coherence of the original whole ideal gives actual whole marked
identity pullback equal to $M$, preserving carrier, every indexed
boundary and the mark. Thus the given $T'$ canonicality and terminality
supply the true source inputs of the original identity-map factory.
Original ambient comparison existence and uniqueness internally
construct the actual biholomorphism $\eta$ over the whole original
projections. Its forward map is genuinely holomorphic and has true
whole commutativity. The preceding identity-square construction gives
its full holomorphic universal property internally. The intrinsic
final-map characterization on the SAME actual source tower $T'$
therefore identifies this actual comparison with the factory map.
No comparison or universal property is an original caller premise;
no differently sized towers or exceptional lists are equated.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original finite terminal smooth maps are natural under independent source and target histories]
\label{hir:smooth-original-terminal-independent-history-naturality}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionOriginalTerminalChoiceNaturality.terminalSmoothMap_independent_history_naturality}
\leanok
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:smooth-original-finite-terminal-intrinsic-smooth-map,hir:smooth-terminal-full-square-biholomorphic-transport,hir:original-ambient-terminal-choice-intrinsic-unique-comparison,hir:smooth-original-ambient-domain-value-specialization}
From only the original target marked-domain $M$, genuine smooth
$f:Y\to X$, TWO GIVEN original canonical terminal histories $S',S$
of the actual pulled record on $Y$, and TWO GIVEN original canonical
terminal histories $T',T$ of $M$, construct their intrinsic source and
target comparisons $\eta:S'_{\rm final}\to S_{\rm final}$,
$\theta:T'_{\rm final}\to T_{\rm final}$ internally. The actual
finite smooth terminal maps $g',g$ satisfy the ENTIRE naturality
identity $\theta g'=g\eta$. Thus replacement of both original
histories gives the genuine conjugate map
$g'=\theta^{-1}g\eta$. All ambient dimensions and empty histories
are included, without caller comparison, source-domain, desired
naturality or universal certificates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Original smooth-domain transport internally supplies the actual
marked-domain of $f^*M$. Apply the proved original ambient comparison
constructor to the two given source histories, and separately to the
two target histories. The genuine comparisons $\eta,\theta$ have
both holomorphic maps and true whole projection identities. The
original finite map factory internally supplies $g$ with holomorphicity,
whole commutativity and its genuine universal property. Construct the
actual map $c=\theta^{-1}g\eta$. Its holomorphicity follows from
these three actual holomorphic maps. Its projection satisfies
$p_{T'}c=p_Tg\eta=f p_S\eta=f p_{S'}$, where
$p_{T'}\theta^{-1}=p_T$ is derived from the true target comparison
and its actual inverse. The first lemma supplies the genuine full
universal property of $c$, not a presumed transported certificate.
The original factory independently supplies $g'$ on the SAME actual
source tower $S'$. Intrinsic final-map uniqueness identifies $c=g'$.
Applying the actual target comparison cancels its actual inverse and
gives $\theta g'=g\eta$ as whole functions. All comparisons and
universal properties are genuine internal producers from the original
inputs; noncompact schedule existence and global gluing remain OPEN.
\end{proof}
