\section{Actual zero-contact stalks and admissible clipped original centres}
Let $L$ be the actual enlarged contact coefficient model and let $k$ be the
number of original historical boundary entries. Write $A$ for the true union
of components of $L.N$ not contained in its actual cosupport, $B$ for the
selected union, and $L^{\rm rem}$ for the already constructed remainder.
The ordered remainder boundary retains exactly $k$ empty historical entries.
All towers below are the SAME literal original towers; their maps blow up
their original centres. Admissibility of a clipped centre does not identify
any of these maps with a blowup along that clipped centre.

\begin{theorem}[Entire controlled ideals agree on the actual selected-component complement]
\label{hir:zero-contact-remainder-open-ideal-dictionary}
\leanok
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaStepOneZeroContactClippedCentres.remainder_controlled_ideal_open_eq}
\uses{hir:zero-contact-actual-remainder-definition,hir:member-tail-actual-clopen-strict,hir:coherent-transform-restriction-identity}
For closed smooth $L.N$, coherent $L.ideal$, every natural $k$, and every
actual test tower $T$ of $L$, put $G=M\setminus B$. The entire controlled
ideals of $L^{\rm rem}$ and $L$ restricted to $T^{-1}G$ are equal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both component subunions are closed smooth and disjoint, with union $L.N$.
Thus $G$ is genuinely open and $A=L.N\cap G$. At each strict point of $A$,
closedness of the strict selected union gives a neighborhood where strict
remaining and full contact carrier membership agree. The actual parent test
tower gives smooth full strict carriers; these identical local germs and
closedness give smooth remaining strict carriers at every literal prefix.
Induction on the original tower now proves coherence of the entire remaining
controlled ideal: pull back the actual coherent ideal and take its coherent
colon along the actual smooth strict carrier by the power of the actual
exceptional ideal. This does not require the centre to lie in the remainder.

Initially on $G$, the remaining and full records have the same carrier, ideal
and mark. Replacing the full record's boundary by the remaining boundary makes
the whole restricted records equal. Changing only the boundary leaves the
entire transformed carrier, ideal and mark unchanged by the literal recursion.
Coherent controlled transformation commutes with genuine open restriction
at every prefix, since both source and target ideals have just been proved
coherent. Induct on the original tower and compare these two restrictions.
This gives equality of entire ideals on the whole inverse image of $G$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All actual stalks and all ordinary-order traces agree on the remaining carrier]
\label{hir:zero-contact-remainder-all-stalk-orders}
\leanok
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaStepOneZeroContactClippedCentres.remainder_actual_stalk_and_all_orders}
\uses{hir:zero-contact-remainder-open-ideal-dictionary,hir:member-tail-actual-clopen-strict}
Under the preceding genuine inputs, at EVERY point of the actual remaining
strict carrier, membership of EVERY ambient function in the two controlled
ideal stalks is equivalent. At that point, membership in their along-carrier
ordinary-order cosupports is equivalent for EVERY natural threshold.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The strict transform of the closed remaining carrier lies over that carrier,
so every such point belongs to $T^{-1}G$. A coherent ideal restricted to an
open set has exactly its original stalk there: restrict an ambient function,
then extend it back; the extension agrees with the original near the point,
and coherent germ membership is invariant under this agreement. Apply this
in both directions to the entire open ideal equality just proved. This gives
the asserted equivalence for all functions, without finite-generator or
coordinate-only truncation.

The closed strict partition also gives identical remaining and full carrier
membership germs near the point. In the definition of order at least $q$,
a function agrees on the carrier with a germ in the $q$th power of the point
ideal. Intersect its witnessing neighborhood with the neighborhood of exact
carrier membership to transfer this witness in each direction. Combine this
all-order identity with all-function ideal-stalk equivalence and with actual
point membership in both carriers. This proves the claimed cosupport trace
dictionary for every $q$, including zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An actual parent centre internally produces an admissible clipped centre]
\label{hir:zero-contact-remainder-clipped-admissible}
\leanok
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaStepOneZeroContactClippedCentres.remainder_clipped_centre_isAdmissible}
\uses{hir:zero-contact-remainder-all-stalk-orders,hir:zero-contact-actual-remainder-definition,hir:member-tail-actual-clopen-strict}
Assume in addition $k\leq\#L.E$. For any actual parent test prefix $T$ and
actual admissible parent centre $C$, the set
$C\cap (L^{\rm rem})_T.N$ is an admissible centre of the actual entire
remaining controlled datum at that SAME prefix.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The intersection is closed. At a point of it, actual parent admissibility
puts the entire centre in the full current carrier. The exact remaining/full
carrier membership germ therefore makes the clipped centre germ equal to
the original centre germ. Its actual regular coordinate presentation
transfers across this identity, proving closed smoothness. The all-order
stalk dictionary at the unchanged marking transfers the original
centre's true cosupport inclusion to the remaining controlled datum.

The boundary recursion gives precisely $k$ empty entries followed by the
full transformed boundary with its first $k$ entries dropped. Indeed strict
transform preserves an empty entry; mapping commutes with dropping, and each
new exceptional entry is appended beyond the first $k$ entries, whose length
bound persists at every prefix. Normal-crossing coordinates of the original
centre restrict to this actual dropped sublist. Transfer those same
coordinates to the clipped centre by its exact membership germ, shrinking
the chart source within the witnessing neighborhood. Adding the $k$ empty
entries imposes no additional incident coordinate condition. This internally
proves all three fields of actual admissibility. No new blowup map is claimed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every literal original centre has an admissible actual clipping]
\label{hir:zero-contact-remainder-all-literal-clipped-centres}

\lean{Hironaka.HironakaStepOneZeroContactClippedCentres.remainder_all_literal_clipped_centres}
\uses{hir:zero-contact-remainder-clipped-admissible}
For the same genuine full contact test tower, EVERY actual recorded centre,
evaluated at its SAME literal prefix, has an admissible intersection with
the current true remaining carrier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual tower. A zero-length tower records no centre. At a literal
last blowup the true parent test condition supplies both its actual earlier
test tower and admissibility of its actual last centre. Apply the induction
hypothesis to all earlier recorded centres, then the preceding theorem at
the actual last prefix. Their conjunction is exactly the recursive property
of all recorded centres. This is produced as output, never supplied as input.
\end{proof}
