\section{Actual coherent controlled transforms across order-mask boundaries}

The constructed general invariant forms its literal companion on an open
order mask. The following producers use the actual globally defined
companion and coefficient ideals, with their actual colon-controlled
transforms, and prove their exact restriction identities. In particular
the extension is the same ideal used by the actual invariant on the mask,
not an independently supplied ideal or extension certificate.

\begin{lemma}[Coherence of the actual colon along a carrier without inclusion]
\label{hir:coherent-transform-colon-without-inclusion}
\uses{ana:restricted-ideals,oka:coherent-colon}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCoherentTransformExtension.isCoherent_colonAlong_without_inclusion}
\leanok
If the genuine ideals $I$, $\mathcal I_N$, and $L$ are coherent, their actual
colon along $N$ is coherent, without any inclusion of $I|_N$ in $L|_N$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By its actual definition the colon along $N$ is
$(I+\mathcal I_N):L$. The actual restricted ideal is coherent by coherent
sum, and the actual colon of two coherent ideals is coherent by the already
proved Oka colon theorem. No invertible quotient interpretation or restricted
inclusion is used in this coherence conclusion.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual blowup coherence and nonzero-along preservation]
\label{hir:coherent-transform-step-without-admissibility}
\uses{hir:coherent-transform-colon-without-inclusion,bcalc:strict-submanifold,bcalc:transform-off-centre,bcalc:inv-domain-stable}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCoherentTransformExtension.isCoherent_geoTransform_ideal_without_inclusion,Hironaka.HironakaCoherentTransformExtension.isNonzeroAlongAt_geoTransform_without_admissibility}
\leanok
For an actual blowup along $C\subseteq N$, a genuine closed smooth carrier
$N$ and coherent $I$, the actual colon-controlled transform $I'$ is coherent.
If $I$ is genuinely nonzero along $N$ at every point, $I'$ is genuinely
nonzero along the actual strict transform $N'$ at every point. The centre
need not lie in the cosupport of this candidate marked ideal.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual strict transform of the closed smooth $N$ is closed smooth.
Its vanishing ideal is coherent. The actual pullback of $I$ is coherent,
as are the actual pullback centre ideal and its actual mark power.
The actual transformed ideal is exactly the colon along $N'$ of these
ideals, so the preceding producer proves coherence.
Away from the actual centre, the actual blowup is locally biholomorphic,
and its actual controlled transform is nonzero along $N'$ by the genuine
off-centre transform identity. At a point over the centre, suppose every
germ of the transformed coherent ideal vanished on $N'$ near that point.
Actual finite coherent generators make this vanishing true for every germ
at nearby points. Genuine points of the actual strict transform lying over
$N\setminus C$ accumulate at this point, by the actual closure definition.
Choose one such nearby point. Its off-centre nonzero germ contradicts the
produced local vanishing. This proves the actual nonzero property without
any candidate admissibility hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Entire actual candidate transforms along a true carrier tower]
\label{hir:coherent-transform-entire-tower}
\uses{hir:coherent-transform-step-without-admissibility,hir:tower-split}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCoherentTransformExtension.geoTransformAlong_N_eq_of_N_eq,Hironaka.HironakaCoherentTransformExtension.geoTransformAlong_E_eq_of_E_eq,Hironaka.HironakaCoherentTransformExtension.isClosedSubmanifold_and_isCoherent_geoTransformAlong_sameCarrier,Hironaka.HironakaCoherentTransformExtension.isNonzeroAlongAt_geoTransformAlong_sameCarrier}
\leanok
Let $T$ be an actual geometric test tower for $\mathfrak m$. Any genuine
candidate $\mathfrak j$ with the same initial carrier, a closed smooth
carrier and coherent ideal has an actual globally coherent colon-controlled
transform at every prefix of $T$ and exactly the same transformed carrier.
If its initial ideal is genuinely nonzero along this carrier, its actual
entire transformed ideal remains so. No test-tower condition for the
candidate is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual carrier recursion depends only on the previous carrier and
literal centre and map, so induction proves equality of the transformed
carriers. If the initial boundaries also agree, the entire transformed
boundaries agree by the identical map-and-append recursion; initial carrier
equality alone is not used to assert boundary equality. Each actual centre
of the true test tower is contained in the
transformed carrier of $\mathfrak m$, hence in the candidate's identical
carrier. Apply the preceding actual blowup producers at the literal step.
Induction retains genuine closed smoothness, coherence, and nonzero along
at every step. Reflexive towers and empty centres are included without
removing any literal tower steps.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact open restriction of the entire actual controlled transform]
\label{hir:coherent-transform-restriction-identity}

restriction of the candidate along the actual restricted tower equals the
open pullback of its globally defined actual controlled transform, as marked
ideals: carrier, divisor, ideal and mark all agree.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual tower. The genuine one-step restriction equality uses
only coherence of the input and output controlled ideals; both have been
internally produced at every actual prefix by the preceding theorem.
The true open-preimage restriction keeps the literal centre preimage and
literal restricted blowup map. Apply that actual one-step equality and the
inductive marked-ideal equality. The result is an exact equality, including
the actual ambient germs, not just equality of supports or stalk orders.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual invariant domain for entire candidates]
\label{hir:coherent-transform-entire-domain}
\uses{hir:coherent-transform-entire-tower,bcalc:inv-domain-stable}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCoherentTransformExtension.isInvDomain_geoTransformAlong_sameCarrier}
\leanok
If the candidate is in the actual dimension-$k$ invariant domain and shares
the genuine initial carrier and boundary with the well-formed template,
its actual entire controlled transform along the true template test tower
is in the same actual invariant domain, even without candidate admissibility.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the produced actual closed smooth carrier, coherent ideal and nonzero
along property. The genuine transformed carrier and boundary agree exactly
with the template's, so its actual SNC and transversality conclusions apply.
The candidate's actual initial carrier dimension bound transfers to the
template's equal initial carrier; genuine strict-transform dimension
preservation then gives this bound at every literal stage and equality of
carriers returns it to the candidate. The actual mark is unchanged and
positive. These are precisely the real invariant-domain fields.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Genuine nonzero-along coefficient companion]
\label{hir:coherent-transform-coefficient-nonzero}
\uses{hir:step-one-lower-domain,hir:coherent-transform-entire-domain}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaCoherentTransformExtension.isNonzeroAlongAt_barMarked,Hironaka.HironakaCoherentTransformExtension.isInvDomain_barMarked}
\leanok
For a genuine coherent positive-mark ideal nonzero along its carrier, its
actual coefficient-of-homogenized ideal is genuinely nonzero along that
carrier. Consequently this actual marked construction preserves the true
invariant domain.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose an actual original ideal germ $g$ nonzero at arbitrarily nearby
carrier points. The genuine homogenized ideal contains the original ideal,
so it contains the same actual germ. The coefficient marked sum has its
actual index-zero summand, since the mark is positive. This summand contains
the actual power $g^e$ for its actual coefficient-sum exponent $e$, and the
actual sum therefore contains this genuine power germ. It is nonzero at
every one of the same nearby carrier points, including when $e=0$.
This proves genuine nonzero along internally. Coherence, positive mark,
and equality of the actual carrier and boundary follow from the actual
coefficient construction. Retaining the same real dimension bound produces
all invariant-domain fields.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The literal order-mask companion has a true coherent entire transform]
\label{hir:coherent-transform-literal-companion-extension}

\leanok

actual true tail, and a positive order $o$, the actual globally defined
companion formed at that head has a coherent, genuinely nonzero-along,
entire controlled transform along the tail. Its pullback to the actual
order-mask preimage equals exactly the literal transformed companion used
by the constructed general invariant on that actual restricted tail.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true head-stage datum lies in the actual invariant domain. The actual

producer along the actual true tail. At the initial mask the literal
companion equals the restriction of this global companion by the actual
companion/open pullback theorem. The exact open restriction identity then
identifies its literal transformed marked ideal with the open pullback of
the produced actual entire controlled transform. Thus the literal invariant
uses this exact entire ideal on the mask.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

\end{proof}
