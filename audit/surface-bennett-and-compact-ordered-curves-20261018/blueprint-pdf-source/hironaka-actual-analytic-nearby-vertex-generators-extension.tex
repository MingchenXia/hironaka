\section{The same genuine analytic vertices generate every nearby original stalk}

Let $\mathcal A$ be the original analytic germ-ideal predicate in
$\mathbb C^n$, with a genuine finite coherent presentation near the origin.
Let $I$ be its entire original origin ideal in the holomorphic germ ring.

\begin{lemma}[An entire original finite germ basis spreads to actual nearby generators]
\label{hir:actual-original-origin-basis-nearby-generators}
\uses{hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-finite-span,hir:relative-normal-coherent-saturation-neighborhood-stability}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticNearbyVertexGenerators.exists_actual_nearby_representatives_of_origin_span}
\leanok
For any finite family $(G_i)$ generating the entire $I$, there are internally
chosen actual holomorphic representatives $g_i$ with original germs $G_i$
and one genuine open neighbourhood $U$ of the origin such that all $g_i$
are holomorphic on $U$ and, at every $y\in U$, the original whole
$\mathcal A_y$ is generated by those same functions $g_i$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose actual holomorphic representatives of the finitely many $G_i$.
Their genuine holomorphic germs persist near the origin, and finiteness
gives one actual open neighbourhood on which every representative is
holomorphic. The predicate of finite holomorphic combinations of these
functions consequently has a genuine finite presentation there.

At the origin, the independent entire-original-ideal dictionary identifies
$\mathcal A_0$ with $I$, and the whole finite-span dictionary identifies
the newly chosen functional combinations with $(G_i)=I$. These are equal
as complete predicates, including the case of a function which is not
holomorphic at the origin: neither predicate contains such a function.
The genuine finite coherent equality theorem spreads this whole equality
to one actual neighbourhood, by propagating the finite functional witnesses
in both directions. Intersect with the common holomorphicity neighbourhood.
This gives the asserted same functions and complete equality at every
nearby point, with no invented map from all origin germs to nearby stalks.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original real-open coherence internally supplies nearby generators with genuine monic coefficient data]
\label{hir:actual-original-nearby-monic-vertex-generators}
\uses{hir:actual-original-origin-basis-nearby-generators,hir:actual-original-analytic-vertex-coefficient-data-existence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticNearbyVertexGenerators.exists_actual_original_nearby_vertex_coefficient_data}
\leanok
Suppose $\mathcal A$ is coherent on a genuine original open $W$ containing
the origin in coordinates $(u,y,z)$ of dimensions $k,s,t$.
From these original inputs alone one can construct analytic vertex
coefficient data $P$ for $I$, actual representatives $g_\alpha$ of its same
generators, and one open $U\subseteq W$ containing the origin such that
every $g_\alpha$ is holomorphic on $U$ and generates the entire original
$\mathcal A_y$ with its companions at every $y\in U$.
The projection of $U$ to the $(u,y)$ variables lies in the actual coefficient
domain of $P$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Real-open coherence supplies a genuine finite original presentation near
the origin. Apply the internally constructed analytic vertex coefficient
data theorem to its entire original origin ideal $I$, obtaining $P$.
Its actual generators give the whole origin span required by the preceding
lemma. Apply that lemma to obtain the same actual analytic representatives
and their whole nearby stalk generation. Intersect its neighbourhood with
$W$ and with the inverse image of the genuine coefficient domain of $P$
under the actual linear coordinate projection. Both sets are open and
contain the origin, so the resulting $U$ does too. Holomorphicity and
entire nearby generation restrict to $U$, and the projection inclusion is
literal. The actual monic Taylor coefficients and complete original
coefficient identities are retained from $P$.
The result asserts nearby generation by this same analytic family; it
does not assert constant nearby initial diagrams, identify the coefficient
cosupport with the full Hilbert--Samuel maximum locus, or prove the source
semicoherent derivative/Jacobian and transformed-stability conditions.
\end{proof}
