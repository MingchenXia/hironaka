\section{Higher-dimensional incident-centre ambient trace continuation}

Let $N$ be a closed complex submanifold and let $H$ be an ambient SNC
hypersurface, with $N\subset\overline{N\setminus H}$.  A smooth centre
$C\subset N$ is locally contained in every member it meets if
\[
 p\in C\cap H\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \text{there is an open }U\ni p\text{ with }C\cap U\subset H\cap U.
\]
This condition allows positive-dimensional centres and disconnected centres
with both incident and nonincident components.  Throughout, the ambient
dimension is positive.  Write $\operatorname{Res}_N$ for the whole
relative ideal restriction, and $\mathcal N_N(-,E)$ for removal of the
full greatest exceptional monomial factor.

\begin{lemma}[Whole trace geometry for a locally incident centre]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-incident-centre-whole-trace-geometry}
\uses{hir:sharp-local-incident-containment-actual-centre-normal-crossings,hir:sharp-actual-first-ambient-strict-trace-greatest-factor-relative-strict,hir:sharp-actual-mixed-discrete-centre-whole-strict-trace-geometry-density}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualIncidentCentreTraceResidual.actual_incident_centre_whole_strict_trace_geometry}
\leanok
For an actual blowup $\pi:Y\to X$ along such a centre, put
$N'=\operatorname{Strict}_\pi N$, $H'=\operatorname{Strict}_\pi H$,
$D=\pi^{-1}C$, and $I'=\operatorname{Res}_{N'}\mathcal I_{H'}$.
Then $N'$ is smooth, $[H',D]$ is SNC, $I'$ is coherent and nonzero
along $N'$, $N'\subset\overline{N'\setminus H'}$, and at each point
of $N'$ the entire ideal $I'$ has a holomorphic relative principal
generator, in both ideal-membership directions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Local incident containment and the SNC coordinates for $H$ give normal
crossings of $C$ with $[H]$: shrink around a centre point so that the
incident hypersurface contains the whole restricted centre, then insert
its coordinate into smooth centre coordinates.  The actual blowup SNC
transformation theorem therefore makes $[H',D]$ SNC.  The strict carrier
$N'$ is smooth because $C\subset N$.  Coherence of the two smooth
vanishing ideals gives coherence of their relative restriction $I'$.

The original density gives a nonzero germ of $\mathcal I_H$ along every
point of $N$.  The actual zero-mark transform preserves nonzero germs
along $N'$ and equals $\operatorname{Res}_{N'}\pi^*\mathcal I_H$.
Every pulled-back vanishing germ vanishes on the actual closed strict
transform $H'$, so the whole raw transform is contained in $I'$.
Its nonzero witnesses thus give nonzero witnesses for $I'$.  A finite
relative-span expression for a germ in $I'$ vanishes on $N'\cap H'$;
a frequent nonzero witness forces density of $N'\setminus H'$.
Finally the smooth SNC member $H'$ has a local principal vanishing
ideal, and restriction of its two principal-span directions gives both
asserted relative generator directions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The whole trace residual is the independently saturated raw ideal]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-incident-centre-trace-residual}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-incident-centre-whole-trace-geometry,hir:sharp-actual-raw-total-full-open-residual-germs,hir:sharp-actual-any-member-raw-residual-independent-allpower-strict,hir:sharp-actual-first-ambient-strict-trace-greatest-factor-relative-strict,hir:sharp-actual-nonmonomial-whole-open-germ-dictionary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualIncidentCentreTraceResidual.actual_incident_trace_nonmonomial_eq_relative_strict,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualIncidentCentreTraceResidual.actual_incident_trace_greatest_factor_dictionary}
\leanok
Under the preceding hypotheses let
$J'=\operatorname{Sat}_{\mathcal I_D}^{\infty}
(\operatorname{Res}_{N'}\pi^*\mathcal I_H)$, defined independently by
all-power saturation.  Then, as whole germ ideals,
\[
 \mathcal N_{N'}(I',[D])=J',\qquad
 I'=\operatorname{Res}_{N'}\bigl(M'J'\bigr),\quad
 M'=\mathcal M_{N'}(I',[D]).
\]
The actual $M'$ is coherent, nonzero along $N'$, and monomial in $[D]$.
The outer restriction and every exceptional factor are retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a point above $C\cap H$, local incident containment supplies an actual
base open $U$ on which $C_U\subset H_U$.  Restrict the same blowup to its
whole preimage.  Actual strict transforms commute with this restriction.
The contained-centre whole trace factor theorem identifies the local
trace residual with the independently defined local all-power saturation.
The independent raw residual theorem identifies the local raw residual
with that same saturation.  Whole pullback identities and the open
dictionary for greatest-factor removal transport both memberships to
the two global residuals, for every local function.  Restricting and
extending a global function changes none of its coherent ideal germs.

At a point projecting outside $H$, the closed complement of $H$ makes
the original vanishing ideal a unit ideal.  Its actual pullback and the
actual strict-member vanishing ideal are both units there, so both
residuals consist of all holomorphic germs.  At a point outside $D$,
the exceptional monomial ideal contains $1$, and both colon directions
identify each residual with its own normalized input.  The actual
isomorphism off the centre identifies the raw and strict vanishing
ideals there.  These cases cover the whole ambient manifold and prove
whole residual equality.  The independent raw theorem now gives $J'$.
The genuine greatest-factor theorem supplies the geometry of $M'$ and
the two factor inclusions; relative restriction extensionality and
idempotence give the displayed whole factorization.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The same residual comparison retains every old exceptional member]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-incident-centre-all-exceptional-residual}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-incident-centre-trace-residual,hir:sharp-actual-whole-monomial-product-residual,hir:sharp-actual-any-member-raw-residual-independent-allpower-strict}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualIncidentCentreTraceResidual.actual_incident_accumulated_trace_nonmonomial_eq_total}
\leanok
Suppose in addition that $E$ is SNC, $N$ is transverse to $E$, and the
actual centre $C$ has normal crossings with $E$.  Put
$E'=\operatorname{Strict}_\pi(E)\mathbin{+\!+}[D]$ and
$P'=\operatorname{Res}_{N'}\pi^*\mathcal I_H$.
Then $E'$ is SNC, $N'$ is transverse to $E'$, and
\[
 \mathcal N_{N'}(I',E')=\mathcal N_{N'}(P',E').
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual admissible blowup theorem gives the whole ordered exceptional
list $E'$ and transversality of $N'$.  Independently factor $P'$ along
$[D]$.  Its residual is the actual all-power saturation $J'$ by the raw
residual theorem.  The preceding theorem factors $I'$ with the same
$J'$.  Both single-exceptional monomial factors remain monomial with
respect to the full list $E'$, because $[D]$ is a subfamily.  Removing
all exceptional monomial factors from a whole normalized monomial
product leaves the residual of its other factor.  Applying this fact
to both factorizations gives the same $\mathcal N_{N'}(J',E')$ and hence
the asserted equality.  This proves a higher-dimensional continuation
identity.  It does not assert strict decrease of a modified invariant
or termination of a sharp resolution history.
\end{proof}
