\section{The actual differential exceptional locus of a normal-coordinate chart}

\begin{theorem}[The actual manifold differential retains the tangent rows]
\label{hir:actual-normal-blowup-differential-coordinates}
\uses{hir:actual-point-blowup-differential-coordinates,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality}
For the unchanged polynomial chart with selected-coordinate set $A$,
the actual holomorphic manifold differential has row $v_i$ outside
$A$, row $v_e$ at its exceptional selected coordinate, and row
$v_e(b_i+z_i)+z_ev_i$ at every other selected coordinate.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalBlowupExceptionalLocus.mfderiv_actual_selected_blowup_apply}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose the genuine actual Fr\'echet differential with the original
continuous linear coordinate projection. The chain rule gives the
actual scalar-row derivative. On a selected coordinate that scalar
function equals the already proved point-chart scalar function at
every actual point, so uniqueness of the differential identifies its
row with the genuine point-chart derivative. Outside the selected
set the scalar function is exactly the original linear coordinate
projection, whose derivative is itself. The existing self-model
manifold/Fr\'echet differential dictionary gives the asserted actual
manifold formula. No Jacobian certificate is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Normal codimension at least two supplies a genuine nonzero differential kernel]
\label{hir:actual-normal-blowup-hyperplane-exceptional}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-blowup-differential-coordinates}
For the actual last normal block of size $t\ge2$, an actual
$j\in\{0,\ldots,t-1\}$ and $e=d+j$, every point with $z_e=0$
belongs to the original differential exceptional locus of the actual
normal blowup chart.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalBlowupExceptionalLocus.exists_actual_normal_blowup_nonzero_kernel,Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalBlowupExceptionalLocus.mem_exceptionalLocus_actual_normal_blowup_of_coordinate_eq_zero}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose a genuine different normal index $k\ne j$, possible because
$t\ge2$, and take the actual standard vector supported at $d+k$.
Its $d+k$ coordinate is one, so it is nonzero. Every tangent coordinate
and its exceptional coordinate $d+j$ are zero. The preceding actual
row formula gives zero in the exceptional output and every other
normal output because $v_e=z_e=0$. Every tangent output is its zero
tangent coordinate. Thus the entire actual differential sends a
genuine nonzero vector to zero and is not injective, hence not
bijective. This is precisely the original exceptional-locus meaning.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Normal codimension one is the actual identity in every tangent dimension]
\label{hir:actual-normal-blowup-codimension-one-identity}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality,hir:actual-whole-vanishing-origin-ideal-meaning,hir:actual-model-zero-whole-stalk-hs-dictionary}
If the normal block has size one, the entire original chart map is
the identity for every tangent dimension $d$ and every chart parameter.
Its original exceptional locus is empty. For every original subset
$W$, its original geometric strict transform is $\overline W$,
and the unchanged intrinsic HS value at zero equals that of $W$ at
every order.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalBlowupExceptionalLocus.actual_normal_blowup_one_eq_id,Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalBlowupExceptionalLocus.exceptionalLocus_actual_normal_blowup_one,Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalBlowupExceptionalLocus.strictTransform_actual_normal_blowup_one,Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalBlowupExceptionalLocus.hilbertSamuelAt_actual_normal_blowup_one}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every tangent coordinate is fixed by definition. The unique normal
coordinate is the exceptional one and is also fixed. Thus the whole
actual map is the identity, regardless of the total dimension. Its
actual differential is bijective everywhere, so the original
exceptional locus is empty. The original strict-transform definition
reduces to the closure of $W$. Genuine continuity of holomorphic
representatives identifies the entire original vanishing germ ideals
of $W$ and $\overline W$, even for arbitrary subsets. The actual
model-zero whole-stalk dictionary identifies their unchanged intrinsic
HS values, giving equality at every order.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A genuine exceptional product vanishes on the actual exceptional-complement closure]
\label{hir:actual-holomorphic-product-quotient-closure-vanishing}
\uses{hir:actual-local-continuous-vanishing-closure}
Let $\Pi$ be an actual map of the original complex model, holomorphic
near zero and fixing zero. If an original holomorphic germ $g$ vanishes
on an arbitrary subset $W$ near zero and the actual product identity
$x_e^qH=g\circ\Pi$ holds as whole original germs, then a genuine
representative of $H$ vanishes near zero on
$\overline{\Pi^{-1}(W)\setminus\{z_e=0\}}$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalBlowupExceptionalLocus.actual_quotient_zero_on_exceptional_complement_closure}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original whole germ identity means the actual representative
identity $z_e^qH(z)=g(\Pi(z))$ on a neighbourhood of zero.
Actual holomorphicity of $\Pi$ and its original zero identity give
continuity at zero, so the original near-zero vanishing neighbourhood
of $g$ pulls back to a genuine near-zero neighbourhood. At its points
of $\Pi^{-1}(W)$ outside $z_e=0$, the scalar $z_e^q$ is nonzero.
Cancelling it gives actual vanishing of $H$. The existing genuine
local continuity theorem carries that vanishing to the full closure
germ. No global analytic extension, coherence or output restriction
certificate is assumed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The entire original normalized ideal lies in the actual geometric strict vanishing ideal]
\label{hir:actual-normal-geometric-strict-normalized-inclusion}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-blowup-hyperplane-exceptional,hir:actual-holomorphic-product-quotient-closure-vanishing,hir:actual-whole-vanishing-origin-ideal-meaning,hir:actual-model-zero-whole-stalk-hs-dictionary,hir:actual-analytic-normalized-member-ideal}
For an actual normal block of size $t\ge2$ and every original subset
$W$ of the original model, the unchanged entire analytic
normalized-member ideal of its original vanishing ideal is contained
in the unchanged original vanishing ideal of the original geometric
strict transform under the actual normal chart. Consequently every
original intrinsic HS value of that strict transform at zero is at
most the original whole normalized-ideal HS value at that order.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalBlowupExceptionalLocus.actual_normal_normalized_ideal_le_geometric_strict_vanishing,Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalBlowupExceptionalLocus.hilbertSamuelAt_actual_normal_strict_le_normalized}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
It suffices to treat every original generator of the entire normalized
ideal. Its original numerator belongs to the original vanishing ideal,
so genuine whole-ideal vanishing semantics gives an actual near-zero
vanishing representative on $W$. Its defining genuine normalized
product gives the preceding actual quotient vanishing on the closure
outside the exceptional coordinate hyperplane. By the actual
differential-kernel theorem, that hyperplane lies in the original
exceptional locus. Thus the complement of the original exceptional
locus is contained in the complement of the coordinate hyperplane.
Taking inverse-image intersections and then closures preserves this
direction. The original geometric strict set is therefore contained
in the closure on which the actual quotient vanishes. The same
whole-vanishing semantics puts the generator in the entire original
geometric strict vanishing ideal. Span induction gives the whole
inclusion. Original whole-ideal HS antitonicity and the true model-zero
intrinsic dictionary yield the stated all-order bound.
\end{proof}

The codimension-one geometric identity is deliberately separate from
the normalized ideal, which may lose a divisor even though the actual
geometric map is the identity. The higher-codimension bound here is
by the unchanged normalized ideal, with no source-HS comparison or
coefficient compatibility certificate. General positive-dimensional
permissible-centre Bennett remains open.
