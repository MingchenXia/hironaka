\begin{lemma}[Every actual holomorphic field is tangent off a closed carrier]
\label{hir:submersion-tangent-contact-off-carrier-field}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionTangentContact.isTangentField_of_notMem}
\uses{mc:derivative-along,mc:field-chain-rule}
\leanok
For an original closed carrier $N$, a true point outside $N$, an actual chart
containing that point and actual holomorphic field coefficients, the field is
genuinely tangent to $N$ there. No tangency or vanishing-output certificate is
supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The complement of the actual closed carrier is a genuine neighbourhood of the
point. Every holomorphic function germ, including every true field derivative,
therefore belongs to the actual vanishing ideal of $N$: its required equality
on nearby carrier points is vacuous on this neighbourhood. The ordinary field
product/derivative calculus gives holomorphicity of the derivative of every
member of the vanishing ideal, whose function is itself holomorphic. The
original chart membership and coefficient holomorphicity are exactly the
remaining two fields of the actual tangency definition.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A true nonzero original tangent derivative has a genuine source tangent derivative]
\label{hir:submersion-tangent-contact-nonzero-field-lift}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionTangentContact.exists_nonzero_tangentField_preimage_add,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothSubmersionTangentContact.exists_nonzero_tangentField_preimage}

\leanok

holomorphic at $fy$, and a genuine original tangent field with nonzero
function derivative at $fy$ internally produce a genuine holomorphic field
at $y$ tangent to $f^{-1}N$ whose derivative of the entire pulled function is
nonzero. Dimensions may differ and $f$ need not be surjective. No lifted field,
chart, tangent-space or derivative comparison certificate is supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At a point over $N$, internally choose actual flattening coordinates of $N$.
The original whole field/flat-partial decomposition, together with tangency,
makes all normal-coordinate coefficient values zero at that point. A nonzero
whole derivative therefore has a genuinely nonzero tangential partial with
index $j<k$. Construct the true fibre-first source chart from the actual
submersion. Its whole preimage carrier is the contiguous plane of dimension
$t+k$, and its actual coordinate field at index $t+j$ is tangent to this
carrier. The proved genuine function chain rule identifies its derivative of
$u\circ f$ with the nonzero original partial.

At a point outside $N$, use genuine centred ambient coordinates and regard
the original field as tangent to the whole ambient manifold. The same whole
flat decomposition yields a nonzero ambient partial. Its lifted coordinate
field has the same nonzero derivative by the actual chain rule. Source field
coefficients are holomorphic; node one makes it tangent to the true closed
preimage carrier outside that carrier. Thus no off-carrier zeros are discarded.
If the source is nonempty actual submersion geometry internally gives
$m=n+t$; otherwise there is no point at which a fictitious field is demanded.
The argument itself excludes a nonexistent nonzero derivative in dimension
zero without introducing a false positive-dimension hypothesis.
\end{proof}
