\section{Actual initial-boundary compatibility of the whole history map}

The indexed Step One histories retain the original boundary list, including
empty or inactive repetitions. This supplies the compatibility between the
initial-boundary insertion formula and the true level-dependent history map
needed for higher original recursive histories. The argument adapts the
indexed recursive route of \cite{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
by deriving the necessary empty prefix from the actual original geometry.

\begin{lemma}[The true lower empty prefix contains the clamped history index]
\label{hir:original-initial-boundary-actual-lower-history-prefix}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-member-filtered-prefix-exact-count,hir:original-invariant-empty-actual}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryHistoryMap.original_initial_lower_index_bound,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryHistoryMap.original_initial_lower_history_empty_prefix}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an original well-formed marked record, $H$ an actual test history
for its original barred record, $p$ an actual endpoint, and $j\leq |M.E|$.
Put $\ell=\operatorname{level}(M,H,p)$ and $d=j-\ell$, with subtraction
clamped at zero. The ACTUAL selected lower record $L(M,\ell,H,p)$ has at
least $d$ boundary entries and its first $d$ entries are empty. At positive
level, $d$ is bounded above by the actual retained-prefix insertion index.
No lower record, empty-prefix or index comparison is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $E$ for the full actual barred boundary, $m=|M.E|$ and
$E_0=E\vert_m$ for its original prefix. Actual testness and original
well-formedness give the true SNC boundary. The established indexed SNC
count therefore says that the retained original prefix, filtered by
$\neg(W\in E_0\mathbin{\wedge}p\in W)$, has length $m-\ell$.
Also $\ell\leq m$, since it counts a subset of the $m$ original indices.
Split the literal original prefix into its first $j$ entries and its next
$m-j$ entries. Filtering preserves concatenation; the retained suffix has
length at most $m-j$. If $c$ is the actual retained length of the first
$j$ entries, then $m-\ell\leq c+(m-j)$, hence $j-\ell\leq c$.
The actual original lower-prefix producer makes the first $c$ lower entries
empty at positive level. Taking their first $d\leq c$ entries proves the
claim with its boundary-length bound. At level zero the actual chosen-contact
lower prefix already has its first $j$ entries empty; here $d=j$.
No equality of independent contact choices is needed: this is the actual
lower record selected by the unchanged original input.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The entire actual Step One value has one compatible initial-boundary history map]
\label{hir:original-initial-boundary-entire-step-history-map-compatibility}
\source{wlodarczyk05-hironaka,bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:original-initial-boundary-actual-lower-history-prefix,hir:original-short-history-step-map,hir:original-invariant-empty-actual,hir:original-crossing-run-base-regime}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryHistoryMap.actual_lower_history_map_compatibility,Hironaka.HironakaOriginalBoundaryHistoryMap.actual_entire_step_history_map_compatibility}
\leanok
For EVERY original well-formed $M$, entire barred test history $T$, actual
point $y$, every actual original initial-boundary index $j\leq |M.E|$,
and EVERY family depth $k$, the entire actual Step One value $v$ satisfies
\[
 \mathcal H_{k,j}(v)=\operatorname{pairShift}(\operatorname{shiftFamily}(k,0))(v),
 \qquad v=\operatorname{StepOne}(R_k;M,T,y).
\]
The value is unrestricted in its level, chosen-contact branch and all inner
nonmonomial orders. No canonicality, lower-value, branch or compatibility
certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At any actual assignment head $H$ and suffix $Q$, take the level of the
actual assignment point $p=Qy$. The preceding result gives a true empty
prefix of length $j-\ell$ in the ACTUAL lower marked record. If $p$ misses
the actual lower mask, the masked lower value is infinite and both maps
fix it. If it lies in the mask, restrict the actual whole suffix and use
the specific-family empty-prefix theorem on this actual lower record.
It gives $\operatorname{shiftFamily}(k,j-\ell)$ equal to
$\operatorname{shiftFamily}(k,0)$ on its ENTIRE $R_k$ value, at every inner
order. Coercion into the masked value gives the same equality.
For the whole Step One value, a special point has infinite lower coordinate,
so the two pair maps agree directly. At a nonspecial point put
$\ell=\operatorname{level}(M,T,y)$, let $r$ be the actual run of that regime,
and take the literal full split $T=H Q$ with suffix length $\min(r,|T|)$.
Current regime membership and this true suffix length imply regime membership
at the original assignment head, by the established reaching-run induction.
Thus its ACTUAL point level is exactly $\ell$. Whole testness supplies actual
barred testness of that head. Apply the masked comparison just proved and
expand the unchanged Step One definition. Both first coordinates are $\ell$;
the second coordinates coincide by that actual lower comparison. This gives
the displayed entire value identity, including empty suffixes and level zero.
It supplies a compatibility producer; higher outer canonical-history transport
and global scheduling remain separate obligations.
\end{proof}
