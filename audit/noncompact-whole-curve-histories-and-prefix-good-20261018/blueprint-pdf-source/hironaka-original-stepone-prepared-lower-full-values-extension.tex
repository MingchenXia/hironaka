\section{Actual higher StepOne preparations produce lower FULL values}

The first higher numerical lower carrier has bound two, arising from an
upper StepOne input of bound three. Its old lower history is canonical,
but the appended one need not be. We construct the precise actual lower
preparation from the original upper data before invoking the proved
bound-two prepared FULL-value theorem. No lower TEST, admissibility,
maximum or numerical-cut certificate is assumed.

\begin{theorem}[The frozen original lower receives an actual full-cut preparation]
\label{hir:original-stepone-frozen-lower-actual-full-cut-step}
\uses{hir:original-stepone-cut-genuine-all-level-lower-preparation}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStepOnePreparedLowerFullValues.original_stepone_cut_actual_lower_preparation}
\leanok
Let $n>0$, let $M\in\mathrm{DOMAIN}(k+1)$ be an actual maximal-order
input, and let $T$ be CAN for its ORIGINAL StepOne invariant based on the
depth-$k$ original FULL invariant. Let $y$ be a nonspecial current
cosupport point. Take a genuine ORIGINAL-admissible centre $C$ above the
StepOne value of EVERY old current cosupport reference and an actual
blowup $\pi$ along $C$.

Use the literal original run, head, tail and selected member/contact
mask at $y$. Let $J$ be that exact lower marked record, $R$ its actual
masked old tail, and $V$ the old terminal mask. The true restricted
blowup has centre $C_V=V\cap C$, map $\pi_V$ and actual history
$R\pi_V$. Internally construct original DOMAIN$(k)$ of $J$, OLD FULL
CAN of $R$, genuine original lower admissibility of $C_V$, and
\[
 \forall p\in\operatorname{cosupp}J_R\;\forall c\in C_V,\qquad
 \operatorname{inv}_{k}(J,R,p)\le\operatorname{inv}_{k}(J,R,c).
\]
All records, masks, maps and orders of positions are the literal original
ones. Empty restricted centres are retained as genuine steps.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The proved all-level frozen-lower preparation supplies original lower
DOMAIN, OLD canonicality, actual appended lower TEST and the inclusion
of the ENTIRE traced centre in the true lower FULL maximum locus. Its
proof uses genuine old lower membership and the two exact fixed-lower
StepOne value formulas to reflect the upper cut, so it assumes no lower
numerical comparison.

The actual restriction of $\pi$ to the terminal inverse-image open is a
blowup along precisely $C_V$. Unfolding only the literal tower restriction
identifies the supplied appended lower TEST with the step $R\pi_V$.
The actual step component of that TEST is real lower admissibility.
For every traced centre point, its actual maximum-locus membership
contains both lower cosupport membership and the full bound for EVERY
lower cosupport reference. Reading that second component gives the
entire claimed cut. No equality of the centre with the whole maximum,
nor next canonicality, is inferred.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[An original bound-three StepOne cut gives actual prepared lower FULL smooth values]
\label{hir:original-stepone-bound-three-prepared-lower-full-smooth-values}
\uses{hir:original-stepone-frozen-lower-actual-full-cut-step,hir:original-domain-two-prepared-full-general-smooth-values}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalStepOnePreparedLowerFullValues.original_stepone_bound_three_prepared_lower_smooth_values}
\leanok
Specialize the original upper input to DOMAIN$(3)$ and StepOne based on
the depth-two FULL invariant. Keep the SAME literal frozen lower record
$J$, old tail $R$, actual restricted step and its appended history above.
Take ANY GIVEN actual smooth Cartesian history over that actual appended
lower history, in positive ambient dimensions $m,n$, with its true initial
map $f$, source history $Q$ and terminal map $g$.

For EVERY actual source endpoint $z$ whose image lies in the actual
current lower cosupport, and EVERY $d\in\mathbb N$, prove
\[
 \operatorname{inv}_{(m-n)+d+2}(f^*J,Q,z)
  =\operatorname{familyDimensionMap}_{m-n,d+2}
       \bigl(\operatorname{inv}_{d+2}(J,R\pi_V,gz)\bigr).
\]
Neither a lower numerical cut nor a lower TEST or numerical isomorphism
is supplied. The given source history is not replaced.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the first theorem to the actual upper bound-three input. This
constructs genuine lower DOMAIN$(2)$, its OLD FULL canonicality, the
actual restricted blowup, real lower admissibility and the entire old
lower FULL cut. That entire cut supplies the positive-reference
subcondition of the proved prepared bound-two FULL smooth-value theorem.
Apply that theorem to these actual lower inputs and the SAME given
Cartesian source history. Literal unfolding of the restricted appended
tower identifies both actual histories and gives exactly the displayed
unchanged original FULL-value identity at every $d+2$.

This is the actual numerical recursive lower producer for a frozen
original reference, including contact level zero and positive member
levels. It is not a claim that the newly computed post-step run equals
that old run, nor a comparison of independently selected OWN-large
models. Reset/increment identification and the higher joint SOURCE-bound
induction remain downstream obligations. No next CAN is asserted.
\end{proof}
