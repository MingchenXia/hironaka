\section{Actual comparison cocycles for a common blowup centre}

The universal property of the analytic blowup supplies unique comparison maps.
For gluing, these maps must be produced simultaneously with their cocycle
identities. This section proves that step for a family of genuine blowups of
the same centre; alignment of centres of independently chosen local canonical
towers and analytic manifold descent remain separate obligations.

\begin{theorem}[The actual holomorphic comparison cocycle]
\label{hir:actual-blowup-comparison-cocycle}
\uses{hir:blowup-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaBlowupComparisonCocycle.exists_comparison_cocycle}
\leanok
Let $(\pi_i:Y_i\to X)_{i\in I}$ be any family of actual blowups along the
same closed smooth centre $C$. There are bijective holomorphic maps
$f_{ij}:Y_i\to Y_j$ satisfying
\[
 \pi_j f_{ij}=\pi_i,\qquad f_{ii}=\mathrm{id},\qquad
 f_{jk}f_{ij}=f_{ik}\quad(i,j,k\in I).
\]
No comparison maps or cocycle identities are required as inputs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each pair $(i,j)$, the universal property of the actual blowup $Y_j$
applied to $\pi_i$ produces a unique holomorphic map $f_{ij}$ over $X$.
The comparison in the opposite direction is an inverse: both composites
are holomorphic maps of the respective blowup over $X$, and uniqueness
identifies each with the identity. Consequently every $f_{ij}$ is bijective.
Choose these actual maps simultaneously over all pairs. The identity is
itself a holomorphic comparison from $Y_i$ to itself, so uniqueness gives
$f_{ii}=\mathrm{id}$. The composite $f_{jk}f_{ij}$ is holomorphic, and
$\pi_k f_{jk}f_{ij}=\pi_j f_{ij}=\pi_i$. It is therefore the unique
comparison from $Y_i$ to $Y_k$, which gives the displayed cocycle. This
argument applies to arbitrary index sets and needs neither finiteness nor
compactness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Biholomorphic comparison data with the actual cocycle]
\label{hir:actual-blowup-biholomorphism-cocycle}
\uses{hir:actual-blowup-comparison-cocycle}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaBlowupComparisonCocycle.exists_biholomorphism_cocycle}
\leanok
Under the same geometric hypotheses, there are actual biholomorphisms
$\Phi_{ij}:Y_i\simeq Y_j$ with inverse $\Phi_{ji}$, commuting with the
actual blowup projections, whose underlying maps satisfy the identity
and triple-overlap cocycle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the produced maps $f_{ij}$ and $f_{ji}$ as the forward and inverse
maps. The cocycle with indices $(i,j,i)$, followed by the identity result,
gives $f_{ji}f_{ij}=\mathrm{id}_{Y_i}$; the indices $(j,i,j)$ give the
other inverse equality. Both maps are holomorphic by the preceding theorem,
so these data define an actual biholomorphism $\Phi_{ij}$. Its projection,
identity, inverse and cocycle assertions are precisely the already proved
equalities of those actual maps.
\end{proof}
