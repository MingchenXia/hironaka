\begin{lemma}[An actual full-coordinate reader under a local biholomorphism]
\label{hir:general-nc-actual-local-biholomorphic-coordinate-reader}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsBiholomorphicMultiplicity.exists_pullback_flat_coordinates}
\leanok
\uses{hir:general-nc-actual-local-snc-branches}
A holomorphic map with bijective differential at a point internally
produces, from any actual original centred holomorphic coordinate map,
an actual coordinate map at that point. It reads the entire same
original coordinate vector, and maps its whole genuine domain into the
whole original coordinate domain.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $e$ be the actual preferred chart at the source point $z$, and $e'$
the preferred chart at its image. On the genuine open set
$N=e.\mathrm{target}\cap e^{-1}(g^{-1}(e'.\mathrm{source}))$,
read the actual map as $F=e'\circ g\circ e^{-1}$. This open set contains
$e(z)$, and the manifold holomorphic coordinate formula gives actual
holomorphicity of $F$ there. The genuine manifold derivative formula
identifies its derivative with the original bijective manifold
differential. The actual holomorphic inverse-function theorem thus
produces a genuine local holomorphic equivalence $\Psi$ with actual
source contained in $N$ and actual function equal to $F$ on that
source. Compose it with the original coordinate equivalence $\Phi$,
using the genuine composite source $\Psi.\mathrm{source}\cap
\Psi^{-1}(\Phi.\mathrm{source})$. This source lies in the actual
preferred chart target and contains $e(z)$. Actual chart inverse
identities show at every point of its whole flat domain that the
image lies in the original flat domain and that the entire coordinate
vector is exactly $\Phi(e'(g(y)))$. Its centre value is the original
centre value. No hyperplane or branch correspondence is a hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine intrinsic original branch multiplicity invariance]
\label{hir:general-nc-biholomorphic-intrinsic-multiplicity}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsBiholomorphicMultiplicity.branchMultiplicity_preimage,Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsBiholomorphicMultiplicity.branchMultiplicity_diffeomorph}
\leanok
\uses{hir:general-nc-whole-biholomorphic-carrier-pair,hir:general-nc-intrinsic-branch-multiplicity,hir:original-normal-crossing-branch-overlap}
The intrinsic original branch count of the actual pulled-back whole
carrier at $z$ equals the intrinsic original count at $g(z)$. In
particular it is invariant under every actual biholomorphism of
whole NC carriers, independently of all initial coordinate branch
labels or coordinate choices.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the original actual centred coordinates and selected finite
set at $g(z)$. The entire pulled-back union coordinates were just
produced with exactly that same selected set. The genuine intrinsic
cardinality theorem, based on unique actual coordinate-germ overlap
bijections, identifies the two intrinsic counts with the cardinality
of this same actual set. Thus they are equal. Arbitrary proofs of
the NC property do not change this intrinsic number. In the
biholomorphic case the original map has bijective differential by
its actual holomorphic inverse and the two derivative chain rules;
therefore the preceding actual local proof applies at every point.
No count equality or branch permutation is a supplied certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual whole superlevels and unique attained maximum alignment]
\label{hir:general-nc-biholomorphic-whole-highest-centre}

\leanok
\uses{hir:general-nc-biholomorphic-intrinsic-multiplicity,hir:general-nc-highest-multiplicity-smooth-centre}
Under an actual biholomorphism, the entire original branch-count
value sets, every actual superlevel, all uniform bounds and the
count-at-most-one stopping condition agree by genuine preimage.
Every nonempty original carrier internally has one unique attained
positive whole global maximum. Its entire highest centre is
therefore uniquely determined and corresponds by the exact
biholomorphic preimage, without a supplied maximum or centre
alignment certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The pointwise actual count identity gives exact equality for every
superlevel. Actual surjectivity gives equality of the two whole
value sets and both directions for every uniform bound and stopping
condition. The genuine finite count-image theorem internally
produces a positive attained maximum for a nonempty original
carrier. If $r$ and $s$ are two attained bounds, evaluate the $s$
bound at an actual $r$-attaining point and the $r$ bound at an actual
$s$-attaining point: $r\le s$ and $s\le r$, hence $r=s$.
Surjectivity transports attainment as well as the bound, so the
maximum is the same on the actual pulled-back whole carrier. The
entire maximum stratum is its actual superlevel at that same
internally produced integer, giving the exact whole-centre
preimage identity. The empty-carrier case has identically zero
count and an invariant terminal condition; it requires no positive
maximum. These statements make no assertion that restriction to
an arbitrary open subset preserves a global maximum.
\end{proof}
