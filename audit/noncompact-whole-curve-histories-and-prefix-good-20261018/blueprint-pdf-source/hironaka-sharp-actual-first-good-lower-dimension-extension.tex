\section{A genuine dimension drop for the actual sharp lower inputs}

The lower recursive input must have a smaller TRUE carrier dimension,
not just an ambient dimension bound. Prove actual pure-dimension
preservation through the same finite geometric history, then use uniqueness
of real regular dimension to identify the genuine reduced traces with
pure dimension one lower. No compactness or canonicality is needed for
this dimension argument. The actual accumulated boundary is retained as
an ambient SNC family with proved lower density; lower transversality
remains a different assertion.

\begin{theorem}[Actual geometric-template history preserves the genuine original pure carrier dimension]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-template-history-pure-regular-dimension}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0088}
\uses{bcalc:strict-submanifold}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstGoodLowerDimension.actual_template_regular_dimension}
\leanok
An original closed smooth carrier of pure regular dimension $k$ retains
THAT dimension at EVERY point of its actual strict carrier after ANY
genuine finite geometric-template history. No compactness, postulated
current purity, canonicality or history comparison certificate is input.
Every actual position, including empty-centre steps, is retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the literal actual tower. The empty tower has precisely the
original carrier and original pointwise regularity. At a true last
blowup, original template wellformedness supplies the actual previous
carrier's closedness; its actual admissibility supplies centre containment
in that carrier. The genuine strict-submanifold theorem transports the
inductively obtained pure regular dimension through THIS same blowup,
both over and away from its actual centre. This is exactly the next
carrier field. No step is deleted or replaced, and the proof makes no
compactness assumption.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ONLY original geometry constructs actual pure dimension-one-lower FIRST-good traces]
\label{hir:sharp-original-first-good-trace-pure-lower-dimension}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0088}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-template-history-pure-regular-dimension,hir:sharp-actual-first-good-open-whole-reduced-trace-carriers}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstGoodLowerDimension.exists_original_first_good_trace_dimension}
\leanok
ONLY original smooth pure dimension $d+1$, true original ambient SNC
boundary, whole-boundary density and a genuine finite template history
internally construct the actual FIRST-Z complement open on which EACH
actual reduced trace is a closed smooth carrier of pure dimension $d$.
The same true carrier is used; no lower pure-dimension certificate is
assumed. The conclusion includes $d=0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the actual computed FIRST-good open and true closed smooth traces
from the genuine reduced-trace theorem. At each actual trace point that
producer supplies an actual carrier dimension $k$ and trace dimension
$k-1$. The preceding finite-history theorem independently proves that
SAME actual upper carrier is regular of dimension $d+1$ at the SAME
original point. Uniqueness of actual regular dimension therefore gives
$k=d+1$. Substituting into its actual open trace regularity gives dimension
$d$. This proves pointwise pure lower dimension on the whole computed
open, including empty traces and the zero-dimensional lower case.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine sharp lower data retain actual boundary density and a strictly smaller real invariant input]
\label{hir:sharp-original-first-good-lower-dimension-domain-boundary-density}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0088}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-first-good-trace-pure-lower-dimension,hir:sharp-original-first-good-actual-lower-product-domain,hir:sharp-original-first-good-whole-exceptional-trace-density}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualFirstGoodLowerDimension.exists_original_first_good_lower_dimensional_input}
\leanok
ONLY the same original data construct ONE computed FIRST-good open such
that its TRUE accumulated exceptional family is ambient SNC, EACH actual
lower trace is pure dimension $d$ and genuinely dense outside that WHOLE
boundary, and the TRUE remaining normalized lower product with EMPTY
auxiliary boundary and mark one is an actual invariant-domain input with
bound $d$. Its bound has genuinely dropped from the original pure carrier
dimension $d+1$. No selected open, lower ideal, coherence, nonzero,
dimension, density or domain certificate is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding theorem, genuine remaining-product constructor and actual
exceptional-density constructor each produce the literal open complement
of the SAME independently defined FIRST Z. Equality of the actual open
sets identifies their underlying opens and ALL their real point/ideal
objects. Thus the actual ordered accumulated boundary, actual reduced
trace, actual normalized lower product, proved nonzero and genuine
pure dimension $d$ are on ONE and the SAME domain. Keep the whole genuine
product's wellformedness and nonzero. At every true lower-carrier point,
choose its now-proved actual regular dimension $d$; the dimension bound
$d\le d$ then constructs the actual mark-one domain of bound $d$.
The EMPTY auxiliary boundary remains explicit. Actual ambient-boundary
SNC and lower density do not imply that the lower carrier is transverse
to it. The full ambient strict ORIGINAL boundary family, joint sharp SNC,
higher original invariant descent, FIRST-Z-erasing and recursive drivers,
exceptional tauD and termination remain separate unmet producers.
\end{proof}
