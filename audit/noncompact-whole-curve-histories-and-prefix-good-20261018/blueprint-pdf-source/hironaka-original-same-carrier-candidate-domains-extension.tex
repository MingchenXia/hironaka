\section{Actual same-carrier candidate geometry in every carrier bound}

Candidates below are not declared admissible histories. Their current
coherence, nonzero germs and entire smooth carrier/boundary geometry are
constructed from a genuine reference TEST. The carrier bound is arbitrary;
in particular a smooth pullback of a curve is not called a curve.

\begin{theorem}[Same actual carrier and ordered boundary supply every candidate domain]
\label{hir:original-arbitrary-bound-same-carrier-candidate-domains}
\uses{hir:original-modeled-untested-curve-domain-offboundary,hir:original-untested-controlled-smooth-entire-step}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalSameCarrierCandidateDomains.same_carrier_candidate_domains}
\leanok
Let $M$ be a reference record, let $K,B$ have the SAME actual initial
carrier and ordered boundary, and let $B$ have genuine DOMAIN$b$.
Suppose the actual $K$ transforms on every genuine $M$ TEST have
DOMAIN$b$. Construct DOMAIN$b$ of the actual raw controlled transforms
of $B$ on every such TEST. No $B$ TEST or candidate admissibility is
assumed, and the ideal and positive mark of $B$ may differ from $K$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on actual tower constructors. At a successor, read the real
reference next carrier and ordered boundary from the independently
known $K$ domain. Actual strict carrier and ordered-boundary transform
formulas depend only on their initial carrier and ordered boundary;
thus they are exactly the candidate's next carrier and boundary.
The genuine smooth-strict-carrier controlled transform theorem gives
coherence of the candidate ideal without candidate admissibility.
The genuine nonzero-along transform theorem gives its nonzero germs.
Reference geometry supplies smoothness, SNC, transversality and the
same actual dimension bound $b$; the candidate's positive mark is
unchanged. This constructs every field of its real domain. The
argument does not use $b=1$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original positive companions have every actual bar domain on any original TEST]
\label{hir:original-arbitrary-bound-companion-bar-actual-domains}
\uses{hir:original-arbitrary-bound-same-carrier-candidate-domains,bcalc:inv-domain-stable,bcalc:companion-domain}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalSameCarrierCandidateDomains.original_companion_bar_domains}
\leanok
For ONLY an original DOMAIN$b$ record $M$ in positive ambient dimension $n$
and any positive integer $q$,
construct DOMAIN$b$ of the actual bar of its actual positive companion
on EVERY genuine original $M$ TEST. No companion or bar TEST is
supplied or inferred.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual original companion construction gives its initial domain;
real homogenization/coefficient geometry gives the bar domain. The
actual carrier and entire ordered boundary of the bar companion are
exactly those of $M$. Genuine original TEST stability independently
gives every current DOMAIN$b$ of $M$. Apply the preceding theorem with
$K=M$ to obtain the actual bar-companion domains on all original tests.
All geometry inputs are internal constructions from $M,q$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Independent raw bar companions have whole smooth current records]
\label{hir:original-arbitrary-bound-independent-companion-bar-smooth-current}
\uses{hir:original-arbitrary-bound-companion-bar-actual-domains,hir:original-untested-modeled-controlled-smooth-history,hir:submersion-relative-parts-actual-whole-companion}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalSameCarrierCandidateDomains.original_companion_bar_smooth_current}
\leanok
For ONLY such original data, ANY genuine original upper TEST and a real
smooth Cartesian pullback, the independently computed source bar
positive-companion raw controlled record is the ENTIRE pullback of
the actual target bar-companion record at the current stage. Their
whole cosupports have both preimage directions. The target candidate
has real DOMAIN$b$, and the source candidate has the actual shifted
DOMAIN$b+(m-n)$. No candidate TEST or current-member certificate is
an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First construct all target candidate bar domains by the preceding
theorem. These actual coherence and smooth-strict-carrier facts supply
the genuine raw controlled smooth history dictionary. Actual smooth
companion pullback and actual bar pullback, independently justified by
the original coherent nonzero record, identify ALL FOUR initial
fields with the independently computed source constructor. Substitute
that initial identity into the whole controlled and cosupport
preimage dictionaries. The target domain was already constructed;
true smooth domain pullback and the proved whole current equality
supply the actual shifted source domain. No candidate admissibility
has been used to obtain these whole records.
\end{proof}

\end{proof}
