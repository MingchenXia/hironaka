\section{Controlled restriction to a smaller strict carrier}

Marked ideals on a smooth carrier represent ideals of its structure sheaf;
their ambient representatives are compared by actual restricted holomorphic
spans. The controlled transform of \cite[Definition~2.2]{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
uses the same exceptional power for two records with the same marking.
The following comparison requires admissibility only for the full reference
record. The smaller carrier need not contain the centre, and its history
need not be a test history.

\begin{theorem}[One-sided controlled restriction from a genuine admissible reference]
\label{hir:original-controlled-reference-restriction-step}
\uses{hir:restricted-calculus,hir:marked-operations-along-step,hir:geo-transform-basic,bcalc:strict-geo,bcalc:function-transform,mc:transform-along-pullback}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalControlledReferenceRestriction.restrictLe_geoTransform_of_admissible_reference}
\leanok
Let $B=(X,N,E,I,d)$ and $K=(X,S,F,A,d)$ be actual marked records.
Assume that $N$ is closed smooth, $I$ is coherent, $S$ is closed,
$S\subseteq N$, and $A|_S\subseteq I|_S$ in the literal restricted
holomorphic-span sense. Let $C$ be admissible for $B$, and let
$\pi:Y\to X$ be an actual blowup of $C$. Put
$N'=\overline{\pi^{-1}(N\setminus C)}$ and
$S'=\overline{\pi^{-1}(S\setminus C)}$, and let $I'$ and $A'$ be the
actual controlled ideals of these same records. Then
\[
                 A'|_{S'}\subseteq I'|_{S'}.
\]
The marking may be zero. No admissibility for $K$, containment $C\subseteq S$,
smoothness of $S'$ or coherence of $A'$ is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set $L=(\pi^*\mathcal I_C)^d$. The definition of the restricted colon gives
$LA'|_{S'}\subseteq(\pi^*A)|_{S'}$ without any coherence or divisibility
hypothesis: each generating product $u v$, with $u\in L$ and
$v\in A'$, is by the colon definition a restricted holomorphic span of
$\pi^*A$; expand a finite sum of such products and compose its finite
span relations.

Since $S$ is closed, continuity gives $\pi(S')\subseteq S$.
Pulling back the original restricted span relations, with their actual
holomorphic coefficient functions, therefore gives
$(\pi^*A)|_{S'}\subseteq(\pi^*I)|_{S'}$.
The genuine Hadamard divisibility theorem for the admissible reference
$B$ gives $(\pi^*I)|_{N'}=LI'|_{N'}$. Monotonicity of closure gives
$S'\subseteq N'$, so restriction and transitivity yield
\[
                    LA'|_{S'}\subseteq LI'|_{S'}.
\]
The reference controlled ideal $I'$ is coherent, by its genuine
admissibility and coherent numerator.

To cancel $L$, choose at $z\in S'$ a true holomorphic generator $h$ of
$L$ on an open neighbourhood $U$. It vanishes on no nonempty relatively
open subset of $S'$. Indeed every open set meeting $S'$ meets the dense
set $\pi^{-1}(S\setminus C)$; at such a point $L$ is the unit ideal, so
its generator cannot vanish. This argument also covers the zeroth power.
For $f\in A'_z$, the actual colon makes $f$ holomorphic. The displayed
product inclusion writes $hf$ on $S'$ as a finite holomorphic span of
germs of $LI'$. Expand each generating product and express its $L$
factor as $h$ times a genuine holomorphic cofactor. Coherence of $I'$
then collects the other factors into germs $t_i\in I'_z$, giving
$hf=h\sum_i a_i t_i$ on $S'$ near $z$.
The nonzero locus of $h$ is relatively dense in $S'\cap U$;
continuity of the two holomorphic functions extends their equality
from that dense locus to all of $S'$ near $z$. Thus
$f=\sum_i a_i t_i$ on $S'$ near $z$, precisely the required restricted
span. This proves the inclusion at every point without a smaller-record
test or a supplied quotient germ.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Entire original reference histories preserve the one-sided restricted comparison]
\label{hir:original-controlled-reference-restriction-history}
\uses{hir:original-controlled-reference-restriction-step,hir:wellformed-tower,hir:geo-transform-basic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalControlledReferenceRestriction.restrictLe_geoTransformAlong_of_reference_test}
\leanok
Let $B$ be a genuine well-formed original marked record and $K$ any
actual marked record with closed carrier contained in $B$'s carrier,
the same marking, and $K.\mathrm{ideal}|_{K.N}\subseteq
B.\mathrm{ideal}|_{K.N}$. For every genuine original $B$ test history
$T$, the actual current $K$ carrier is closed and contained in the
actual current $B$ carrier, the markings remain equal, and
\[
 (K_T.\mathrm{ideal})|_{K_T.N}
       \subseteq (B_T.\mathrm{ideal})|_{K_T.N}.
\]
This concerns the literal original controlled history of $K$; it neither
assumes nor constructs a $K$ test history.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the genuine blowup-history constructor. For the reflexive
history the four assertions are exactly the original hypotheses.
For a successor, the original test predicate first supplies the genuine
shorter $B$ test history and admissibility of its next centre for the
actual current $B$ record. Original well-formedness along that shorter
test supplies its actual smooth carrier and coherent ideal. The
induction hypothesis supplies the closed smaller carrier, its inclusion,
the same marking and the restricted ideal comparison. Apply the first
theorem to these actual current records and that same actual blowup.
The new smaller carrier is closed because it is a closure. Its inclusion
in the new reference carrier follows by applying preimage and closure
to the old carrier inclusion, after deleting the same centre. The
controlled construction retains both old markings. These four facts
are exactly the induction assertions at the successor. All original
positions and empty steps remain part of the same history.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Reference cosupport on the actual smaller current carrier]
\label{hir:original-controlled-reference-current-cosupport}
\uses{hir:original-controlled-reference-restriction-history,hir:marked-operations-along-step}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalControlledReferenceRestriction.reference_cosupp_inter_carrier_subset}
\leanok
Under the hypotheses of the preceding theorem, at every genuine
original reference test history $T$ one has
\[
                    B_T.\mathrm{cosupp}\cap K_T.N
                    \subseteq K_T.\mathrm{cosupp}.
\]
The smaller-carrier membership is retained explicitly here. It will be
constructed from the actual retained-positive regime of the original
member run, rather than assumed in that original-input consumer.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take a point in the displayed intersection. Every germ of the actual
reference ideal has order at least the current reference marking along
its current carrier. Restrict its genuine maximal-ideal-power witness
to the contained current smaller carrier; the same witness still proves
the same order. The history theorem expresses each germ of the current
smaller ideal on that carrier as a finite holomorphic combination of
these reference germs. Each product and finite sum retains the common
order, so every smaller germ has that order. The markings are equal by
the history theorem, and actual smaller-carrier membership was part of
the given intersection. This is exactly membership in its cosupport.
No converse inclusion or smaller test predicate is used.
\end{proof}
