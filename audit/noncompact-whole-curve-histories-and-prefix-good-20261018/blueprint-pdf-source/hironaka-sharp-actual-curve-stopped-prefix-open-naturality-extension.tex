\section{Stopped terminal identifications and actual open restriction}

\begin{theorem}[Every genuine open prefix flattening preserves the same stopped terminal point]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-prefix-open-terminal-square}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-prefix-terminal-biholomorphism,hir:sharp-actual-stagewise-tower-comparison-unique,hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding-comparison,mc:tower-isomorphism-restrict-append}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedPrefixOpenNaturality.stoppedPrefix_open_terminal_square}
\leanok
Let $T$ be a genuine finite blowup history, $U$ a genuine original base
open, and $k$ at least the literal length of $T$. Any actual full
stagewise open-prefix flattening from the $k$th uniform prefix of
$T|_U$ to the restriction of the $k$th uniform prefix of $T$ has this
ENTIRE terminal square: identifying its image with the SAME original
terminal point gives exactly the underlying terminal point of the
stopped restricted prefix. No desired point formula or terminal
compatibility certificate is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Restrict the actual stopped-prefix whole-history isomorphism of $T$ to
$U$. This is a genuine stagewise isomorphism between the restricted
uniform prefix and the restriction of $T$ followed by its literal empty
identity tail. Its terminal map on underlying points is precisely the
actual stopped terminal identification. Actual concatenation restriction
and actual identity-tail restriction identify the latter history with
$T|_U$ followed by exactly the SAME number of empty identity steps. The
two terminal flattenings in this construction are identities on the
underlying terminal points, and all original positions remain present.

Compose the given actual prefix flattening with those genuine
isomorphisms. Its whole source history is the uniform prefix of $T|_U$,
and its target history is $T|_U$ followed by $k-\operatorname{length}(T)$
identity steps. Genuine open restriction preserves literal length, so
this is exactly the target of the independently constructed stopped
restricted-prefix isomorphism. Real fixed-history universal uniqueness
identifies these TWO entire terminal maps. Evaluate at an arbitrary
actual point and pass to the underlying original terminal manifold.
The true underlying-point identities of open restriction, concatenation
flattening and identity-tail flattening give the claimed exact square.
No projection injectivity or removal of exceptional points is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual unique open-prefix flattening has its terminal square internally]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-prefix-open-terminal-natural}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-prefix-open-terminal-square,hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-open-restriction}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStoppedPrefixOpenNaturality.exists_unique_stoppedPrefix_open_restriction}
\leanok
For ONLY a genuine finite history, original base open and actual stopped
stage, the genuine open-prefix construction internally supplies the
unique full stagewise flattening, together with the exact ENTIRE stopped
terminal square of the preceding theorem. No flattening, open endpoint
identity, terminal map compatibility or numerical certificate is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine uniform-prefix restriction producer constructs its unique
stagewise flattening from actual append restrictions and actual split
restrictions, retaining every original and auxiliary empty position.
Apply the preceding theorem to this internally supplied genuine
flattening and its actual whole-history isomorphism. This proves its
entire terminal square. A competing flattening with both conclusions
is in particular an actual full-history flattening on the SAME two
histories, so the original producer's full uniqueness identifies it
with the constructed one. Thus the complete strengthened existence
and uniqueness follow, without a supplied point-square witness.
\end{proof}
