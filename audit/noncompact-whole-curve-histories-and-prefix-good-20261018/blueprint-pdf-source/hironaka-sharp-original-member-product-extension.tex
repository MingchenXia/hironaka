The literal product of the original member ideals is constructed before
any normal form or sharp defect is claimed. This is the local product

\begin{lemma}[Actual products of original germ ideals retain whole principal membership dictionaries]
\label{hir:sharp-original-member-product-genuine-principal-dictionary}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0087}
\uses{bcalc:member-trace-ideal}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalMemberProduct.idealListProduct,Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalMemberProduct.unit_mem_iff_principal,Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalMemberProduct.mul_mem_iff_principal,Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalMemberProduct.paired_list_product_principal,Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalMemberProduct.restricted_span_iff_principal}
\leanok
The literal finite list product of germ ideals has the unit ideal as
empty product. If each actual factor is generated at a point by a true
holomorphic function, in both whole germ-membership directions, the
actual product is generated by the true product of those functions,
again with both whole membership directions. The actual restriction to
the original carrier retains the same principal span dictionary.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For two true factors, actual product membership is the finite holomorphic
span of products of genuine member germs. Substitute both original
principal membership dictionaries. Every such true product is a
holomorphic multiple of the genuine product generator, by multiplying
the actual coefficients and their two germ equations. Conversely the
actual factor generators belong to their true factors, so their genuine
product belongs to the literal product ideal; substituting that true
generator in its finite spans proves the reverse direction. The unit
case is the literal holomorphic unit ideal generated by one. Induction
on the actual finite factor list gives the whole stated dictionary.
Restricting actual ambient relations and substituting actual generators
in both finite restricted spans gives the last assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}

incident free coordinates. Its actual ambient order is the number of

theorem: it belongs to that power and not to the next. The actual flat
carrier retraction keeps each genuine free coordinate, hence keeps the
whole product as a germ. Its actual restricted order therefore equals
that actual ambient order. The true product generator belongs to its
literal product ideal; any larger claimed ideal order would make that
genuine generator vanish to higher restricted order and contradict the
actual retraction/point-ideal dictionary. Conversely every real product
ideal germ is a holomorphic multiple of that genuine generator, by the
already proved full membership dictionary. Its ambient order at least
the factor count therefore gives every required lower restricted order.
The independently constructed actual quotient injection identifies
this factor count with the true DISTINCT proper restricted germ-ideal
count on this real transverse geometry. Apply these actual statements
on the independently produced original whole transverse neighborhood.
The count at arbitrary bad points remains the genuine quotient count;
this proof does not replace it by indexed incidence there.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual original transverse coordinates construct the true member-product normal form]
\label{hir:sharp-original-member-product-actual-normal-form}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0087}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-member-product-genuine-principal-dictionary,hir:sharp-original-multiboundary-distinct-proper-trace-germs,hir:snc}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalMemberProduct.originalMemberProduct,Hironaka.HironakaSharpOriginalMemberProduct.exists_actual_original_member_product_normal_form}
\leanok
On genuine original transverse SNC geometry, at every actual carrier
point the literal product of ALL original member ideals has a genuine
principal normal form equal to the product of the distinct free carrier
coordinates belonging to the truly incident members. Both whole ideal
membership directions and both original-carrier restricted-span

\begin{proof}

whole original SNC list, with distinct free coordinates for incident
members. Genuine Hadamard division gives the whole original ideal
dictionary for each incident member coordinate. Every nonincident
closed member has its real open complement, where its actual ideal is
the unit ideal. Apply the actual finite product dictionary to these
internally constructed original factor functions. The nonincident
unit factors disappear; the remaining literal product is exactly the
product of the actual incident free coordinates. Actual finite-list
and indexed-product dictionaries identify the same original factors
and no replacements. This proves the ambient and restricted normal
forms for the true original product ideal.
\end{proof}
