\section{Actual recentred polynomial coordinates for a smooth-centre blowup}

\begin{lemma}[True monomial recentering retains the tangent block]
\label{hir:actual-smooth-centre-monomial-recentring}
\uses{bcalc:monomial-lift}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualSmoothCentreBlowupCoordinates.actual_smooth_centre_monomial_recentre}
\leanok
Let the genuine monomial blowup chart of the coordinate centre
$\mathbb C^k\times0\subset\mathbb C^n$ have pivot $j$. Suppose the
actual vector $v$ has $v_j=0$ and every tangent coordinate $v_i=0$
for $i<k$. The original monomial map at $w$ is exactly the actual
analytic polynomial blowup map with normal variables $i\ge k$ and
parameters $b_i=v_i$, evaluated at $w-v$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In a tangent coordinate, the original map is $w_i$ and the recentred
expression is $w_i-v_i=w_i$. At the pivot, both expressions are $w_j$
because $v_j=0$. At any other normal coordinate, the recentred
expression is $(w_j-v_j)(v_i+w_i-v_i)=w_jw_i$, exactly the original
monomial coordinate. Equality in every coordinate proves the vector
identity, including empty tangent blocks.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The original full blowup and centre regularity construct actual smooth-centre coordinates]
\label{hir:actual-smooth-centre-blowup-coordinate-normal-form}
\uses{hir:actual-smooth-centre-monomial-recentring,hir:actual-monomial-local-inverse-complex-injective,bcalc:snc-chart,ana:local-holomorphic-inverse}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualSmoothCentreBlowupCoordinates.exists_actual_smooth_centre_blowup_coordinates}
\leanok
Let $\pi:Y\to X$ satisfy the original full blowup predicate, and let
its same centre $C$ be regular of dimension $k$ at $\pi(z)$. Then there
are genuine target and source local holomorphic coordinate equivalences
$\Phi$ and $\Theta$, both centred at zero, an actual pivot $j\ge k$,
and actual parameters $b$ with $b_j=0$ and all tangent $b_i=0$, such
that $\Phi$ flattens precisely the original centre to
$\mathbb C^k\times0$ and
\[
 \Phi(e_{\pi(z)}(\pi(u)))
 =\pi^{\mathrm{normal}}_{k,j,b}(\Theta(e_z(u)))
\]
throughout the entire source coordinate neighborhood. That neighborhood
maps into the genuine target flattening domain. All coordinate source
inclusions and the whole centre-flattening equivalence are retained.
No normal-form, centre-compatibility or HS certificate is a premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Unpack the original regularity to obtain a true centred flattening
$\Phi$ of that very centre. The original full blowup universal property
gives a genuine normal monomial chart with pivot $j\ge k$, its
holomorphic lift, and an actual local inverse $W$ near $z$. Its complete
square is the original map expressed in the same flattening, and the
lift composed with $W$ is the identity on a genuine open neighborhood.
The existing injective complex differential theorem for this actual
monomial local inverse applies in every centre dimension. The actual
holomorphic inverse function theorem, followed by translation by
$-W(z)$, therefore gives a genuine centred local equivalence $\Theta$
whose expression is $W(u)-W(z)$ on a smaller actual open neighborhood.
At $z$, the monomial square equals the zero vector. Every tangent row
of that square is $W(z)_i$, and its pivot row is $W(z)_j$, so these
coordinates are all zero. Set $b=W(z)$. The preceding recentering
identity turns the actual monomial square on the entire smaller
neighborhood into the displayed actual polynomial square. The local
inverse construction already places every original image in the same
target flattening domain. Retain, without altering it, the original
equivalence describing membership in $C$ in those coordinates.
\end{proof}

This is a coordinate producer for the original blowup along its genuine
smooth centre. Compatibility of an original analytic ideal with that
centre and the general positive-dimensional Bennett inequality remain
separate mathematical assertions.
