\begin{theorem}[True stopped-open maps identify the entire conventional strict curves]

\leanok

of every original CLOSED set from its actual finite recipe to the same
uniform prefix, retaining all real empty suffix positions. For each
positive global stage, on its ENTIRE projection preimage of the actual
stopped base open, the already constructed prefix-to-endpoint
biholomorphism identifies the conventional strict curve of that SAME
original prefix with the conventional strict curve of the full endpoint.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual stopped full-history isomorphism over the original identity
to the old finite recipe followed by its true identity-empty suffix.
Whole strict-set transport gives the preimage identity. Iterated strict
transforms of a closed set are closed, so every identity-empty step leaves
this closed transformed set unchanged. Normalize the genuine suffix,
without deleting its positions, to obtain the first assertion.

Take an actual point over the stopped base open. Its very membership
supplies a genuine recipe neighbourhood whose actual finite length is no
larger than the requested stage. The entire projection preimage is the
actual local inclusion range, so choose an actual local representative.
The proved full local/global history dictionary identifies the finite
global iterated strict set there with the local uniform-prefix strict
set. Apply the preceding stopped strict-set identity and then the genuine
old endpoint local conventional-strict dictionary. The constructed
stopped-open map's exact local formula identifies these SAME points.

Finally, the original member-product DOMAIN1 and actual global TEST,
proved before this comparison, identify its whole transformed carrier
with the conventional strict transform of its entire original projection.
Its carrier is also the iterated strict transform of the original curve.
Thus the preceding local comparison concerns the existing conventional
strict curves, including all exceptional points, on the ENTIRE stopped
open. No equality of projections alone is used to choose a history map.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact sets acquire actual open neighbourhoods with all genuine later tails empty]

actual finite subcover of K supplies a positive stage and hence its

ENTIRE stage projection preimage of this open. The exact image identity
for the growing sequence's genuinely chosen next centre transfers this

a different centre or supplying an avoidance certificate.

The existing actual finite-tail constructor retains the genuine stage
maps and blowup proofs. Its whole append identity identifies the prefix
followed by this tail with the recorded later stage. Consequently the
proved finite-tail avoidance induction applies to EVERY tail length.
The genuine restriction-admissibility dictionary turns this geometric

is removed, and no canonicality or numerical invariant hypothesis enters.
The chosen prefix-to-endpoint map and all its strict-curve and ordered

\end{proof}
