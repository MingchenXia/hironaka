\section{Actual normal crossings branch compatibility}

The local normal crossings branch construction produces genuine SNC
lists on actual open coordinate domains.  The following identities and
actual-sequence constructions remove dependence on branch labels and
make actual divisor transforms commute with open restriction.  These
are compatibility ingredients; comparison and descent of canonical
centres under changes of coordinates remain separate obligations.

\begin{lemma}[Intrinsic ideals ignore branch labels]
\label{hir:nc-intrinsic-ideal-member-independence}
\uses{hir:monomial-part-ambient}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCompatibility.monomialPart_eq_of_mem_iff,Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCompatibility.nonmonomialPart_eq_of_mem_iff}
\leanok
If two finite boundary lists $E,F$ have the same members, the actual
monomial and nonmonomial ideals of every ideal $I$ agree for these lists.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The monomial ideal consists of holomorphic germs $g$ such that, for
every member $H$ through the given point $y$, the germ $g$ lies in
$I_H^{e_H(I,y)}$.  This condition depends only on membership in the
family of sets, not on the index or ordering.  For each index in $F$,
its member occurs at some index in $E$, so the latter condition implies
the former with the identical carrier, exponent and power ideal.
The reverse implication uses the reverse membership equality.
This proves equality of every complete ideal stalk.  The nonmonomial
ideal is the actual colon $(I:\mathcal M_E(I))$, so this proved equality
of monomial ideals proves equality of their nonmonomial ideals as well.
The argument requires no SNC, coherence or nonvanishing premise.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Relabelling a genuine admissible blowup sequence]
\label{hir:nc-actual-sequence-permutation}
\uses{hir:admissible-sequence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCompatibility.exists_permuted_admissible_sequence}
\leanok
Let $f:Y\to X$ be an actual finite admissible blowup sequence with
initial list $E_0$, accumulated final list $E$, and centres over $Z$.
For every permutation $F_0$ of $E_0$, there is an actual final list
$F$ which is a permutation of $E$, such that the same actual $f$
is an admissible blowup sequence from $F_0$, with centres over $Z$
and final list $F$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual finite blowup sequence.  The empty sequence is
the identity; take $F=F_0$.  For the last genuine blowup
$\pi:Y\to Y_1$ with actual centre $C$, induction relabels the
preceding sequence with final list $F_1$, a permutation of $E_1$.
For each index in $F_1$, choose the identical actual member
in $E_1$ and assign its adapted coordinate set.  Keep the actual
centre coordinates and the actual holomorphic chart unchanged.
Every adapted equation and singleton-coordinate condition is then
the identical old condition, proving normal crossings of the same
centre $C$ with $F_1$.  Keep the same actual blowup, preceding projection, and
containment of the centre over $Z$.  Their actual step constructor
gives a genuine admissible sequence with final list
\[
 F=\bigl(\overline{\pi^{-1}(H\setminus C)}\bigr)_{H\in F_1}
       \mathbin{+\!+}[\pi^{-1}C].
\]
Applying the strict-transform set function to the permutation of
$E_1,F_1$ and adjoining the identical exceptional member proves
that this actual $F$ is a permutation of the actual final $E$.
The projection and every geometric centre and blowup were preserved
in the construction.  No comparison of independently selected
canonical centres is asserted.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Actual finite admissible sequences preserve SNC]
\label{hir:nc-actual-sequence-preserves-snc}
\uses{hir:admissible-sequence}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCompatibility.isSNCDivisor_of_admissible_sequence}
\leanok
An actual finite admissible blowup sequence starting at an SNC list
has an SNC accumulated final list.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual sequence.  For the identity the accumulated
list is the given initial SNC list.  At a genuine last blowup,
induction makes the preceding accumulated list SNC, and actual
admissibility makes its centre have normal crossings with that list.
The single-blowup SNC coordinate theorem therefore proves SNC
for the list of actual strict transforms followed by the actual
exceptional preimage.  This is exactly the accumulated list in
the finite-sequence construction, proving the assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual divisor blowup transforms commute with open restriction]
\label{hir:nc-actual-divisor-blowup-open-square}
\uses{hir:actual-divisor-open-germ-exponent-dictionary,hir:base-change-open}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCompatibility.totalTransform_restrictDivisor}
\leanok
For an actual blowup $\pi:Y\to X$ with genuine smooth centre, an
actual effective divisor $D$ on $X$, and an open subset $U\subset X$,
let $V=\pi^{-1}U$ and $\pi_U:V\to U$ be the actual restricted blowup.
There is an equality of genuine effective divisors
\[
 \pi_U^*(D|_U)=(\pi^*D)|_V.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original smooth-centre blowup and its actual open restriction are
genuine blowups, hence their exceptional loci have empty interior.
Their actual pullback ideals are therefore invertible and give the
stated effective divisors.  Write the actual inclusions as
$\iota_U:U\to X$ and $\iota_V:V\to Y$.  Their maps satisfy the actual
commuting square
$\iota_U\circ\pi_U=\pi\circ\iota_V$.
Pullback composition for the coherent ideal $I_D$ gives
\[
 \pi_U^*(\iota_U^*I_D)
  =(\iota_U\circ\pi_U)^*I_D
  =(\pi\circ\iota_V)^*I_D
  =\iota_V^*(\pi^*I_D).
\]
These are the complete actual divisor ideals, not just equations of
supports or chosen generators.  An effective divisor is determined
by its invertible ideal, so the ideal equality proves the asserted
actual divisor equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual transformed divisors retain the full branch dictionary]
\label{hir:nc-actual-sequence-transformed-divisor-dictionary}
\uses{hir:nc-actual-sequence-preserves-snc,hir:locally-finite-original-component-product}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaNormalCrossingsCompatibility.exists_transformed_component_dictionary}
\leanok
Let $D$ be an actual effective divisor supported on the initial SNC
list of a genuine finite admissible sequence $f:Y\to X$.  Its actual
total transform $D'$ has ideal $f^*I_D$, and its genuine connected
final-boundary component family is locally finite.  One actual
coefficient function on these carriers equals the exponent of $D'$
at every point of each carrier.  Near every point of $Y$ there is a
finite positive list of distinct genuine carriers whose ideal-power
product agrees with the complete ideal stalk of $D'$ at every point
of that neighbourhood.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct $D'$ by induction on the actual finite blowup sequence.
For the identity use $D$; coherence gives the actual identity pullback
equality.  For a genuine last blowup $\pi$, pull back the preceding
actual effective divisor.  Its exceptional locus has empty interior,
so this pullback ideal is invertible.  Coherent pullback composition
identifies its ideal with the pullback of the original $I_D$ under
the actual composite projection.  Its support is the preimage of the
preceding actual support.  At every actual step, a preimage point on an old boundary
member either lies over the centre, hence belongs to the actual
exceptional preimage, or belongs to the preimage of that member
minus the centre, hence to its actual strict transform.  Induction
therefore puts $f^{-1}\bigcup E_0$ inside the union of the final
accumulated boundary.  This proves the required support containment
of $D'$.  The preceding SNC induction supplies a genuine SNC final
list.  Apply the genuine SNC locally-finite divisor theorem to this
actual $D'$ and actual final list.  Its produced coefficients and
positive finite lists give precisely all asserted original
$D'$-exponent and complete-stalk identities.  This assumes an actual
geometric sequence, but supplies no factorization or coefficient
certificate and makes no claim that a canonical sequence has yet
been chosen for a general normal crossings carrier.
\end{proof}
