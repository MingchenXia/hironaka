\begin{lemma}[Actual original kept remainder is the remainder of the genuinely empty-position padded contact record]
\label{hir:original-kept-contact-remainder-actual-empty-padding}
\uses{hir:zero-contact-remainder-literal-history-dictionary,hir:zero-contact-tail-empty-history}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaOriginalContactChoiceLowerHistories.actual_kept_contact_remainder_padded}
\leanok
For ANY actual original $K$, function $w$, genuine tower prefix $T_0$ and
actual stage open, let $A_0$ be its true contact record with empty INITIAL
boundary. Pad $A_0$ by $\#K.E$ empty entries, preserving its WHOLE actual
new exceptional list, to get $A$. Then the ACTUAL kept-position remainder
of $K$ is exactly $\operatorname{remainder}_{\#K.E}(A)$ as an ENTIRE marked
record. No geometry, test or desired record equality is an input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The previously proved literal dictionary identifies the original kept
remainder with the true remainder of $A_0$ padded by $\#K.E$ empty entries.
For the padded parent $A$, its carrier, ideal and mark are those of $A_0$,
so its actual selected components and remaining carrier are identical.
Dropping its first $\#K.E$ boundary entries drops EXACTLY the just-added
empty list and leaves the SAME whole exceptional list. The remainder
replaces that initial block by the very same empty block. Thus all four
marked fields agree, including the full ordered boundary with multiplicities.
\end{proof}

separate obligations.
\end{proof}
