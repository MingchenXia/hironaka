\begin{theorem}[True original good points remain good on any actual open restriction]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-original-good-open-restriction}
\uses{hir:snc-invariance}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalGoodIsomorphism.originalGoodLocus_preimage_val}
\leanok
For ANY original carrier $W$, finite ordered family $E$, actual open $U$
and point $x\in U$, membership of $x$ in the ORIGINAL whole-family good
locus implies membership of its true subtype point in the good locus
of $W\cap U$ and the ENTIRE ordered preimage list $E|_U$. No SNC or good
membership certificate for the restricted family is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Outside $W$ the implication is the literal carrier preimage dictionary.
At a good point in $W$, take its real pointwise normal-crossings witness.
The true open-subtype coordinate theorem pulls back its whole
option-indexed adapted family. Reindex finite positions by the actual
length-map equality, retaining every ordered position. The entire get
of the mapped list equals the subtype preimage of the same original
get, before applying the witness. The same actual incident-member
identity transports its cardinal-one assertions. The carrier remains
the NONE member and all original members remain their corresponding
SOME positions. This constructs the actual restricted good witness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Whole saturated chart ranges descend genuine local isomorphisms]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-global-projection-isomorphism-descent}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-endpoint-saturated-local-range,hir:sharp-actual-original-global-complex-endpoint}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGlobalGoodIsomorphism.gluedProjection_isIsomorphismOver}
\leanok
For an actual endpoint-history atlas covering its genuine base, suppose
its literal finite local projections are isomorphisms over the actual
traces of a base subset $G$. Then the already constructed global
projection is an ORIGINAL isomorphism over the WHOLE $G$. These genuine
local isomorphisms are the inputs of this geometric descent lemma;
no global bijectivity or differential certificate is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For injectivity, two actual global endpoints over the same $x\in G$
belong to the SAME saturated chart range for any actual base window
containing $x$. Represent both in that genuine chart. The exact full
projection square identifies their two finite-history images with the
same subtype point. The local actual good-locus injectivity identifies
the representatives, hence the original global endpoints.

For surjectivity choose an actual base window containing any $x\in G$.
The local isomorphism gives a genuine endpoint over its subtype point;
include this point in the already constructed quotient. The exact
projection square proves its global image is $x$.

For the differential statement represent any global endpoint over $G$
in a genuine chart. The local projection is nonexceptional at that
representative. The actual open inclusion has identity differential,
so the composite of that local projection with the base inclusion is
nonexceptional. The ENTIRE projection square identifies this map with
the global projection composed with the actual holomorphic chart
inclusion. The genuine exceptional-locus chain rule implies that an
exceptional global point would give an exceptional point of this same
composite, a contradiction. Thus the original differential is
invertible at EVERY point over $G$, on the SAME actual map.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[ONLY original curve data give the global good-locus isomorphism]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-original-global-good-isomorphism}

ORIGINAL isomorphism over the ENTIRE original good locus. Its entire
exceptional image is therefore contained in the true original bad

\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original-only constructor supplies its real covering atlas and,
on the SAME original first map and literal terminating tail, every
retained finite-history isomorphism over its own original good locus.
The first theorem places every trace of a global original good point
inside that actual local good locus. Restrict each local bijection
and nonexceptionality assertion to this actual trace, obtaining all
inputs of the second theorem internally. Its genuine descent proves
the whole global isomorphism. If an exceptional point projected into
the original good locus, that conclusion would contradict its
nonexceptionality. The exact complement dictionary identifies this
with containment of the entire exceptional image in the original bad
locus. No new history or inverse witness is retrofitted to the old
existential endpoint. A global finite blowup schedule, conventional
strict-closure identity and the general sharp driver remain separate.
\end{proof}
