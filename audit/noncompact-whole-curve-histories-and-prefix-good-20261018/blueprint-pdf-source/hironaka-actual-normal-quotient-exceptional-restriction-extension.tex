\section{Actual normal initial coefficients in genuine exceptional quotients}

\begin{theorem}[Actual normal-power membership identifies the canonical homogeneous polynomial]
\label{hir:actual-normal-power-canonical-homogeneous-polynomial}
\uses{hir:actual-relative-coefficient-polynomial-truncations,hir:relative-normal-basis-actual-coefficient-separation,hir:actual-normal-homogeneous-polynomial}
Let $g$ be an actual original ambient holomorphic germ belonging to the
whole original normal ideal power $N^q$. Its genuine normal Taylor
polynomial $T_{q+1}(g)$ is homogeneous of normal degree $q$.
Consequently the actual polynomial
$P_q(g)=\operatorname{homogeneousComponent}_q(T_{q+1}(g))$ equals
$T_{q+1}(g)$, and $g-\operatorname{normalPolynomialEval}(P_q(g))$
belongs to the whole original power $N^{q+1}$.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalQuotientExceptionalRestriction.actualNormalTaylorPolynomial_isHomogeneous_of_mem_normal_pow,Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalQuotientExceptionalRestriction.actualNormalHomogeneousPolynomial_eq_truncation_of_mem_normal_pow,Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalQuotientExceptionalRestriction.actual_normal_homogeneousComponent_remainder}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine Taylor remainder is in $N^{q+1}\subseteq N^q$.
Since $g\in N^q$, subtracting that remainder shows that evaluation
of the whole finite Taylor polynomial lies in $N^q$. Actual original
normal-power coefficient separation therefore kills every coefficient
of normal degree below $q$ as an entire tangent germ. The original
Taylor polynomial's proved high-coefficient identity kills every
coefficient of degree at least $q+1$. Thus every nonzero supported
coefficient has normal degree exactly $q$, which is ordinary
homogeneity for the genuine normal polynomial. Its degree-$q$
homogeneous component is itself. Substitution in the original genuine
Taylor remainder proves the last whole-ideal membership.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The actual blowup fixes every tangent coefficient and factors the whole homogeneous polynomial]
\label{hir:actual-normal-polynomial-blowup-factorization}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality,hir:relative-normal-entire-germ-retraction,hir:relative-normal-basis-actual-derivation-evaluation}
Use the actual normal block, $j\in\{0,\ldots,t-1\}$ and $e=d+j$.
The genuine original analytic blowup pullback $B$ fixes
$\mathrm{tangentPullback}(a)$ for every original tangent germ $a$.
For every actual homogeneous normal polynomial $P$ of degree $q$
with genuine tangent-germ coefficients,
\[
 B(\operatorname{normalPolynomialEval}(P))
 =x_e^q\operatorname{eval}_2(\mathrm{tangentPullback},r)(P),
 \qquad r_j=1,\quad r_i=b_i+x_i\ (i\ne j).
\]
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalQuotientExceptionalRestriction.actualAnalyticBlowupPullback_tangentPullback,Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalQuotientExceptionalRestriction.actualAnalyticBlowupPullback_normalPolynomial_homogeneous}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual polynomial blowup fixes each tangent coordinate at every
actual point. Hence its composition with the original tangent
projection is exactly that projection. Genuine holomorphic
representatives show that composition by this map fixes every entire
original tangent coefficient germ. Apply finite polynomial evaluation
under the actual ring homomorphism $B$. The proved original normal
coordinate formulas are $B(x_j)=x_e$ and
$B(x_i)=x_e(b_i+x_i)$ for $i\ne j$. Thus every normal variable
acquires one factor $x_e$, with the stated remaining entry. Expand the
finite polynomial into its supported coefficient monomials. Each
exponent has degree $q$, so its genuine product has precisely the
factor $x_e^q$. Factoring this from the finite sum proves the entire
original germ equality with the same actual tangent coefficients.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every actual normalized quotient has the canonical entire exceptional restriction]
\label{hir:actual-normal-quotient-canonical-exceptional-restriction}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-power-canonical-homogeneous-polynomial,hir:actual-normal-polynomial-blowup-factorization,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-exceptional-quotient-formal-dictionary}
Let $g\in N^q$ be an original ambient holomorphic germ, and let $H$
be any actual original normalized quotient with the genuine product
identity $x_e^qH=B(g)$. For the internally constructed canonical
polynomial $P_q(g)$ from the first theorem,
\[
 H-\operatorname{eval}_2(\mathrm{tangentPullback},r)(P_q(g))
 \in (x_e)
\]
in the whole original analytic germ ring.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalQuotientExceptionalRestriction.actual_analytic_normal_quotient_initial_restriction}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $D$ for the displayed actual finite dehomogenized polynomial
evaluation. The original remainder $R=g-\operatorname{normalPolynomialEval}(P_q(g))$
lies in $N^{q+1}$. The genuine blowup sends the whole original normal
ideal to $(x_e)$ and its $(q+1)$st power to $(x_e^{q+1})$.
Therefore $B(R)=x_e^{q+1}K$ for an actual original holomorphic germ
$K$. The preceding true homogeneous factorization and the genuine
product identity give $B(R)=x_e^q(H-D)$. The actual coordinate germ
$x_e$ is nonzero, as certified by its genuine nonzero coordinate
derivative. The original holomorphic germ ring is a domain, so cancel
the nonzero factor $x_e^q$ and obtain $H-D=x_eK$. This is exactly
membership in the whole original principal ideal $(x_e)$. No
restriction equality, convergence certificate or formal nonzero-point
evaluation was supplied.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Only original normal-power membership produces the quotient and its canonical restriction]
\label{hir:actual-normal-initial-quotient-exists-with-restriction}
\uses{hir:actual-normal-quotient-canonical-exceptional-restriction,hir:actual-analytic-blowup-exceptional-quotient-formal-dictionary}
From the original input $g\in N^q$ alone, there exists an actual
original holomorphic germ $H$ with both the true product
$x_e^qH=B(g)$ and the entire canonical exceptional restriction of the
preceding theorem.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualNormalQuotientExceptionalRestriction.exists_actual_analytic_normal_initial_quotient}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The original normal ideal equals the actual selected-coordinate ideal
for the same normal block. The existing genuine analytic exceptional
division theorem, applied to its whole $q$th power membership,
constructs the original holomorphic quotient and true product identity.
Apply the preceding proved canonical restriction theorem to that
internally produced quotient. Both required conclusions follow from
the original input, with no supplied output certificate.
\end{proof}

The canonical polynomial is the literal degree-$q$ homogeneous
component of the original $(q+1)$-truncation, matching the separate
actual normal homogeneous-component producer by definition. The
general positive-dimensional permissible-centre Bennett theorem,
coefficient-cosupport compatibility and subsequent whole-ideal HS
composition remain separate and open.
