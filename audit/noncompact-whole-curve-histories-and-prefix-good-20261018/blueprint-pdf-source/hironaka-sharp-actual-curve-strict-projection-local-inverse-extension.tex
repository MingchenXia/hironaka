\section{A genuine local inverse of the actual strict projection on a smooth curve}

The first-step exponent comparison requires actual raw pullback order
preservation on the curve even above the centre. An off-centre ambient
biholomorphism alone cannot supply this. Construct the genuine local inverse
along the two actual carriers first. This uses the real monomial blowup
charts behind \cite[Proposition 3.13 and Remark 12.6]{bierstone-milman97-invent-math};
it does not assume the desired curve isomorphism or an order certificate.

\begin{lemma}[The genuine zero-centre monomial chart is the identity on the actual curve plane]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-zero-centre-monomial-curve-plane-identity}
\uses{bcalc:monomial-lift,bcalc:strict-submanifold}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStrictProjectionLocalInverse.actual_monomial_on_curve_plane_eq_self}
\leanok
For the genuine monomial blowup map with centre dimension zero, if the
chosen actual chart index $j$ satisfies $j<1$ and $w$ belongs to the
actual coordinate curve plane $\mathbb C^1\times0$, then $\sigma_j(w)=w$.
This is a literal equality of the actual coordinate vectors, including
$w=0$ and ambient dimension one.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual inequality forces the index to be zero. At that coordinate
the genuine monomial map keeps $w_j$. Every other coordinate has index
at least one and is zero by the true curve-plane membership; its product
with $w_j$ remains zero. Coordinate extensionality proves the equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual discrete-centre smooth-curve strict projection has a real local inverse along its carriers]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-strict-projection-local-inverse}
\source{bierstone-milman97-invent-math:page-0087}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-zero-centre-monomial-curve-plane-identity,bcalc:strict-submanifold,bcalc:monomial-lift}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveStrictProjectionLocalInverse.exists_actual_curve_strict_projection_local_inverse}
\leanok
Let $\pi:Y\to X$ be an ACTUAL blowup with $C\subset N$, where $N$
is genuinely closed smooth and regular of dimension one at all its
points, and $C$ is genuinely regular of dimension zero at all its points.
Let $N'$ be the ACTUAL geometric strict transform, and let $z\in N'$
lie above $C$. These original geometric inputs internally construct an
ambient map $s:X\to Y$, holomorphic near $\pi z$, with $s(\pi z)=z$,
such that near $\pi z$ EVERY actual $x\in N$ satisfies
$s(x)\in N'$ and $\pi s(x)=x$, and near $z$ EVERY actual $y\in N'$
satisfies $s(\pi y)=y$. No selected chart, local inverse, curve model,
pullback ideal identity or order-preservation certificate is input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual nested flattening at $\pi z$ simultaneously gives the true centre
plane of dimension zero and the true carrier plane of dimension one.
The genuine blowup local monomial inverse has some actual chart index
$j$, a true open neighborhood of $z$, and holomorphic coordinate map
$W$ with its genuine monomial lift $\ell$. Actual strict-carrier
membership forces $j<1$ and $W(z)$ into the real curve plane. The first
lemma therefore makes the monomial map the identity on this plane.
The genuine equality $\ell(W(y))=y$ and
$\pi\ell(w)=\Phi^{-1}(\sigma_j(w))$ give, for every nearby actual
strict-carrier point, the literal identity
$W(y)=\Phi(\pi y)$. At $z$, nested centering yields $W(z)=0$.

The actual monomial domain is open and contains this real zero vector.
Use its genuine lifted-open model to extend its lift to an ambient map
near zero; outside that domain choose the fixed actual point $z$.
Its coordinate map is the identity near zero, so the real lifted-open
holomorphic criterion and the actual lift prove holomorphicity there.
Compose this genuine map with the actual flattening coordinates to
obtain $s$. The already proved equality at $z$ gives $s(\pi z)=z$.
For nearby actual carrier points, the first lemma and BOTH true inverse
flattening laws imply $\pi s(x)=x$.

True centre regularity of dimension zero supplies an actual neighborhood
on which $C$ is the singleton $\pi z$. A nearby carrier point outside
$C$ lifts into $N'$ by the genuine off-centre strict-transform membership
dictionary. A point inside $C$ is precisely $\pi z$, whose lift is the
already known $z\in N'$. Thus the entire actual carrier neighborhood is
mapped into $N'$. Finally the independently proved nearby identity
$W(y)=\Phi(\pi y)$ and the genuine monomial lift inverse law give
$s(\pi y)=y$ for EVERY nearby strict-carrier point, including points over
$C$. These are both actual inverse equations along the actual carriers,
not a replacement ambient inverse asserted at exceptional points.

Relative germ/order transport, actual contact-order decrease, mixed
incident-member chronological factors, the separate $\tau_D$ phase,
full sharp $\Sigma$ and termination remain subsequent consumers.
\end{proof}
