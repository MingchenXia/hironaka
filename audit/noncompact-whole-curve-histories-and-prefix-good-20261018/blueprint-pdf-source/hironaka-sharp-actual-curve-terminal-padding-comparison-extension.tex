\section{Actual terminal sharp-curve recipes with unequal stopping lengths}

The histories in this chapter retain their actual literal difference
recipes. A terminal geometry witness alone does not determine such a
recipe. Each empty padding position is a genuine blowup and remains in
the ordered exceptional list. We compare those real histories after
padding to a common length and prove that the resulting terminal map
does not depend on that length.

\begin{theorem}[Literal difference recipes concatenate exactly]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-difference-recipe-concatenation}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-curve-literal-ambient-difference-history-order-bound,hir:geo-transform-basic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveTerminalPaddingComparison.actual_difference_recipe_append_iff}
\leanok
For actual towers $P$, $S$, $R$, the appended tail $SR$ is a literal
difference recipe on $P$ if and only if $S$ is such a recipe on $P$ and
$R$ is such a recipe on the actual full prefix $PS$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the actual constructors of $R$. For the reflexive tail,
appending changes neither tower and the second recipe condition is
true. At a successor, the recipe consists of the shorter recipe and
the equality between its actual next centre and the independently
computed difference on its full old prefix. The induction hypothesis
splits the shorter recipe into the claimed two pieces. Actual tower
associativity identifies $(PS)R$ with $P(SR)$, so the two next-centre
equalities are literally the same. Reassemble the conjunction in both
directions. No step, including an empty centre, is removed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual terminated recipes can be padded to every larger length]
\label{hir:sharp-original-curve-terminal-recipe-actual-padding}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-difference-recipe-concatenation,hir:sharp-original-curve-terminal-difference-actual-padding}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveTerminalPaddingComparison.original_curve_terminal_recipe_padding}
\leanok
Take positive ambient dimension and the original smooth curve, ordered
SNC and whole-family density data. Let $P$ be ANY original product TEST,
let $S$ be a genuine literal-difference recipe on $P$, and assume its
ACTUAL endpoint difference is empty. For every $L\ge\operatorname{length}S$,
construct the actual tail
\[
 S_L=S\,B_{L-\operatorname{length}S}(Y_S).
\]
It has length exactly $L$, is a genuine literal-difference recipe on
the SAME original prefix, and its actual endpoint difference is empty.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The real original recipe TEST theorem first supplies the genuine TEST
of $PS$. The preceding actual identity-empty producer then applies to
this true current prefix and its computed empty difference. It supplies
the real padding recipe and the actual empty endpoint difference. The
first theorem concatenates the original and padding recipes. Exact
tower length additivity and the proved length of the identity-empty
tail give $\operatorname{length}S+(L-\operatorname{length}S)=L$.
The endpoint manifold is the actual old endpoint because the padding
projections are identities; all added positions are retained.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Unequal terminated actual recipes have unique common-length comparisons]
\label{hir:sharp-original-curve-unequal-terminal-recipe-comparison}
\uses{hir:sharp-original-curve-terminal-recipe-actual-padding,hir:sharp-original-curve-equal-length-difference-recipe-comparison,hir:sharp-actual-stagewise-tower-comparison-unique}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveTerminalPaddingComparison.exists_unique_terminal_recipe_comparison}
\leanok
For the SAME original curve data, let $P',P$ have an actual stagewise
isomorphism over the identity, and let $P$ be a genuine original TEST.
Let $S',S$ be actual literal-difference recipes on these respective
prefixes with ACTUAL empty endpoint differences. Their lengths may
differ. For every $L$ at least both lengths, construct a UNIQUE genuine
terminal biholomorphism that is the terminal map of an actual stagewise
isomorphism of their full padded histories $P'S'_L$ and $PS_L$ over the
identity.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Actual tower-isomorphism transport of the original TEST internally
supplies the TEST of $P'$. Apply the preceding theorem independently
to both real terminal recipes. Both resulting recipes have exactly
length $L$. The proved equal-length actual recipe comparison now
constructs a genuine tail isomorphism over the supplied prefix map and
the actual full-history isomorphism over the identity. The fixed-tower
stagewise uniqueness theorem proves uniqueness among ALL terminal
biholomorphisms that give such actual stagewise isomorphisms. No
terminal-map or centre-matching certificate is supplied. This does not
claim uniqueness among arbitrary holomorphic maps over a composite
projection.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine terminal comparison is independent of the padding length]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-terminal-recipe-padding-independence}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding-comparison,hir:sharp-actual-stagewise-tower-comparison-unique,mc:tower-isomorphism-append}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveTerminalPaddingComparison.terminal_padding_comparison_extend,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveTerminalPaddingComparison.terminal_padding_comparison_independent}
\leanok
For fixed actual prefixes and tails, an actual padded stagewise
comparison at any common length $L$ extends to $L+r$ with the SAME
terminal map. Consequently two genuine comparisons constructed at
possibly different common lengths have exactly the same terminal
biholomorphism. This extension fact itself only requires the actual
stagewise comparisons and the true length bounds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Append to the given comparison the actual comparison between $r$
identity-empty tails induced by its own genuine terminal map. Actual
tower associativity and the exact concatenation law for identity-empty
tails identify the resulting histories with the histories padded to
$L+r$. The elementary subtraction equality
$(L+r)-s=(L-s)+r$, valid for $s\le L$, supplies the exact numbers of
retained slots on each side. The terminal map remains unchanged.

For comparisons at $L$ and $L'$, extend the first by $L'$ and the second
by $L$. Both now compare the same actual full histories at length
$L+L'$, using commutativity of addition for the second. Fixed-tower
stagewise uniqueness equates their terminal maps. The argument does
not delete the empty slots, identify the two differently sized tower
objects, or assert canonical compression. Actual restriction of local
recipes and their overlap cocycles remain subsequent consumers.
\end{proof}
