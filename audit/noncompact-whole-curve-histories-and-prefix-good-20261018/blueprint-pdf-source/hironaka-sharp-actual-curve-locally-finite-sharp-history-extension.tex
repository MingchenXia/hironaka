\label{hir:sharp-actual-original-locally-finite-sharp-history-existence}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-original-global-sharp-endpoint-existence,hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-growing-history,hir:sharp-actual-curve-growing-centre-images-locally-finite,hir:sharp-actual-curve-growing-stopped-open-strict-curve,hir:sharp-actual-curve-growing-stopped-open-boundary,hir:sharp-actual-curve-growing-compact-open-empty-tails}

In a positive-dimensional complex ambient manifold, let the original
closed smooth curve be pure of dimension one, let its original finite
ORDERED family be an SNC boundary, and suppose the part of the curve
outside that family is dense in the whole curve. Then these original
inputs construct a genuine growing blowup sequence over the ORIGINAL
ambient manifold and a full sharp endpoint, with the following coupled
properties.

The sequence is the actual original-input sequence, and the endpoint is
the SAME original-input full sharp endpoint. Stage zero is the original
reflexive tower, every successor is an actual blowup extending the SAME
preceding tower, and stage k has exactly k actual positions. Every stage
is a genuine original member-product TEST and, independently, a TEST of
the entire original ambient template with its full ordered transformed
SNC family. The indexed images of the actual next centres are locally
finite and their entire union is closed.

For EVERY ordinary compact subset K there are an internally chosen
positive stage and an actual original open neighbourhood U of K, with a
genuine biholomorphism from the ENTIRE prefix projection preimage of U
onto the ENTIRE full endpoint projection preimage of U. Its whole
projection square commutes. It identifies the conventional strict curves
at every point and EVERY natural-number boundary readout of the same
finite prefix with the SAME full endpoint boundary. Original ordered
members, born members, actual empty entries and default empty values all
retain their positions. EVERY actual finite later tail of the SAME
growing sequence restricts over U to a genuine empty-centre tower.

The full sharp endpoint retains its proper holomorphic surjective
projection, original good-locus isomorphism, smooth pure strict curve with
image the original curve, actual locally finite SNC and transverse
coordinate witnesses, reduced effective born Cartier divisor and whole
ideal/support identities, and genuine original finite local presentations
with FIRST and ambient-difference terminality. None of these data,
histories, bounds, comparisons or readout identities is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the actual original-input growing sequence and the actual full
sharp endpoint already constructed from precisely the stated original
geometry. Retain explicit equalities to these constructors in the output;
do not forget an existential endpoint and later attach an unrelated
recipe. The sequence constructor proves its original reflexive stage and
literal successor blowups, retaining all positions. Whole transport
preserves lengths. At every stage the proved original product TEST and
the independent curve DOMAIN1 ambient-boundary argument supply the two
actual TESTs and the entire transformed original SNC list. The genuine
whole-step centre-image dictionary and local recipe stationarity give
local finiteness of the indexed actual images; their existing proper
projection closedness then gives their closed union.

Given an ordinary compact K, apply the genuine compact-open empty-tail
producer to THIS sequence. It internally supplies a positive stage whose
actual stopped base open contains K, together with every actual finite
later tail's empty-centre restriction. Choose U to be exactly that stopped
open. Use the already constructed actual stopped-prefix-to-endpoint
biholomorphism; the explicit stage and endpoint constructors identify its
source and target with the two entire projection preimages in the
statement. Its genuine projection formula gives the whole square.

Apply the stopped-open conventional strict-curve dictionary at every
point and the stopped-open boundary dictionary at every natural index
and point. They concern precisely this prefix, this open and this same
biholomorphism, so they provide all requested coupled readouts without
assuming an atlas, comparison or stopping certificate. Retain the actual
empty-tail witnesses supplied before choosing the map. All other sharp
geometric and Cartier properties are fields of the SAME full endpoint
constructed from the original data, including its actual finite local
presentations and full original boundary readouts.

This proves the whole locally finite sharp-history existence statement
for the stated smooth pure curve situation. No finite maximum over a
noncompact ambient atlas, canonical scheduling, general higher-dimensional
resolution driver, or additional monomial principalization theorem is
asserted.
\end{proof}
