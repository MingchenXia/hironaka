\section{Actual fixed-flattening whole original stalks at every nearby point}

A genuine flattening is used on its real source and target. For each
actual target point $a$, put $p=\Phi^{-1}(a)$ and
$y=\operatorname{chart}_x^{-1}(p)$. The translated local equivalence
\[
 \Psi_a(z)=\Phi(z+p)-a,\qquad
 \Psi_a^{-1}(w)=\Phi^{-1}(w+a)-p
\]
has real open source $\{z:z+p\in\Phi.\mathrm{source}\}$ and real open
target $\{w:w+a\in\Phi.\mathrm{target}\}$, both containing zero.
All claims below are local on these true domains.

\begin{lemma}[The genuine nearby translated nonlinear equivalence]
\label{hir:nonlinear-nearby-actual-germ-equivalence}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:real-centre-domain-actual-flattened-coherent-family}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreNearbyStalks.actualNearbyEquiv,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreNearbyStalks.actualNearbyEquiv_zero,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreNearbyStalks.actualNearbyGermEquiv}
\leanok
For every $a\in\Phi.\mathrm{target}$, the displayed $\Psi_a$ is an
actual local holomorphic equivalence fixing zero. Composition with it
induces an equivalence $E_a$ of the entire actual ambient holomorphic
germ algebras.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The source and target are inverse images of the actual open sets under
continuous translations. Since $p=\Phi^{-1}(a)$ lies in the source and
$\Phi(p)=a$, zero lies in both translated domains and $\Psi_a(0)=0$.
Holomorphicity follows by composing the original holomorphic maps with
translations and subtracting constants. The identities
$\Phi^{-1}(\Phi(z+p))=z+p$ and
$\Phi(\Phi^{-1}(w+a))=w+a$, on the true corresponding domains,
give the two inverse identities after subtracting $p$ and $a$.
Composition of actual germ representatives with these maps is well
defined by continuity at zero and preserves sums, products and
constants. Composing in the two orders gives the identity by those
actual local inverse identities, which proves the entire algebra
equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Every actual composed generator is its original nearby chart germ]
\label{hir:nonlinear-nearby-actual-generator-transport}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:nonlinear-nearby-actual-germ-equivalence,hir:real-centre-domain-whole-nearby-translated-ideals}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreNearbyStalks.actualNearbyGermEquiv_original_function}
\leanok
Let $G$ be one actual original holomorphic function near $y$, and let
$H=G\circ\operatorname{chart}_x^{-1}\circ\Phi^{-1}$. Whenever these
actual functions are holomorphic on their real neighbourhoods,
$E_a$ sends the actual germ of $H(a+w)$ to the actual original chart
germ of $G$ centred at $y$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For $z$ in the translated source, $z+p$ lies in the true source of
$\Phi$ and hence in the original chart target. On that actual open,
\[
 H(a+\Psi_a(z))
 =G\bigl(\operatorname{chart}_x^{-1}
       (\Phi^{-1}(\Phi(z+p)))\bigr)
 =G(\operatorname{chart}_x^{-1}(z+p)).
\]
The chart identity gives $\operatorname{chart}_x(y)=p$, so the last
function is exactly the original translated chart representative.
This equality holds on a genuine neighbourhood of zero and therefore
gives equality of the entire actual holomorphic germs.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original input internally supplies every nearby whole stalk dictionary]
\label{hir:nonlinear-nearby-original-whole-stalk-dictionary}
\source{bierstone-milman08-functoriality}
\uses{hir:nonlinear-nearby-actual-generator-transport,hir:real-centre-domain-whole-nearby-translated-ideals}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreNearbyStalks.wholeChartStalkIdeal_eq_span_of_original_generators,Hironaka.HironakaSmoothCentreNearbyStalks.exists_original_regular_same_open_nearby_whole_stalk_dictionary}
\leanok
Original regular-centre geometry at $x$ and original coherence of $A$
internally supply one genuine flattening $\Phi$, one actual open $U$
containing zero inside its real target, and for every $a\in U$ the
actual original nearby point $y=\operatorname{chart}_x^{-1}(\Phi^{-1}(a))$
and entire algebra equivalence $E_a$, such that
\[
 E_a\bigl(I((f^*A)_a)\bigr)=I_{A,x,y}.
\]
The right side is the whole original ideal generated by all actual
original membership germs in the fixed chart at $y$. Equivalently,
the actual translated ideal on the left is its full comap by $E_a$.
The caller supplies no nearby ideal, ring equivalence or ideal-map
certificate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The real-domain coherent-family theorem supplies actual original
finite generators $G_i$ on an open $O$, their literal composites
$H_i=G_i\circ f$, and one actual $U$ mapped by $f$ into $O$.
Fix $a\in U$. At $y=f(a)$ the complete original membership relation
is the finite holomorphic span of the $G_i$. Reading each relation in
the actual inverse chart and translating by its actual chart value
preserves the equality and holomorphic coefficients. Therefore every
whole original membership germ belongs to the ideal generated by the
translated chart germs of $G_i$. Conversely each $G_i$ is an actual
original member by that same finite relation, so each displayed germ
belongs to the whole original stalk ideal. This proves equality of
the whole original ideal with that finite generated ideal.
The preceding real-domain theorem identifies the whole actual
translated ideal with the germs of $H_i(a+w)$. Mapping its span by the
entire algebra equivalence maps its generators to the corresponding
original chart germs by the preceding lemma. Thus its whole image is
the whole original stalk ideal. Bijectivity of $E_a$ gives the stated
full comap equality. The same original $U$ and $\Phi$ work for every
$a$; no uniform assertion outside that real domain is used.
\end{proof}

Localized uniform graded relations and freeness, the whole nearby
vanishing-centre dictionary and intrinsic sheaf normal-flatness remain

germs with nearby germs; each equivalence is constructed from the
actual recentered nonlinear maps at its actual nearby point.
