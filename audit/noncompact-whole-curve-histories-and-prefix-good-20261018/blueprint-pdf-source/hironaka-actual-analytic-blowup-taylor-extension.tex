\section{Actual holomorphic blowup coordinate substitution and genuine analytic quotients}

We use the genuine polynomial map of an exceptional coordinate chart, whose
coordinates are $x_e=u_e$, $x_i=u_e(b_i+u_i)$ for other centre coordinates,
and $x_i=u_i$ outside the centre. This constructs an actual map and an actual
pullback of original holomorphic germs. It does not assert global chart
existence or convergence of an arbitrary prescribed formal series.

\begin{theorem}[The whole genuine Taylor map commutes with actual blowup coordinate pullback]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-germ-hom-coordinate-uniqueness,hir:actual-formal-blowup-coordinate-power-image}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.actualAnalyticBlowupMap,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.differentiable_actualAnalyticBlowupMap,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.actualAnalyticBlowupMap_zero,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.actualAnalyticBlowupPullback,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.actualAnalyticBlowupPullback_coordinate,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.actualTaylor_analyticBlowup_coordinate,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.actualTaylor_analyticBlowupPullback}
\leanok
For every finite coordinate centre, exceptional index and complex direction,
the actual polynomial coordinate map is holomorphic and sends the origin to
the origin. Its genuine whole-germ pullback has the literal coordinate
formula. The entire original Taylor homomorphism after this actual pullback
is exactly the existing independently defined formal blowup substitution
after the original Taylor homomorphism.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the actual vector-valued map by assembling its polynomial
coordinate functions using the genuine continuous linear coordinate equivalence. Each coordinate
function is holomorphic by addition and multiplication of genuine linear
coordinate functions, so the actual vector map is holomorphic. Every
coordinate is zero at the origin. Actual germ composition therefore gives a
genuine algebra homomorphism. Evaluate each original coordinate germ using
the actual coordinate formula. The two independently defined algebra maps
to the formal series ring then agree on all genuine coordinate germs, by the
original Taylor coordinate dictionary and the existing true formal blowup
coordinate formula. Their coordinate images lie in the genuine formal
maximal ideal: each centre image has the actual exceptional-variable factor,
and every other image is a formal variable. The previously proved whole-germ
coordinate uniqueness theorem gives equality on the entire original germ
ring, without a supplied continuity or desired map-identity certificate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Genuine exceptional division produces an analytic quotient with the exact original normalized formal series]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-blowup-exceptional-quotient-formal-dictionary}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-blowup-coordinate-pullback-taylor-naturality,hir:actual-formal-normal-power-contracts-exact-original-analytic-power,hir:actual-formal-blowup-normalized-member}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.actualAnalyticCoordinateIdeal,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.map_actualAnalyticCoordinateIdeal_taylor,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.actualTaylor_mem_coordinateCentre_pow,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.map_actualAnalyticCoordinateIdeal_blowup,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.map_actualAnalyticCoordinateIdeal_pow_blowup,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.exists_actualAnalyticBlowup_quotient,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.actualAnalyticCoordinateIdeal_normal,Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticBlowupTaylor.exists_actualAnalyticBlowup_quotient_of_formal_normal_pow}
\leanok
Let the exceptional index $e$ belong to the coordinate-centre set $A$.
For any actual analytic germ in that original coordinate-centre ideal power
of order $q$, its actual holomorphic coordinate-chart pullback is divisible
by the genuine exceptional coordinate germ to power $q$, with an actual
holomorphic germ quotient. The Taylor series of that quotient is precisely
the existing normalized formal member of the Taylor series of the original
analytic germ. The same conclusion for a standard normal centre follows
from formal normal-power membership of the Taylor series alone.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual coordinate formulas identify the image of the entire original
coordinate-centre ideal with the principal ideal of the actual exceptional
coordinate: each centre coordinate image has this factor, and the
exceptional coordinate itself is among the original centre generators.
The actual ideal-map power identity therefore puts the pullback of every
member of the original centre power into the actual principal power.
Membership in that principal ideal constructs a genuine analytic quotient
with the original product identity.

Apply the genuine Taylor homomorphism to that product identity. The actual
coordinate dictionary maps the exceptional germ to its literal formal
variable, and whole-map naturality identifies the numerator with the
existing formal blowup substitution. Thus the genuine quotient's Taylor
series satisfies the exact defining product identity of the existing
normalized formal member. The genuine formal exceptional variable is
nonzero, and cancellation in the actual formal series domain gives its
uniqueness. For the standard normal centre, the already proved exact
normal-power contraction first recovers genuine analytic power membership
from the formal hypothesis; the same actual quotient construction applies.
No analytic preimage for an arbitrary formal reduced vertex is assumed or
constructed by this argument.
\end{proof}
