\begin{lemma}[True supported recipes retain centre support through every actual uniform prefix]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-uniform-prefix-centre-support}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-genuine-global-prefix-tower,hir:sharp-actual-curve-recipe-centres-over-first-defect,bcalc:restrict-empty-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGrowingGoodPreservation.centresOver_uniformPrefix,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveGrowingGoodPreservation.original_localRecipe_centresOver_bad}
\leanok
If an actual finite recipe has every centre over an original set F, every
actual uniform prefix has the same property, including stages after its
length. For the actual original sharp curve recipes, F can be taken to be
the inverse image of the ORIGINAL bad locus. This support is constructed
from the actual FIRST step and literal ambient-difference recipe, not
from a desired numerical maximum or an assumed terminal isomorphism.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every centre of an actual identity-empty tower is empty, hence lies over
any prescribed original set. The genuine append support dictionary gives
support over F for the full recipe followed by the true identity-empty
suffix. A uniform prefix is exactly the genuine head of that whole tower.
The actual head support theorem therefore proves the first assertion,
with every real empty position retained.

On an actual original recipe neighbourhood, genuine open restriction
preserves smoothness, the ordered SNC family, density and pure curve
dimension. Its actual geometric dictionary supplies the true FIRST
blowup and every literal subsequent ambient-difference step. The proved
recipe support induction puts every centre over the initial FIRST defect.
The original good-locus theorem says that this initial defect misses the
original good locus; its genuine open-preimage identity puts that defect
in the inverse image of the original bad locus. Monotonicity of support
along the actual finite constructors gives the required whole support.
\end{proof}

The proved whole-step centre-image identity for the actual growing
sequence identifies the API-selected centre image with this true global
image, including the first transported step. Substitute this exact image
identity. No arbitrary map or centre is inferred from projection equality.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original growing prefix is an original-boundary admissible sequence and preserves the good locus]

recorded centre image, which lies in the original bad locus by the

induction hypothesis and transport the ENTIRE proposition along the

original bad locus, with no change of underlying stage or chosen map.

constructed locally finite curve history; general higher-dimensional
canonical scheduling remains separate.
\end{proof}
