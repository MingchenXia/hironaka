\section{A genuine original growing curve history and locally finite centre images}

The ambient dimension is positive. No compactness of the original ambient
space is assumed. Every empty centre remains an actual blowup position.
The following growing history is built from the actual sharp local recipes;
it is not assumed canonical for a numerical invariant.

\begin{theorem}[The actual transported prefixes form an original growing admissible history]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-growing-history}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-genuine-global-prefix-tower,hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-global-prefix-tower,hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-global-prefix-test,hir:sharp-original-curve-test-history-full-original-ambient-boundary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOriginalGrowingHistory.uniformGrowingSeq,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOriginalGrowingHistory.uniformGrowingSeq_length,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOriginalGrowingHistory.originalGrowingSeq,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOriginalGrowingHistory.originalGrowingSeq_test,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOriginalGrowingHistory.originalGrowingSeq_ambient_test}
\leanok
The actual global prefixes, transported by their internally proved initial
projection biholomorphism, form a genuine growing sequence over the ORIGINAL
ambient manifold. Stage zero is literally its reflexive tower. Every next
stage appends the actual transported centre and blowup, and stage k has
exactly k constructors. For ordinary original smooth pure curve, ordered
SNC boundary and density inputs, every stage is a TEST of the original
member product and, independently, a TEST of the original ambient template
with its ENTIRE original ordered boundary.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At stage k take the actual last manifold and tower of the genuine base
transport of the k-step global tower. At zero the transport definition is
the reflexive tower on the original manifold. At the next constructor the
same definition appends the inverse image of the true global centre under
the preceding last-stage biholomorphism, with the composite of its inverse
and the true global blowup map. The original blowup transport theorem proves
this is a genuine blowup. This is an equality of whole towers with the SAME
preceding stage, not an equality of projections. Length is preserved by
transport, so every actual constructor, including every empty one, remains.

At a positive stage the actual transported tower is exactly the proved
original positive prefix. Its original member-product TEST follows from
the original-input global TEST producer. At zero it is reflexive. For each
stage separately, the genuine curve DOMAIN1 and current zero-dimensional
cosupport argument supplies actual normal crossings of every centre with
the full original ambient family, and therefore its ambient-template TEST
and entire transformed SNC list. The member product's initial auxiliary
empty boundary is never substituted for the original ordered family.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual transported centre images form a locally finite closed family]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-growing-centre-images-locally-finite}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-growing-history,hir:sharp-actual-curve-global-centres-locally-stationary,hir:sharp-actual-original-global-projection-proper}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOriginalGrowingHistory.centreImage_uniformGrowingSeq,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOriginalGrowingHistory.locallyFinite_originalGrowingSeq_centreImage,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOriginalGrowingHistory.isClosed_originalGrowingSeq_centreImage_union}
\leanok
The image in the original ambient manifold of each ACTUAL transported next
centre equals the image of its true global centre under the same stage
projection. For the original-input atlas, these centre images are closed
and locally finite; consequently their entire union is closed. No local
finiteness or support equality is supplied as a premise.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine whole transport projection square holds on every point of the
transported stage. Apply the initial projection biholomorphism to it and
then use the actual global prefix projection identity. The resulting
pointwise equality identifies the transported projection with the true
stage projection after the actual last-stage biholomorphism. Surjectivity
of that biholomorphism identifies the ENTIRE images of its pulled-back
centre with the true global centre image, including the first stage.

At an original point choose its actual recipe neighbourhood and its actual
finite recipe length L. At EVERY k at least L the entire stage projection
preimage of that neighbourhood is disjoint from the true centre. A point
in the intersection of the centre image and the neighbourhood would lift
to an actual centre point in that preimage, contradicting this disjointness.
Thus the neighbourhood meets only centre images indexed below L, a genuine
finite set. This proves local finiteness without a maximum over the atlas.
Each true global centre is closed; the actual stage projection is closed
by its already proved properness. Its image is therefore closed. The union
of a locally finite family of closed sets is closed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every ordinary compact subset encounters only finitely many actual centres]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-growing-history-compact-encounters}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-growing-centre-images-locally-finite,hir:sharp-actual-curve-compact-stopped-open-bound}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveOriginalGrowingHistory.originalGrowingSeq_compact_encounters}
\leanok
For every ordinary compact subset K of the original ambient manifold,
there is an internally constructed natural N such that EVERY actual centre
image at index at least N is disjoint from K. The actual set of encountered
indices is finite. The assertion allows an empty K and does not assert a
uniform finite length on a noncompact ambient manifold.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual stopped base opens increase and cover the original manifold.
Ordinary compactness gives a genuine finite subcover of K; the maximum
of the finitely many chosen lengths, increased by one, gives N with K
contained in the N-th stopped open. Every later centre avoids the ENTIRE
projection preimage of K by the proved stopped-open centre theorem. Lift
a hypothetical point of its image intersecting K to an actual centre point
to obtain a contradiction. All encountered indices therefore belong to
the finite initial interval below N. This also handles an empty compact
set and keeps every actual empty step of the growing sequence.
\end{proof}
