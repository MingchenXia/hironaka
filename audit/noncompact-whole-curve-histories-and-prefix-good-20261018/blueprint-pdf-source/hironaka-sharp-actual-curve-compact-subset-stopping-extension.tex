\section{Actual finite stopping over every original compact subset}

\begin{lemma}[Compactness internally bounds a finite chosen collection of actual stopped opens]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-compact-stopped-open-bound}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-stopped-open-cover,hir:sharp-actual-curve-global-centres-locally-stationary}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactSubsetStopping.exists_compact_stopped_open_bound,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactSubsetStopping.globalCentres_avoid_subset_of_stopped_open}
\leanok
For the SAME actual retained atlas and its true original open cover, every
compact subset $K$ of the original ambient manifold lies in the actual
stopped open $U_{N+1}$ for some natural $N$. Every true global centre at
all stages $k\ge N+1$ avoids the ENTIRE actual projection preimage of $K$.
No bound, finite atlas or stationarity certificate is supplied.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The actual increasing opens $U_k$ cover the entire original ambient space.
Apply the genuine finite-subcover theorem to the ordinary compact subset
$K$. Take the maximum of this FINITE chosen set of natural indices and
increase to a positive stage. Each chosen open lies in the resulting
actual stopped open by proved monotonicity, so it contains $K$. This
construction also handles the empty compact set; no least bound is claimed.

For any later stage and any point projecting into $K$, membership in the
constructed stopped open gives an actual original chart containing its
projection, with literal full history length at most the constructed
bound and hence at most this later stage. The genuine local stationarity
theorem makes the actual whole centre disjoint from the ENTIRE projection
preimage of that chart. It therefore excludes the given point. This is
whole-set disjointness for every later centre, retaining actual empty steps.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The genuine original-base finite prefix identifies the whole compact preimage on an actual neighborhood]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-compact-finite-prefix-terminal-identification}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-stopped-open-global-biholomorphism,hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-global-prefix-tower}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactSubsetStopping.positivePrefixTerminalBiholomorphism,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactSubsetStopping.positivePrefixTerminalBiholomorphism_projection,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactSubsetStopping.positivePrefixTerminalBiholomorphism_symm_projection,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactSubsetStopping.positivePrefixTerminalBiholomorphism_preimage}
\leanok
At every positive stage $N+1$, the SAME already constructed original-base
finite global blowup prefix has a genuine terminal biholomorphism on the
ENTIRE preimage of its actual stopped open $U_{N+1}$ onto the ENTIRE
terminal projection preimage of that SAME open. Its literal length is
$N+1$ and its original projection square commutes. Under this SAME map,
membership in the preimage of ANY original subset, and in particular a
compact $K\subseteq U_{N+1}$, agrees in BOTH directions. No new history or
endpoint map is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the original-base prefix already transported by the internally proved
zero-stage projection biholomorphism. Its endpoint is EXACTLY the actual
positive uniform stage, its literal length is the stated positive natural
number, and its whole projection is the actual stage projection. That
projection identity identifies its genuine open preimage of $U_{N+1}$
with the actual stopped-stage open, on every point. The genuine equal-open
biholomorphism therefore transports the previously constructed stopped-open
biholomorphism to this SAME original-base prefix endpoint. The equal-open
map preserves underlying points, so the previously proved entire stopped
projection identity is exactly the original finite-prefix projection
identity. Taking membership in any original subset gives BOTH directions
on its entire inverse image; evaluating at the genuine inverse gives the
inverse point identity as well. No manifold structure is put on a possibly
nonopen compact subset.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Only original curve geometry and ordinary compactness give a genuine compact finite stopping witness]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-curve-original-compact-subset-finite-stopping}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-curve-compact-stopped-open-bound,hir:sharp-actual-curve-compact-finite-prefix-terminal-identification,hir:sharp-actual-original-endpoint-comparison-atlas}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactSubsetStopping.originalPositivePrefixTerminalBiholomorphism,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveCompactSubsetStopping.original_compact_subset_finite_stopping}
\leanok
Only the original positive-dimensional smooth pure curve, ordered SNC and
density data, together with an ordinary compact subset $K$ of the original
ambient space, internally give a natural $N$, an actual original open
neighborhood $U_{N+1}$ of $K$, and the SAME genuine finite original-base
global blowup prefix and terminal identification above. All later true
centres avoid the whole preimage of $K$. The target is the SAME retained
global sharp endpoint and the SAME local histories, not an unrelated
terminal witness. No uniform bound over a noncompact ambient space is
asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Construct the retained actual atlas and its genuine original cover from
ONLY the original geometric inputs. Apply the first lemma to the actual
ordinary compact $K$, producing the positive finite stage and the actual
open neighborhood containing it. Apply the second theorem at THIS
internally produced stage, using the already constructed actual global
successors, original zero-stage base transport and stopped terminal maps.
All original local prefix/tail histories and their empty positions are
therefore the SAME retained data throughout. The resulting biholomorphism
identifies every point of the entire compact preimage by the proved
projection identity and both inverse laws. The first lemma gives every
later-centre avoidance statement. This is genuine finite stopping over
compact subsets; the general original ambient result remains local
stationarity without a maximum over its point-indexed infinite family.
\end{proof}
