\section{Actual empty-blowup padding of terminal sharp-curve histories}

An empty centre is a genuine position in an actual blowup history. We use
actual identity blowups of the empty centre, retaining their exceptional
slots and proving their precise geometric effect. No deletion of steps
or canonical-history compression is used.

\begin{definition}[Actual identity empty tails]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding}
\uses{hir:geo-transform-basic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveEmptyPadding.identityEmptyTail,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveEmptyPadding.identityEmptyTail_length,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveEmptyPadding.identityEmptyTail_proj,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveEmptyPadding.identityEmptyTail_isEmptyTower}
\leanok
For a genuine complex manifold $Y$ and $r\in\mathbb N$, construct an
actual tower $B_r(Y):Y\to Y$ by starting with the reflexive tower and
appending $r$ genuine identity blowups of the empty centre. Its length
is exactly $r$, its actual projection is the identity and every actual
centre is empty. Both inverse laws of its projection are literal
identity laws; the tower itself has $r$ positions.
\end{definition}
\begin{proof}
The existing identity blowup theorem supplies an ACTUAL blowup predicate
for $\operatorname{id}_Y$ at the empty centre. Use it at each successor
of the recursion. The tower constructor increases the length by one,
composes the previous projection with the identity and appends the
actual empty centre. Induction from length zero proves each assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual empty padding preserves carrier and cosupport and records every empty boundary slot]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding-geometric-ledger}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding,hir:wellformed-tower,hir:geo-transform-basic}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveEmptyPadding.identityEmptyTail_geometric_dictionary}
\leanok
Let $M$ be an actual well-formed marked record and $P$ a genuine original
$M$ TEST. Appending $B_r(Y_P)$ gives another genuine original TEST. Its
actual current carrier, marking and cosupport equal those at $P$, and
its ENTIRE ordered current exceptional list is
\[
                         E_P\mathbin{+\!+}\operatorname{replicate}(r,\varnothing).
\]
No equality of the old and new ambient ideal representatives is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An empty centre is admissible for EVERY actual marked record, so induction
constructs the actual appended TEST before current geometry is used.
For one step, the strict carrier is the closure of its preimage under
the identity after deleting the empty set. The old carrier is closed,
so it is unchanged. Every old exceptional member is closed by actual
well-formedness, hence the same computation fixes each of them. The new
exceptional member is exactly the empty preimage. The marking is retained
by the actual controlled transform definition.

At every point the actual projection is off its empty centre. The
independently proved off-centre controlled cosupport equivalence gives
both cosupport directions; its smooth-carrier and coherent-ideal
requirements are supplied by actual current well-formedness. Iterating
these one-step identities proves the stated carrier, marking, whole
cosupport and ordered-list formulas. The controlled colon can normalize
an ambient ideal along its carrier even in this empty step, which is why
no literal whole-ideal equality follows from these geometric identities.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A terminal literal difference remains empty along actual recipe padding]
\label{hir:sharp-original-curve-terminal-difference-actual-padding}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding-geometric-ledger,hir:sharp-original-curve-literal-ambient-difference-history-order-bound}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveEmptyPadding.original_curve_terminal_difference_padding}
\leanok
For positive ambient dimension $n$ and the original curve/SNC/density data, ANY genuine original product
TEST $P$ with its ACTUAL literal difference $\Delta_P=\varnothing$ and
ANY $r$, the genuine tail $B_r(Y_P)$ is a literal-$\Delta$ recipe tail
on that SAME original prefix, and the actual difference at its endpoint
is still empty. Every original position and every padded empty centre is
retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At $r=0$ the recipe is reflexive and the difference is the stated actual
terminal difference. At a successor, the genuine original TEST theorem for
actual literal-difference recipes applies to the shorter recipe already
constructed by induction, supplying the original shorter TEST internally.
The induction hypothesis says its ACTUAL computed difference is empty;
therefore the true next identity empty blowup is precisely a blowup of
that computed difference. Actual whole-difference support localization
gives $\Delta_{\rm next}\subseteq\pi^{-1}(\Delta_{\rm old})$.
The right side is empty, proving the actual new difference empty. The
shorter recipe plus equality of the REAL next empty centre with its
computed old difference is exactly the next recipe condition. No new
termination, order bound or canonicality assertion is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual empty padding concatenates and transports through genuine biholomorphisms]
\label{hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding-comparison}
\uses{hir:sharp-actual-identity-empty-padding,mc:tower-isomorphism-basic,mc:tower-isomorphism-append}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveEmptyPadding.identityEmptyTail_append,Hironaka.HironakaSharpActualCurveEmptyPadding.identityEmptyTail_isTowerIso}
\leanok
The actual towers satisfy $B_{r+s}(Y)=B_r(Y)B_s(Y)$.
For EVERY genuine biholomorphism $e:Y'\to Y$ and EVERY $r$, construct an
actual stagewise isomorphism between $B_r(Y')$ and $B_r(Y)$ over $e$,
whose terminal map is the SAME actual $e$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induction on $s$ gives the first equality: both successor histories are
the same actual identity empty blowup appended to their shorter equal
histories. For the second assertion, start from the reflexive tower
isomorphism over $e$. At each successor both actual centres are empty,
whose true preimages under $e$ are empty; both next projections are the
identity, so $\operatorname{id}\circ e=e\circ\operatorname{id}$ is the
actual next square. The genuine tower-isomorphism constructor extends
the shorter comparison with terminal map still $e$. This construction
keeps all empty entries. Unequal stopping-length comparison, padding
independence and actual overlap cocycles require the separate finite
recipe comparison and remain subsequent consumers.
\end{proof}
