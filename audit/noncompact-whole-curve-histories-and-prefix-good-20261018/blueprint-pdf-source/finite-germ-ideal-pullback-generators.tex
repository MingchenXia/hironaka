\section{Literal finite generators of the actual pulled-back ideal}

These supplementary bridges use the actual function-germ definition of
ideal pullback. They retain the literal original finite family under
composition with a holomorphic map. They do not substitute a zero-set
relation for generation and need no assumed principalization or target
finite presentation. The second bridge obtains the original family
from genuine coherence.

\begin{theorem}[The complete finite stalk pulls back to its literal family]
\label{trace:actual-finite-germ-ideal-pullback-generation}
\uses{ana:ideal-sheaves,oka:coherent-colon,fpgeom:complex-manifold}
\source{demailly-cadg}
\lean{Hironaka.FiniteGermIdealPullbackGenerators.mem_pullback_iff_of_finite_generation}
\leanok
Let $f:Y\to X$ be holomorphic near $y$, and let $\mathfrak a$ be an
actual ideal of function germs on $X$. Suppose $G_i$ are actual
holomorphic germs at $f(y)$ and present its complete stalk:
\[
 \forall a,\quad a\in\mathfrak a_{f(y)}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad [a]\in([G_i])_{f(y)}.
\]
Then for every literal $F:Y\to\mathbb C$,
\[
 F\in(f^*\mathfrak a)_y
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad [F]\in([G_i\circ f])_y.
\]
The source and target dimensions may differ, and may be zero; empty
finite generator families use the actual empty span.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The actual pullback stalk consists of finite holomorphic combinations
of $a\circ f$ with $a\in\mathfrak a_{f(y)}$. For such a generator,
unpack the given complete finite generation into actual coefficients
$b_i$ holomorphic near $f(y)$ and a genuine neighborhood identity
$a=\sum_i b_iG_i$. Holomorphy of $f$ supplies its continuity and the
actual holomorphic pullbacks of $a$ and each $b_i$. Pull the
neighborhood equality back under $f$. This gives
$a\circ f=\sum_i(b_i\circ f)(G_i\circ f)$ near $y$, so every
pullback generator is in the finite pulled-back span. Substitution
of these actual relations in a finite span gives the forward inclusion.

Each $G_i$ belongs to its own original finite span by its actual
holomorphy. The reverse original generation implication therefore
places $G_i$ in $\mathfrak a_{f(y)}$. Consequently $G_i\circ f$ is
an actual generator of the pullback stalk and is holomorphic near
$y$. Substitution in every finite combination gives the reverse
inclusion. No coherence, measure or comparison certificate is used
in this first bridge.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Coherence internally supplies the literal pulled-back finite presentation]
\label{trace:actual-coherent-pullback-finite-generators}
\uses{trace:actual-finite-germ-ideal-pullback-generation,ana:ideal-sheaves,fpgeom:complex-manifold}
\source{demailly-cadg}
\lean{Hironaka.FiniteGermIdealPullbackGenerators.exists_finite_generators_pullback_of_isCoherent}
\leanok
For an actual coherent ideal $\mathfrak a$, a map $f$ holomorphic near
$y$, and arbitrary $y$, there are an actual open $U\ni f(y)$ and
literal finite $G_i$ holomorphic on $U$ that present every original
stalk on $U$, with the complete additional equivalence
\[
 \forall F,\quad F\in(f^*\mathfrak a)_y
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad[F]\in([G_i\circ f])_y.
\]
No target presentation is an input.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the actual coherence property at $f(y)$. It supplies $U$, all
literal holomorphic generators $G_i$, and their complete generation
relation at every point of $U$. Holomorphy on the genuine open $U$
implies holomorphy of each generator germ at its center. Apply the
preceding pullback theorem to the supplied relation at $f(y)$.
Return the same original $U$ and family with their original all-stalk
relation and the new full pullback relation. Thus later genuine
principalization consumers receive the literal original generators,
not an independently assumed coordinate presentation.
\end{proof}
