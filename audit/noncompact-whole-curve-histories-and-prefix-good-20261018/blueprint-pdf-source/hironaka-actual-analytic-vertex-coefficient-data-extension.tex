\section{Original finite coefficient data for genuine analytic monic vertices}

Write $R$ for the original analytic germ ring in coordinates $(u,y,z)$
of dimensions $k,s,t$, and $T:R\to S$ for its actual unshifted Taylor map.
Let $I\subseteq R$ be any original ideal, $J=T(I)S$, and let $V$ be the
actual degree-lex vertices of $J$. The coordinate centre is $y=z=0$.
Write $w_\alpha$ for its true weight at $\alpha\in V$.

\begin{definition}[Actual analytic vertex coefficient data]
\label{hir:actual-analytic-vertex-coefficient-data}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticVertexCoefficientData.ActualAnalyticVertexCoefficientData}
\leanok
Such data consist of actual analytic generators $g_\alpha\in I$, monic
with true Taylor initial exponent $\alpha$ and generating the whole $I$;
one genuine open neighbourhood $U$ of the origin in the $(u,y)$ variables;
and actual holomorphic normal coefficient germs represented by functions
$c_{\alpha,\gamma}$ there. Their entire Taylor series equal the literal
$z^\gamma$ coefficients of $T(g_\alpha)$ for every $\gamma$.
For $|\gamma|<w_\alpha$ all these functions are holomorphic on the same
$U$, their actual principal germ ideals are coherent there, and their
original germs belong to those same ideals.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Every original analytic ideal internally supplies these finite coefficient data]
\label{hir:actual-original-analytic-vertex-coefficient-data-existence}
\uses{hir:actual-analytic-vertex-coefficient-data,hir:actual-analytic-centre-compatible-monic-vertex-family,hir:actual-finite-local-coherent-coefficient-ideals}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticVertexCoefficientData.nonempty_actualAnalyticVertexCoefficientData}
\leanok
For every original analytic ideal $I$, actual analytic vertex coefficient
data exist. No analytic generators, coefficient germs or domains are supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the analytic monic vertex construction with the empty coordinate centre.
Its formal centre condition is automatic: the true empty-centre weights are
zero and the zeroth ideal power is the whole ring. It internally supplies
actual monic $g_\alpha\in I$ at every true vertex, generating the entire $I$.
The true vertex set is finite. Let $q=\sum_{\alpha\in V}w_\alpha$, so each
$w_\alpha\leq q$, including when the set is empty or the sum is zero.
Apply the actual finite analytic coefficient construction at cutoff $q$
to this internally chosen family. It supplies one genuine $U$, actual
$c_{\alpha,\gamma}$, their entire Taylor identities, and real-open
holomorphicity, coherence of their actual principal ideals and original
membership for $|\gamma|<q$. These properties hold in particular when
$|\gamma|<w_\alpha$. Keeping the genuine initial coefficients and
whole ideal generation from the first construction gives all the stated
data. No canonical reduced formal member is asserted to converge.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original coefficient cosupport produces canonical compatibility and genuine analytic strict generators]
\label{hir:actual-original-vertex-coefficient-cosupport-strict-generators}
\uses{hir:actual-original-analytic-vertex-coefficient-data-existence,hir:actual-canonical-centre-compatibility-iff-monic-family,hir:actual-local-coefficient-presentation-centre-power,hir:actual-analytic-normalized-member-finite-generation}
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualAnalyticVertexCoefficientData.exists_actual_original_coefficient_strict_generators}
\leanok
The data above can be internally chosen so that the following holds.
Suppose, for every $|\gamma|<w_\alpha$, the actual plane
$U\cap\{y=0\}$ lies in the original cosupport of the actual principal
coefficient ideal $(c_{\alpha,\gamma})$ at order
$w_\alpha-|\gamma|$. Then every actual canonical reduced formal vertex
member belongs to the true centre power $C^{w_\alpha}$.
In every actual exceptional chart there is an internally constructed finite
analytic family generating the entire analytic normalized-member ideal,
whose whole Taylor extension is exactly the source formal strict ideal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Choose the original coefficient data by the preceding existence theorem.
Coherence and original membership on the same genuine $U$, together with
the assumed ordinary coefficient cosupport, imply whole centre-power
membership for the actual formal series $T(g_\alpha)$: apply the local
coefficient-power theorem to the actual coefficient functions and their
entire Taylor identities. The $T(g_\alpha)$ form a genuine monic vertex
family for $J$, since their original ideal membership, actual coefficient
$1$ and true initial exponent supply ideal membership and every preceding
coefficient vanishing. The canonical compatibility theorem for any such
family therefore puts the actual reduced formal members in the required
whole centre powers. The genuine analytic normalized-member generation
theorem applies to this derived compatibility in each real exceptional
chart, internally constructing the finite analytic family and both entire
ideal equalities. No formal compatibility or convergence certificate is
an input. The hypothesis is the stated original geometric coefficient
cosupport; identifying it with all permissible centres, the full source
semicoherent derivative and Jacobian conditions, transformed stability and
Bennett inequalities remain separate goals.
\end{proof}
