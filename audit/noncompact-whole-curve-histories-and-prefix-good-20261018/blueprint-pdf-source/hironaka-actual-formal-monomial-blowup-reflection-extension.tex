\section{Genuine monomial blowup coefficients reflect the whole center-power filtration}

Keep the actual coordinate center $P=(X_i:i\in A)$ and $e\in A$.
For this chapter take the actual zero-direction chart $\sigma_0$ of the
preceding genuine formal blowup construction. Put $B=A\setminus\{e\}$.
Its literal exponent map is
\[
 T(\alpha)=\alpha+\bigl(\sum_{i\in B}\alpha_i\bigr)\mathbf e_e.
\]
This is the monomial chart underlying the substitutions in
\cite[\S7, pp.~267--272]{bierstone-milman97-invent-math}.
The conclusion is a genuine reflection theorem, rather than a supplied
exceptional-valuation or saturation comparison. Other chart directions
and the all-power strict-transform saturation bridge remain subsequent work.

\begin{lemma}[The actual chart exponent map is injective and gives the true monomial product]
\label{hir:actual-formal-monomial-blowup-exponent-embedding}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-blowup-coordinate-power-image}
The actual map $T$ is additive and injective. For every genuine exponent
$\alpha$,
\[
 T(\alpha)_e=\sum_{i\in A}\alpha_i,
 \qquad\prod_i\sigma_0(X_i)^{\alpha_i}=X^{T(\alpha)}.
\]
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalMonomialBlowupReflection.actualMonomialBlowupExponent,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalMonomialBlowupReflection.actualMonomialBlowupExponent_other_weight,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalMonomialBlowupReflection.actualMonomialBlowupExponent_injective,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalMonomialBlowupReflection.actualMonomialBlowupExponent_exceptional,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalMonomialBlowupReflection.actualFormalBlowup_zero_coordinates,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalMonomialBlowupReflection.actualFormalBlowup_zero_coordinate_product}
\leanok
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The true $B$-weight and the actual single-coordinate insertion are
additive, proving additivity. The actual $B$-weight of $T(\alpha)$
equals the $B$-weight of $\alpha$, because $e\notin B$.
If $T(\alpha)=T(\beta)$, apply this true weight equality, then evaluate
at $e$ to recover $\alpha_e=\beta_e$. At every other index the inserted
single exponent is zero, so equality gives every remaining coordinate.
Thus $\alpha=\beta$.
Since $e\in A$, the true $A$-weight is the sum of the true $B$-weight
and the exponent at $e$, proving the displayed coordinate formula.
The literal zero-direction coordinate image is
$X_iX_e^{\mathbf1_B(i)}$. Genuine power multiplicativity and the finite
product over the actual support therefore give
$X^\alpha X_e^{\sum_{i\in B}\alpha_i}=X^{T(\alpha)}$ in the true formal ring.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every original coefficient survives at its literal true blown-up exponent]
\label{hir:actual-formal-monomial-blowup-coefficient-reflection}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-monomial-blowup-exponent-embedding}
For EVERY actual formal series $F$ and EVERY exponent $\alpha$,
\[
 [X^{T(\alpha)}]\sigma_0(F)=[X^\alpha]F.
\]
In particular the genuine formal algebra map $\sigma_0$ is injective.
\lean{Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalMonomialBlowupReflection.actualFormalBlowup_zero_coefficient,Hironaka.HironakaActualFormalMonomialBlowupReflection.actualFormalBlowup_zero_injective}
\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the genuine formal substitution coefficient formula to the finite
zero-constant coordinate family. Its actual summand at a source exponent
$\beta$ is $[X^\beta]F$ times the coefficient at $T(\alpha)$ of the
true product $\prod_i\sigma_0(X_i)^{\beta_i}$.
By the preceding literal monomial formula, this second coefficient is
one when $T(\beta)=T(\alpha)$ and zero otherwise. Actual injectivity of
$T$ makes the sole surviving exponent exactly $\beta=\alpha$.
The genuine coefficient sum therefore equals the claimed original
coefficient. If two actual image series agree, this identity recovers
every original coefficient; actual coefficient extensionality proves
the original series equal, establishing injectivity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Both whole actual center-power reflection directions and true normalized nonvanishing]
\label{hir:actual-formal-monomial-blowup-whole-power-reflection}
\uses{hir:actual-formal-monomial-blowup-coefficient-reflection,hir:actual-formal-coordinate-power-coefficient-kernel,hir:actual-formal-blowup-normalized-member}
For EVERY actual $F\in S$ and every $d\in\mathbb N$,
\[
 F\in P^d\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad X_e^d\mid\sigma_0(F).
\]
Thus the genuine inverse image of the whole exceptional principal power
$(X_e^d)$ is exactly the actual whole $P^d$.
For every nonzero actual $F\in P^d$, the internally constructed genuine
normalized quotient $[F]_d$ in this chart is nonzero.

\leanok
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Forward divisibility is supplied internally by the already proved
whole-power chart image formula. Conversely assume the actual exceptional
power divides $\sigma_0(F)$. The true variable-power coefficient kernel
kills every target coefficient whose exponent at $e$ is less than $d$.
For any actual source $\alpha$ of $A$-weight less than $d$, the preceding
exponent formula gives $T(\alpha)_e<d$. The true coefficient-reflection
identity therefore kills the actual source coefficient at $\alpha$.
The genuine whole coordinate-power kernel puts $F$ in $P^d$.
This proves both membership directions at every actual numerator and
therefore equality of the actual whole ideal inverse images.
Finally, if the actual normalized quotient of a nonzero $F$ were zero,
its defining literal exceptional-product identity would give
$\sigma_0(F)=0$. The independently proved actual substitution injectivity
would force $F=0$, a contradiction. No target-shaped valuation,
normalization or saturation premise is used.
\end{proof}
