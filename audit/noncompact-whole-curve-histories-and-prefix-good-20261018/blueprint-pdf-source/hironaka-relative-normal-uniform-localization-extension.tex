\section{One genuine original base denominator for the entire normal polynomial quotient}

field, and $H\subset B[Z_1,\ldots,Z_t]$ an arbitrary whole ideal. Fix an actual
well-founded monomial order. Every family and denominator below is internally
produced from $H$; no leading-family, Groebner, basis, freeness or localization
result certificate is supplied by a caller.

therefore give complete leading coverage of every actual nonzero member of
$H_K$ after coefficient extension.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The genuine polynomial ring $K[Z]$ is the localization of $B[Z]$ at the image
under the constant-polynomial map of the ACTUAL nonzero elements of $B$.

constant multiplier over the field preserves the entire actual support and

has precisely the leading exponent of $g_i$. Choose these actual membership
witnesses internally for the finite field family. The original zero and unit
ideal cases are included literally; an empty selected family is allowed.
\end{proof}

the actual coefficient map $B_b\longrightarrow K$ is injective. The same $b$
is used for the entire polynomial quotient, without dependence on a degree
or truncation parameter.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Form the actual finite product $b$ of the leading coefficients of the actual
nonzero original lifts. Each factor is nonzero in the actual domain $B$, so
the true product is nonzero; the empty product is $1$. Each factor divides
$b$, and the image of $b$ is a unit in the ACTUAL localization away from $b$.
Hence every factor becomes a genuine unit there. The actual map into $K$
is constructed by the universal localization lift of the original fraction
map, since the actual nonzero image of $b$ is a unit in the true field.
A localized element mapping to zero can be represented by an original
numerator divided by a power of $b$. Its original numerator maps to zero in
$K$, and injectivity of the original domain fraction map makes it zero.
The actual localized element is therefore zero. This proves true map
injectivity and actual support preservation of coefficient extension.
\end{proof}
